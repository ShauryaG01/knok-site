01 Salary Overview

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Manager (35-60 LPA) is the first rung of people leadership, typically covering someone who has moved from a senior individual contributor track to owning a team. Hyderabad roles at product companies and mid-size tech firms generally land within this band, per knok jobradar data.

Senior Manager (55-90 LPA) usually means overseeing multiple teams or a sizeable headcount, often with budget responsibility and cross-functional scope. The overlap with the Manager band at the lower end reflects inconsistent title usage across companies.

Director (90-150+ LPA) is where you own an engineering function or a major product area. The '+' is significant: self-reported numbers on Glassdoor and levels.fyi at large tech companies and MNCs in Hyderabad frequently exceed the floor of this range, though the sample size is small given how rarely these roles are filled through public listings.

A pattern to keep in mind: GCCs tend to pay toward the top of each band. Indian product companies and IT services firms generally fall in the middle or lower end, per industry surveys.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Ask for the band before sharing your number. When a recruiter calls without listing a salary, open with: 'What is the budgeted range for this role?' Most recruiters will share it. If they push for your current CTC first, respond with a market-based range from your own research rather than anchoring on your last package.

Separate fixed from variable. An offer where a large portion is variable, tied to ratings and company performance, is worth less in practice than an equivalent fully-fixed amount. Ask for the fixed-vs-variable split before you respond to any verbal offer.

Use competing processes as leverage. Even being in interview at one other company shifts your position. You do not need to exaggerate anything. Mentioning that you are 'evaluating a couple of options' is accurate if true, and gives the recruiter a reason to move faster.

Negotiate equity after the verbal offer. ESOPs and RSUs rarely come up before an offer is extended. Once you have a number in writing, ask about the grant size, vesting schedule, and cliff separately. At Senior Manager and Director levels, this can be a material part of total compensation.

Ask about a joining bonus. If you are leaving before your annual bonus pays out at your current company, a joining bonus to bridge that loss is a standard ask at this level. Frame it as covering a specific financial gap, not as a general negotiating tactic.

Counter once, with a reason. A clear counter backed by one piece of evidence (a competing offer, a market rate, a specific skill you bring) is far easier for a hiring manager to act on than vague hesitation.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Fixed CTC is the base and the starting point for all negotiation. For Engineering Managers in Hyderabad, this ranges from 35-60 LPA at the Manager level, going up through 55-90 LPA at Senior Manager and 90-150+ LPA at Director, per knok jobradar data.

Performance bonus is commonly cited at 10-20% of fixed CTC for manager-level tech roles, but the actual payout depends on both your rating and the company's annual performance. Before accepting, ask what the historical payout rate has been at that company, not just the target percentage stated in the offer letter.

ESOPs or RSUs vary significantly by company type. Funded product companies and listed MNCs typically include equity grants at the manager level, with vesting over multiple years and an initial cliff being standard, per industry surveys. IT services companies rarely offer meaningful equity at this level.

Joining bonus is a one-time payment, usually subject to a clawback if you leave within a set period. Negotiate it separately after the CTC conversation, especially if you are forgoing unvested equity or a pending bonus at your current employer.

CTC vs. take-home: Indian CTC structures include provident fund contributions and gratuity provisions that do not appear as monthly cash. Your actual in-hand salary will be noticeably lower than CTC divided by 12. At the higher end of the manager bands, income tax is a significant deduction, so confirm the net-in-hand figure before signing.

Frequently asked

What is a realistic Engineering Manager salary in Hyderabad in 2026?

Based on knok jobradar data from July 2026 covering 16 active openings, Manager-level roles in Hyderabad fall in the 35-60 LPA range. Senior Manager roles sit in the 55-90 LPA range, and Director-level positions start at 90 LPA with meaningful upside beyond that. GCCs and global product companies tend to pay toward the top of each band, while services firms typically land lower.

How does Hyderabad compare to Bangalore for Engineering Manager jobs?

Bangalore had 182 active Engineering Manager openings in the July 2026 knok jobradar snapshot, compared to Hyderabad's 16. Bangalore offers far more volume and faster job mobility between companies. Hyderabad's strength is its GCC ecosystem, which provides structured pay and stability at established MNCs. If frequent moves are part of your career strategy, Bangalore gives you considerably more room to maneuver.

How do I benchmark my salary when most companies do not list pay?

Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for self-reported compensation at specific companies you are targeting. Communities like Blind (Teamblind) have active offer-sharing threads with real data points. Look at LinkedIn profiles of people in similar roles at those companies to understand typical trajectories. Go into every recruiter call with a target range already in mind so you are not negotiating from a blank slate.

What is the difference between CTC and take-home at this salary level?

CTC (Cost to Company) includes components like provident fund contributions and gratuity provisions that are not paid out as monthly cash. Take-home is typically lower than CTC divided by 12, sometimes noticeably so. At the 35-60 LPA range, income tax is a significant deduction given the applicable slabs. Always ask the employer to share a take-home illustration before you accept an offer.

Do Engineering Managers in Hyderabad typically receive ESOPs?

It depends on the company type. Funded startups and pre-IPO product companies almost always include ESOPs. GCCs of globally listed companies offer RSUs instead, with vesting typically spread over multiple years. IT services companies rarely offer meaningful equity at the manager level. Always ask about equity separately during negotiation, and check the cliff, the total grant size, and the current valuation before comparing offers.
