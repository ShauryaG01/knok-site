01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

InMobi is one of India's best-known homegrown tech companies, built around mobile advertising and marketing technology. Headquartered in Bengaluru, it serves clients across the globe and is recognised for building globally competitive products out of India. As of July 2026, InMobi has 91 open roles across engineering, product, data, sales, and operations, which means there is a real window of opportunity right now if your skills match what they need.

The company hires both freshers (for select programmes) and experienced professionals. Most roles go through a structured multi-round process that tests technical depth, problem-solving, and cultural fit. InMobi also owns Glance, the lock-screen content platform, and several other ventures, so the breadth of roles goes well beyond just ad tech.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

InMobi's hiring typically follows these stages, though the exact rounds can vary by role and team.

Application and resume screen: Your resume is reviewed by a recruiter. If your profile fits the job description, a recruiter usually reaches out within a week or two. For engineering roles, this screen may include a short online coding assessment.

Recruiter call: A brief conversation to confirm your background, current role, notice period, and salary expectations. This is also your chance to ask basic questions about the team and the position.

Technical or domain rounds (one to three rounds): For engineering, expect coding problems, system design questions, and sometimes a take-home assignment. Data roles focus on SQL, statistics, and case-based problem solving. Product roles involve product sense, metrics discussions, and prioritisation exercises. Sales and marketing roles often include a presentation or case study.

Hiring manager round: A deeper conversation with the person you would directly report to. They assess your past work, how you think through problems, and whether your approach fits the team.

Culture or HR round: A final round focused on your values, work style, and motivation for joining InMobi. Compensation is typically discussed here or shortly after.

The full process commonly takes three to six weeks from application to offer, though this can stretch for senior or specialised roles.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

InMobi values people who can move fast, think from first principles, and own problems end to end. Here is what tends to stand out across roles.

For engineers: Strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms, experience with large-scale systems, and comfort working through ambiguous problem spaces. Familiarity with mobile ecosystems, real-time bidding, or ad tech is a plus but is not required for most roles.

For product managers: The ability to translate business goals into clear product decisions, comfort with data, and experience shipping features at scale. They want PMs who can work closely with both engineering and business stakeholders.

For data scientists and analysts: Solid SQL skills, familiarity with A/B testing and experimentation frameworks, and the ability to turn analysis into decisions. Domain knowledge in advertising or marketing is valued but not mandatory.

For sales and business development: A track record of hitting targets, strong communication, and the ability to understand client needs and connect them to InMobi's product offerings.

Across all roles, InMobi looks for people who are self-starters, comfortable with ambiguity, and genuinely curious about how technology is reshaping marketing.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with the job description: Read it carefully and map your experience to every major requirement. Identify the two or three things they care about most and make sure those come through clearly in your resume and your interviews.

For engineering roles: Practise coding problems focusing on arrays, graphs, dynamic programming, and system design. Walk through your reasoning as you code rather than jumping straight to a solution. For senior roles, prepare two or three examples of systems you have designed or scaled.

For product roles: Prepare structured answers to questions like 'how would you improve X product' or 'what metric would you use to measure Y.' Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for past experience questions, and be ready to back up your product decisions with data.

For data roles: Brush up on SQL (window functions, joins, aggregations), statistics basics, and be ready to explain your analysis clearly to a non-technical audience. Practice walking through a business problem using data from start to a recommendation.

Research InMobi: Understand their main products, including their DSP platform, Glance, and data products. Read recent news about the company. Showing genuine curiosity about the business and the problems they are solving stands out in interviews.

Prepare your questions: InMobi interviewers appreciate candidates who ask thoughtful questions about the team, roadmap, or business challenges. It signals that you are serious about the role and have done your homework.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

InMobi describes itself as an entrepreneurial company where people are expected to take ownership, move fast, and think big. A few things that define the culture:

Ownership over hierarchy: Employees are expected to take end-to-end responsibility for their work rather than waiting for direction from above. This is especially visible in product and engineering teams, where individuals are trusted to drive outcomes.

Global mindset, Bengaluru roots: InMobi is headquartered in Bengaluru but teams work with clients and colleagues across time zones. Cross-functional and cross-geography collaboration is part of everyday work, which means strong communication skills matter a lot.

Learning and internal growth: InMobi has historically invested in internal learning and encourages people to grow across functions. Many employees have moved between product, engineering, and business roles during their time there.

High performance bar: The culture is results-oriented. People who thrive here tend to be comfortable being measured on outcomes and enjoy a challenging, sometimes demanding environment.

If you are looking for a slower-paced corporate structure, InMobi may not be the right fit. But if you want to work at real scale with a team that has built globally competitive products out of India, it is a strong option worth pursuing.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How do I apply to InMobi?

You can apply through InMobi's official careers page or via LinkedIn. Referrals from current InMobi employees significantly improve your chances of getting noticed, so if you know someone at the company, ask them to refer you before applying directly. As of July 2026, there are 91 open roles across functions, so check the listings and apply only to roles that genuinely match your background.

Does InMobi hire freshers?

Yes, InMobi does hire freshers, primarily through campus hiring and internship or apprenticeship programmes for engineering and analyst roles. For most mid-level and senior roles, they look for relevant work experience. If you are a fresher, look specifically for roles tagged as 'associate', 'junior', or 'campus hire' in their listings.

How long does the InMobi hiring process take?

The process commonly takes three to six weeks from first contact to offer. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds can sometimes take longer, especially at senior levels. Staying in regular touch with your recruiter point of contact helps you stay updated on where you stand in the process.

What is the salary like at InMobi?

Publicly reported salaries on sites like Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest InMobi is competitive with other Indian product-stage tech companies, though the exact package depends on your role, level, and negotiation. Total compensation for senior roles commonly includes a base salary, variable pay, and ESOPs. It is worth researching specific role levels on these platforms before your HR round so you can negotiate with confidence.

Can I negotiate the offer at InMobi?

Yes, negotiation is expected and normal. Come prepared with a clear sense of your market value, based on publicly reported benchmarks from Glassdoor or levels.fyi for your specific role and level. If you have a competing offer from another company, it is appropriate to mention it professionally during the negotiation conversation.

Is remote work available at InMobi?

InMobi generally follows a hybrid model for most roles, with some expectation of in-office presence in Bengaluru. The exact arrangement varies by team and role, so it is worth clarifying during your recruiter call. Fully remote roles exist but are less common and tend to be for specific functions or very senior positions.
