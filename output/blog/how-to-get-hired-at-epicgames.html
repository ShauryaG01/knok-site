01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Epic Games is the company behind Fortnite, Unreal Engine, and some of the most influential technology in interactive entertainment and real-time 3D. The studio operates at a scale that few game companies match, while maintaining strong creative standards across every product it ships. As of July 2026, knok jobradar shows 123 open roles at Epic, spanning engineering, art, design, production, marketing, and business operations.

Many of these positions are remote-friendly, which means Indian professionals, particularly those in software engineering and technical disciplines, have a genuine shot at applying. Competition is real, but the door is open if you know how to present yourself and what the team is actually looking for.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Epic Games moves through several stages, though the exact structure varies by team and role. Here is how it generally unfolds.

Application and resume screen: A recruiter reviews your resume for relevant skills and experience. Matching your resume language to the specific job description matters at this step, since applicant volume at Epic is high and generic resumes rarely make the cut.

Recruiter call: An introductory conversation covering the role scope, your background, and expectations on both sides. Salary range and location flexibility are typically discussed here as well, so come prepared with a clear number in mind.

Technical or portfolio screen: For engineering roles, this is commonly a coding challenge or a problem focused on systems and algorithms. For creative roles such as art and design, this stage involves sharing portfolio work and sometimes a brief take-home exercise. The format depends on the specific team, so asking your recruiter what to expect is perfectly reasonable.

Panel interviews: Multiple conversations with team members, often including a technical deep-dive, a hiring manager discussion, and a cross-functional check. Some teams run these in a single back-to-back session while others spread them across separate calls over a few days.

Offer and references: If the panel stage goes well, the recruiter moves to references and then extends an offer. The full process can span several weeks, so following up politely after each stage is reasonable and expected.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Epic Games hires specialists more than generalists. Across roles, a few qualities appear consistently in what the team responds to.

Depth in your discipline: Engineers applying for Unreal Engine or gameplay roles are expected to have strong C++ knowledge and an understanding of real-time systems. Artists need mastery of the tools and pipelines relevant to their specific area. Surface-level familiarity across a wide range of topics is far less valued than genuine expertise in a focused domain.

Shipped work and portfolio evidence: A list of skills on a resume is less convincing than concrete examples of things you have built, shipped, or contributed to. Game credits, open-source projects, Unreal Marketplace content, or personal game-jam projects all carry real weight in the review process.

Cross-discipline communication: Games require tight collaboration across engineering, art, design, and production. Epic looks for people who can explain their decisions clearly to colleagues with different backgrounds and who can give and receive direct feedback on creative or technical work without taking it personally.

Ownership and initiative: Epic's culture rewards people who identify problems and address them without waiting for direction. Candidates who can share specific examples of spotting an issue and driving it to resolution tend to stand out strongly.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparation is where candidates with similar qualifications separate themselves. Here is a practical approach for each stage of the process.

Get hands-on with Unreal Engine: Even if the role description does not explicitly require it, a large share of Epic's work touches Unreal Engine in some way. Building something with it, even a small personal project, demonstrates genuine interest in the company's core product and gives you something concrete to talk about.

Review Epic's published content: Epic shares a large amount of technical and creative knowledge through GDC talks, Unreal Online Learning, and developer documentation. Reading or watching material relevant to your target role gives you specific topics to bring into the conversation during interviews.

Curate your portfolio for the role: Do not present everything you have ever made. Pick two or three strong examples that are directly relevant to the kind of work the team does. For technical roles, a write-up or demo that shows your reasoning and process is more valuable than just the finished result.

Practice for the technical screen: For software roles, review data structures, algorithms, and patterns common in game development, such as entity-component systems, memory management, and concurrency. Systems design questions at Epic tend to be grounded in real-time and large-scale scenarios rather than generic backend web patterns.

Research the specific team: Epic ships very different products across its teams, from Fortnite live operations to engine tooling to digital humans. Understanding what your target team actually works on makes your answers sharper and your interview questions far more credible.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Epic Games is a privately held company that has maintained an unusually flat structure for its size. Individual contributors often have direct influence on product decisions, and the expectation is that people will advocate clearly for their point of view and genuinely update their thinking when someone else makes a stronger argument.

The company places a high premium on craft and quality. Epic has shown a willingness to delay or rework things that do not meet its internal bar, which reflects how seriously the team takes the end result. Candidates who can share specific examples of holding a high standard in their own work, even when it was inconvenient or required pushing back, tend to resonate well in interviews.

Epic operates globally with a distributed workforce and has made remote work a genuine part of how the company functions, not just a policy accommodation. That said, collaboration across time zones is a real part of the job, and comfort with asynchronous communication matters for candidates based in India.

Beyond games, Epic has been vocal about its interest in open standards for the 3D internet, creator revenue models, and real-time technology for industries outside entertainment. Candidates who have genuine opinions on these topics, rather than rehearsed talking points, often connect better in cross-functional interviews.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Epic Games hire candidates based in India?

Yes, Epic hires for a number of remote-friendly roles that are open to candidates globally, including those based in India. Engineering and technical positions tend to have the most location flexibility. That said, some roles require working from a specific office, so checking the location requirement on each individual job posting before you apply is important.

What programming languages does Epic look for?

C++ is the primary language for most engineering roles at Epic, particularly anything related to Unreal Engine, gameplay systems, or engine-level development. Python is commonly used in pipeline and tooling roles. Some teams work with other languages depending on their product area, so reading the specific job description carefully will tell you exactly what a given team expects.

How long does the Epic Games hiring process take?

The timeline varies by role and team. Publicly reported experiences suggest the process from application to offer commonly spans several weeks, and sometimes longer for senior positions where more stakeholders are involved in the decision. Sending a polite follow-up to your recruiter after each stage is a normal and reasonable way to stay informed on where things stand.

Do I need a background in gaming to join Epic?

Not necessarily, though genuine interest in games and real-time technology is something Epic values across most roles. Positions in engineering, finance, legal, and operations do not require prior games industry experience. For creative and production roles, having shipped game projects or a strong relevant portfolio carries considerably more weight than enthusiasm alone.

What salary can I expect at Epic Games?

Salary figures vary by role, level, and location. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list publicly reported compensation data for Epic Games that can give you a rough benchmark, though the sample sizes for India-based remote roles tend to be small. Epic is generally considered a strong payer relative to the games industry, and discussing the range openly during the recruiter call is both normal and expected.

How do I stand out in the Epic Games application process?

The strongest applications pair a focused, role-specific resume with concrete portfolio evidence of relevant work. Showing hands-on experience with Unreal Engine, even through personal projects or game jams, signals genuine alignment with the company's core technology. In interviews, being specific about what you built, why you made the decisions you made, and what you learned from the process carries far more weight than broad claims about being a team player or a fast learner.
