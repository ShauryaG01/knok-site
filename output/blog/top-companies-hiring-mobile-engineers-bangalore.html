01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Mobile Engineer compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

As of July 2026, knok's jobradar tracked 21 active Mobile Engineer openings in Bangalore. Every one of those 21 roles sits within the city, confirming Bangalore as the dominant hub for this role in India right now. For context, Delhi and Pune each had 3 openings, Mumbai had 2, Chennai had 1, and Hyderabad had none at the same snapshot.

The hiring mix is notable: this is not a GCC (Global Capability Centre) story. Large IT services firms are absent from the active list. Demand is coming almost entirely from product-first startups and one large product company (PhonePe). That signals hands-on work, direct feature ownership, and faster career visibility for engineers who are comfortable moving fast in smaller teams.

08 Salary Range

Salary Range

The knok dataset for this snapshot does not include salary disclosures, so treat what follows as rough context from external sources rather than verified numbers.

Glassdoor and levels.fyi publicly report that mobile engineering salaries in Bangalore vary widely by company size and seniority. Industry surveys commonly cite large product companies like PhonePe paying at the higher end of the market. Early-stage startups on this list typically offer lower base salaries alongside equity. If equity is part of your offer, ask for the terms in writing: vesting schedule, cliff period, and the last known valuation. Publicly reported data on early-stage startup equity in India is thin, so negotiate based on the company's funding stage and your own leverage rather than on broad benchmarks.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Given that active employers lean heavily toward fintech startups and product companies, a few things will set your profile apart from the crowd.

Show platform depth, not breadth. Hiring managers at startups want someone who owns a platform (Flutter, React Native, Swift, Kotlin) end to end. List specific apps you have shipped, with download counts or store ratings if the app is public. Side projects and open-source contributions count here too.

Highlight fintech-specific signals. BLVDPay, PhonePe, lendingkart, and tradelab all touch payments or lending. If you have worked on UPI integrations, biometric auth, or fraud-detection UX, call that out clearly. Experience with secure local storage and API security is a strong signal for this employer mix.

Talk about performance wins. Startups ship fast and often accumulate performance debt. Candidates who can describe reducing app startup time, fixing frame drops on lower-end Android devices, or cutting APK size stand out in screening calls. Frame past wins with specific before-and-after context wherever you can.

Keep your GitHub active. Several startups on this list will look at your code directly. A clean, well-documented personal project in your primary mobile framework is worth more than a vague 'personal projects' line on a resume.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Segment the 10 employers into three groups before you start applying, so you can match your effort to the opportunity.

For PhonePe, expect a structured process with multiple rounds, including a system design interview. Prepare for questions about scale, reliability, and mobile architecture at a platform operating at national consumer scale. Their publicly available engineering content is a good starting point for research.

For BLVDPay, apply quickly. With 5 open roles, they are actively building a mobile team, and early applicants tend to get faster responses at growth-stage companies. Personalise your message to mention payment or fintech mobile experience specifically.

For the 8 smaller startups, a short direct message to the hiring manager or engineering lead on LinkedIn often works better than a portal application alone. Keep it to two sentences: what you have built and why their product interests you.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company is hiring the most Mobile Engineers in Bangalore right now?

BLVDPay has 5 open roles as of the July 2026 snapshot, the highest count of any single employer on the list. This suggests active team growth, which often means faster hiring cycles and more than one entry point into the company. Apply early and tailor your resume toward fintech and payment flows to match their product focus.

Are there GCC or IT services company roles in this Bangalore snapshot?

No. All 10 employers in the July 2026 snapshot are product startups or one large product company (PhonePe). GCCs and large IT services firms do hire mobile engineers but they did not appear in these active openings. If you specifically want a GCC role, filter job portals by company type and run a separate search.

Is Bangalore really the best city in India for Mobile Engineer jobs right now?

Based on the July 2026 knok snapshot, yes, by a clear margin. Bangalore had 21 openings versus 3 each in Delhi and Pune, 2 in Mumbai, 1 in Chennai, and none in Hyderabad. Remote roles exist but were not captured in this city-specific count, so search separately if you are open to working remotely.

What mobile platforms are these companies likely hiring for?

The data does not break down by platform (Android, iOS, Flutter, React Native). Given the fintech and startup mix, cross-platform frameworks are commonly requested by growth-stage companies trying to ship on both Android and iOS with smaller teams. Platform requirements vary by company, so read each job description carefully before applying.

How do I check if a startup on this list is financially stable before applying?

Check publicly reported funding rounds on Crunchbase or Inc42 for Indian startups. Look at LinkedIn headcount trends over the past year: steady growth is a positive signal, sudden drops are a warning. For fintech companies specifically, RBI registration or payment aggregator licences are public information and indicate regulatory standing, which matters for long-term stability.

Should I negotiate salary differently at a startup versus PhonePe?

Yes. At PhonePe, levels.fyi publicly reports structured pay bands, giving you external benchmarks to reference during negotiation. At early-stage startups, base salary may have less room to move, but equity, joining bonus, and performance review timelines are often more negotiable. Always ask for equity terms in writing and clarify the vesting cliff before accepting an offer.
