01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

UKG (Ultimate Kronos Group) builds HR, payroll, and workforce management software used by businesses globally, with India offices mainly in Pune and Noida handling product engineering, cloud infrastructure, customer support, and operations. They have 57 open roles as of July 2026, spread across software engineering, product management, sales, and support functions. UKG is a stable, mid-to-large technology employer, and getting hired here is competitive but very achievable with the right preparation. Their products serve HR and people-management teams, so the company tends to value candidates who understand business workflows as much as they do code or process.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

UKG's hiring process runs through several stages. The exact steps vary by team and location, but candidates commonly describe a sequence like this.

Application and screening. A recruiter reviews your profile against the job description. Strong keyword alignment with the role helps you clear the initial filter.

Recruiter call. A short call to confirm your location preference, notice period, and salary expectations. This is also where you get a feel for the team and timeline.

Technical or functional assessment. For engineering roles, expect a coding test covering data structures and algorithms. Non-technical roles may involve a written case study or domain-specific exercise.

Technical interview rounds. One or two rounds with engineers or team leads, covering your core domain skills, past project walkthroughs, and sometimes system design for senior roles.

Managerial or cross-functional round. A behavioral discussion focused on how you handle deadlines, conflict, and ambiguity. Past examples structured in STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) work well here.

HR and offer round. Background verification and offer discussion. UKG uses third-party BGV vendors, so keep your documents ready. The full process from first recruiter contact to offer letter typically spans a few weeks.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

UKG hires people who combine technical depth with clear communication and genuine interest in how organisations manage their workforce.

For engineering roles, they look for solid fundamentals in data structures and algorithms, hands-on experience with Java, .NET, Python, or cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), and familiarity with agile development and CI/CD pipelines. Senior candidates should also be comfortable discussing distributed systems and microservices architecture.

For non-engineering roles in sales, support, or HR operations, they value people who can explain complex product features simply and who have experience working with enterprise clients. A background in payroll, workforce scheduling, or compliance is a genuine differentiator because their products are built around those very domains.

Across all roles, they consistently look for ownership, proactive communication, and the ability to work across time zones, since India teams collaborate closely with counterparts in the US and Europe.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product first. Spend time on UKG's website reading about UKG Pro, UKG Ready, and UKG Dimensions. Being able to connect your past work to the problems their software solves signals real interest rather than opportunistic job-hopping.

Strengthen your technical fundamentals. For engineering roles, practice problems covering arrays, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming on platforms like LeetCode. Review system design concepts if you are applying for senior positions.

Prepare your project stories. UKG interviewers often ask deep follow-up questions about decisions you made in past projects. Pick two or three projects and be ready to explain the context, your specific contribution, tradeoffs you navigated, and the outcomes.

Look up real interview experiences. Glassdoor and levels.fyi have interview reviews from UKG India candidates. Filtering by your role and city gives you a realistic picture of which question areas to focus on.

Get your documents in order early. BGV at UKG checks education certificates, employment history, and payslips. Gaps or mismatches slow the process down considerably.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

UKG's stated culture is built around a 'Life-work Technology' philosophy, the idea that caring for employees leads to better business performance. In practice this shows up as flexible working options, employee resource groups focused on inclusion and wellness, and a management style that candidates often describe as collaborative rather than competitive.

India teams work closely with US counterparts, which means early-morning calls are a regular part of the job in product and engineering functions. This setup gives you meaningful exposure to global product decisions, but it does require some schedule flexibility on your side.

Career growth at UKG tends to be steady rather than fast. Internal mobility across teams and functions is genuinely encouraged, and many employees move into new roles after a year or more without leaving the company. If rapid title progression matters to you, discuss your growth expectations openly with your manager from the beginning rather than assuming it will happen on its own.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does UKG hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

UKG does hire at entry level, mainly through campus programs and graduate-trainee tracks. Most of the 57 open roles currently listed lean toward candidates with a couple of years of experience. If you are a fresher, focus on their campus hiring announcements or internship programs rather than applying directly to experienced-hire job postings.

What salary can I expect at UKG India?

Glassdoor and levels.fyi list compensation data for UKG India roles, and publicly reported figures suggest packages that are broadly competitive with other product-based MNCs in Pune and Noida. Filter both platforms by your specific job title and city to get the most relevant numbers before you enter the offer discussion.

How long does the UKG hiring process take?

Most candidates report completing the process within a few weeks from the recruiter call to the offer letter. Delays typically happen between rounds when multiple internal approvals are needed. Following up politely with your recruiter after each stage helps keep things moving without coming across as pushy.

Can I negotiate the offer at UKG?

Yes, negotiation is a normal part of the process. Come prepared with a specific number backed by Glassdoor or levels.fyi data for similar roles in your city. Recruiters typically have some flexibility, especially if you can reference a competing offer or clearly explain the gap between their number and your expectation.

What happens if I do not clear the coding test?

UKG generally allows re-application after a waiting period. Use the gap to strengthen weak areas and practice more consistently on coding platforms before reapplying. Some candidates have also found success approaching a different team or role within UKG, though this is not a guaranteed reset of the evaluation process.

Is UKG a good company for long-term career growth in India?

UKG offers stable employment, meaningful exposure to enterprise-scale HR technology, and genuine internal mobility for people who actively seek it out. Growth tends to reward consistent delivery and proactive conversations with your manager rather than just tenure. If you have clear ambitions around seniority or scope, raise them early so your manager can create aligned opportunities rather than waiting for a formal review cycle.
