01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Data Scientist compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Pune's Data Science market runs across three tracks, and what impresses interviewers depends heavily on which type of company you are targeting.

GCC and services roles (NICE, Roche, EXL, Zensar, Virtusa): Depth matters here. Show work where you designed a rigorous experiment, communicated findings to a non-technical audience, and directly influenced a business decision. Cross-functional or global project experience is a strong differentiator. Roche in particular will value any exposure to clinical or life sciences data.

Product and startup roles (PubMatic, ZeMoSo, Kaleidofin, Deepintent, UsefulBI): Ownership is the keyword. Interviewers at these companies want to see that you took a problem from raw, messy data all the way to a deployed model and a measurable outcome. A detailed GitHub profile, a well-documented Kaggle project, or a personal data project with real-world results will carry more weight here than at MNCs. Adtech roles at PubMatic and Deepintent also look for familiarity with large-scale pipelines and real-time data processing.

Skills that appear consistently across all Pune Data Scientist postings right now: Python (pandas, scikit-learn), SQL, and hands-on experience with at least one cloud platform such as AWS or Azure.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start by sorting the ten companies into the track that fits your background and goals (GCC, services, or product). Tailor your resume once per track rather than sending the same CV everywhere. A GCC-facing resume leads with statistical depth and stakeholder impact; a product-facing one leads with end-to-end ownership and deployed results.

For each company, search for the hiring manager or Data Science lead on LinkedIn and send a short, specific note referencing something concrete about their team or product. This lifts response rates noticeably and matters more at lean teams like Kaleidofin, ZeMoSo, or UsefulBI, where a direct message can sometimes bypass the applicant queue entirely.

Timing is important: with only 18 openings in Pune at any moment, individual roles fill quickly. Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company in Pune is hiring the most Data Scientists right now?

NICE leads with 3 open Data Scientist roles in Pune as of July 2026, followed by Zensar and ZeMoSo Technologies with 2 each. The remaining seven companies on the list each have 1 opening. NICE is an enterprise tech MNC with a GCC setup in India, so its roles tend to be more structured and process-heavy compared to the startup-style openings at ZeMoSo or Kaleidofin.

Is Pune a good city for Data Scientists compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has significantly more volume (166 openings vs. 18 in Pune right now), but Pune is a real and active market with serious employers across GCCs, services firms, and product startups. Pune is publicly reported as more affordable than Bangalore, which improves the effective value of a Pune offer. The tradeoff is fewer options at any given time, so you may need a longer search window or a willingness to also track openings in Hyderabad and Delhi.

What skills do Pune Data Science interviewers focus on?

Across both GCC and product roles in Pune, Python (pandas, scikit-learn), SQL, and cloud platform experience (AWS or Azure) come up consistently. GCC-side interviews at companies like NICE or Roche tend to go deeper on statistical methods, experimental design, and communicating findings to business stakeholders. Product-side interviews at PubMatic or Kaleidofin usually want to see a project you owned from start to finish, with a clear business result attached.

Should I target GCCs or startups in Pune as a mid-level Data Scientist?

That depends on what you want next. GCCs like NICE and Roche offer structured career paths, global team exposure, and compensation that typically sits in the 18-30 LPA mid-level band. Product companies and startups like Kaleidofin or ZeMoSo offer faster scope expansion and the chance to own more of the pipeline, often with equity alongside salary. If your next move is about deepening expertise, GCCs are a strong fit; if it is about breadth and ownership, product-first companies make more sense.

Do I need domain-specific experience for roles at Roche or Kaleidofin?

Roche is a pharma MNC, so familiarity with clinical data, life sciences datasets, or regulatory environments is a genuine advantage, though not always a hard requirement for every role. Kaleidofin operates in fintech and financial inclusion, so experience with credit, lending, or financial data is helpful. For both, a strong Data Science foundation and a clear track record of picking up new domains quickly will usually get you into the conversation.

How competitive is the Pune Data Scientist job market right now?

With 18 openings spread across ten companies, Pune is a focused rather than abundant market. Each role likely receives a healthy number of applications, which makes early application and direct outreach to hiring managers more important than in higher-volume cities. Your best edge is a resume and pitch that clearly match the track you are going after (GCC, services, or product), rather than a generic CV sent to every posting.
