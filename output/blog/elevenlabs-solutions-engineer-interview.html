01 Overview

Overview

ElevenLabs is one of the fastest-growing AI voice companies globally, known for its text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, and conversational AI APIs. As of mid-2026, the company has 192 open roles, signalling active expansion across go-to-market and technical functions. A Solutions Engineer here sits at the crossroads of technical depth and customer success: you help businesses integrate ElevenLabs' voice AI into their products, run proof-of-concepts, handle escalations, and consult on implementation architecture.

Interviews typically include a recruiter screen, a technical round, and a customer-facing scenario or live demo component. Candidates report that some processes also include a take-home API integration exercise. The overall process is generally fast-moving given the company's growth stage, so prepare thoroughly before you apply rather than after the first call.

02 Most Asked Questions

Most Asked Questions

ElevenLabs Solutions Engineer interviews typically test three things: technical depth with APIs and audio pipelines, the ability to translate that depth into business value for customers, and comfort with a fast-changing AI product. Candidates report these questions coming up most often:

  1. Walk me through how you would onboard a new enterprise customer onto the ElevenLabs API, from kickoff to go-live.
  2. A customer wants to use voice cloning for their e-learning platform. What clarifying questions do you ask before scoping a pilot?
  3. How would you explain text-to-speech latency trade-offs to a product manager with no technical background?
  4. Describe a situation where a customer's use case was at the edge of what the platform could support. How did you handle it?
  5. How do you demo ElevenLabs' dubbing feature to a media company that is evaluating three competing vendors?
  6. A large broadcaster says AI-generated voices sound unnatural in regional Indian languages. How do you investigate and respond?
  7. How have you built proof-of-concepts or sandbox environments to help a customer make a faster decision?
  8. How would you prioritize support across five enterprise customers all requesting custom integration help at the same time?
  9. A customer brings up a competitor's pricing. How do you handle the objection without dismissing the concern?
  10. How do you stay current with fast-moving AI voice research and communicate relevant updates to non-technical customers?
  11. Describe a time you discovered a bug or limitation during a live customer demo. What did you do?
  12. How would you scope and run a pilot for a healthcare company that wants AI voice for patient-facing communication?

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Q: Walk me through how you would onboard a new enterprise customer onto the ElevenLabs API.

*Situation:* At my previous company, a fintech client signed up for our API product but had no internal developer ready to lead integration.

*Task:* I was assigned as their Solutions Engineer with four weeks to get them to a working demo before their board review.

*Action:* I started with a kickoff call to map their existing tech stack and identify the simplest integration path. I built a Postman collection pre-loaded with their credentials, wrote a short reference guide in plain language, and scheduled two weekly syncs. When their developer got stuck on audio format conversion, I wrote a small Python utility with inline comments and sent it over directly.

*Result:* They had a working voice notification feature in three weeks, a week ahead of schedule. The board approved the full rollout, and the utility I wrote was later shared internally as a template for similar onboarding cases.

---

Q: A customer says AI-generated voices sound unnatural in their use case. How do you investigate?

*Situation:* A media client using ElevenLabs for narration told me their listeners were complaining the voice sounded 'flat' during emotional scenes.

*Task:* I needed to diagnose whether the issue was a model limitation, a settings problem, or a script formatting issue, and respond quickly because they had a publication deadline.

*Action:* I asked for three sample scripts and the corresponding audio output files. Comparing their settings against recommended parameters, I noticed they were using an extreme stability setting that was causing inconsistency. Their scripts also had no punctuation cues for pauses. I walked them through adjusting the stability and expressivity sliders and showed them how to add pause markers for dramatic effect.

*Result:* Revised samples passed their editorial review within two days. I documented the settings guide and proactively shared it with three other media customers who had reported similar feedback.

---

Q: How do you handle supporting multiple enterprise customers at the same time?

*Situation:* In one quarter I was the primary Solutions Engineer for five active enterprise accounts, two of which were in critical go-live phases simultaneously.

*Task:* I needed to keep all five customers moving without any of them feeling deprioritized.

*Action:* I mapped each account by urgency and business impact in a simple tracker. For the two go-live accounts I set up a short daily check-in. For the other three I sent proactive weekly status notes so they knew where things stood without needing to chase me. I flagged the resource crunch to my manager early and got a second SE assigned to one account.

*Result:* Both go-live accounts launched on schedule. Customer satisfaction scores across all five accounts were positive in the quarterly review. My manager adopted the prioritization tracker as a team-wide template.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

Most Solutions Engineer interview questions at ElevenLabs fall into a few categories. Here is how to frame your thinking for each.

Technical 'how would you' questions: Start with discovery (what does the customer actually need?), then move to approach (what is the simplest integration path?), then risk (what could go wrong and how do you mitigate it?). Do not jump to architecture before showing customer empathy first.

Behavioural questions: Use STAR: Situation (brief context), Task (your specific responsibility), Action (what you actually did, step by step), Result (measurable outcome or clear learning). Keep Situation and Task short. Spend most of your answer on Action and Result.

Competitor and objection-handling questions: Acknowledge the competitor's point honestly, then redirect to ElevenLabs' specific strengths: voice quality, multilingual coverage, and API flexibility. Show that you understand the customer's real concern, usually risk or ROI, not just price.

Diagnosis and troubleshooting questions: Structure your answer as: reproduce the issue, isolate variables, test a hypothesis, communicate clearly to the customer throughout. Interviewers want to see systematic thinking, not just 'I searched online for an answer.'

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

ElevenLabs Solutions Engineer interviewers typically look for four qualities.

Technical credibility without arrogance. You do not need to have built a TTS model. But you should understand how APIs work, be comfortable reading documentation, and be able to write or read basic code. Candidates who bluff technical depth are caught quickly in follow-up questions.

Customer-first thinking. Every technical answer should connect back to a customer outcome. Why does latency matter? Because a call centre bot with multi-second pauses loses callers. Interviewers want to see that instinct applied naturally, not just stated as a principle.

Comfort with ambiguity. AI voice is still a new space and many customer use cases are undefined when they first arrive. You will be expected to help shape the solution, not just execute a fixed playbook. Show examples of times you figured things out without a clear guide.

Ownership and communication. Solutions Engineers at a fast-growing company are often the single thread holding a customer relationship together. Interviewers look for evidence that you communicate proactively, flag problems early, and close loops without being chased.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Give yourself at least two weeks of focused preparation.

Week 1: Product and fundamentals. Sign up for the ElevenLabs free tier and test the API yourself. Build a small project, even a basic script that converts text to speech and saves an audio file. Read the API documentation end to end. Understand key concepts: voice cloning, multilingual support, streaming audio, stability and expressivity settings, and the difference between plan tiers. Review the ElevenLabs changelog and any public blog posts about product direction.

Week 2: Stories and delivery. Write out your three to five best professional stories using the STAR structure. Practice saying them out loud, not just writing them. Run at least one mock demo where you introduce ElevenLabs to a fictional customer. Prepare two or three questions to ask interviewers that show you have thought about the product direction and the customer segments they serve.

On the day: Have the ElevenLabs developer docs and your own test project open if the format allows. If asked to do a live demo or technical task, narrate your thinking as you go. Interviewers are evaluating how you think, not just what you produce.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Treating it like a pure engineering interview. Solutions Engineers are assessed on customer empathy as much as technical skill. If all your answers are architecture with no customer outcome attached, you will score poorly regardless of your technical depth.

Vague STAR answers. Saying 'I helped the customer solve their problem' tells the interviewer nothing. Be specific: what was the problem, what exactly did you do, what was the measurable result?

Not knowing the product. Candidates who have not tried the ElevenLabs API before the interview stand out immediately for the wrong reason. Spend an hour with the free tier before any screening call.

Badmouthing competitors. If asked to compare ElevenLabs to another vendor, be factual and professional. Dismissing competitors signals poor judgment and makes interviewers nervous about how you would behave in front of their customers.

Jumping to solutions before understanding the need. In scenario-based questions, start with clarifying questions before proposing an answer. Interviewers often design questions where the obvious technical fix is wrong because there is a simpler business solution.

Leaving no time for your own questions. Candidates who ask nothing at the end signal low curiosity or low interest. Prepare two or three genuine questions about the team, the customer base, or the product roadmap.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What background do Solutions Engineers at ElevenLabs typically come from?

Candidates report coming from technical customer success, pre-sales, developer relations, and software engineering roles. The common thread is comfort at the boundary of technical depth and customer communication. A background in audio, ML, or API-heavy products is a plus but typically not a hard requirement based on what candidates share publicly.

Is coding tested in the interview?

Candidates commonly report at least one component involving reading or writing code, often a short API integration task or a take-home exercise. Python is the most frequently mentioned language. You do not need to be a full-stack engineer, but you should be able to write a working script and clearly explain what it does.

How important is knowledge of AI or machine learning for this role?

You are not expected to train models or understand transformer architectures in depth. What matters is that you can explain AI voice concepts, such as why voice quality varies by language or accent, in plain terms that a customer's product team can act on. Staying current with the ElevenLabs changelog and public blog is a practical way to cover this area before interviews.

How long does the interview process typically take?

Candidates report the process ranging from two to four weeks from recruiter screen to offer, though this varies with team availability and role urgency. With 192 open roles globally as of mid-2026, hiring is active, and timelines can move faster than at larger legacy enterprises.

How do I stand out if I do not have a voice AI background?

Build something with the ElevenLabs API before the interview, even a simple personal project. Being able to say 'I spent time integrating the streaming API and noticed X behaviour' shows initiative and genuine curiosity. Most candidates who apply have not done this, so it is an easy and concrete way to differentiate yourself from the pool.

What salary range should I expect for this role in India?

ElevenLabs is a US-headquartered company and compensation for India-based roles varies by level, contract type, and whether the role is full-time or contractor.
