01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Increff is a Bangalore-based B2B SaaS company that builds inventory management and supply chain software for fashion and retail brands. Their products help brands reduce overstock, avoid stock-outs, and run warehouses more efficiently. The company has scaled steadily and counts several well-known Indian and global retail brands as clients.

As of early July 2026, Increff has 15 open roles across engineering, product, analytics, sales, and operations. If you are a tech professional with interest in supply chain, retail technology, or SaaS products, Increff is a strong option worth pursuing. The company sits at an interesting intersection of software depth and real-world logistics, so the work tends to be applied and outcome-focused rather than purely theoretical.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at Increff commonly runs across 3-4 rounds, though the exact number can vary by role and seniority. Here is what the typical journey looks like.

Application and resume screening: Your profile is reviewed by HR or a hiring manager. Candidates who match the requirements are contacted, usually within a week or two of applying.

Initial HR call: A short introductory call to check your background, notice period, and salary expectations. This is also a good moment to ask basic questions about the role and team structure.

Technical round(s): For engineering roles, expect one or two technical discussions. These typically cover data structures, problem-solving, and system design for senior candidates. Some teams use a take-home assignment instead of, or alongside, a live round. For product, analytics, or operations roles, this stage is usually a case study or a domain knowledge conversation.

Managerial round: A conversation with a team lead or senior manager. Expect questions about how you have handled past projects, how you approach ambiguity, and how your skills connect to what Increff is building.

Final discussion and offer: Some roles include a final conversation with a senior leader before the offer is prepared. HR then shares compensation details and handles documentation.

The end-to-end process commonly takes 2-4 weeks from the first contact to an offer, though this can extend if scheduling is difficult or if the role has multiple internal reviewers.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Increff tends to value people who combine technical ability with genuine interest in solving real business problems. The following traits come up repeatedly across roles.

Strong fundamentals: For engineering roles, solid command of backend development, APIs, and databases is important. Java and Python appear frequently in their job descriptions. For non-engineering roles, comfort with data and structured thinking matters more than any specific tool.

Ownership mindset: Since Increff is a mid-sized product company, teams are relatively small. People who can take a problem end-to-end, without waiting to be told each next step, tend to do well here.

Adaptability: You may need to handle tasks outside your core job description, especially in earlier-stage roles. Candidates who treat that as an opportunity rather than an inconvenience are a better fit.

Domain awareness: Knowledge of retail, fashion supply chains, or inventory management is not always required but is a genuine advantage. If you understand concepts like demand forecasting or stock replenishment, say so clearly in your resume and interviews.

Clear communication: Increff works closely with retail clients, so the ability to explain technical ideas to non-technical stakeholders is valued across engineering and product roles alike.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Learn the product first: Spend an hour understanding Increff's two main offerings, their inventory intelligence platform and their merchandising suite. Knowing what problems these solve for retail brands will help you in every round, not just the HR call.

Strengthen your technical base: For engineering roles, revise data structures, algorithms, and SQL fundamentals. For senior roles, read up on system design concepts like caching, queuing, and service architecture. Even a basic refresher helps you perform more confidently under pressure.

Pick up supply chain vocabulary: You do not need to be a domain expert, but knowing terms like inventory turnover, fill rate, and markdown management signals seriousness. Publicly available explainers on retail supply chain basics are a good starting point.

Prepare your project stories: Increff interviewers commonly ask about specific past work. Prepare 3-4 clear examples using the STAR format, meaning Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Keep each story focused and honest about your personal contribution versus the team's.

Come with questions: Candidates who ask thoughtful questions about the product roadmap, team dynamics, or how success is measured tend to leave a stronger impression. Prepare at least 2-3 questions per round rather than saying you have none.

Confirm logistics early: Many Increff roles are hybrid or in-office in Bangalore. Clarify work arrangements and notice period expectations during the HR call so there are no surprises at the offer stage.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Increff operates with a startup pace even though it has grown well beyond the early-stage phase. Teams are typically small and cross-functional, which means you get visibility into how multiple parts of the business work, often earlier than you would at a larger company.

The culture values directness. People are expected to raise problems early, share opinions openly in meetings, and flag blockers rather than quietly working around them. If you prefer a highly structured environment where every task comes with detailed instructions, the pace here may feel uncomfortable at first.

The company places a strong emphasis on client outcomes. Internal conversations tend to be anchored around what is actually working for the retail brand on the other end, rather than internal metrics alone. This makes the work feel grounded and meaningful if you care about seeing the real impact of what you build.

Most engineering and product teams work out of Bangalore, and collaboration across teams is common because the product spans multiple technical and business domains. For professionals who are self-driven, curious about retail technology, and comfortable making decisions with incomplete information, Increff can be a strong fit and a good place to grow quickly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Increff hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Increff primarily hires experienced professionals, but they do bring in freshers and recent graduates for select engineering and operations roles. Check the job description carefully since entry-level openings are less common than mid-level ones. If you are a fresher, lead with strong internships, college projects, or open-source contributions to stand out.

What salary can I expect at Increff?

Salary depends on the role, seniority, and team you are joining. Publicly reported ranges on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest compensation is competitive for a product-stage SaaS company of this size. Verify current numbers directly with HR during your initial call, since these figures shift as the company grows and as market conditions change.

How long does the entire hiring process take?

Most candidates report the process takes around 2-4 weeks from the first HR call to a final offer. Timelines can stretch if panels are difficult to schedule or if the role requires sign-off from multiple stakeholders. Following up politely with HR after each round is a reasonable way to keep things moving.

Is there a coding test or take-home assignment in the process?

Engineering candidates commonly go through at least one coding round, which may be a live problem-solving session or a take-home assignment depending on the team. Non-engineering candidates may be asked to complete a short case study relevant to the role. Clarify the format with HR before each round so you can prepare accordingly.

Are there remote or work-from-home options at Increff?

Many Increff roles are hybrid or in-office, particularly in Bangalore. Some positions may allow partial remote work but this is not a standard arrangement across all teams. Always confirm the work model with HR during the initial call before investing time in multiple rounds.
