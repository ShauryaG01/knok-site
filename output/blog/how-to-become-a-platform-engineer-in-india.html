01 Role Overview

Role Overview

Platform Engineering is about building the internal tools, pipelines, and cloud infrastructure that product teams rely on every day. Where a traditional DevOps engineer runs operations, a Platform Engineer builds a product for internal users: a developer platform that makes shipping code faster, safer, and less frustrating for the whole engineering team.

In India, demand for this role is growing. Knok's job radar found 204 active Platform Engineer openings as of July 2026. Bangalore leads with 29 listings, Delhi has 12, Pune has 10, Hyderabad has 5, and Chennai has 2. The spread shows that while Bangalore is the clear hub, roles are appearing in other major cities too.

Companies hiring range from product startups to global delivery centres and large enterprises modernising their engineering stacks. If you enjoy infrastructure but also want to write real code and shape how developers work, Platform Engineering is one of the more compelling career paths in Indian tech right now.

03 Step By Step Path

Step By Step Path

  1. Get comfortable with Linux first. Before anything else, you should navigate the command line confidently, write Bash scripts, and understand how processes, permissions, and networking work on Linux. Spend a focused stretch on this if you are new to it.
  1. Learn Docker and containerisation. Containerisation is the foundation of modern platform work. Run real applications in Docker, understand image layers, networking between containers, and how to write efficient Dockerfiles.
  1. Go deep on Kubernetes. Kubernetes is the de facto standard for container orchestration in Indian companies hiring Platform Engineers. Set up a local cluster with kind or minikube, deploy real applications, and understand RBAC, networking, and storage. Work toward the CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) certification. It is performance-based, not multiple choice, so preparing for it genuinely builds skill.
  1. Pick one cloud provider and get certified. AWS has the widest adoption in India, but GCP is strong at product companies. An associate-level certification signals structured, validated knowledge to recruiters.
  1. Learn Terraform for Infrastructure as Code. Write real Terraform modules, manage state, and understand workspaces. IaC is non-negotiable for Platform Engineer roles in India.
  1. Build a complete CI/CD pipeline from scratch. Do not just follow a tutorial. Choose a real (even simple) application and take it from source control through build, test, containerisation, and deployment to Kubernetes using GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
  1. Add observability to your projects. Instrument an application with Prometheus metrics, build Grafana dashboards, and configure alerting. Platform Engineers own reliability, and observability is central to that.
  1. Build a portfolio project. Create a small Internal Developer Platform: a self-service setup where developers can spin up environments, check logs, or trigger deployments. Host it on GitHub with clear documentation. This is your proof of work in interviews.
  1. Contribute to open source. The CNCF ecosystem has projects at every skill level. Even documentation fixes or test contributions show you can work in large codebases and engage with a community.
  1. Apply, network, and iterate. Start applying while you are still building. Use LinkedIn and Naukri to track openings. Reach out directly to Platform Engineers at companies you admire and ask for a short conversation.
04 Timeline And Milestones

Timeline And Milestones

This timeline is realistic for someone starting from a general software or DevOps background. If you are starting completely fresh from a non-technical background, add extra time to the foundation phase.

PhaseTimeframeKey milestone
---------
FoundationMonth 1-3Linux, networking, and Docker solid. First containerised app running locally.
Core toolsMonth 4-6Kubernetes cluster running locally, CKA prep underway, first Terraform module written.
Build and certifyMonth 7-12CKA certified, CI/CD pipeline built end-to-end, portfolio project live on GitHub, cloud associate cert done.
Job searchYear 2Actively interviewing, offers coming in, first Platform Engineer role secured.

The foundation phase is where most people slow down. Linux and networking feel abstract until they click. Push through Month 1-3 even when progress feels slow: it compounds fast once the concepts connect.

The certification milestone in Month 7-12 is a real forcing function. The CKA exam requires hands-on performance under time pressure, so preparing for it builds genuine skill, not just exam confidence.

Year 2 is when you shift energy to applications and interviews. You may land a role earlier if your background is already infrastructure-adjacent. The Year 2 framing applies to someone aiming at a company with strong engineering culture, not just any DevOps job with 'Platform' in the title.

05 India Specific Tips

India Specific Tips

Where the jobs are. Of the 204 Platform Engineer openings tracked by knok in July 2026, Bangalore accounts for 29. Delhi has 12 and Pune has 10. Remote and hybrid options are increasingly common in platform roles because the work is largely tool-driven and asynchronous, so do not rule out opportunities based on city alone.

Certifications Indian recruiters recognise. The CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) is the most respected certification for this role in India. The AWS Certified Solutions Architect (Associate) and the HashiCorp Terraform Associate also appear frequently in job descriptions. These are not resume decorations: they signal real hands-on ability because the exams are performance-based.

College background matters less than you think. IIT and NIT graduates have a networking advantage through campus placements, but Platform Engineering is hired primarily on skills and portfolio for lateral roles. Candidates from tier-2 and tier-3 colleges regularly break in through certifications, open-source contributions, and strong GitHub profiles.

Communities to join. The CNCF India community holds Kubernetes Community Days events in Bangalore and other cities. AWS and GCP India communities run free workshops regularly. LinkedIn groups for DevOps India are useful for referrals. Community contacts convert to referrals faster than cold applications on job portals.

Naukri and LinkedIn reality. Recruiters search for 'DevOps Engineer' and 'SRE' alongside 'Platform Engineer'. Include all relevant terms on your profiles. LinkedIn is where direct recruiter outreach happens most for mid-to-senior roles. List the exact tools and certifications, not just generic phrases like 'cloud experience'.

Researching salary. Platform Engineer compensation data for India is not widely published as a separate category. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported ranges for related titles like 'SRE India' and 'DevOps Engineer India', though sample sizes for 'Platform Engineer' specifically are small. Research the individual company before salary conversations. Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the difference between a Platform Engineer and a DevOps Engineer in India?

A DevOps Engineer typically focuses on operations, automation, and running infrastructure. A Platform Engineer builds an internal product for developers, writing more code (Go, Python) and thinking more about developer experience and self-service tooling. In practice, many Indian companies use the titles interchangeably, especially at smaller organisations. As you grow, the Platform Engineer track leads to owning a full Internal Developer Platform and influencing how the entire engineering team ships software.

Do I need a CS degree to become a Platform Engineer?

No. Platform Engineering is skills-driven, and many practitioners in India come from non-CS backgrounds or tier-2 and tier-3 colleges. What matters to hiring managers is your GitHub portfolio, certifications like the CKA and AWS Solutions Architect, and your ability to solve real infrastructure problems under interview conditions. A degree helps with campus placements, but for lateral hires, skills and proof of work carry most of the weight.

Which certifications help the most for Platform Engineer jobs in India?

The CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) is the most recognised and most useful because it is a hands-on performance exam, not a multiple-choice test. The AWS Certified Solutions Architect (Associate) is valued widely by Indian recruiters, especially at product companies. The HashiCorp Terraform Associate is a good addition once you have cloud basics solid. If you can only pursue one certification, start with the CKA: preparing for it makes you genuinely better at the job.

What salary can I expect as a Platform Engineer in India?

Platform Engineer salary data for India is not widely published as a separate category, so treat any single figure you encounter with caution. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported ranges for related titles like 'SRE India', and industry surveys commonly cite Platform Engineering compensation as higher than general DevOps at equivalent seniority, reflecting the broader skill set required. Research the individual company on Glassdoor before salary negotiations rather than relying on averages.

How important is open-source contribution for getting hired as a Platform Engineer?

It is not required, but it is a meaningful differentiator, especially for candidates without a top-tier college name. Contributing to CNCF projects like Kubernetes, Prometheus, or ArgoCD shows you can work in large codebases and engage with an engineering community. Even documentation fixes or small test contributions count. Hiring managers at strong product companies regularly cite an active GitHub profile as something that stands out when reviewing candidates.

Is Bangalore the only city worth targeting for Platform Engineer jobs in India?

Bangalore has the most openings: 29 out of 204 tracked by knok in July 2026. But Delhi has 12 and Pune has 10, and remote or hybrid roles are increasingly common because platform work is largely tool-driven and location-independent. If relocating is not an option, filter specifically for remote roles from the start and build your portfolio to the point where companies reach out to you rather than the other way around.
