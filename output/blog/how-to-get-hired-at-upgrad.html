01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

upGrad is one of India's well-known edtech platforms, offering online degrees, professional certifications, and upskilling programs in partnership with universities and corporates. As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 43 open roles at upGrad across functions including technology, product management, content, sales, marketing, and business operations.

The company hires at multiple career stages, from fresh graduates entering content or sales roles to experienced professionals joining in product, engineering, or senior leadership. Many roles are based in Mumbai and Bangalore, though remote and hybrid arrangements have become more common in recent years.

If you are working in edtech, B2C sales, or a related domain, upGrad is worth watching closely. The current volume of open roles points to active hiring, and the mix of business and tech positions means candidates from a variety of backgrounds can find a fit.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at upGrad typically runs three to five rounds, though the exact structure varies by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Round 1: Application and resume screening. The talent team reviews your resume against the job description. A strong match in domain, such as edtech, B2C sales, SaaS, or relevant tech, helps you move forward faster.

Round 2: HR or recruiter call. A short call covering your background, why you want to work in edtech, and basic expectations around role, location, and availability.

Round 3: Technical or domain assessment. For tech roles, this may be a coding test or a system design discussion. For product roles, expect a case study or product thinking exercise. Sales and marketing candidates often face a situation-based discussion or a mock pitch.

Round 4: Managerial or panel interview. A deeper conversation with the hiring manager or a cross-functional panel. Expect questions on past work, how you handled specific challenges, and your understanding of the edtech space.

Round 5: Final HR discussion. Covers compensation expectations, joining timelines, and any last questions. Offers are typically rolled out within a few weeks after this round, though timelines can vary by team.

Based on publicly reported candidate feedback, the overall process from application to offer can run two to four weeks. Staying responsive to recruiter messages helps keep your application moving.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

upGrad looks for people who genuinely care about learning and education, not just those chasing a job title. Across most roles, a few qualities tend to stand out:

Domain curiosity. Whether you are a software engineer, a content writer, or a B2C sales executive, interviewers want to see that you understand why online education matters and how it is changing careers in India.

Ownership mindset. upGrad operates at pace, and teams are relatively lean. Candidates who can point to outcomes they drove independently, rather than tasks they merely completed, tend to do well.

Communication skills. A large part of the business involves explaining complex courses and career paths to learners. Even for backend tech roles, clear communication is valued.

Data comfort. Product, marketing, and operations roles expect you to be comfortable reading metrics, drawing conclusions from data, and using numbers to back your decisions.

Sales aptitude (for business roles). A significant share of upGrad's open roles sit in learner acquisition, course sales, and relationship management. Comfort with targets, rejection, and consultative selling is a real advantage here.

For tech roles, expect standard assessments on data structures and algorithms for engineering positions, and system design for senior engineers. For product roles, knowing how to write clear PRDs and run user research is a plus.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product before you apply. Browse upGrad's course catalogue, read their learner success stories, and get a feel for how they position programs against competitors. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually used or researched the platform.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. With 43 open roles across different functions, there is no single upGrad resume. If you are applying for a sales role, lead with revenue targets and conversion outcomes. If you are applying for a product role, lead with feature launches and impact metrics.

Prepare edtech-specific case studies. For product and strategy rounds, practice cases like 'How would you improve learner completion rates?' or 'How would you price a new professional program?' These are more relevant than generic consulting cases.

Brush up on the edtech landscape. Know who the key players are, what trends are shaping online education in India, and where upGrad fits in. You do not need to have all the numbers memorised, but showing awareness of the space signals genuine interest.

Practice your 'why edtech' answer. Almost every interview includes a question about why you want to work in this sector. A generic answer about 'passion for learning' is not enough. Connect it to something specific, such as a skill you built through an online course or a gap you see in how India trains its workforce.

Follow up after interviews. A short, professional note thanking the interviewer and reiterating your interest is a small gesture that stands out. Keep it brief and genuine.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

upGrad describes itself as a mission-driven organisation focused on making quality higher education and upskilling accessible to working professionals across India and beyond. In practice, the culture tends to be fast-paced, target-oriented, and entrepreneurial.

Learning is core, not just a tagline. Many employees mention that internal learning is encouraged, and the company often provides access to its own courses for skill-building. This resonates more with people who are genuinely curious and self-driven.

High accountability. Teams are expected to move quickly, and there is less hand-holding than at a large MNC. If you thrive on ownership and dislike slow bureaucracy, this environment tends to suit you. If you prefer well-defined processes and clear escalation paths, it can feel intense at times.

Sales culture is prominent. A significant part of the organisation is geared around learner acquisition and revenue growth. Even in non-sales roles, you will likely interact with or support business targets. Being comfortable with a commercial mindset helps.

Collaboration across functions. Product, tech, content, and sales teams work closely together, especially on new course launches or market expansion. People who communicate well across domains and do not guard their work in silos tend to thrive.

Employee reviews publicly available on job platforms describe a range of experiences, as is common at a company that has grown quickly. Reading recent reviews from 2024 onward gives a more accurate picture than older ones.

How many jobs are open at upGrad right now?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 43 open roles at upGrad. The roles span tech, product, sales, content, marketing, and operations. The number changes regularly as positions are filled and new ones open, so check back often if you are actively applying.

Does upGrad hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

upGrad hires both. Freshers often enter through business development, content writing, or learner support roles. Experienced professionals are recruited for product, engineering, data, and senior sales positions. Check individual job descriptions for the experience range they specify, as it varies quite a bit by team.

What is the salary range at upGrad?

Salary data for upGrad is available on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi, and the figures vary widely by role, level, and function. Always verify current numbers from those platforms before your negotiation conversation.

Is the interview process at upGrad difficult?

Difficulty depends on the role. Tech and product interviews are moderately rigorous, with coding assessments and case studies that require solid preparation. Sales interviews focus more on situational questions and your track record with targets. Most candidates report that the process is manageable if you research the company and prepare role-specific examples beforehand.

Can I apply to upGrad if I have no edtech background?

Yes, many upGrad employees come from outside edtech, particularly in engineering, product, and data roles. What matters more than an edtech background is genuine curiosity about the space and the ability to learn quickly. In your application and interviews, make a clear case for why you are making this move and what you bring from your current industry.

How long does the upGrad hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate feedback, the process typically runs two to four weeks from application to offer, assuming you stay responsive and the role is actively being filled.
