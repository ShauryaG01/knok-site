01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Classplus is a Noida-based edtech startup that helps coaching institutes and independent tutors run their online teaching businesses through a branded app platform. The company has been growing its team since 2024 as it expands into newer product verticals and markets across India. Right now, Classplus has 13 open roles spanning engineering, product, growth, sales, and operations. Most positions are aimed at people with a few years of experience, though senior and lead roles do appear regularly.

Because Classplus is a growth-stage startup, the culture values speed and ownership over heavy process. Hiring timelines are generally quicker than at large IT firms, and the interview focus is less on rote testing and more on how you think and what you have actually shipped or sold.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages below reflect publicly reported candidate experiences and should be treated as a typical pattern. The exact number of rounds can vary by role, team, and seniority.

Stage 1: Application and resume screening
Your profile is reviewed by the talent team or a recruiter. For tech roles, your stack, past projects, and problem-solving signal matter most. For business roles, specific outcomes such as revenue driven or leads converted carry weight. LinkedIn profiles are commonly checked, so keep yours current before applying.

Stage 2: Online assessment or take-home task
Engineering candidates typically face a coding round covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes a basic system design question. Product and marketing candidates often receive a short case study or written assignment. Sales roles may skip this stage and move straight to a conversation. The task is usually self-paced with a deadline of a day or two.

Stage 3: Technical or functional interview
This is a video call, commonly with a senior team member or a small panel. Engineers discuss past projects, code decisions, and their approach to system design. Product candidates walk through a product they have built or a problem they would tackle at Classplus. Sales and operations candidates discuss past performance and how they have handled difficult customer or team situations.

Stage 4: Managerial round
A hiring manager or senior leader checks for ownership mindset, comfort with ambiguity, and whether you would thrive in a startup pace. Expect situational questions along the lines of: 'Tell me about a time you disagreed with a call but still made it work.'

Stage 5: HR discussion and offer
The final call covers compensation expectations, notice period, and joining date. If everything aligns, offers typically come within a few days. Background verification follows after acceptance.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Classplus looks for people who can take a problem from start to finish without needing guidance at every step. A few qualities that come up repeatedly in hiring conversations:

Ownership over assistance means you have shipped things, not just helped ship them. Be ready to talk about your specific contribution and the measurable outcome, not just the team effort.

Comfort with ambiguity matters because the product and the market are still evolving. Candidates who need a fully defined brief before they start often struggle in this environment.

Customer empathy is central to the product mission. The users are teachers and institute owners across India, many of them first-time app users. Understanding that context helps in every function, not just product or sales.

For engineers specifically, strong fundamentals in data structures and system design are expected. Experience with mobile (Android or React Native) or backend services is particularly relevant given the core product.

For sales and growth roles, a track record of working with small and medium businesses or in the edtech sector is a plus, since the primary customer base is coaching institutes of varying sizes.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before your first call. Download the Classplus app or explore their educator-facing platform as a potential user. Think about what works well and what you would improve. This shows genuine interest and gives you concrete material for both technical and product discussions.

For engineering roles, revise core data structures, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming. Practice on platforms like LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks. For senior roles, also prepare a system design walkthrough for a feature that is relevant to Classplus, such as a live class scheduler or a payment flow for small businesses.

For product roles, prepare two or three product teardowns of apps used by similar audiences. Be ready to explain trade-offs you would make, not just what you would build.

For sales and business roles, look up how Classplus compares to competitors in positioning and pricing. Prepare specific examples of past deals or campaigns with actual outcomes. Use numbers where you can, with the context that makes them credible.

Prepare sharp questions to ask. Classplus interviewers tend to respond well to candidates who ask pointed questions about the roadmap, the customer problem, or team structure. Questions like 'What does success look like in your first few months?' signal that you are already thinking like someone on the team.

Follow up after your interviews. A short thank-you note referencing something specific from the conversation keeps you memorable in a competitive process.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Classplus describes itself as a mission-driven company working to make quality education accessible to learners across India through the educators who serve them. In practice, this translates into a culture that is fast-moving and execution-focused.

Teams are generally small, which means your work has direct visibility. A feature you build, a campaign you run, or a deal you close shows up quickly in team metrics. This is energising for people who want to see impact, and can feel overwhelming for those who prefer slower, more structured environments.

The office culture is collaborative and relatively informal. Classplus has Noida as its main base, and most roles involve in-office presence at least part of the week. Fully remote positions are less common here compared to some other edtech companies.

Growth opportunities tend to be organic. Because the company is still scaling, new responsibilities come up often, and people who perform get visibility into larger projects earlier than they would in a traditional company. A mid-level hire taking on a lead function within a year or two is not unusual here.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many roles is Classplus currently hiring for?

As of mid-2026, Classplus has 13 open roles. These span engineering, product, sales, growth, and operations. The number shifts as positions are filled and new ones open, so it is worth checking their careers page and aggregator sites regularly. Setting up job alerts is the easiest way to stay current.

How long does the Classplus hiring process take from application to offer?

Publicly reported candidate experiences suggest the full process can run anywhere from two to four weeks for most roles. Engineering positions with multiple technical rounds may take a little longer. Classplus moves faster than large IT companies, but the timeline still depends on how many candidates are in the pipeline and how quickly the hiring manager is available for final rounds.

What kind of salary can I expect at Classplus?

Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry salary data shared by Classplus employees and candidates, though the sample sizes for individual roles can be small. Compensation is generally in line with what other growth-stage edtech startups in the Delhi NCR region offer. It is worth researching those platforms before your HR discussion so you come in with a well-informed number tied to your experience level.

Does Classplus hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Classplus does hire freshers on occasion, particularly through campus drives or by converting strong interns to full-time employees. Most active listings target candidates with at least one to two years of relevant experience. If you are a fresher, look specifically for internship openings, do well in the role, and use that as a natural path toward a full-time position.

How difficult is the Classplus interview, especially for engineering roles?

Candidates publicly describe the process as moderately challenging. Coding rounds focus on practical problem-solving rather than extremely hard algorithmic puzzles, so solid preparation on standard data structures and algorithms is usually enough. The managerial rounds are conversational but probe your ownership mindset and past decisions closely. Coming in with two or three well-practised stories about things you have built or fixed makes a big difference.

Can I apply to Classplus if I am currently based outside Delhi NCR?

Yes, applications from other cities are considered, particularly for roles where the company is willing to wait for a relocation period. That said, most Classplus positions involve regular in-office presence in Noida. If you are applying from another city, state clearly in your application that you are open to relocating and give a realistic joining timeline, since this affects how the recruiter prioritises your profile.
