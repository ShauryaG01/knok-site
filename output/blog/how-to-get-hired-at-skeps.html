01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Skeps is a US-based fintech company building embedded lending infrastructure. Their platform connects banks, retailers, and lenders so they can offer point-of-sale financing and consumer credit products through a single integrated system. Think of Skeps as the behind-the-scenes technology that powers 'buy now, pay later' and installment lending for financial institutions.

As of July 2026, Skeps has 11 open roles across its careers page. A focused count like this tells you two things: the team is lean and deliberate, and each hire carries real weight. You will not be lost in a mass recruitment drive. Your application gets genuine attention, and so does every stage of the process.

Skeps hires across engineering, product, operations, and business functions. Whether you come from a pure technology background or have experience in financial services, there is likely a team at Skeps where your skills fit well.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Skeps follows a structured, multi-stage hiring process. The exact sequence can vary by role and seniority, but candidates commonly report the following stages.

Application and Resume Review
The talent team screens your resume against the job description. A clear match between your skills and the listed requirements moves things forward quickly. Tailor your resume to each role rather than sending a generic document.

Recruiter or HR Call
A short introductory call to confirm your background, interest in the role, and salary expectations. This is conversational, not technical. Come prepared to explain why Skeps and why this specific role appeals to you.

Technical or Domain Assessment
For engineering roles, expect a coding exercise or take-home assignment. Problem areas typically include data structures, algorithms, and system design, with some fintech context likely woven in. For product or business roles, a written case study or scenario-based exercise is more common.

Technical Interview Rounds
One or more video interviews with the hiring manager or a senior engineer. Engineering candidates walk through their code, explain architectural choices, and discuss past projects in depth. Product candidates can expect questions on roadmap thinking, prioritization, and cross-functional collaboration.

Culture and Leadership Interview
A conversation with a senior leader or a cross-functional stakeholder. Expect questions about how you handle ambiguity, communicate with different teams, and tie your work to business outcomes, not just technical delivery.

Offer
If all rounds go well, the HR team shares a written offer. Fintech companies at Skeps' growth stage typically move from final round to offer within a few weeks.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Skeps operates at the intersection of financial services and software engineering, so the bar for technical depth is genuinely high. But domain knowledge alone is not enough. The company looks for people who think clearly about product, communicate well across teams, and care about the quality of what they ship.

Technical Strength
For engineering roles, strong fundamentals in backend development, APIs, and system design matter most. Familiarity with fintech concepts like credit decisioning, payment rails, or lending workflows is a real advantage, though not always a hard requirement.

Product and Business Thinking
Skeps builds infrastructure that financial institutions rely on. Everyone on the team, engineers included, is expected to understand why they are building something, not just how. Show that you think about the customer problem behind every feature.

Communication and Collaboration
The team is lean, which means clear communication has an outsized impact. Candidates who can explain complex ideas simply, flag blockers early, and work well across functions and time zones tend to do well here.

Ownership and Initiative
Skeps is in an active growth phase. They look for people who step up when something needs doing, rather than waiting for direction. Specific examples from past roles carry more weight than general claims about being a 'self-starter.'

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the Product Deeply
Skeps builds embedded lending infrastructure. Before any interview, spend time understanding how their platform works, who their customers are (banks, retailers, and lenders), and what problems they are solving in the consumer credit space. Reading fintech industry coverage from 2024 to 2026 will help you speak the language fluently.

Sharpen Your System Design Skills
Engineering interviews at fintech companies often involve designing systems that handle financial transactions, user data, or integrations with third-party payment networks. Practice designing reliable, scalable systems and be ready to talk through trade-offs clearly.

Prepare Strong Stories From Past Experience
For every story you tell, cover the situation, your specific action, and the measurable outcome. If the outcome had a business impact, mention it using numbers from your own work history. Vague answers like 'I helped the team' do not land as well as specific, concrete examples.

Know Your Resume Cold
Skeps interviewers go deep on past projects. Be ready to explain every technical decision you made, what you would do differently in hindsight, and what the final outcome was. Do not list skills you cannot discuss fluently.

Prepare Sharp Questions to Ask
Asking thoughtful questions signals genuine interest. Good topics include how Skeps thinks about product roadmap prioritization, how engineering and product teams collaborate day-to-day, and what success looks like in the role over the first few months.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Skeps operates like a focused fintech startup: high ownership, direct communication, and a clear emphasis on building reliable financial infrastructure. The culture tends to reflect the nature of the product. When you are building systems that banks and lenders depend on, precision and accountability are not optional extras.

Ownership Over Hierarchy
Teams at Skeps are lean. There are fewer layers between an engineer or product manager and real business impact. That means more autonomy, but also more responsibility. People who thrive here are comfortable making decisions without waiting for someone to hand them a plan.

Fintech-First Mindset
Working in embedded lending means understanding the regulatory and compliance context that comes with financial products. The culture values people who take that context seriously, not just the technology side of the problem.

Collaboration Across Functions
Because the team is small, engineering, product, and business functions work closely together. Clear written communication and a genuine willingness to engage outside your immediate area are traits the team values.

High Standards for Quality
Fintech infrastructure needs to be both fast and trustworthy. Skeps looks for people who hold their own work to a high standard and are honest about where things can be improved.

Skeps has an active engineering and operations presence and hires candidates from India, particularly for technology roles. The full process at Skeps typically spans a few weeks from application to offer, though timelines can vary depending on team availability and role urgency. Skeps does not publish salary bands publicly for most roles. Do I need fintech experience to apply to Skeps?

Fintech or lending domain knowledge is a genuine advantage at Skeps, especially for product and business roles.
