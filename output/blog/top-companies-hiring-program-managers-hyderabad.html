01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Technical Program Manager compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Show delivery, not just coordination. Hyderabad hiring managers, especially at GCCs, want to see that you have moved programs across the finish line, not just tracked them. Quantify the scope: teams coordinated, releases shipped, risks resolved and how.

Match your profile to the employer type. GCC roles (JP Morgan Chase, Warner Bros Discovery) value experience working across time zones and geographies. Product companies (Microsoft, LiveRamp, Cornerstone) want TPMs who partner closely with PMs and engineers on roadmap execution. Consulting roles (Trianz) prize client-facing communication and structured reporting.

Highlight domain depth where it applies. Nextracker works in solar energy hardware-software, so even a passing familiarity with hardware program management or supply chain logistics will set you apart. Cornerstone is HR tech, so SaaS delivery experience is a clear plus.

Certifications help, but are not a gate. PMP and PRINCE2 are commonly cited credentials for senior TPM roles. Agile certifications (CSM, SAFe) carry weight at product companies. Real project examples beat paper credentials, but having both is the strongest position.

Optimise your LinkedIn headline for search. Recruiters at these companies search LinkedIn actively. A headline like 'Technical Program Manager, Agile, Cross-functional Delivery, BFSI / SaaS' is more findable than just a job title.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply with intent, not volume. With 12 openings across 8 companies, this is a manageable target list. Research each company before applying: understand their product, any recent news, and what the TPM role specifically owns.

Prioritise early applications. Roles at companies like Microsoft and JP Morgan Chase fill quickly. If you spot a posting, apply within the first few days. Recruiters often shortlist before the official deadline closes.

Follow up after applying. Find the recruiter or hiring manager on LinkedIn and send a short note: one line on your background, one line on why this specific role interests you. Keep it brief, under five sentences. This works better than a generic connection request.

Keep two resume versions ready. One focused on delivery metrics and cross-functional ownership for product companies, and one focused on client management and stakeholder reporting for GCCs and consulting firms. Switching the top section for each application is a small effort that meaningfully improves how well you match the job description.

Frequently asked

Which company has the most TPM openings in Hyderabad right now?

Nextracker leads with 3 open roles, followed by Trianz and Cornerstone with 2 each. The remaining 5 openings are single roles spread across Microsoft, JP Morgan Chase, Warner Bros Discovery, LiveRamp, and Waferwire Technology Solutions. This data is from knok jobradar as of July 2026 and can change as companies open or close positions.

Are Hyderabad TPM roles mostly at GCCs or product companies?

Both are well represented. JP Morgan Chase and Warner Bros Discovery are GCCs, where you work on globally scoped programs from Hyderabad. Microsoft, LiveRamp, Cornerstone, and Nextracker are product companies. Trianz is an IT consulting firm. Each type has a different culture and rewards different strengths, so it is worth identifying which suits your background before you apply.

How does Hyderabad compare to Bangalore for TPM jobs?

Bangalore has the most openings nationally at 41, making it the primary market for TPMs in India right now. Hyderabad's 12 openings still represent real demand, particularly from GCCs and product companies. If you are based in Hyderabad, the competition per role may be relatively lower, and you avoid the relocation cost entirely. For candidates open to moving, Bangalore offers a wider set of options.

What skills are Hyderabad TPM roles typically looking for?

Based on the employer mix, the most relevant skills are cross-functional program delivery, stakeholder management, Agile or Scrum, and risk tracking. GCC roles commonly also require experience coordinating across time zones and geographies. Product companies lean toward roadmap execution and close engineering partnership. Domain knowledge in clean energy (relevant for Nextracker) or HR tech (relevant for Cornerstone) is a bonus at those specific companies.

Do I need a technical background to apply for these TPM roles?

Most TPM roles expect some technical grounding, since you coordinate engineering teams and need to understand scope, dependencies, and technical risk. A background in software engineering, IT, or a related field is commonly required or preferred. The depth varies: GCC and consulting TPMs often need program management skills more than deep coding knowledge, while product company TPMs are expected to engage more directly with engineering decisions.

How quickly do TPM roles in Hyderabad get filled?

Roles at larger companies like Microsoft and JP Morgan Chase can move fast, with shortlisting often happening within the first week or two of a posting going live. Smaller firms and services companies may keep roles open longer. Applying within a few days of a posting appearing and following up with the recruiter on LinkedIn are the two most reliable ways to stay ahead of the shortlisting cycle.
