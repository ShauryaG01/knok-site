01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Data Analyst compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Delhi NCR has 22 open Data Analyst roles as of 8 July 2026 (per knok jobradar). That makes it the second-most active city in India for this role right now, behind Bangalore (41 roles) and ahead of Mumbai (19), Hyderabad (14), Pune (10), and Chennai (5).

One thing stands out about the Delhi NCR mix: this is not a GCC-heavy market. Unlike Bangalore and Hyderabad, where many Data Analyst openings sit inside Global Capability Centres of multinational companies, Delhi NCR's current hirers are mostly Indian companies. The list spans a large analytics services firm, an NBFC, a Tata group subsidiary, and a range of funded startups across fintech, D2C, edtech, and agri. If you want variety in sector exposure without relocating, the options here are genuine.

Company type shifts where you land within a band. Product startups like Atlys, commonly cited on Glassdoor and in analyst communities as paying toward the upper end of their band, typically offer more than analytics outsourcing roles at the same experience level. Power BI is the dominant BI tool in Delhi NCR enterprise and financial services roles, so that skill specifically carries weight here over alternatives.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

With 22 roles across 10 companies, this is a small market. Generic applications get filtered out quickly. Here is what actually helps:

Tailor your resume to the company type. For EXL and Tata Business Hub, emphasise scale: large datasets, repeatable reporting pipelines, and process improvements with measurable outcomes. For startups like Atlys or Nutrabay, lead with business impact. A line like 'built the dashboard that helped the growth team identify and cut high-cost acquisition channels' lands far better than a generic list of tools used.

Make SQL non-negotiable before you apply. Every company on this list expects solid SQL. It is the single skill that opens the most interview doors regardless of sector or seniority. Python with pandas is a baseline expectation at mid level and a strong bonus at entry level. Power BI is the dominant BI tool across Delhi NCR enterprise and financial services companies, so if you are choosing between BI tools to learn, start there.

Show domain curiosity. Capri Global Capital will respond better to a candidate who mentions an interest in credit or collections data in their cover note. Agrim and FairDealMarket will notice candidates who have read about agri supply chain or marketplace pricing. You do not need deep expertise. A short paragraph showing you understand their business is enough to stand out from generic applications.

Have something visible to show. A clean GitHub analysis project, a published Kaggle notebook, or a well-structured personal dashboard gives recruiters something concrete to evaluate. At startups especially, hiring managers often do the first screen themselves and appreciate candidates who demonstrate their work rather than just describing it.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

A focused approach works better than applying everywhere when the total pool is 22 positions.

Start with the highest-volume companies. EXL (4 roles), Atlys (2 roles), and Capri Global Capital (2 roles) give you the best odds simply because more openings means more shortlist slots. Apply to these first with tailored resumes before widening your search.

Do not overlook the single-role companies. Each of the seven companies with one opening gets far fewer applications than EXL does. A strong, relevant application to Khan Academy or GoNoise often results in a faster response and a less crowded process than a generic application to the biggest name on the list.

Update just the top section of your resume per application. You do not need a full rewrite for each company. Adjusting your summary line and repositioning two or three bullet points to match the JD keywords is usually enough to clear the ATS and catch a recruiter's attention.

Follow up on startup applications. For Atlys, Nutrabay, Agrim, and Thriwe, a short LinkedIn note to the hiring manager a week after submitting is normal and expected. Keep it brief: one sentence on why the role fits your background, and a polite request to connect. Long pitches in cold messages rarely work.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company in Delhi NCR is the best starting point for a fresher Data Analyst?

EXL Service is typically the most accessible for freshers because they hire in volume and run structured analyst programmes with on-the-job training. Khan Academy and D2C startups like Nutrabay are also open to candidates with strong portfolios even without extensive work experience. The right choice depends on whether you prefer a structured environment with clear growth tracks or the broader, hands-on scope of a startup role.

How does Delhi NCR compare to Bangalore for Data Analyst opportunities?

Bangalore currently has 41 open roles versus Delhi NCR's 22, so raw volume is higher there. The key difference is the mix: Delhi NCR skews toward Indian conglomerates and homegrown startups rather than MNC Global Capability Centres, which dominate Bangalore's list. If you are based in Delhi NCR or not open to relocating, the market is active and varied enough to build a strong career without leaving the city.

Are Data Analyst salaries in Delhi NCR competitive with other cities?

At entry and mid levels, the differences between cities are modest. Publicly reported compensation on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, commonly cited by the analyst community, shows that senior roles in Bangalore's MNC and GCC segment can carry a premium, but Delhi NCR's financial services and Tata group roles are broadly competitive at those same levels. Always negotiate from your specific offer rather than city-level averages, and factor in cost of living, which also differs between cities.

What tools should I learn before applying to these companies?

SQL is non-negotiable across every employer on this list. Python with pandas is a strong baseline at mid level and a clear bonus at entry level. Power BI is the dominant BI tool in Delhi NCR enterprise, NBFC, and Tata group environments, so it is worth learning if you are choosing between options. Excel proficiency is still explicitly asked for in several JDs, particularly at financial services and traditional enterprise companies.

How many interview rounds should I expect at these companies?

Startup hiring at companies like Atlys, Nutrabay, and Agrim typically involves 2-3 rounds over a couple of weeks, often including a short take-home SQL task. Larger companies like EXL and Tata Business Hub commonly run more rounds and a longer timeline, often including a written SQL or case-study assignment before the first conversation. Applying early in a posting's lifecycle genuinely helps, as roles that have been live for several weeks often already have a shortlist forming.

Should I apply to all 10 companies at once or focus on a few?

A targeted approach consistently works better. Pick the 3-4 companies that best match your background and domain interest, tailor your resume and cover note for each, and submit those first. A strong, relevant application to EXL or Atlys will outperform five generic ones sent across the whole list. Once your priority applications are in and you are waiting for responses, you can extend to the remaining single-role companies that align with your sector preference.
