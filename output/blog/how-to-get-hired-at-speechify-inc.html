03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Speechify's hiring process is selective but moves faster than most large tech companies. The stages below are based on publicly reported candidate experiences and may vary depending on the role.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screen

Your resume is the first filter. The team looks for direct relevance to the role, clear statements of impact, and evidence that you have shipped things in fast-moving environments. A generic application is unlikely to pass this stage, so tailor your resume and cover note to the specific position.

Stage 2: Recruiter or Hiring Manager Call

If your profile clears the initial screen, you will typically get a short introductory call, commonly cited as lasting 20-30 minutes, with a recruiter or directly with the hiring manager. Expect questions about your background, what draws you to Speechify specifically, and your current situation. This is also a good time to ask about the team setup and day-to-day expectations.

Stage 3: Technical or Skills Assessment

For engineering roles, this is usually a take-home problem or a live coding session. Product and growth roles may receive a case study or short assignment. Speechify tends to assess practical, job-relevant skills rather than purely algorithmic puzzles, so the focus is on whether you can solve problems similar to the ones the team faces.

Stage 4: Deep-Dive or Panel Conversations

This stage typically involves one or two longer conversations with team members or cross-functional leads. Expect questions that dig into past projects, how you handle ambiguity, and your reasoning process. Engineering candidates often face a system design discussion here.

Stage 5: Final Round and Offer

The final conversation is commonly with a senior leader or a founder. Mission alignment, long-term thinking, and culture fit carry real weight at this stage. If it goes well, an offer discussion usually follows within a short turnaround.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Speechify operates in a competitive AI space with a small team, so the bar for ownership and self-direction is high. A few themes come up consistently when looking at what the company values in candidates.

Genuine product curiosity. The team wants people who have actually used Speechify or similar tools and can speak thoughtfully about the experience. Specific, honest observations about the product stand out far more than rehearsed enthusiasm.

Comfort with ambiguity. Speechify runs lean and ships fast. People who thrive when given a goal and the freedom to figure out the path tend to do well here. If most of your experience is in large, structured organisations, come ready with examples that show you can work without hand-holding.

Impact over activity. Both on your resume and in interviews, frame your contributions in terms of outcomes. What actually changed because of the work you did? Be specific, and only cite numbers from your own experience that you can speak to confidently.

Connection to the accessibility mission. Speechify was built around founder Cliff Weitzman's personal experience with dyslexia. The team takes the mission seriously, and candidates who connect to it genuinely, rather than as a talking point, tend to resonate more strongly during final rounds.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before you interview. Download Speechify, use it for a week, and form your own opinions about what works well and what could be improved. Bring one or two specific, constructive observations into the conversation. This signals real interest in a way that is hard to fake.

Research the AI and accessibility space. Read recent news about Speechify, note any product updates or announcements, and understand who the main competitors are. You do not need to have everything memorised, but showing awareness of the competitive landscape helps you hold a more credible conversation.

Prepare for 'why Speechify' questions. Recruiters notice generic answers quickly. Connect your personal background or career direction to something specific about the company's mission or product. The more concrete and honest, the better.

Practice talking about past impact. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. For engineering roles, also brush up on the technical areas most relevant to the job description, whether that is mobile, backend, ML infrastructure, or growth engineering.

Prepare thoughtful questions to ask. Asking about how the team makes decisions, what the biggest current challenge is, or how success is measured in the role shows genuine engagement. Avoid questions whose answers are already on the website.

Since Speechify currently has 4 open roles, apply only to positions where your background genuinely fits, and customise each application. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Speechify's culture is shaped directly by its origin story. The company was founded to help people with dyslexia and other reading challenges, and that mission is not background decoration. It influences who gets hired, what gets prioritised in the product, and how the team talks about its work.

Speed and ownership are central to the day-to-day. This is not a company with long approval chains or detailed specs handed down from above. People are expected to own problems end-to-end, which can feel energising if you like autonomy and difficult if you prefer clear structure.

Remote-friendly setup means candidates in India can realistically apply to several roles. However, some positions may require overlap with US time zones, and the employment arrangement, whether direct hire, contractor, or India-entity, can vary. Always clarify this during the recruiter call so there are no surprises later.

Flat and collaborative describes how team members tend to characterise the working style. Junior and mid-level people often have a real voice in product decisions, which is a meaningful difference from working at a large company where influence takes years to build.

For Indian candidates considering a move to a US-headquartered startup, it is worth understanding how compensation, equity, leave, and tax treatment will work in your specific arrangement. These details matter and are worth raising early in the process rather than at the offer stage.

How many open roles does Speechify currently have?

As of July 2026, Speechify has 4 open roles listed across its hiring channels. Given the small number of openings, the team is selective, and each position gets meaningful attention during the review process. Check the Speechify careers page regularly, as new roles can open up as the product and team grow.

Can candidates based in India apply to Speechify roles?

Speechify is a remote-friendly company and has hired internationally. Whether any given role is open to India-based candidates depends on the specific listing, so read each job description carefully for location requirements and time zone expectations. Some roles may be offered as contractor arrangements rather than full-time employment, which affects how taxes, benefits, and compensation are structured.

What does the Speechify interview process look like from start to finish?

The process typically runs through five stages: resume screen, a short introductory call, a technical or skills assessment, one or two deeper panel conversations, and a final round with a senior leader. Publicly reported candidate experiences suggest the full process commonly takes a few weeks from first contact to offer, though this varies by role and team availability. Speechify generally moves faster than large tech companies.

What technical background do I need for an engineering role at Speechify?

Speechify's product spans mobile apps, AI audio processing, and growth infrastructure, so the technical requirements vary significantly by role. Generally, the team values engineers who can ship end-to-end features and work across areas when needed. Check the specific job description for the tools and stack mentioned, and tailor your preparation to those areas rather than studying a generic set of topics.

Speechify does not publish salary bands publicly, and compensation for remote or international roles varies depending on the arrangement.
