01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Henkel is a German multinational with three core business units: Adhesive Technologies, Beauty Care, and Laundry and Home Care. In India, the company operates across sales, marketing, supply chain, R&D, finance, and digital functions. Henkel is an active recruiter, with 310 open roles listed as of mid-2026, spanning multiple countries and disciplines.

Hiring happens through a mix of channels: Henkel's own careers portal, LinkedIn, and partner job platforms. India-specific hiring tends to focus on commercial roles (sales and marketing), supply chain, and technical or engineering positions for the adhesives business.

Whether you are a fresh graduate targeting a management trainee programme or an experienced professional looking for a mid-senior role, Henkel runs structured hiring cycles throughout the year.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs in four stages, though the exact steps can vary by role, level, and business unit.

Stage 1: Application and initial screening

Submit your application through Henkel's careers portal or a job platform. The talent team reviews resumes against the role requirements. If shortlisted, you will receive an email or call to confirm your interest and check basic details such as notice period and location preference.

Stage 2: HR discussion

A recruiter or HR business partner connects with you for a structured conversation. Expect questions about your background, why you want to join Henkel, and your career goals. This round also covers compensation expectations, so have a clear number ready before the call. The conversation is typically brief and informal in tone.

Stage 3: Functional or technical round

This is the core evaluation, usually with the hiring manager or a panel from the relevant business unit. Expect competency-based questions ('Tell me about a time you...') alongside role-specific questions. For commercial roles this often includes a case or scenario exercise. For technical roles in adhesives or R&D, expect domain knowledge questions. There may be one or two rounds at this stage depending on the seniority of the role.

Stage 4: Senior leadership or final round

For mid-senior and leadership roles, a final round with a director or VP-level interviewer is common. This round focuses on strategic thinking, cultural alignment, and long-term ambition. Feedback and offer discussion typically follow within a few weeks of this stage.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Henkel looks for a specific combination of skills and mindset, shaped by its global operating model and its focus on sustainability.

Drive and ownership: Henkel interviewers consistently probe for examples of initiative. They want to see that you pushed for results independently, not just executed instructions.

Cross-functional collaboration: Because Henkel's business units work closely with shared services and global teams, the ability to work across functions and cultures matters a lot. Mention experiences where you coordinated with teams outside your direct function.

Analytical thinking: Whether you are applying to marketing, supply chain, or finance, Henkel places a premium on data-informed decisions. Be ready to walk through how you approach a problem numerically.

Sustainability awareness: Henkel has made public commitments around climate and circular economy targets. Showing genuine knowledge of these, and connecting them to the role you are applying for, signals cultural fit.

Adaptability: Henkel operates in fast-changing categories. Candidates who demonstrate comfort with ambiguity and an ability to pivot tend to score well.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A structured preparation plan covering Henkel's business, the role, and your own story will put you ahead of most candidates.

Know the three business units: Adhesive Technologies, Beauty Care, and Laundry and Home Care are distinct businesses with different competitive dynamics. Know which one you are interviewing for and research its key brands and market position in India.

Study the 'Pioneer, Achieve, Care' values: Henkel's stated values (PAC) show up directly in interview questions. Prepare one or two concrete examples from your past that map to each value before you walk in.

Prepare STAR stories: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Henkel interviewers use structured competency-based interviewing, so rehearsed STAR answers to common themes such as leading change, working cross-functionally, and handling failure are essential.

Research sustainability commitments: Henkel publishes annual sustainability reports. Reading even the highlights lets you speak to the company's direction with confidence rather than vague generalities.

Practice a case or scenario: For commercial and supply chain roles, prepare to work through a simple business problem out loud. You do not need a consulting-style deck, just a structured verbal approach that shows how you think.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Henkel describes its culture through three words: Pioneer, Achieve, Care. In practice, the culture reflects its German-multinational roots: structured, process-oriented, and long-term in thinking, while also pushing teams in India to move fast in competitive consumer markets.

Pioneer signals that Henkel wants employees who challenge the status quo and bring new ideas rather than waiting for top-down direction.

Achieve reflects the company's performance orientation. Henkel ties individual goals to business outcomes, and performance reviews are taken seriously across levels.

Care covers people, communities, and the planet. The company has publicly stated targets around zero-carbon manufacturing and sustainable packaging, and this value shows up in how teams are expected to make day-to-day decisions.

For Indian professionals, Henkel's culture tends to reward those who take visible ownership of outcomes, communicate proactively with global stakeholders, and show comfort working in matrix structures where your functional head and business unit lead may be in different countries.

How long does Henkel's hiring process take from application to offer?

The timeline varies by role and business unit. Generally, candidates report the full process taking a few weeks from first contact to offer, though senior or specialist roles can run longer. Following up politely with your recruiter after each round is perfectly acceptable and usually welcomed.

Does Henkel hire freshers in India?

Yes. Henkel runs management trainee and graduate programmes targeting campus hires in disciplines like engineering, management, and science.
