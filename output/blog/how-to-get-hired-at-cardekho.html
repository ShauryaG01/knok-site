01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

CarDekho is one of India's most recognised auto-tech platforms, connecting buyers, sellers, dealers, and financiers across the new and used car market. The company is part of GirnarSoft, headquartered in Jaipur, and has expanded over the years into car insurance, vehicle loans, and EV listings.

As of July 2026, knok job radar shows 7 open roles at CarDekho. These span functions like software engineering, product management, data science, digital marketing, sales, and customer operations. Whether you are a fresh graduate or an experienced professional, the company hires across levels and cities.

CarDekho competes in a fast-moving space where product quality, dealer relationships, and data capabilities matter a great deal. Hiring tends to reflect these priorities: the company looks for people who can move quickly, handle ambiguity, and stay close to real user problems.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at CarDekho typically runs three to five rounds, though the exact structure varies by role, team, and seniority. Here is a stage-by-stage picture based on patterns that candidates commonly report.

Round 1: Application and Resume Screen
The recruiter or an automated system checks if your profile matches the role. For technical roles, your GitHub profile, portfolio, or past company names carry weight at this stage. Make sure your resume clearly highlights relevant skills and measurable outcomes before you apply.

Round 2: HR / Recruiter Call
A short call to confirm basic details: notice period, location, salary expectations, and a quick background check. This round is mostly a formality, but be ready to explain any gaps or frequent job changes concisely and without hesitation.

Round 3: Technical or Functional Assessment
For engineering roles, this is usually a coding round covering data structures, problem-solving, and sometimes a system design question. For product, marketing, or operations roles, expect a case study or a take-home assignment. Depending on seniority, there may be one or two rounds at this level before moving forward.

Round 4: Hiring Manager Round
A deeper conversation with the team lead or hiring manager. Expect questions on past projects, how you handled specific situations, and your thinking process. For senior roles, this round may include a live problem-solving session or a short presentation to a small panel.

Round 5: Final HR Round
Compensation discussion, culture fit check, and offer formalities. Having your documents ready and being clear on your notice period helps speed things up considerably at this stage.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

CarDekho looks for a combination of technical competence and business awareness, particularly around how technology serves real users in a price-sensitive, high-consideration market like car buying.

For engineering roles, strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms matter, but so does practical experience building scalable systems. The platform handles large volumes of listings, leads, and transactions, so candidates who have worked on performance, reliability, or data pipelines tend to stand out.

For product roles, the company values people who understand the Indian consumer, can read data to form opinions, and have shipped features end-to-end. Experience with marketplace or fintech products is a clear plus.

For sales and operations roles, the emphasis shifts to communication, target orientation, and dealer or partner relationship management. CarDekho works with a large dealer network across India, so field experience in automotive or consumer products is valued highly.

Across all roles, interviewers commonly note a preference for people who take ownership, adapt quickly, and do not wait for perfect information before acting.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study the product before the interview. Spend time on CarDekho's app and website, the used car platform, and the insurance and loan products. Interviewers appreciate candidates who reference real product observations rather than generic answers.

For engineering candidates, revise core data structures, algorithms, and at least one area of system design relevant to your target role, for example search, recommendations, or payment flows. Knowing the broad shape of CarDekho's platform helps you ask sharp questions too.

For product and business roles, prepare two or three structured case responses based on metrics you have actually moved in past roles. Be ready to discuss how you would improve a specific feature on the CarDekho platform.

Brush up on the auto-tech space. Know the key players, understand where CarDekho sits in the market, and have a point of view on the EV shift and how it may affect the platform. Interviewers ask this more often than candidates expect.

Prepare your 'why CarDekho' answer honestly. The company is a mid-size tech firm competing with both startups and large platforms. A genuine answer about the problem space, the scale, or the product stage tends to land better than vague enthusiasm.

Follow up after rounds. CarDekho's process can sometimes stretch across several weeks. A polite follow-up to your recruiter after three to five business days shows interest without being pushy. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

CarDekho operates with the energy of a company that has been building for over a decade but still sees large parts of its market as untapped. The Jaipur headquarters gives the culture a grounded, execution-focused feel, distinct from the more cosmopolitan startup cultures of Bengaluru or Mumbai.

Ownership is central. Teams are expected to drive outcomes, not just complete tasks. You will likely get real responsibility early, but vague delivery or excessive hand-holding is not the norm here.

The pace is fast but not chaotic. CarDekho has lived through multiple market cycles, and the company values people who can sustain performance over time rather than just sprint in short bursts.

Cross-functional collaboration matters. The lines between product, engineering, and business teams are relatively thin. Engineers are expected to understand business context; product managers are expected to get into implementation details.

The company values learning. Several CarDekho alumni have gone on to senior roles at other tech companies, and internal mobility is something the company actively encourages. If you are interested in growing into new areas, that tends to be easier here than at more rigid organisations.
