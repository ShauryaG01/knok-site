01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

G-P (formerly Globalization Partners) is an Employer of Record (EOR) platform that helps companies hire people in other countries without setting up local legal entities. The company is fully remote and operates across dozens of countries worldwide, making it one of the more genuinely global workplaces you can join.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar shows 7 open roles at G-P. These span functions like sales, customer success, engineering, product, legal, and HR. Because G-P is remote-first, many roles do not require you to be in a specific city, which gives Indian professionals more flexibility than a typical on-site job.

Competition for roles at a recognized global EOR provider can be real, so a tailored application that shows you understand what G-P does and how your work connects to their mission will stand out.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The interview process at G-P typically moves through several stages. The exact number of rounds and who you speak with can vary by role and team, so treat this as a general guide.

Stage 1: Application and resume review. The talent acquisition team screens applications for fit with the job description. Highlighting relevant experience in global HR, SaaS platforms, international compliance, or customer-facing roles helps at this stage.

Stage 2: Recruiter screening call. If shortlisted, you will have a short call with a recruiter from G-P's talent team. Expect questions about your background, your current notice period, salary expectations, and your interest in G-P specifically. Come prepared with your own questions about the team and role.

Stage 3: Hiring manager interview. This is a more in-depth conversation with the person you would report to. The focus shifts to your actual experience, how you handle real situations, and your domain knowledge. For technical roles, this may include a practical exercise or case study.

Stage 4: Panel or peer interviews. For many roles, you will meet colleagues from the same team or from cross-functional teams. These conversations often explore how you collaborate, communicate, and approach problems. G-P values diverse perspectives, so expect genuine dialogue rather than a one-way quiz.

Stage 5: Final or leadership conversation. Some roles include a final round with a senior leader. This is usually more strategic and forward-looking, covering how you think about the space and what you want to build in your career.

Offer stage. Offers are extended centrally. Because G-P is a remote-first company, the onboarding process is fully virtual.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

G-P hires people who are genuinely curious about the future of global work. A few traits consistently matter across roles.

Domain relevance. For HR and legal roles, familiarity with international employment law, payroll, or compliance is a strong signal. For product and engineering roles, experience with B2B SaaS platforms matters. For sales and customer success, a track record of managing complex, multi-stakeholder relationships is valued.

Remote-work maturity. Since G-P operates fully remotely, they look for people who can manage their own time, communicate clearly in writing, and collaborate across time zones without needing close supervision.

Customer empathy. G-P's clients are businesses expanding globally, often navigating unfamiliar territory. Candidates who can put themselves in the customer's shoes and explain complex ideas clearly have an edge.

Mission alignment. G-P talks a lot about making it possible for 'anyone to work anywhere.' Candidates who find that mission genuinely interesting, not just as a talking point, tend to do well in interviews.

Adaptability. G-P has grown and changed its product significantly over recent years. They value people who can handle ambiguity, iterate quickly, and stay focused on outcomes rather than processes.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply. G-P's core product is their Employer of Record platform. Before any interview, spend time understanding what an EOR does, why companies use one, and how G-P positions itself in the market. Read their public blog and any recent news about the company.

Know their recent direction. G-P rebranded from Globalization Partners and has invested heavily in AI-powered HR tools. If you can reference their product direction intelligently in an interview, it signals genuine interest rather than a generic application.

Prepare STAR-format answers. G-P interviewers tend to ask behavioural questions. Have clear, specific stories ready for common themes: a time you managed a difficult stakeholder, a time you handled ambiguity, a time you had to learn something quickly.

Tailor your resume to the job description. Mirror the language in the job posting. If the role mentions 'global compliance,' make sure those words appear in your resume where relevant, not just 'regulatory work.'

Prepare questions that show depth. Asking about the team's current priorities, how success is measured in the role, or what the biggest challenge the team is solving right now signals that you are thinking like a future employee, not just a candidate.

Check your remote setup. Because G-P is fully remote, interviewers notice things like video quality, audio clarity, and background. A clean, professional setup signals that you are ready for distributed work from day one.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

G-P describes itself as a remote-first, mission-driven company. A few cultural traits come through consistently in publicly available employee reviews and company communications.

Remote-first, not remote-friendly. There is a meaningful difference. G-P built its culture around distributed teams from the start, which means asynchronous communication is normal and expected. Written communication skills matter more here than at office-first companies.

High ownership. Publicly reported employee feedback suggests that G-P gives people significant ownership over their work. This is energising for self-starters but can feel unstructured if you prefer a more directive environment.

Fast-moving. G-P has gone through significant changes in product strategy and company positioning over the past few years. The culture rewards people who are comfortable with change and can reprioritise quickly.

Globally minded. With teams across multiple continents, G-P's culture is genuinely international. Indian professionals joining G-P often find themselves working with colleagues across the US, Europe, Latin America, and Asia on a daily basis.

Growth-oriented. G-P tends to promote from within and encourages employees to explore lateral moves across teams. If you want to build a broad skill set in the global HR tech space, the environment supports that.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does G-P hire people in India?

Yes. Because G-P is a fully remote company, many of their roles are open to candidates in India. The 7 open roles currently tracked by knok jobradar include positions across different functions. Check each job description for any location restrictions, as some roles tied to specific regional customers may have timezone requirements.

What salary can I expect at G-P in India?

G-P does not publish salary bands publicly for India-based roles. Glassdoor and industry surveys suggest compensation at global EOR companies is competitive with other multinational SaaS firms hiring in India, but sample sizes for G-P India specifically are small. The best approach is to ask the recruiter directly during the screening call and share your expectations early to avoid wasted time on both sides.

How long does the G-P hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process from application to offer can stretch across several weeks to over a month, depending on the role and how quickly panels can be scheduled across time zones. Staying responsive and following up politely with your recruiter after each stage helps keep things moving.

Is there a coding test or assignment for tech roles?

For engineering and product roles, G-P commonly includes a take-home exercise or live problem-solving session as part of the process. The format varies by team. Ask your recruiter at the screening stage what to expect so you can prepare accordingly and budget your time well.

What is the best way to stand out in a G-P application?

Tailor your resume to the specific job description and make your understanding of the EOR space visible. A cover note that references G-P's product or mission specifically, rather than a generic letter, gets noticed. If you have worked in global HR, international payroll, or cross-border compliance, make sure those details are easy to find at a glance.
