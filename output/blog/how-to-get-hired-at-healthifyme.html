01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

HealthifyMe is one of India's most recognized health and fitness platforms, built around calorie tracking, AI-powered coaching, and access to certified dietitians and fitness trainers. The platform sits at the intersection of consumer technology and preventive healthcare, which means it hires across a wide range of functions: engineering, product, data science, nutrition, content, sales, and operations.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 22 open roles at HealthifyMe. That figure spans multiple seniority levels and teams, so whether you are early in your career or have several years of experience, there may be a relevant opening worth exploring.

Getting in takes more than a polished resume. HealthifyMe tends to favor candidates who genuinely care about health and wellness, can build or think for a large Indian consumer base, and are comfortable with the pace of a growth-stage startup.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs three to five rounds, though the exact stages vary by role and team. Here is what most candidates report:

Resume and application screen. A recruiter or hiring manager reviews your profile to check whether your background fits the role. A targeted resume that highlights measurable outcomes, rather than a generic one sent to every company, improves your chances at this stage.

Online assessment or take-home task. For tech and product roles, this is usually a coding test, SQL problem, or a short assignment that tests role-specific skills. Budget a few hours for this step.

Technical or functional interview. One or two rounds where the conversation goes deeper into your core skills. Engineers typically face data structures, algorithms, and system design questions. Product managers can expect product critique, metrics discussions, and prioritisation problems. Data roles usually involve SQL, analytical reasoning, and business interpretation.

Manager or cross-functional round. This round tests how you think beyond your immediate function. Interviewers look for clear communication, structured thinking, and a genuine connection to the company's health mission.

HR and offer discussion. A conversation about your background, career goals, and the role details. Compensation negotiation typically happens here.

Timelines vary. Some candidates complete the full process in two to three weeks; others report a longer cycle when multiple stakeholders need to be involved in the decision.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

HealthifyMe is mission-driven in the health space, so a genuine interest in wellness tends to stand out. Beyond that, a few qualities come up consistently across roles.

Data-first thinking. The platform makes decisions based on user behavior, retention metrics, and health outcomes. Being comfortable with data and using it to support your reasoning matters regardless of your function.

User empathy for the Indian consumer. HealthifyMe serves a large and diverse Indian user base. Understanding what motivates someone to track a meal, book a session with a dietitian, or stick to a fitness plan is valued across engineering, product, and content teams alike.

Ownership mindset. As with most growth-stage startups, the expectation is that you pick up problems, see them through to resolution, and do not wait for step-by-step direction.

Cross-functional communication. Health tech combines clinical knowledge, software, design, and business thinking. Teams work closely together, so the ability to explain your work clearly to people outside your domain is a real advantage.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product first-hand. Use the HealthifyMe app before your interview. Track a few meals, explore the AI coach, and think about what you would improve. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually spent time with the product rather than just read the website.

Sharpen your core skills. For engineering roles, practice data structures, algorithms, and system design. For product roles, practice product critique and metric-driven problem solving. For data roles, SQL and analytical reasoning are essential.

Prepare specific examples from past work. HealthifyMe interviewers typically ask behavioral questions rooted in real situations. Use concrete examples: what the problem was, what you did, and what resulted. Keep answers focused rather than broad.

Research the health tech landscape in India. Knowing who the users are, what gaps exist in digital health, and where HealthifyMe fits shows that you are engaged with the mission and not just looking for any job title.

Prepare questions to ask. Asking about team structure, the product roadmap, or how success is measured in the role signals genuine interest and helps you assess whether the company is the right fit for you.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

HealthifyMe operates with the energy and pace of a growth-stage startup. Priorities can shift, scope can expand quickly, and the expectation is that people step up when needed. If you prefer a heavily structured environment with defined processes for every decision, this may take some adjustment.

The company has a strong mission thread running through it. Employees commonly mention the sense of purpose that comes from working on a product that directly affects people's health. This shows up in how product decisions get made and what teams prioritize when resources are limited.

Relatively flat hierarchy. Teams tend to be lean and collaborative. Well-reasoned ideas from junior members get taken seriously, particularly when backed by data or user evidence. Credit for outcomes is generally shared rather than concentrated at the top.

Continuous learning as a baseline. Health tech moves fast, and HealthifyMe updates its product frequently. Staying current, picking up new skills, and being open to feedback are part of the job rather than optional extras.

The Bangalore office is the primary hub. Some roles support hybrid or remote arrangements, but this varies by team. Check the specific role listing to confirm location expectations before applying.

How many open roles does HealthifyMe currently have?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 22 open roles at HealthifyMe, spanning engineering, product, data, operations, sales, and content. How long does the HealthifyMe hiring process take?

Most candidates report completing the process in two to four weeks from application to offer, though senior roles or those involving multiple interviewers can stretch longer. If you have not heard back within a week of completing a round, a short, polite follow-up to the recruiter is completely appropriate and usually welcomed.

Does HealthifyMe hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

HealthifyMe hires at multiple levels, including freshers for roles in engineering, content, operations, and customer success. Product management and data science roles typically require some prior hands-on experience. Read each job listing carefully, as requirements differ noticeably by team and function.

What salary can I expect at HealthifyMe?

HealthifyMe does not publish fixed pay bands publicly. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry community-submitted figures that vary by role, level, and team. Your best move is to benchmark against those platforms before the HR conversation, and come in with a specific, researched number rather than a vague range.

Is HealthifyMe a good place to grow your career?

Employee reviews commonly cited on platforms like Glassdoor suggest HealthifyMe scores well on learning opportunities and mission alignment. Growth speed can depend heavily on which team and manager you land with. Asking your interviewers directly about internal mobility, past team promotions, and how career progression is structured will give you a more honest picture than any aggregate rating.

How should I follow up after applying or after an interview round?

After applying, give it about a week before following up, since teams often review applications in batches rather than one by one. After an interview round, send a brief note to the recruiter within a day, acknowledging the conversation and asking for a timeline on next steps. Keep it short and specific, mentioning the role and round, rather than sending a generic message.
