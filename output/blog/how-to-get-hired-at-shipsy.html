01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Shipsy is a logistics intelligence platform built for the modern supply chain. The company helps logistics providers, e-commerce businesses, and enterprises manage freight, last-mile delivery, and cross-border operations through a single platform. Headquartered in Gurugram, Shipsy has grown steadily as India's logistics tech space has matured.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 5 open roles at Shipsy. The team is lean and the hiring bar is high, so competition for each seat is real. If you are in tech, product, or operations and want to work on problems that directly affect how goods move across India and beyond, Shipsy is worth targeting.

Shipsy works with clients across large courier networks, freight forwarding, and e-commerce supply chains. The work has real scale and real consequences, which shapes the kind of people they hire and how seriously they treat the interview process.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Shipsy's hiring process typically runs across 3 to 5 rounds, though the exact number varies by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Application and resume screen: Your resume goes through an initial filter by the recruiter or via an applicant tracking system. A clear, outcome-focused resume with relevant logistics-tech or SaaS keywords performs better here.

Recruiter call: A short call to check your background, notice period, and salary expectations. Be ready to speak about your current role in plain, direct terms.

Technical or skills round: For engineering roles, this is usually a coding or system-design interview. For product roles, expect a case study or product critique. For operations or sales roles, expect situational questions rooted in real logistics scenarios.

Hiring manager round: A deeper conversation about your past work, your thinking process, and how you handle ambiguity. Shipsy moves fast and wants to see that you can keep up.

Final or leadership round: Some roles include a round with a senior leader or co-founder. This tends to focus on culture fit, long-term goals, and how you think about creating impact at scale.

Offers typically follow within a week of the final round, though timelines can stretch depending on team availability.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Shipsy builds complex software for a domain, logistics, that is messy, real-time, and full of edge cases. They look for people who can handle that complexity without losing sight of the end user.

Problem-solving over rote knowledge: Whether you are an engineer or a product manager, Shipsy wants to see how you think through a problem, not just whether you have memorised the right answer. Expect open-ended questions with no single correct solution.

Logistics or SaaS domain awareness: You do not need prior logistics experience, but showing that you understand the space, last-mile challenges, freight visibility, or supply chain constraints, goes a long way in interviews.

Ownership mindset: Shipsy is a growth-stage company. They hire people who take end-to-end ownership of their work and do not wait to be told what to do next. This comes up in both technical and behavioural rounds.

Communication clarity: Because the product touches clients across multiple countries, clear written and verbal communication is valued across all roles, not just client-facing ones.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply: Use Shipsy's website and any public case studies or press coverage to understand what the platform actually does. Knowing the difference between their last-mile and freight modules before your interview makes a strong impression.

Brush up on logistics basics: Even for tech roles, knowing terms like POD (proof of delivery), freight forwarder, or 3PL helps you speak the language of the domain naturally in interviews. A few hours of reading goes a long way.

Prepare STAR-format answers: Shipsy interviewers commonly ask behavioural questions about ownership, dealing with ambiguity, and cross-functional collaboration. Structure your answers with a clear situation, task, action, and result.

For engineers, practice system design: Logistics systems are inherently distributed and event-driven. Practice designing systems like a real-time delivery tracking platform or a route optimisation engine. These are the kinds of problems Shipsy actually solves.

For product roles, prepare a product critique: Pick a logistics app you have used, think food delivery or courier tracking, and walk through what works, what does not, and how you would improve it with clear prioritisation reasoning.

Stay on top of open roles: Shipsy's openings change quickly and there are currently only 5 of them. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Shipsy operates with the energy of a startup that has found product-market fit and is now scaling. The work pace is fast, the problems are real, and the team is expected to move with urgency.

Bias for action: Shipsy's culture rewards people who make decisions with the information they have, rather than waiting for perfect data. If you thrive in structured, slow-moving environments, this may not be the right fit.

Customer obsession: The company's clients are logistics businesses whose operations depend on the platform working correctly. There is a strong culture of taking customer issues seriously and resolving them quickly.

Collaborative but accountable: Teams work closely together, but individuals are expected to own their outcomes. You will get support, but you are also expected to drive your own work forward without being micromanaged.

Career growth potential: Being a growth-stage SaaS company, Shipsy offers the chance to work on problems that scale across borders and to grow your responsibilities faster than you might in a large enterprise. Based on publicly reported employee accounts, many people who join take on significantly broader scope within a year or two of starting.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs is Shipsy currently hiring for?

Knok jobradar is tracking 5 open roles at Shipsy as of July 2026. The count shifts as roles are filled or new ones open, so it is worth checking regularly. With a small number of openings, competition for each role is real, and a strong, tailored application makes a noticeable difference.

Does Shipsy hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Shipsy is a growth-stage SaaS company and primarily hires for roles that need prior experience, particularly in engineering, product, and sales. That said, they do occasionally open roles for recent graduates, especially in tech. Your best bet as a fresher is to apply directly and highlight any logistics-related projects, internships, or domain research in your resume.

What is the typical interview timeline at Shipsy?

From first contact to offer, the process commonly runs two to four weeks depending on the role and how quickly rounds can be scheduled. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds tend to take a little longer. Offers have commonly been reported to arrive within a week of the final round in straightforward cases.

Is domain knowledge in logistics required to get hired at Shipsy?

It is not a hard requirement, but it is a real advantage. Shipsy operates in a specialised domain, and candidates who can speak intelligently about logistics challenges, even at a surface level, tend to stand out. Spend a few hours reading about last-mile delivery, freight management, and supply chain visibility before your rounds and you will already be ahead of most applicants.

Does Shipsy offer remote or hybrid work?

Shipsy's primary office is in Gurugram. Whether a specific role allows remote or hybrid work depends on the team and the position. It is best to ask the recruiter directly during the first call rather than assuming either way, as policies can vary across teams.

How can I make my Shipsy application stand out?

Tailor your resume to highlight outcomes relevant to logistics tech or SaaS, such as systems you built that handled real-time data, products you improved through customer feedback, or B2B deals you closed in a complex sales environment. A brief cover note showing you understand what Shipsy does and why you want to work on that specific problem also helps, especially when there are only a handful of open seats at any given time.
