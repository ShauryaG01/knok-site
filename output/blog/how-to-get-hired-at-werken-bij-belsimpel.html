01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Belsimpel is one of the Netherlands' best-known telecom retailers, selling mobile phones, sim-only plans, and accessories across the Dutch market. Werken bij Belsimpel (which translates to 'Working at Belsimpel') is their dedicated hiring platform, bringing together roles from across the business under one roof.

The company started as a small Groningen-based startup and has grown into a multi-department organisation covering sales, customer service, tech and IT, marketing, logistics, and corporate functions. As of mid-2026, Belsimpel has 57 open positions listed, spanning entry-level to senior roles, so there is a reasonable variety of opportunities to explore regardless of your background.

For Indian professionals, most Belsimpel roles are based in the Netherlands, which means you would need a valid European work permit or be open to relocation. Tech and product roles sometimes carry hybrid or remote flexibility, but this varies by team. If you are already based in the Netherlands or hold an eligible visa, you are well-placed to apply directly.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Belsimpel's hiring process is generally described as structured but approachable, with a focus on getting to know you as a person as much as testing your skills. The exact stages can vary by department, but the typical flow looks like this:

Application and CV screen - The recruiter team reviews your application against the job requirements. A clear, role-specific CV with measurable achievements tends to move through faster than a generic one. Highlight relevant experience in telecom, retail, tech, or customer-facing work where it applies.

Recruiter call - A short video or phone conversation with an HR or talent acquisition contact. They will ask about your background, why you are interested in Belsimpel, and your availability or visa situation if relevant. This stage is as much about culture fit as qualifications, so come prepared with a clear story about why this company and this role.

Skills or task assessment - Depending on the role, you may be asked to complete a practical assignment. Tech candidates might see a coding challenge or system design question. Commercial or marketing roles might get a brief case study or presentation task. These assessments are typically sent digitally and completed in your own time.

Team interview - A deeper conversation with your potential direct manager and one or two future colleagues. Expect questions about how you work, how you handle challenges, and how you collaborate in a team. Belsimpel reportedly values people who are direct, take initiative, and are comfortable in a fast-moving environment.

Final interview or offer conversation - Some candidates, especially for senior roles, have a final round with a department head or senior stakeholder. If all goes well, the recruiter will follow up with a verbal offer, after which the formal contract and onboarding details follow.

Timelines are not publicly reported in detail, but most candidates hear back within a few weeks of each stage. Following up politely after each round is acceptable and often appreciated.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Belsimpel hires across a wide range of functions, so the specific skills depend heavily on the role. That said, a few themes come up consistently across departments:

Ownership and initiative - Belsimpel has an entrepreneurial culture rooted in its startup origins. They tend to favour people who spot a problem and fix it rather than waiting to be told what to do. In interviews, examples of times you took ownership of a project or improved a process carry real weight.

Clear communication - The company operates in Dutch, so for most non-tech roles, Dutch language proficiency is either required or strongly preferred. For tech roles, English is more commonly sufficient, but check each job description carefully. If Dutch is required and you are learning, mention it proactively.

Customer focus - Given that Belsimpel's core business is serving consumers, they look for people who genuinely care about the customer experience, whether you are on the shop floor or building backend systems.

Collaboration - Teams at Belsimpel are reportedly close-knit. They value people who contribute positively to team energy, share knowledge, and support colleagues.

Relevant technical or domain skills - For tech roles, proficiency in the relevant stack matters. For commercial roles, experience in retail, telecom, or e-commerce is a plus. For logistics or operations, familiarity with supply chain or fulfilment processes helps.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the company thoroughly - Belsimpel is a Dutch consumer brand, so spend time understanding the telecom retail landscape in the Netherlands. Know their product range, how they position themselves against competitors, and any recent news about the company. Showing genuine market awareness stands out.

Tailor your CV for each role - With 57 open positions across functions, it is worth targeting the role that best fits your actual experience rather than applying broadly. Customise your CV to mirror the language in the job description, and lead with the most relevant experience.

Prepare your 'why Belsimpel' story - Generic answers do not work well here. Think about what genuinely draws you to this company: its culture, its growth story, the specific team, or the product. Recruiters can tell when a candidate has done their homework.

Practice with the STAR format - Situational questions are common. Prepare several strong examples from your past work that demonstrate initiative, problem-solving, collaboration, and customer focus. Use the Situation, Task, Action, Result structure to keep your answers crisp.

Prepare questions for them - Asking thoughtful questions about team structure, growth opportunities, or how success is measured in the role signals genuine interest and helps you evaluate whether it is the right fit for you.

Sort out logistics early - If you are applying from India or are not yet based in the Netherlands, be upfront about your visa situation in your cover letter or the recruiter call. It saves time for both sides and shows maturity.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Belsimpel has a reputation for a youthful, energetic work culture that still carries traces of its startup roots even as it has grown into an established retailer. The Groningen headquarters is often described by employees as informal and collaborative, with relatively flat hierarchies compared to large corporate employers.

Entrepreneurial spirit - The company was founded by students and grew into a major Dutch retailer. That origin story shapes the culture: people are expected to be proactive, think commercially, and not be afraid to try new approaches. If you thrive in environments where you can make an impact quickly, this is likely a good cultural fit.

Team and community feel - Belsimpel is known for investing in team events, social activities, and a collegial atmosphere. This matters more for roles based at the Groningen office, where the team is physically together regularly.

Growth orientation - The company has expanded its product lines and digital capabilities steadily. Employees who want to grow with a company, pick up new skills, and move into broader responsibilities tend to find opportunities here.

Transparency - Publicly available employee feedback commonly cites open communication from management as a positive, though as with any company, experience can vary by team and manager.

If you value a professional but not overly formal environment, where your contributions are visible and your ideas can find an audience, Belsimpel's culture is worth serious consideration.
