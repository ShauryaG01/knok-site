01 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Knok jobradar counted 27 Machine Learning Engineer openings in Hyderabad as of 8 July 2026. That places Hyderabad fourth among the major metros tracked. Bangalore leads by a wide margin with 165 openings, followed by Delhi with 50, Mumbai with 15, and then Hyderabad with 27. Pune and Chennai each show 14 roles.

Hyderabad's ML job market is active but concentrated. A small set of companies drives most of the hiring, which means a targeted approach works better than applying to everything in sight. If you are open to relocation, Bangalore gives you roughly six times more options right now. But if Hyderabad is your preference or your base, 27 live roles is a solid pipeline, and new postings come in regularly across the year.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Build a portfolio that reflects the industries hiring here. Warner Bros Discovery, aquaexchange, and Amgen are not looking for generic ML. A project that touches media recommendation, agricultural sensor data, or biomedical signals will get more attention from their teams than a standard Kaggle notebook, however well executed.

Show production experience, not just model accuracy. Hyderabad ML roles at product companies frequently ask for experience deploying models to real systems. Familiarity with MLflow, Docker, Kubernetes, or cloud ML services like AWS SageMaker or GCP Vertex AI, and being able to discuss monitoring model drift in production, will separate you from candidates who only know the research side.

Sharpen your fundamentals. Roles at companies like Infosys and Cornerstone often test core ML concepts: bias-variance trade-off, feature engineering, evaluation metrics, and system design for ML pipelines. Do not let framework familiarity substitute for understanding the basics.

Get active on GitHub and Kaggle. A public profile with real projects gives recruiters something concrete to look at before the first call. Even one well-documented end-to-end project covering data prep, model training, and a deployment step stands out more than a list of tools on a resume.

Network inside the Hyderabad ML community. Events like DataHack Summit and local ML meetups attract recruiters and engineering leads from the same companies on this list. A warm introduction consistently beats a cold application from the same candidate.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start with companies that have multiple openings. Warner Bros Discovery has 5 roles, and several others have 2. Multiple openings often signal an active hiring push, which means faster responses and sometimes more flexibility on exact requirements.

Customise your resume for each role, but do not rewrite it from scratch. Pick two or three skills from the job description that genuinely match your background and make sure they appear clearly in your resume's skills section and work experience bullets. This helps with ATS filters and makes a recruiter's job easier.

Apply early in the posting cycle. ML roles at product companies in Hyderabad tend to fill faster than in IT services. If a role went up this week, apply this week, not next.

Follow up on LinkedIn after applying. Find the recruiter or hiring manager and send a short, specific message: something like 'I applied for the ML Engineer role and wanted to flag my interest directly. Happy to share more about my work on [relevant project].' Keep it to two sentences.
