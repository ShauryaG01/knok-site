01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Zepto is one of India's fastest-growing quick-commerce startups, built around ultra-fast grocery delivery. The company has scaled at a remarkable pace and is now a significant employer across tech, operations, supply chain, and business functions. As of mid-2026, there are 6 open roles at Zepto spanning a range of functions. If you are looking to join a company that moves fast, ships quickly, and rewards people who handle ambiguity well, Zepto is worth targeting seriously.

Zepto competes in a space where execution speed is everything, which means the bar for hiring is high and the process is designed to filter for people who can deliver under pressure. Understanding what Zepto looks for before you apply will give you a meaningful edge.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Zepto's hiring process typically runs in four to five stages. The exact steps can vary by role and team, but here is what candidates commonly report:

Stage 1: Application Screening
Zepto's recruiters screen applications for relevant experience and skills. Getting past this stage usually comes down to how clearly your resume matches the job description. Generic profiles get filtered out quickly.

Stage 2: Recruiter Call
If your profile passes screening, a recruiter reaches out for a short introductory call to confirm your interest, understand your current situation, and explain what the role involves. Keep your answers crisp and direct.

Stage 3: Technical or Functional Assessment
Depending on the role, you may receive a take-home assignment or an online assessment. For engineering roles, this usually involves coding problems. For product or business roles, it might be a case study or an analytical task. You will generally have a few days to complete this.

Stage 4: Technical or Panel Interviews
This is the core evaluation stage. You can expect a few rounds of interviews with senior team members or hiring managers. For tech roles, expect data structures, system design, and problem-solving questions. For non-tech roles, expect scenario-based and analytical questions tied to the function.

Stage 5: Founder or Leadership Round
For senior or strategic roles, there is often a final round with a founder or a senior leader. This focuses on vision alignment, how you make decisions, and cultural fit.

After your final round, Zepto's teams tend to move relatively quickly. Expect to hear back within a few business days if things go well.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Zepto hires for speed and ownership. They want people who have built or operated something under pressure, not just managed processes. A few qualities come up repeatedly in candidate feedback:

Bias for action: Zepto moves fast. They want people who make decisions with incomplete information and course-correct quickly, rather than waiting for perfect clarity before acting.

First-principles thinking: Especially for product and business roles, the ability to break down a problem from scratch matters more than citing industry playbooks.

Ownership mindset: Zepto is in a high-growth phase. People who wait for permission to act do not thrive here. You are expected to define your scope and drive outcomes independently.

Relevant domain depth: For tech roles, strong fundamentals in data structures, system design, and the relevant stack. For operations or supply chain, hands-on experience with logistics or last-mile delivery carries real weight.

Comfort with ambiguity: Roles at Zepto often lack rigid boundaries. Being comfortable figuring out what needs to be done, without a detailed brief, is important.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research Zepto's business model deeply. Understand how ultra-fast delivery works operationally, where the margin pressure comes from, and what problems Zepto is still solving. Read publicly available interviews with the founders and press coverage of the business.

For engineering roles, revisit core data structures and algorithms, practice system design questions focused on high-throughput and low-latency systems, and be ready to explain the trade-offs in your past projects. Be specific about your individual contribution, not just what the team built.

For product roles, prepare a sharp point of view on Zepto's own product experience. Use the app, note friction points, and think through how you would improve them. Be ready to walk through a product decision you have owned end to end, including what you got wrong along the way.

For operations and supply chain roles, be ready to talk about metrics you have owned, how you improved unit economics, and how you managed cross-functional teams under deadline pressure. Numbers matter, but the thinking behind them matters more.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Mirror the language in the job description where it is accurate and lead with impact rather than responsibilities. A role-specific resume almost always outperforms a generic one.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Zepto is a startup that has scaled at an unusual pace, and the culture reflects that. It is high-intensity, results-driven, and expects a lot from people at every level. Hierarchy is relatively flat, which means faster decisions but also less structured mentoring compared to larger, more established companies.

People who thrive at Zepto tend to be comfortable with change. Roles evolve, priorities shift, and what you were hired to do might look quite different a few months later. That is not a warning, it is simply how fast-growth companies operate.

Zepto has publicly talked about building a culture of 'extreme ownership', where individuals are expected to drive outcomes without needing to be pushed. This shows up clearly in how performance is evaluated. Strong contributors get visibility quickly, and career growth tends to move faster than at larger companies.

If you are looking for a slow-paced or highly process-driven environment, Zepto is probably not the right fit. But if you want to work on a genuinely hard problem at scale, alongside sharp people, and see your work affect a large number of users directly, it is a compelling place to build your career.

Zepto hires at both levels, but the majority of open roles tend to require at least some relevant experience. Based on what candidates have shared publicly, the process can range from a few weeks to over a month, depending on the role and how quickly interview rounds can be scheduled. Following up politely with your recruiter after each round is a reasonable way to stay visible without being pushy.
