03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Allied Global ITS is generally straightforward, though it can vary depending on the role, team, and location. Based on what candidates commonly report, here is a stage-by-stage picture.

Stage 1: Application and initial shortlisting. After you apply, the recruitment team screens resumes for basic fit. For BPO and support roles, communication skills and relevant experience are the first filters. For IT roles, they look at your technical background and the tools you have worked with.

Stage 2: HR screening call. If your profile clears the initial filter, a recruiter reaches out for a brief call. This covers your background, current or expected compensation, notice period, and location preference. It is also their first check on your spoken English and overall communication confidence.

Stage 3: Aptitude or skills assessment. Many roles include a written or online test. For customer-facing roles, this often tests English comprehension, basic reasoning, and typing speed. For IT or process roles, the assessment may include domain-specific questions or situational judgement scenarios.

Stage 4: Operations or technical interview. This is the core round. A team lead, operations manager, or subject matter expert will assess your practical knowledge. For support roles, expect questions on how you handle difficult customers, escalations, or high-volume work. For IT roles, expect technical questions specific to the domain.

Stage 5: Final HR round. The last round usually covers compensation, joining timelines, shift preferences (common in BPO environments), and background verification requirements. This is also your chance to ask questions about the team and growth path.

The total process can run from a few days to a couple of weeks. Candidates who respond promptly and come prepared for each stage typically move faster through the pipeline.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Allied Global ITS looks for a combination of domain knowledge and soft skills, with the exact mix depending on the role. Here are the qualities that matter most across most hiring tracks.

Communication skills. For customer-facing and BPO roles, clear spoken and written English is non-negotiable. Interviewers will gauge your confidence, clarity, and ability to hold a conversation under pressure from the very first call.

Process orientation. The company serves clients who expect consistent, accurate delivery. Whether it is processing transactions, handling tickets, or running quality audits, they want people who follow defined processes carefully and flag deviations rather than improvise.

Adaptability. BPO and IT services environments involve shift work, changing client requirements, and evolving processes. Showing that you can adjust quickly and stay productive through change is a real differentiator.

Customer empathy. For support roles, technical knowledge alone is not enough. They want people who can make a customer feel heard, even when the resolution takes time or is outside the agent's direct control.

Team collaboration. Allied Global ITS operates in team-driven setups. Being someone who shares knowledge, supports teammates, and escalates issues constructively matters more than individual brilliance.

Technical proficiency (for IT and back-office roles). Specific tools, platforms, or certifications will vary by role. Check the job description carefully and be ready to demonstrate hands-on competence, not just theoretical awareness.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A few concrete steps can meaningfully improve your chances at Allied Global ITS.

Tailor your resume to the role. Allied Global ITS posts roles across a wide range of functions. A generic resume hurts your shortlisting odds. Highlight the experience and tools that match the specific job description. Glassdoor and industry surveys suggest that quantifying your impact, rather than just listing responsibilities, improves recruiter callbacks.

Practice your spoken English. If you are applying for a customer-facing or support role, your communication skills will be tested from the very first recruiter call. Record yourself answering common HR questions and listen back. Work on pacing, clarity, and confidence.

Research the company and its clients. Allied Global ITS works with global clients. Understanding what industries they serve and what problems BPO and IT services companies solve for those clients will help you give more relevant, grounded answers in interviews.

Prepare for situational questions. Operations and HR interviews often include scenarios like 'how would you handle an angry customer' or 'describe a time you managed a high-pressure situation.' Use a simple structure: describe the situation, explain your action, and share the result.

Know your numbers before the HR call. Be clear on your current compensation (if applicable), your expected compensation, your notice period, and your shift and location flexibility. Hesitating on these basics creates unnecessary friction and can signal unpreparedness.

Follow up after each round. A short, polite message after your interview signals genuine interest and professionalism. Most candidates skip this, so it is an easy way to stay memorable.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Allied Global ITS operates in the BPO and IT services space, which shapes its culture in specific ways. Here is an honest picture of what you can typically expect.

Client-first mindset. The company's business depends on client satisfaction and contract renewals. Teams are measured on quality scores, turnaround times, and customer satisfaction. If you thrive when you have clear targets and can see the direct impact of your work, this environment suits you well.

Structured work environment. BPO and IT services companies tend to have defined processes, reporting structures, and escalation paths. This is a plus if you like clarity about your role and responsibilities. If you prefer a loose, startup-style setup, it may feel constraining.

Shift flexibility expectations. Many roles, especially those supporting international clients, involve rotational shifts or non-standard hours. This is standard across the industry and worth factoring into your decision before you apply.

Learning and growth opportunities. Within the company, there are typically paths to move from frontline roles into quality assurance, team lead positions, training, or operations management. Growth usually comes from demonstrating consistency and a willingness to take on more responsibility over time.

Diverse, cross-functional teams. Allied Global ITS works across geographies, so teams often include colleagues from different cities and backgrounds. Cross-cultural communication becomes a skill you use daily, which adds to your professional profile over the long run.

If you value stability, a clear growth ladder, and international exposure without relocating abroad, Allied Global ITS is a solid option to put on your list.
