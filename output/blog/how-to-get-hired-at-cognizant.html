01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Cognizant is one of India's largest IT services employers, with major delivery centres across Chennai, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, and Mumbai. As of July 2026, there are around 30 open roles tracked on knok jobradar, spanning software engineering, cloud, data analytics, testing, and business process services.

Cognizant hires through two main tracks. The campus track, called the GenC programme, recruits fresh graduates in large batches through online assessments and campus drives. The lateral track brings in experienced professionals year-round for mid-level and senior roles. Both tracks follow a structured interview process, though the exact number of rounds and format varies by team, location, and role level.

Cognizant is known for high hiring volumes, so while competition is real, openings come up frequently. If you are applying off-campus, the company's own careers portal and employee referrals are the two most reliable entry points.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Cognizant interview process differs for freshers and experienced candidates, but it typically follows a clear stage-by-stage sequence.

Fresher track (GenC programme)

The first stage is an online assessment. It commonly covers aptitude (quantitative, logical, and verbal reasoning), a coding section with a small number of programming problems, and sometimes a communication module. Candidates who clear this are shortlisted for the next stage.

The second stage is a technical interview. A panellist typically asks about programming fundamentals, data structures, your final year project, and sometimes works through a short coding problem with you. Questions tend to test conceptual clarity rather than competitive-programming depth.

The third stage is an HR interview. This is primarily a culture fit and offer discussion. Expect questions about relocation willingness, preferred domain, and a few behavioural prompts like 'tell me about a time you solved a problem in a team.'

Lateral track (experienced hiring)

The first stage is resume shortlisting. Recruiters screen for keyword alignment with the job description, so matching your resume language to the JD matters here.

The second stage is a technical or domain round, sometimes split into two calls for more senior roles. This covers your past project experience, depth in your primary technology stack, and sometimes a scenario-based problem.

The third stage is a managerial round. The hiring manager typically wants to understand how you handle delivery pressure, stakeholder situations, and team challenges. Speaking concretely about past outcomes is more useful than general answers.

The fourth stage is an HR round covering compensation, notice period, and joining expectations.

On timeline: candidates commonly report the full process taking around 2-4 weeks from assessment to offer letter, though lateral hiring for specialised roles can take longer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Cognizant hires across a wide range of skill levels, so what they look for varies by track. Across both, a few qualities come up consistently in candidate feedback and publicly reported recruiter guidance.

Strong fundamentals, not just tool familiarity. For tech roles, interviewers check whether you understand how things work, not just which commands to run. Freshers are expected to have solid computer science basics. Experienced candidates need to explain the decisions they made on past projects, and why.

Communication skills. Cognizant works closely with global clients. Internal surveys and publicly reported employer feedback highlight spoken English and the ability to explain ideas clearly as a consistent screening factor, not just in the HR round but in technical rounds too.

Willingness to upskill. The GenC programme is built around training freshers on a specific technology after joining, so showing curiosity and a learning mindset carries weight.

Collaboration and delivery focus. For lateral roles, being able to describe how you worked within a team to deliver something, including what went wrong and how you handled it, is valued over solo accomplishments.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Get your resume right first. Tailor it to the job description by matching relevant keywords, especially technology names and methodologies. Recruiters use applicant tracking systems, and a mismatch between your resume language and the JD can filter you out before a human reviewer ever sees your profile.

Brush up on aptitude for the online test. Fresher candidates should practise quantitative, logical, and verbal reasoning questions. The coding section typically involves basic problems on arrays, strings, and loops. You do not need competitive programming experience, but you should be comfortable writing clean code in at least one language.

Prepare a few strong project stories. For both freshers and experienced candidates, talking about a specific project clearly makes a real difference. Use a simple structure: what was the problem, what did you do, and what was the result. For experienced candidates, include concrete outcomes wherever possible.

Research Cognizant's service lines. The company works across digital engineering, cloud, data and AI, and industry platforms. Knowing which practice area aligns with the role you applied for shows genuine interest and helps you ask better questions in the interview.

Practise communication out loud. Many candidates are technically strong but struggle to explain their work clearly under interview conditions. Recording yourself answering common questions and listening back is surprisingly effective, especially for HR and managerial rounds.

Use referrals if you can. Cognizant employees can refer external candidates through an internal portal. A referral does not guarantee an offer, but it typically speeds up shortlisting and ensures a recruiter actually reviews your resume.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Cognizant's publicly stated values centre on integrity, respect, excellence, and community. In practice, the day-to-day experience varies noticeably by team, delivery unit, and client account, which is common for large IT services firms.

Work environment. Most Cognizant employees work in a hybrid or on-site setup tied to client requirements. Project-based work means your experience after joining is shaped heavily by the project you land on, including the client, the manager, and the team dynamics.

Learning and growth. Cognizant has an internal learning platform and encourages certifications, particularly on cloud and emerging technologies. For freshers, the GenC training period is a structured entry into a specific technology track before client deployment.

Appraisals and promotions. Cognizant follows a performance rating system that influences annual increments and promotion timelines. Publicly reported employee feedback on platforms like Glassdoor suggests that visibility on active delivery projects matters for career growth, so staying engaged with your work and your manager matters.

Diversity and inclusion. The company has publicly reported initiatives around gender diversity, including return-to-work programmes and women leadership tracks. These tend to be more visible at larger delivery centres.

Overall picture. For someone early in their career, Cognizant offers scale, exposure to large enterprise clients, and a structured path into IT. For experienced professionals, the quality of the role depends heavily on the specific account and manager you end up with.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Cognizant currently have?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking around 30 open roles at Cognizant. This number changes frequently as positions are posted and filled, so checking regularly or setting up job alerts is a good idea. The actual count across all channels is typically higher, since not every role appears on every job site at the same time.

Does Cognizant hire freshers off-campus?

Yes, Cognizant does hire freshers off-campus, though the primary route for fresh graduates is the GenC campus programme. Off-campus, the company periodically announces drives through its careers portal and platforms like AMCAT. Applying with a referral from a current Cognizant employee significantly improves your chances of getting shortlisted, since referrals tend to bypass the initial automated filter.

What is the GenC programme at Cognizant?

GenC stands for 'Generate Commerce' and is Cognizant's flagship fresher hiring programme. After selection, candidates go through a training period where they are upskilled in a specific technology track before being placed on a client project. The programme is designed for recent graduates and includes both technical training and communication skill development.

How long does the Cognizant hiring process take from start to offer?

Candidates commonly report the process taking around 2-4 weeks from the online assessment to the offer letter for fresher roles. Lateral hiring can take longer, especially for specialised or senior positions where additional rounds and internal approvals are involved. Applying through a referral or a targeted recruitment drive sometimes shortens the overall timeline.

Is coding knowledge required for all Cognizant roles?

Not for all roles. Cognizant also hires for business process, consulting, finance, and HR functions where coding is not a core requirement. For technology and engineering roles, coding fundamentals are tested during the online assessment stage. For experienced tech roles, depth in your primary language or technology stack is a key evaluation criterion throughout the process.

What salary can I expect at Cognizant?

Cognizant does not publicly publish a fixed pay scale, and compensation varies by role, experience level, and location. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list community-reported salary data for various roles and bands at Cognizant, which can give you a useful benchmark before entering negotiations. For fresher roles, publicly reported offer figures from campus placements are sometimes shared by colleges after placement season ends.
