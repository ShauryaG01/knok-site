01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Freshdesk is the flagship customer support product of Freshworks, a publicly listed SaaS company originally founded in Chennai. The platform helps businesses manage support tickets, live chat, and customer conversations at scale, and it stands as one of India's most recognised 'made-in-India' SaaS success stories.

As of July 2026, Freshdesk has 23 open roles across engineering, product, design, sales, marketing, and customer success. Most positions are based in Chennai, Bengaluru, or Hyderabad, with select roles open to remote candidates. If you want to work inside a product-first company that has scaled globally while keeping strong roots in India, Freshdesk is a strong target.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs across four to five stages, though the exact number varies by role and team.

Application and resume screen. A recruiter or ATS reviews your application first. Tailor your resume to the specific job description and highlight any SaaS, customer support, or product experience you have.

Online assessment (tech and product roles). Engineering candidates commonly face a coding test covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes a short system design question. Product and analyst roles may receive a case study or a brief written assignment instead.

Technical or functional interviews (one to two rounds). These go deeper into your domain. Engineers can expect questions on past projects, system design, and problem-solving approaches. Non-tech roles focus on role-specific scenarios and your track record of results.

Hiring manager round. The hiring manager assesses how you think, how you communicate, and whether your goals align with the team's direction. Expect 'tell me about a time when...' questions alongside a two-way conversation about the role and the team.

HR and offer round. The recruiter covers compensation, joining date, and any remaining questions. Freshworks typically moves fairly quickly once a decision is made, though timelines differ across teams.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Freshdesk hires for a combination of domain skill and cultural mindset. Across most roles, a few themes consistently come up.

Customer-first thinking. Freshdesk's entire reason for existing is helping companies serve their customers better. Candidates who naturally think about end-user impact, not just completing a task, tend to stand out.

Ownership and initiative. The company moves fast and expects people to take responsibility without waiting to be told. In interviews, show examples where you drove something to completion even when the situation was ambiguous.

Clear communication. Whether you are in engineering, product, or sales, you will work with cross-functional teams and sometimes with global customers. Concise, structured communication is a baseline expectation at all levels.

Problem-solving depth. For technical roles, this means comfort with algorithms, system design, and debugging under pressure. For business roles, it means breaking down a complex situation and finding a practical path forward.

Growth mindset. Freshworks talks about a 'learn-it-all' culture rather than a 'know-it-all' one. Showing curiosity, asking good questions, and being honest about gaps can actually work in your favour during interviews here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your interview. Sign up for a Freshdesk trial account and navigate it as a support agent would. Understanding the core workflows (ticket creation, automation rules, reporting dashboards) gives you genuine talking points and signals real interest to the interviewer.

Study Freshworks' public materials. The company publishes engineering blogs, product announcements, and customer case studies. Reading a few of these helps you understand how the teams think about product decisions and technical trade-offs, which is useful for the hiring manager conversation.

Prepare role-specific examples using the STAR format. Situation, Task, Action, Result. For every major theme (ownership, conflict, failure, customer impact) have a crisp, specific story ready. Freshdesk interviewers commonly probe for concrete examples rather than accepting generic answers.

For engineering roles, practise data structures and system design. Focus on arrays, trees, graphs, and common algorithmic patterns. For mid-to-senior roles, be ready to design a simplified support ticketing system or a real-time notification service. Practice explaining your design choices out loud as you go.

For sales and customer success roles, study SaaS metrics. Understand MRR, churn, NRR, and customer health scores at a conceptual level. Be ready to talk about how you have handled difficult customer situations or helped grow an account.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Freshworks describes its culture using the acronym CHAT: Craftsmanship, Happy customers, Agility, and Trust. In practice, this shows up in a few patterns that candidates and employees commonly describe.

Flat(ish) structure. Freshdesk teams tend to be lean, and individual contributors often have direct access to senior leadership. This works well if you like making decisions and moving quickly, but it also means you need to be comfortable with ambiguity and incomplete information.

Chennai roots, global ambition. The company built a globally competitive SaaS product from India and is proud of that fact. There is genuine respect for talent across all offices, and the Chennai and Bengaluru teams are not treated as back-office support for a Western headquarters.

Speed and iteration. Freshdesk ships features frequently. Teams learn quickly from customer feedback, iterate, and avoid over-engineering solutions before they are validated by real usage. If you prefer long planning cycles and detailed specs before you start, this culture may feel fast at first.

Internal mobility. The company encourages people to switch functions or move into management as they develop. Several senior leaders at Freshworks started as individual contributors and grew into their roles internally.

Diversity and inclusion. Freshworks has publicly committed to improving gender diversity across engineering and leadership teams. This is something the company actively tracks and reports on, which is visible in the composition of several of its senior teams.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs are open at Freshdesk right now?

As of July 2026, Freshdesk has 23 open roles across functions including engineering, product, design, sales, and customer success. The mix of openings changes frequently, so check Freshworks' careers page regularly for the most current listings.

What salary can I expect at Freshdesk?

Compensation varies by role, level, and location. Based on publicly reported data on Glassdoor and AmbitionBox, software engineers at Freshworks commonly cite packages that are competitive within the Chennai and Bengaluru market. For specific figures, check those platforms for self-reported numbers from current and former employees, as data directly from Freshworks is limited.

Does Freshdesk hire freshers or new graduates?

Yes, Freshworks runs campus hiring programmes and lists entry-level roles for new graduates periodically. These positions are most common in engineering and customer success. If you are a fresher, look specifically for roles labelled 'associate' or 'junior', or keep an eye on Freshworks' campus recruitment announcements.

How long does the Freshdesk hiring process take?

From application to offer, the process commonly takes a few weeks, though this varies by team and the volume of candidates at each stage. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds can take a bit longer. Following up politely with your recruiter after each stage is perfectly acceptable and often appreciated.

Is there a work-from-home or remote option at Freshdesk?

Freshworks has moved toward a hybrid model for most roles, with an expectation to be in office on a regular basis. Some roles in sales or customer success may offer more location flexibility. Check the specific job listing for location requirements, as the policy varies by team and function.

Does Freshdesk offer ESOPs or equity?

Freshworks is a publicly listed company, and equity in the form of RSUs is commonly included in compensation packages for mid-to-senior roles. For junior roles, equity inclusion can vary. Confirm the details during the offer stage and ask about vesting schedules so you can compare offers accurately.
