01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Solutions Engineer roles posted in India do not show a salary. Of the 20 openings tracked in Delhi NCR by knok jobradar (as of July 2026), not one listed a pay band. This pattern holds across Indian job platforms generally: companies prefer to disclose pay only after screening, which puts you at a disadvantage unless you already know the market range before you apply.

Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest Solutions Engineers in India earn anywhere from around 10 LPA at the entry level to upward of 35 LPA for senior or pre-sales specialist profiles. Delhi NCR, as a major enterprise and SaaS hub, tends to track close to Bangalore for mid-senior roles according to industry surveys, even if the raw job count (20 in Delhi versus 55 in Bangalore right now) is smaller. Use these publicly reported ranges as your anchor when a recruiter asks your expectations before either of you shares a number.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because salary bands were not disclosed in any of the 20 Delhi NCR listings captured by knok jobradar, the figures below come from Glassdoor and publicly reported industry surveys. Treat them as a directional guide, not a guarantee, and always verify against recent offers in your own network.

Fresher or intern-to-full-time (0-2 years). Glassdoor and publicly reported data point to roughly 6-12 LPA for entry-level Solutions Engineers or Associate SE roles in Delhi NCR. Companies hiring at this level usually want strong technical grounding in scripting, APIs, and cloud basics, combined with reasonable communication skills for client-facing demos.

Mid-level (3-6 years). Industry surveys and publicly reported offer letters shared in professional communities suggest this band sits around 15-25 LPA in Delhi NCR, sometimes higher at product-led SaaS companies. At this stage you are expected to own demos end-to-end and influence deal outcomes, which the market does reward.

Senior or Principal SE (7+ years). Publicly reported figures on levels.fyi and Glassdoor for senior Solutions Engineers at larger enterprise or cloud companies range from 28-40 LPA in total fixed pay. Those numbers can shift further once variable pay and ESOPs are factored in.

Listings won't tell you what a role pays, but knok will tell you which ones you're competitive for.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Start with a range anchored to public data. When a recruiter asks your expectation before sharing the budget, give a range based on Glassdoor or publicly reported figures for your experience level, and frame it as: 'based on what I have seen reported for this role in Delhi NCR.' This keeps you from anchoring too low and signals that you have done your homework.

Delay the number as long as you reasonably can. The longer you wait before sharing your current CTC or expectation, the more information you collect about the role, scope, and team. Ask about targets, travel expectations, and whether the role carries quota before committing to a figure.

Negotiate total comp, not just base. Solutions Engineer packages often include variable pay tied to deals or bookings, plus benefits like a joining bonus, ESOPs at startups, or a car allowance at some enterprise firms. Ask for a full breakdown before comparing two offers side by side.

Use competing offers carefully. If you have another offer, you can mention it without revealing the number. Saying 'I have another process at a similar stage' creates urgency without requiring you to prove anything. Bluffing with a specific number usually backfires when the recruiter calls it.

Push on the variable component structure. For pre-sales or SE roles, understand how the variable is calculated, whether it is tied to your direct deals or a team-wide pool, and what realistic attainment looks like. Commonly cited attainment rates in industry forums suggest many SEs hit 80-100% of target variable, but this varies significantly by company and territory.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Fixed base salary is the number most people compare, but it is rarely the whole picture for Solutions Engineers. Here is what a typical total compensation package looks like in Delhi NCR, based on publicly reported offer structures shared in Indian professional communities.

Base salary forms the bulk of take-home and is the figure on which future hike percentages are applied. Negotiating it upward at the time of joining has a compounding effect over several years, so do not trade base for a one-time perk.

Variable or incentive pay is common for pre-sales SE roles and is usually tied to deals closed in your territory or accounts. Publicly reported structures suggest this component ranges from 10% to 25% of total on-target earnings for most SE roles, though some companies weight it significantly higher.

ESOPs or RSUs matter most at funded startups and publicly listed tech companies. At early-stage startups the value is speculative. At publicly listed or late-stage companies, industry surveys suggest a 4-year vesting schedule is standard, so factor in how long you plan to stay.

Benefits in kind worth asking about: health insurance cover for self, spouse, and parents separately; internet or phone reimbursement; and a learning or certification budget. These are not always offered upfront but are often negotiable if you ask before signing.

One-time joining bonus is increasingly used by Delhi NCR employers to close candidates without disturbing the internal salary band. If the fixed offer is lower than you wanted, ask directly whether a joining bonus is an option before you decide.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most Solutions Engineer jobs in Delhi NCR not show a salary?

Indian employers, particularly in IT services and enterprise software, typically do not list pay because they want to gauge your current CTC before making an offer. This gives them negotiating room and keeps internal salary structures opaque. The practice is slowly changing under pressure from candidates but as of 2026 it remains the norm. Your best response is researching publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi before any recruiter conversation so you always walk in with a number in mind.

Is Delhi NCR a good city for Solutions Engineer roles compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore had 55 active SE openings versus 20 in Delhi NCR in the July 2026 knok jobradar snapshot, so Bangalore has more volume. That said, Delhi NCR has a strong base of enterprise accounts, government technology projects, and regional offices of global SaaS companies. If you are already based in the NCR and prefer not to relocate, the market is active enough to find quality roles, especially for candidates with 3 or more years of experience.

Does a Solutions Engineer role typically involve sales targets?

It depends on the company structure. Pre-sales SE roles at SaaS and cloud vendors are usually quota-carrying or tied to team-level targets, with variable pay structured accordingly. More technical or post-sales implementation-focused SE roles may carry no quota at all. Before accepting any offer, ask specifically whether the role has a 'quota overlay' or 'individual attainment' component, and request attainment data from recent quarters to understand what is realistic.

How do I find out what a company is actually paying before I apply?

Check Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and LinkedIn Salary for the specific company and role if enough data points exist. Look for posts in communities like Blind or relevant forums where people share offer breakdowns. If you know someone at the company, a direct conversation is the most reliable source. Some recruitment consultants will share a budget range upfront, so applying through them can surface salary information earlier in the process.

What skills push a Solutions Engineer salary toward the higher end of publicly reported ranges?

Industry surveys and publicly reported job descriptions consistently highlight a few differentiators: hands-on cloud certifications (especially AWS, Azure, or GCP), strong scripting ability in Python or similar languages, and demonstrable experience influencing large enterprise deals. Vertical expertise in fintech, healthcare, or government accounts also commands a premium in Delhi NCR specifically, because many of the region's largest accounts sit in these sectors.
