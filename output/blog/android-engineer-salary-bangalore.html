01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Android Engineer job listings in India skip salary entirely. Of the 16 Android Engineer openings tracked in Bangalore by knok jobradar as of July 2026, salary bands were not disclosed, which is normal: recruiters share the range only after the first call.

What this means for you: any figure you see on a job card is usually the floor, not the ceiling. Companies leave room to negotiate, and candidates who ask politely often land above the posted number.

Android Engineering in Bangalore is in a strong spot. Kotlin has replaced Java as the default, Jetpack Compose is displacing older XML talent, and consumer-tech companies compete hard for engineers who can ship polished, performant apps. That scarcity gives you real leverage, especially at mid-level and above.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Get the band before you share your current salary. Ask 'what is the budgeted range for this role?' early in the recruiter call. Most will share it. If they push for your current CTC first, say you want to confirm a range match before discussing your history.

Use Glassdoor and levels.fyi as anchors. When a recruiter pushes back on your ask, cite publicly reported ranges for Android Engineers at that company tier. This shifts the conversation from your past salary to market data.

Jetpack Compose and Kotlin carry a premium right now. If you have shipped production Compose code, say so explicitly. These skills are in short supply and recruiters are aware of it.

Negotiate total comp, not just base. A company at its base ceiling can often add a joining bonus, an extra ESOP grant, or an accelerated review cycle. Always ask what levers exist beyond the base number.

Get one competing offer if you can. Even a single offer from a similar-tier company shifts the conversation significantly. You do not need to be aggressive about it; simply mentioning you are in process elsewhere is often enough.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Android Engineers in Bangalore typically see total compensation split across several components. Understanding each one helps you compare offers that look different on paper.

Base salary is the fixed monthly amount and is what PF contributions are calculated on. This is the number most job cards show when they post a figure at all.

Variable pay (performance bonus) is commonly cited at 10-20% of base in industry surveys for mid-size tech companies. Early-stage startups sometimes replace this with equity rather than cash.

ESOPs or RSUs are stock grants vesting over a 3-4 year schedule. At listed companies or credible pre-IPO firms, they can meaningfully lift total comp. At early-stage startups, the value is speculative until there is a clear exit path.

Joining bonus is a one-time payment to cover notice-period overlap or forfeited bonuses from your previous employer. It is negotiable and often easier to secure than a base increase when a company is at its ceiling.

Benefits such as health insurance (family floater), meal allowance, and learning budgets vary widely. Ask for the full benefits sheet before you sign, not after.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many Android Engineer jobs are open in Bangalore right now?

knok jobradar tracked 16 Android Engineer openings in Bangalore as of July 2026. This is a point-in-time snapshot and changes daily. Listings for in-demand roles appear and close quickly, so checking regularly matters more than the exact count on any given day.

Do Bangalore companies pay Android Engineers more than other cities?

Bangalore generally leads on total comp, particularly because product companies and well-funded startups there tend to offer ESOPs on top of base salary. Delhi NCR had 12 active openings in the same snapshot and competitive base pay, based on publicly reported data from Glassdoor. The gap has narrowed compared to a few years ago, but Bangalore still leads on equity-heavy packages.

Is Kotlin mandatory, or can I apply with mainly Java experience?

Most Bangalore product companies now list Kotlin as a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have. Java experience is valued for understanding legacy codebases, but candidates who cannot write idiomatic Kotlin are screened out early at consumer-tech firms. If you are still primarily working in Java, investing time in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose before applying will improve your chances significantly.

How do I find the actual salary range when a job posting does not show one?

Ask the recruiter directly in your first call: 'What is the budgeted band for this role?' Most will tell you at that point. If they ask your current CTC first, redirect by explaining you want to confirm a range match before discussing your history. Cross-reference what they share with Glassdoor and levels.fyi to judge whether the number is competitive for your experience level.

What skills matter most for senior Android Engineer interviews in Bangalore?

Senior interviewers in Bangalore focus on architecture decisions (clean architecture, multi-module builds), performance optimization (memory management, startup time, render performance), and your ability to lead technical choices across a team. Concrete examples with measurable outcomes carry more weight than listing tools. Jetpack Compose, Kotlin coroutines, and CI/CD experience are commonly expected at this level.
