04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Delhi NCR has 50 active Machine Learning Engineer openings as of July 2026, according to knok jobradar data. That places it second in India after Bangalore (165 openings), and ahead of Hyderabad (27), Mumbai (15), Pune (14), and Chennai (14). The NCR ML job market is shaped by three forces: a strong GCC presence from global multinationals running India innovation centres, a growing set of AI-native product companies, and established analytics firms embedding ML teams into client delivery. This blend means the kinds of roles on offer vary significantly. Some are research-adjacent (building models from scratch), others are applied engineering (deploying and maintaining ML systems at scale), and some sit closer to data science with a production tilt. Understanding which type a company hires for helps you tailor both your resume and your interview prep.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Match your resume to the company type, not just the job title. A GCC like Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs wants to see production-grade ML work, system design thinking, and cross-functional collaboration. An AI-native company like Level AI will look hard at your model development fundamentals and your ability to move fast. Adobe looks for ML applied to creative or document intelligence problems, so side projects in those domains carry weight.

Show deployed work, not just notebook experiments. Delhi NCR employers consistently ask about real-world deployment. Mention specific frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, MLflow), cloud platforms (AWS SageMaker, Azure ML, GCP Vertex AI), and what happened after your model went to production. Even a personal project deployed as an API counts.

Highlight domain knowledge where it fits. Deutsche Telekom needs telecom-aware ML (network optimisation, churn prediction, anomaly detection). EXL Service works heavily in insurance and healthcare analytics. Bupa is squarely in health insurance. If your background touches any of these domains, say so explicitly in your resume summary.

Build a concise portfolio. A GitHub profile with two or three clean, documented projects is more effective than a long list of unfinished notebooks. Link to it in your resume header. Recruiters at startups like Wingify and Stage often look at this before shortlisting.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With 50 roles spread across very different company types, a targeted approach beats blasting the same resume everywhere. Group the employers into tiers based on your fit. Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs has the most openings, so it is worth applying to multiple roles within that company if your background covers different sub-domains like NLP, computer vision, or forecasting. Apply to GCCs and large product companies first if you want stability and a structured career path. Target AI-native companies and startups if you prefer fast iteration and broader ownership.

Timing matters. Delhi NCR hiring tends to pick up after August as Q3 budgets open. Roles at GCCs often go through an internal approval process, so applications placed early in a hiring cycle have a better shot before headcount freezes.

For direct outreach, find the hiring manager or ML team lead on LinkedIn and send a short note referencing a specific project or paper the team has published. Keep it to three sentences. GCC roles often have a recruiter layer, so connecting with an internal recruiter at Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs or Bupa directly can speed up your process.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Delhi NCR a good city for ML Engineers compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has 165 ML Engineer openings versus 50 in Delhi NCR, so the raw volume is lower. However, Delhi NCR has a strong GCC presence (Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs alone has 18 roles) and is growing as multinationals set up India innovation centres here. If you are based in NCR and do not want to relocate, the market is active enough to find solid opportunities, especially if your background aligns with GCCs or analytics companies.

What kind of ML roles does Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs hire for?

Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs is the R and D arm of Deutsche Telekom in India, focused on telecom-specific AI applications like network optimisation, churn prediction, and intelligent automation. With 18 open roles, they hire across experience levels. Roles typically require strong Python, experience with cloud ML platforms, and ideally some exposure to telecom data or large-scale time-series problems.

Do I need a PhD to get an ML Engineer role in Delhi NCR?

No. Most employers on this list (Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs, EXL Service, Adobe) hire ML Engineers with a B.Tech or M.Tech background. A PhD helps for research-heavy positions, but the majority of open roles are engineering-focused and prioritise hands-on experience with model training, deployment, and monitoring over academic credentials. A strong portfolio and clear production experience matter more for most of these roles.

How is applying to a GCC different from applying to a startup like Wingify or Stage?

GCCs typically have a structured hiring process with multiple rounds including a recruiter screen, technical assessments, and panel interviews, and the process can take a few weeks. Startups move faster and often have a founder or senior engineer in the first interview. At a startup, expect broader questions about owning the full ML lifecycle. At a GCC, expect more depth on specific tools, system design at scale, and cross-team collaboration.

What skills should I focus on to get shortlisted by these companies?

Python and SQL are table stakes. Beyond that, practical experience with at least one major ML framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow) and one cloud ML platform (AWS SageMaker, Azure ML, or GCP Vertex AI) is expected by most companies on this list. MLOps skills (model versioning, CI/CD for ML, monitoring in production) are increasingly important at GCCs and product companies. For EXL Service and Bupa specifically, domain knowledge in analytics, insurance, or healthcare is a strong differentiator.

Is remote work available for ML Engineer roles listed in Delhi NCR?

Most roles from companies like Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs, Adobe, and Bupa are hybrid or on-site in Delhi NCR. Turing is the clear exception, as it specialises in placing engineers in remote-first global roles. Check individual job descriptions carefully before applying. The broader trend among GCCs and large product companies through 2025-2026 has been a return to office-first policies for engineering teams, so do not assume remote without confirming.
