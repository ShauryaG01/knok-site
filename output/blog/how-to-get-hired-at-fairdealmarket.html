01 Hiring Overview

Most openings are filled through direct applications on job portals, the company's careers page, and LinkedIn. Employee referrals tend to move faster through the initial screening stage, so if you have a contact inside the company, a warm introduction is worth pursuing.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Candidates for FairDeal Market roles commonly report a process with several stages, though the exact number of rounds can vary by function and seniority. Here is a typical sequence based on publicly reported experiences.

First stage: Application and resume screening. The HR team reviews applications and shortlists profiles that closely match the job description. Tailoring your resume to the specific role rather than sending a generic one makes a real difference at this stage.

Second stage: HR screening call. A recruiter or HR executive typically reaches out for a brief introductory call. This covers your background, current situation, notice period, and salary expectations. It is also your chance to confirm the role matches what you expected.

Third stage: Functional or technical round. For sales and operations roles, this round often involves case questions, situational scenarios, or a discussion of past targets and achievements. For technology roles, candidates commonly report coding questions, system design discussions, or take-home assignments depending on the level.

Fourth stage: Managerial or business round. The hiring manager or a cross-functional leader assesses whether your experience and approach fit the team's current needs. Expect detailed questions about how you handled specific challenges in previous roles.

Fifth stage: HR final discussion and offer. Compensation, joining date, and other formalities are discussed here. Candidates report that the final offer stage can take a few days to about a week after the last interview, so factor that into your timeline if you have competing offers.

With 32 open roles as of July 2026, FairDeal Market is actively hiring across multiple functions, which typically means the process moves at a reasonable clip. Recruiters at growing companies tend to avoid unnecessary delays.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

FairDeal Market operates in a competitive marketplace segment, and hiring managers tend to prioritise a few qualities consistently.

Execution mindset over theory. Candidates who can point to concrete outcomes from previous roles stand out. Whether it is a sales target you hit, a process you improved, or a team you helped onboard, specifics matter more than general claims.

Comfort with ambiguity. Growing companies change priorities quickly, and interviewers at FairDeal Market commonly ask how candidates have handled situations where the rules were not clearly defined. Showing that you can operate without perfect information is a genuine differentiator.

Customer focus. Given the marketplace nature of the business, most teams are ultimately serving buyers or sellers. Candidates who can demonstrate an understanding of customer pain points, whether through direct customer-facing experience or through product and operations work, tend to be viewed favourably.

Collaboration and communication. Across functions, the ability to coordinate with other teams and communicate clearly is consistently valued. This shows up both in how you answer questions and in what examples you choose to share.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the company before every round. Understand how FairDeal Market makes money, who its customers are, and what sets it apart from competing platforms. Candidates who know the business context ask better questions and give more relevant answers.

Prepare structured stories using the STAR format. For each significant achievement in your career, prepare a short account of the Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Practice saying these out loud so they feel natural rather than rehearsed.

Brush up on function-specific skills. For sales roles, revisit your metrics and be ready to talk about pipelines and conversion. For operations, think through supply chain or fulfilment challenges you have solved. For technology roles, practice problems on platforms like LeetCode and be ready to explain your thinking as you go.

Prepare a few sharp questions for each stage. Asking about team priorities, what success looks like in the first few months, or what challenges the team is currently solving shows genuine interest and leaves a strong impression.

Sort out logistics early. Confirm the interview format (video call or in-person), the panel members if possible, and whether you need to submit any documents or assignments in advance. Surprises on the day add unnecessary stress.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

FairDeal Market, like many Indian marketplace companies, operates with a strong emphasis on speed and results. The environment tends to be collaborative within teams, with cross-functional coordination happening regularly between sales, technology, and operations.

Work style is generally described by current and former employees as target-driven, particularly in sales and account management functions. Recognising this early helps you decide whether the pace fits your own working style.

The company appears to value employees who take initiative rather than waiting for direction. If you thrive in environments where you can identify a problem and move to fix it without being asked, that is a cultural strength worth highlighting in your interviews.

On the career development side, growing companies like FairDeal Market can offer faster movement into larger roles than more established organisations, since new functions and teams are being built out regularly. This is worth asking about directly during your final interview stage.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the FairDeal Market hiring process typically take from application to offer?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the full process can range from a couple of weeks to about a month, depending on the role and how quickly rounds are scheduled. Sales and operations roles often move faster than technology or senior leadership positions. If you have a competing offer with a deadline, it is reasonable to let the recruiter know early so they can try to align timelines.

Does FairDeal Market hire freshers, or do they mainly look for experienced candidates?

FairDeal Market does hire freshers, particularly for roles in sales, customer support, and operations where on-the-job training is part of the model. For technology and senior functional roles, prior experience is generally expected. Checking the specific job description for experience requirements will give you the clearest answer for each role you are targeting.

Are there remote or hybrid work options at FairDeal Market?

Work arrangements at FairDeal Market vary by role and function. Operations and field sales roles are typically in-person by nature. Some technology and product roles have been reported with hybrid arrangements. It is best to ask about the specific work model for your role during the HR screening call, as policies can shift and listings do not always reflect current practice.

What is the best way to negotiate salary at FairDeal Market?

Come prepared with a target range based on what is publicly reported on platforms like Glassdoor or AmbitionBox for similar roles and experience levels. Avoid sharing your current salary before you have an offer on the table, and anchor your ask slightly above your true target to leave room for negotiation. If the base offer is firm, ask whether there is flexibility on joining bonus, variable pay, or the performance review timeline.

How competitive is hiring at FairDeal Market right now?

With 32 open roles as of July 2026, FairDeal Market is actively hiring across multiple functions, which generally means the competition per seat is more manageable than at companies with very few openings. That said, popular roles in technology and sales tend to attract higher volumes of applicants. A well-tailored application and a referral from someone inside the company are the two most effective ways to improve your odds.

How should I follow up after a FairDeal Market interview?

Send a brief thank-you note to the recruiter within a day of your interview, mentioning one specific point from the conversation that reinforced your interest in the role. If you have not heard back within the timeframe they gave you, one polite follow-up message is appropriate. Avoid following up multiple times in quick succession, as it can come across as pressure rather than genuine interest.
