01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Amgen is one of the world's largest biotechnology companies, known for developing medicines in oncology, nephrology, cardiovascular disease, and inflammation. The company operates globally and its India presence, centred in Hyderabad, covers technology, data, clinical operations, and global business services.

As of July 2026, Amgen has 89 open roles across its global and India teams. These span research and development, bioinformatics, software engineering, regulatory affairs, finance, and clinical data management. The breadth of functions means there are relevant paths for professionals from pharma, technology, data science, and business backgrounds alike.

Amgen's hiring process is structured and thorough. The company takes its time to evaluate candidates carefully, and that deliberateness reflects the standards it holds across all functions. Whether you are a scientist, an engineer, or a finance professional, expect a process that goes several rounds deep before you reach an offer.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Amgen interviews typically move through the following stages. Timelines and the number of rounds can vary by role, so treat this as a general picture rather than a fixed sequence.

Application and resume screening: Recruiters review your resume against the job description. The closer the match between your listed skills and the role requirements, the better your chances of moving forward. Generic resumes rarely clear this stage for competitive positions.

Recruiter screening call: A talent acquisition partner will connect with you to confirm your background, discuss the role in detail, and check on compensation expectations and your availability. This is also a good time to ask basic questions about the hiring timeline.

Hiring manager interview: This is a one-on-one conversation with the person you would directly report to. Expect questions about your career history, your approach to problem-solving, and your reasons for interest in this specific team and role.

Technical or functional round: For science and engineering roles, this stage often involves a case study, a coding or data exercise, or a deep-dive discussion of domain knowledge. For business and operations roles, scenario-based questions are common. Preparation specific to the role description matters here.

Panel interview: Amgen commonly includes a panel stage with cross-functional stakeholders. Competency-based and behavioural questions are standard. Panels are designed to assess how you would operate across teams, not just within your own function.

Offer and onboarding: If you clear the final rounds, HR will share a verbal offer followed by written documentation. Background verification is standard before your start date is confirmed.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Amgen consistently looks for candidates who can link their work to the company's core purpose: serving patients. This applies as much to a software engineer as to a clinical scientist.

Domain expertise: Relevant experience in your field is important. For science roles, that means technical depth. For technology roles, it means familiarity with the tools and platforms listed in the job description. Cross-domain knowledge is a bonus.

Evidence-based thinking: Amgen's decisions are grounded in data. Candidates who approach problems rigorously, even in ambiguous situations, tend to stand out. Showing how you have made decisions based on evidence rather than intuition is a strong signal.

Collaboration across functions: Because Amgen's products move through research, manufacturing, regulatory, and commercial teams before reaching patients, working across disciplines is not optional. Candidates who demonstrate strong cross-functional experience and the ability to influence without formal authority do well.

Clear communication: The ability to explain complex ideas to non-specialist audiences is valued at every level. This matters especially in roles that bridge technical and business functions.

Integrity: Amgen holds ethical standards high, and interviewers probe for this through behavioural questions. Candidates who can demonstrate this through specific examples in their answers, rather than just stating it as a value, make a stronger impression.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the company and its science: Before any Amgen interview, spend time understanding the therapy areas the company works in and the products in its current pipeline. You do not need to become an expert, but showing genuine curiosity about the science signals that you see this as more than just another job.

Prepare STAR-format answers: Amgen interviewers rely heavily on competency-based questions. Prepare strong examples from your career covering themes like handling failure, influencing without authority, driving results under pressure, and navigating disagreement. Each answer should clearly describe the Situation, Task, Action, and Result.

Know your resume in depth: Be ready to discuss any role or project you have listed, including what you would do differently and what happened after the work was done. Interviewers probe beyond bullet points.

Practice role-specific preparation: For technical and engineering roles, review the tools and frameworks mentioned in the job description. Amgen's technical rounds focus on applied problem-solving, so practicing with realistic scenarios from your domain is more useful than memorising pure theory.

Ask thoughtful questions: Interviewers respond well to candidates who ask about current team challenges, how success is measured, or what the onboarding experience looks like. It signals genuine engagement with the role, not just with the offer.

Follow up after each round: A brief, professional note after each interview stage is a small gesture that many candidates skip. It keeps you in the interviewer's mind positively, especially when panels are reviewing multiple candidates at the same time.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Amgen describes its culture as science-based, patient-centric, and held to high ethical standards. In day-to-day terms, this shapes how work gets done across all functions.

Decisions at Amgen are made carefully rather than quickly. The company invests in getting the science and the process right before moving forward, which means teams value rigour over speed. If you are used to a high-velocity startup environment, this more deliberate pace is worth factoring into your expectations before you join.

Collaboration across functions is the norm, not a stated aspiration. Because a medicine travels through research, manufacturing, regulatory, and commercial functions before reaching a patient, cross-team work is built into how Amgen operates. People who thrive here are strong relationship builders who are comfortable working with stakeholders who have different perspectives and priorities.

For professionals joining the Hyderabad centre, the culture also reflects global expectations around written communication, documentation standards, and working across time zones with counterparts in the US and Europe. Being comfortable with async communication and concise written updates is a practical advantage.

Amgen has made publicly stated commitments around inclusion, environmental responsibility, and access to medicines. These show up in how teams are structured and in the kinds of conversations leaders have. Candidates genuinely motivated by purpose-driven work tend to find the environment rewarding.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Amgen hire freshers or entry-level candidates in India?

Amgen does post entry-level and early-career roles, particularly at its Hyderabad centre in functions like data management, software development, and global business services. Competition for these positions is high. A strong academic record, relevant internship or project experience, and a genuine interest in life sciences or technology will make an entry-level application more competitive.

How long does the full Amgen hiring process take from application to offer?

The process commonly spans several weeks to a couple of months, depending on the role, the function, and how quickly panel schedules can be coordinated. Roles with multiple technical assessment stages can take longer. Staying in regular contact with your recruiter after each completed round is the best way to track progress without coming across as impatient.

What salary can I expect from Amgen India roles?

Amgen does not publish official salary bands publicly. Based on Glassdoor and publicly reported data, Amgen India compensation is considered competitive within the pharma and biotech sector. Exact figures vary widely by role, level, and function, so the recruiter screening call is the right moment to ask about the range for your specific position.

Can I apply to Amgen without a pharma or biotech background?

Yes, for many technology, data, and business roles, a direct pharma background is not a hard requirement. What Amgen consistently looks for is the ability to learn the domain and connect your work to patient outcomes. Candidates from technology, consulting, and finance backgrounds have successfully joined Amgen India teams in data engineering, analytics, and business services roles.

How useful is LinkedIn when applying to Amgen?

Very useful. Amgen recruiters actively search LinkedIn, especially for mid-level and senior roles. Keep your profile current with specific achievements, clear domain keywords, and an updated summary. Being reachable on LinkedIn also makes it easier for internal referrals to connect your profile to an open position before it closes.

Are Amgen India roles remote, hybrid, or in-office?

Amgen has adopted hybrid working models for many roles globally, including at the Hyderabad office. The specific arrangement depends on the role and team. Lab, manufacturing, and operations-adjacent positions typically require in-person presence, while technology and business services roles often offer more flexibility. Confirm the expected working model with the recruiter early in the process.
