01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Technical Program Manager (TPM) job listings in Bangalore do not show a salary figure. This is the norm on Indian job boards, not the exception, and it means you can apply to several roles without knowing whether the range even fits your expectations. The 41 active TPM openings tracked by knok jobradar in Bangalore as of July 2026 followed this pattern: pay information was not disclosed in the listings.

What public benchmarks do tell us is that Glassdoor and levels.fyi consistently place Bangalore at the top of TPM pay across Indian cities. The concentration of global tech companies, product-first startups, and engineering captives in the city drives this premium. Industry surveys also show wide variation within Bangalore itself, based on company tier and the funding stage of the business.

Since no salary bands were available in the current listings, this guide draws on publicly reported and commonly cited figures from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys. Treat these as orientation points, not guaranteed ranges. Sample sizes, especially at senior levels, are thin on public platforms, so the upper ends of any range should be cross-checked with your own network before you anchor to them.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Most HR teams in India will not share a salary range unless you ask directly. Going in without an anchor puts you on the back foot from the start.

Ask for the range before sharing your expectation. When asked 'what are your salary expectations,' try responding with: 'could you share the band budgeted for this role so I can give you a realistic number?' Many interviewers will share it. If they do not, anchor on publicly reported figures from Glassdoor and levels.fyi for your level and the company's tier before you name a number.

Negotiate on total compensation, not just base. If the base offer feels firm, ask about the variable pay structure, ESOP cliff and vesting schedule, and joining bonus before deciding. According to industry surveys, these components can meaningfully change the overall value of an offer, especially at senior levels.

Use a competing offer if you have one. A real offer from another company is the strongest negotiation tool available. Even a letter of intent changes the conversation. Avoid bluffing without one, as experienced recruiters recognise it quickly.

Time your ask correctly. The best window to negotiate is after the verbal offer and before the written letter. Once you sign, the conversation effectively ends.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

A TPM offer in Bangalore typically has four components beyond base salary. Understanding each one matters because companies structure packages differently, and a higher headline CTC can sometimes mean less money in hand.

Base salary is the fixed monthly payout. It is the safest number to compare across offers because it does not depend on performance targets or vesting schedules.

Variable pay (bonus) is linked to individual and company performance. Industry surveys commonly cite this component at 10-20% of base for mid-to-senior TPMs, though actual payouts depend on how each company defines a 'targets met' year.

Stock (ESOPs or RSUs) is increasingly standard at product companies and funded startups. Vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff is publicly reported as the most common structure. The real value depends on the company's trajectory, and levels.fyi tracks RSU grant data in detail for publicly listed companies.

Joining bonus is a one-time payment, sometimes used to offset unvested stock you leave behind at your current employer. It does not repeat in future years.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most TPM jobs in Bangalore not show a salary?

Indian employers, especially mid-to-large companies, typically prefer to negotiate salary based on a candidate's current CTC rather than advertise a fixed band. This gives them flexibility to make offers case by case. It puts the candidate at a disadvantage, which is exactly why building a benchmark from publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi before every interview round matters.

How do I figure out what salary to ask for as a TPM in Bangalore?

Start with Glassdoor and levels.fyi to see what publicly reported offers look like for your level and the company tier you are targeting. Cross-check with peers in similar roles, since these platforms have thin data at senior levels and can underrepresent top-end offers. Use that range as your anchor, and try to get the recruiter to share their budget before you name a number.

Does a TPM role at a product company pay more than one at a services company?

Yes, the gap is real and widely documented. Industry surveys and Glassdoor data consistently show that TPMs at product companies earn more than those at services or IT consulting firms at the same experience level. The difference comes from higher base pay, RSU grants, and performance bonuses that product companies use to compete for talent. If you are moving from a services background to a product company, be prepared to negotiate well above your current CTC.

What skills push a TPM's salary toward the higher end of the range?

Commonly cited factors in industry surveys include experience managing cross-functional programs across engineering, product, and business teams, along with a clear track record of delivering at scale. Certifications like PMP are table stakes at many companies. What moves you to the top of the band is demonstrated ownership of outcomes, not just coordination, plus familiarity with OKR frameworks, data-driven planning, and distributed team environments.

Is Bangalore worth relocating to for a TPM role if I am based elsewhere?

The July 2026 snapshot shows 41 openings in Bangalore versus 14 in Delhi NCR and 13 in Pune, so opportunity density is significantly higher here. Glassdoor and levels.fyi also show Bangalore at the top of TPM pay benchmarks across Indian cities. Whether the move makes financial sense depends on your personal situation, but from a career optionality standpoint, Bangalore gives you the widest set of choices for this role right now.

Can I negotiate if HR says the budget is fixed?

'The budget is fixed' is a common opening position, not always a final one. Ask whether there is flexibility on the joining bonus, stock grant size, or variable pay percentage, even if the base is firm. If you have a competing offer, share it. According to industry surveys, candidates who counter at least once tend to secure better outcomes than those who accept the first offer, even when told the band is non-negotiable.
