01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Zenoti builds cloud software for spas, salons, fitness studios, and wellness businesses worldwide. The company is headquartered in Bellevue, USA, with its main engineering and product hub in Hyderabad. It has built a strong reputation as one of the more focused B2B SaaS platforms in the global wellness tech space, serving enterprise chains as well as independent studios.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 52 open roles at Zenoti. These span software engineering, quality assurance, product management, customer success, and sales functions. Most India-based positions are in Hyderabad, with a mix of on-site and hybrid arrangements depending on the team. Whether you are an experienced backend engineer, a product manager with SaaS background, or a customer success professional who has handled enterprise accounts, Zenoti has active openings right now.

The company targets candidates who can connect technical work to real customer outcomes. If you understand software products deeply and want to see your work used by real businesses every day, Zenoti is worth targeting.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Zenoti hiring process typically runs four to five stages. The exact structure can vary by team and role, so treat this as a general guide rather than a guarantee.

Stage 1: Application and resume screening. The recruiting team filters based on role fit, relevant experience, and technical skills. If you have prior B2B SaaS experience, make it visible in the first few lines of your resume. Applications without a clear match to the job description tend to be screened out early.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A short introductory call to confirm your interest, notice period, and general expectations. Be ready to explain why you want to move and what draws you to Zenoti specifically. A clear, concise answer here sets a positive tone for the rest of the process.

Stage 3: Technical or skills assessment. For engineering roles, this is usually an online coding test covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes SQL. For product management roles, expect a written case study or product thinking exercise. Customer success candidates may be asked to complete a mock presentation or a role-play scenario.

Stage 4: Technical or functional interviews. Engineers commonly go through one to two live interview rounds covering problem-solving and coding. Senior engineers additionally face a system design discussion. Product managers face rounds covering product sense, prioritisation, and execution. Each interviewer typically evaluates one specific dimension, so structured and concise answers serve you better than long-winded ones.

Stage 5: Hiring manager and leadership round. This is a mix of behavioural and situational questions. The interviewer wants to understand how you handle ambiguity, work with cross-functional teams, and stay focused on customer outcomes. This round carries significant weight at Zenoti.

After all rounds clear, HR typically extends a verbal offer before sending the written letter. Turnaround time is commonly reported as one to three weeks from the first round to offer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Zenoti looks for people who can own outcomes, not just complete tasks. Here is what tends to matter across the main roles.

For software engineers: Strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms are a baseline expectation. Backend engineers are typically expected to know Java, Python, or Node.js. Frontend engineers should be solid in React or Angular. Cloud platform experience (AWS or Azure) and familiarity with API design and microservices architecture give you an edge. Prior experience building multi-tenant SaaS products is a real differentiator.

For product managers: Zenoti wants PMs who can translate customer pain into product decisions. Product sense, structured prioritisation, and the ability to work closely with engineering teams matter here. Experience managing a product for enterprise or SMB clients is preferred. Interviewers commonly probe for metrics ownership: what did you measure, and how did you move those numbers?

For customer success and sales: Strong communication, relationship management, and the ability to explain a technical product to non-technical business owners are key. Experience handling enterprise accounts in a SaaS environment stands out. Candidates who show genuine curiosity about the wellness industry tend to do well.

Across all roles: Zenoti values clear communication, a customer-first mindset, and the willingness to take ownership beyond your immediate job description. Cultural fit carries real weight in the final rounds.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

1. Understand Zenoti's product before you apply. Watch product demo videos and read case studies about how wellness businesses use the platform. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually engaged with what the company builds. This matters especially for product, sales, and customer success roles.

2. Practice coding fundamentals for engineering roles. Medium-level problems on data structures and algorithms are the commonly cited benchmark for Zenoti's coding rounds. If you are targeting a senior role, also prepare for system design questions on topics like scalable APIs, database design for multi-tenant systems, and event-driven architecture.

3. Prepare your behavioural stories using the STAR format. Think through situations where you handled a difficult customer, delivered under pressure, or disagreed with a decision but still executed well. Zenoti's interviewers probe for ownership and accountability, so vague answers hurt you here.

4. Research the wellness tech landscape. Know who Zenoti competes with and what sets the product apart. This gives you strong material for 'why Zenoti' questions and signals that you have done genuine homework, not just skimmed the homepage.

5. Have your metrics ready. For product and business roles, be prepared to speak to specific outcomes from your past work: how many accounts you managed, what retention or adoption metrics you influenced, and what you learned when things did not go as planned. Specific is always better than general.

6. Tailor your resume to each job description. With 52 open roles across functions, there is likely more than one position that fits your profile. Apply to the roles that genuinely match your experience, and mirror the language in the job description in your resume.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Zenoti describes its culture as customer-obsessed and outcomes-driven. A few consistent themes emerge from what employees and candidates commonly report.

Customer problems are everyone's problem. Zenoti builds software for salon owners, spa managers, and wellness chains. The internal expectation is that every team, not just customer-facing ones, understands what those customers actually deal with day to day. Engineers who have never spoken to a customer are the exception here, not the norm.

Ownership over hand-offs. The culture discourages a 'my job ends here' mindset. Whether you are an engineer shipping a feature or a CS manager onboarding a new client, the expectation is that you follow through until the outcome is achieved, not just the task completed.

Working across time zones. Zenoti has teams in India and the USA and serves customers globally. Comfort with async communication and occasional overlap with US working hours is part of working here, especially at senior levels.

Learning and internal mobility. Employees commonly report that Zenoti invests in internal career development. Moving across teams or taking on broader scope within the company is a realistic path, not just a statement in the employee handbook.

High performance bar. Zenoti moves fast and sets a high execution standard. Candidates who want to build things that matter and see the impact of their work in real businesses tend to find that motivating.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Zenoti a good company to work for in Hyderabad?

Zenoti has a solid reputation among Hyderabad-based tech professionals, particularly in the B2B SaaS space. The company offers exposure to a global product and a real engineering culture, which appeals to engineers who want to work on scale problems. Employee reviews on publicly available platforms point to strong learning opportunities and a performance-driven environment. Like most fast-growing SaaS companies, the pace can be demanding, so it tends to suit people who want to grow quickly rather than those seeking a slower setting.

What salary can I expect at Zenoti?

Zenoti does not publicly disclose salary bands, so specific figures are hard to verify. Based on Glassdoor and industry surveys, compensation at Zenoti is broadly in line with Hyderabad SaaS market rates, which vary significantly by level and role. For accurate and current LPA figures, check Glassdoor or levels.fyi filtered to Zenoti reviews. Negotiating with a competing offer in hand is a commonly cited way to improve the initial offer.

Does Zenoti hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Zenoti primarily targets experienced professionals for most of its open roles, especially in engineering and product. However, the company does hire freshers and recent graduates for select roles in quality assurance, technical support, and customer success. If you are a fresher, look specifically for roles tagged as 'associate' or 'junior' in the job listing. Strong performance in the coding or skills assessment round can offset limited work experience.

How long does the Zenoti hiring process take from first round to offer?

The end-to-end process commonly takes two to four weeks, depending on the role and how quickly both sides can schedule interviews. Scheduling delays are the most frequent reason for a longer timeline. If you have a deadline such as an expiring offer from another company, communicate it early to the recruiter. Zenoti's recruiting team is generally responsive once the process is underway.

Is remote work available for India-based roles at Zenoti?

Most India-based roles at Zenoti are based in Hyderabad, and the company has moved toward a hybrid or in-office model for the majority of teams. Fully remote roles are limited and tend to appear only for senior or specialist positions. If remote work is a priority for you, check the specific job listing carefully and clarify the arrangement with the recruiter before proceeding through multiple interview rounds.

How can I stand out in the Zenoti application and interview process?

Candidates who stand out tend to do three things well: they engage with Zenoti's product before the interview rather than arriving cold, they bring specific metrics and outcomes from past roles instead of vague descriptions, and they show genuine interest in the wellness or SaaS space rather than treating Zenoti as just another application.
