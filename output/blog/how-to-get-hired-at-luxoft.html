01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Luxoft is a global software engineering company, now part of DXC Technology. Teams here build products and platforms for clients in automotive, financial services, telecom, travel, and healthcare sectors, mostly for companies headquartered in Europe and North America. India-based engineers typically work on embedded systems, digital transformation projects, enterprise software, and quality engineering for these global clients.

As of mid-2026, Luxoft has 10 open roles. Hiring is project-driven: new client contracts or expanding engagements trigger openings, so the mix of roles shifts regularly. Current openings span software engineering, architecture, and delivery management functions.

Luxoft recruits through its own careers portal, LinkedIn, and partner job sites. Most India-based hiring is concentrated in Pune, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru, though some roles allow remote work.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at Luxoft runs across several stages. The exact sequence varies by team and domain.

Stage 1: Application and resume screening. A recruiter checks your resume against the job description. Because roles are tied to specific client projects, keyword alignment matters more than at product companies. Tailor your resume to the domain and technologies mentioned in the posting.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A talent acquisition person covers basics: notice period, salary expectations, tech stack familiarity, and location fit. Be direct and honest about your current situation and timeline.

Stage 3: First technical interview. Usually conducted by a senior engineer or tech lead. Expect questions on the core technologies listed in the job description, system design (for mid-to-senior roles), and domain knowledge. Automotive roles may test embedded C or C++, real-time operating systems, or applied knowledge of automotive functional safety principles. Financial services roles may cover data pipelines, low-latency systems, or risk calculation logic.

Stage 4: Second technical or domain interview. Some teams add a round focused on deeper architecture, problem-solving, or a live coding exercise. For senior roles, this round may include a client-side interviewer.

Stage 5: HR and offer discussion. If the technical rounds go well, HR discusses the offer, benefits, and joining date. Negotiation is normal and expected at this stage.

Luxoft currently has 10 open roles. Because openings are tied to active projects rather than bulk hiring, each role tends to attract focused, relevant candidates.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Luxoft hires for technical depth first. Engineers work directly on client deliverables, so shallow knowledge shows up quickly. Here is what interviewers consistently focus on.

Domain expertise. If the role is in automotive, they want engineers who understand embedded constraints, safety-critical thinking, and real-time system behavior. If it is in financial services, they look for people who understand transaction processing, data integrity, and performance at scale. Generic experience without domain context is a weak fit for most Luxoft roles.

Strong fundamentals. For software roles, expect questions on data structures, algorithms, concurrency, and memory management, particularly for C++ and Java positions. Architecture questions test whether you can reason about trade-offs, not just name patterns.

Client communication skills. Luxoft engineers often interface directly with the client's technical teams. Clear written and spoken English, the ability to explain trade-offs, and comfort with structured status updates matter alongside coding ability.

Ownership mindset. Interviewers ask about situations where you caught a problem before it reached production, improved a process without being asked, or pushed back on a requirement for sound technical reasons. Passive executors are a poor fit for a consulting-model company.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study the job description carefully. Luxoft postings are specific. If they list a particular messaging system, testing framework, or embedded toolchain, know it at a working level, not just by name.

Build domain knowledge, not just coding skills. For automotive roles, revisit concepts like functional safety principles, RTOS scheduling, and bootloader architecture. For financial services, refresh your understanding of order management systems, real-time data processing, or risk engine design, whichever fits the role.

Prepare system design answers with trade-off reasoning. Luxoft values engineers who can argue for a design choice, not just describe one. Practice explaining why you chose one approach over another, including the costs and constraints that shaped the decision.

Sharpen your English communication. Many rounds involve English-medium interviews, and client-facing roles are assessed partly on communication quality. Practice explaining technical decisions in plain language to a non-technical listener.

Research Luxoft's client verticals. A look at their publicly available case studies shows which industries they serve most deeply. Showing awareness of their client context in the interview signals genuine interest beyond just clearing a round.

Have your salary expectation ready. Publicly reported data and Glassdoor figures for software engineering roles at similar global IT services firms give you a market reference. Come with a number you can justify based on your experience and the role's scope.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Luxoft operates as an engineering delivery company inside a larger corporate structure (DXC Technology). The culture leans technical and delivery-focused rather than startup-exploratory.

Project ownership over process. Teams are expected to deliver against client commitments. Engineers who wait for perfect requirements or escalate every ambiguity upward tend to struggle. Those who take a problem, clarify what they need, and move tend to do well.

International collaboration. Most India-based roles involve regular interaction with colleagues or clients in Europe (Germany, Switzerland, the UK) or North America. Time zone differences require some flexibility, and communication clarity across cultures matters as much as technical output.

Specialisation is valued. Luxoft does not rotate engineers across random domains. If you join an automotive embedded team, you will go deep in that space. This suits engineers who want to become a recognised expert in a domain rather than a generalist who jumps between stacks.

Stability with corporate structure. Being part of DXC means processes, approvals, and tooling decisions can feel slower than at a startup or product company. Engineers who find large-org structure frustrating should weigh this honestly before applying.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the Luxoft hiring process usually take?

The timeline varies by team and project. Commonly, candidates move through the recruiter call and first technical round within the first couple of weeks, but client approval can add time at the end. If you have not heard back within a week of completing any round, a polite follow-up with the recruiter is appropriate and expected.

Does Luxoft hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Luxoft primarily hires engineers with meaningful professional experience, because most roles are billed directly to clients who expect working-level skill from day one. Fresher hiring does happen but tends to be limited to specific campus programmes rather than regular job postings. If you are early in your career, building strong domain-specific skills before applying will significantly improve your chances.

Are Luxoft salaries competitive with Indian IT product companies?

Publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggests Luxoft compensation is broadly in line with other global IT services firms but typically below top-tier product companies for equivalent seniority. The trade-off is deep domain exposure and international client experience, which can accelerate your market value over time. Always negotiate: the first offer is rarely the final one.

Can I work remotely at Luxoft India?

Some roles are hybrid or remote-friendly, but many require on-site presence in Pune, Hyderabad, or Bengaluru, particularly during project kick-offs or client visits. The job description usually states the arrangement clearly. Ask the recruiter during the screening call to confirm before you invest time in the full interview process.

What tech stacks does Luxoft mainly hire for in India?

Embedded C and C++ for automotive projects, Java and Python for financial services platforms, and cloud-native technologies for digital transformation work are among the most common. The specific mix depends on which client engagements are active when you apply, so the current open roles listing gives you the most accurate picture.

Is it worth applying if my background is from a services company rather than a product company?

Yes. Luxoft's own model is delivery-oriented, so they understand and respect services-company experience. What matters more is domain depth and the ability to work directly with client technical teams. If your resume shows clear ownership of specific deliverables rather than just participation in large projects, it will read well to Luxoft interviewers.
