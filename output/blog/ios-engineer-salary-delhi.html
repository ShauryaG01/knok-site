01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most iOS Engineer listings in Delhi NCR do not disclose pay upfront. Of the 21 iOS Engineer openings tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026, none showed a salary band in the listing itself. This is common in Indian tech hiring: CTC is revealed at the offer stage, which leaves candidates negotiating without clear benchmarks.

Because the current Delhi NCR dataset carries no disclosed salary figures, this guide draws on Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and publicly reported industry surveys to give you directional ranges. Treat every figure as a starting point for your own research, not a guaranteed outcome. The iOS developer talent pool in Delhi NCR is smaller than in Bangalore, which can work in a strong candidate's favour when a company has a genuine iOS-first product.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

The figures below come from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and publicly reported industry surveys for iOS engineers in India. Delhi NCR-specific sample sizes are limited, so use these as directional benchmarks rather than precise targets.

Freshers (0-2 years). Glassdoor commonly cites entry-level iOS engineer pay in Delhi NCR in the 6-10 LPA range. Candidates with App Store releases or internship experience at a product company tend to land at the higher end of what is commonly cited.

Mid-level (3-5 years). Industry surveys and Glassdoor data commonly place mid-level iOS engineers in Delhi NCR in the 14-22 LPA band. SwiftUI fluency, Core Data experience, and a track record of shipping to the App Store are the factors most often cited when employers justify offers toward the upper end.

Senior and lead (6+ years). Levels.fyi and industry surveys commonly cite senior iOS engineer packages in India above 25 LPA for candidates with architecture ownership or team lead experience. In Delhi NCR, such roles are fewer in number than in Bangalore, so competition for the strongest packages tends to be lower.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Research before you speak to HR. Look up the role on Glassdoor and levels.fyi before your first recruiter call. Walking in with publicly reported ranges means you can anchor the discussion rather than react to a number you have not had time to evaluate.

Let HR name a figure first when possible. Many Delhi NCR recruiters ask for your current CTC and then offer a fixed increment on top. If you can redirect with something like 'I am looking at market rate for this role and level,' you avoid anchoring on a figure that may be below what the role is actually budgeted for.

Variable pay is not guaranteed pay. When you receive an offer, ask what portion of the variable component was actually paid out in the previous year. Glassdoor reviews commonly note that variable pay in Indian tech companies lands below the stated target in years when business performance is flat.

Benefits have more flexibility than base salary. Joining bonuses, extra leave, remote-work days, and equipment allowances are commonly cited as areas where employers can move, especially for mid-level and senior hires who have a competing offer or a specific ask.

Follow up in writing. After a verbal negotiation, send a short email restating your ask. This creates a record and gives the hiring manager something concrete to take to finance for approval.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Indian tech offers are rarely a single salary number. Here is how a typical iOS Engineer CTC in Delhi NCR is structured.

Fixed base salary. This is what reaches your bank account monthly. It is the most important component to negotiate because it drives your PF contribution, future increment calculations at this company, and the starting point for offers at your next employer.

Variable or performance pay. Many employers include a target bonus, which is commonly cited in industry surveys as a percentage of annual CTC for mid-level and senior roles. Glassdoor reviews show that actual payouts vary based on company results and individual ratings. Always ask what the real payout was in the previous year, not just what the stated target is.

Provident Fund (PF). Both you and your employer contribute to PF. The employer share is often included inside the quoted CTC. Ask whether PF is included in or added on top of the number being discussed, because this changes your effective in-hand pay.

Gratuity. Applicable after five years of continuous service. Larger companies include it in the CTC breakdown. Worth clarifying so you compare any two offers on a consistent basis.

Allowances. House Rent Allowance, Leave Travel Allowance, and food or transport vouchers reduce your taxable income and raise your effective in-hand pay compared to a pure basic salary at the same CTC level.

ESOPs or RSUs. A minority of Delhi NCR employers offer equity to iOS engineers, but funded startups are increasingly doing so. Levels.fyi tracks grant sizes and vesting schedules for some companies. Treat equity as upside rather than guaranteed pay, since its value depends entirely on the company's growth.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most iOS Engineer job listings in Delhi NCR not show the salary?

Indian law does not require employers to disclose pay in job postings, and most choose not to. The reason commonly given by HR teams is that compensation depends on the candidate's experience, but it also gives employers more flexibility during negotiation. Checking Glassdoor reviews filtered to the specific company and role is one of the more reliable ways to gauge a realistic range before you apply.

Is Delhi NCR a good city for iOS engineers to find work right now?

Based on the July 2026 knok jobradar snapshot, Delhi NCR has 21 active iOS Engineer openings, more than Bangalore (6), Mumbai (3), and Chennai (2) combined. That is a strong signal for a city that does not usually top iOS hiring charts. Whether the roles match your seniority level and product-versus-services preference is worth checking before you apply in bulk.

How do I judge whether an offer is fair when the listing shows no salary?

Use a combination of sources: Glassdoor reviews filtered to the company and role level, levels.fyi for product companies that have been catalogued, and direct conversations with peers in developer communities or LinkedIn groups. When you have data points from two or more publicly reported sources, you have a reasonable anchor to reference during negotiation rather than relying on a single number.

What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary, and why does it matter?

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total amount the employer spends on you, including PF contributions, gratuity provisions, and allowances that may not reach your bank account directly. In-hand pay is what you actually receive after tax and PF deductions. The gap can be significant, so always ask for a full salary breakup in writing before you sit down to compare any two offers.

Is switching companies a good way to get a meaningful pay increase as an iOS engineer?

Switching employers is commonly cited in Indian tech industry surveys as one of the faster routes to higher pay, especially for iOS engineers where the talent pool is relatively small compared to other engineering disciplines. The size of any increase depends on your current pay, the demand for your specific skills, and whether you have a competing offer to use as leverage. Glassdoor and levels.fyi data for your target company can help you set a realistic ask before you negotiate.

Should I push back if the recruiter says the offer is 'best and final'?

'Best and final' is a negotiating position, not a binding statement. In most cases it is worth one polite, specific counter grounded in publicly reported data or a competing offer letter. Keep your tone professional, make a single clear ask rather than an open-ended request, and accept that the answer may still be no. Publicly reported accounts from Indian tech hiring suggest that a well-reasoned counter rarely puts the offer itself at risk.
