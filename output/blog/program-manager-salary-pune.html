01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Finding reliable salary data for Technical Program Managers in Pune is genuinely difficult. Most Indian job listings do not show pay ranges, and TPM roles are no exception. As of July 2026, knok's jobradar tracked 13 active TPM openings in Pune, and not one of them publicly listed a salary band.

The most useful data comes from self-reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, supplemented by industry surveys from product and engineering communities. These sources give a directional picture, but keep in mind that sample sizes for Pune are thin compared to Bangalore or Delhi.

Pune's TPM market skews toward mid-size IT services companies and product arms of large conglomerates. Packages here tend to be more fixed-heavy than the equity-heavy structures common at Bangalore-based product startups. If you are evaluating an offer or planning a move, the ranges below will help you calibrate.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because Pune's 13 tracked TPM listings did not disclose salaries, the figures below come from Glassdoor and levels.fyi self-reported data, cross-referenced with industry surveys. Sample sizes for Pune are limited, so treat these as directional anchors rather than hard ceilings.

Junior or Associate TPM: Glassdoor and levels.fyi commonly show around 18-28 LPA for TPMs with 3-5 years of experience in Pune. Roles here typically involve managing a single product area and working closely under a senior TPM on cross-team dependencies.

Mid-level TPM: Publicly reported levels.fyi data and industry surveys suggest a range of 28-45 LPA for Pune-based TPMs with 6-10 years of experience managing multi-team programs. Fixed pay makes up most of the CTC at this stage, with variable pay as a smaller portion.

Senior TPM or TPM Lead: Glassdoor and levels.fyi data commonly place professionals with 11-15 years of experience at 45-70 LPA in Pune, for those driving organisation-wide programs and owning delivery across multiple business units.

Principal or Director: For TPMs with 16 or more years of experience, data from Glassdoor and levels.fyi in Pune is sparse, but industry surveys suggest compensation above 70 LPA is achievable at large MNC engineering centres in the city.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Know your number before the first conversation. Look up self-reported salaries on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company and role level. A TPM at a large MNC's Pune engineering centre is in a different compensation bracket from one at a mid-size product startup.

Anchor on total CTC, not just fixed pay. Many Pune offers blend fixed salary, variable pay, and a joining bonus. Ask for the split upfront. A publicly reported pattern from hiring communities is that variable pay is sometimes stated 'at target' but rarely paid in full during the first year.

Use competing offers when you have them. A real competing offer is the strongest lever in any negotiation. Even one alternative commonly shifts the final number upward. If you do not have a competing offer, referencing Glassdoor market data for the role and level is the next best anchor.

Negotiate the title alongside the salary. A move from 'TPM' to 'Senior TPM' is not just a label. It often unlocks a higher pay band at your next company. If the salary band is fixed, ask whether the title can reflect your actual scope of work.

Get equity terms in writing before signing. If the company offers ESOPs, ask for the vesting schedule, the current strike price, and the last valuation date. Industry surveys show that many Indian professionals skip this step, which can significantly affect total wealth over a four-year tenure.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Fixed salary (base pay): The guaranteed component and usually the largest share of a TPM's CTC in Pune. For mid-to-senior roles, Glassdoor and levels.fyi data suggest base pay accounts for roughly 70-80% of total CTC at most non-startup companies, according to industry surveys.

Variable pay (performance bonus): Tied to individual and company performance. Industry surveys and publicly reported data from hiring communities suggest variable pay typically runs 10-20% of fixed salary for mid-level TPMs, rising for Director-level roles.

Joining bonus: Common when a company asks you to forgo unvested equity at your current employer. This is a one-time payment and should not be confused with recurring compensation.

ESOPs or RSUs: More common at product startups and listed MNCs than at IT services firms. Glassdoor data for equity grants varies widely and is hard to benchmark without knowing a company's current valuation and vesting cliff.

Benefits and allowances: Medical insurance, gratuity, meal allowances, fuel reimbursement, and LTA all form part of gross CTC at many Indian companies. Always ask for the 'take home in hand' figure alongside the headline CTC when comparing offers.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most TPM job listings in Pune not show a salary?

Indian labour law does not require companies to disclose pay ranges in job postings, and most employers prefer to gauge a candidate's expectations before revealing their budget. This is especially common at mid-size IT services firms, which dominate Pune's TPM market. The practical workaround is to check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company before applying, so you enter the conversation with a realistic anchor rather than guessing.

Is Pune a good city for a TPM career compared to Bangalore?

Pune is a solid choice if you prefer lower living costs and are open to roles at IT services majors, automotive tech companies, or fintech firms. Bangalore had 41 active TPM openings versus 13 in Pune in knok's July 2026 snapshot, and it concentrates more product-first companies where Glassdoor-reported salaries tend to be higher. That said, Pune salaries have been narrowing the gap, and remote-friendly roles have blurred the city distinction for many senior TPMs.

What skills help a TPM command a higher salary in Pune?

The biggest salary lifts commonly come from three areas: experience managing large-scale cross-org programs, deep familiarity with enterprise agile frameworks like SAFe, and domain expertise in a high-demand vertical such as cloud infrastructure, fintech, or automotive electronics. Certifications like PMP or CSM help at mid-level, but publicly reported hiring feedback suggests they matter far less than a proven track record of delivering complex, multi-team programs on time and at scope.

How is a Technical Program Manager different from a Project Manager or a Product Manager?

A TPM focuses on the engineering execution layer: coordinating across multiple engineering teams, resolving technical dependencies, and ensuring delivery milestones are met. A Project Manager is often process-focused and may not need deep technical fluency. A Product Manager owns the 'what and why' of a product, while the TPM owns the 'how and when' of building it. In Pune, many listings blur these boundaries, so read the responsibilities section carefully before applying.

Does the total CTC for a TPM in Pune typically include equity?

It depends heavily on employer type. IT services companies and traditional conglomerates, which make up a large share of Pune's TPM market, rarely include ESOPs in the package. Product startups and listed MNCs with Pune engineering centres are more likely to offer equity. Industry surveys suggest professionals in Pune tend to undervalue equity compared to their Bangalore peers, partly because fewer local companies include it. Always ask for the vesting cliff and the most recent valuation before factoring equity into your decision.

How should I evaluate a TPM offer that does not disclose the salary band?

Start by researching Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the company and the specific TPM level before your first call. If the recruiter deflects on salary, share your expected CTC range based on Glassdoor data and industry surveys, which puts the anchor in your favour. Once an offer arrives, break it into fixed, variable, joining bonus, and equity components so you are comparing like-for-like with any competing offers. Never evaluate the headline CTC number alone.
