01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

UI/UX Designer compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Lead with case studies, not just screens. Recruiters at startups want to see how you think, not only what the final product looks like. Document your process: the user research you did, the pain points you found, how your wireframes evolved, and the reasoning behind your final decisions.

Match your portfolio to the company. For Signzy and Expertiaai, highlight work where you simplified a technically complex flow for a non-expert user. For Housing, show experience with search interfaces, map-based navigation, or multi-step journeys at scale. For Hyreo and Happylocate, lean into onboarding flows or task-heavy workflows that reduce friction for busy professionals.

Nail the tools baseline. Figma is expected at every company on this list. If you have built or maintained a design system, call it out prominently. Prototyping skills and working knowledge of accessibility guidelines (WCAG) will separate you from candidates who only deliver static mockups.

Show outcome awareness. Even rough metrics matter: task completion improvements, drop-off reductions, satisfaction scores. If you do not have hard numbers, describe the qualitative outcome clearly. Startups hire designers who think about impact, not just aesthetics.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With 6 roles in Bangalore right now, the pool is small enough that you should apply to every opening that fits your experience level. Do not wait to perfect your portfolio. A solid application today beats a polished one next week if a role closes in the meantime.

Personalize your cover note. One paragraph connecting your past work to the company's actual product is enough. Signzy and Housing receive many generic applications. A note that references their onboarding flow or search experience stands out immediately.

Go direct when you can. Startups at the Happylocate and Expertiaai stage sometimes post roles on their own careers pages before they appear on major job boards. Follow these companies on LinkedIn and check their sites directly every few days.

Keep an eye on other cities. Delhi has 8 openings right now, more than Bangalore. Q Questions

Frequently asked

Are there any GCC or MNC UI/UX roles currently open in Bangalore?

The July 2026 knok snapshot shows no GCC or large MNC openings for UI/UX Designers in Bangalore right now. All 6 active roles are at startups or product companies. If GCC roles are a priority for you, Delhi currently shows more openings and may have a better mix of company types. Check back in a few weeks as the GCC hiring cycle in Bangalore tends to pick up toward quarter-end.

Which company on the list is best for a junior UI/UX Designer?

Hyreo and Happylocate are early-stage enough that they often bring on junior designers who can grow with the team and take ownership quickly. Housing (housing.com) is the most established name here and offers junior designers exposure to a high-traffic product with more structured processes. Signzy and Expertiaai work in technically complex domains, fintech KYC and AI workflows, so some prior exposure to enterprise or SaaS UX would help you ramp up faster there.

What tools should I know before applying to these companies?

Figma is the baseline at every company on this list, including prototyping within Figma and clean developer handoff. Experience building or maintaining a design system is a strong differentiator. User research skills (interviews, usability testing) and familiarity with accessibility standards like WCAG are increasingly asked for at product companies like Housing. For Expertiaai and similar AI-focused roles, knowing how to design for AI-assisted workflows is a growing advantage.

What kind of salary can I expect from these companies?

Salary data for these specific roles is not in the current snapshot, so use Glassdoor and industry surveys as your reference point. Publicly reported figures for UI/UX Designers in Bangalore are commonly cited in the 4-8 LPA range at entry level and 10-18 LPA at mid level, with senior roles commonly cited above 20 LPA. Startups at the Hyreo and Happylocate stage may offer ESOPs alongside a base that is lower than a scaled product company, so ask about equity vesting terms during the offer discussion.

Is Bangalore a good city for UI/UX Designers compared to Delhi or Mumbai?

Based on the current snapshot, Delhi has more openings right now (8 vs 6 in Bangalore), but Bangalore is the stronger long-term market for product-focused UX roles because of its concentration of SaaS and consumer tech startups. Mumbai leans more toward agency work and fintech design. If you are targeting startup product companies specifically, Bangalore is your best bet for depth of opportunity over time, even when the current snapshot is on the smaller side.

How quickly do UI/UX roles at startups like these close?

Startup roles, especially at smaller companies like Happylocate or Expertiaai, can close within days of going live because the hiring team is small and moves fast once they find a strong fit. Apply within the first few days of spotting a role, and personalize your application enough to prompt a quick callback. Setting up job alerts or using a tool that monitors postings daily ensures you do not miss a role that fills before you even see it.
