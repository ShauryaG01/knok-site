01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Astellas Pharma is a global pharmaceutical company headquartered in Tokyo, with operations across Asia, Europe, and the Americas. In India, Astellas runs teams in clinical research, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, medical affairs, IT, and shared services, with a strong focus on oncology, urology, and transplantation.

As of July 2026, Astellas has 116 open roles across its global operations, making it an actively hiring organization. Most India-based positions are concentrated in cities like Bengaluru and Mumbai, though hybrid and remote options appear regularly.

If you are a pharma professional, a clinical research associate, a regulatory specialist, or a finance or IT professional with life sciences exposure, Astellas is a credible next step. The company offers the stability of a large MNC alongside the depth of a science-led culture.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Astellas hiring process typically moves through four to five stages, though the exact flow varies by function and level.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screening
Recruiters review your application against the specific requirements of each role. For clinical positions, GCP certification matters. For regulatory roles, hands-on dossier submission experience is often a key filter. Tailoring your resume closely to the job description improves your chances here.

Stage 2: HR Screening Call
A recruiter or HR business partner reaches out to confirm your background, current and expected compensation, and availability. This call is also your chance to clarify the role, team structure, and next steps in the process.

Stage 3: Technical or Functional Interview
This is the domain-depth round. Clinical research applicants commonly face questions on ICH-GCP guidelines, site monitoring, and protocol deviations. Pharmacovigilance candidates are tested on adverse event classification and reporting timelines. IT and shared services candidates face questions specific to their stack or process area.

Stage 4: Panel or Hiring Manager Round
A senior stakeholder or cross-functional panel explores your experience and problem-solving approach in greater depth. Behavioral questions in the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) are commonly used at this stage.

Stage 5: Offer and Background Verification
Successful candidates receive a verbal offer followed by a formal letter. Standard background checks are part of the process and typically take a week or two to clear. Having your documents organized in advance avoids delays.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Astellas looks for professionals who combine strong domain expertise with a collaborative, patient-first mindset. Here is what tends to stand out across most roles:

Domain Knowledge and Compliance Awareness
Familiarity with ICH guidelines, GCP, GMP, or GDP (depending on your function) is important. Regulatory roles also value direct experience with submissions to the CDSCO, US FDA, or EMA.

Cross-Functional Communication
Astellas operates as a global matrix organization. Strong written and verbal communication, especially across time zones and with international colleagues, is highly valued.

Analytical Thinking
Clinical data managers, biostatisticians, and medical affairs professionals should demonstrate comfort with data analysis and scientific reasoning. Being able to walk an interviewer through your thinking process matters as much as knowing the answer.

Adaptability
The pharmaceutical industry moves fast, particularly in oncology and specialty areas. Interviewers appreciate candidates who have navigated change, whether a protocol amendment, a regulatory update, or a team restructuring.

Cultural Fit with a Global Organization
Astellas is a Japanese-founded company with a strong emphasis on respect, precision, and long-term thinking. Candidates who demonstrate reliability, attention to detail, and comfort with cross-cultural collaboration tend to stand out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A bit of structured preparation goes a long way at Astellas. Here is where to focus your energy:

Know the Therapeutic Areas
Astellas is known for its work in oncology, urology, transplantation, and ophthalmology. Read the company's publicly available pipeline updates and understand where its current research priorities lie. This context helps you ask sharper questions and shows genuine interest beyond the job description.

Brush Up on Regulatory and Clinical Guidelines
For clinical, regulatory, or pharmacovigilance roles, revisit ICH E6 (GCP), ICH E3, and any CDSCO-specific guidelines relevant to your function. Interviewers commonly ask scenario-based questions that test how you apply these in practice, not just whether you can recite them.

Prepare STAR-Format Answers
For behavioral rounds, structure your answers around a specific Situation, the Task you were given, the Action you took, and the Result you achieved. Think of three to four strong examples from your current or past roles that show problem-solving, cross-team collaboration, and how you handled difficult situations.

Research the Team and Role
Look up your interviewers on LinkedIn before each round. Understanding their background helps you tailor your answers and ask relevant questions. Also map your experience point by point to what is listed in the job description before every interview.

Prepare Thoughtful Questions
Astellas interviewers appreciate candidates who ask informed questions. Good ones include: 'How does this team collaborate with the global function?' or 'What does success look like in the first six months in this role?'

Sort Out Your Documents Early
Keep your offer letters, payslips, educational certificates, and experience letters ready for background verification. Having these organized prevents delays once an offer is made.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Astellas describes its purpose as 'turning innovative science into value for patients.' That mission shapes a culture that is patient-centric, science-driven, and globally collaborative.

Patient Focus
Everything traces back to patient outcomes. Employees across functions, from clinical operations to IT to finance, are encouraged to connect their daily work to the end goal of improving patient lives. This is not just a tagline. It shapes how priorities are set and how decisions are made.

Commitment to Integrity
Astellas places a high premium on ethical conduct, regulatory compliance, and data transparency. This is especially visible in clinical and pharmacovigilance teams, where data integrity is non-negotiable and corners are simply not cut.

Collaboration Across Cultures
As a Japanese company with a large global footprint, Astellas values respectful, inclusive collaboration. Meetings often involve colleagues from Japan, the US, and Europe. Navigating this diversity with patience and clarity is a key soft skill for anyone joining the organization.

Continuous Learning
The company invests in employee development through structured learning programs and encourages professionals to grow within the organization. Internal mobility is reasonably well-regarded, based on publicly reported employee feedback.

Work-Life Awareness
Astellas has made public commitments to employee wellbeing. Hybrid and flexible working arrangements are available in many roles, particularly in shared services and corporate functions.

How many open roles does Astellas currently have?

As of July 2026, Astellas has 116 open roles across its global operations, including positions in India. What departments does Astellas hire for in India?

In India, Astellas typically hires for clinical operations, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, medical science liaison roles, IT and data management, and shared services functions like finance and HR. Is the Astellas interview process difficult?

The process is rigorous but fair. Technical rounds are thorough, especially for clinical and regulatory roles where knowledge of ICH guidelines and submission processes is tested in real-scenario depth. Behavioral rounds follow a structured STAR format, so preparing your examples well in advance makes a real difference. Astellas does not publicly disclose salary bands for India-based roles. Researching comparable roles on Glassdoor before your HR screening call helps you share a well-informed salary expectation rather than anchoring too low or too high.

How long does the full Astellas hiring process take?

The end-to-end process, from application to offer, commonly takes four to eight weeks, though it can vary by role and the availability of interview panels. Background verification adds a week or two after the offer stage. Staying responsive to recruiter messages and having your documents ready in advance can help avoid unnecessary delays.

Does Astellas hire freshers or entry-level candidates?

Astellas does hire at the entry level in some functions, particularly clinical data management, pharmacovigilance, and shared services. Most entry-level roles expect at minimum a relevant degree in pharmacy, life sciences, or a technical field, and prior internship experience is a clear advantage. For mid and senior levels, a proven track record in the relevant pharmaceutical function is typically required.
