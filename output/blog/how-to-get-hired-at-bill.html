01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

BILL (formerly Bill.com) is a financial operations platform built for small and mid-sized businesses. The company helps businesses automate accounts payable, accounts receivable, expense management, and spend control. BILL has grown into a publicly listed fintech company with a strong product suite and a global workforce.

As of mid-2026, BILL has 56 open roles across engineering, product, sales, customer success, and operations. Hiring happens across their US offices as well as some remote positions, though many senior roles are US-based. Indian candidates will find the most traction in engineering, data, and support functions.

The hiring process at BILL is structured and moves at a reasonable pace, with most candidates going through a few rounds depending on the role and seniority level. Preparation matters here. The company attracts strong applicants, so a tailored approach makes a real difference.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screen
After you apply, a recruiter or an automated system reviews your resume. Getting past this stage depends heavily on how well your resume matches the job description. Keywords from the JD, relevant fintech or SaaS experience, and measurable outcomes in your past roles all help.

Stage 2: Recruiter Call
If your profile stands out, a recruiter reaches out for a short introductory call. They cover your background, why you want to join BILL, your current notice period, and compensation expectations. Be honest and clear here. This is also your chance to ask about the role and the team.

Stage 3: Hiring Manager or Technical Screen
For engineering roles, this is typically a coding round on a shared platform. For product or business roles, expect a case discussion or a walk-through of your past work. The hiring manager wants to see whether you can do the job, not just talk about it.

Stage 4: Technical or Domain Interview Panel
This is usually 2-3 back-to-back interviews with team members. Engineers face algorithm and system design questions. Product managers discuss product sense, metrics, and prioritization. Sales and customer success candidates go through role plays or scenario-based questions about handling SMB clients.

Stage 5: Final or Leadership Round
Senior candidates often meet a director or VP. This round tests cultural fit, decision-making, and leadership thinking. It is also a chance for you to evaluate whether this is the right team and the right challenge for your career.

Stage 6: Offer and Background Check
Once the panel debrief is done, BILL typically moves quickly. Offers include a base salary, equity (RSUs for most tech roles), and benefits. Background verification follows the offer. Having your documents ready speeds this up.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

BILL looks for people who combine strong functional skills with a customer-first mindset. Since the company serves small businesses, the team values people who genuinely care about making complex financial workflows simple for everyday business owners.

For engineering roles: Strong fundamentals in data structures, system design, and clean code matter. Experience with payment systems, financial APIs, or SaaS infrastructure is a plus. BILL commonly works with Java, Python, and modern cloud stacks.

For product roles: Product managers who have shipped features in B2B or fintech, who can frame decisions with data, and who communicate clearly across engineering and business teams tend to do well here.

For sales and customer success: BILL wants people who understand the SMB market, can build relationships quickly, and are comfortable with a consultative selling approach. Prior SaaS or fintech sales experience is an advantage.

Across all roles, BILL values clear communication, ownership of outcomes, and a track record of getting things done in fast-moving environments. People who wait to be told what to do tend to struggle here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply. Use the BILL demo or free trial before your interview. Understand how their AP and AR automation works, who their customers are, and what problems they solve. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually used the product and can speak to the user experience.

Align your resume to the JD. BILL's job descriptions are specific. Mirror the language used in the JD in your resume and interview answers. If the JD says 'payment processing experience', talk about your relevant work in those exact terms.

Prepare STAR stories. For behavioral questions, structure your answers around Situation, Task, Action, and Result. BILL interviewers often probe for ownership and impact, so make sure your examples show what you personally did and what changed because of it.

Brush up on fintech basics. Even for non-finance roles, understanding concepts like accounts payable, reconciliation, and spend controls will help you ask smart questions and connect your work to BILL's mission. A short read on how SMB finance works goes a long way.

Prepare questions for them. Ask about team structure, how success is measured in the role, and what the product roadmap looks like. Good questions signal genuine interest and help you stand out from candidates who only answer questions.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

BILL describes itself as a company where the mission to help small businesses succeed drives everyday decisions. The culture is collaborative, with an emphasis on data-driven thinking and continuous improvement rather than gut-feel choices.

Teams at BILL are generally described as friendly and approachable. The company has invested in diversity and inclusion programs, and publicly reports progress on representation. Work-life balance is commonly cited as better than typical fintech startups, though expectations ramp up during product launches or quarter-end periods.

BILL encourages employees to take ownership of their work rather than waiting for direction. If you prefer clearly defined tasks and close management, the culture may feel ambiguous at first. But if you like autonomy and can manage your own priorities, most employees find it rewarding and growth-oriented.

The company also places value on learning and development. Many employees on professional review platforms report that internal mobility is possible, and that managers are open to career conversations. If you want to move from one function to another over time, BILL is a place where that path exists.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does BILL hire in India or offer remote roles for Indian candidates?

BILL's primary offices are in the US. Some engineering and support roles have been posted for remote or India-based candidates, but this varies by team and the time of year. Check the BILL careers page for current location eligibility on each open role before applying. Remote availability can change quickly depending on team headcount plans.

How long does the BILL hiring process take from application to offer?

Most candidates report a process spanning a few weeks from first recruiter call to offer, though timelines vary by role and team. Engineering panels can sometimes move faster if the team has an urgent opening to fill. Staying responsive to recruiter communication and being ready with references helps keep the process moving without unnecessary delays.

What salary can I expect at BILL?

For India-based or remote roles, compensation varies widely by level and function. For US-based roles, Glassdoor and levels.fyi list publicly reported figures for different levels across engineering, product, and sales. Always discuss your compensation expectations early in the recruiter call so there are no surprises later in the process.

How competitive is it to get hired at BILL?

BILL is a well-known fintech brand, so open roles attract strong applicants. Core engineering and product roles tend to be more competitive than sales or operations positions. With 56 open roles currently listed, there are opportunities across multiple functions, and a tailored resume that speaks directly to the JD helps you stand out.

Is prior fintech experience required to join BILL?

Not always. BILL hires from SaaS, enterprise software, and even non-tech industries for certain roles. What matters more is whether you understand the problem they are solving and can show relevant skills that transfer. Fintech experience is a genuine plus but is not a hard requirement for most roles outside of specialized finance or compliance positions.

What is the best way to get noticed as an applicant?

A referral from a current BILL employee significantly increases your chances of getting a recruiter call. If you do not have a referral, a well-tailored resume that mirrors the JD language and a complete LinkedIn profile are your best tools. Following BILL on LinkedIn and engaging thoughtfully with their content can also help you stay visible to their talent team over time.
