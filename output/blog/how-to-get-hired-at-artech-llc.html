01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Artech LLC is a US-based IT staffing and technology solutions company, widely recognized as a leading minority-owned staffing firm in the United States. They work with major corporations across banking, telecom, healthcare, and technology to place contract and permanent professionals.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar shows 162 open roles at Artech, covering a broad mix of IT, engineering, finance, healthcare IT, and administrative positions. Most openings are contract or contract-to-hire roles placed at Artech's end clients, though direct-hire positions appear regularly too.

If you are an Indian professional aiming to work in the US or with US-aligned teams, Artech is worth targeting. Their scale means new openings appear frequently, and they have a track record of sponsoring work visas for eligible candidates. Indian professionals in IT, data, SAP, and healthcare IT will find Artech particularly relevant.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Artech follows a recruiter-led model because Artech places you with an end client, not directly into an Artech internal team. The steps below are typical, though the client's own process adds variability.

Stage 1: Recruiter screen. An Artech recruiter reaches out after reviewing your profile or application. Expect a short call covering your experience, availability, compensation expectations, and visa or work-authorization status. Be clear and specific here, as the recruiter uses this information to match you with the right client opening.

Stage 2: Profile submission. If the recruiter thinks you are a fit, they submit your resume to the end client. This step can take a few days, and you may not hear anything during that window.

Stage 3: Client interview. This is usually one or two rounds, depending on the client. Technical roles typically involve a coding test or a domain-skills discussion covering Java, Python, SAP, data pipelines, cloud, and similar areas. Non-technical roles lean toward competency or situational questions. Most interviews are conducted over video call.

Stage 4: Offer and onboarding. Once the client selects you, Artech issues the offer with contract terms, bill rate, and duration. A background check follows before your start date is confirmed.

The whole process from first recruiter call to start date commonly takes a few weeks, though complex roles at large clients can stretch longer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Because Artech places professionals across many industries and client types, what they look for varies by role. A few traits matter across the board.

Clear, verifiable skills. Artech recruiters scan for specific tools, platforms, and years of experience because that is what client job descriptions ask for. Vague summaries hurt you. List the exact technologies you have used, the versions where relevant, and the scale of projects you have handled.

Responsiveness and professionalism. The staffing model moves fast. Candidates who reply quickly to recruiter messages, show up on time to interviews, and communicate clearly about availability get prioritized. Ghosting or being hard to reach is a quick way to lose an opportunity.

Work-authorization clarity. Many Artech clients have strict requirements around visa status and authorization. Be upfront about your current status (H-1B, OPT, GC, citizen, etc.) from the very first call. Artech does sponsor H-1B for some roles, but not all client positions allow it.

Domain depth in high-demand areas. Roles that appear most frequently in Artech's pipeline (based on the 162 open roles tracked by knok) span IT staffing, SAP, data and analytics, software engineering, healthcare IT, and finance-adjacent technology. Depth in any of these areas raises your chances of matching active openings.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Polish your resume for keyword matching. Artech recruiters and their clients filter by specific keywords. Make sure your resume lists tools, platforms, certifications, and methodologies by their exact names. 'AWS' is not the same as 'Amazon Web Services' in a keyword search, so include both where relevant.

Set up a crisp LinkedIn profile. Many Artech recruiters source directly from LinkedIn. A complete profile with your title, skills, current location, and an 'Open to Work' signal (even set to recruiters only) significantly increases inbound recruiter outreach.

Know your expectations before the recruiter call. Be ready to state your expected compensation (in LPA if India-based, or hourly and annual if US-based), your notice period, and your visa status. Recruiters appreciate candidates who have done this homework.

Prepare for a fast-moving process. Once a client shortlists you, interviews can be scheduled within a few days. Have your calendar cleared, your tech setup ready for video interviews, and your documents (resume, certifications, ID proofs) organized in advance.

Practice the core technical areas for your role. For software roles, revisit data structures, system design basics, and the primary language listed on your resume. For SAP roles, be ready for module-specific scenario questions. For data roles, brush up on SQL, pipeline concepts, and your primary tool such as Spark, dbt, or Databricks.

Stay in touch with your recruiter. If you applied and heard nothing after a week, a polite follow-up is appropriate. Artech handles high volumes of candidates, and a brief check-in keeps your profile visible.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Artech places a strong emphasis on diversity and inclusion, which is central to its identity as a minority-owned business. The company has publicly received recognitions for diversity hiring practices, and this shapes how they position themselves to both clients and candidates.

Because Artech is a staffing firm, most employees experience two distinct cultures: the Artech internal culture (your recruiter, account manager, and HR contacts) and the end client's culture (where you actually sit and work day to day). Both matter.

On the Artech side, the environment is relationship-driven. Recruiters who build long-term pipelines with candidates tend to place them repeatedly. Being a reliable, communicative candidate means you are more likely to be called first when a new role matches your profile.

On the client side, culture varies widely because Artech serves clients across banking, healthcare, telecom, and technology. A placement at a large bank will feel very different from one at a mid-size healthcare technology company. Research the specific client during your interview process, not just Artech itself.

Artech has publicly stated a commitment to workforce development and ethical hiring. Candidates who encounter any pressure to misrepresent visa status or experience should treat that as a red flag and escalate immediately.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Artech LLC sponsor H-1B visas?

Artech does sponsor H-1B visas for some positions, but not every role in their pipeline allows it. Sponsorship depends on the end client's requirements, not just Artech's own policy. Always confirm visa sponsorship eligibility with the recruiter at the very first call, before your profile is submitted to any client.

Are Artech jobs permanent or contract?

The majority of Artech openings are contract or contract-to-hire positions placed at end clients. Permanent direct-hire roles exist but are less common. Contract durations vary by client project, with many running for several months and frequently extending. If a permanent role matters to you, ask the recruiter to clarify the nature of the opening before proceeding.

How do I get a recruiter at Artech to notice my profile?

Keep your LinkedIn profile complete and set to 'Open to Work' (recruiters-only visibility is fine). Use exact keywords matching your domain, since Artech recruiters search by specific skills and tools. Applying directly through Artech's careers page also puts you in their applicant tracking system, where recruiters search when a new client requirement comes in.

What salary or compensation can I expect from an Artech placement?

Compensation depends entirely on the end client, the role, and the location. Publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi shows wide ranges across IT staffing placements depending on specialization and geography. For India-based or remote roles, ask the recruiter for the LPA or CTC upfront. For US placements, hourly contract rates vary by specialization and are commonly cited during initial recruiter screening calls.

How long does the Artech hiring process take?

The process commonly takes a few weeks from the first recruiter call to a confirmed start date, though this varies significantly by client. Some urgent openings move within days, while roles at larger enterprises with multiple interview rounds can stretch to several weeks. Staying responsive at every stage keeps your timeline as short as possible.

Can I apply to Artech jobs from India?

Yes, Artech hires candidates based in India for remote roles that serve US clients, as well as candidates who are in the process of relocating. If you are applying for a US-based role and are currently in India, be transparent with the recruiter about your timeline and visa situation from the start. Roles requiring immediate on-site presence in the US will typically need you to already be authorized to work there.
