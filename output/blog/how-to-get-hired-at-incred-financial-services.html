01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

InCred Financial Services is a tech-driven NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company) focused on lending across personal, education, home, and SME finance segments. The company has built its identity around using data and technology to speed up credit decisions, positioning itself as a faster, more customer-friendly alternative to traditional lenders.

As of July 2026, InCred has around 6 open roles listed across job platforms, covering functions like credit and risk, technology, sales, and operations. These span entry-level analyst positions and mid-senior management openings, making InCred a viable target whether you are a recent graduate or a professional with several years of experience.

Most hiring is concentrated in Mumbai, with select roles in Bengaluru and Delhi NCR. InCred tends to favour candidates who are comfortable with data, communicate clearly, and genuinely understand how a lending business works from the inside.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process generally runs through three to four rounds, though the exact number varies by role and seniority. Here is what candidates typically experience.

Resume screening and HR call. A recruiter reviews your resume for relevant background in lending, fintech, or financial services. If shortlisted, they call to confirm your notice period, current location, and basic salary expectations. This round is short and conversational, lasting around twenty to thirty minutes.

Technical or domain interview. For credit and risk roles, expect questions on credit underwriting concepts, financial statement analysis, and metrics like NPA and FOIR. For tech roles, expect coding problems or system design discussions. For sales roles, the focus is on your past performance, how you handle customer objections, and your familiarity with loan products.

Case study or assignment (role-dependent). Some roles in analytics and credit include a take-home case study. You might be asked to analyse a dataset, draft a credit note, or propose a distribution strategy for a loan product. This round tests how you think through real business problems, not just whether you know the theory.

Senior leadership discussion. The final round is usually with a department head or business unit leader. Expect less grilling on technical knowledge and more conversation about your long-term goals, how you handle ambiguity, and whether your working style fits the team.

Offer timelines vary. Junior-level candidates sometimes hear back within a few weeks. Senior roles can take longer depending on how many stakeholders are involved in the final decision.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

InCred looks for a specific combination of analytical thinking, commercial awareness, and clear communication. The priorities shift by function.

Credit and risk roles call for solid credit appraisal skills, familiarity with RBI guidelines for NBFCs, and comfort working with Excel or SQL to analyse data quickly.

Tech roles require experience with relevant programming languages and frameworks, plus genuine interest in building financial products rather than generic software. Candidates who can connect their engineering work to real business outcomes tend to stand out.

Sales roles reward a verifiable track record in loan distribution or relationship management, comfort with monthly targets, and the ability to build trust quickly with customers or channel partners like DSAs.

Operations and collections roles value process orientation, composure under pressure, and familiarity with lending workflows from sourcing to disbursement.

Across all roles, InCred consistently looks for people who take ownership, communicate their reasoning clearly, and adapt without needing constant direction from above.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparation for InCred interviews is straightforward if you focus on three areas.

Understand the NBFC lending model. Know how NBFCs differ from banks in terms of regulation and funding sources. Be ready to explain what metrics like NPA (Non-Performing Assets), FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio), and LTV (Loan to Value) mean in practice, and why they matter to a lender's portfolio health.

Sharpen your core domain skills. For credit roles, practise reading P&L statements and balance sheets until you can spot red flags quickly. For tech roles, work through standard coding problems and review system design basics relevant to financial applications. For sales, prepare specific examples of targets you hit, deals you closed, and difficult customer conversations you navigated.

Research InCred specifically. Check their website and publicly available news for product launches, new loan categories, or geographic expansions from 2024-2026. Interviewers respond well to candidates who show genuine curiosity about what the company is building, not just generic fintech enthusiasm.

Practise structured answers. InCred interviews often include situation-based questions. Describe the situation clearly, what you were responsible for, what you actually did, and what resulted. Keep answers specific and grounded in real examples rather than hypotheticals.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

InCred positions itself as a builder's environment. Teams are lean, decisions move faster than at large PSU banks, and individuals are expected to take ownership rather than wait for direction.

Data-backed thinking is central to how the company operates. Whether you are in credit, product, or sales, you will be expected to support your decisions with numbers and logic. At the same time, InCred works in a market where relationships matter, so analytical skills without commercial awareness will only carry you so far.

The pace is generally described as fast by candidates who have joined from older financial institutions. People used to layers of approval and slow decision cycles sometimes find the expectation to move quickly and figure things out independently to be a meaningful adjustment.

Early ownership is common. Managers and senior individual contributors often own significant parts of the business with relatively light oversight. If you want to build something and see your work have a direct impact on a product or a loan portfolio, that opportunity exists here. If you prefer a more structured, process-heavy environment, expect a period of acclimatisation.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does InCred hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

InCred does hire freshers, primarily into analyst roles in credit, operations, and technology. For fresher hires, the process often includes an aptitude or domain assessment before the interview rounds. That said, most of the 6 currently listed roles appear targeted at candidates with some prior experience in financial services or tech, so check the job descriptions carefully before applying.

How long does the InCred hiring process typically take from application to offer?

For junior to mid-level roles, the process from first contact to offer can take a few weeks, assuming rounds are scheduled without major delays. Senior roles tend to take longer because more stakeholders are involved in the final decision. Following up politely with the recruiter after each round is reasonable if you have not heard back in about a week.

Do non-tech roles at InCred involve any written test or assignment?

Yes, some non-tech roles, particularly in credit and analytics, include a case study or take-home assignment. These are typically practical: analysing a borrower profile, reviewing a financial statement, or recommending a credit decision with supporting reasoning. The goal is to see how you think through a real problem, not just whether you have memorised the theory.

How important is prior NBFC or lending experience for getting hired at InCred?

Lending or NBFC experience is a strong advantage for credit, risk, and operations roles, but it is not always a strict requirement. Candidates from banking, insurance, or consulting backgrounds have joined InCred by demonstrating a solid grasp of credit fundamentals and the ability to pick up company-specific processes quickly. For tech roles, financial services experience matters less than strong engineering skills and genuine interest in fintech.

Is salary negotiable at InCred, and how should I approach that conversation?

Salary negotiation is generally possible at InCred, as it is at most private financial services firms. Industry surveys suggest that well-funded NBFCs in India are broadly competitive with private banks at similar seniority levels, though specific compensation varies widely by role, city, and individual background. Come prepared with publicly reported benchmarks from sources like Glassdoor and a clear sense of your own minimum expectation so the conversation stays grounded and productive.

What is the best way to get my application noticed by InCred recruiters?

Tailoring your resume to highlight lending, data, or sales outcomes (rather than just listing responsibilities) makes a real difference at a company that values measurable results. Reaching out to InCred employees on LinkedIn with a specific, well-researched message can also open doors, especially for roles that have not yet been widely advertised. Applying early matters too, because NBFC roles at this scale tend to close quickly once the right shortlist is built.
