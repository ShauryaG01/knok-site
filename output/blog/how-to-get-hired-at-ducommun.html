Ducommun is a US-based aerospace and defence manufacturer with operations spanning structural assemblies, electronic systems, and specialty components for aircraft and defence programmes. 03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Application and resume screening
Ducommun uses an applicant tracking system to filter resumes. Candidates who match the core qualifications (relevant degree, industry keywords, years of experience) move to a recruiter call.

Recruiter phone screen
A talent acquisition partner typically reaches out within one to two weeks of applying. This call covers your background, why you are interested in aerospace or defence manufacturing, salary expectations, and basic logistics such as location and work authorisation. Be direct and specific here.

Hiring manager interview
If the recruiter screen goes well, you meet the hiring manager, usually over video or phone. Expect a mix of technical questions about your domain (engineering, quality, supply chain, etc.) and situational questions about how you have handled production issues, tight deadlines, or cross-functional coordination.

Technical or panel round
For engineering and quality roles, a panel interview with two to four team members often follows. Questions dig into your hands-on experience: how you have read or created engineering drawings, how you have managed non-conformances, and how you have worked within a regulated manufacturing environment.

Offer and background check
If rounds go well, HR extends a verbal offer before sending a written letter. A formal background check and, for some roles, a drug screen follow. Security-clearance roles add an additional government-side verification step that can extend the overall timeline.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Domain knowledge in aerospace or defence manufacturing
Ducommun's work sits inside tightly regulated supply chains. They want people who already understand how a production floor in this sector operates, even at an entry level. Experience with precision machining, composite structures, avionics assembly, or electronic manufacturing services is a strong signal.

Quality mindset
Quality assurance is non-negotiable in aerospace. Candidates who can speak to how they have caught defects early, documented non-conformances, or worked within structured quality management systems stand out. Mentioning methodologies like NADCAP certification processes, first-article inspection, or root-cause analysis shows you speak the language of the industry.

Engineering fundamentals
For technical roles, a degree in mechanical, electrical, manufacturing, or aerospace engineering is the baseline. Beyond the degree, practical experience with engineering drawings, GD&T (geometric dimensioning and tolerancing), and manufacturing process documentation matters a great deal.

Programme and project coordination
Ducommun manages long-cycle defence contracts. Programme managers and coordinators who have worked on milestone-driven, multi-year projects and understand earned value or integrated programme schedules are valued.

Soft skills that matter here
Clear written and spoken communication, attention to detail, and the ability to work across engineering, operations, and customer-facing teams are consistently important. Comfort with structured processes and cross-departmental sign-offs is essential in an environment like this one.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Tailor your resume to aerospace and defence language
Ducommun's ATS and hiring managers look for terms specific to the sector. Use words like 'precision manufacturing,' 'regulatory compliance,' 'quality management systems,' 'defence contracts,' and 'engineering change orders' where they honestly apply to your experience. Avoid generic phrases that could apply to any manufacturing company.

Research Ducommun's product lines before every interview
Ducommun makes structural assemblies for commercial aircraft, electronic systems for defence platforms, and specialised components across several programmes. Knowing which segment the role falls under, and being able to name a product line or customer programme, signals genuine interest and preparation.

Prepare STAR-format answers for quality and process questions
Interviewers will ask how you have handled specific situations. Structure your answers using Situation, Task, Action, Result. Relevant scenarios include catching a production defect before it reached the customer, improving a process step, or resolving a supplier issue under deadline pressure.

Brush up on GD&T and drawing interpretation
Even non-engineer roles at Ducommun often require basic drawing literacy. If your skills here are rusty, practise reading engineering drawings and understanding common tolerancing symbols before your technical round.

Ask smart questions in the interview
Questions like 'What does success look like in the first six months for this role?' or 'How does this team interact with the programme management office?' show strategic thinking. Avoid asking about salary in early rounds unless the interviewer raises it first.

Follow up promptly after each round
Send a brief thank-you note within a day of each interview. In a structured hiring environment like Ducommun's, small professionalism signals matter and interviewers do compare notes across rounds.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Ducommun positions itself around precision, safety, and long-term customer relationships in aerospace and defence. The culture rewards people who are methodical and thorough over people who move fast and break things. If you thrive in structured environments where documentation and process discipline are expected, you will fit in well.

Team and hierarchy
Ducommun is a mid-sized publicly traded company, so there is real structure and hierarchy. Decision-making tends to involve multiple stakeholders, and changes move through formal approval processes. Newer employees often describe the environment as stable and professional rather than fast-paced or experimental.

Commitment to compliance
Because their customers include major defence contractors and commercial aviation OEMs, Ducommun employees are expected to take compliance seriously at every level. This is not just a legal obligation; it shapes daily work rhythms and how teams communicate about quality issues.

Career development
Ducommun has enough breadth across functions and sites that internal moves are possible for high performers. People who are proactive about expressing interest in growth tend to find more opportunities than those who wait to be noticed.

Diversity and inclusion
Ducommun publicly reports a commitment to building a workforce that reflects diverse backgrounds. For Indian professionals, your background is unlikely to be a barrier, though US-based roles may still require work authorisation discussions with the recruiter.
