01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Bolna is a Bengaluru-based AI startup building voice AI agents that help businesses automate phone calls and customer conversations. Think of it as the layer that turns a plain phone line into a smart, human-sounding AI that can handle sales calls, customer support, or appointment booking without a human picking up.

As of mid-2026, Bolna has 11 open roles across engineering, product, and go-to-market functions, which signals that the team is actively expanding. For job seekers in India who want to get in early at a fast-moving AI company, this is a meaningful window. The roles span both technical and non-technical functions, so the hiring net is wider than you might expect for a startup at this stage.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Bolna is a startup, so the process is typically leaner and faster than at a large IT services firm. Based on what candidates at similar early-stage AI startups commonly report, here is what the process usually looks like, stage by stage.

Application and resume screen: Your resume is reviewed by the founding or hiring team directly, not a large HR department. Tailoring your resume to show any AI, voice tech, or developer tooling experience helps you stand out at this stage.

Introductory call: A short video or phone call with a founder or a senior team member. They typically want to understand why you are interested in voice AI, what you have built before, and whether your values align with a small, fast-moving team.

Technical or skills round: For engineering roles, this usually involves a take-home task or a live coding session focused on Python, API integrations, or LLM-related work. For non-technical roles like sales or growth, this round often involves a case study or a mock scenario. Expect the problem to be directly related to something Bolna actually works on, not a generic puzzle.

Founder or culture interview: At a startup of this size, it is common to meet at least one founder before an offer is made. This round is less about testing hard skills and more about checking whether you can operate independently, communicate clearly, and thrive with minimal hand-holding.

Offer: If the interviews go well, the offer stage at a startup like Bolna tends to move quickly. Compensation at early-stage AI startups in India, as commonly cited in industry surveys, is typically a mix of a fixed salary and employee stock options (ESOPs), so clarify both components when you receive an offer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Bolna is building in a technical, fast-changing space, so they tend to value a specific combination of skills and traits.

For engineering roles: Strong Python skills are close to a must-have, given that most voice AI and LLM tooling is Python-first. Experience with REST APIs, WebSockets, or real-time audio streaming is a meaningful bonus. Familiarity with large language models (OpenAI, Gemini, or open-source equivalents) and how to prompt or fine-tune them will make your profile more competitive. If you have worked on telephony integrations (Twilio, Plivo, Exotel) even as a side project, mention it clearly on your resume.

For product and growth roles: Bolna needs people who can translate a customer's messy business problem into a clean product requirement or a convincing pitch. Clear written and verbal communication, comfort with ambiguity, and the ability to work without a large team around you matter more here than years of experience at a big company.

Across all roles: Startups at this stage look for people who can take ownership of a task end-to-end rather than waiting for instructions. A history of building things on your own (open-source projects, side businesses, freelance work) signals exactly this kind of self-direction.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparing for a Bolna interview is less about memorizing answers and more about showing genuine depth in AI and voice technology.

Start by actually using Bolna's product. Sign up, build a simple voice agent, and understand where it fits in the market. Being able to say 'I tried your product and noticed X' in an interview immediately separates you from candidates who only read the website.

Read up on how large language models are used in real-time voice pipelines. Concepts like latency optimization, turn-taking in conversations, and text-to-speech quality are directly relevant to what Bolna builds. You do not need to be an expert, but showing curiosity and basic fluency goes a long way.

For technical candidates, practice problems around API design, async Python, and working with audio data. Look at open-source voice AI frameworks to understand how the pieces fit together before your coding round.

For non-technical candidates, prepare a short story about a time you figured out what a customer really needed, not just what they asked for. Bolna's customers are businesses trying to automate calls, and the team needs people who can empathize with that use case and translate it into action.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Bolna operates with the urgency and directness typical of an early-stage AI startup. The team is small, which means your work will be visible and your impact will be measurable from day one. This can be energizing if you like ownership, or uncomfortable if you are used to clearly defined roles and slow decision cycles.

The company is building in the voice AI space, which is genuinely new territory. That means the team values curiosity and a willingness to experiment over following a fixed playbook. Mistakes are expected. Learning from them quickly is what matters.

Communication tends to be direct and async-friendly, as is common in product-led startups. If you prefer frequent check-ins or a structured review process, it is worth asking about team norms during your interview so you can calibrate expectations on both sides before you join.

If you are applying from a city outside Bengaluru, confirm the remote or hybrid policy for the specific role during your first conversation, as startup norms vary by team and function.
