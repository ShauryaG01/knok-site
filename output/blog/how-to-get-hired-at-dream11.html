01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Technical depth, not just breadth. For engineering and product roles, Dream11 wants people who can go deep on a problem. Surface-level answers do not impress interviewers here. If you have worked on high-scale systems or data-heavy products, be ready to discuss the tradeoffs you made.

Sports and product intuition. Dream11's business is built on the passion Indian fans have for sports. Knowing how fantasy sports works, what makes users come back, and how to think about engagement metrics is a genuine advantage. Even if you are applying for a backend or data role, showing that you understand the product gives you an edge.

Ownership mindset. Interviewers commonly ask about situations where you took initiative beyond your job description. They want people who treat the company's goals as their own, not people who wait to be told what to do.

Communication and collaboration. Dream11 works in cross-functional teams. Being able to explain your thinking clearly, whether to a fellow engineer or to a product manager, matters as much as the answer itself.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with the product. Download the Dream11 app if you have not already. Play a few contests, notice the UX decisions, and think about what you would build or fix. Being able to talk about the product naturally in an interview signals genuine interest.

Brush up on system design. For engineering roles, system design is almost always part of the process. Review concepts like caching, load balancing, database sharding, and real-time data pipelines. Dream11 operates at very large scale, so questions often involve handling a high volume of concurrent users.

Practise structured storytelling. Behavioural questions follow the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Prepare three to four strong examples from your work history that show ownership, problem-solving under pressure, and cross-team collaboration.

Know your resume cold. Interviewers at Dream11 often dig deep into whatever you have listed. If you have mentioned a project, be ready to explain every technical decision, every alternative you considered, and every result you achieved.

Research the team and role. Check LinkedIn for people who work in the team you are applying to. Understanding the team's current focus areas can help you frame your answers in a way that resonates with the interviewers.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Dream11 has a startup-inside-a-unicorn feel. The company grew rapidly on the back of India's cricket craze, and that energy still shapes how teams operate. Decision-making is fast, hierarchies are relatively flat, and people are expected to take ownership without being asked.

The culture rewards people who are curious about the product and the user. Unlike a services company where you build what a client tells you, Dream11 teams are expected to think about why something should be built and whether it will actually move the needle for users.

Work intensity is high, especially during major cricket tournaments like the IPL. Publicly reported employee reviews mention that the pace can be demanding during peak seasons, but that the work itself is engaging because the product has such a large and passionate user base.

Dream11 has invested in employee growth through internal mobility and learning budgets. Team culture varies by department, but across the board, people who are proactive and data-driven tend to do well here.
