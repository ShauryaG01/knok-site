01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Clear (formerly ClearTax) is one of India's most recognised fintech companies, known for its tax filing platform, GST software, and financial products for individuals and businesses. As of July 2026, Clear has 188 open roles, meaning the company is actively hiring across engineering, product, design, sales, finance, and operations.

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Clear serves millions of individual taxpayers and lakhs of businesses and chartered accountants across India. That scale means the company needs people who can build and run products reliably for a very large, diverse user base.

Hiring runs consistently throughout the year, with engineering and product roles among the most active at any given time. Most positions are hybrid and Bengaluru-based, though select roles allow remote work. If you want to work at a product-driven fintech where your work touches a problem almost every earning Indian deals with, taxes and financial compliance, Clear is a serious option worth targeting.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Clear's hiring process typically spans four to five stages. The exact steps vary by role and team, but here is what candidates commonly experience.

Application and resume screening: Your resume is reviewed by the talent team. Tailoring it to match the job description helps, especially for competitive roles. Relevant experience in fintech or SaaS gives your application a head start.

Recruiter call: A short introductory call to confirm your interest, notice period, current and expected compensation, and fit for the role. Be clear and specific on these details since they shape the rest of the process.

Technical or domain round: For engineers, this usually covers coding problems and, for senior roles, system design. Product managers can expect case studies, prioritisation questions, and metrics discussions. Finance and compliance candidates typically face domain questions on GST, TDS, or financial reporting.

Manager or panel interview: A deeper conversation with the hiring manager or a small panel, focused on how you approach problems, your past work, and your ability to collaborate across functions. Senior candidates may be asked to walk through a past project in detail or present a structured case study.

Culture and leadership round: Some roles include a final round with a senior leader. This is less about technical skills and more about how you handle ambiguity, what motivates you, and whether your goals align with where Clear is headed.

Offer and verification: Offers typically come within a couple of weeks of the final round. A standard background verification follows before your joining date is confirmed.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Clear looks for people who combine solid domain knowledge with a genuine willingness to take ownership. A few qualities that consistently matter across roles:

For engineers: Strong computer science fundamentals matter more than any specific language. System design skills become critical at mid and senior levels. Experience with high-scale systems or fintech products is a real advantage, though not always a hard requirement.

For product managers: Clear wants PMs who can define problems precisely, work closely with data, and drive alignment across engineering, design, and business teams. Prior experience in B2B SaaS or fintech is valued. Equally important is the ability to communicate trade-offs clearly and make decisions with incomplete information.

For sales and customer success: Clear's customers include Indian SMEs, startups, and chartered accountants. People who understand the real compliance pain points of these customers and engage consultatively rather than just pitching do well here.

Across all roles: Clear values intellectual honesty. Candidates who are upfront about what they do not know, ask sharp questions, and show genuine curiosity about the product consistently leave a stronger impression than those who only give polished rehearsed answers.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your interview. Spend time on Clear's tax filing app, the GST software, or the business compliance tools. Interviewers notice immediately when a candidate has real hands-on familiarity. For product and business roles, this is close to essential.

Learn the basics of Indian tax and compliance. Even for engineering roles, knowing the difference between ITR filing and GST returns, or understanding what TDS means in practice, helps you frame answers in context. For product, sales, and finance roles, this knowledge is a baseline expectation.

Prepare structured stories from your past work. Clear interviewers often ask about situations where you owned a problem end to end, navigated conflict across teams, or made a call with limited data. Prepare a handful of strong examples: the situation, your specific actions, and the outcome.

For engineers, practise coding and system design. Focus on data structures, algorithms, and designing scalable backend services. Problems related to financial data processing, batch jobs, or notification systems at scale are particularly relevant.

Research where Clear is growing. The company has expanded into lending, wealth management, and business payments beyond its original tax focus. Showing awareness of this growth in your interview signals you have taken the role seriously and thought about how you can contribute beyond your immediate job description.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Clear is a mission-driven company with a focus on making financial life simpler for every Indian. Internally, the environment is fast-paced and product-led. Teams are given significant ownership over their areas, and the expectation is that you will take initiative, raise issues early, and find solutions without waiting to be directed.

Communication at Clear tends to be direct. Feedback is shared openly, and people are expected to speak up with ideas or concerns rather than staying quiet. If you are used to a more hierarchical or approval-heavy workplace, this takes some adjustment.

Work intensity picks up during peak tax season, broadly January through July. Teams close to the core product often operate under real pressure during these months and experience a calmer pace afterward. This cycle is worth knowing before you join.

Many employees mention that working on problems that affect a large number of real Indians, whether helping someone file their taxes correctly or keeping a small business GST-compliant, gives the work a sense of purpose that sustains engagement over time. Clear also invests in internal learning, with knowledge-sharing sessions and opportunities to work across different problem areas as the company grows.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Clear hire freshers?

Yes, Clear does hire freshers, mainly for engineering and operations roles. Campus hiring happens through select institutions, but off-campus applications are reviewed as well. A strong coding profile, a relevant internship in fintech or SaaS, or open source contributions all help a fresher stand out. Getting a referral from someone inside the company alongside a direct application significantly improves your odds.

How long does the Clear hiring process usually take?

For most candidates, the process from the first recruiter call to receiving an offer takes somewhere between two and four weeks. Roles that involve more senior stakeholders or additional panel rounds can stretch a little longer. Sending a polite follow-up to your recruiter after each stage is acceptable and helps keep you visible in the pipeline.

Is Clear a hybrid or remote workplace?

Clear follows a hybrid model for most roles, with teams expected to come in on certain days of the week. Fully remote options exist for select technical or leadership positions, but they are not the default. The arrangement for a specific role is usually mentioned in the job listing, and confirming it during the recruiter call saves you from surprises after you receive an offer.

What salary can I expect at Clear?

Publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi vary quite a bit by role, seniority, and team. Research those platforms for your specific position, arrive with a clear ask based on your current compensation and market data, and be ready to discuss it openly during the recruiter screening call. Clear generally benchmarks against other Bengaluru-based fintech and SaaS companies.

Do I need prior fintech experience to get hired at Clear?

Not always. For engineering roles, strong fundamentals and the ability to learn quickly matter more than a fintech background. For product, sales, and finance roles, domain knowledge around taxes, GST, or SME finance becomes significantly more important. If you are coming from outside fintech, showing that you have done your homework on the product and the industry goes a long way in interviews.

What is the best way to apply for a role at Clear?

Applying directly through Clear's careers page gives you the most direct path, and LinkedIn applications are reviewed as well. A referral from a current Clear employee can significantly improve your chances of getting noticed, especially for competitive roles. Reaching out to someone in your network who works at Clear, or connecting with them on LinkedIn before applying, is worth trying alongside your direct application.
