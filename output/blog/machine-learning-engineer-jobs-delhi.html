01 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Delhi NCR has 50 live Machine Learning Engineer openings as of July 2026, based on knok jobradar data. That puts it firmly as the second-largest ML hiring market in India right now, behind Bangalore (165 openings), and well ahead of Hyderabad (27), Mumbai (15), Pune (14), and Chennai (14).

The hiring here is spread across Gurugram and Noida, where global companies have built large India engineering centres. You will find a healthy mix of MNC product companies, analytics-driven BPO firms, SaaS players, and telecom tech outfits. Roles span mid-level and senior positions, with a smaller count of junior openings.

If pure volume is your priority and you are open to relocating, Bangalore has more than three times the Delhi NCR count. But for professionals who want to stay in the NCR belt, 50 active roles is a solid pool, and competition per role is lower than in Bangalore.

04 Salary Range

Salary Range

Our current data snapshot does not include salary figures for this specific search. For broader context, based on publicly reported compensation data on Glassdoor and industry surveys, ML Engineers in Delhi NCR fall into the following bands.

Engineers with 1-3 years of experience are commonly cited at 8-18 LPA at services companies and 12-25 LPA at product companies. Mid-level professionals with 4-7 years are commonly cited at 20-40 LPA. Senior roles at product companies like Adobe are publicly reported at higher bands, with levels.fyi showing top-of-band packages commonly cited well above 40 LPA for experienced engineers.

Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs, EXL Service, and Bupa are enterprise or services-side employers where pay structures may differ from pure-product companies. Check Glassdoor reviews for each company before your interview to calibrate your expectations. Always negotiate: the first offer is rarely the best offer.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

The Delhi NCR ML market leans toward applied ML and production-ready engineering, not pure research. Here is what hiring managers in this market consistently look for.

Build depth in deployment and MLOps. Most roles on the Delhi NCR list involve taking models to production. Skills in model serving, monitoring, feature stores, and tools like MLflow, Kubeflow, or SageMaker will differentiate you from candidates who only know model training.

Show LLM and GenAI work. From 2024 onwards, most ML job descriptions have added GenAI requirements. A public project using LLMs, RAG pipelines, or fine-tuning gets noticed. Put it on GitHub with a clear README so recruiters can verify it quickly.

Tailor your resume to the company type. Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs is telecom-focused, so if you have worked on network data, customer analytics, or time-series problems, lead with that. Adobe and Level AI are product companies where your impact on metrics and product outcomes matters more.

A strong LinkedIn presence matters in Delhi NCR. Recruiters in the NCR market are active on LinkedIn. A specific headline, regular project posts, and skills endorsements all help you surface in searches before a recruiter even sees your resume.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start by mapping the 50 openings to the companies you genuinely want to work for. A targeted application to Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs, Level AI, or Adobe Systems with a tailored resume will outperform sending the same CV to all 50 roles.

Prioritise the high-count employer first. Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs has 18 open roles. Read each job description for differences in seniority, tech stack, or team focus. Apply to the two or three that match your background most closely, not all 18.

Use referrals where you can. Delhi NCR has a large ML professional community. A LinkedIn first-degree connection at any of these companies can refer you internally, which typically fast-tracks your CV past the initial screen.

Follow up after 7-10 days. A short, polite message to the recruiter on LinkedIn confirming you have applied and are keen is acceptable and often appreciated. Keep it to two sentences.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Delhi NCR a good market for Machine Learning Engineers compared to Bangalore?

Delhi NCR has 50 active ML Engineer openings, making it the second-largest ML market in India after Bangalore (165 openings). Competition per role is lower in Delhi NCR, and salaries at senior levels are broadly comparable based on publicly reported data. If you prefer not to relocate, Delhi NCR offers a strong set of employers across product companies, enterprise tech, and analytics firms.

Which companies are hiring the most ML Engineers in Delhi NCR right now?

Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs leads with 18 open roles as of July 2026. Level AI, Lateral - APAC, and EXL Service each have 3 openings, while Anaplan, Bupa, and Adobe Systems have 2 each. This data is from knok jobradar and reflects live openings, so counts will shift as roles are filled and new ones are posted.

What skills do ML Engineer roles in Delhi NCR ask for most?

Based on current job descriptions in this market, the most commonly listed skills are Python, TensorFlow or PyTorch, MLOps tools like MLflow or Kubeflow, SQL, and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure). From 2024 onwards, LLM experience and GenAI project work have become a significant differentiator. Roles at product companies like Adobe and Level AI also emphasise A/B testing and experimentation skills.

What salary can I expect as an ML Engineer in Delhi NCR?

Our current data does not include salary figures for this snapshot. Based on Glassdoor and publicly reported data, entry-level ML Engineers with 1-3 years of experience are commonly cited at 8-18 LPA, while mid-level professionals with 4-7 years are commonly cited at 20-40 LPA. Product companies generally pay more than services companies at the same experience level, so benchmark by company type before your salary discussion.

Do I need telecom domain experience to apply at Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs?

Not necessarily. Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs runs product and engineering teams where many ML roles focus on model deployment, data pipelines, and applied ML rather than deep telecom domain knowledge. Read each job description carefully before applying. If your background is in customer analytics, time-series data, or applied ML in any industry, the role is worth pursuing.

How long does the interview process take for ML roles in Delhi NCR?

It varies by company. Startups like Level AI and Wingify often move in 2-4 weeks from application to offer. Larger enterprises such as Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs and EXL Service may take 4-8 weeks due to multiple rounds including a technical screen, an ML design round, and a hiring manager discussion. Applying early after a role goes live typically speeds things up, since hiring teams are most active when a position is freshly posted.
