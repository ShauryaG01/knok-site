01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Data Engineer compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Pune has 23 Data Engineer openings as of 2026-07-08, according to knok jobradar. That puts it level with Hyderabad (also 23 openings) and ahead of Mumbai (8) and Chennai (14), though it sits well behind Bangalore (92) and Delhi (66) in overall volume.

What makes Pune stand out is the variety of companies hiring. You have large IT services firms, automotive and telecom GCCs (Global Capability Centres), cloud-native product companies, and early-stage startups all active in the same pool. That mix means the interview style, tech stack expectations, and growth path can differ significantly from one opening to the next. Knowing who is hiring, and why, gives you a real edge here.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

With 23 openings across a varied set of employers, there is no single profile that fits the whole Pune market. Tailor your preparation based on where you are applying.

For IT services roles (Zensar, Cornerstone, UnionSys Technologies): Emphasise the number and variety of pipelines you have built, the clients or industries you have served, and your ability to work across different tech stacks. Soft skills, communication, and delivery track record matter here. If you have worked on AWS Glue, Azure Data Factory, or similar managed services, call that out clearly.

For GCC roles (BMW TechWorks India, Vodafone, Mastercard): Shift focus to scale, reliability, and governance. How large were the data volumes you handled? Have you worked on data quality frameworks or compliance pipelines? For BMW TechWorks in particular, any exposure to manufacturing, supply chain, or connected-vehicle data is worth highlighting even if it was a side project.

For product roles (Snowflake, Helpshift): Depth matters more than breadth. Data modelling, dbt or Airflow experience, and the ability to work closely with analysts and PMs will set you apart. A GitHub repo with a well-documented pipeline project, including a write-up of the design choices you made, carries more weight than most certifications at this level.

Across all types, Python and SQL are the baseline. Spark and at least one major cloud platform are close to baseline now as well. The actual differentiator in a focused market like Pune's is showing you have researched the company before the first call.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Pune's 23 openings across 10 companies is a manageable list. You can realistically research every employer in a day, which is worth doing before you apply to any of them.

Start with the multi-opening companies. Zensar (7 roles), Cornerstone (3 roles), and BMW TechWorks India (3 roles) together account for the majority of the 23 openings. Multiple roles means multiple teams, so even if one team does not shortlist you, another might. Apply to these first.

Then target single-opening companies that match your background. If you have fintech or payments experience, Mastercard is your best fit. If you have worked with customer-support or product-analytics data, Helpshift is a strong match. Cloud-native background? Snowflake is the obvious pick, though it is also the most competitive.

Customise your resume for each company type. A single generic resume will not perform equally well across a mix this varied. Keep a services version and a product/GCC version, and adjust the summary and bullet points based on which you are sending.

Do not overlook the startups. Buystars and NextJobHunt each have 1 opening. If your experience is lighter, these are often easier to get into, and the scope is broad enough to build a strong portfolio quickly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company in Pune is hiring the most Data Engineers right now?

Zensar leads with 7 openings, the highest count of any company in the Pune Data Engineer market as of 2026-07-08. Cornerstone and BMW TechWorks India each have 3 openings, and the remaining companies each have 1. If you want to maximise your chances of getting shortlisted in Pune specifically, starting with Zensar makes sense given the volume of roles available across likely multiple internal teams.

Is Pune a good city for Data Engineer jobs compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has 92 openings versus Pune's 23 in the same dataset, so raw volume strongly favours Bangalore. That said, Pune's roles cover a healthy mix of GCCs, IT services, and product companies, and the city's cost of living is generally lower. If you prefer Pune for personal or lifestyle reasons, the market is real enough to build a strong career here, especially at mid to senior level where GCCs are actively hiring.

What is the difference between a GCC role and an IT services role for a Data Engineer?

In a GCC like BMW TechWorks India or Mastercard, you work on the parent company's own internal data infrastructure. Projects tend to be longer-term, data volumes are larger, and there is more emphasis on governance and reliability. In an IT services firm like Zensar, you work on projects for multiple external clients, which means more variety but also more context-switching. GCCs generally pay toward the higher end of the salary band and offer more stability, while services firms are often easier to enter at the junior level.

Do I need a Snowflake certification to apply to Snowflake the company?

Not strictly, but it helps signal platform familiarity and seriousness. Snowflake the company looks for strong fundamentals in data modelling, pipeline architecture, and cloud-native tooling. Interviewers probe deeply on system design and how you reason through data problems, so pair any certification with real project experience you can walk through in detail. A GitHub portfolio showing actual pipeline work is often more convincing than certifications alone.

What salary can a fresher expect from Zensar in Pune?

Based on knok jobradar data, entry-level Data Engineer roles fall in the 6-12 LPA range, and IT services firms like Zensar typically sit closer to the midpoint of that band. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported ranges for Zensar that you can reference when preparing for salary discussions. Exact offers depend on your college, prior internships, and how you perform in the technical rounds.

How competitive are single-opening roles at companies like Mastercard or Snowflake?

Single-opening roles at high-visibility companies tend to attract a large number of applicants relative to the one available position. Mastercard and Snowflake in particular have strong brand pull in the market, so expect more interview rounds and a higher technical bar than you would see at a services firm. The best way to stand out is researching the company's known data stack in advance and tailoring your resume accordingly, rather than sending a generic application and trying to customise later.
