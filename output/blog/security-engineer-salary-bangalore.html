01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

The first thing to know about Security Engineer salaries in Bangalore is that most companies do not tell you what they will pay. Of the 69 Security Engineer openings tracked in Bangalore on knok jobradar as of July 2026, not one published a salary band. This is the norm across Indian job boards, not an exception. Employers keep numbers hidden so they can anchor your offer to your current CTC rather than the market rate. Your best defence is to build your own picture using Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys before any HR screening call.

Security is one of the strongest hiring categories in Indian tech right now. Demand from fintech, SaaS, banking, and large MNCs for skilled Security Engineers is rising, particularly for cloud security, AppSec, and red team roles. Bangalore, with its density of product companies and MNC security teams, sits at the centre of that demand. Because the listings themselves gave us nothing to work with on pay, every salary figure in this guide comes from publicly reported sources and you should treat them as market benchmarks, not guarantees.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because none of the 69 Bangalore listings in this snapshot published salary information, every figure below comes from publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, cross-checked against industry surveys. Your actual number will depend on your specialisation, company type (MNC vs. product startup vs. IT services), and how well you negotiate.

Fresher to 2 years (SOC Analyst, Junior Pentester, Junior Security Engineer): Entry-level roles are commonly cited at 5-10 LPA on Glassdoor for Bangalore. Freshers joining top-tier product companies through campus placements may see higher offers, but those are not typical for lateral or off-campus hiring.

3-5 years (Mid-level Security Engineer): Specialisation starts to pay off here. Engineers with hands-on VAPT, cloud security, SIEM, or incident response skills are publicly reported at 12-25 LPA in industry surveys for Bangalore. Certifications like OSCP, CEH, or AWS Security Specialty push you toward the upper half of that range.

6-10 years (Senior Security Engineer, Security Architect): Senior roles are commonly cited at 25-45 LPA on levels.fyi and Glassdoor. Product companies and funded fintechs in Bangalore sit at the higher end. Red team leads and cloud security architects with strong public cloud experience attract the strongest bids.

10-plus years (Principal, Security Lead, CISO-track): Principal engineers and security architects are publicly reported above 45 LPA in industry surveys, with total compensation climbing further once ESOPs at growth-stage startups or retention bonuses at larger firms are included.

Listings won't tell you what a role pays, but knok will tell you which ones you're competitive for.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Negotiating a security offer in India requires preparation, because you are almost always going in without knowing what the company budgeted for the role.

Research before the first call. Pull salary data from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and recent industry surveys from bodies like ISACA or (ISC)2. Know the band for your specialisation before HR asks 'what are your expectations?' because once you name a number, you own it.

Do not anchor to your current salary. Many companies ask for your current CTC and apply a standard increment on top, as commonly cited in industry hiring discussions. That formula can lock you below market if you have been underpaid. You are allowed to say your expectation is based on market research, not your current package.

Certifications give you real leverage. If you hold OSCP, CISSP, or a cloud security certification, name it explicitly in the negotiation. Security talent with verified skills is scarce, and recruiters know it. Use that scarcity as your advantage.

Negotiate total comp, not just base. Variable pay, ESOPs, joining bonuses, certification reimbursement, and remote flexibility all have real value. If base has a hard ceiling due to internal pay bands, push on these components instead.

Take your time. Asking for two days to review an offer is reasonable and normal. Use that window to compare against other active conversations or to get a competing offer in writing if you have one. A competing offer is the strongest card you can play.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

A salary number in an Indian offer letter is rarely the full picture. Security Engineers in Bangalore should examine every component before deciding whether an offer is competitive.

Fixed base salary. This is what income tax is calculated on and what future employers will ask about as your 'current CTC.' Push to maximise this because it compounds into every future offer and increment.

Variable or performance bonus. Paid annually or quarterly, this can be a modest or substantial top-up depending on the company. Always ask what percentage of employees actually receive the target variable. 'Up to X LPA' is not the same as 'guaranteed X LPA.'

ESOPs and RSUs. Funded startups and listed tech companies in Bangalore often include equity as a meaningful part of total compensation. Levels.fyi tracks RSU grant sizes at large MNCs. For early-stage startups, factor in liquidity risk since the value depends on an eventual exit event.

Joining bonus. Often used when a company cannot raise base due to internal pay bands. It is non-recurring, fully taxable, and sometimes comes with a clawback clause if you leave within the first year. Weight it accordingly.

Benefits that reduce real costs. Health insurance, certification reimbursement (particularly valuable in security, where OSCP, CISSP, or cloud certifications cost real money), internet allowances for remote roles, and meal or transport allowances all cut your effective monthly outgoings.

WFH flexibility. Not a cash component, but Glassdoor reviews and industry surveys consistently show that fully remote or hybrid roles attract candidates even at slightly lower base pay, because the commute time and cost savings are real and recurring.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why don't most Security Engineer job listings in India show a salary?

Indian employers are not legally required to disclose salary in a job posting, unlike some countries that now mandate pay transparency. Companies prefer to keep numbers hidden so they can calibrate each offer to the candidate's current CTC rather than committing to a market-rate band. This gives them room to offer less to candidates who are already underpaid. Your defence is to research Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys before the first call so you walk in with a specific number in mind rather than waiting to hear theirs first.

What salary should a Security Engineer with 4 years of experience expect in Bangalore?

Four years of hands-on experience places you in the mid-level band. Based on publicly reported data on Glassdoor and industry surveys, this range broadly sits around 12-25 LPA in Bangalore for engineers working in VAPT, cloud security, or SOC operations. Candidates with certifications like OSCP or cloud security credentials tend to land toward the upper half. Your exact number will depend on your specialisation, the company type, and how firmly you negotiate on market data rather than your current package.

Which security specialisation pays the most in Bangalore?

Cloud security (AWS and Azure focus), application security (AppSec), and offensive security or red teaming are consistently the highest-paying areas, based on publicly reported data on levels.fyi and Glassdoor. Fintech and large SaaS companies in Bangalore compete hardest for these profiles. GRC and compliance roles are easier to find in volume but typically sit lower in the pay range. If you are early in your career, building cloud or AppSec skills pays off disproportionately over time compared to staying in security operations.

Do certifications like OSCP, CISSP, or CEH actually improve your salary offer?

Yes, in security more so than in many other engineering disciplines, because verified offensive or governance skills are genuinely scarce. OSCP is a strong signal for pentesting and red team roles, and publicly reported community data on Glassdoor shows a meaningful premium for OSCP holders at mid to senior levels. CISSP carries weight for leadership and governance-track roles. CEH is widely recognised but considered less technically rigorous than OSCP by hiring managers at product companies, so its impact on offers tends to be smaller.

Is Bangalore really much better than other cities for Security Engineer jobs?

The numbers from this snapshot are clear: Bangalore had 69 active Security Engineer listings compared to 12 in Delhi, 12 in Pune, and 10 in Hyderabad in the same period. The concentration of MNCs, product companies, and funded startups in Bangalore creates demand that other cities cannot match in volume. Delhi is a solid option if you prefer government-adjacent or consulting work, and Hyderabad is growing steadily. For sheer volume of security roles, Bangalore leads by a wide margin right now.

How do I find out what a company actually pays before going into a salary discussion?

Start with Glassdoor filtered by company, role, and city. Levels.fyi is particularly useful for product companies and MNCs and covers total compensation including equity. LinkedIn Salary and community platforms like Blind and Reddit threads focused on Indian tech careers also carry publicly reported ranges from current and former employees. If you have a contact inside the company or in the same industry, a direct conversation is still the most accurate source. Going in with a specific number rooted in research puts you in a far stronger position than waiting to hear their offer and reacting to it.
