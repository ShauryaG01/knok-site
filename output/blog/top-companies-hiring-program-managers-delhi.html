01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Technical Program Manager compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

08 Salary Range

Salary Range

The knok jobradar snapshot for this role and city did not include verified salary figures for these specific openings, so treat any benchmark here as indicative only. Glassdoor and levels.fyi commonly cite total compensation for mid-senior TPMs at product companies and global firms in Delhi NCR in the 25-45 LPA range. 09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Delhi NCR's TPM openings span very different employer types, so what makes you stand out depends on where you apply.

For global product companies (MongoDB, Anaplan). These employers run structured hiring with system design and programme management rounds. Emphasise cross-functional influence, experience working with distributed or global engineering teams, and familiarity with planning frameworks such as OKRs, SAFe, or roadmapping tools. A track record of driving programmes across time zones is a genuine differentiator here.

For Indian fintech companies (Paytm, cashfree). Speed and execution credibility matter most. Highlight programmes you shipped end-to-end, how you handled regulatory timelines or compliance constraints (common in payments), and how you kept engineering, product, and risk aligned under pressure. Quantified outcomes carry more weight than certifications.

For identity-tech startups (Sign3, IDfy). These companies want TPMs who can work with ambiguity and set up processes from scratch, not just follow existing ones. Experience in KYC, fraud, or identity infrastructure is a bonus but rarely a hard requirement.

For IT-services and cloud firms (SOFTPATH, Movate, Cloudsufi). Client-facing delivery experience, stakeholder management with enterprise customers, and certifications like PMP or PRINCE2 carry more signal here than at product companies.

Across all types: clear written communication, a resume that maps your experience to programme scope (team size, complexity, delivery outcomes), and the ability to explain your operating model as a TPM will set you apart from candidates with similar years of experience.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start by targeting the two companies with multiple openings. Paytm and MongoDB each have two active roles, which means more interview slots and a higher chance that at least one position matches your seniority and domain. If your background is genuinely relevant to both roles at a company, apply to each separately.

Research the employer before applying. A short, specific message that references cashfree's payments platform or IDfy's identity verification work signals preparation and saves everyone time. Generic applications for senior roles like TPM are easy for recruiters to move past.

For startups and mid-sized companies on this list, reach out on LinkedIn directly to the engineering lead or hiring manager, not just the recruiter. A brief note on why you are a strong fit for their specific programme challenges often gets a faster response than an ATS submission alone.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Delhi NCR a good market for TPM roles compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore is the largest TPM market by volume, with 41 openings versus Delhi NCR's 14 in this snapshot. However, Delhi NCR offers a meaningful number of roles with a diverse employer mix across product companies, global firms, and funded startups. If you are based in the Gurugram-Noida corridor, the local market is active enough that relocation is not a necessary step to find quality opportunities.

Which companies in this list are best for long-term career growth as a TPM?

MongoDB and Anaplan offer structured TPM career ladders with exposure to global engineering teams, which is strong for long-term progression. Paytm offers scale and deep product exposure in a complex fintech environment. Startups like Sign3 and IDfy can accelerate growth faster if the company scales, but come with higher uncertainty. The right choice depends on whether you prioritise structured progression, learning speed, or equity upside.

Do I need a fintech or identity-tech background to apply to most of these companies?

Most TPM roles prioritise programme management fundamentals over deep domain knowledge. Fintech companies like Paytm and cashfree appreciate regulatory awareness or payments experience, but it is rarely a hard filter. Startups like Sign3 and IDfy care more about your ability to operate with ambiguity than your industry background. Global companies like MongoDB primarily hire for engineering programme management skills, regardless of which sector you come from.

Are the 14 Delhi NCR openings mostly senior roles or do they include mid-level TPM positions?

The knok jobradar data does not break down seniority levels within the 14 openings. In practice, the TPM title spans a wide range across companies. Check each job description individually, since some companies use 'TPM' for mid-level programme roles while others reserve it for senior leaders managing multiple engineering teams or cross-org programmes.

How should I approach salary negotiation at a startup versus a global company like MongoDB?

At startups like Sign3 or IDfy, the equity component matters as much as the base pay. Ask specifically about the ESOP pool, vesting schedule, and the company's last valuation or funding round before evaluating the offer. For global product companies like MongoDB, levels.fyi publishes data that is commonly cited as a useful starting benchmark for total compensation at these employers. In both cases, knowing your target range before the negotiation puts you in a stronger position.

Should a product company TPM apply to IT-services roles on this list?

You can, but go in with clear expectations on both sides. Product company TPMs often find IT-services roles offer less product ownership and more delivery coordination across client accounts. That said, firms like Cloudsufi and Movate handle technically complex programmes and can offer breadth of exposure and stability. If client delivery and large-scale programme governance genuinely interest you, these roles are worth exploring rather than ruling out by default.
