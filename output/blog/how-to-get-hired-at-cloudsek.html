01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

CloudSEK is a Bangalore-based AI-driven cybersecurity company focused on digital risk protection and threat intelligence. Its core products help organizations monitor their attack surface, detect data leaks, and track adversary activity in real time. It is a product company, not a services firm, which shapes everything about how it hires and what it values in candidates.

As of mid-2026, CloudSEK has 33 open roles spanning threat research, engineering, product, sales, and operations. The company sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and security, so it tends to attract and seek out people who are genuinely curious about both domains. If you are looking at a career in cybersecurity products rather than traditional IT services or consulting, CloudSEK is worth pursuing seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

CloudSEK's hiring process typically moves through four to five stages, though the exact steps vary by role. Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, here is what you can generally expect.

Application and resume screening. Your resume goes through an internal review. Roles in threat intelligence and engineering filter heavily on domain-specific keywords and demonstrated project work, so a generic resume rarely gets through. Tailor your application to highlight relevant skills and projects clearly.

Initial HR or recruiter call. A short screening call to check your background, current situation, notice period, and compensation expectations. This is also where the recruiter explains the role in more detail. Come prepared with your target number and a clear summary of your experience.

Technical or domain assessment. Depending on the role, this could be a take-home assignment, a live coding round, or a case-based threat analysis task. Engineering roles focus on coding ability and security fundamentals. Threat research roles often involve analyzing a sample report or identifying indicators of compromise from a given dataset.

Technical interview(s). One or two rounds with the hiring manager or senior team members. Expect deep questions on your domain, your past project decisions, and how you approach ambiguous security problems. For product roles, expect scenario-based questions around roadmap prioritization and user needs.

Founder or leadership round. CloudSEK is known for involving founders or senior leadership in final-stage interviews, particularly for senior or strategic hires. This round focuses on your long-term ambition, your understanding of the cybersecurity landscape, and whether you will thrive in a high-ownership environment.

Offer and onboarding. Offers typically follow within a week of the final round. With 33 open roles currently listed, the company is actively hiring across multiple teams, so qualified candidates are moving through pipelines regularly.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

CloudSEK looks for people who combine technical depth with genuine curiosity about threats and adversary behavior. A few themes come up repeatedly across publicly reported interview experiences.

Domain passion. CloudSEK is a product company in a specialized field. They want people who follow threat intelligence news, understand attacker motives, and can speak fluently about the current threat landscape, not just people who know the tools.

Hands-on experience. CTF participation, open-source security contributions, bug bounty programs, and real-world security projects carry more weight than certifications alone. Demonstrable work is what stands out at the screening stage.

AI and data familiarity. CloudSEK's products are AI-driven. Engineers and analysts who understand how machine learning is applied to security problems have a clear edge over candidates who do not.

Communication skills. Threat research and intelligence work requires turning complex findings into clear, actionable reports for non-technical stakeholders. Strong written and verbal communication is valued across all roles, not just client-facing ones.

Ownership mindset. As a growth-stage company, CloudSEK expects people to take initiative without waiting for detailed instructions. Candidates who can point to self-directed projects or moments of proactive problem-solving consistently perform well in interviews here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study the product before any interview. Spend time on CloudSEK's website and their published threat reports, blog posts, and research papers. Understanding what their key products do and how they work signals genuine interest and gives you concrete talking points that generic candidates will not have.

Brush up on threat intelligence fundamentals. Know the MITRE ATT&CK framework, common attack vectors (phishing, supply chain attacks, credential stuffing), and how OSINT tools are used in threat hunting. Even if you are applying for an engineering role, this context matters at CloudSEK because the products are built around it.

Prepare STAR-format examples. For behavioral questions, have two or three stories ready that show how you handled a complex security problem, a fast-moving incident, or a situation where you worked without clear guidance and delivered anyway.

Do at least one CTF or bug bounty before your interview. Platforms like HackTheBox, TryHackMe, or Bugcrowd give you concrete, verifiable talking points. CloudSEK interviewers respond well to candidates with active hands-on experience outside of coursework.

Research compensation benchmarks independently. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list commonly cited compensation ranges for cybersecurity roles in Bangalore. Go into the HR call knowing your target number rather than leaving it open-ended, which tends to put you at a disadvantage.

Prepare thoughtful questions for the leadership round. Ask about the product roadmap, how the threat research team operates day to day, and what success looks like in the role over the first six months. This round is two-directional, and strong questions leave a lasting impression.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

CloudSEK operates as a high-ownership, fast-moving environment. Because it is a product company rather than a services firm, the work is focused on building and improving a platform, not delivering client projects on a fixed scope. Teams are relatively lean, which means individuals carry meaningful responsibility early in their careers.

Based on publicly reported employee experiences, the culture leans toward intellectual curiosity and mission-driven work. Many employees cite the subject matter itself (defending organizations against real-world threats) as a strong motivating factor day to day. The company has a visible founder presence, which tends to make decision-making faster but also means expectations are high and individual contributions are clearly visible.

CloudSEK is Bangalore-headquartered, and most technical roles are based there. The pace aligns with a startup environment: priorities can shift, scope can expand quickly, and people who thrive are those who adapt without needing rigid process or heavy oversight.

If you are coming from a large IT services company, the contrast will be noticeable. The work is more ambiguous, the feedback loop is shorter, and the opportunity to see your work shipped and used by real clients comes much faster. For people who want that kind of environment, CloudSEK is genuinely well-regarded within the Indian cybersecurity product space.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does CloudSEK's full interview process take from application to offer?

Based on publicly reported candidate timelines, the process commonly runs two to four weeks from the initial application to a final offer. The duration depends on how quickly you move through each stage and the seniority of the role. Senior or leadership roles tend to take longer because they involve additional rounds with founders or cross-functional stakeholders. Staying responsive and following up politely after each stage can help keep things moving on their end.

Does CloudSEK hire freshers or is prior experience required?

CloudSEK does hire freshers and recent graduates, particularly for threat research analyst and junior engineering roles. The key differentiator for fresher candidates is demonstrable hands-on experience: CTF participation, bug bounty reports, personal security projects, or relevant internships carry significant weight in the review process. A strong academic record alone is less likely to get you through without some form of applied work to point to. Check individual job descriptions carefully, as some roles explicitly require prior full-time experience.

What kinds of roles are most commonly open at CloudSEK?

CloudSEK regularly hires in threat intelligence research, software engineering (with a security or AI focus), product management, and sales or business development. As of mid-2026, there are 33 open roles across these functions. Engineering roles tend to focus on backend development and machine learning applied to security data. If you are from a non-technical background, openings in marketing, operations, and customer success also appear periodically, so it is worth checking their careers page regularly.

Is a cybersecurity certification like CEH or OSCP necessary to get hired?

Certifications like CEH, OSCP, or CompTIA Security+ are useful signals but are not strictly required for most roles at CloudSEK. The company tends to value hands-on proof of work more than paper credentials: active bug bounty participation, CTF rankings, or open-source contributions often carry more weight in the actual interview conversation. That said, for senior or specialized roles, a credential like OSCP can strengthen your profile if you lack direct industry experience. Always check the specific job description for any requirements listed.

What salary can I expect at CloudSEK?

Specific compensation figures for CloudSEK are not publicly confirmed in detail, but Glassdoor and levels.fyi list commonly cited ranges for cybersecurity product roles in Bangalore that you can use as a reference point. Your best move is to research those platforms before the HR call and go in with a clear target number based on your experience level and what the market data shows. The HR screening call is the right moment to discuss compensation, and having done your homework beforehand puts you in a much stronger position.

How important is the founder round and how should I approach it?

The founder or leadership round at CloudSEK is genuinely important and should not be treated as a formality after clearing the technical stages. It focuses less on technical depth and more on your understanding of the cybersecurity landscape, your long-term career vision, and whether you are the kind of person who takes ownership in ambiguous situations. Prepare by reading CloudSEK's publicly released threat research and thinking about where the company fits in the broader market. Come with real questions about the product roadmap and team direction rather than generic ones.
