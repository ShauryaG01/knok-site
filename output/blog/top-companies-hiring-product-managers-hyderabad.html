01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Product Manager compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Hyderabad had 24 Product Manager openings live on July 8, 2026, tracked via knok jobradar. That is a concentrated set compared to Bangalore (271) or Delhi (177), but the city has a distinct character worth understanding before you start applying.

The tech corridor stretching across HITEC City, Gachibowli, and Kondapur is home to two types of PM employers: product-first SaaS companies that have built globally used platforms out of Hyderabad, and GCCs (Global Capability Centres) of large multinationals that need PMs to drive product work for their parent organisations. A handful of fintech and D2C startups round out the picture.

SaaS product companies (Zenoti, AbhiBus, HighRadius) anchor this list. Zenoti alone has 6 openings, making it the single largest hirer in Hyderabad right now. 09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Hyderabad's PM market is smaller than Bangalore's, which means hiring managers know their candidate pool. A generic application will not cut through. Here is what actually moves the needle.

Show product depth, not just process fluency. Companies like Zenoti and HighRadius want PMs who can talk about customer problems in their specific domain. Before applying, spend time actually using or researching the product. Come with a concrete observation: what works well, what feels missing, what you would prioritise on the roadmap next.

Prove you work well with engineers. Hyderabad's tech companies are engineering-heavy. In interviews, use examples where you worked through technical trade-offs with your team, not just handed over a spec. Any experience with APIs, data pipelines, or SQL is worth mentioning.

Prepare stakeholder stories for GCC roles. Warner Bros Discovery, Optum, and New Relic need PMs who can align distributed teams. Have a specific story ready about a time you brought a remote or cross-functional group to agreement on a hard decision.

Quantify your impact clearly. 'Led feature launch' is weak. 'Led a feature launch that improved activation' is better. Use your own numbers from your own work. A sharp impact story from a less famous company beats a vague claim from a brand-name one.

Build a Hyderabad-specific network. Product communities in the city are active on LinkedIn and meet offline periodically. A warm referral at Zenoti or HighRadius will lift your application above most cold applies.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With 24 openings across 10 companies, you can run a focused, high-quality campaign rather than sending dozens of generic applications.

Prioritise by fit, not brand. Zenoti has the most roles (6), making it the natural starting point for volume. But match your background first. Fintech experience? HighRadius and CreditVidya are your strongest fits. Healthcare or enterprise software background? Optum is a clear target. Consumer brand or D2C exposure? Swiss Beauty Cosmetics is worth a look.

Apply in the first week. PM roles in a smaller market like Hyderabad fill faster than in Bangalore. An application on day 7 may land into a shortlist that is already nearly closed.

Tailor your resume headline. Swap 'Product Manager' for something role-specific: 'Product Manager, SaaS / B2B' for Zenoti, or 'Product Manager, Fintech' for HighRadius. Applicant tracking systems and recruiters both scan for this kind of specificity.

Send a short LinkedIn message to the right person. Find the PM leader or recruiter at the company and send three sentences: who you are, one specific observation about their product, and why you want to work there. This approach works especially well at smaller companies like CreditVidya or AbhiBus where the hiring team is close-knit.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hyderabad a good city for Product Manager jobs?

Yes, particularly if you want to work at established SaaS or fintech companies. Hyderabad has a strong base of product-first companies like Zenoti and HighRadius, plus GCCs of global multinationals like Optum and Warner Bros Discovery. The market is smaller than Bangalore but roles tend to skew mid-to-senior and the competition is proportionally lower.

Which companies are actively hiring PMs in Hyderabad right now?

As of July 2026, Zenoti leads with 6 openings, followed by AbhiBus and HighRadius with 2 each. Optum, New Relic, Warner Bros Discovery, CreditVidya, Swiss Beauty Cosmetics, Softobiz Technologies, and NOMISO India each have 1 opening. The mix covers SaaS product companies, GCCs, fintech startups, a D2C brand, and IT services firms.

What salary can I expect as a Product Manager in Hyderabad?

Based on knok data, Associate PMs can expect 12-20 LPA, PMs with 3-6 years of experience typically land in the 24-40 LPA range, Senior PMs see 40-60 LPA, and Group or Principal PMs can reach 55-90+ LPA. GCCs and well-funded SaaS companies tend to pay at or near the top of each band, and levels.fyi users commonly report additional RSU or ESOP components at senior levels.

What is the difference between a PM role at a GCC and a product startup in Hyderabad?

At a GCC like Optum or Warner Bros Discovery, you are building or improving products for a large global organisation with established processes and teams spread across time zones. Compensation is often higher and more structured. At a startup like CreditVidya, you own more of the roadmap, move faster, and operate in a smaller team with more ambiguity. Both are valid paths but suit different working styles.

How competitive are PM jobs in Hyderabad compared to Bangalore?

Hyderabad had 24 PM openings versus Bangalore's 271 as of July 2026. Fewer openings also mean fewer applicants competing in the city, so the difficulty is not necessarily higher. A well-tailored application to the right Hyderabad company can outperform a spray-and-pray approach aimed at Bangalore's higher volume.

Do Hyderabad PM roles require domain-specific experience?

Many mid and senior roles do. HighRadius and CreditVidya look for fintech familiarity, Zenoti values SaaS and B2B product experience, and Optum benefits from healthcare domain knowledge. That said, strong product fundamentals combined with genuine curiosity about the domain can close a gap in direct experience, especially at the Associate PM or early PM level.
