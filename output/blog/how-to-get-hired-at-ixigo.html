01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

ixigo is one of India's most recognized travel-tech brands, built around AI-powered flight and train search, real-time PNR status tracking, and bus and hotel bookings. The company went public in 2024 and has continued to invest in its product and engineering teams. Its apps serve a large user base across India, with a strong focus on tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

As of July 2026, ixigo has 14 open roles across engineering, product management, data science, marketing, and business development. Most openings are in the Gurugram headquarters, with some hybrid-friendly positions. The hiring pace is steady rather than a high-volume spree, which means each opening gets serious attention and the process moves at a reasonable speed.

If you are someone who loves both technology and travel, ixigo is worth your time. The team is lean by the standards of publicly listed tech companies, so you will likely work on problems that have real user impact quickly rather than being one small cog in a very large machine.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

ixigo's hiring process typically runs through four to five stages, though the exact structure depends on the role and seniority level. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Stage 1: Application screening
Recruiters review your resume and shortlist based on relevant skills and experience. For engineering roles, a link to your GitHub profile or a portfolio of past projects can strengthen your application. Make sure your resume clearly shows the tech stack or domain you have worked in.

Stage 2: Recruiter or HR call
A short introductory call where the recruiter checks your background, current notice period, and interest in the role. Be ready to explain why you want to join a travel-tech company and why ixigo specifically. This is also a good time to ask about team size and role expectations.

Stage 3: Technical or functional assessment
For software engineers, this usually involves a coding round focused on data structures and algorithms, followed by a system design discussion for senior candidates. Product managers can expect a case study, often around a consumer app or a travel-specific problem. Data roles typically include SQL, Python-based analysis, and a discussion of past projects.

Stage 4: Hiring manager round
A conversation with the team lead or a senior manager. Expect deep questions about your past work, how you handled ambiguous problems, and how you think about scale and user experience. This round also gauges whether you will fit into the team's working style.

Stage 5: HR and offer discussion
Once the hiring manager approves, HR initiates the offer process. Turnaround at this stage is generally quick. Salary negotiation is possible, so come prepared with market data from Glassdoor or levels.fyi for your role and level.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Strong fundamentals, not just framework knowledge
ixigo's engineering team cares about how you think, not just which tools you know. Interviewers test your ability to reason through problems cleanly, write efficient code, and design systems that can handle real-world scale.

Product thinking across all roles
Even non-product roles at ixigo benefit from understanding the user. Engineers are expected to have an opinion about the features they build. Data analysts are expected to frame insights in terms of user behavior, not just numbers. If you can connect your work to how it makes travel easier for someone in a tier-2 city, you will stand out.

Ownership and initiative
ixigo is not a company where you wait for tasks to be assigned. Interviewers look for candidates who have proactively solved problems, driven projects to completion, and taken responsibility for outcomes rather than just output.

Domain curiosity
You do not need to be a travel expert before you join, but genuine curiosity about how the travel industry works, especially Indian Railways, low-cost airlines, and the informal bus sector, will come across positively in interviews.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply
Use the ixigo app before your interview. Explore the train booking flow, the flight price prediction feature, and the PNR tracker. Think about what works well, what could be improved, and why certain design choices may have been made. Interviewers appreciate candidates who have clearly used the product and can speak about it with specifics.

Practice the right coding topics
For engineering roles, focus on arrays, strings, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming. System design practice should cover topics like designing a flight search engine, a notification system for train delays, or a real-time price tracker. These are the kinds of problems that map directly to what ixigo works on.

Prepare strong STAR-format stories
Behavioural questions are common at every level. Prepare three to five stories from your past work that show ownership, problem-solving under pressure, and cross-functional collaboration. Structure each one clearly: situation, task, action, result.

Understand the travel-tech space
Read about how OTAs (online travel agencies) make money, how Indian Railways APIs work, and what challenges exist in last-mile travel booking. Industry surveys and news from 2024-2026 can give you a solid picture of where ixigo sits in the competitive landscape.

Know your salary data
Before the offer stage, check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for publicly reported salary ranges for your role and experience level in Gurugram. This gives you a data-backed starting point for negotiation.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Travel-first mission
ixigo was built around the idea of making travel accessible to every Indian, not just metro-city professionals booking premium flights. That mission shows up in how the team talks about users, how products are designed for low-bandwidth connections, and how success is measured in terms of user impact rather than just revenue.

Lean and fast-moving
Compared to larger tech companies, ixigo's teams are smaller, which means faster decision cycles and more direct access to senior leaders. You are unlikely to spend months on a project that gets shelved. The flip side is that you are expected to move quickly and manage ambiguity without needing constant direction.

Data-informed decisions
The team leans heavily on data to make product and business decisions. A culture of experimentation, A/B testing, and rapid iteration is common across teams. If you enjoy working with clear metrics and seeing the results of your work quickly, this environment suits you.

Collaborative but independent
Team members are expected to work well across functions, especially between engineering, product, and business. At the same time, there is a strong expectation that individuals will own their work end-to-end rather than waiting for direction from managers.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the ixigo hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on what candidates commonly report, the process typically takes two to four weeks from the first recruiter call to the offer. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds may run slightly longer, especially for senior positions. Following up with the recruiter after each stage is a good way to keep the process moving without being pushy.

Does ixigo hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

ixigo primarily hires experienced professionals, but they do take on freshers for select engineering and analyst roles, often through campus hiring or specific entry-level openings. If you are a fresh graduate, look for roles labeled as 'junior' or 'associate' and make sure your resume highlights internships and projects clearly. New-grad hiring can open up in batches, so check their careers page regularly.

What tech stack does ixigo use?

ixigo's engineering teams work with Java, Python, and Kotlin for backend and mobile development. For data engineering and analytics, they use tools common across the industry such as Spark, Kafka, and SQL-based analytics platforms. The specific stack can vary by team, so ask the recruiter which technologies are most relevant for your target role during the initial call.

Is remote work possible at ixigo?

Most ixigo roles are based out of their Gurugram office, and in-person presence is generally expected. Some roles may offer hybrid flexibility, but fully remote positions are not the norm. Confirm the work mode with the recruiter early in the process so there are no surprises when the offer arrives.

How should I negotiate my salary at ixigo?

Come to the negotiation with publicly reported salary data from Glassdoor or levels.fyi for your specific role and experience level in the Gurugram market. Be clear about your current CTC, your expectations, and the specific value you bring to the team. Negotiation is expected and acceptable at ixigo, so do not accept the first number without a conversation.

What is the best way to get noticed by ixigo recruiters?

A clear, well-structured resume that highlights your impact rather than just your responsibilities is the first step. Applying directly through the ixigo careers page is effective, but a referral from someone inside the company significantly improves your visibility. If you do not have a referral, reaching out to ixigo employees on LinkedIn with a short, specific message about the role you are targeting can also open doors.
