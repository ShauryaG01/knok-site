01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

TripAdvisor is one of the world's largest travel platforms, helping millions of people find hotels, restaurants, and experiences through reviews and price comparisons. The company has a strong technology presence in India, with engineering, product, data, and design teams working on search, personalisation, and platform reliability.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 99 open roles at TripAdvisor. Openings cover software engineering, data science, product management, UX design, and corporate functions. The mix includes mid-level and senior positions, with some entry-level opportunities as well.

TripAdvisor's hiring process is deliberate and structured. The company rewards candidates who prepare well and can speak clearly about the impact of their past work. India-based roles are often integrated with global product teams, so you may collaborate with colleagues across time zones from your first week.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages below are based on publicly reported candidate experiences and can vary by role or team. Treat this as a general guide, not a guarantee.

StageFormatWhat to expect
Resume screenRecruiter reviewTurnaround in a few days
Recruiter callShort phone or video callBackground, notice period, and expectations
Skills assessmentCoding test or take-home taskSent after the recruiter call

Behavioural roundHiring manager interviewValues, ownership, and past decisions

Resume screen: Your application goes through an automated filter before a recruiter reviews it. Tailor your CV to the specific job description and highlight products you have shipped, the scale you worked at, and measurable outcomes.

Recruiter call: A talent acquisition partner will discuss your background, notice period, and compensation expectations. This is a two-way conversation. Ask about the team structure and what a successful first six months looks like.

Skills assessment: Engineering candidates typically receive a timed coding challenge covering data structures and algorithms, sometimes with a short system design component. Product and data candidates often get a case study or take-home assignment instead.

Technical rounds: These are the most demanding part of the process. Engineers face one or two coding rounds and one system design discussion. Data science candidates get SQL, statistics, and product sense questions. Product managers answer questions on prioritisation, metric definition, and user research.

Behavioural round: A senior stakeholder will explore how you handle ownership, cross-functional friction, and decision-making under ambiguity. Concrete examples from your actual work hold up far better than general statements.

Offer: After a verbal confirmation, you receive a written offer letter followed by standard background verification.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

TripAdvisor hires people who are data-driven, user-obsessed, and take genuine ownership of outcomes rather than just tasks.

For engineers, strong fundamentals in algorithms and system design are the baseline. Experience building reliable, high-traffic consumer products is a clear advantage. Based on publicly reported job descriptions, the engineering stack includes Java, Python, and React, among other technologies.

For product managers, the ability to define sharp success metrics, prioritise across competing demands, and communicate clearly to both engineering and business partners is what stands out. Domain knowledge in travel or e-commerce helps but is not a hard requirement.

For data scientists and analysts, comfort with SQL, experiment design, and A/B test interpretation is essential. Translating analysis into decisions the business can act on is valued as much as technical depth.

Across all roles, TripAdvisor values four things consistently:

  • Ownership: driving outcomes, not just delivering tasks
  • Curiosity: genuine interest in how travellers research and decide
  • Collaboration: working smoothly across functions and geographies
  • Impact: being able to say clearly what your work achieved

Candidates who can explain the 'why' behind their past decisions, not just describe what they built, consistently make a stronger impression.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Sharpen your fundamentals first. For engineering roles, revise data structures, algorithms, and distributed systems design. For data roles, brush up on SQL window functions, experiment design, and statistical inference. TripAdvisor's technical bar is consistently reported as high for engineering and data positions.

Use TripAdvisor's own product as study material. Think about how their search ranking works, how they handle review authenticity, or how they personalise destination recommendations. Interviewers respond positively when candidates connect their answers to the actual product.

Prepare STAR-format stories. Choose four or five situations from your career that show ownership, handling failure, shipping under pressure, and collaborating across teams. The behavioural round goes deep, so vague answers will not hold up.

Align your resume to the job description. TripAdvisor posts detailed listings. Match your language to the keywords and responsibilities listed. Before each interview round, review the LinkedIn profiles of your interviewers if available, so you understand their perspective.

Practice talking about impact. Even if you cannot share exact figures, saying 'we improved checkout completion, confirmed through a statistically significant A/B test' is stronger than describing what you built without mentioning results.

Prepare questions to ask. Candidates who ask thoughtful questions stand out. Good ones include: what is the team's biggest technical challenge right now, how is success measured in the first six months, and how are product and engineering decisions made together.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

TripAdvisor operates as a consumer tech company with a genuine focus on travel. The culture is data-driven and product-centric: decisions are backed by experiments and user research rather than gut feel alone.

Collaboration across time zones is a regular part of life for India-based roles, since product ownership often sits globally. Written communication skills matter more here than in purely local teams, and some overlap with US time zones is expected.

The working environment is described in publicly reported employee reviews as relatively open and flat. Access to senior stakeholders is reportedly easier than at some larger tech companies. Reviews on Glassdoor mention good work-life balance compared to industry norms, though this varies by team and business season.

Diversity and inclusion are stated company priorities. Employee resource groups exist for women in tech, LGBTQ+ employees, and other communities. India offices participate in global inclusion programmes.

Travel is woven into the culture. Employees get access to travel tools and discounts, and the company genuinely encourages people to use the products they help build. If travel and hospitality interest you beyond a job category, that enthusiasm tends to show in interviews and is noticed.

One thing worth knowing: TripAdvisor has gone through periods of restructuring in recent years, as publicly reported in industry news. Ask your recruiter about team stability and the product roadmap so you go in with clear expectations.
