01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Signzy is a Bangalore-based fintech company that builds AI-powered digital onboarding and KYC (Know Your Customer) tools for banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions across India. Their platform helps lenders verify customer identities, process documents, and run compliance checks entirely online, without any branch visits or paper forms.

As of mid-2026, Signzy has 44 open roles across software engineering, data science, product management, enterprise sales, and operations. The hiring pace reflects the company's push into new areas like lending automation and cross-border compliance. Whether you come from a fintech background or are a strong technologist ready to learn the domain, there are genuine opportunities here if you prepare with intention.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Signzy hires across technical and business functions, so expectations differ by role. A few qualities show up consistently across teams.

For software engineers: Strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms matter at the screening stage. Backend experience and comfort with REST APIs and microservices are important. If you have worked with OCR, ML models, or document processing pipelines, that is a direct match for Signzy's core product work.

For product managers: Signzy wants people who can balance user needs against strict regulatory constraints. Experience working on compliance-heavy products, or familiarity with RBI guidelines around Video KYC, DigiLocker, or CKYC integrations, is a clear differentiator.

For enterprise sales and business development: A track record in B2B SaaS or fintech sales, particularly selling to banks or NBFCs, is valued. The ability to manage long sales cycles and build relationships with senior stakeholders matters a lot.

Across all roles: Signzy is a growth-stage startup. They value people who take ownership, adapt quickly, and can explain complex technical or compliance topics to a non-technical audience. If you have worked in fast-moving environments where priorities shift often, that experience translates well here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Getting ready for a Signzy interview means combining solid fundamentals with domain-specific knowledge. Here is a practical, stage-by-stage approach.

Understand their products before your first call. Spend time with Signzy's actual offerings: Video KYC, CKYC integration, DigiLocker-based onboarding, and API-driven identity verification. Knowing how these work, and why a bank or NBFC needs them, gives you talking points that most candidates miss.

For tech roles, sharpen your problem-solving. Practice data structures and algorithms at a medium difficulty level on platforms like LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks. Also review system design concepts, particularly around distributed APIs and high-throughput verification systems, since Signzy's platform processes large volumes of identity checks.

Learn the regulatory context. Even for engineering roles, having a basic grasp of what RBI's KYC Master Directions cover, or what Video KYC guidelines require, shows you understand the constraints the product operates under. You do not need to be a compliance expert, just fluent enough to hold the conversation.

Prepare STAR-format answers for behavioural questions. Managerial and HR rounds at Signzy typically include questions about handling ambiguity, meeting tight deadlines, or influencing decisions without direct authority. Have two or three strong stories ready from your own experience.

Research their growth story. Look at Signzy's recent partnerships, product launches, and client announcements. Showing that you know where the company is headed signals genuine interest and helps you ask sharper questions at the end of each round.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Signzy operates with the urgency of a growth-stage startup and the accountability of a company serving regulated financial institutions. That combination shapes the day-to-day experience in a distinctive way.

Teams are lean and cross-functional. Engineers, product managers, and compliance leads often work directly together with relatively few layers of management in between. This is good if you want ownership and visibility early in your career. It can feel intense if you prefer clearly scoped work and structured processes.

The core mission, eliminating paper-based friction from financial services, resonates strongly with people who care about the impact of their work. A faster KYC process means a small business owner gets credit sooner, or a migrant worker can open a bank account digitally without travelling to a branch. Many employees cite this purpose as a reason they joined and stayed.

The pace is genuinely fast. Priorities can shift as the company responds to new RBI circulars, large client requirements, or product pivots. If you thrive in that kind of environment, Signzy can offer rapid growth and early exposure to complex, meaningful problems. If you prefer a more structured environment, it is worth asking specific questions about team planning cycles and processes during your interviews.

Commonly cited positives include flexible working arrangements, meaningful technical challenges in AI and ML, and early exposure to senior decision-makers at large financial institutions for those in client-facing roles.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open positions does Signzy have right now?

As of mid-2026, Signzy has 44 open roles across engineering, product, sales, and operations. Positions span multiple seniority levels, so there are entry points for both early-career candidates and experienced professionals. Checking their careers page regularly is worthwhile since new roles are added as teams expand.

What salary can I expect at Signzy?

Signzy does not publish fixed salary bands publicly, and compensation varies significantly by role and experience. Based on Glassdoor and AmbitionBox listings, pay is generally in line with other growth-stage fintech startups of similar size. Come prepared with your current CTC and a clear expected CTC backed by your own market research for your specific function and level.

Does Signzy hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Signzy does hire freshers, mostly into engineering and operations roles, though the majority of openings target candidates with prior relevant experience. If you are a recent graduate, lead with internship projects, personal builds, or open-source contributions that demonstrate hands-on skills. Tailoring your resume tightly to the specific role you want makes a real difference in getting shortlisted.

How long does the Signzy hiring process take from application to offer?

Candidates typically report a timeline of two to four weeks from first contact to offer, though this can stretch if scheduling delays arise on either side. Following up politely with your HR contact after each completed round is perfectly appropriate. If you have a competing offer or a joining deadline, communicate that early so Signzy can prioritise your rounds accordingly.

Is prior fintech experience required to join Signzy?

Not always, but it helps significantly depending on the function. For software engineering and data science roles, strong technical fundamentals can offset limited fintech exposure, as long as you show genuine curiosity about the domain during interviews. For product management, sales, and compliance-adjacent roles, experience in banking, lending, or regtech is a much stronger requirement and will weigh heavily in the evaluation.

What is the best way to apply to Signzy?

Start by checking whether you have any LinkedIn connections at Signzy, since a referral from a current employee dramatically improves your chances of getting a callback. If not, apply through their official careers page and tailor your resume to highlight any API, KYC, or financial services experience even if it is tangential. Writing a short, specific note explaining why you are interested in fintech compliance tools can also help you stand out from generic applicants.
