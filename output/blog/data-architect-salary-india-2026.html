01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Indian job listings for senior tech roles skip the pay range, and Data Architect is no exception. Of the 57 Data Architect openings tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026, none came with verified salary bands. Companies at this seniority level prefer to negotiate individually: expectations differ sharply between a large IT services firm and a funded product startup, and publishing a number early limits their flexibility.

This means the figures you see on job boards or in LinkedIn posts are mostly self-reported or estimated. They can still give you a useful starting frame. Cross-check Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and LinkedIn Salary together rather than relying on any one source, and always filter by company size and city to get closer to your actual market.

Context helps set expectations. Data Architects design the data infrastructure that every analyst, engineer, and business team depends on. Companies pay for that combination of technical depth and strategic judgment, which places this role near the top of data team compensation.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Verified pay bands are not available in this dataset, so the figures below come from publicly reported data and Glassdoor. Treat them as a directional frame, not a guarantee. Sample sizes for this specific role on these platforms are small, so individual variation is significant.

Early career (0-4 years, moving into architecture responsibilities for the first time): Glassdoor commonly cites this stage in the 15-25 LPA range, typically at IT services firms or as the first senior data hire at a growing startup.

Mid-level (5-9 years, established Data Architect): Industry surveys commonly place experienced architects in the 25-50 LPA range at product companies, with services and consulting firms tending toward the lower end of that band.

Senior or principal (10+ years, leading architecture across multiple teams or business units): Publicly reported data from levels.fyi suggests senior Data Architects at large MNCs or well-funded product companies can reach 60-100 LPA or beyond. These figures come from a thin sample and skew toward the most competitive employers.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Anchor on verified market data, not rumours. Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and LinkedIn Salary each show different slices of the market. Pull numbers from all three, filter by city and company size, and you will have a much stronger anchor than any WhatsApp-forwarded figure.

Share a range, not a single number. A band signals confidence and keeps the conversation open. If HR asks for your number first, it is reasonable to say you are expecting a competitive offer in line with the market and your experience, then ask what they have budgeted.

Separate fixed CTC from total CTC. Many companies in India use equity, variable pay, or joining bonuses to inflate the headline number. Always ask what the fixed annual CTC is before comparing offers side by side.

Expect a meaningful jump when switching to an architect title. Publicly reported data suggests hikes of 30-50% are common when moving from a senior engineer to a Data Architect role at a new company, though this varies widely by employer and sector.

A competing offer is your strongest tool. If you have more than one conversation active, a second offer (even informal) removes the company's ability to anchor you to a low number. Run parallel processes whenever possible.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Senior tech roles in India bundle several pay components. Understanding each one helps you compare offers accurately rather than getting anchored to a large CTC headline.

Fixed base salary: The amount you receive regardless of company or individual performance. This is the number to anchor on when comparing offers, since everything else can fluctuate or disappear entirely.

Variable or performance bonus: Commonly cited at 10-20% of fixed base for senior individual contributors at large companies. Actual payout depends on both company-wide and individual performance ratings, so mentally discount it when evaluating an offer.

ESOPs or RSUs: Product companies and funded startups frequently offer equity. The value depends on vesting schedules and the company's growth stage. Treat pre-IPO ESOPs as speculative upside, not guaranteed income.

Joining bonus: Sometimes offered to offset a bonus you are leaving behind at your current employer. It is typically taxable and may come with a clawback clause if you exit before a set period, so read the terms carefully before signing.

Allowances: HRA, LTA, and meal or phone allowances are standard components that reduce your tax liability. They are part of CTC on paper but do not always arrive as direct cash.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What does a Data Architect do, and why does it pay more than a Data Engineer?

A Data Architect sets the blueprint: which databases, pipelines, and governance frameworks the whole data team will build on. A Data Engineer builds within that blueprint. The architect role involves fewer hands-on coding hours and more decision-making, stakeholder alignment, and long-term system design. Companies pay more for that judgment because a poor architecture decision can cost months of rework across the entire team.

Is a Data Architect the same as a Solution Architect or Cloud Architect?

Not exactly, though there is overlap. A Data Architect specializes in data systems: warehouses, data lakes, pipelines, data modeling, and governance. A Solution Architect or Cloud Architect covers a broader tech stack and may not go deep on data. Some companies use these titles interchangeably, so always read the job description carefully. If the role focuses on data modeling, ETL pipelines, or data platform design, it is a Data Architect role regardless of what the title says.

How many Data Architect jobs are open in India right now?

knok jobradar tracked 57 Data Architect openings across India as of July 2026. Delhi led at 8 roles, followed by Bangalore at 7 and Chennai at 5. Mumbai showed zero public listings in this snapshot, which likely reflects BFSI firms there preferring internal referrals or specialist recruiters over public job boards.

Do I need a specific certification to become a Data Architect?

No single certification is universally required, but cloud platform certifications from AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure are commonly cited in job descriptions and do open doors. A strong portfolio of data modeling decisions, hands-on experience with tools like dbt, Snowflake, or Databricks, and a track record of making real architecture calls carries more weight than any certificate alone. Certifications get you the interview; demonstrated judgment closes the offer.

How do I move from Data Engineer to Data Architect?

The most common path is to take on architecture decisions within your current team, even informally. Volunteer to design new pipelines, propose data modeling standards, or lead a platform evaluation. Document these decisions and their outcomes, because when you apply for architect roles, being able to say 'I designed X, here is why I chose it, and here is what happened' matters far more than years of experience on a resume.

Should I target product companies or IT services firms for a Data Architect role?

Both have trade-offs. Product companies (funded startups, SaaS firms, large tech) typically pay more and give you ownership over the full architecture. IT services and consulting firms offer exposure to many different client environments and more predictable career progression, but publicly reported salaries tend to be lower for equivalent experience. If pay is your primary goal, product companies in Bangalore and Delhi are the stronger bet based on publicly reported market comparisons.
