01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

TartanHQ is a B2B fintech startup that builds payroll and employment data APIs. Lenders, fintechs, and HR platforms use Tartan's technology to verify income, employment history, and salary data in real time, reducing the paperwork that typically slows credit approvals and background checks.

As of mid-2026, TartanHQ has 11 open roles across engineering, product, business development, and operations. The team is compact and growing fast, which means each new hire is expected to contribute from day one. The process is selective, but a focused application and solid preparation make a real difference.

Most roles are based in major metro cities, with some remote flexibility depending on the function. If you want to work on developer-facing financial infrastructure with genuine traction across Indian fintech, TartanHQ is worth a serious look.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The TartanHQ hiring process typically covers four to five stages, though the exact count can vary by role and seniority. The breakdown below reflects what candidates commonly report; your experience may differ slightly.

StageFormatWhat to Expect
Resume ScreeningAsync reviewSkills and experience checked against the JD
Recruiter CallBrief phone or videoNotice period, location, salary expectations, role questions
Technical AssessmentTake-home or liveCoding challenge for engineers, case study for product or business roles

OfferWrittenCompensation discussion and offer letter

Resume Screening: Recruiters scan for relevant skills, clean project descriptions, and clear role-match signals. Tailor your resume to mirror the job description and front-load your most relevant work.

Recruiter Call: A short conversation to confirm your availability, location, and salary expectations. Be direct and specific. This is also a good moment to ask one focused question about the team or the product to signal genuine interest.

Technical Assessment: Engineering candidates typically receive a coding problem covering data structures, API design, or SQL. Product and business candidates may get a case study instead. Aim for clean, working code and a clear explanation of your reasoning.

Technical or Domain Interview: A deeper conversation with a senior engineer or hiring manager. Expect questions on system design, how you have tackled ambiguous problems, and your understanding of the product domain. Knowledge of fintech concepts like KYC, payroll flows, or credit underwriting is a bonus, though rarely a hard requirement.

Culture and Leadership Interview: Often with a co-founder or senior leader. The goal is to assess whether you can thrive in a fast-moving startup. Be ready to talk about why you want to join a small team, how you handle shifting priorities, and what excites you about Tartan's problem space.

Offer: Compensation is discussed after the final round. Negotiation is possible, especially for senior or specialized roles.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

TartanHQ looks for people who can move fast and take ownership without waiting for a detailed brief. Because the team is small, the expectation is that you pick up a problem, figure out the edges, and deliver, not just execute a spec.

For engineering roles, the core asks are solid backend fundamentals (REST APIs, databases, system design), comfort with at least one backend language such as Python, Node.js, or Go, and experience integrating with third-party APIs, which is central to what Tartan builds. Exposure to fintech domains like payroll, KYC, or credit data is a genuine advantage.

For product and business roles, structured thinking matters most: the ability to break down an ambiguous problem, communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical audiences, and stay close to the customer. Prior B2B or fintech experience is valued but not always mandatory.

Across all roles, intellectual curiosity, a bias toward action, and genuine excitement about the problem Tartan is solving tend to distinguish candidates who move forward from those who do not.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before any round. Spend time with Tartan's developer documentation and understand what their APIs actually do. Being able to speak concretely about a use case, for example how a lender might use payroll verification to reduce fraud, signals real interest and sets you apart from candidates who only skimmed the homepage.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. With 11 open roles across different functions, each JD has a distinct focus. Mirror the language of the JD in your resume bullets and put your most relevant experience at the top. A generic resume is easy to skip over at a startup where hiring decisions move fast.

Prepare your project stories. Pick two or three past projects you can explain end to end: what the problem was, what you built, what went wrong, and what you learned. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure non-technical examples cleanly.

Brush up on fintech basics. You do not need to be a domain expert, but understanding terms like KYC, NACH mandates, payroll disbursement, and credit underwriting helps you ask smarter questions and connect your background to Tartan's world.

Practice system design for engineering roles. For mid to senior positions, system design questions are common. Practice designing APIs and data pipelines with attention to edge cases like rate limiting, data consistency, and third-party API failures.

Prepare genuine questions for every stage. Avoid generic questions. Ask about the team's current technical challenges, how product decisions get made, or what a successful first quarter looks like in the role. It shows you are thinking like a future team member, not just a candidate.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

TartanHQ operates with a startup culture that values speed, directness, and ownership. The team is small and the hierarchy is flat, which means decisions get made quickly and you are likely to work directly with founders and senior leaders from very early on.

Because the company builds financial infrastructure, there is a strong emphasis on reliability and data accuracy. A bug in a payroll API or an employment verification flow can affect someone's loan approval or job offer, so engineers in particular are expected to understand the real-world stakes of their work, not just the technical implementation.

Collaboration tends to be cross-functional. Engineers, product managers, and business teams work closely together, and there is a shared expectation that everyone understands the customer problem, not just their specific piece of the pipeline.

For candidates moving from large corporations, the shift can feel significant. Fewer processes, less documentation, and more ambiguity are part of the deal. For many people, that is exactly the appeal. It is worth being honest with yourself about whether that kind of environment energizes or drains you before you apply.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many roles is TartanHQ currently hiring for?

As of mid-2026, TartanHQ has 11 open roles spanning engineering, product, business development, and operations. The company is actively hiring across multiple functions at once. At a startup of this size, roles can be filled quickly, so applying early and following up after submission gives you a real edge.

Does TartanHQ hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

TartanHQ primarily hires candidates with professional experience given the specialized nature of their product. That said, freshers with strong fundamentals, relevant internship work, or standout projects in fintech or API development may be considered for entry-level engineering roles. Always check the minimum experience requirement listed in the specific JD before applying.

How long does the full interview process take?

Candidates commonly report that the process takes two to four weeks from application to offer, depending on role complexity and scheduling. Startups at Tartan's stage tend to move faster than large companies, but delays can happen. Sending a polite follow-up a few days after each stage is always appropriate and keeps you visible.

What salary can I expect at TartanHQ?

TartanHQ does not publicly disclose salary bands. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry some community-reported compensation data for comparable B2B fintech startups in India, which can give you a starting reference point. Come to the recruiter call with a clear number in mind based on your experience and current market rates, since expectations are typically discussed early in the process.

Do I need a fintech background to join TartanHQ?

Not always. For engineering roles, strong backend and API development skills matter more than domain-specific experience. That said, familiarity with concepts like KYC, payroll flows, or credit data is a genuine advantage and signals that you can get productive quickly. For product and business roles, prior B2B or fintech experience carries more weight in the evaluation.

Is remote work an option at TartanHQ?

TartanHQ's remote policy is not publicly detailed and may vary by role and team. Some candidates report hybrid working arrangements, while others work primarily from office. The safest approach is to ask directly during the recruiter call so you have a clear picture before you invest time in further rounds. The answer may also depend on your location and the seniority of the role.
