06 Key Findings

Key Findings

  • AI and GenAI roles lead demand in 2026. The knok job index (snapshot: July 8, 2026) shows AI/ML Engineer and Generative AI roles among the fastest-growing job types across the 150+ sites knok monitors, with nearly every large tech company and funded startup actively hiring in this space.
  • Cloud and DevOps hiring has stabilised at a high floor. AWS, Azure, and GCP roles have not cooled. Platform engineering and DevOps appear across product companies, IT services firms, and Global Capability Centres, pointing to structural rather than cyclical demand.
  • Data roles are splitting into two tiers. Data Analyst listings are high in volume but draw heavy competition. Data Engineer and Analytics Engineer roles are fewer but attract stronger employer intent, and the hiring managers behind them are harder to reach through standard portals.
  • Full-stack roles dominate by sheer listing volume. React and Node.js combinations account for the largest share of listings in the index, with the 3-7 year experience band seeing the most activity.
  • Cybersecurity hiring is quietly accelerating. Security engineering openings grew steadily from 2024 to 2026, driven by compliance mandates and breaches publicly reported across Indian fintech and healthcare sectors.
  • Technical Product Manager roles are on the rise. Titles like 'Technical PM' and 'AI Product Manager' appear more frequently than in the 2024 baseline, reflecting a shift toward product managers who can work directly with engineering teams.
07 Methodology

Methodology

The data in this report comes from the knok job index, a nightly crawl of 150+ Indian job sites including Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed India, and direct company career pages. The snapshot date for this report is July 8, 2026. Listings are deduplicated by URL and employer, so the index counts unique active openings rather than total postings. The total snapshot size is not disclosed in this public summary.

Salary figures referenced in this report are sourced from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys, not from the knok index itself. Job listings do not always include salary information, and self-reported salary databases carry their own biases. Treat salary ranges as directional references, not guarantees. Demand levels in the tables below are relative to other roles within the same index snapshot and are not absolute listing counts.

11 What It Means

What It Means

Read this before updating your resume. The knok job index snapshot from July 8, 2026 covers a broad set of active listings but is a point-in-time picture. Tech hiring can shift within weeks, especially for AI-adjacent roles where product pivots and funding cycles move quickly. Use this report as a directional guide, not a guarantee of what you will find when you apply.

For job seekers with 0-3 years of experience, the data points to full-stack and data analyst roles as the widest entry points by listing volume. Wide entry also means more applicants per seat. Differentiating with a specific framework (dbt for data, Kubernetes for DevOps) or a domain focus (fintech, healthtech) can help your application stand apart from the crowd.

For job seekers with 4-8 years of experience, AI/ML and cloud architecture roles appear to have stronger employer demand relative to available candidates in the index. If your background is adjacent (for example, backend Python with some model serving experience), this gap is worth closing with a targeted side project or a relevant certification before your next application cycle.

On salaries, Glassdoor and levels.fyi report that senior AI/ML engineers in Bengaluru commonly earn in the 25-40 LPA range. Mid-level full-stack engineers are commonly cited at 15-25 LPA. These are self-reported figures with small sample sizes for some roles, so treat them as a negotiating reference rather than a number to anchor your expectations on.

On location, most listings from IT services firms in the index specify in-office or hybrid. Startups and GCCs carry more remote-friendly roles, especially for senior individual contributors.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which tech role has the highest demand in India in 2026?

The knok job index (July 2026 snapshot) shows AI/ML Engineer and Full-Stack Engineer roles at the top by listing volume and employer breadth. Full-stack roles lead in raw numbers, but AI/ML roles are growing faster year on year. Both categories cover many sub-specialisations, so the specific tech stack in a listing matters as much as the job title itself.

Is Bengaluru still the best city for tech jobs in India?

Yes, Bengaluru accounts for the largest share of tech listings in the knok index, especially for product companies, SaaS startups, and Global Capability Centres. That said, Hyderabad and Pune have grown significantly between 2024 and 2026 for cloud, DevOps, and data roles. If you are open to relocating, comparing offers across all three cities is worth the effort before you decide.

What skills should I add to my resume to get more interview calls in 2026?

The knok index shows employers most frequently listing Python, Kubernetes, React, AWS, and dbt in their job descriptions. For AI-focused roles, experience with LLM APIs, RAG pipelines, or model fine-tuning appears consistently across listings. Showing a demonstrable project, a GitHub repo or a deployed side project, tends to carry more weight with recruiters than a certification alone.

Are IT services companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro still worth applying to?

They consistently appear among the highest-volume hirers in the knok index, which means more open seats but also more applicants and a structured hiring process. If you are early in your career, these companies can be a strong starting point for building skills and getting your first enterprise experience. Mid-career professionals moving from IT services to product or GCC roles often face a skills gap conversation, so it is worth preparing for that transition specifically.

How do I get noticed when my application gets lost in a large applicant pool?

Many companies post roles exclusively on their own career pages or on niche platforms that do not appear in a standard Naukri search. Applying early matters because recruiters often shortlist from the first batch of applications they receive. Knok crawls 150+ sites nightly and applies on your behalf the night a role goes live, which gives you a timing advantage without requiring you to monitor every site manually.

Do I need a degree from an IIT or NIT to land a top tech job in 2026?

Publicly reported hiring data from companies like Razorpay, CRED, and Meesho shows engineering teams with a mix of tier-1 and tier-2 college backgrounds. Skills demonstrated through open-source contributions, side projects, or competitive programming carry significant weight, especially for roles below the senior level. For roles at FAANG-equivalent companies, preparation for system design and DSA rounds matters more than the name on your degree.
