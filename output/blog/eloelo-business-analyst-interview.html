01 Overview

Overview

eloelo is a fast-growing Indian social gaming and live streaming platform that combines real-time interactive games with creator-driven content. The company currently has 28 open roles, signalling active hiring across product, business, and tech functions. As a Business Analyst here, you sit at the intersection of data, product, and growth, helping teams understand how users discover games, engage with creators, and move toward paid features.

The interview process typically spans 2-4 rounds. Candidates report a mix of analytical exercises (often SQL or spreadsheet tasks), product thinking questions, and behavioural interviews. The pace reflects eloelo's startup culture: expect quick scheduling and interviewers who value clear reasoning over polished presentations. Preparation should focus on gaming and streaming metrics, funnel analysis, and eloelo's specific product features.

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Use the STAR format for all experience-based questions. Here are three worked examples tailored to the kind of analytical and stakeholder work a BA does at a product-first gaming company.

Q: Tell us about a time you used data to change a product or business decision.

*Situation:* At my previous company, the product team was planning to build a new feature based on qualitative feedback from a small group of power users.

*Task:* I was asked to validate whether this feature was worth prioritising by looking at actual usage patterns.

*Action:* I pulled session-level data from our analytics tool, segmented users by engagement tier, and found that the feedback came almost entirely from a niche group that behaved very differently from the typical user. I built a cohort comparison and presented it alongside the original qualitative feedback, with a clear recommendation.

*Result:* The team chose to run a smaller pilot instead of a full build, saving significant engineering time. The pilot data confirmed the feature only resonated with that niche segment, so the scope was adjusted accordingly.

---

Q: How would you measure the success of a newly launched game format on eloelo?

*Situation:* Imagine eloelo has just launched a new trivia game format alongside its existing interactive games.

*Task:* As the BA on the growth team, I need to define what 'success' means and track it from day one.

*Action:* I would first align with the product manager on the primary goal: is this about new user acquisition, session length, or monetisation? I would then pick a primary metric (such as 7-day repeat play rate for the new format) and a guardrail metric (overall platform session time, to confirm we are not cannibalising other games). I would set up a daily cohort dashboard from launch and flag anomalies in the first week.

*Result:* This structured approach lets the team make a data-backed call on whether to invest further, iterate, or sunset the format within the first 4 weeks after launch.

---

Q: A senior stakeholder wants a weekly report you think adds little value. How do you handle it?

*Situation:* A business head at my previous company asked for a manual weekly report covering a large list of metrics, many of which were already visible on a live dashboard.

*Task:* I needed to manage the request without damaging the relationship or ignoring the real underlying need.

*Action:* I scheduled a short call to understand which decisions the report was actually supporting. I found that only a handful of the metrics drove any real action. I proposed replacing the manual report with an automated email summary covering those core metrics plus an alert if anything moved significantly.

*Result:* The stakeholder agreed immediately. The new format took a fraction of the time to maintain, and the stakeholder later said it was more useful than the original.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

STAR for behavioural questions. Every experience-based answer needs a clear Situation (context), Task (your specific responsibility), Action (what you did, in first-person steps), and Result (outcome or learning). When spoken aloud, aim for 2-3 minutes per answer and cut anything that does not move the story forward.

The 3-metric structure for product measurement questions. When asked how you would measure a feature, name three things: a primary metric (proves the goal is met), a secondary metric (supporting signal), and a guardrail metric (what must not break). Naming all three signals analytical maturity and shows you think about trade-offs.

Layered segmentation for root-cause questions. Start by asking whether this is a data collection problem or a real product problem. Then segment by platform (Android, iOS, web), by user type (new vs. returning), by geography, and by time of day. Eliminate hypotheses one by one rather than jumping to a conclusion early.

Structured assumptions for estimation questions. State your assumption clearly, work through the calculation step by step, and sanity-check the result against something you know (for example, India's internet user base or a typical app's weekly retention). Being roughly right with clear logic beats a precise answer with no explanation.

Empathy-first for stakeholder management questions. Acknowledge the relationship and the underlying need before proposing a change. Interviewers at startups want to see that you can push back constructively, not just comply or refuse.

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

eloelo interviewers typically look for three qualities above everything else.

Product curiosity. They want a BA who actually uses the product and can speak to specific features, not someone who has only read the company's LinkedIn page. Download the app, play a few games, and form a genuine point of view on what is working and what could be improved.

Structured thinking under ambiguity. Startup BA roles involve incomplete data and shifting priorities. Interviewers will deliberately ask vague questions to see whether you ask clarifying questions before diving in. Candidates who jump straight to an answer without scoping the problem rarely progress to the next round.

Communication that drives decisions. A BA at eloelo is not only an analyst: they present findings to product managers, business leads, and sometimes senior leadership. Interviewers look for clarity in how you explain your reasoning, not just whether your analysis is technically correct.

Beyond these three, candidates report that eloelo values speed of execution and comfort with experimentation. If you have run A/B tests, worked with live-event data, or operated in a high-velocity product environment, make those experiences explicit in your answers.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Week 1: Know the product and the business

Download eloelo and spend time across different game formats and creator streams. Note the engagement mechanics in place (gifting, leaderboards, streaks) and how monetisation is presented to users. Read any publicly available coverage of eloelo's growth story from 2024 onwards to understand the company's trajectory.

Week 2: Sharpen SQL and metrics fundamentals

Practice queries involving GROUP BY, window functions, and cohort calculations. Focus on problems that mirror gaming analytics: session length, DAU/MAU ratios, retention curves, and funnel drop-off. Mode Analytics' public SQL library and Leetcode's database problems are useful for structured practice.

Week 3: Mock interviews and case practice

Work through the 12 questions listed above out loud, ideally with a peer. Time yourself: behavioural answers should run 2-3 minutes, analytical cases 5-8 minutes. Record one session to catch filler words and unclear transitions.

Before the interview:

Prepare 2-3 genuine questions to ask the interviewer. Strong options include: 'What does the data stack look like for the BA team?' and 'What is the biggest analytical question the team is trying to answer this quarter?' These signal real interest and help you assess whether the role is the right fit.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Giving generic answers. Saying 'I would look at the data' without naming specific metrics or tools is the most common reason candidates do not progress. Always be specific: name the metric, name the tool (SQL, Excel, Mixpanel, Amplitude), and name the stakeholder you would bring the finding to.

Not knowing the product. Candidates who cannot name a single eloelo feature give answers that feel disconnected from the company's actual context. Spend time on the app before the interview and form an opinion on what makes the product work.

Skipping the clarifying question. For case and metric questions, diving straight into an answer without asking 'what is the goal here?' signals poor problem-solving habits. Interviewers at product-first companies expect you to scope the problem before solving it.

Overstating results. Inflating the impact of past work is a fast path to rejection. Candidates report that interviewers sometimes probe with follow-up questions to test whether a cited result is real. If the data is thin or the outcome was shared across a team, say so honestly.

Treating every question as a data problem. Some questions are about stakeholder management, communication, or prioritisation. Defaulting to 'I would run an analysis' for every question misses the point. Read what the question is actually asking before you answer.

Leaving the 'any questions for us?' slot empty. Preparing no questions for the interviewer reads as low interest. Have at least 2 genuine questions ready about the team, the data infrastructure, or current product priorities.

How many rounds does the eloelo BA interview typically have?

Candidates report the process typically covers 2-4 rounds. This usually includes an initial screening call with HR or a recruiter, a technical or analytical round involving SQL or a case study, and a final round with a senior stakeholder or the hiring manager. The timeline can move quickly given eloelo's startup pace, so respond promptly to scheduling messages.

What salary can I expect for a BA role at eloelo?

eloelo does not publish salary bands publicly. Industry surveys and Glassdoor listings for BA roles at similar-stage Indian gaming startups can give you a useful reference point. Also check levels.fyi for any reported data points, and factor in the company's growth stage and any equity component when comparing offers to other opportunities.

Is SQL tested in the eloelo BA interview?

Candidates report SQL is part of most technical rounds. You should be comfortable writing queries with JOINs, GROUP BY, window functions, and subqueries. You do not need to be a data engineer, but you should be able to independently pull and manipulate data to answer a product question without relying on a separate data team.

How important is it to know eloelo's product before the interview?

Very important. Interviewers at product companies expect BAs to have a genuine point of view on the product, not just a summary from the company website. Candidates who cannot reference specific features or engagement mechanics tend to give generic answers that do not resonate. Download the app, use it across a few sessions, and form a clear opinion on what works and what could be better before you walk in.

Is there a take-home assignment in the eloelo BA process?

Some candidates report receiving a take-home data task or a live case study during the technical round. These typically involve analysing a dataset or working through a product metric question with a short presentation attached. Prepare by practising structured walkthroughs of analytical problems and by knowing how to quickly build a summary table or chart in Excel or Google Sheets.
