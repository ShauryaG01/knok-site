01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Smartsheet at a glance. Smartsheet is a US-based SaaS company that builds cloud work-management and automation software. Its platform helps businesses plan, track, and automate work across teams, and it counts many large enterprises among its customers. As of mid-2026, Smartsheet has 117 open roles spanning software engineering, product management, sales, customer success, solutions engineering, and corporate operations.

Who they hire globally. Smartsheet has offices in the US, UK, Australia, and Costa Rica, and its remote-friendly culture means strong candidates in other locations can be considered for select roles. Indian professionals with SaaS, enterprise software, or B2B experience are well-positioned to apply, particularly for engineering and customer-facing roles where demand is consistently high.

What this hiring window means for you. With 117 active openings, Smartsheet is clearly in a growth phase. Roles span from individual contributor to senior leadership, so whether you are building your SaaS career or bring years of enterprise software experience, there is likely a relevant opening worth exploring.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Smartsheet's process typically runs four to five stages, though the exact flow varies by role and team. Here is what most candidates report going through.

Stage 1: Recruiter screen. A recruiter contacts you after your application and sets up a short call covering your background, why you are interested in Smartsheet, and logistics like location, notice period, and compensation expectations. This stage is conversational and not technical.

Stage 2: Hiring manager conversation. You speak with the person who would manage you. They assess whether your experience fits the role and get a sense of how you work. For engineering roles, expect a few light technical questions. For business roles, expect scenario-based questions about how you have handled specific situations in the past.

Stage 3: Skills assessment or take-home task. Engineering candidates typically get a coding challenge on a platform or as a take-home assignment. Product and design candidates may be asked to present a case study or complete a brief exercise. Sales and customer success candidates sometimes do a short role-play scenario instead.

Stage 4: Panel or loop interviews. This is the most intensive part. You meet multiple stakeholders, which may include peers, cross-functional partners, and sometimes a senior leader. Expect a mix of behavioural questions, deeper technical or domain questions, and culture-fit conversations. For engineering roles, there is often a system design round at this stage.

Stage 5: Reference checks and offer. Smartsheet typically checks a few professional references before extending a formal offer. Have your references ready and give them a heads-up so they respond promptly. The offer letter covers base pay, equity (for eligible roles), and benefits.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Ownership and accountability. Smartsheet values people who take end-to-end responsibility for their work. In interviews, they probe for examples where you drove something to completion without being told every next step. Think of a time you spotted a problem and fixed it without being asked.

Customer empathy. Smartsheet's product is used by both non-technical business users and developers, so they want hires who genuinely care about making things easier for the end user. This shows up in product, engineering, and especially customer-facing roles.

Collaboration and clear communication. Smartsheet is a work-management tool, so it is no surprise they expect strong collaboration skills. They look for candidates who communicate clearly, give and receive feedback well, and can work across functions without friction.

Relevant technical or domain depth. For engineering roles, strong command of the languages and systems listed in the job description matters. Engineering postings at Smartsheet commonly mention skills like cloud infrastructure, distributed systems design, and modern web frameworks. For business roles, B2B SaaS experience, particularly in enterprise sales cycles or customer success, carries significant weight.

Growth mindset. Smartsheet operates in a competitive SaaS market. They favour candidates who show curiosity, a willingness to learn new tools, and a track record of adapting to change rather than waiting for stability.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product before you interview. Sign up for a Smartsheet free trial and spend time inside the platform. Understand how sheets, automations, dashboards, and integrations work. Being able to say 'I used your product and noticed X' is a strong signal in any interview.

Prepare structured stories for behavioural questions. Smartsheet uses competency-based interview questions heavily. Prepare several stories from your past work covering ownership, collaboration, conflict resolution, handling ambiguity, and customer impact. Use a clear situation, action, and result structure when you tell them.

For engineering candidates: brush up on system design and your core language. System design questions are common at the panel stage. Review concepts like scalability, caching, API design, and distributed data storage. Be ready to write clean, readable code rather than just talking through it.

For sales and customer success candidates: know your SaaS metrics. Be ready to talk about how you have managed pipelines, handled churn, or driven expansion revenue. Glassdoor and levels.fyi have candidate-reported interview questions that can give you a realistic picture of what topics come up most in business-role interviews.

Tailor your resume to the job description. Match your language to the words Smartsheet uses. If the job description says 'cross-functional collaboration', use that phrase when describing your experience, not a synonym. Applicant tracking systems score on keyword match before a human ever reads your application.

Follow up after each interview stage. Send a short thank-you note within a day of each conversation and mention one specific thing that came up in the discussion. This habit is common at US-headquartered companies and is noticed, even if it is less standard practice in India.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Built around 'work smarter'. Smartsheet's culture centres on the idea that better work processes lead to better outcomes. Employees are expected to model this in how they run their own work, not just in the product they build. People who thrive here tend to be organised, proactive, and genuinely interested in process improvement.

Inclusive and globally minded. Smartsheet publishes diversity and inclusion reports and has active employee resource groups. The company signals that it values different perspectives, and this comes through in how interviews are structured, with defined evaluation criteria rather than purely gut-feel hiring.

Remote-friendly but team-connected. A significant portion of Smartsheet employees work remotely or in hybrid setups. For Indian candidates, time-zone overlap with US teams is worth planning for. Roles that require regular US West Coast collaboration may need you to be available for at least a few hours in the afternoon IST window.

Visibility matters. Smartsheet ties performance conversations to clear outcomes and expects employees to communicate their impact proactively. If you come from a work environment where staying quiet about your achievements was the norm, expect to shift that habit here. Sharing what you have accomplished is a cultural expectation, not self-promotion.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Smartsheet hire candidates based in India?

Smartsheet does not currently have a dedicated India office, but it hires globally for remote roles across engineering, product, and some business functions. Check each job listing carefully for location requirements and whether it specifies eligible countries. If a role is listed as remote without a country restriction, it is worth applying and clarifying eligibility with the recruiter on the first call.

How long does Smartsheet's interview process usually take?

Most candidates report the full process taking several weeks from first recruiter contact to offer, though timelines vary by role and how busy the hiring team is. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds tend to run longer than business roles. Responding quickly to recruiter messages and having your references ready to respond promptly can help speed things up on your end.

What salary can I expect at Smartsheet?

Compensation at Smartsheet varies widely by role, level, and location. For India-remote roles, publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi show significant variation across functions and seniority levels. Research the specific role and level on those platforms for the most current data, and be ready to share your expectations with the recruiter during the first call.

Does Smartsheet have an employee referral programme?

Yes, like most large SaaS companies, Smartsheet has an internal employee referral programme. A referral does not guarantee an interview, but it does mean your application gets a faster human review compared to a cold apply through the careers page. If you have a connection at Smartsheet, even a LinkedIn contact, it is worth reaching out before you submit your application.

How important is it to know the Smartsheet product before interviewing?

It matters a lot, especially for customer success, solutions engineering, and product management roles. Interviewers in these functions often ask how you would explain a feature to a non-technical user or how you would help a customer solve a specific workflow problem. Spending time in a free trial before your interviews gives you concrete, hands-on examples to reference instead of speaking in generalities.
