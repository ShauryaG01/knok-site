01 Role Overview

Role Overview

An iOS Engineer builds apps for iPhones and iPads using Apple's tools and languages. In India, the role sits at the intersection of product thinking and engineering: you write Swift code, work with UIKit or SwiftUI, and ship features that millions of users tap every day.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracked 70 open iOS Engineer roles across Indian cities. Delhi leads with 21 openings, followed by Bangalore with 6, Mumbai with 3, and Chennai with 2. The role spans startups, product companies, and service firms, so your options are wider than many developers expect.

Companies typically expect you to own an entire feature end-to-end: design review, coding, writing tests, submitting to the App Store, and fixing post-release bugs. The more senior you go, the more architecture decisions land on your plate.

02 Skills You Need

Skills You Need

Language and frameworks are the foundation. Swift is the primary language for all new iOS work; Objective-C knowledge helps if you join a company with older codebases. UIKit remains common in enterprise apps, while SwiftUI is the preferred choice for new projects in 2025 and 2026.

Core iOS concepts you must know:

  • Auto Layout and responsive design for different iPhone screen sizes
  • Navigation patterns: UINavigationController, tab bars, modals
  • Networking with URLSession or third-party libraries like Alamofire
  • Persistence using Core Data, UserDefaults, or SQLite wrappers
  • Push notifications and background tasks
  • App Store submission, provisioning profiles, and code signing

Architecture patterns separate junior from mid-level engineers. MVC is the Apple default, but MVVM and Clean Architecture appear frequently in job descriptions. Understanding why patterns exist matters as much as knowing the acronyms.

Version control and collaboration mean Git fluency, pull request reviews, and working inside a CI/CD pipeline. Fastlane is commonly used for automated iOS builds in most teams.

Soft skills that Indian hiring managers specifically call out: writing clear bug reports, communicating release timelines, and working with designers who hand off Figma files.

03 Step By Step Path

Step By Step Path

  1. Set up your Mac and Xcode. iOS development requires macOS and Xcode, Apple's free IDE. Download both. A recent MacBook Air handles everyday development without issues.
  1. Learn Swift from first principles. Start with Apple's free Swift Playgrounds app or the official Swift documentation. Paul Hudson's free Swift course on Hacking with Swift walks you through the language day by day and is widely recommended by the Indian iOS community.
  1. Build your first app from scratch. Do not start with templates. Build a simple to-do list or weather app manually so you understand how views, view controllers, and data flow connect. Every iOS engineer in India has a to-do app in their past.
  1. Move to SwiftUI for new projects. Apple is clearly pushing SwiftUI as the future. Once you understand UIKit basics, spend focused time on SwiftUI's declarative syntax. The two frameworks co-exist in most real codebases, so knowing both is a job requirement by 2026.
  1. Integrate an API. Fetch data from a free public API (weather, movies, cricket scores) and display it in your app. This teaches URLSession, JSON decoding with Codable, and async/await, which are all standard interview topics.
  1. Add a database layer. Implement Core Data or a simpler solution like Realm to persist user data locally. Interviews at product companies frequently ask about offline-first design.
  1. Publish at least one app to the App Store. Even a simple utility app counts. Indian recruiters treat a live App Store link as a stronger signal than any GitHub repo, because it proves you navigated provisioning profiles, screenshots, and review guidelines.
  1. Practice data structures and algorithms in Swift. LeetCode and GeeksforGeeks both have Swift solutions. Most Indian tech companies, including service firms, run DSA rounds for iOS roles.
  1. Contribute to open source or build a portfolio project. Pick a problem you care about, solve it, and make the code public. A strong GitHub profile accelerates interview conversions, based on community feedback in iOS developer groups.
  1. Apply systematically. Use LinkedIn, Naukri, and Instahyre. Tailor your resume for each role, highlight your App Store link, and follow up with the hiring manager.
04 Timeline And Milestones

Timeline And Milestones

If you are starting from scratch with no programming background, expect to spend roughly the first two to three months learning Swift syntax and basic programming concepts before writing your first real app.

Month 1-2: Complete a structured Swift course, understand variables, functions, closures, and optionals. Build toy programs in Swift Playgrounds.

Month 3-4: Build two or three small apps using UIKit. Learn Auto Layout. Understand the Model-View-Controller pattern. Start using Git for every project.

Month 5-6: Build a SwiftUI project. Integrate a real API. Add persistence with Core Data. Polish one app enough to submit to the App Store.

Month 7-9: Start applying for junior iOS Engineer roles or internships. Keep a live App Store app. Begin DSA practice in parallel. Expect rejections early; use them to identify gaps.

Month 10-12: With interview feedback, deepen your weak areas. Architecture patterns, unit testing with XCTest, and CI/CD with Fastlane are common gaps that junior candidates report.

Year 2: With a job, focus on shipping features in a real codebase. Code review feedback from senior engineers is the fastest learning accelerator at this stage.

Year 3-4: Move toward mid-level. Own modules independently. Mentor newer joiners. Explore specialisations like ARKit, Core ML, or WidgetKit depending on your company's product direction.

If you already have a programming background in another language, compress the first phase and expect to reach the application stage by month 3 or 4.

05 India Specific Tips

India Specific Tips

College and degree reality: A computer science degree from a tier-1 or tier-2 college helps at large product companies like Flipkart, Swiggy, or Zepto, but many iOS engineers in India are self-taught or come from non-CS backgrounds. Startups care more about your App Store link than your marksheet.

Cities where jobs are concentrated: Based on the July 2026 knok data, Delhi NCR (21 openings) has the most active iOS hiring in India right now. Bangalore (6) and Mumbai (3) follow. Hyderabad and Pune showed no active roles in this snapshot, which likely reflects timing rather than a permanent gap. Chennai had 2 roles, mostly at product services companies.

Naukri and LinkedIn are the primary sourcing channels for Indian recruiters. Keep both profiles updated with your App Store link in the summary section. Naukri still dominates tier-2 city hiring; LinkedIn is stronger for product companies and startups.

Communities worth joining:

  • iOS Dev India Telegram and Slack groups have active members who share job leads and do code reviews
  • Swift meetups in Bangalore and Mumbai occasionally run online sessions open to all
  • Twitter/X has a small but active Indian iOS developer community; following senior engineers publicly signals your interest

Certification question: Apple does not offer an official iOS developer certification. Some developers list course completions on their resume; this signals learning intent but does not substitute for a live app or a strong interview performance.

Salary expectations: Salary band data for this role is not yet available in the knok dataset. For reference, Glassdoor and Levels.fyi track iOS compensation at various company types and experience levels if you want a benchmark before negotiations.

Service companies vs. product companies: TCS, Infosys, and Wipro hire iOS engineers for client projects, which gives you exposure to multiple domains but often involves slower-paced development. Product companies like Meesho, PhonePe, or Razorpay offer faster learning but are more selective. Starting at a service company is a valid path if you use the time to build personal projects on the side.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a Mac to become an iOS engineer?

Yes, Xcode runs only on macOS, and Apple requires macOS to submit apps to the App Store. A used or refurbished MacBook Air or MacBook Pro is the most common starting point for Indian developers. Cloud Mac services exist but are generally not recommended for daily development because of latency and ongoing cost.

Should I learn SwiftUI or UIKit first?

Learn UIKit basics first so you understand how Apple's view system works under the hood. Most existing codebases in Indian companies still use UIKit heavily. Once you have that foundation, invest seriously in SwiftUI because it is the direction Apple is pushing and new projects in 2025 and 2026 tend to start with it.

Is iOS development a good career choice in India compared to Android?

Android has more job openings in India overall because Android phones dominate market share. However, iOS roles tend to cluster at product companies where app quality is a priority, which can mean more interesting work. Both are strong career paths; the choice depends more on which platform you enjoy building for than on pure job volume.

How important is DSA for iOS engineer interviews in India?

Very important at large tech companies and service firms. Most Indian companies run dedicated DSA screening rounds regardless of the specialisation. Product-focused startups sometimes skip or shorten DSA rounds in favour of take-home iOS projects, but it is safer to prepare for both. Practice in Swift rather than switching to another language mid-interview.

Can I get an iOS engineer job without a computer science degree?

Yes, many working iOS engineers in India are self-taught or switched from other disciplines. What matters most to recruiters is a live App Store app, a clean GitHub profile, and passing the technical rounds. A degree from a recognised college helps at larger companies that filter by education, but it is not a hard requirement at startups.

How do I stand out when applying for iOS roles on Naukri and LinkedIn?

Put your App Store link in the very first line of your resume summary and your LinkedIn headline. Recruiters spend seconds scanning profiles, and a live app is immediately credible. In addition, tailor your resume to match keywords in the job description, and send a short personalised note to the hiring manager rather than only clicking Apply through the portal.
