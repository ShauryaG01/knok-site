01 Role Overview

Role Overview

Platform Engineers build and maintain the internal developer platform: the Kubernetes clusters, CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and self-service tooling that product teams rely on to ship code without waiting for manual approvals. The role sits at the intersection of DevOps, site reliability engineering, and cloud infrastructure, with a specific focus on improving developer experience and deployment velocity.

Demand in India is growing steadily. Knok jobradar data from July 2026 counted 204 active Platform Engineer openings across the country. Bangalore leads with 29 openings, Delhi has 12, Pune has 10, Hyderabad has 5, Chennai has 2, and Mumbai has 1. A large share of listings are remote or hybrid, which is why city-tagged openings add up to much less than the national total.

Companies hiring span product startups, global capability centres (GCCs) of large multinationals, and IT services firms building out cloud practices. The role rewards engineers who can think both like a developer (building good internal tooling) and like an operator (keeping systems reliable and scalable at production load).

02 Skills You Need

Skills You Need

Containers and orchestration
Docker and Kubernetes are the core. Kubernetes knowledge needs to go beyond basic deployments: Helm charts, RBAC, network policies, storage classes, and cluster upgrades all come up in interviews and on the job.

Infrastructure as Code
Terraform is the standard. Writing reusable modules, managing remote state, and reviewing infrastructure pull requests are daily activities in most Platform Engineering teams. Ansible is a useful addition for configuration management.

CI/CD and GitOps
GitHub Actions and Jenkins are the most common pipeline tools in Indian tech stacks. ArgoCD is increasingly requested for GitOps-style delivery. Being able to design a pipeline from code commit to production deployment is a core skill.

Cloud platforms
AWS is the most in-demand cloud across Indian job listings. GCP appears frequently in GCCs and data-heavy companies. Go deep on one cloud first, covering networking, IAM, and managed Kubernetes services, then branch out only after you have genuine depth.

Linux and scripting
Bash scripting and Python are essential for automation. Comfortable Linux administration, covering permissions, networking, systemd, and log analysis, is a baseline expectation even in senior interviews.

Observability
Prometheus, Grafana, and either the ELK stack or OpenSearch for log management form the standard set. Setting up dashboards, creating alerts, and investigating incidents using metrics and traces separates solid candidates from strong ones.

Developer empathy
Platform Engineering is a service role inside an engineering organisation. Clear documentation, responsive support, and the ability to explain infrastructure decisions to developers who are not infra-minded matter as much as technical depth.

03 Step By Step Path

Step By Step Path

1. Build a Linux foundation
Get comfortable with the command line, file permissions, processes, networking basics, and Bash scripting. Every other skill on this roadmap builds on top of Linux comfort.

2. Learn Docker thoroughly
Write Dockerfiles, build images, run multi-container setups with Docker Compose, and understand how container networking works. Containerise a project you can show on GitHub.

3. Go deep on Kubernetes
Work through the official Kubernetes documentation and a structured hands-on course. Cover Pods, Deployments, Services, Ingress, ConfigMaps, Secrets, RBAC, and Helm. Run a local cluster with Minikube or kind. Kubernetes is the skill that unlocks most Platform Engineering roles in India.

4. Get certified on one cloud
AWS is the safest first choice given Indian market demand. Target the AWS Solutions Architect Associate certificate while working through hands-on labs: VPC, EC2, IAM, S3, and EKS are the most relevant services.

5. Learn Terraform by building real infrastructure
Write modules for a VPC, an EKS cluster, and a database. Manage remote state. Version your infrastructure code on GitHub. Practical Terraform projects are what recruiters want to see, not just course completion badges.

6. Build a CI/CD pipeline from scratch
Use GitHub Actions to build, test, and deploy a containerised app to Kubernetes. Add ArgoCD for GitOps delivery. This single project, done properly, demonstrates more than most certifications.

7. Add observability to your cluster
Deploy Prometheus and Grafana into your Kubernetes cluster. Build a dashboard tracking pod health, CPU, and memory. Set up at least one alert. This mirrors real Platform Engineering work and strengthens your portfolio significantly.

8. Document and share your work
Write clear GitHub READMEs and one or two LinkedIn posts explaining what you built. Indian recruiters do check GitHub portfolios when shortlisting. Writing also forces you to understand your own projects well enough to explain them in interviews.

9. Sit the CKA exam
The Certified Kubernetes Administrator is the most recognised credential for Platform Engineering roles in India. It is a practical, hands-on exam, so only attempt it after you are genuinely comfortable with steps 3 and 6.

10. Apply before you feel ready
Tailor your resume keywords to each job description. Reach out to Platform Engineers on LinkedIn for short conversations. Each interview round shows you exactly what to sharpen next.

04 Timeline And Milestones

Timeline And Milestones

Timelines depend on your starting point, but here is a realistic path for someone moving from a developer, sysadmin, or junior DevOps background:

Months 1 to 3: Foundation
Linux, Docker, and introductory cloud. By the end of this phase you can explain containers clearly, run a basic cloud setup, and have your first project on GitHub.

Months 3 to 6: Kubernetes and cloud certification
Deep Kubernetes practice and the AWS Solutions Architect Associate exam. This is where most of the hard learning happens. Do not rush it.

Months 6 to 9: Terraform and pipelines
Build real Terraform projects and a full CI/CD pipeline that deploys to Kubernetes. Your GitHub portfolio starts looking like actual Platform Engineering work by the end of this phase.

Months 9 to 12: Observability, CKA, and first applications
Add observability to your project, sit the CKA exam, and begin applying. Many candidates land their first Platform Engineer role in this window.

Year 2: First role, learning fast
Focus on absorbing how your team manages releases, handles incidents, and makes architecture decisions. Contribute to internal tooling and build a reputation for making developers' lives easier.

Year 3 onward: Mid-level and senior
Industry surveys commonly cite strong compensation growth for engineers who combine hands-on Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud experience with platform design skills. Senior roles involve owning platform architecture, defining reliability targets, and mentoring junior engineers.

05 India Specific Tips

India Specific Tips

Where to search for jobs
Naukri and LinkedIn are both essential. Naukri uses keyword matching heavily, so include 'Kubernetes', 'Terraform', 'Platform Engineering', and 'CI/CD' explicitly in your profile. LinkedIn matters more for GCC and product company roles at mid-to-senior levels.

Certifications Indian recruiters recognise
The CKA is the most valued credential for this role in the Indian market. AWS certifications, starting with the Solutions Architect Associate, are recognised across companies of all sizes. The Google Cloud Professional DevOps Engineer certificate is a strong option if you are targeting GCP-heavy companies or GCCs.

Communities worth joining
The CNCF (Cloud Native Computing Foundation) India chapter runs meetups in Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Pune. DevOps India and Platform Engineering India groups on LinkedIn are active. Attending meetups occasionally leads to referrals, which can bypass the Naukri keyword filter entirely.

The college background reality
A CS or IT engineering degree helps in initial screening at some companies, but it is not a hard barrier. What matters most in shortlisting is demonstrable, hands-on skill: a working GitHub portfolio, a CKA cert, or a referral consistently outweighs a college pedigree at product companies and GCCs.

Open source as a differentiator
Contributing to CNCF projects, even through documentation or issue triage, is a signal that senior hiring managers at product companies notice. It is not required, but it strengthens a profile meaningfully for roles above the junior level.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the difference between a Platform Engineer and a DevOps Engineer in India?

DevOps Engineers often own the full delivery pipeline, including deployment scripts, environment management, and sometimes production support. Platform Engineers focus specifically on building the internal platform that other engineers use, treating developer experience as a product to maintain and improve. In India, the two titles overlap significantly at smaller companies, but at larger product firms and GCCs the Platform Engineer role is distinct: you build the rails others run on, rather than running deployments yourself.

Which certifications matter most for Platform Engineers in India?

The CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) is the most recognised credential for this role in the Indian job market. AWS certifications, starting with the Solutions Architect Associate, are valued across companies of all sizes. If your target companies use GCP, the Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer certificate is a strong addition. Most experienced Platform Engineers hold at least one cloud cert and the CKA.

Do I need a CS degree to become a Platform Engineer in India?

A CS or IT engineering degree helps in initial resume screening at some companies, particularly larger IT services firms. However, many Platform Engineers in India have moved into the role from non-CS backgrounds through self-study, certifications, and portfolio projects. At product companies and GCCs, hands-on skill shown through a strong GitHub profile and the CKA consistently matters more than college background during actual hiring decisions.

What salary can I expect as a Platform Engineer in India?

Salary data for this specific role in India varies widely by company type, city, and experience level. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list compensation ranges for Platform Engineer roles at Indian companies, and industry surveys commonly cite higher bands for engineers with proven Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud experience at mid and senior levels. GCCs and product companies generally pay above IT services firms for equivalent experience. Checking Glassdoor for specific company names you are targeting gives the most accurate current picture.

Is Bangalore the only strong city for Platform Engineering jobs in India?

Bangalore has the highest concentration of openings, with 29 out of 204 listings in knok jobradar data from July 2026, but Delhi (12) and Pune (10) also have active markets. A meaningful portion of the 204 total openings are fully remote or hybrid, meaning city of residence matters less than it did a few years ago. Hyderabad and Chennai are smaller markets for this specific role but are growing, particularly as GCCs expand there.

How do I transition from a software developer role to Platform Engineering?

Start by learning Kubernetes and Docker alongside your current job, then build a side project that uses Terraform to deploy a containerised application to a cloud environment. The CKA certification is a credible signal to hiring teams that you have moved beyond developer-only skills. On your resume, highlight any infrastructure, deployment pipeline, or automation work you have already done in your developer role, since this context helps recruiters see you as a viable candidate.
