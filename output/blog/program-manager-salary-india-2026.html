01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Technical Program Manager listings in India do not publish a salary range. Of the 313 TPM roles tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026, pay was not disclosed for the vast majority, which is a common pattern across Indian job boards. Employers typically prefer to negotiate compensation after shortlisting, so job postings alone tell you very little about what the role actually pays.

For realistic benchmarks, publicly reported data from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys is more reliable than what appears in listings. TPM pay in India spans a very wide band, from entry-level positions at IT services firms to Principal or Staff TPM roles at product companies with global pay scales. An 'average' figure is often misleading without knowing the company tier, city, and years of relevant experience.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because salary bands were not disclosed in this dataset, the figures below draw on publicly reported data from Glassdoor and levels.fyi. Treat them as directional ranges, not guarantees.

Entry-level TPM (0-3 years in the role): Glassdoor commonly cites 15-25 LPA for first-time TPMs at product and IT services companies. Most candidates at this stage are transitioning from software engineering, QA, or business analysis roles.

Mid-level TPM (4-7 years): Glassdoor and levels.fyi publicly report a range of 25-45 LPA at mid-to-large product firms and Indian offices of global tech companies. Domain specialisation in cloud infrastructure, fintech, or platform engineering tends to push the number toward the higher end.

Senior TPM (8-12 years): Publicly reported figures on levels.fyi for senior and lead TPMs at top-tier tech companies in India commonly range from 45-80 LPA. The spread is wide because stock grants vary significantly across firms.

Principal TPM or Director of Program Management: levels.fyi publicly reports total compensation above 80 LPA at FAANG-equivalent employers, with a significant portion coming from ESOPs or RSUs rather than base salary.

Listings won't tell you what a role pays, but knok will tell you which ones you're competitive for.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Do your homework before the call. Before any salary discussion, check publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company tier you are targeting. A data point from a named source is far stronger than a gut feeling when countering an offer.

Ask for the full breakup in writing. Many companies structure TPM offers with a significant variable pay or stock component. Comparing base salaries across two offers without knowing the variable element can give you a distorted picture. Get every component itemised before you compare.

Anchor on market, not on current salary. Several Indian states have issued advisories against mandatory salary history disclosure. Even where it is legally permitted, you are not obligated to share it. Frame your ask around what the role is worth in the market, citing Glassdoor or levels.fyi if pressed.

Negotiate the whole package. If the base is fixed, there is often room on joining bonus, stock vesting schedule, remote work allowance, or the timing of your first performance review. These can add real value even when the headline number does not move.

Get competing offers if you can. A genuine competing offer is the single most effective negotiation tool. Even one alternative gives you real leverage to ask a company to match or improve.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

TPM compensation at Indian product companies typically has four components. Understanding each helps you evaluate an offer accurately.

Base salary: The fixed monthly amount. This is what most people focus on, but at senior levels it is often the smallest growth lever compared to stock.

Variable pay or performance bonus: Usually expressed as a percentage of base, paid annually or semi-annually. Industry surveys indicate that actual payout depends on both individual ratings and company performance, so ask about the average payout history in recent years, not just the target number on paper.

ESOPs or RSUs (stock grants): At product companies and well-funded startups, stock can form a significant share of total compensation. RSUs at listed companies vest on a multi-year schedule and carry a known current value. Startup ESOPs involve liquidity risk and should be valued conservatively until there is a clear liquidity event.

Joining bonus: A one-time payment, sometimes used to offset unvested stock you are leaving behind at your current employer. It is typically clawed back if you exit in the first year, so read the clause carefully before signing.

When comparing two offers, convert everything to a single annual number using the current stock price, not the theoretical value at grant date.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most TPM job listings in India not show a salary range?

Indian labour law does not require companies to disclose pay in job postings, and most choose not to. Employers prefer flexibility to negotiate individually based on your current CTC, competing offers, and internal band constraints. This is why Glassdoor and levels.fyi are more useful for benchmarking TPM pay in India than job boards are. Third-party publicly reported data at least gives you a range to anchor on before you enter any conversation.

Is the TPM role well established in India, or is it mostly at MNC offices?

The TPM title is most common at Indian offices of US-origin tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and similar), and at homegrown product companies that have adopted US-style engineering org structures. IT services firms sometimes use the title but the scope and pay can differ significantly from a product-company TPM role. Of the 313 listings in this dataset, the bulk are in Bangalore, Delhi, Pune, and Hyderabad, which matches the product and GCC cluster in those cities.

How is a TPM different from a Project Manager or a Product Manager?

A Technical Program Manager coordinates the delivery of large, cross-team engineering programmes. Unlike a Project Manager, a TPM is expected to go deep on technical architecture, dependencies, and engineering risk. Unlike a Product Manager, a TPM does not own the product roadmap or vision, but owns delivery coordination across multiple engineering teams. The three roles overlap at smaller companies, which is why job descriptions vary widely and salary comparisons can be tricky.

What skills make a TPM profile stand out for higher pay?

Companies consistently pay more for TPMs who have hands-on engineering backgrounds, especially in cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, or platform engineering. Experience managing multi-team programmes with hard deadlines (regulatory launches, major product releases) is valued over pure coordination experience. Certifications like PMP are sometimes listed as a requirement, but publicly reported offers on levels.fyi suggest they do not move the number much compared to demonstrated technical depth and cross-functional leadership.

Should I disclose my current CTC during the hiring process?

You are generally not required to share your current salary, and several Indian states have issued advisories recommending against mandatory disclosure. Frame your expectation around market data from Glassdoor and levels.fyi rather than anchoring to your existing CTC, especially if you are moving from an IT services role to a product company where pay scales differ significantly. Sharing your current salary early can cap the offer below the actual band for the role.
