01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

NxtWave is one of India's fastest-growing ed-tech companies, best known for its CCBP (Continuous Capacity Building Program), which trains students in full-stack development, data science, and other in-demand tech skills. The company hires across a wide range of functions: Business Development Associates (BDA), Content and Curriculum Developers, Software Engineers, Student Success Managers, and Operations staff.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 26 open roles at NxtWave. The majority of openings lean toward business development and student support, but tech and content roles appear regularly too.

NxtWave is particularly welcoming to fresh graduates and early-career professionals. If you have strong communication skills and genuinely believe in making quality tech education accessible across India, you are already a good fit on paper. The company grows fast, so people who join early often move up quickly.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The exact number of rounds can vary by role and the hiring team, so treat this as a general guide based on publicly reported candidate experiences.

Stage 1: Online Application
Apply through NxtWave's careers page or a job portal like Naukri or LinkedIn. For BDA roles, your communication skills and attitude carry more weight than your college branch or specialisation. For tech roles, include links to any projects or a GitHub profile.

Stage 2: Aptitude Test or Video Submission
Many candidates report an online test covering logical reasoning and English comprehension. For Business Development roles specifically, you may be asked to record a short self-introduction video or respond to a sales scenario on camera. This is NxtWave's way of checking your spoken clarity and on-camera confidence before calling you in.

Stage 3: HR Screening Call
A recruiter calls to confirm your availability, location preference, and interest in the role. Expect questions like 'Why ed-tech?' and 'Why NxtWave specifically?' Have a genuine answer ready. Compensation expectations typically come up here too.

Stage 4: Technical or Functional Round
For engineering and content roles, expect questions on domain knowledge, past projects, and problem-solving. For BDA roles, this round usually tests your ability to pitch NxtWave's program to a student, handle common objections, and show that you can build rapport quickly under pressure.

Stage 5: Final Managerial Round
A senior manager or team lead conducts this round. Expect situational and behavioural questions: 'Tell me about a time you handled rejection' or 'How would you motivate a hesitant student?' This is also your chance to ask about growth paths, team structure, and what success looks like in the first few months.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Communication above everything else
NxtWave's BDA and student success teams speak with students from every corner of India. Clear, confident spoken English is a baseline requirement. Comfort in Hindi or a regional language is a real plus for many roles.

Sales drive and resilience for BDA roles
Business Development Associates handle a high volume of student counselling calls. Interviewers want to see that you can build trust quickly, handle a 'no' without losing energy, and genuinely help a student make the right decision for their career rather than just closing a number.

Mission alignment
Candidates who can explain why accessible tech education matters, and connect that to their own story or experience, tend to stand out. NxtWave interviews almost always include a 'Why us?' question, and a generic answer will not land well.

Learning agility
NxtWave's product, targets, and processes evolve fast. They look for people who pick up new tools and scripts without needing to be hand-held through every change.

Hunger over pedigree
NxtWave hires heavily from Tier 2 and Tier 3 colleges. Your college name matters far less than your energy, attitude, and willingness to work hard through the initial learning curve.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research NxtWave's product thoroughly
Go through the CCBP website and understand what the program covers, what it promises students, and how it is structured. In a BDA interview, you will almost certainly be asked to pitch the course to the interviewer playing a hesitant student. You cannot do that convincingly if you do not know the product inside out.

Practise a confident self-introduction on camera
For roles with a video round, record yourself speaking for one to two minutes. Watch it back. Check for filler words, eye contact with the camera, and pace. Do this repeatedly until it feels natural rather than rehearsed.

Prepare specific answers for 'Why NxtWave?' and 'Why ed-tech?'
These questions come up in almost every round. Connect your answer to something real: a gap you noticed in your own education, a desire to work at a mission-driven company, or the energy of a fast-growing startup. Vague answers about 'passion for education' will not stand out.

Practise objection-handling for BDA roles
Common student objections include 'I cannot afford it right now', 'I am not sure about placements', and 'I need to think about it'. Practise responding to these with empathy and specific information, not just by repeating the pitch more loudly.

Do a few aptitude warm-up sets
The online test is not very difficult, but do a handful of logical reasoning and reading comprehension practice sets before the day. Walking in cold is avoidable.

Prepare smart questions for the final round
Ask about your first few months on the job, how performance is measured, and what the growth path looks like from your role. Avoid making salary the first and only topic in early rounds.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

NxtWave runs at startup pace. The culture is target-driven, especially for the BDA team, where daily and monthly call and conversion targets are a real and visible part of the job. Publicly reported reviews describe the environment as high-energy and sometimes intense, particularly around month-end.

On the positive side, many employees who joined early report rapid promotions and meaningful responsibility within their first year. The company is still young, which means there is less bureaucracy and more room to take initiative compared to a large IT services firm.

NxtWave puts a genuine emphasis on student outcomes. Team meetings and internal communication often circle back to student success stories, which many employees find motivating. If you care about the mission, that energy is reported as real and not just a poster on the wall.

NxtWave's main base is in Hyderabad, with hiring spread across multiple cities. Work-from-home flexibility varies by role and team. BDA and student success roles tend to be more office-based or city-specific, while some content and engineering roles have offered hybrid arrangements. Confirm the specific model for your role with the recruiter before accepting.

Does NxtWave hire freshers with no prior work experience?

Yes, NxtWave is one of the more fresher-friendly companies in the Indian ed-tech space. Many BDA and Student Success Manager roles are explicitly open to fresh graduates. What matters most is your communication skills, attitude, and willingness to learn. A decent academic record helps, but it is not a strict filter and your college tier is rarely a barrier.

How many interview rounds does NxtWave typically have?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, most roles involve three to five rounds: an online aptitude test or video submission, an HR screening call, a functional or technical interview, and a final managerial round. Some roles may combine stages. The exact count can vary by role and the hiring team, so ask your recruiter what to expect after the first round.

What is the salary for BDA roles at NxtWave?

Glassdoor and publicly reported candidate reviews suggest BDA compensation at NxtWave includes a fixed base plus a performance-linked variable tied to monthly targets.
