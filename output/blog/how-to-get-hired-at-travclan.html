01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

TravClan is a B2B travel technology company that powers bookings for travel agents across India. Its platform gives travel agents access to hotels, flights, and holiday packages through a single interface, and the company earns from the transaction volume its agent network generates. The focus is squarely on the trade side of travel, not direct-to-consumer bookings.

As of July 2026, TravClan has 53 open roles across functions including engineering, product, growth, sales, account management, and operations. This level of active hiring points to a company in a clear scaling phase. Roles span junior to senior levels, so both freshers from travel or tech backgrounds and experienced professionals have a realistic shot at finding something relevant.

Hiring at TravClan runs through their careers page, LinkedIn, and a handful of job boards. Referrals from current employees carry meaningful weight, as they do at most startups at this stage. Reaching out to TravClan employees on LinkedIn before you apply is a smart first move. Even a brief introductory message can help your application stand out in a busy pipeline.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Most candidates at TravClan go through three to five rounds, though the exact number varies by role and seniority. Here is what the typical pipeline looks like, based on patterns common at travel tech startups of this size.

Round 1: Application screening. The talent team reviews your resume for relevant experience. For tech roles this is primarily about your stack and past projects. For sales and operations roles, they look for domain exposure in travel, hospitality, or B2B services.

Round 2: HR or recruiter call. This is a standard screening call to check basic fit, salary expectations, notice period, and your understanding of what TravClan does. Be ready to explain why you want to work in travel tech specifically. Vague answers about 'passion for travel' do not land well here.

Round 3: Technical or domain interview. For engineers, expect a coding or system design discussion. For product managers, you may get a case study around a travel booking flow. For sales and account management roles, this round often involves a scenario question about handling a travel agent or a B2B client objection.

Round 4: Manager or cross-functional round. You will meet a team lead or a cross-functional stakeholder. Questions here tend to probe how you think through problems, how you collaborate, and how you have handled pressure or ambiguity in past roles.

Round 5: Founder or leadership round (for senior roles). Senior hires commonly report an additional conversation with a co-founder or department head. Expect a broader discussion about your vision for the function and your overall fit with where the company is going.

Offers typically follow fairly soon after the final round, though timelines can stretch if multiple candidates are being evaluated at the same time.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

TravClan wants people who understand or are genuinely excited about the travel industry, not just people looking for any tech or sales job. Here are the traits and skills that come up consistently across functions.

Travel or B2B domain awareness. For non-engineering roles especially, having worked with travel agents, in hospitality, or in a B2B services company gives you a real edge. If you do not have direct travel experience, showing that you have done genuine homework on how the trade travel industry works matters nearly as much.

Ownership and speed. The team is lean. They want people who pick up tasks without waiting for direction and who see things through to completion. Expect interviewers to ask about times you took initiative or moved fast to fix a problem without a clear playbook.

Clear communication. Whether you are pitching to a travel agent, writing a product spec, or explaining a bug, TravClan values directness. If you tend to over-explain or rely on jargon, practice trimming that before your interviews.

Commercial thinking. Even for engineering roles, showing that you understand how the product makes money and who the end customer is will set you apart. TravClan operates in a margin-sensitive industry, so cost-awareness and revenue impact matter across all teams, not just in sales.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product before your first call. Spend time reading about TravClan's platform and the problem it solves: a single booking interface that helps independent travel agents access hotel, flight, and package inventory that was previously spread across dozens of suppliers. Know who their users are and what those agents gain by using the platform.

Brush up on B2B travel basics. Terms like GDS, hotel contracting, net rates, and agent commission structures come up in conversations. You do not need to be an expert, but familiarity signals that you are serious about the space and not treating this as a generic job application.

Prepare your 'why travel tech' answer. Interviewers will push on motivation. Connect your answer to the specific problem TravClan is solving: giving smaller travel agents access to inventory and booking tools that were once only available to large operators. The more specific your answer, the more it lands.

Practice scenario-style questions. For product, sales, and ops roles, prepare examples where you solved a customer problem, handled a difficult stakeholder, or improved a process. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) but keep answers tight. Interviewers will redirect you if you ramble.

Sort out your logistics before the first call. Know your expected CTC range and your notice period before the recruiter asks. Startups move fast and appreciate candidates who are equally ready. Hesitating on basics signals that you have not thought the move through.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

TravClan operates with the energy of an early-stage startup even as it scales. Teams are lean, which means you will often wear multiple hats. A sales manager might also run onboarding for new agents. An engineer might sit in on product planning sessions. If you thrive in structured environments with clearly defined role boundaries, that is worth thinking through before you join.

Pace and ambiguity. Processes are still being built. That is an opportunity if you like shaping how things work, but it can feel chaotic if you need detailed playbooks to operate effectively.

Travel-first mindset. The team genuinely cares about improving how travel agents work. You will find more enthusiasm in conversations about the industry than about the tech stack or the org chart. People who share that curiosity tend to fit in faster.

Direct feedback culture. Feedback tends to be given quickly and without much softening. Disagreement is expected to surface in meetings, not after them. If you prefer a more formal feedback structure, adjust your expectations accordingly.

Room to grow. Because the company is actively scaling, people who deliver tend to take on broader scope relatively quickly. Several early team members have moved into leadership positions as the company expanded its agent network and product offering.

Frequently asked

How many interview rounds does TravClan typically have?

Most candidates go through three to five rounds depending on the role and seniority. Junior individual contributor roles often have three rounds: a screening call, a skills round, and a manager conversation. Senior or cross-functional roles typically add a founder or leadership conversation at the end. The full process usually wraps up within a few weeks of applying, though timelines vary by how many candidates are in the pipeline.

Do I need prior travel industry experience to get hired at TravClan?

It depends on the function. For sales, account management, and operations roles, prior experience in travel, hospitality, or B2B services gives you a noticeable edge and is often a stated preference in job descriptions. For engineering and product roles, travel experience is helpful but not always required. Showing genuine curiosity about the industry and the problems travel agents face goes a long way regardless of your background.

Does TravClan hire freshers or entry-level candidates?

Yes, TravClan does hire freshers, particularly for operations, inside sales, and travel support roles. With 53 open roles as of July 2026, there is a spread of seniority levels across functions. Freshers should highlight any internships in travel, hospitality, or B2B environments, and be ready to show genuine enthusiasm for the industry alongside basic functional skills.

How long does it take to hear back after applying to TravClan?

Shortlisted candidates often hear back within a couple of weeks. If you do not hear back after a reasonable wait, a polite follow-up to the recruiter on LinkedIn is appropriate and expected. TravClan, like most startups, sometimes has roles close quickly or shift in priority, so following up once shows initiative without coming across as pushy.

What roles are most actively hiring at TravClan right now?

With 53 open roles as of July 2026, TravClan appears to be hiring across sales, account management, engineering, product, and operations simultaneously. How should I talk about salary expectations at TravClan?

Come into the conversation knowing your current CTC and a target range. Publicly reported ranges for similar roles at travel tech startups can be found on Glassdoor, which gives you a reasonable benchmark to reference. Be straightforward with the recruiter early about your expectations rather than leaving it until the offer stage. Most startup recruiters appreciate directness, and it saves time on both sides.
