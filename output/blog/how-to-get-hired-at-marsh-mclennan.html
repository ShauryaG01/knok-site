01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Marsh McLennan is one of the world's leading professional services firms, operating through four major businesses: Marsh (insurance broking and risk management), Guy Carpenter (reinsurance), Mercer (HR consulting and investment management), and Oliver Wyman (management consulting). In India, the company runs significant operations through Mercer and Marsh India, with offices in cities including Mumbai, Noida, Bengaluru, and Chennai.

As of July 2026, Marsh McLennan has 70 open roles across its businesses. These span consulting, data analytics, technology, finance, HR, and client service functions, ranging from fresh-graduate analyst positions to experienced senior consultant and manager levels.

Getting hired here is competitive but structured. The company values analytical thinking, domain knowledge in risk or HR consulting, and strong communication skills. If you are targeting a role in insurance, risk management, investment consulting, or people analytics, Marsh McLennan is one of the most credible employers to aim for.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process typically unfolds across several stages, though the exact number of rounds varies by business unit (Marsh, Mercer, Guy Carpenter, or Oliver Wyman) and seniority level.

Application and Resume Screening
You apply through the Marsh McLennan careers portal with your resume and sometimes a cover letter. Recruiters screen for relevant experience, educational background, and role fit. For tech and analytics roles, automated screening may flag keywords from your profile.

HR Screening Call
A recruiter or HR business partner reaches out for a short call. Expect questions about your background, why you want to join Marsh McLennan specifically, your salary expectations, and your notice period. This is also a good time to clarify role scope and team structure.

Technical or Domain Interview
This round focuses on your core skills. For consulting roles, especially Oliver Wyman, this often includes a case study or structured business problem. For Mercer roles in HR consulting or investments, expect questions on compensation benchmarking, actuarial concepts, or portfolio analysis depending on your function. Tech roles typically involve a coding or data assessment.

Behavioral and Competency Round
One or more panel interviews with team leads or senior managers follow. Interviewers use structured behavioral questions, asking for specific examples from your past work. The STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) works well here.

Final Round
For senior roles, a final conversation with a director or business head is common. For analyst-level positions, this may be combined with the behavioral round. Offers typically follow within a week or two after the final stage, though timelines can vary by team.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Marsh McLennan hires across four distinct businesses, so priorities vary, but a few qualities matter everywhere.

Analytical thinking: Whether you are solving a client's risk exposure problem or modelling employee compensation data, strong quantitative and structured reasoning skills matter. Be ready to walk through your thinking, not just your conclusions.

Domain knowledge: For Mercer roles, familiarity with HR consulting, actuarial science, or investment concepts is a strong advantage. For Marsh roles, understanding of insurance products and risk advisory counts. For Oliver Wyman, prior consulting experience or a strong academic record in economics, finance, or strategy helps considerably.

Communication: Marsh McLennan works with large corporate clients and senior stakeholders. Clear written and verbal communication, the ability to simplify complex topics, and client-facing confidence are valued across all roles.

Collaboration: Many India-based roles support global clients or work alongside teams in the US, UK, and Asia Pacific. People who work well across teams and time zones stand out.

Attention to detail: In businesses like insurance broking and investment consulting, accuracy is non-negotiable. Interviewers notice candidates who double-check their work and spot inconsistencies.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the specific business you are applying to
Marsh McLennan has four distinct operating companies. Read up on the one your role sits in, understand who its clients are and how it makes money, and know how it differs from the others. Showing you understand whether you are joining Mercer versus Oliver Wyman signals genuine interest, not a generic application.

Prepare for case and scenario questions
Even for non-consulting roles, expect scenario-based thinking. For Oliver Wyman roles, practise structured case interviews. For Mercer, brush up on HR consulting frameworks, total rewards methodology, or investment fund structures depending on your track.

Have your STAR stories ready
Pick five to seven strong examples from your past work covering: analytical problem-solving, handling ambiguity, client or stakeholder management, and working under pressure. Practise delivering each in two to three minutes.

Follow the industry
Risk management, insurance, and HR consulting are being reshaped by AI and data tools. Reading about trends in Marsh McLennan's sectors helps you ask sharper questions and connect your skills to current business needs.

Prepare smart questions
Interviewers notice when candidates ask thoughtful questions about team structure, client portfolios, or career growth paths. Avoid questions you could answer with a quick search.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Marsh McLennan describes its culture as client-first, inclusive, and values-driven. Employees across India offices commonly describe a professional, process-oriented environment where expertise is respected and cross-team collaboration is expected.

The firm has made public commitments to diversity and inclusion, reflected in its hiring practices and employee resource groups. For Indian candidates, Marsh McLennan's India operations are substantial, with teams that handle meaningful analytical and advisory work, not just back-office support.

The work pace tends to be structured rather than startup-chaotic. Deadlines are real and client commitments are taken seriously, but the environment is generally described as more measured than investment banking or high-growth tech companies. Growth paths within Mercer India in particular are considered clear and fairly well-defined.

The company places emphasis on continuous learning, and internal mobility between businesses is possible and encouraged for high performers. This makes Marsh McLennan an attractive long-term option if you want to build deep expertise in risk, consulting, or HR services rather than jumping employers every two years.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many interview rounds does Marsh McLennan typically have?

Most candidates go through three to five rounds: an HR screening call, a technical or domain interview, a behavioral round, and sometimes a final conversation with senior leadership. The exact count varies by business unit and role level. Oliver Wyman roles tend to have more structured rounds including case interviews, while analytics or support roles may have fewer.

What salary can I expect at Marsh McLennan India?

Glassdoor and publicly reported data suggest analyst and associate-level roles at Marsh McLennan India are commonly cited in the range of 8-18 LPA, while senior consultants and managers are reported higher, though these figures vary significantly by business unit, city, and years of experience. Sample sizes on public platforms are limited, so treat these as a starting reference rather than a guarantee. Always cross-check with recent Glassdoor reviews before negotiating, and factor in the bonus and benefits structure alongside base pay.

Is Marsh McLennan a good company for freshers or recent graduates?

Yes, Marsh McLennan does hire fresh graduates, particularly through campus recruitment for Mercer and select tech roles. Freshers typically enter as analysts or associates and go through structured onboarding and training. Competition for campus roles is high, so a strong academic record and relevant internship experience help. If you want to build a career in consulting, HR services, or risk management, it is a solid foundation.

How long does the Marsh McLennan hiring process take?

The process typically takes three to six weeks from application to offer, though it can stretch longer for senior roles or during high-volume hiring periods. HR screening calls are usually scheduled within one to two weeks of applying if your profile is shortlisted. Final offers tend to come within a week or two after the last interview round, though this varies by team and location.

What is the difference between applying to Marsh, Mercer, Guy Carpenter, and Oliver Wyman?

All four are part of Marsh McLennan but operate as separate businesses with different clients, work cultures, and interview styles. Marsh focuses on insurance broking and risk advisory. Mercer covers HR consulting, retirement benefits, and investment services. Guy Carpenter specialises in reinsurance. Oliver Wyman is a top-tier management consulting firm with a rigorous case interview process. When you apply, tailor your resume and preparation to the specific business, not just 'Marsh McLennan' generically.

Does Marsh McLennan hire experienced professionals laterally?

Yes, and a significant share of hiring is lateral. With 70 open roles as of July 2026, many positions are mid-to-senior level. Professionals from insurance, actuarial, investment management, HR technology, management consulting, and data analytics backgrounds are frequently hired. Lateral candidates are expected to contribute quickly, so demonstrating immediate domain value matters more than for campus hires.
