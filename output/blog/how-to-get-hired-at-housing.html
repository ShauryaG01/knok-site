01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Housing.com is one of India's leading proptech platforms, built to help home buyers, renters, and sellers find their next property. The company operates across real estate search, home loans, and adjacent services, and has steadily grown its footprint across Indian cities.

As of mid-2026, Housing has 188 open roles across engineering, product, sales, marketing, and operations. That level of hiring activity signals the company is in an expansion phase, which means more opportunities and faster career growth than you typically find at a mature, slow-moving organisation.

The company is headquartered in Mumbai, with teams across Bangalore, Delhi NCR, and other cities. If you want to work at the intersection of technology and one of India's largest sectors, Housing.com is worth pursuing seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at Housing.com varies by role and level, but here is a typical path you can expect.

Recruiter screening: The first contact is usually a short call from HR. They want to confirm your current role, notice period, salary expectations, and basic fit. Keep your answers crisp and be ready to summarise your experience in a couple of minutes.

Technical or domain round: Engineering candidates can expect coding questions and a discussion on system design. Product candidates often get a case study or a product thinking exercise. Sales and operations candidates typically walk through past performance and handle scenario-based questions about how they would approach specific situations.

Hiring manager round: This is a deeper conversation with the team lead or direct manager. Expect questions about how you have solved problems before, how you handle disagreement, and why you are interested in proptech specifically.

Final round: Senior leadership or cross-functional interviewers step in here. Culture fit, long-term ambitions, and your understanding of the Indian real estate market are common topics.

The overall timeline can vary. Follow up with the recruiter after each stage if you have not heard back within a week.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Housing.com looks for people who combine functional depth with genuine curiosity about the Indian real estate space.

Engineering candidates: Strong foundations in data structures, system design, and your primary language. Experience with consumer-facing, high-traffic products is a plus. Housing's scale means your code will touch a large number of real users quickly.

Product candidates: Customer empathy, the ability to prioritise ruthlessly, and comfort with data-driven decision making. If you have personally navigated the home-buying or renting process in India, that lived experience tends to resonate with interviewers.

Sales and business candidates: A clear track record of hitting targets, comfort with relationship-building across builders, brokers, and home buyers, and the ability to work in a fast-moving environment.

Across functions, Housing values people who take ownership, move quickly, and do not wait to be told what to do. If you are the kind of person who spots a gap and fixes it before anyone asks, you will fit the culture well.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your interview. Open the Housing.com app and spend real time on it. Browse listings, try the home loan calculators, and notice what works well and what could be improved. Interviewers frequently ask what you think of the product, and a vague answer signals low interest.

Learn the Indian proptech landscape. You do not need to be a real estate expert, but you should know who the major players are, what problems Indian home buyers and renters commonly face, and how Housing positions itself. Showing genuine curiosity about the domain makes a strong impression.

Build STAR stories from your past work. Most rounds include behavioural questions about how you have handled difficult situations, disagreements, or pressure. Prepare a handful of structured stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) so you can adapt them to different questions on the fly.

For engineering candidates: Practice coding problems focused on arrays, strings, graphs, and dynamic programming. Also revise system design concepts relevant to consumer apps, such as search ranking, recommendation engines, and handling large listing databases.

For product and business candidates: Come with a crisp opinion on a product you admire and why. Have a clear point of view on how you would improve one specific part of the Housing experience.

Follow up after each round with a short, professional note to your recruiter. It keeps you visible and signals genuine interest.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Housing.com operates with the pace and mindset of a consumer internet company. Teams are relatively flat, and individuals are expected to take initiative rather than wait for direction.

Ownership first: The culture rewards people who identify problems and solve them without needing someone else to assign the task. If you prefer a structured environment where responsibilities are tightly defined, the pace here may feel intense at first.

Customer obsession: Housing's users are often making the biggest financial decision of their lives. The team takes that seriously. Whether you are writing code, designing a feature, or selling to a broker, the end customer's experience is expected to stay front of mind.

Data over gut feel: Decisions at Housing are expected to be backed by evidence. Whether you are in product, growth, or sales, expect to justify your approach with numbers and be comfortable when colleagues challenge your assumptions.

The company has invested in building more structured processes in recent years, so new joiners typically find a mix of startup energy alongside clearer operational guardrails than existed earlier in Housing's history.

Does Housing.com hire freshers?

Yes, Housing does hire freshers, particularly for engineering and some business development roles. Campus hiring happens through select colleges, and off-campus applicants are considered as well. Make sure your resume highlights projects, internships, and any hands-on work that shows you can contribute from day one.

How long does the hiring process take?

The timeline varies by role and seniority. Some candidates move through the full process in a couple of weeks; others take longer, especially for senior or specialised positions. If you have not heard back after a week from any stage, a brief and polite follow-up to your recruiter is completely appropriate.

Is relocation to Mumbai required?

Many roles are based in Mumbai, where Housing's headquarters is located, but the company also has teams in Bangalore, Delhi NCR, and other cities. Remote and hybrid arrangements depend on the specific team and role. Check the job listing carefully and clarify with the recruiter during your screening call.
