04 Salary Range

Salary Range

The jobradar data for this snapshot does not include posted salary figures, which is common as most Delhi NCR employers list a range on request or disclose it only during screening.

What publicly reported sources suggest: Glassdoor and industry surveys commonly cite entry-level BA roles in Delhi NCR in the 5-10 LPA band, while mid-level profiles are commonly cited between 10-18 LPA. Senior BAs with domain depth in BFSI or consulting can go higher, with levels.fyi and publicly reported data pointing to figures above 20 LPA at larger firms, though sample sizes for Delhi NCR specifically are thin at that tier.

For companies like EXL Service and Marsh McLennan, Glassdoor reviews from current and former employees give the most grounded sense of compensation. Filter by 'Delhi' and 'Business Analyst' on Glassdoor for the freshest data before walking into any negotiation.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Lead with domain, not just tools. Delhi NCR's BA market skews heavily toward BFSI (Natwest Digitalx, Marsh McLennan, EXL Service) and emerging sectors like logistics tech (Zingbus Technology) and travel (travclan). Tailoring your resume to show domain knowledge, not just generic SQL or Excel skills, gets you past initial screening much faster.

Certifications that actually get noticed. CBAP, PMI-PBA, and Agile/Scrum certifications are commonly cited in Delhi NCR job descriptions. If you are early in your career, even a recognised course completion on a platform like Coursera or LinkedIn Learning can signal intent.

Quantify everything. Phrases like 'improved process efficiency' mean nothing to a screener. 'Reduced invoice processing time by working with ops and tech teams to streamline a manual workflow' is concrete, even without a precise percentage. Use specifics wherever you can.

SQL and data fluency are now baseline. Across the top employers in this list, comfort with querying data, writing basic reports, and using BI tools like Power BI or Tableau comes up repeatedly. If you are light here, a short focused course and a portfolio project can close the gap.

Stakeholder communication is the real differentiator. The BA role is as much about bridging business and tech as it is about analysis. Case studies from past jobs where you managed competing priorities, ran requirements workshops, or resolved scope creep land better than a long list of tools.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply early in the listing cycle. Roles at companies like EXL Service with multiple openings often fill specific band levels quickly. Applying within the first week of a job going live increases your chances of a human review versus an ATS rejection.

Customise your resume for each role, not just each company. A Natwest Digitalx role in core banking analysis needs a different emphasis than a Marsh McLennan role in risk consulting, even if your underlying experience is the same. Adjusting your top bullet points per application makes a real difference.

Reach out to the hiring manager directly on LinkedIn. For BA roles especially, a short, specific message referencing the role and one relevant project you have worked on gets responses more often than a cold connection request. Keep it brief and to the point.

Do not ignore smaller employers. agrim, Gozing, and Zingbus Technology each have 2 openings. Startups and growth-stage companies often offer faster career progression and broader scope, even if the brand name is less recognisable.

Is Delhi NCR a good city for Business Analyst jobs compared to Bangalore?
