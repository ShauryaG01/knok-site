01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Exotel is one of India's leading cloud communication platforms, helping thousands of businesses handle calls, SMS, and customer engagement at scale. From fintech and e-commerce to healthcare and logistics, companies rely on Exotel to power their contact centres, sales diallers, and support helplines.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracked 65 open roles at Exotel spanning engineering, product, sales, customer success, and operations. That breadth signals active hiring across multiple teams at once. Whether you are a backend engineer, a product manager, or a customer success specialist, there is a good chance a relevant opening exists right now.

Exotel is headquartered in Bengaluru with a distributed team, and several roles carry hybrid or remote options. The company has grown its product portfolio steadily and continues to expand into new verticals, which means internal mobility and growth opportunities tend to be real, not just on paper.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Exotel's hiring process typically moves through five to six stages, though the exact number can vary by role and team. What follows is a stage-by-stage breakdown based on commonly reported candidate experiences.

Stage 1: Application and resume shortlisting
The recruiter team reviews applications against the job description. A tailored resume that mirrors the role's keywords (cloud communications, APIs, SaaS, customer experience) tends to move forward faster than a generic one.

Stage 2: HR or recruiter screening call
A short introductory call to confirm basics: your current role, notice period, location preference, and salary expectation. Be honest about all four. Mismatches caught at this stage can delay or kill an offer later.

Stage 3: Technical or functional assessment
For engineering roles, this is commonly a coding test or take-home assignment covering data structures, system design, or API integration. For product roles, expect a case study or product teardown. For sales or customer success roles, this stage may be a written pitch or a role-play scenario.

Stage 4: Technical or domain interview rounds
Engineers typically face one or two rounds with senior engineers or tech leads, covering problem-solving, system design, and past project deep-dives. Product candidates discuss roadmap thinking and prioritisation. Sales candidates walk through their pipeline management and deal-closing approach.

Stage 5: Hiring manager round
This round is less about skills and more about fit. The hiring manager wants to understand how you think, how you handle ambiguity, and whether your working style suits the team. Expect questions about past conflicts, decisions made with incomplete information, and what you look for in a company.

Stage 6: Leadership or final round
For mid-to-senior roles, a final conversation with a VP or business head is common. This is a two-way discussion: they assess your strategic thinking, and you assess whether the leadership vision resonates with you.

Offer and background check
If all rounds go well, HR shares a verbal offer followed by a formal letter. Background verification is standard and typically takes a few working days.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Exotel builds communication infrastructure, so the bar across all roles is high on understanding customer problems and translating them into reliable, scalable solutions.

For engineering roles, the company values strong fundamentals in backend development (commonly Python, Java, or Go), comfort with distributed systems and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP), and experience with APIs and real-time communication protocols. Clean, testable code matters more than flashy frameworks.

For product roles, they look for people who can sit with a customer, understand a messy operational problem, and define a clear, scoped solution. Product sense matters, but so does the ability to work closely with engineering without becoming a bottleneck.

For sales and customer success roles, the focus is on consultative selling, pipeline discipline, and the ability to manage enterprise relationships. Knowledge of the SaaS or cloud communications space gives you a visible edge.

Across all roles, Exotel consistently values: ownership (people who follow through without being chased), clarity of communication both written and verbal, and adaptability to a fast-moving product environment. Candidates who can show concrete impact from past roles with specific examples, rather than vague claims, tend to stand out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before your first call. Sign up for Exotel's free trial or watch their product demos. Understanding how their call routing, IVR, and messaging products actually work gives you an edge in every round, not just the technical ones.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Exotel's job descriptions often list specific tools, platforms, or metrics. Mirror that language in your resume where it is accurate. A resume that shows measurable outcomes is more compelling than one that only lists responsibilities.

Prepare a strong 'why Exotel' answer. Generic answers about growth and learning put off interviewers. Instead, tie your answer to a specific problem Exotel is solving, for example helping mid-market businesses in India move from manual calling to automated, data-driven customer outreach, and explain why that problem excites you.

For engineering candidates, practise system design problems around high-availability telephony systems, real-time event processing, and multi-tenant SaaS architecture. Also brush up on REST API design and database optimisation.

For product and sales candidates, come prepared with a teardown of one Exotel product feature. What works, what you would change, and how you would prioritise the change. This shows initiative and product thinking in one move.

On salary negotiation: research publicly reported compensation on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for similar roles before your HR screen. Know your number going in so you are not anchored by the first figure the recruiter mentions.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Exotel operates like a company that is past the chaos of early-stage startup life but has not yet settled into the slow pace of a large enterprise. That middle ground suits people who want meaningful ownership without having to reinvent processes from scratch every quarter.

Ownership is the dominant cultural value. Teams are expected to define their own success metrics, spot problems early, and propose solutions without waiting for direction from above. If you thrive with clear structure and detailed processes, the culture may feel uncomfortable. If you like figuring things out and moving fast, you will likely find it energising.

Customer obsession shapes how decisions get made. Engineering and product teams are expected to understand the customer's operational context, not just the technical spec. Cross-functional collaboration between sales, customer success, and engineering is the norm rather than the exception.

On work-life balance, industry surveys and publicly reported employee feedback paint a mixed picture. Most roles operate on a hybrid model, and expectations during product launches or major client onboarding cycles can be intense. Exotel has invested in people practices and manager training in recent years, which commonly reported employee reviews reflect positively.

Growth paths are real but not automatic. Promotions at Exotel are tied to impact, not tenure. If you deliver results and communicate them clearly, progression tends to happen. If you wait to be noticed, it may not.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many interview rounds does Exotel typically have?

Most candidates report five to six rounds: an HR screen, a technical or functional assessment, one or two technical or domain rounds, a hiring manager conversation, and sometimes a final leadership round. The exact number varies by seniority and team. Senior roles tend to have more rounds than junior ones.

Does Exotel hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Exotel does hire freshers, primarily into engineering and business development roles, but most of their open positions target candidates with relevant industry experience. If you are a fresher, look specifically for roles tagged 'associate' or 'junior' and lean on strong internship projects or open-source contributions rather than work history to make your case.

What salary can I expect at Exotel?

Exotel does not publish salary bands publicly. Based on Glassdoor and levels.fyi data for comparable SaaS companies in Bengaluru, compensation varies widely by role, seniority, and function. Research those platforms for current benchmarks before your recruiter call so you can negotiate from an informed position rather than accepting the first number mentioned.

How long does the Exotel hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on commonly cited candidate experiences, the end-to-end process typically runs two to four weeks for most roles. Delays usually happen at the scheduling stage between rounds rather than during the rounds themselves. Following up politely with your recruiter after each stage helps keep things moving without being pushy.

Can I apply to more than one role at Exotel at the same time?

Yes, applying to multiple roles is fine. That said, applying to many very different positions in the same week can signal that you are not clear on what you want, which can reduce your credibility with the recruiter. Pick the two or three roles that genuinely fit your background and tailor each application separately.

Does Exotel offer remote or work-from-home options?

Many Exotel roles offer hybrid work arrangements, especially for engineering and product functions. Fully remote roles exist but are less common. The job description for each opening will state the location requirement clearly, so check it before applying. If flexibility matters to you, ask the recruiter about the specific team's arrangement early in the process.
