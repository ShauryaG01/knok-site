01 Overview

Overview

CarDekho is one of India's largest online automotive platforms, built around helping consumers research, buy, sell, and finance cars. The product spans a high-traffic consumer app, a used-car buying and selling engine, auto insurance, and vehicle loans. As of July 2026, CarDekho had 7 open Product Manager roles, sitting inside a broader India-wide market of 2009 PM openings. Bangalore leads that market with 271 PM jobs, followed by Delhi (177), Pune (31), and Hyderabad (24).

Candidates report a process that typically runs 3-5 rounds: an initial HR or recruiter call, a take-home product case or written assignment, two or three product and analytical interviews with senior PMs or the hiring manager, and a final culture or leadership discussion. Questions are consistently grounded in auto-commerce and fintech, so domain familiarity with CarDekho's product gives a real edge.

02 Most Asked Questions

Most Asked Questions

Candidates report these topics coming up most often across CarDekho PM interview rounds. Prepare a crisp, structured answer for each before your first call.

  1. CarDekho's used-car listings see low engagement in tier-2 cities. How would you diagnose and improve this?
  2. Design a feature that helps a first-time buyer decide between a new car and a used car on the CarDekho platform.
  3. What metrics would you use to measure the success of CarDekho's EMI calculator?
  4. CarDekho wants to expand its presence in the electric vehicle segment. Outline a product roadmap for the first year.
  5. An A/B test shows the redesigned car detail page has a lower lead submission rate than the old design. What do you do next?
  6. How would you prioritise features for the dealer-facing app versus the consumer-facing app when engineering bandwidth is limited?
  7. CarDekho competes with CarWale and OLX Autos. How do you think about meaningful differentiation?
  8. How would you build a trust and verification layer for used-car listings to reduce buyer anxiety before purchase?
  9. Walk us through a product you took from idea to launch. What would you do differently knowing what you know now?
  10. The insurance cross-sell step in the used-car purchase flow has a low take rate. How would you improve it?
  11. How would you design a personalised car recommendation engine for CarDekho's app home page?
  12. A key dealer partner tells you that lead quality from CarDekho has dropped over the past quarter. How do you respond?

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for all behavioural and product execution questions. The three examples below are modelled on topics commonly covered in CarDekho PM interviews.

Q: Tell me about a time you improved a low-converting funnel step.

*Situation:* At my previous company, the loan application step inside our used-vehicle checkout had a completion rate well below industry survey benchmarks, and a large share of users were dropping off before submitting.

*Task:* My goal was to find the exact friction point and lift completions without a large engineering investment.

*Action:* I ran session recordings alongside a short in-app survey and found that users were confused by a 'net income' field that asked them to calculate a number manually. I rewrote the field label, added a tooltip with a plain example, and ran an A/B test on half of traffic for three weeks.

*Result:* Completion on that step improved meaningfully, which was called out in the next quarterly business review. The engineering effort was a single day.

---

Q: Describe a time you had to push back on a stakeholder request.

*Situation:* A senior sales leader asked me to add a mandatory phone-number capture field to the top of the car search page, believing it would help the call centre reach buyers earlier in their journey.

*Task:* I needed to either fulfil the request or make a data-backed case for a better alternative.

*Action:* I pulled funnel data showing that interrupting discovery with a lead-capture step correlated with higher bounce rates on similar flows. I proposed a softer alternative: a 'save my search' prompt shown when users applied filters, capturing intent without blocking the search experience. I presented both options with projected trade-offs to the leadership team.

*Result:* The alternative was approved. The sales team received warmer, more intent-rich leads, and the stakeholder later acknowledged the approach worked better than the original ask.

---

Q: Tell me about a product decision you made with incomplete data.

*Situation:* Our team was choosing between building a dedicated EV comparison section and expanding used-car inventory filters. Both were on the roadmap but we only had bandwidth for one.

*Task:* I had to make a call with only a couple of weeks of EV page traffic data, which was too small a sample to be reliable.

*Action:* I triangulated using three signals: internal search keyword volume, publicly reported industry data on EV buyer intent, and a small user research call with a handful of recent car buyers. I framed the decision as a reversible six-week bet: ship a thin EV section, measure click-through to dealer contact, and revisit.

*Result:* After six weeks the EV section showed strong engagement. The team doubled down with a fuller build, and it became one of the more-visited sections of the site within the quarter.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

For product design questions (such as 'design a feature' or 'improve X on CarDekho'), start with the user segment and their core job-to-be-done. Identify the biggest friction in their current journey. Propose a solution tied directly to that friction. Then name a success metric before closing. Avoid jumping to the solution before establishing the problem.

For metrics questions, separate leading indicators (for example, sessions on the car detail page, or search-to-detail click rate) from lagging outcomes (lead submissions, dealer connects, insurance policy purchases). Always name a guardrail metric too, something that should not drop as you optimise the primary number.

For prioritisation questions, the RICE framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) works well in CarDekho's context because it can balance consumer-facing and dealer-facing work on the same platform. State your framework explicitly, apply it to the specific options in the question, and be honest about trade-offs rather than treating one choice as obviously right.

For analytical or root-cause questions, use top-down funnel decomposition: start at the broadest metric (site or app visits), break it into funnel steps (search, car detail page, lead form, lead submission), and locate where the curve bends before diagnosing causes. Candidates who skip directly to hypotheses often get interrupted mid-answer.

For competitor questions, a simple comparison across CarDekho, CarWale, and OLX Autos covering audience type, monetisation model, and one key differentiator each is usually enough to anchor a solid three-to-four-minute answer.

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

CarDekho PM interviewers typically look for a few things beyond standard product instincts.

Domain curiosity. You do not need to be a car enthusiast, but you should have used the CarDekho app, explored at least one competitor, and formed a view on where auto-commerce is heading, especially around used cars and EVs. Interviewers notice when a candidate has done this homework versus when they have not.

Two-sided marketplace thinking. CarDekho serves both buyers and dealers. Candidates who frame every answer around only the consumer side typically get challenged with follow-up questions about dealer economics. Show that you understand how lead quality and dealer trust matter as much as raw lead volume.

Data-first instincts. Interviewers reward candidates who ask clarifying questions about data before proposing solutions, and who can name a specific metric for every feature they describe. A vague statement like 'I would track engagement' is a red flag.

Execution credibility. CarDekho moves at a fast pace. Candidates report that interviewers probe whether you have actually shipped products or only theorised about them. Have two or three concrete examples of things you built ready before every round.

Clear communication. PMs at CarDekho interface with sales teams, engineering, and dealer partners. Interviewers listen for whether you can simplify complex trade-offs into language a non-technical stakeholder can act on.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Spread your prep across two to three weeks if possible.

Week 1: Ground yourself in the product. Use the CarDekho app across at least two separate sessions. Try the buying and selling flows, the EMI calculator, and the insurance cross-sell step. Write down three things you find friction-heavy and three things that work well. Read any publicly available news about CarDekho's recent launches or business direction.

Week 2: Sharpen your frameworks and stories. Work through the twelve questions in this guide and draft a two-minute answer for each. Practise saying them out loud, not just writing them down. For STAR stories, prepare at least three or four covering: a product launch you owned, a data-driven decision, a stakeholder conflict you navigated, and a time you course-corrected after a mistake.

Week 3: Mock interviews and logistics. Do at least two mock interviews with someone who can give feedback on clarity, not just on content. Review your resume and be ready to walk through every product on it in detail. Candidates report that 'take me through your resume' at CarDekho typically goes deeper than at many other companies.

On the day: Restate the problem before solving it. If an interviewer adds a constraint mid-answer, treat it as a signal to adapt rather than a correction to apologise for.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Ignoring the dealer side. CarDekho's revenue depends heavily on dealer relationships. Candidates who design features or set metrics only around the consumer experience typically receive follow-up questions that reveal the gap. Always consider how your proposal affects lead quality and dealer trust, not just buyer experience.

Jumping to features too fast. When asked to improve a product, many candidates list features immediately. Interviewers at CarDekho usually want to see user problem identification, funnel analysis, and hypothesis formation before any feature is named.

Using frameworks without grounding them. Saying 'I would use RICE to prioritise' without actually applying it to the specific CarDekho scenario sounds rehearsed. Apply the framework to the real options in front of you, with your reasoning made explicit.

Vague metrics. 'I would track engagement' is not a metric. Name the specific event, the numerator, and the denominator. For example: 'lead submission rate, defined as lead form completions divided by car detail page views, segmented by new versus used.'

Not asking clarifying questions. Candidates who jump into answers without scoping the problem signal that they skip discovery in real work too. Take a moment to ask one or two targeted questions before you start solving.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many rounds does a CarDekho PM interview typically have?

Candidates report a process of typically 3-5 rounds. This usually includes an HR or recruiter screen, a take-home or live product case, two or three product and analytical interviews with senior PMs or the hiring manager, and a final leadership or culture conversation. The exact number can vary by level and team, so confirm the format with your recruiter after the first call.

Does CarDekho give a take-home assignment?

Many candidates report receiving a take-home case or written product exercise early in the process, though this is not universal across all teams. The assignment typically asks you to analyse a product problem or propose a roadmap for a CarDekho feature. Treat it like a real product spec: structure your answer clearly, name your metrics, and address trade-offs directly.

What salary can I expect as a PM at CarDekho?

Based on knok jobradar data as of July 2026, PM roles in India fall in the range of 24-40 LPA for mid-level PMs with 3-6 years of experience, and 40-60 LPA for Senior PMs. Associate PMs typically see 12-20 LPA, and Group or Principal PM roles can reach 55-90+ LPA. Actual offers depend on your experience level, the specific team, and how you negotiate.

Do I need automotive industry experience to get a PM role at CarDekho?

Automotive experience is helpful but not required. Candidates report that CarDekho values strong product thinking, data fluency, and marketplace experience more than domain-specific background. You should, however, come prepared with a working understanding of their product, their key competitors such as CarWale and OLX Autos, and the direction the used-car and EV market is heading.

How important is SQL or data analysis in the CarDekho PM interview?

Analytical thinking is tested in most rounds, though not always through a live SQL exercise. You should be comfortable reading a funnel, spotting drop-off points, and explaining how you would instrument or query data to answer a product question. If you have SQL skills, be ready to use them; if not, focus on demonstrating structured data thinking through frameworks and precise metric definitions.
