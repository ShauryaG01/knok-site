01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Forbes Advisor is the personal finance and product review division of Forbes Media, built to help millions of readers make smarter decisions about banking, insurance, credit cards, and investing. As of 2026, the team has 23 open roles across editorial, marketing, product, and technology functions. The brand operates primarily in digital publishing, so most positions involve content creation, SEO, data analysis, or product development.

Hiring at Forbes Advisor tends to be selective. The team values domain expertise alongside strong written communication, because accuracy and reader trust are central to the brand's identity. If you are targeting one of their current openings, expect a structured process with multiple rounds, each designed to test your subject matter knowledge and your ability to think clearly under pressure.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Forbes Advisor hiring process runs through several distinct stages, though the number of rounds can vary by department and seniority. Here is what candidates generally report:

Application and Resume Screen
You submit your application through the Forbes Advisor careers page or a job aggregator. Recruiters scan for domain relevance, writing samples (for editorial roles), and measurable results from past positions.

Recruiter or HR Call
If your profile clears the initial screen, a recruiter reaches out for a brief phone call. Expect questions about your background, what draws you to Forbes Advisor, and logistics like availability. This round is conversational rather than technical.

Hiring Manager Interview
This is the core round. For editorial roles, expect questions on SEO strategy, content workflows, and your understanding of personal finance products. For product and tech roles, expect questions about past projects and decision-making. The hiring manager typically asks you to walk through a piece of work you are proud of.

Skills Assessment or Test Assignment
Many roles, especially editorial and SEO-focused ones, include a written assignment or practical task. You might draft a sample article, audit a content gap, or analyse keyword data. This step carries significant weight in the final decision.

Final Round or Panel Interview
Shortlisted candidates meet senior stakeholders or cross-functional team members. This round tests cultural fit, collaboration style, and how you handle feedback. For senior roles, there may be a strategy discussion or a short presentation of your assignment findings.

Offer and Background Check
Successful candidates receive a verbal offer, followed by a formal offer letter and a standard background verification.

With 23 open roles live across the organisation right now, there is meaningful breadth in what Forbes Advisor is hiring for, so it is worth reviewing all relevant listings before deciding which role to target.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Forbes Advisor looks for people who combine genuine subject matter expertise with clear, reader-first communication. A few qualities appear consistently across roles:

Domain credibility. Whether you are applying for a finance writer, product manager, or data analyst position, you need to show real understanding of the space. Reviewers at Forbes Advisor read widely and can tell quickly when a candidate has only surface-level knowledge.

Writing and clarity. Even in non-editorial roles, strong written communication matters. Emails, briefs, and internal documents are expected to be clean and precise.

SEO and digital fluency. A large part of Forbes Advisor's revenue depends on organic search. Candidates who understand how content ranks, what readers search for, and how to interpret analytics data stand out from the crowd.

Ownership mindset. The team values people who take initiative and follow through without constant direction. In interviews, they probe for examples where you led something from start to finish.

Feedback-readiness. Cross-functional collaboration is common, and the process partly tests whether you handle critique constructively and work well with colleagues across teams.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product thoroughly. Spend time reading Forbes Advisor articles in your target domain. Notice how they structure comparison guides, what topics they prioritise, and where their content sits on Google. If you can articulate what they do well and where you see opportunity, you will stand out immediately.

Build a strong portfolio. For editorial roles, select a few pieces that show traffic growth, ranking improvements, or measurable reader engagement. Include a brief note on what you did, what changed, and what you learned from each example.

Practice explaining your thinking aloud. Interviewers often ask 'how would you approach this' questions. Walking through your reasoning step by step, rather than jumping straight to a conclusion, shows the analytical depth they are looking for.

Treat the assignment as a real deliverable. If you receive a test task, submit work you would genuinely publish. Check facts carefully, proofread thoroughly, and follow any format or length guidelines exactly. Half-finished or rushed submissions are a common reason candidates drop out at this stage.

Prepare questions about the team's goals. Asking about content priorities, growth targets, or team structure signals genuine interest. It also gives you the information you need to decide whether the role is a good fit for where you want to go next.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Forbes Advisor operates as a high-accountability, output-focused editorial and digital product team within a globally recognised media brand. The culture rewards people who work independently, communicate proactively, and take responsibility for quality without needing close supervision.

The team is largely distributed, with collaboration happening across time zones. Clear written communication and responsiveness are practical necessities, not just soft skills the job description lists. Feedback cycles in editorial are relatively fast, so you need to be comfortable iterating quickly on your work and not treating early drafts as final.

Given its position in personal finance publishing, there is a strong emphasis on accuracy and trust. Getting facts wrong or cutting corners on sourcing is taken seriously. Everyone on the team is expected to protect the brand's credibility with readers, regardless of their function.

For Indian professionals considering a remote or India-based role, the working style aligns with global digital media norms: asynchronous collaboration, deliverable-focused performance reviews, and relatively flat day-to-day hierarchy.

How many open roles does Forbes Advisor currently have?

As of mid-2026, Forbes Advisor has 23 open roles spanning editorial, SEO, product, technology, and marketing. The mix of available positions shifts as new projects launch and existing ones close, so it is worth checking regularly or using a tool that monitors listings nightly on your behalf.

Does Forbes Advisor hire remotely in India?

Forbes Advisor has published remote and hybrid roles in the past, and some positions have been open to candidates outside the United States. Eligibility for India-based applicants depends on the specific role and its location requirements. Read each job listing carefully and confirm directly with the recruiter during the initial call.

What kind of writing sample should I submit?

Submit work that is as close as possible to what Forbes Advisor publishes: comparison guides, product reviews, or data-driven explainers in personal finance or a related category. If your best samples are behind a paywall or a CMS login, export them as PDFs. Including a note on traffic or ranking results, if you have them, strengthens your application considerably.

What salary range should I expect at Forbes Advisor?

Publicly reported figures for digital media editorial and product roles vary widely by seniority, function, and location. Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for current data on Forbes Advisor specifically. How long does the full hiring process take from application to offer?

Candidate experiences vary by role and team. The process from application to offer can span a few weeks to well over a month depending on how many rounds are involved and how quickly the panel aligns. If you have not heard back after a reasonable interval following an interview, a polite follow-up email to the recruiter is appropriate and expected.

What are the most common reasons candidates do not get through?

Based on what candidates commonly report for similar digital media roles, the most frequent reasons are a weak or irrelevant writing sample, limited demonstrable SEO knowledge for editorial positions, and underperformance on the test assignment.
