04 Salary Range

Salary Range

The current knok radar snapshot does not include salary band data for Platform Engineer roles, so the figures below draw from public sources and should be read as market reference points.

For mid-level engineers with 3-6 years of relevant experience, Glassdoor and industry surveys commonly cite a range of 18-35 LPA at product companies in Bangalore. The wide band reflects how much compensation varies by company type, funding stage, and how deeply a candidate specialises in areas like Kubernetes internals or developer-platform tooling.

At the senior and staff level, publicly reported numbers on levels.fyi put compensation at 40-80 LPA and above at well-funded startups and global firms, though that data reflects a relatively small, high-signal sample and is not representative of the broader market.

Entry-level Platform Engineering roles are uncommon. Most positions expect hands-on production experience with infrastructure tooling. A DevOps or SRE role is the more realistic starting point for early-career engineers, with a move into Platform Engineering after building a concrete track record.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Platform Engineering is a discipline where your toolkit is your resume. Hiring managers scan for a specific set of skills before anything else.

Infrastructure as Code. Terraform and Pulumi are the dominant tools right now. If you have production experience managing cloud infrastructure through code, lead with that. Personal projects count if they mirror real-world complexity and you can talk through the tradeoffs you made.

Kubernetes, deep not wide. Every applicant lists Kubernetes. What stands out is specifics: cluster hardening, custom operators, cost optimisation, or multi-tenant workload isolation. Write about what you actually solved and what broke, not just what you used.

CI/CD and developer experience. Platform teams exist to make other engineers faster. Experience building or improving internal developer platforms, reducing build times, or migrating pipelines to tools like GitHub Actions, ArgoCD, or Tekton will get attention in a way that generic pipeline experience will not.

Observability. Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry show up as requirements across most of the active roles. If you have built alerting frameworks or defined SLOs from scratch, that is a concrete differentiator worth calling out explicitly.

Cloud certifications. AWS, GCP, or Azure associate or professional certifications are not mandatory but they help when employers are filtering large applicant pools. They signal a verified baseline and save the interviewer time in early screening rounds.

Contributions and writing. Open source contributions to platform tooling, a technical post about a production incident you debugged, or a public GitHub repository with a working internal developer platform demo all do more work than a bullet point on a resume.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Platform Engineer roles at Bangalore companies tend to fill quickly because the qualified talent pool is narrower than for general software engineering. A reactive approach of checking job boards every few days means you will miss many roles before they close.

Apply early. Roles at firms like Goldman Sachs, Okta, and Adyen attract high application volumes. Getting in within the first couple of days of a posting going live meaningfully improves your chance of a human review before the pipeline fills.

Tailor each application. A generic resume will not clear the initial filter at fintech and global tech companies. Mirror the specific tools mentioned in the job description and frame your experience around outcomes you delivered, not just tools you operated.

Message the hiring team directly. A short LinkedIn note to the engineering manager or recruiter, sent the same day you apply, keeps your name visible. One line on why you are relevant, one line asking if they are open to a quick call. That is enough.

Track your applications. With 29 active roles across multiple companies, it is easy to lose track of where you applied and when to follow up. A simple spreadsheet with company, date applied, and contact name is sufficient.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Bangalore the best city in India for Platform Engineer jobs right now?

Based on the knok radar dated 8 July 2026, Bangalore has 29 open Platform Engineer roles, more than any other city in the snapshot. Delhi shows 12 and Pune shows 10, so both are worth watching if you are open to relocation. Bangalore's concentration of global tech offices and funded startups makes it the most active market, but it also means you are competing against a deeper pool of experienced candidates.

What skills do I need to get a Platform Engineer job in Bangalore?

The core stack employers ask for most consistently includes Kubernetes, Terraform or another infrastructure-as-code tool, at least one major cloud provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure), and CI/CD tooling like GitHub Actions, ArgoCD, or Jenkins. Observability experience with Prometheus and Grafana is a strong secondary requirement across most job descriptions. Python or Go scripting ability is expected at the majority of companies currently hiring.

Are Platform Engineer roles available for freshers or early-career engineers?

Rarely. Most Platform Engineer job descriptions ask for hands-on infrastructure or DevOps experience in production environments. The more accessible entry point is an SRE or DevOps Engineer role, where you can build the practical skills that Platform Engineering interviews test. After 2-3 years in one of those roles with meaningful infrastructure ownership, the move into Platform Engineering is very achievable.

How is a Platform Engineer different from a DevOps Engineer or SRE?

The roles overlap significantly, but the emphasis differs. A DevOps Engineer typically focuses on CI/CD pipelines and release automation. An SRE focuses on reliability, uptime, and incident response. A Platform Engineer builds the internal platform that other developers use to deploy and operate their services, so the primary customer is the internal engineering team rather than end users or external customers. In practice, many Bangalore companies use these three titles interchangeably, so read each job description carefully rather than going by the title alone.

Which companies are actively hiring Platform Engineers in Bangalore right now?

As of 8 July 2026, the knok radar shows Bosch Group (4 roles), Okta (2), Goldman Sachs (2), and Aspora (2) as the top hirers. Sarvam, CodeRabbit, e6data, Adyen, Visa, and Skylark Drones each have one open role. This mix spans automotive tech, fintech, identity security, and AI, showing that Platform Engineering demand cuts across industries rather than clustering in a single sector.

How do I negotiate salary for a Platform Engineer role at a Bangalore company?

Glassdoor and levels.fyi are the most commonly cited benchmarks for Platform Engineering compensation in India, and both are worth checking for the specific company before you enter any negotiation. Come prepared with a concrete number or a competing offer rather than asking what their range is. Publicly reported data on levels.fyi suggests senior roles at global firms carry a meaningful premium over roles at domestic product companies, so the type of employer matters as much as the title when anchoring your ask.
