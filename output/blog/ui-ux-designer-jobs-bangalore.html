04 Salary Range

Salary Range

The current dataset does not include salary band data for these specific Bangalore openings, so treat the figures below as market context rather than guarantees.

Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor for UI/UX Designers in Bangalore show junior and entry-level profiles commonly in the 4-8 LPA range. Your negotiation leverage increases if you can show measurable impact from past design work, such as improved task completion or reduced user drop-off. The companies hiring right now are mostly startups, so expect faster career growth alongside leaner base packages compared to MNC design roles.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

UI/UX Designer roles at Bangalore startups have a specific bar. Hiring managers here are usually product managers or founders, not design leaders, so your portfolio needs to explain the 'why' behind decisions, not just show polished screens.

Build case studies, not a gallery. Each project should cover the problem, your process (research, wireframes, iterations), and the outcome. If you improved task completion or reduced support load, say so clearly.

Get fluent in Figma. All 5 companies hiring right now are product companies and Figma is the standard. Comfort with auto-layout, component libraries, and interactive prototyping separates working designers from those still learning the tool.

Understand frontend basics. You do not need to write code, but knowing how React components or CSS constraints work makes you a better collaborator with engineers. Startups value designers who reduce back-and-forth with the dev team.

Show domain relevance. The current openings span fintech, real estate, HR tech, and AI. If you have prior work in any of these areas, lead with it in your portfolio and in your message to the recruiter.

Write a specific outreach note. A short message to HR explaining why you want to work on their product specifically lands far better than a generic cover letter.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With 6 openings across 5 companies, the Bangalore UI/UX market right now rewards precision over volume.

Start by researching each company's product before you apply. For companies like Signzy or Housing, look at their app, note what works and what does not, and reference it briefly in your message to HR. This signals genuine interest and sets you apart from applicants who clearly have not used the product.

Apply within the first few days of a job going live. Startup hiring moves fast, and early applications often get reviewed more carefully before the pipeline fills up.

Prepare a short video walkthrough of your best case study. Many founders and product managers skip reading long PDFs but will watch a 3-4 minute video if you share the link directly in your application message.
