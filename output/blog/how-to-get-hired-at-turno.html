Hiring is active across functions, which means real opportunities exist for candidates at different experience levels. Whether you are entering the EV space for the first time or bringing solid experience in fleet management, product, or engineering, there is likely a relevant opening worth exploring.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Turno's hiring process typically runs through a few rounds, with the exact number depending on the role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report, though steps can vary:

Stage 1: Application and resume review. Your resume is screened for relevant experience and skill fit. Tailoring your resume to the specific role, whether in sales, operations, or tech, improves your chances at this stage.

Stage 2: Recruiter or HR screening call. A short introductory call where the recruiter confirms your background, current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period. They will also walk you through the role and check for basic alignment.

Stage 3: Technical or functional round. For technology roles, expect problem-solving questions, coding discussions, or system design. For sales and operations roles, expect scenario-based questions around customer handling, field logistics, or EV product knowledge.

Stage 4: Managerial round. A senior team member or cross-functional manager assesses how you think, communicate, and handle challenges in a fast-moving startup environment.

Stage 5: HR final and offer. This round covers cultural fit, career goals, and compensation expectations. Come prepared to discuss your CTC expectations clearly and confidently.

With 47 open roles as of July 2026, Turno's hiring pipelines are active across multiple functions simultaneously.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Based on what candidates and employees share publicly, Turno typically looks for a blend of functional skill and startup mindset:

Ownership and initiative. Turno is a growth-stage company. They want people who can take a problem and run with it, not just follow a checklist. Come ready with examples where you drove outcomes independently.

EV or mobility awareness. You do not need deep EV engineering knowledge for most roles, but showing that you understand why the EV shift matters for commercial fleets in India signals genuine interest and gives you an edge.

Customer empathy (especially for non-tech roles). A large part of Turno's business involves helping fleet operators make the switch to EVs. Even in operations-adjacent roles, comfort with customer interaction is valued.

Analytical thinking. Whether you are in product, tech, or operations, the ability to make decisions with data is a recurring theme in Turno's job descriptions.

Adaptability. Startups change direction quickly. Demonstrating that you handle ambiguity without losing momentum is a strong signal for any role at Turno.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research Turno's business model. Understand how they sell and finance electric three-wheelers to fleet operators. Reading recent news about EV adoption in India's last-mile delivery sector will give you real talking points across any interview round.

Know the EV market context. Questions about the competitive landscape, government EV incentives like the FAME scheme, and why businesses are choosing electric over CNG or petrol are fair game. You do not need technical depth, but informed interest is clearly noticed.

Prepare stories using the STAR format. Turno interviewers, like most startup teams, ask behavioral questions. Structure your answers around Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Prioritise examples where you solved problems with limited resources or in ambiguous situations.

Practice role-specific skills. For tech roles, revisit relevant data structures, system design, or frameworks. For sales roles, practice articulating a value proposition and handling objections. For ops roles, think through examples of managing logistics or field teams under pressure.

Prepare smart questions for the interviewer. Ask about team structure, what success looks like in the first few months on the job, and how Turno approaches expansion into newer markets. Good questions signal curiosity and genuine interest.

Know your numbers before the screening call. Have your current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period ready before you pick up the phone. Turno recruiters ask these early, and being unprepared here can create a poor first impression.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Turno operates as a growth-stage startup in a sector moving at speed. Based on what current and former employees share publicly, the culture tends to be:

Mission-driven. The EV transition is central to how Turno frames its work. Employees who care about sustainability and India's clean mobility future tend to find the environment energising.

Fast-paced and execution-focused. Decisions are made quickly. Teams are expected to move, learn, and adapt. If you prefer highly structured processes, the startup pace will take some adjustment.

Collaborative but lean. Teams are small and cross-functional work is common. You will regularly work with people across sales, product, and operations, even as an individual contributor in a focused role.

Growth-oriented for employees. Early-stage companies like Turno can offer faster career progression than larger corporates, though this comes with the expectation that you stretch beyond a narrow job description.

Turno's work is grounded in a real-world problem (cleaner commercial transport in Indian cities) that is visibly playing out on roads around the country. For many employees, that tangibility is a meaningful part of why they joined.

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Turno have right now?

As of July 2026, Turno has 47 open positions across sales, operations, technology, and customer success. The active hiring across multiple functions means there are several entry points, whether you are a fresher exploring the EV space or an experienced professional in fleet management or engineering.

What salary can I expect at Turno?

Turno does not publish a standard pay scale. Based on Glassdoor and community forums, compensation is commonly cited as competitive for a growth-stage EV startup, though it may be positioned below what large tech firms offer. Research role-specific ranges on Glassdoor or LinkedIn before your HR screening call so you can negotiate with confidence.

Does Turno hire freshers?

Yes, Turno does hire freshers, particularly for field sales and operations roles in cities where it is expanding. For tech roles, strong internship experience or project work can substitute for full-time experience. Always check the specific job description for any stated requirements before applying.

How long does the Turno hiring process take from application to offer?

The timeline varies by role and team. Candidates commonly report the process completing within a few weeks for non-tech roles, and somewhat longer for engineering or product positions. Following up politely with the recruiter after each stage is a reasonable step to keep things moving.

Is there a coding test for tech roles at Turno?

Most tech candidates report at least one problem-solving or coding round. It may be a take-home assignment or a live coding discussion, depending on the team and role. Practising data structures, algorithms, and being ready to talk through past projects is solid preparation.

Does Turno offer remote or hybrid work?

Turno's roles tend to be in-person or field-based, especially in sales and operations. Tech and product roles may have more flexibility depending on the team and city. Check the individual job listing or ask the recruiter directly during the screening call to get clarity before accepting an offer.
