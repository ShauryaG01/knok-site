01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Unbox Robotics is a Pune-based warehouse robotics startup that builds autonomous systems for e-commerce and logistics companies. Their robots handle sortation and movement of goods inside large fulfilment centres, cutting down the manual effort warehouses rely on today.

As of mid-2026, Unbox Robotics has 31 open roles across software engineering, embedded systems, mechanical and hardware engineering, operations, and business functions. Hiring is active and the company is growing, which means decisions tend to move faster than at a large corporation.

If you want to work on physical robots that actually ship to warehouse floors rather than simulations or prototypes that never leave a lab, Unbox Robotics is one of the more accessible Indian startups in this space.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs three to four rounds, though the exact structure varies by role and team. Expect the whole thing to wrap up in two to three weeks for most positions.

Round 1: Application and resume screen. The talent team reviews your profile and may reach out with a short call to confirm availability, location fit, and basic background. Some technical roles include a take-home problem or an online coding or aptitude assessment at this stage.

Round 2: HR discussion. A recruiter or HR partner walks through your background, career goals, and compensation expectations. This is conversational rather than deeply technical. Be ready to explain why robotics interests you and what draws you to a startup over a larger company.

Round 3: Technical interview. For engineering roles, expect one or two technical sessions covering your core domain. Software engineers typically face questions on data structures, algorithms, and system design. Embedded and firmware engineers discuss real-time systems, microcontrollers, and past project work. Mechanical and hardware engineers talk through design decisions, hands-on experience, and problem-solving trade-offs. These sessions run longer than the earlier rounds, so plan for a substantial block of time in your calendar.

Round 4: Hiring manager or leadership round. The final conversation usually involves the hiring manager or a senior leader. Focus shifts to how you think, how you handle ambiguity, and how you would approach real problems the team faces. Offer timelines after this stage tend to be quick for strong candidates.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Across roles, a few qualities come up consistently in how robotics startups at this stage assess candidates.

Hands-on experience with physical systems. Candidates who have built, tested, or debugged hardware or firmware in a real setting stand out. Academic projects count, but being able to talk through what broke, what you changed, and why it worked matters more than a polished project description.

Comfort with ambiguity. At a startup of this size, requirements shift and problems do not always have a clean answer. Interviewers tend to probe whether you can make reasonable decisions with incomplete information rather than waiting for a perfect spec.

Strong domain fundamentals. For software roles: solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and how software interacts with hardware. For embedded roles: C or C++, RTOS concepts, communication protocols. For mechanical roles: CAD proficiency, manufacturing awareness, and the ability to explain trade-offs in design choices.

Ownership mindset. Robotics startups need people who take problems to completion. Interviewers often ask about situations where you identified a gap and fixed it without being told to.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study the product and the problem it solves. Understand how warehouse robotics works, what sortation systems do, and why Indian e-commerce logistics needs automation. Being able to connect your background to the company's actual product signals genuine interest rather than a generic application.

Brush up on fundamentals relevant to your role. For software engineers: data structures, algorithms, and at least one systems-level topic such as concurrency or memory management. For embedded engineers: C or C++ basics, interrupt handling, and common communication protocols like UART, SPI, or I2C. For mechanical engineers: GD&T, tolerance stack-up, and manufacturing processes.

Prepare two or three strong project stories. Pick projects where you can clearly describe the problem, your specific contribution, the obstacles you hit, and the outcome. Robotics interviewers almost always ask about real work you have done.

Practice thinking aloud. Interviewers at startups care as much about how you reason as whether you reach the right answer. If you get stuck, narrate your thinking rather than going silent.

Have honest compensation expectations ready. Startups appreciate directness. Research what engineers at companies of similar stage and size are paid (Glassdoor and levels.fyi have data for comparable roles) so you can give a grounded range.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Unbox Robotics operates as an early-stage deep-tech startup, which shapes day-to-day culture in tangible ways.

Speed and iteration. The team moves quickly from idea to prototype to deployment. People who enjoy seeing their work in production fast, even if it means revisiting decisions later, tend to thrive here.

Cross-functional work. Software engineers work closely with hardware and firmware teams. Silos are harder to maintain when a bug might be in the code, the firmware, or the mechanical assembly. Comfort collaborating across disciplines is genuinely valued, not just listed on a careers page.

Learning on the job. Warehouse robotics is a narrow domain and most new hires arrive without deep prior experience in it. The expectation is that you pick up the domain quickly. If you prefer a structured onboarding with every answer documented, a startup at this stage may feel uncomfortable.

Impact visibility. With a smaller team, individual contributions are visible to leadership. This can be motivating for people who want their work noticed, and it also means there is less cover when something goes wrong.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Unbox Robotics hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Unbox Robotics does hire freshers, particularly for engineering roles where strong project work or academic research in robotics, embedded systems, or mechanical design is evident. Competition for fresher spots at a startup of this size is real, so having at least one substantial project you can discuss in depth is important. Experienced candidates with a few years in a relevant domain tend to have more openings available to them across the 31 current roles.

Is it necessary to be based in Pune to apply?

Most roles at Unbox Robotics require you to work from their Pune facility, since the work involves physical robots and hardware that cannot be done remotely. A small number of business and sales roles may offer flexibility. Confirm the location requirement for your specific role in the job description before applying, so there are no surprises later in the process.

What programming languages does Unbox Robotics use for software and embedded roles?

For software roles, Python is commonly used for backend systems and robotics middleware such as ROS. C and C++ are standard for embedded and firmware roles. Familiarity with ROS or ROS 2 is a strong plus for robotics software positions, though it is not always a strict requirement. Check the specific job description for the stack details of the role you are targeting.

How long does the full hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on publicly reported patterns for Indian robotics startups at a similar stage, the process typically wraps up in two to four weeks from first contact to offer. Delays can happen when multiple teams are involved or when the role has a small candidate pool. Following up politely after each round is reasonable and generally welcomed by the recruiting team.

What salary can I expect at Unbox Robotics?

Unbox Robotics does not publish salary bands publicly. Glassdoor and levels.fyi have some data points for comparable robotics startup roles in Pune, though sample sizes are small and figures vary widely. Startup compensation at this stage often combines a base salary with equity or ESOPs, so ask the HR team about the full package rather than focusing only on fixed pay.

Is there an online assessment before the technical interview?

Some roles, particularly software engineering positions, include an online coding or aptitude assessment after the initial resume screen. This is not universal across all roles at Unbox Robotics. Hardware, mechanical, and operations roles are more likely to move directly to a call. The recruiter will typically tell you what to expect before sending any assessment link.
