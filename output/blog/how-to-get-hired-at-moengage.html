01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

MoEngage is a Bangalore-based SaaS company that builds customer engagement and marketing automation software. Brands use the platform to send personalised push notifications, emails, SMS, and in-app messages at scale. The platform serves major consumer names across India, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and beyond.

As of mid-2026, MoEngage has 32 open roles across functions including engineering, product, sales, customer success, and marketing. They hire across experience levels, from fresh graduates joining as associate engineers or business development reps to senior leaders in product and go-to-market roles.

The company competes with global names in the marketing technology space and has been growing steadily. That growth means active hiring, and roles here are competitive but very approachable if you prepare well.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The MoEngage hiring process typically runs across 4-5 rounds, though the exact number varies by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Round 1: Application screening
After you apply, the HR or talent acquisition team reviews your resume. For tech roles, this may be followed by an automated screening or a take-home coding assignment to filter applicants before the interview rounds begin.

Round 2: Recruiter call
A recruiter reaches out to discuss your background, interest in MoEngage, notice period, and salary expectations. This round is mostly a fit-check before deeper technical or domain rounds begin.

Round 3: Technical or domain round
For engineering roles, expect coding problems covering data structures and algorithms, or a system design discussion for senior candidates. Product candidates typically face a case study or product teardown exercise. Sales and customer success candidates usually do a role-play or mock pitch scenario.

Round 4: Hiring manager round
You meet the team lead or manager. They go deeper into your past work, how you handled specific situations, and whether your thinking matches what the team needs. Scenario-based and behavioural questions are common here.

Round 5: Leadership or culture round
For senior roles, a final conversation with a director or VP-level leader focuses on culture fit, long-term goals, and strategic thinking rather than technical depth.

Candidates typically hear back within a week or two after each round, though timelines can stretch during busy hiring periods.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

MoEngage looks for people who combine strong fundamentals with genuine curiosity about how products and customers connect.

For engineers, they want clean problem-solving skills, comfort with distributed systems or data pipelines (depending on the team), and experience working in fast-moving product environments. For senior roles, system design depth matters considerably.

For product managers, they value structured thinking, the ability to translate customer feedback into roadmap decisions, and familiarity with engagement or analytics platforms.

For sales and customer success roles, they prefer people who understand SaaS metrics, can speak the language of marketing or product teams at other companies, and are proactive about building long-term relationships.

Across all roles, MoEngage values ownership. Candidates who have driven something end-to-end rather than just completing tasks assigned to them tend to stand out clearly in interviews.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before any round. Sign up for a MoEngage trial or watch their product demos online. Understanding how push notifications, in-app messaging, and analytics work on the platform helps you give grounded answers in every round.

Study their customer case studies. MoEngage publishes case studies from well-known Indian consumer brands. Referencing these in your answers shows you understand the real problems they solve, not just the marketing pitch.

For engineering roles, practise data structures and algorithm problems at a medium level and prepare at least one system design discussion. Real-time data processing and scalability are relevant areas given what MoEngage builds.

For product and GTM roles, prepare a product critique of a competitor or adjacent tool. Use the STAR format (situation, task, action, result) for behavioural questions about past experience.

Craft your 'why MoEngage' answer carefully. Generic answers about growth and culture do not land well. Connect your background specifically to the customer engagement space or the type of brands MoEngage serves.

Ask sharp questions. Questions about how the team measures success, what the current roadmap looks like, or how cross-functional collaboration works signal genuine preparation. Interviewers at product companies notice when candidates ask good questions.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

MoEngage describes itself as customer-first and data-driven. In practice, this means teams are expected to back decisions with data, and there is a high bar for clarity in communication, whether in a product spec, a sales deck, or an engineering design document.

The company has offices in Bangalore, Mumbai, and internationally, and operates a mix of in-office and hybrid setups depending on the team and location. Publicly shared employee reviews commonly describe a collaborative environment where individual contributors are given real ownership over outcomes.

MoEngage invests in employee development through internal learning programs and encourages participation in product-led growth conversations and community events. For professionals who enjoy working in a fast-growing B2B SaaS company where the product is used by recognisable consumer brands, the culture tends to resonate well.
