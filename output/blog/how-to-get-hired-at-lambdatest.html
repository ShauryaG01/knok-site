01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

LambdaTest is one of India's fastest-growing SaaS companies, building a cloud-based browser and app testing platform used by developers and QA engineers worldwide. The company is headquartered in San Francisco but runs a strong engineering hub in India, making it a compelling choice for tech professionals who want to work on a global product without relocating. As of mid-2026, LambdaTest has 16 open roles spanning engineering, product, sales, and customer success.

Hiring at LambdaTest is practical and skills-first. The team values people who can actually build, test, or sell, not just talk about it. If you are targeting a role in QA automation, backend engineering, DevOps, or enterprise sales, these are active hiring areas for the company right now. The process is structured enough to be predictable but moves reasonably fast compared to larger MNC setups, which is a relief if you are tired of month-long hiring loops.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs across four to five stages. The exact number varies by role and seniority, so treat this as a general map rather than a guarantee.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Shortlist. Your resume goes through an initial screen by the talent team. For technical roles, a visible GitHub profile, portfolio links, or evidence of real projects can help you stand out here. Make sure your resume clearly lists tools, languages, and frameworks you have actually used.

Stage 2: Recruiter Call. If shortlisted, a recruiter will reach out for a short introductory call. Expect questions about your current role, notice period, salary expectations, and why LambdaTest interests you. This round is mostly about fit and logistics, not deep technical questions.

Stage 3: Technical Assessment. For engineering and QA roles, a take-home assignment or an online coding test is common at this stage. The problems tend to be practical: write a Selenium script, debug a failing test suite, or solve a data structures problem. Candidates for non-technical roles may skip this and go directly to a skills interview.

Stage 4: Technical Interview(s). This is the core evaluation round. For engineers, expect questions on your primary stack, testing frameworks, CI/CD pipelines, and debugging approaches. For QA-specific roles, browser testing concepts, test planning, and automation tools like Playwright, Cypress, or Selenium are commonly assessed. Senior candidates typically face one additional round at this stage.

Stage 5: System Design or Leadership Round. Senior engineers and tech leads are usually asked to walk through a system design problem. This is less about textbook answers and more about how you think through trade-offs, scale, and failure scenarios. For non-engineering roles at senior levels, this stage often becomes a case discussion or a strategy conversation.

Stage 6: HR and Final Round. The last round is typically with HR or a senior leader. Compensation, role expectations, and team fit are discussed here. Come prepared with your own questions about growth, team structure, and what success looks like in the role.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

LambdaTest builds developer tooling, so the bar for engineering roles is hands-on and practical. Here is what consistently matters across roles.

For engineering and QA roles: Comfort with browser automation tools such as Selenium, Playwright, and Cypress is a strong plus, even for backend engineers, because you will be building or integrating with a testing platform. Solid fundamentals in your primary language, an understanding of CI/CD workflows, and the ability to write clean, testable code all matter. Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure is useful in DevOps and infrastructure roles.

For sales and customer success roles: LambdaTest sells to developers and engineering teams, so you need to be comfortable talking tech without being a software engineer yourself. Understanding what a QA team actually does, what 'cross-browser testing' means, and why automation matters will help you sell more convincingly. Prior SaaS sales experience or experience working with developer tools is a clear advantage.

Across all roles: Clear communication, ownership of past work, and genuine curiosity about the product come up repeatedly in how LambdaTest describes its hiring bar. The company moves fast, so people who self-direct and do not wait to be told what to do tend to do well here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product before your first call. Sign up for the free trial on LambdaTest and actually run a test or two. Understanding what the platform does, how it feels to use, and where you see room for improvement will make every interview conversation sharper. Interviewers notice when a candidate has genuinely used the product versus when they have only read the 'About Us' page.

Brush up on browser testing fundamentals. Even if you are applying for a backend or infrastructure role, having a working knowledge of Selenium, Playwright, or Cypress helps. The LambdaTest blog publishes extensively on testing topics and is a good way to understand how their customers use the platform.

Prepare a specific 'why LambdaTest' answer. 'I want to work at a fast-growing startup' is a weak answer. A stronger answer connects your background to what LambdaTest is building. If you have a QA background, talk about a specific problem in test infrastructure that excites you.

Have concrete examples ready. Many of the interviews are competency-based. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for questions about past work. Quantify outcomes where you can, even rough numbers help.

Prepare your own questions. Asking about the team's current technical challenges, the roadmap for the product area you would own, or how success is measured signals that you are serious. Avoid generic questions like 'what is the work culture like' unless you want a generic answer.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

LambdaTest has the energy of a company that grew very fast and is still figuring out how to scale its culture alongside its product. For the right person, that is exciting. For someone who prefers slow deliberation and rigid process, it can feel like a lot.

Ownership is central. People at LambdaTest are expected to own their areas completely, from writing the code to shipping it to watching what happens after. The org is flat enough that junior engineers can directly influence product decisions, which is unusual compared to larger companies.

The pace is real. Publicly reported employee reviews mention that priorities can shift quickly and the workload is high. If you thrive in that environment, you will likely grow fast here. If you need stable roadmaps and long planning cycles, expect an adjustment period.

The India team is close to the product. Unlike some global SaaS companies where the India office handles support work, LambdaTest's Indian engineering teams are core to the product. Engineers here build features that ship to global customers, which is a meaningful difference from a career-growth perspective.

Growth is available but you have to reach for it. There is no rigid promotion timeline. People who take on more and demonstrate results move up. People who wait for annual reviews to surface their contributions tend to find the process less satisfying.

Frequently asked

How long does the LambdaTest hiring process take from application to offer?

The process commonly takes a few weeks for most roles, though this can stretch or compress depending on how quickly both sides move. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds tend to take a bit longer than sales or support roles. Staying responsive to recruiter messages and turning around assessments promptly on your end helps keep the process moving.

Does LambdaTest hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

LambdaTest does hire freshers, particularly for QA, support, and some engineering roles, though many open positions ask for at least some relevant hands-on experience. A strong internship background, open-source contributions, or a solid portfolio can help offset the experience gap. Check the specific job description closely, as requirements vary significantly by team.

What is the salary range at LambdaTest for software engineers in India?

Based on publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, software engineer salaries at LambdaTest in India vary by level, city, and negotiation. The ranges commonly cited reflect the broader SaaS startup market rather than legacy IT pay scales. Always negotiate based on your specific offer and verify current numbers on Glassdoor before going into that conversation.

Is there a coding test in the LambdaTest interview process?

For most engineering and QA roles, yes, there is typically an online assessment or take-home task early in the process. The format varies: some roles use HackerRank-style coding problems while others give a practical automation task involving browser testing tools. Reviewing data structures basics and brushing up on the testing framework most relevant to your role will help you do well.

Does LambdaTest offer remote work for India-based employees?

LambdaTest has offered hybrid and remote options for many roles, though this varies by team and role type. Customer-facing or sales roles may prefer candidates in specific cities. The job description for each opening usually specifies the work model, so read it carefully before applying rather than assuming all roles are fully remote.

What is the best way to get noticed by LambdaTest recruiters?

A direct application with a tailored resume that mentions browser testing, CI/CD, or automation tools relevant to LambdaTest tends to perform better than a generic CV. Having a visible GitHub profile with real projects, or being active in the developer testing community, can also put you on a recruiter's radar. Referrals from current employees are another strong path in, so check whether anyone in your network works at LambdaTest before applying cold.
