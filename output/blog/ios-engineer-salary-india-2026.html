01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most iOS Engineer job listings in India do not show a salary figure. Recruiters write 'as per industry standards' or leave the field blank, which means you walk into the first screening call without knowing the budget. Of the 70 iOS Engineer openings tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026, salary data was absent from nearly all listings.

What you do know going in: iOS engineering is a specialist skill with fewer trained practitioners than Java or Python backend roles. That scarcity gives iOS engineers a real negotiating edge, especially if your profile shows Swift and SwiftUI experience rather than Objective-C only. Stack depth matters too: engineers who have worked with ARKit, CoreML, or complex push-notification architectures can justify a premium over someone who has only built standard CRUD apps.

Company type shapes the offer as much as experience does. A product company in Bangalore will pay differently from a mid-size IT services firm in Delhi, even for the same role title and years of experience. Knowing your market number before the call is the single most useful preparation you can do.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

The 70 openings tracked carry no in-built salary data, so the figures below come from Glassdoor and publicly reported industry surveys. Use them as a market reference, not a guarantee, because actual offers depend on company size, funding stage, and the depth of your iOS stack.

Junior iOS Engineer (1-3 years): Glassdoor and industry surveys commonly cite 8-15 LPA for this band. Engineers who have shipped at least one live App Store product tend to land nearer the top of that range even without many years of experience.

Mid-level iOS Engineer (3-6 years): Publicly reported figures from Glassdoor and levels.fyi place this band at 15-28 LPA. Strong code-review skills, ownership of a feature module, and CI/CD experience for iOS apps help push offers higher.

Senior iOS Engineer (6-10 years): Glassdoor and industry surveys commonly cite 28-50 LPA at this level. Candidates who can lead architecture decisions or mentor junior developers command the upper part of this range.

Staff or Principal iOS Engineer (10+ years): Levels.fyi and publicly reported data suggest 50 LPA and above, with RSUs and performance bonuses making up a substantial share of the total package at top-tier product companies.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Get your benchmark from Glassdoor and levels.fyi before the recruiter call. Walk in knowing the publicly reported range for your level so you can anchor the conversation rather than react to the first offer.

Delay revealing your current salary for as long as the conversation allows. Many companies no longer require disclosure, and the longer you wait, the more leverage you retain.

Negotiate the full package, not just the fixed component. Ask about ESOPs or RSUs, annual bonus structure, joining bonus, and the performance review cycle. A company with a fast review cycle can move you up a band within a year or two.

iOS scarcity is real. If a recruiter has been searching for several weeks, they are under pressure. That is the right moment to hold firm on your stated range rather than concede.

Get the complete offer in writing before you resign from your current role. Verbal commitments from hiring managers are not binding, and offer letters sometimes differ from what was discussed on the call.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Total compensation for an iOS Engineer in India combines several elements beyond the basic salary figure.

Fixed salary (CTC): This is what most offer letters lead with. It includes basic pay, HRA, and standard allowances, and is the number that appears on your monthly payslip.

Variable pay: A performance-linked bonus, usually expressed as a percentage of fixed CTC. Based on publicly reported industry surveys, product companies typically offer a higher variable percentage than IT services firms.

ESOPs or RSUs: Equity grants that vest over time. At funded startups, ESOPs typically vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. At listed companies or MNCs, RSUs convert to shares on a vesting schedule. Always ask for the current valuation basis before factoring equity into your decision.

Joining bonus: A one-time payment to cover notice-period gaps or as a sweetener. It is often clawback-protected for one year, meaning you repay it if you leave early.

Benefits: Health insurance for yourself and dependents, laptop, internet allowance, and leave encashment add real value. Compare these carefully when two offers look close on the base number.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most iOS Engineer job listings in India not show salary?

Indian hiring culture has historically treated salary as something discussed after shortlisting, not before. Employers believe that showing a number upfront either limits their candidate pool or invites candidates to inflate expectations. This practice is slowly changing at product companies influenced by global norms, but it remains the default on most Indian job boards as of 2026. Your best defence is to research Glassdoor ranges before any recruiter call.

Is Delhi really the top city for iOS jobs right now?

Based on the 70 openings tracked by knok jobradar in July 2026, Delhi had 21 open roles, more than three times Bangalore's 6. This reflects the concentration of enterprise and digital-agency work in the NCR region. Bangalore remains preferred for product-first companies, but those roles may be listed under broader engineering titles or filled through referrals before appearing on public job boards.

Should I negotiate even for my first iOS job?

Yes, and iOS specialists are relatively scarce, which gives freshers more negotiating room than candidates in crowded tracks like backend Java. Before your first offer conversation, check Glassdoor reviews for that company to understand the typical junior band. Ask whether the fixed salary is negotiable and whether there is a joining bonus, even at entry level. The worst outcome is a polite no.

What skills push an iOS salary toward the higher end?

Employers commonly reward depth in SwiftUI, Combine, and async/await patterns because many teams are still migrating from older UIKit codebases. Experience with App Store Connect, push notifications at scale, and performance profiling using Instruments also commands a premium. If you have shipped an app with strong ratings or significant monthly active users, put those metrics front and centre in your resume.

How do I compare a startup offer against an MNC offer?

Start by converting ESOPs or RSUs to a realistic rupee value using the current valuation or share price, then discount for risk. A pre-Series B startup ESOP is speculative; a listed MNC RSU is much closer to cash. Compare fixed CTC, variable payout history (ask what percentage of employees actually received their target bonus last year), health cover, and growth speed. A smaller fixed at a fast-growing product company can outperform a larger fixed at a flat-growth services firm if the equity vests well.
