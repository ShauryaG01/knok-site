01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

INFINIDAT builds enterprise storage infrastructure, best known for InfiniBox, a high-performance storage platform used by large banks, healthcare organisations, and telecoms worldwide. The company runs a lean, highly technical team, which means every hire is expected to contribute from day one and the screening process reflects that standard.

As of July 2026, INFINIDAT has 45 open roles spanning software engineering, solutions engineering, cloud and infrastructure, sales, and technical support. In India, most openings tend to be in software development and customer-facing technical roles, though the mix changes as the company grows.

If you are targeting INFINIDAT, expect a structured multi-round process, a strong technical bar, and a culture that prizes deep expertise and direct communication. The sections below break down exactly what to expect and how to prepare.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screening
Most candidates apply through the INFINIDAT careers page or are referred via LinkedIn. Recruiters screen for relevant experience in enterprise software, storage infrastructure, or systems engineering. A resume that clearly maps your skills to storage, distributed systems, or enterprise sales will move through faster than a generic one.

Stage 2: Recruiter or HR Call
A short introductory call with a recruiter covers your background, current notice period, location, and general interest in the company's technology. This is a fit check, not a technical round. Come prepared with a crisp summary of your experience and clear questions about the role and team structure.

Stage 3: Technical Phone Screen
For engineering roles, this is typically a live coding or problem-solving session with a senior engineer. Expect questions on data structures, algorithms, and systems concepts. For solutions or pre-sales roles, the focus shifts to how clearly you can explain complex storage architecture to a customer who is not an engineer.

Stage 4: Deep Technical Rounds
This is the most intensive part of the process. Engineering candidates commonly face questions on OS internals, storage protocols (NVMe, iSCSI, NFS), system design, and debugging under real constraints. Solutions engineering candidates may be asked to walk through a mock customer scenario or architecture review. Panels are typically small, often two or three interviewers per session.

Stage 5: Hiring Manager or Leadership Round
A conversation with the hiring manager or a senior leader covers your past projects, how you handle ambiguity, and why you want to work on enterprise storage. This round also assesses culture fit and your ability to give and receive direct feedback without defensiveness.

Stage 6: Offer and Onboarding
HR presents the offer after the panel debrief. Onboarding at INFINIDAT typically includes structured product deep dives and paired work with experienced team members before you take on independent ownership.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Technical Depth Over Breadth
INFINIDAT works on hard infrastructure problems. They consistently look for engineers and technical staff who understand systems at a low level, not just people who have used tools but people who know how those tools work internally. Knowledge of storage protocols, file systems, Linux internals, or distributed systems is a strong signal for engineering roles.

Ownership and Initiative
The team is lean relative to the scale of product they ship. Interviewers pay close attention to whether you have driven projects from start to finish, caught and fixed problems before being asked, and made calls under uncertainty. Bring specific examples from your past work where your individual decision changed an outcome.

Clear Communication
Because INFINIDAT sells to large enterprise customers, everyone on the team, including engineers, is expected to communicate clearly with both technical peers and non-technical stakeholders. Your ability to explain a complex idea simply is tested in technical rounds and in leadership conversations alike.

Customer and Product Mindset
Even for purely engineering roles, INFINIDAT interviewers tend to ask how you think about the customer impact of technical decisions. Showing that you connect your work to business outcomes, not just code quality, resonates well across teams.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the Product First
Before your first call, read up on InfiniBox and InfiniGuard. Understand the core value proposition: extreme reliability and performance for mission-critical enterprise workloads. Being able to articulate why a bank or hospital would choose INFINIDAT over a generic cloud option shows genuine interest and saves interview time for the harder questions.

Sharpen Your Systems Knowledge
For engineering roles, review storage fundamentals: block vs. file vs. object storage, RAID concepts, NVMe and iSCSI protocols, caching architectures, and Linux I/O scheduling. Practice explaining these from first principles, not just reciting definitions. Interviewers will probe whether you truly understand the trade-offs involved.

Practice Coding in a Time-Boxed Setting
Technical screens are live and timed. Practice data structures and algorithms problems on platforms you are already comfortable with. Focus on explaining your thought process out loud as you code, since interviewers evaluate both the answer and how you reason through uncertainty when you hit a dead end.

Prepare Your Project Stories
Choose two or three past projects where you had clear ownership, faced a hard technical or organisational problem, and delivered a measurable outcome. Structure each story around the situation, your specific actions, and the result. Avoid stories where you were a passive contributor to a team win.

Ask Smart Questions
INFINIDAT interviewers notice when a candidate has done their homework. Prepare questions about the team's current engineering challenges, how on-call responsibilities are structured, what the product roadmap looks like for the next year, and how success is measured in the role. This signals that you are evaluating them as much as they are evaluating you.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Performance-Driven and Technically Serious
INFINIDAT was founded by the same team that built core technology behind some of the most widely deployed enterprise storage systems in the world. That founding DNA shapes the culture: the company takes technical excellence seriously and expects engineers to engage with hard problems rather than work around them. Cutting corners or shipping without understanding the system is not the norm here.

Direct and Low-Hierarchy
The team operates with a relatively flat structure and a direct communication style. Feedback is given plainly, decisions are made quickly, and people are expected to push back when they disagree. If you come from a large-company culture where decisions move slowly through many layers, expect an adjustment period when you join.

Customer Outcomes as the North Star
Infrastructure failures at a large bank or hospital have real consequences. That weight shapes how the team thinks about quality, testing, and reliability. People who take pride in their work holding up under pressure, and who feel a sense of responsibility toward the end user, tend to thrive here.

Global Team, Local Execution
INFINIDAT has offices across North America, Europe, and Asia. India-based employees often collaborate closely with teams in other time zones, which means strong async communication skills and some flexibility in working hours are practical realities of the role rather than just nice-to-haves.
