01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most job listings on Indian platforms skip the salary field entirely, writing 'as per industry standards' or leaving it blank. That is why the figures here come from knok jobradar data, which tracked 33 active Product Designer openings in Delhi NCR as of July 8, 2026. These numbers reflect what companies are actually offering in the current market, not what they are willing to advertise publicly.

Product Designers in Delhi NCR can expect salaries ranging from 6 LPA at the entry level to 55+ LPA at the Lead or Principal tier. The biggest pay jumps happen at the mid-to-senior transition, where independent research skills and end-to-end product ownership push the number significantly higher. Delhi NCR's market is shaped by a mix of funded startups in Gurugram, enterprise software companies, edtech firms, and large MNC offices in Noida, each with different pay structures and growth paths.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Getting the offer is the first half. Getting the right number is the second half.

Know your target range before the screening call. Recruiters often ask 'what are your salary expectations' in the very first conversation. Saying 'negotiable' gives away your anchor. Use the band for your experience level and state a figure at the upper end if your portfolio supports it.

Ask for the full CTC breakup in writing. Base salary, variable pay, ESOPs, joining bonus, and benefits all make up the total package. Two offers with the same headline CTC can have very different monthly in-hand amounts, so always ask for the full structure before comparing.

Time your offers if you can. If two companies are interested at the same time, try to push both toward a decision together. A real competing offer, stated politely and factually, is the most effective negotiation tool available to a job seeker in India.

Lead with impact in your follow-up email. After a verbal offer, send a short note restating one outcome from your past work using your own real numbers. Impact language gives the hiring manager something concrete to justify a higher figure to their finance team.

Get the offer letter before resigning. A verbal commitment is not a guarantee. Wait for the written CTC breakup, confirm the joining date, and only then put in your papers.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

The CTC (Cost to Company) figure on your offer letter and your actual monthly in-hand salary are two different things. Here is what a typical Product Designer offer in Delhi NCR includes.

Base salary is the fixed monthly component. Industry surveys commonly describe base pay as the largest fixed portion of CTC for mid to senior designers, and it is what you should use for financial planning and day-to-day budgeting.

Variable pay (also called performance bonus or PLI) is paid once or twice a year, depending on your individual rating and sometimes on overall company performance. Ask specifically what the last two payout cycles actually delivered for your band, since the stated target is rarely paid out in full every year.

ESOPs or RSUs are offered at many funded startups and larger product companies. If equity is on the table, ask about the vesting schedule, the strike price relative to the last valuation round, and whether a liquidity event is on the horizon. Equity can be significant but stays illiquid until a buyback, secondary sale, or IPO.

Retirals like Provident Fund and gratuity are counted inside the CTC by most Indian employers. Check whether PF is calculated on basic salary or on the full CTC, as this changes your monthly in-hand amount more than most candidates expect.

Benefits such as group health insurance, meal allowances, internet reimbursements, and learning budgets vary widely across companies. A family health cover is worth factoring in when comparing two otherwise similar offers.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the typical salary for a Product Designer in Delhi NCR in 2026?

Based on knok jobradar data from July 8, 2026, Product Designers in Delhi NCR earn between 6 LPA at the entry level and 55+ LPA at the Lead or Principal tier. A mid-level designer with 3-5 years of experience typically lands in the 14-24 LPA range, which is the most active hiring band right now. The wide spread reflects the variety of employers hiring, from early-stage startups to large MNCs and well-funded product companies.

Is Delhi NCR a good city for Product Designers, or should I consider Bangalore?

Both cities have active markets, but Bangalore leads with 62 open roles versus Delhi NCR's 33 as of July 8, 2026. Bangalore has more volume and a higher density of early-stage startups, while Delhi NCR offers strong opportunities in fintech, edtech, and enterprise software. Salary bands are broadly comparable across the two cities, so the decision often comes down to company type and personal fit rather than pay alone.

How do I know if a salary offer is fair for a Product Designer role?

Compare the offer against the band for your experience level, using knok jobradar data: 6-12 LPA for 0-2 years, 14-24 LPA for 3-5 years, 26-40 LPA for 6-9 years, and 36-55+ LPA for Lead or Principal roles. Also ask for the full CTC breakup, not just the headline number, since base pay, variable pay, and equity can vary significantly between companies. A lower base with strong ESOPs at a high-growth startup may be worth more over time than a higher base at a slower-moving company.

Do startups pay Product Designers differently from MNCs in Delhi NCR?

Generally yes. Startups often offer a lower fixed salary but compensate with ESOPs, faster career growth, and broader ownership of design decisions across the product. MNCs and large product companies tend to offer more predictable pay, structured appraisal cycles, and stronger retiral benefits. The right choice depends on your risk appetite and whether you value equity and growth speed over monthly in-hand certainty.

What skills help a Product Designer reach the higher salary bands?

Designers commanding senior or lead-level salaries typically combine strong visual craft with product thinking: they run user research, write clear design specs, define design systems, and use data to influence product decisions. Proficiency in Figma is expected at every level in Delhi NCR. What separates designers earning 26+ LPA from those in the mid range is usually the ability to show measurable impact from past work and to lead design decisions without waiting for direction.

How important is a portfolio versus years of experience when negotiating salary?

Both matter, but portfolio quality can outweigh years of experience at many companies. A designer with strong end-to-end case studies that show clear outcomes (improved task completion, reduced drop-off, a shipped design system used by the whole team) can often negotiate toward the upper end of their band. Years of experience set the baseline expectation, but the portfolio is what pushes you to the top of the range and makes a case for crossing into the next one.
