01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

PepsiCo India is one of the country's largest FMCG employers, with a presence spanning snacks, beverages, and foods. The company hires across functions including sales, marketing, supply chain, manufacturing, finance, human resources, and technology.

As of mid-2026, knok jobradar is tracking 26 open roles at PepsiCo across India. Openings span multiple levels, from early career and campus hires to experienced professionals and senior leaders, across cities such as Gurugram, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and regional hubs.

PepsiCo recruits through campus programmes at top business and engineering institutes, direct applications on their careers portal, and lateral hiring throughout the year. The process is structured and typically runs through multiple rounds, so knowing what to expect at each step gives you a real advantage.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

PepsiCo's hiring process usually runs through four to six stages, depending on the role and level. Here is what candidates commonly go through:

StageWhat Happens
Application ReviewYour resume is screened against the job description. ATS filters are standard practice, so keywords from the job posting matter.
HR Screening CallA recruiter gets in touch to verify your background, discuss the role, and understand your notice period and salary expectations.
Functional or Technical RoundOne or two rounds with the hiring manager or team. Expect questions on your domain, past projects, and how you handle specific business situations.
Case Study or PresentationCommon for marketing, sales strategy, and finance roles. You may be given a business problem to solve and present to a panel.
Senior Leader RoundA conversation with a director or VP level leader, focused on leadership approach, long-term goals, and cultural fit.
HR and Offer DiscussionFinal discussion with HR covering compensation, joining date, and documentation.

The full process from application to offer commonly spans several weeks. Timelines can stretch for senior roles or when multiple candidates are being evaluated simultaneously. If you have not heard back within two weeks of a round, a polite follow-up with your recruiter is perfectly appropriate.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Ownership and accountability. PepsiCo values candidates who take initiative and see tasks through without needing constant direction. In interviews, they frequently ask for examples where you owned a project end-to-end and drove clear outcomes.

Commercial thinking. Whether you are in sales, supply chain, or HR, the ability to connect your work to business results matters. Candidates who speak in terms of revenue impact, cost savings, or market growth tend to stand out.

Consumer and customer focus. PepsiCo is a consumer goods company at its core. Understanding shopper behaviour, retailer dynamics, and what drives purchase decisions is valued across functions, not just in marketing.

Collaboration across teams. Most roles involve working across functions, markets, and sometimes global teams. The ability to align stakeholders and move work forward without direct authority is a regularly tested skill.

Adaptability and learning agility. The FMCG sector moves quickly. PepsiCo looks for people who pick up new skills, operate comfortably in ambiguity, and recover fast when plans change.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study PepsiCo's product portfolio and recent news. Know the major brands (Lay's, Kurkure, Pepsi, Tropicana, Quaker) and be ready to discuss the competitive landscape in snacks and beverages in India. Read any recent sustainability or business announcements before your interview.

Prepare STAR-format answers for behavioural questions. PepsiCo commonly uses competency-based interviewing. Structure your answers as: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Have four to five stories ready that demonstrate leadership, problem-solving, collaboration, and handling a setback.

Practise a case study if applying to commercial or strategy roles. Case prompts often involve market sizing, a new product launch, or fixing a distribution challenge. Walk through your thinking out loud rather than rushing straight to a conclusion.

Tailor your resume to the job description. Mirror the language in the posting. If the role mentions 'go-to-market execution', use that phrase where it accurately describes your experience, not a synonym that an ATS might miss.

Prepare thoughtful questions for the panel. Questions about team structure, how success is measured in the role, and what challenges the team is currently working on signal genuine interest and commercial curiosity.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

PepsiCo operates under a global agenda called 'pep+' (PepsiCo Positive), which centres on sustainable business practices, healthier product portfolios, and community impact. In India, this includes water conservation programmes, partnerships with farmers, and reducing the environmental footprint of manufacturing.

Day-to-day culture. Employees commonly describe the environment as fast-moving and performance-driven. Cross-functional collaboration is built into how most projects run, so expect to regularly work with teams outside your immediate function.

Seven Behaviours framework. PepsiCo uses an internal set of leadership behaviours that shape how performance is discussed and evaluated. Themes include consumer focus, winning together, and acting with speed. Familiarising yourself with these before your interview helps you frame your answers in language that resonates with interviewers.

Diversity and inclusion. PepsiCo India has publicly committed to increasing gender diversity at senior levels. Women returnee programmes and structured mentoring exist within the organisation, though experience of these programmes varies by team and location.

Growth opportunities. Lateral moves across functions and geographies are fairly common. PepsiCo's global footprint means high performers sometimes get exposure to regional or global projects, depending on business needs at the time.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does PepsiCo currently have in India?

As of mid-2026, knok jobradar is tracking 26 open roles at PepsiCo across India. The mix shifts frequently as positions are filled and new requirements come up. Setting up job alerts and checking regularly is the most reliable way to catch new postings early.

Does PepsiCo hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

PepsiCo runs structured campus hiring at select business and engineering institutes, so freshers can enter through those routes. For lateral roles, most positions expect a few years of relevant experience, though this varies by function. Front-line sales and certain supply chain roles can have lower experience requirements than corporate or strategy positions.

Is an MBA required for marketing or commercial roles at PepsiCo?

For brand management and marketing strategy roles, an MBA from a recognised institute is commonly preferred, though not always a strict requirement. Sales and trade marketing roles tend to place more weight on field experience and a track record of meeting targets. Publicly reported hiring patterns at large FMCG companies suggest an MBA significantly improves access to senior marketing tracks over time.

What is the typical salary at PepsiCo India?

Compensation varies widely by role, location, and experience level. Based on Glassdoor and publicly reported data, pay at PepsiCo India is broadly competitive with other large FMCG multinationals operating in the country. The best reference point is Glassdoor reviews filtered by the specific role title and city you are applying for, as band ranges differ considerably across functions.

How long does the PepsiCo hiring process take?

The process commonly spans several weeks from application to offer, depending on the seniority of the role and how many candidates are being evaluated at the same time. Senior and leadership roles tend to take longer than individual contributor positions. A polite follow-up with your recruiter after two weeks of silence following any round is considered normal and acceptable.

Does PepsiCo India offer remote or hybrid work?

Most corporate roles at PepsiCo India follow a hybrid model where employees come into the office on set days each week. The exact arrangement depends on the team and location. Field-based sales and supply chain roles are largely on-site by the nature of the work. It is worth clarifying the working model for your specific role during the HR screening call so there are no surprises after you join.
