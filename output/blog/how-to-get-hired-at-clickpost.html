01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Clickpost is a logistics intelligence and post-purchase experience platform used by ecommerce brands across India and South-East Asia. The company helps online retailers track shipments, automate returns, and keep buyers informed after every order. As of July 2026, Clickpost has 13 open roles listed via knok jobradar, spanning engineering, product management, sales, customer success, and operations.

Hiring at Clickpost is selective and focused. The team is lean and looks for people who can own their domain from day one. If you are aiming for a role here, expect a process that values practical thinking over textbook answers. Most roles are based out of their Gurugram (Delhi NCR) headquarters, with some remote-friendly positions in engineering.

Roles appear on major job platforms but tend to fill quickly, especially in engineering and customer success. If you are serious about landing a spot, applying early and tailoring your resume to the logistics-tech domain makes a real difference.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs across three to five rounds, though the exact stages vary by role and seniority. What follows is a general pattern based on publicly shared candidate experiences.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screening
The recruiter reviews your resume for relevant domain experience. For tech roles, a GitHub profile or portfolio of past projects helps. For sales and customer success, metrics from previous roles (targets hit, accounts managed) get attention. Response times at this stage vary, so follow up politely if you hear nothing after a week.

Stage 2: Recruiter or HR Screening Call
A short call to check intent, notice period, and general fit. Expect questions about why Clickpost and what you know about the logistics-tech space. Compensation expectations are also discussed here, so have a number in mind before you pick up.

Stage 3: Technical or Domain Round
For engineering roles, this is typically a coding or system design interview. Problems lean towards real-world scenarios: designing a shipment tracking system, handling webhook failures, or working with large-scale data pipelines. For product and business roles, expect a case study or short assignment related to ecommerce logistics, customer retention, or SaaS metrics.

Stage 4: Hiring Manager Round
A deeper conversation with the team lead or department head. Expect to walk through your past projects in detail. Interviewers want to understand how you approach ambiguous problems and whether you can work cross-functionally without hand-holding.

Stage 5: Culture and Leadership Fit
A final round with a senior leader or co-founder, especially for mid-to-senior roles. This is as much about values alignment as it is about skills. Come ready to talk about a time you took full ownership of something that did not go as planned and what you learned from it.

Clickpost currently has 13 open roles across functions, so the exact number of rounds you see may differ from what is described here. Always confirm the full process during your recruiter call.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Across roles, a few traits come up consistently in how Clickpost describes its ideal hires.

Ownership mindset. Clickpost is a growth-stage company, so team sizes are small and each person handles more than a narrow job description. They look for people who identify problems and fix them without waiting to be told. If you need a detailed playbook for every situation, this environment will feel uncomfortable.

Domain curiosity. Whether you are in engineering, sales, or operations, genuine interest in how ecommerce logistics works gives you an edge. Understanding concepts like NDR (non-delivery reports), RTO (return to origin), and post-purchase notification flows signals that you have done your homework before the interview.

Data fluency. Even for non-technical roles, the ability to read a dashboard, interpret trends, and make decisions from numbers matters. Product and customer success candidates who speak in terms of measurable outcomes stand out clearly from those who speak only in activities.

Communication clarity. Clickpost works with large enterprise clients. The ability to explain complex logistics issues in plain language, especially to non-technical stakeholders, is valued across every function in the company.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Learn the product. Sign up for Clickpost's free trial or explore their public resources. Understanding their tracking, returns, and notification features lets you speak specifically during interviews, which makes a strong impression compared to candidates who only read the 'About Us' page.

Study the ecommerce logistics landscape. Know the key players: courier aggregators, warehouse management systems, and direct logistics providers. Understand why post-purchase experience matters for customer retention in Indian ecommerce, and be ready to discuss it from a business perspective.

Prepare for system design if you are in engineering. Focus on distributed systems problems relevant to logistics: tracking event pipelines, webhook reliability, multi-carrier integrations, and large-scale notification delivery. Think through failure modes and how you would handle them at scale.

Rehearse your impact story. For non-tech roles, prepare a clear narrative around results from your past work. What was the scope of accounts you managed? What growth did you contribute to? Being specific about scale and outcomes (even in general terms) is far more persuasive than listing responsibilities.

Research their clients. Clickpost serves major ecommerce brands. Looking up their publicly listed clients and understanding the challenges those brands face helps you tailor your answers to what actually matters to the business, rather than giving generic interview responses.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Clickpost operates with a startup culture that prizes speed, directness, and accountability. The team is relatively small for the scale of clients they serve, which means individuals carry meaningful responsibility early in their tenure.

Bias for action. The culture rewards people who move quickly and iterate rather than waiting for perfect information. If you prefer structured, slow-moving environments, Clickpost may feel intense. If you thrive on variety and fast feedback loops, you will likely enjoy it.

Transparency. Leadership communicates openly about company goals and challenges, and they expect the same from team members. Feedback tends to be direct and frequent rather than reserved for formal review cycles.

Customer-first thinking. Because Clickpost's clients are ecommerce brands who in turn serve millions of buyers, there is a strong emphasis on understanding the downstream impact of every decision. Empathy for the end customer is woven into how teams discuss product changes and priorities.

Clickpost is a good fit if you want to grow fast, work close to real business problems, and be part of building something in a sector that is reshaping Indian commerce. Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the Clickpost hiring process take?

Most candidates who have shared their experience publicly report a timeline of two to four weeks from first contact to offer. Senior roles can run longer. The process moves quickly by startup standards, so be ready to respond to interview requests within a day or two of receiving them.

Does Clickpost hire freshers or entry-level candidates?

Clickpost occasionally hires freshers for engineering and analyst roles, particularly through campus referrals or internship-to-hire conversions. Most of the 13 current open roles are aimed at candidates with at least one year of relevant experience. Check individual job descriptions carefully before applying to confirm the level of experience expected.

What salary can I expect at Clickpost?

Glassdoor and publicly shared offer data show compensation that is competitive for a growth-stage B2B SaaS company, but figures vary widely by role, level, and the negotiation itself. Check current Glassdoor listings for the most recent reported ranges before your compensation discussion with the recruiter, so you go in with a grounded number.

Is remote work available at Clickpost?

Some engineering roles have been listed as remote or hybrid. Most business-facing roles in sales, customer success, and operations prefer candidates who can work from their Gurugram office at least part of the time. Confirm the exact working arrangement during your recruiter screening call before you progress further.

How important is it to know the logistics domain before applying?

You do not need deep logistics expertise for every role, but showing that you understand how post-purchase experience works will set you apart from the majority of applicants. For product and customer success roles especially, familiarity with terms like NDR, RTO, and shipment tracking is a clear differentiator in the screening stage.

What is the best way to get noticed by Clickpost recruiters?

A tailored resume that speaks directly to ecommerce logistics challenges is more effective than a generic application. A referral from someone inside the company helps significantly and is worth pursuing on LinkedIn before you apply cold. If you have built anything related to shipment tracking, returns automation, or SaaS integrations, highlight it prominently in both your resume and your cover note.
