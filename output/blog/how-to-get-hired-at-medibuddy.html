01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

MediBuddy is one of India's largest digital healthcare platforms, helping corporates manage employee health benefits through teleconsultations, hospital cashless services, insurance, and wellness programs. The company has scaled rapidly by signing enterprise clients across sectors, which means hiring is ongoing across multiple functions.

As of July 2026, knok's job radar shows MediBuddy has 58 open roles across functions like technology, product, business development, sales, customer success, and operations. Most openings are based in Bengaluru, where their headquarters sits, with select roles in Mumbai and Delhi or offered as hybrid setups depending on the team.

MediBuddy hires at all experience levels. Early-career candidates find openings in customer success, data, and operations. Mid-senior professionals are in demand for product management, engineering, and enterprise sales. The hiring bar is competitive but approachable, especially if you connect your background to their health-tech mission.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs across several stages, though this can vary by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Application and screening. After you apply online via their careers page or LinkedIn, a recruiter usually reaches out within a week or so if your profile is a strong match. The first call is a brief conversation covering your background, current role, notice period, and interest in health-tech.

Technical or functional round. For engineering roles, this is commonly a coding or system design round conducted over video call. For product roles, candidates report case studies around growth, retention, or feature prioritisation for a healthcare product. For sales and operations, expect situation-based questions around enterprise client handling or process problem-solving.

Hiring manager round. This is a deeper conversation with the team lead or manager. Expect questions about how you have handled specific past challenges, your domain knowledge (especially anything health-related), and how you think about the problem MediBuddy is solving. This round often includes a case discussion or a take-home assignment for product and business roles.

Leadership or cross-functional round. For senior roles, there is usually a round with a VP or senior leader covering strategic thinking, stakeholder management, and cultural alignment. For mid-level roles, this round may be merged with the hiring manager round or skipped entirely.

HR and offer round. The final HR call covers salary expectations, notice period, and joining date. Offers generally come within a few days of the final round, though timelines can stretch depending on internal approvals.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

MediBuddy sits at the intersection of healthcare and technology, so they look for people who care about both sides of that equation.

For engineering and product roles: Strong fundamentals matter, whether that is backend systems, data pipelines, or product thinking around metrics and user behaviour. Because healthcare data is sensitive, candidates who show awareness of data privacy and security practices stand out.

For sales and business development: The ability to sell to HR heads and CXOs at large corporates is essential. MediBuddy's clients are enterprises, so candidates with B2B sales experience, especially in insurance, SaaS, or healthcare, tend to do well. Relationship-building and the ability to explain complex health benefit products in simple terms are highly valued.

For operations and customer success: Empathy is non-negotiable. The end users are employees dealing with health issues, so candidates who show patience, clear communication, and problem-solving under pressure are preferred.

Across all roles, MediBuddy looks for people who are comfortable with ambiguity. As a fast-growing startup, processes change and priorities shift. Candidates who can adapt and take ownership without waiting for perfect instructions tend to progress further in the process.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product well. Before any interview, sign up for the MediBuddy app and explore it as a user. Understand how the teleconsultation flow works, how the corporate benefits portal is structured, and what the experience feels like. Interviewers respond well to candidates who have actually used the product.

Study the business model. MediBuddy earns primarily by selling corporate health benefit packages to employers. Understanding how they monetise through per-employee subscriptions, insurance partnerships, and hospital tie-ins helps you frame your answers in terms of business impact, not just features.

Prepare strong STAR stories. Competency-based questions ('Tell me about a time you handled a difficult client') are common across roles. Prepare a handful of stories that show ownership, problem-solving, and clear impact. For health-tech roles, stories involving cross-functional coordination or data-driven decisions land particularly well.

For engineering candidates: Practice system design with healthcare scenarios, such as appointment booking systems, health record storage, or notification pipelines. Review common backend patterns and be ready to discuss scalability and data privacy.

For product and business candidates: Prepare a case on improving a feature metric (like teleconsult completion rate) or a go-to-market plan for a new corporate client segment. Show that you think in terms of user needs and business outcomes together.

Salary research: For compensation benchmarks, check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for MediBuddy and comparable health-tech companies. Going in with a range based on publicly reported data makes your negotiation more credible.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

MediBuddy operates with a strong sense of mission. The team genuinely believes they are making healthcare more accessible for working Indians and their families, and that shows in how people talk about their work. Candidates who are motivated purely by salary without real interest in the domain often find the pace and the mission-heaviness tiring after a while.

Pace and ownership. It is a startup at scale, which means things move fast, roles are broader than the job description, and you are expected to take initiative. Managers tend to give autonomy rather than micromanage, which works well for self-starters but can feel unstructured to candidates coming from large organisations.

Collaboration. Because MediBuddy's product touches doctors, hospitals, insurers, and corporates, teams work closely across functions. Engineers sit near product managers. Sales teams coordinate daily with operations. Siloed working styles do not fit the culture.

Growth mindset. The company has expanded significantly in recent years, and employees who grew with it frequently mention learning as a major plus. If you want to move from a pure execution role into a broader strategic one, the paths exist, especially as new verticals open up.

For candidates coming from large corporates or MNCs, the adjustment to a leaner, faster environment can take a few months. Going in with that expectation, and genuine excitement about the mission, makes the transition much smoother.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does MediBuddy's hiring process take from application to offer?

The commonly cited timeline is two to four weeks from the initial recruiter call to a final offer, though this varies by role and seniority. Engineering roles sometimes move faster because there are more standardised rounds. Senior or leadership roles can take longer if multiple stakeholders are involved in the final decision. Following up politely with the recruiter after each round is a good way to stay visible without being pushy.

Does MediBuddy hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

MediBuddy does hire freshers, particularly in customer success, data, and operations roles. Engineering freshers may find entry points through internships that convert to full-time offers. For product, sales, and senior engineering roles, they generally expect a few years of relevant experience. Check their careers page directly and filter by experience level to find roles that match where you are in your career.

What is the work-from-home or hybrid policy at MediBuddy?

MediBuddy is primarily an in-office company at their Bengaluru headquarters, though some roles in sales, operations, and certain tech teams offer hybrid arrangements. Policies can differ by team and by how senior the role is. The best approach is to ask the recruiter directly during the initial screening call. Do not assume a role is remote or hybrid unless the job description clearly states it.

How important is healthcare domain knowledge for non-clinical roles?

You do not need a medical background for engineering, product, or business roles at MediBuddy. However, showing genuine curiosity about how healthcare in India works, the challenges of health insurance adoption, or the gap in primary care access gives you a clear edge. Candidates who have thought about these problems tend to ask better questions in interviews, which leaves a strong impression on the panel.

How competitive is it to get an interview at MediBuddy?

With 58 open roles currently listed, there is meaningful hiring activity happening, which improves your chances compared to a company with a frozen pipeline. That said, popular roles like software engineering and product management attract many applicants. A tailored resume that connects your experience to MediBuddy's specific product areas, combined with a warm referral from someone inside the company, gives you the best shot at clearing the initial screening.

What salary range can I expect at MediBuddy?

Compensation data for various roles and seniority levels is available on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, where publicly reported figures cover both MediBuddy and comparable health-tech companies. Packages commonly include a fixed component and, for senior roles, ESOPs. Research these publicly reported ranges before your HR round so you can negotiate from an informed position rather than relying solely on what the recruiter offers first.
