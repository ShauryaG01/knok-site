01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Mattermost is a US-based, fully remote company that builds an open-source messaging and collaboration platform trusted by enterprises, defence organisations, and government agencies worldwide. Because the company operates without a central office, it hires globally, which is good news for Indian professionals who have the right technical profile.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 17 open roles at Mattermost, spread across software engineering, product management, sales engineering, and customer success. The engineering team primarily works in Go and React, and the company places a strong premium on candidates who understand, or have contributed to, open-source software. If you are a backend or full-stack engineer comfortable working asynchronously across time zones, Mattermost is worth a serious look.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages described here are based on publicly available candidate reports and may vary by role or team. Treat each stage as approximate.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. You apply through the Mattermost careers page. A recruiter reviews your profile for baseline fit, focusing on relevant experience, open-source contributions (if any), and comfort with remote work. A cover letter explaining why you care about secure, open communication tools can help you stand out.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A short introductory call covers your background, the role, and compensation expectations. This is also your chance to ask about team structure and day-to-day workflows.

Stage 3: Technical screen. For engineering roles, expect either a take-home coding exercise or a live coding session. Go and React knowledge is commonly tested, but some roles assess Python, infrastructure, or security skills. The take-home format, when used, typically gives you a few days to complete a realistic task.

Stage 4: In-depth technical interviews. One or two deeper conversations with engineers on the team. These sessions often blend system design questions with discussion of your past projects. Because Mattermost is an open-source company, interviewers may ask how you approach code reviews, documentation, and community contributions.

Stage 5: Final interview or values conversation. A senior leader or hiring manager discusses your alignment with the company mission, your experience working asynchronously, and how you handle remote collaboration challenges. Questions about written communication come up frequently at this stage.

Offer. Offers typically include base salary, equity, and a remote work stipend. The full process, from first application to offer, commonly takes a few weeks, though timelines vary by role and team.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Mattermost looks for people who are genuinely comfortable working in a distributed, asynchronous environment. Here is what tends to matter most.

Technical depth. Engineering candidates need solid experience in Go (backend) or React (frontend), or both for full-stack roles. Familiarity with PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure is a plus for backend and DevOps positions. Security-focused roles value experience with compliance frameworks and self-hosted deployments.

Open-source mindset. The company's product is open-source, and so is its culture. Interviewers look for candidates who are comfortable working in public, writing clear commit messages, and giving or receiving constructive code-review feedback. Even small contributions to any open-source project can be an advantage.

Async communication skills. Because there is no shared office, almost everything happens in writing. Mattermost values candidates who can write clearly, document decisions thoroughly, and respond thoughtfully rather than reactively. A strong written application or cover letter signals this skill before the interview even starts.

Mission fit. A meaningful share of Mattermost's customers are in government, defence, and regulated industries. Candidates who understand why data sovereignty and privacy matter, and who care about building reliable software for high-stakes environments, tend to resonate well with the team.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A little focused preparation goes a long way at Mattermost.

Use the product. Download the Mattermost community server and spend time with it before your interviews. Understanding how channels, bots, and integrations work will help you answer product-sense questions and show genuine interest.

Browse the open-source repository. Even if you do not contribute a pull request, reading the codebase on GitHub gives you a feel for the team's coding standards, how they handle issues, and what problems they are actively solving. Mentioning a specific area of the code you found interesting is a strong interview signal.

Practice Go and React fundamentals. For engineering roles, review goroutines, channels, and error handling patterns in Go. For frontend roles, brush up on React hooks, state management, and performance optimisation. System design questions at Mattermost often involve designing for scale, reliability, and security, so think through how you would approach a chat platform that must work in air-gapped environments.

Prepare written communication samples. Mattermost may ask you to write a short document, a technical summary, or a response to a hypothetical scenario. Having examples of clear technical writing ready, whether a blog post, a project README, or detailed Stack Overflow answers, helps demonstrate your async communication style before you even walk into the interview.

Research the company's customers and mission. Look at Mattermost's public case studies and blog posts to understand the kinds of organisations that rely on the platform. Being able to speak to why secure, self-hosted messaging matters in 2026 shows that you understand the business context, not just the technology.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Mattermost describes itself as a remote-first, open-source-first company, and both of those qualities show up in how the team actually works day to day.

Asynchronous by default. The team spans multiple time zones, so live meetings are kept to a minimum. Most decisions happen through written channels, documented publicly inside the company's own Mattermost instance. If you thrive in environments where you can think before you respond and where written clarity is valued over quick verbal updates, you will feel at home.

Radical transparency. Many internal discussions, roadmaps, and decisions are visible to the whole company and sometimes to the public. The culture rewards people who can share work in progress, accept feedback openly, and document the 'why' behind decisions, not just the 'what.'

Mission-driven work. A meaningful share of Mattermost's customers are in government, defence, and regulated industries. The team takes seriously the idea that secure communication infrastructure is critical to organisations that cannot afford data leaks. Engineers often describe a sense of purpose that goes beyond typical enterprise software.

Ownership and autonomy. Because managers cannot walk over to your desk, Mattermost hires people who can identify what needs to be done and move forward without constant check-ins. The culture rewards self-starters who proactively communicate blockers rather than waiting to be asked.

For Indian professionals, the fully remote model is a real advantage. You can work on a global product from any city, with no pressure to relocate.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Mattermost hire engineers based in India?

Yes. Because Mattermost is fully remote, location is not a barrier for most roles. Indian engineers have been hired across engineering, customer success, and solutions engineering functions. Check each job listing carefully, as a small number of roles may specify regional restrictions for legal or compliance reasons.

What salary can I expect if I get hired from India?

Mattermost does not publish a standard India-specific pay band publicly. Based on Glassdoor and publicly reported data, remote engineering compensation at companies of similar size and stage varies widely depending on seniority and role type. Ask the recruiter directly during the first call, as Mattermost is generally transparent about compensation ranges when asked.

Do I need prior open-source contributions to apply?

No, open-source contributions are not a hard requirement, but they are a meaningful advantage. Interviewers appreciate candidates who have worked in public repositories, even on personal projects. If you have not contributed to open source before, spending some time reading the Mattermost codebase and filing a small bug report or documentation fix before your interview is a practical way to show initiative.

How long does the full hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process at Mattermost commonly spans a few weeks from first application to offer, though roles that include a take-home assignment can run longer. Timelines also vary by team and how quickly interview slots open up. If you have not heard back after an interview stage, a polite follow-up email to your recruiter is appropriate.

Is a Go background mandatory for engineering roles?

It depends on the specific role. Backend and core platform positions strongly prefer Go experience, while frontend roles focus on React and TypeScript. For infrastructure and DevOps positions, familiarity with Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud providers matters more than any single language. Read each job description carefully and highlight the skills that match what is explicitly listed.
