01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) is a global technology company known for its edge-to-cloud solutions, networking hardware, storage, compute infrastructure, and IT services. HPE has a large global workforce, with India being one of its biggest engineering and delivery hubs. Offices in Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai handle everything from software development and cloud engineering to sales and customer support.

As of July 2026, HPE has 51 open roles in India, covering a wide range of functions. Engineering and IT roles dominate, but you will also find openings in sales, finance, consulting, and people management. Most roles are posted on HPE's own careers portal and on major job boards.

For Indian professionals, HPE is attractive because it offers global exposure, competitive pay (publicly reported in industry surveys as strong for the IT sector), structured career paths, and the chance to work on enterprise technology used by large organisations worldwide.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

HPE's hiring process typically runs across several rounds, though the exact number and structure vary by role and team. Candidates who apply online or come through a referral usually hear back within a few weeks.

HR screening: A recruiter from HPE's talent acquisition team reaches out for a brief introductory conversation. This call covers your background, the role, your notice period and salary expectations, and what to expect from the process ahead.

Technical interview, round one: A panel or individual from the hiring team assesses your core technical skills. For engineering roles this commonly covers data structures, algorithms, system design, or domain-specific topics such as networking, storage, or cloud. For non-technical roles, expect a competency or case-based discussion.

Technical interview, round two: Many candidates face a second technical round that goes deeper into system design, architecture, or real-world problem-solving. Some teams combine this with a practical exercise or a take-home assignment.

Managerial or panel round: The hiring manager, and sometimes a cross-functional panel, meets you to assess cultural fit, leadership thinking, and how you approach ambiguous problems. Behavioural questions are common here.

HR and offer round: A final HR conversation covers compensation, benefits, start date, and formalities. Background verification follows before the official offer letter is issued.

The full process from application to offer commonly takes several weeks, depending on how quickly rounds are scheduled.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

HPE looks for people who combine strong technical depth with the ability to work across teams and communicate clearly with customers and colleagues.

Technical skills: For engineering roles, solid fundamentals in your domain matter most. Networking engineers should know routing, switching, and security well. Cloud and software engineers are expected to be comfortable with distributed systems, APIs, and at least one major cloud platform. Storage and infrastructure candidates need hands-on experience with enterprise environments.

Problem-solving mindset: HPE values engineers who can break down a complex problem, think through trade-offs, and arrive at a practical solution. You do not need a perfect answer instantly, but interviewers notice whether you structure your thinking clearly.

Customer focus: HPE sells to enterprise clients, so even internal engineers are expected to understand how their work affects end customers. Showing awareness of business impact alongside technical correctness is a plus.

Collaboration: HPE teams span multiple countries and time zones. The ability to work across cultures, communicate asynchronously, and contribute to shared goals is valued at every level.

Ownership and initiative: Managers look for candidates who take responsibility for outcomes, proactively identify issues, and do not wait to be told what to do next.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with the job description: HPE's job descriptions are detailed. Map your experience to each listed requirement and prepare specific examples using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

Brush up on fundamentals: Depending on your role, revisit core concepts. Software engineers should practise data structures, algorithms, and system design. Networking candidates should review OSI layers, protocols, and common enterprise topologies. Cloud roles require familiarity with at least one cloud provider's core services.

Research HPE's products and strategy: HPE's main product lines include HPE GreenLake (cloud services), Aruba Networks (networking), and HPE Alletra (storage). Understanding where the company is investing helps you ask smart questions and frame your experience in relevant terms.

Prepare behavioural stories: HPE interviewers ask about how you handled conflict, managed a difficult project, or led without authority. Have several strong stories ready that you can adapt to different questions.

Practise out loud: Technical and behavioural answers that sound clear in your head can feel jumbled when spoken. Practise with a friend or record yourself to catch filler words and unclear explanations.

Prepare questions for the interviewer: Asking about the team's current challenges, the tech stack, or how success is measured in the first year shows genuine interest and helps you decide whether the role is right for you.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

HPE's stated purpose is to advance the way people live and work. Internally, this translates into a culture that emphasises inclusion, continuous learning, and what HPE calls 'Living Progress', a commitment to making business decisions that are good for people and the planet alongside being commercially sound.

Inclusion and diversity: HPE has made public commitments to gender diversity and inclusion at leadership levels. Employee resource groups (ERGs) are active across major India offices, covering women in technology, LGBTQ+ inclusion, and early-career professionals.

Flexible work: Many HPE India roles offer hybrid working arrangements. The balance between remote and in-office days varies by team and role, so it is worth asking specifically during your interview.

Learning and development: HPE has an internal learning platform and encourages employees to pursue certifications relevant to their roles. New joiners typically go through structured onboarding programmes.

Scale and stability: HPE has been operating for decades and serves large enterprise customers globally. This brings stability and the chance to work on complex, high-stakes technology, though the pace of change can feel slower than at a startup.

Team culture in India: Glassdoor reviewers commonly cite a collaborative and professional work environment at HPE India offices, with mentorship available for those who seek it out.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does HPE have right now?

As of July 2026, HPE has 51 open roles listed. This number changes regularly as roles are filled and new ones open. Check HPE's official careers portal for the latest listings, and filter by location and function to find what fits your background.

Does HPE hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

HPE hires both. For freshers, HPE runs campus recruitment drives at engineering colleges and offers graduate trainee or associate engineer programmes. Experienced professionals can apply directly through the careers portal or through referrals from current HPE employees. Competition for fresher roles is high, so a strong academic record and relevant internship experience help.

How long does the HPE interview process take from application to offer?

The process commonly takes several weeks from application to offer, though it can be shorter for urgent roles or longer if scheduling is difficult. After your final round, the offer and background verification stage can add a week or more. Stay in touch with your recruiter for updates if you have not heard back within a reasonable time.

What salary can I expect at HPE India?

Compensation at HPE India varies significantly by role, level, and location. Industry surveys and Glassdoor reviews commonly cite HPE's pay as competitive within the IT sector. For the most accurate picture, check recent reviews on Glassdoor or levels.fyi filtered by your specific role and level before your offer discussion.

Is there a referral programme at HPE?

Yes, HPE has an internal employee referral programme. Getting referred by a current HPE employee can move your application to the top of the screening queue and sometimes shortens the overall process. If you know someone at HPE, reaching out before applying is a practical step, and LinkedIn is a good place to find mutual connections who work there.
