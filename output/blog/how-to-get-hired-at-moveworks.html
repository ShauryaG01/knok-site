03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Moveworks' process typically runs across several stages, though the exact structure varies by role and team. The following is what candidates commonly report. Moveworks does not publish a fixed process publicly, so treat this as a general guide.

Recruiter screen
A brief introductory call to check your background, interest in Moveworks, notice period, and salary expectations. The recruiter will also give you an overview of the role. Prepare a concise summary of your experience and a clear answer to 'why Moveworks.'

Hiring manager or domain screen
A focused conversation with the hiring manager or a senior team member. For technical roles this may include a walkthrough of your past projects or a short live problem. For go-to-market roles, expect scenario questions about enterprise sales or customer success. Come with specific examples from your past work.

Technical or domain rounds
For engineering roles, expect coding problems and system design. For ML or AI roles, expect questions on NLP, retrieval systems, or LLM application design. For business roles, expect case-style analysis. There are typically a few rounds at this stage, each focused on a different area.

Cross-functional or values round
A conversation with someone outside your immediate team. Behavioural questions using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) are standard. The focus is on how you work with others, handle ambiguity, and communicate across functions.

Final discussion or panel
Some candidates report a final conversation with a senior leader or small panel before an offer is made. This is usually high-level, focused on your long-term goals and strategic thinking.

The full process commonly takes a few weeks from first screen to offer. Keep in touch with your recruiter between stages so you stay visible in the pipeline.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Moveworks hires people who can operate at the intersection of enterprise software and modern AI. A few qualities that appear consistently across candidate reports:

Strong fundamentals. For engineering and AI roles, Moveworks values deep understanding over breadth of frameworks. Clean reasoning about systems, data, and code matters more than knowing every tool.

Genuine interest in enterprise AI use cases. The product solves real operational problems, so candidates who understand why enterprises adopt AI and what makes a deployment successful stand out from those who only know the theory.

Clear communication. Moveworks sells to large companies and works across complex organisations. Whether you are in engineering, sales, or customer success, the ability to explain hard ideas simply is valued.

Ownership and initiative. The company is growing and teams move fast. Candidates who take charge of problems without waiting for permission tend to do well in interviews and on the job.

Cross-functional collaboration. Most projects at Moveworks involve multiple teams. Interviewers look for evidence that you work well across boundaries, listen to others, and share credit.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product before your first call. Spend time on the Moveworks website and read their published case studies. Know what the product does, who uses it, and what problems it solves. Recruiters notice immediately when a candidate has not done this step.

For engineering roles, practise problem-solving and system design. Coding problems at a medium-to-hard level are commonly cited by candidates. For system design, focus on distributed systems, API design, and scalability. Be ready to talk through your reasoning out loud.

For AI and ML roles, be ready to discuss applied NLP and LLMs. Moveworks is deeply invested in language models. Review concepts like retrieval-augmented generation, intent classification, and embedding-based search. Be ready to discuss tradeoffs, not just definitions.

For go-to-market roles, prepare enterprise sales or CS scenarios. Think through examples of how you have sold to or supported large organisations. Be ready to discuss deal cycles, stakeholder management, and how you measure success.

Prepare your STAR stories. Pick several situations from your career that show ownership, cross-functional collaboration, handling failure, and impact. These will serve you in the behavioural rounds regardless of the role.

Have clear answers for the basics. 'Why Moveworks specifically,' 'what you are looking for in your next role,' and 'what you have accomplished in your current or last role.' Keep these concise and honest.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Moveworks operates with a sense of urgency common to companies that are still scaling. The culture is collaborative but moves fast, and people are expected to be self-directed. A few things that define how the company works:

Mission-driven work. The team believes AI can genuinely improve how people work inside large organisations. That belief shows up in how people talk about the product. Candidates who share that conviction fit in more naturally than those who treat it as just another tech job.

High ownership, low hand-holding. Moveworks expects people to identify problems, propose solutions, and follow through without needing step-by-step guidance. This is true from individual contributors to managers.

Respect for depth. The team includes people with strong academic and research backgrounds alongside practitioners. Deep expertise is respected, and people are expected to keep learning as the AI space moves quickly.

Transparency. Internally, the company tries to share context broadly so people can make good decisions. Candidates who ask good questions in interviews, and who are honest about what they know and do not know, tend to leave a positive impression.

Diversity and inclusion. Moveworks has publicly stated commitments to building diverse teams. Indian candidates applying from India or from the diaspora are part of a talent pool the company actively recruits from, particularly for engineering and AI roles.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Moveworks have right now?

As of mid-2026, Moveworks has 6 open roles listed on knok's job radar. The number changes as roles open and close, so check directly on the Moveworks careers page or a job aggregator for the latest listings.

Does Moveworks hire from India or for remote roles?

Moveworks has a presence in India and recruits Indian talent both for roles based in India and for positions abroad. Whether a specific role is remote, hybrid, or in-office depends on the job listing. Check each posting carefully and ask the recruiter to confirm the work arrangement early in the process.

What is the typical interview timeline at Moveworks?

Candidates commonly report the full process taking a few weeks from the first recruiter screen to an offer. This can extend if there are multiple candidates being evaluated in parallel. Staying in touch with your recruiter between stages is a good way to keep things moving.

How hard are the technical interviews?

For software engineering roles, the technical rounds are commonly described as medium-to-hard in difficulty, similar to what you would see at other well-funded AI companies. For ML and AI roles, expect questions on applied NLP, LLMs, and system design rather than purely algorithmic puzzles. Giving yourself several weeks for dedicated preparation is realistic.

Is prior experience with AI or LLMs required?

For engineering and AI roles, familiarity with language models and their applications is a significant advantage and increasingly expected. For go-to-market, operations, or support roles, a strong interest in AI and the ability to explain it to customers matters more than hands-on technical depth. Read the job description closely to judge what applies to your target role.

What should I emphasise in my application and resume?

Highlight projects or roles where you built or worked with AI systems, solved problems at scale, or worked with enterprise customers. Quantify your impact where you can, using figures you can back up from your own work history. Tailor your resume to the specific role by mirroring the language in the job description, since many companies use automated screening before a human reads your application.
