01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Adani Group is one of India's largest and fastest-growing conglomerates, with active businesses in ports, airports, power generation, green energy, data centres, roads, real estate, and FMCG. Hiring happens across a wide spread of subsidiaries: Adani Ports, Adani Green Energy, Adani Electricity, Adani Enterprises, and more. Each subsidiary runs its own HR function, so the recruitment experience can vary depending on which vertical you apply to.

As of mid-2026, the group had 264 open roles listed across its businesses, spanning engineering, operations, finance, IT, and corporate functions. This is a strong signal that the group is actively building headcount rather than running a token hiring page.

Roles at Adani tend to reward people who want to work at scale. If you are drawn to large infrastructure projects, high-growth sectors like renewables, or the chance to be part of a business expanding into new verticals every year, Adani is worth targeting seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at Adani Group varies by role and subsidiary, but most candidates report a pattern that looks roughly like this:

Application and resume screening. You apply through the Adani careers portal or via a job board. An internal recruiter or HR business partner reviews your resume. Response times vary, and for high-volume roles the screening can take a few weeks.

Initial HR call. A short call to confirm your basic fit: current role, notice period, location flexibility, and salary expectation. This is also where they check if you match the seniority and domain they are hiring for.

Technical or domain round. For engineering and operations roles, expect a technical interview covering your area of expertise. For corporate roles (finance, HR, strategy), this round is more of a competency discussion. Some roles include a written assignment or case study at this stage.

Managerial or panel round. A discussion with the hiring manager, sometimes joined by a senior leader from the business unit. Questions here tend to be a mix of domain-specific scenarios and behavioural questions about how you handle large projects, ambiguity, or cross-functional teams.

HR discussion and offer. If the panel round goes well, HR connects to discuss the offer. Background verification is standard across the group. Have your documents ready (identity proof, past offer letters, qualification certificates) so the process moves faster.

Total duration from application to offer can range from a few weeks to a couple of months depending on the seniority of the role and how quickly the business unit moves.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Adani Group hires across a very wide range of functions, but a few themes come up consistently:

Comfort with scale and complexity. Adani builds and operates assets at a size that most Indian companies do not touch. Whether it is a port handling large cargo volumes or a solar plant covering thousands of acres, the projects are substantial. Candidates who have managed or contributed to large-scale operations tend to stand out.

Domain depth over generalist breadth. In technical roles (civil, electrical, mechanical, project management), the group expects strong domain knowledge. They are less interested in someone who knows a little about everything and more in someone who has gone deep in their area.

Execution mindset. Adani is known for aggressive timelines. They value people who can get things done, manage contractors, navigate approvals, and keep projects moving. If you have handled deadline pressure in past roles, make sure that comes through clearly.

Adaptability across verticals. Because the group is expanding into new businesses frequently, candidates who can pick up new domain knowledge quickly (while bringing their core expertise) are valued.

Commercial and regulatory awareness. Many roles touch on government relations, regulatory filings, or project finance. Awareness of how large infrastructure projects are structured and cleared in India is a genuine advantage.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the specific subsidiary. Adani Group is not one company, it is a collection of large businesses. Before your interview, read up on the specific entity you are applying to: its recent projects, expansion plans, and the sector it operates in. Interviewers notice when a candidate has done this work.

Prepare your 'scale' stories. Think through your past work and identify examples where you handled large teams, big budgets, or complex multi-stakeholder projects. Quantify the impact wherever you can (project value, team size, timelines met). These stories land well in Adani interviews.

Know your domain cold. For technical roles, expect to go deep. Refresh your knowledge of the core concepts in your discipline, and be ready to discuss real problems you have solved, not just theory.

Practise behavioural questions. Common themes in Adani interviews include: how you handle pressure, how you coordinate across large teams, how you manage vendors or contractors, and how you have dealt with ambiguity on a large project. The STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) works well here.

Salary negotiation. Adani's compensation is commonly cited as competitive for core engineering and operations roles, with corporate functions benchmarked to large Indian conglomerates. Check Glassdoor and AmbitionBox for current salary ranges shared by employees before your HR discussion.

Keep documents ready. Background checks are standard. Have your identity documents, educational certificates, and past employment records organised in advance so onboarding does not slow down after an offer.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Adani Group has a reputation as a high-intensity, execution-first workplace. The culture is shaped by the founding ethos of moving fast, thinking big, and building things that last. Here is what employees commonly describe:

Fast pace, high expectations. Deadlines are taken seriously. The group has a track record of completing large infrastructure projects in compressed timelines, and that urgency filters into day-to-day work. If you prefer a more process-driven, slower-paced environment, this may not be the right fit.

Hierarchical structure. Decision-making tends to flow from the top, and the hierarchy is visible. Senior leaders carry significant authority. If you are comfortable operating within a clear chain of command and can navigate upward effectively, this works in your favour.

Growth opportunities within the group. Because the group is expanding into new businesses regularly (green hydrogen, airports, data centres), there are more opportunities than at a stable mature company to move into new verticals or take on larger responsibilities. Employees who perform well are often tapped for newer projects.

Values in practice. The group formally espouses values around nation-building, sustainability, and long-term thinking. In practice, these show up as a strong emphasis on infrastructure development and energy transition projects, particularly in renewables.

If you are joining from a startup or a Western multinational, expect an adjustment period. The culture is distinctly Indian corporate at scale, which has its own rhythms and norms.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs is Adani Group currently hiring for?

As of mid-2026, the group had 264 open roles listed across its subsidiaries. These span engineering, operations, IT, finance, and corporate functions. The number changes as roles are filled and new ones open, so check the Adani careers portal and major job boards regularly.

Does Adani Group hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

The group does hire freshers, primarily through campus placements at engineering colleges and IIMs. For off-campus applicants without experience, openings are less common but do appear in IT and entry-level operations roles. Most roles listed on job boards require at least some relevant experience, so freshers should focus on the campus route or internship pathways.

Which subsidiaries hire the most?

Adani Ports and SEZ, Adani Green Energy, Adani Electricity, and Adani Enterprises tend to post the highest volume of roles. The newer businesses (data centres, airports, defence) are growing their teams actively as of 2026. Each subsidiary runs its own HR process, so experiences can differ even within the broader group.

What is the salary range at Adani Group?

Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and AmbitionBox suggest compensation at Adani is competitive with other large Indian conglomerates for core engineering and operations roles. Corporate and finance roles are benchmarked similarly. Salary varies significantly by level, subsidiary, and location, so check those platforms for role-specific data shared by current and former employees before your HR discussion.

How long does the Adani hiring process take?

Most candidates report the process takes a few weeks to a couple of months from application to offer, depending on the role's seniority and which subsidiary is hiring. Senior or specialised roles can take longer as they involve multiple panel members. Following up politely with the recruiter after each round is a good practice to keep your application moving.

Is it worth applying through a referral?

Referrals help at Adani as they do at most large Indian corporates. A referral from a current employee can get your resume in front of the right hiring manager faster than an online application. If you have a contact within the group, reach out and ask if they can refer you internally before you apply through the portal. This is especially useful for roles in newer or more specialised business units where hiring is selective.
