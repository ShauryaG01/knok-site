01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Haptik is one of India's largest conversational AI platforms, part of the Reliance Jio ecosystem. The company builds enterprise chatbots, voice assistants, and AI-powered customer support tools used by major brands across India and internationally. As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 16 open roles at Haptik, spanning engineering, product, data science, and customer success teams.

Haptik hires for roles out of its Mumbai headquarters and supports remote or hybrid arrangements for many positions. If you are keen on working at the intersection of AI, product, and enterprise business, Haptik is a serious option worth pursuing.

Demand for conversational AI has grown sharply across BFSI, e-commerce, and telecom sectors in India, and Haptik sits at the center of that shift. Getting in requires more than technical skill. Interviewers want to see that you genuinely understand how AI is changing customer experience at scale.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Most candidates for technical roles at Haptik go through 4-5 rounds, though the exact number can vary by team and seniority. Here is what the typical journey looks like, based on publicly reported candidate experiences.

Round 1: Resume and profile screening
The recruiter reviews your application and, if shortlisted, reaches out for a brief introductory call to check your background, current role, and interest in Haptik. This is also where they walk you through the role in more detail.

Round 2: Online assessment or take-home task
For engineering roles, expect a coding test covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes SQL. For product and AI/ML roles, this may be a short case study or an assignment you complete and submit within a day or two.

Round 3: Technical interview, first panel
A structured conversation with a senior engineer or domain expert. Expect questions on your core skills, system design basics for backend roles, and scenario-based questions for product or customer success profiles.

Round 4: Technical or domain deep-dive
Some roles include a second technical round that goes deeper into architecture, NLP concepts, or past project walkthroughs. For non-engineering roles, this may be a case presentation or a demo of your thinking process.

Round 5: Hiring manager or leadership round
Typically a culture and fit conversation. The interviewer wants to understand your motivations, how you handle ambiguity, and whether you align with the team's working style.

Offer calls usually follow within a week of the final round, though timelines can stretch during busy hiring periods. Keep follow-up emails polite and spaced apart.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Haptik is building products in a fast-moving space, so they tend to look for people who learn quickly and work without a lot of hand-holding. Here are the traits that show up repeatedly in hiring feedback.

For engineering roles: Strong fundamentals in Python or JavaScript, comfort with REST APIs and cloud platforms like AWS or GCP, and ideally some exposure to NLP libraries or LLM APIs. System design skills matter more as you move into senior positions.

For product roles: Sharp product thinking, the ability to write clear specs, and experience using data to make decisions. Familiarity with conversational UI or chatbot flows is a big plus but is not always required.

For data science and AI/ML: Solid grounding in machine learning concepts, hands-on experience with model training and evaluation, and ideally projects that show you have deployed something in a real environment.

For customer success and sales: Strong communication, the ability to manage enterprise client relationships, and enough technical comfort to have meaningful conversations with clients about how the product works.

Across all roles, interviewers look for genuine curiosity about AI and its real-world applications. Candidates who have used or studied Haptik's products, or who can speak to conversational AI trends, consistently stand out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research Haptik's products before any interview. Explore the Haptik website, try any publicly accessible demos, and read about the enterprise use cases they highlight. Being able to reference specific products or industries they serve signals genuine interest, not just job-hunting.

Brush up on conversational AI basics. Even if you are not applying for an AI/ML role, understanding concepts like intent detection, entity extraction, dialogue management, and large language models will help you speak the company's language with confidence.

Prepare 2-3 strong project stories. Use a simple structure: what the problem was, what you did, and what resulted. Quantify outcomes where you can, but be honest if the project was early-stage or the numbers are hard to pin down.

Practice system design out loud. For engineering and senior product roles, system design is almost always covered. Practice explaining your thinking step by step, as interviewers want to follow your reasoning, not just your conclusion.

Tailor your resume to the job description. Mirror key terms from the posting, highlight relevant tools, and make sure your most impactful work is visible in the first half of the page.

Reach out to the hiring team on LinkedIn. A short, thoughtful note referencing something specific about Haptik's work can get you noticed before you even apply formally.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Haptik operates with the energy of a product startup backed by the resources of a large group. The culture is described by employees as fast-paced and outcome-focused, with a strong emphasis on individual ownership. Teams are generally lean, which means people take on broad responsibilities and are expected to move quickly.

The work is deeply cross-functional. Engineers collaborate closely with product managers and customer success teams, especially when building or iterating on client-facing bots. If you prefer clearly siloed work, this may feel uncomfortable at first and is worth discussing during the interview process.

Haptik's position within the Reliance Jio ecosystem brings enterprise scale and client exposure that most pure startups cannot offer. Employees work on products that reach a very large number of users, which can be motivating if impact at scale matters to you.

Learning and growth are valued, and the company has publicly invested in upskilling employees on new AI capabilities as the technology evolves. That said, if you need a structured mentorship programme or very defined career ladders, it is worth asking specific questions during hiring to set the right expectations.

Overall, Haptik tends to attract people who are excited about AI's commercial applications and who want to work on problems with real enterprise stakes.

Is Haptik a good company for freshers?

Haptik hires primarily for mid-level and senior roles, but entry-level opportunities do appear, especially in engineering and customer success. Freshers with strong fundamentals, a solid portfolio project in AI or ML, and genuine product knowledge will have a better chance than those applying without any preparation. What salary can I expect at Haptik?

Specific compensation figures vary by role, experience, and negotiation. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list salary ranges shared by Haptik employees, and those are worth checking for a realistic benchmark. Come prepared to negotiate, and know the market rate for your skill set and years of experience before the offer call so you can have an informed conversation.

Does Haptik allow remote work?

Haptik's primary office is in Mumbai, but many roles have been listed as remote or hybrid, particularly for engineering and product profiles. The exact arrangement depends on the team and the specific role. Confirm the work mode during your first recruiter call so there are no surprises later in the process.

How long does Haptik's hiring process take?

Most candidates complete the full process in a few weeks from first contact to offer, though it can move faster or slower depending on the team's urgency and how many rounds are involved. Following up politely after each round is acceptable and shows continued interest without coming across as pushy. A short email a week after your last round is a reasonable nudge.

What is the best way to get noticed by Haptik recruiters?
