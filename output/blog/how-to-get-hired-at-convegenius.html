01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

ConveGenius builds AI-powered educational products that help students learn through voice and text conversations in regional Indian languages. The company works closely with state governments, NGOs, and schools to bring quality learning to students who would otherwise not have access to it. That mission shapes not just what they build, but how they hire.

As of 2026, ConveGenius has 30 open roles. Openings span software engineering (with a strong AI and ML flavour), product management, content design, data analytics, and partnerships. The company has grown steadily through government contracts and impact-focused investors, which means the hiring bar is serious but the environment is less cutthroat than a pure-play startup competing only on revenue.

If you want to work at a company where the product reaches real classrooms across India, and you have skills in AI, edtech product thinking, or regional language content, ConveGenius is worth a close look.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at ConveGenius typically runs through four to five stages, though the exact sequence can vary by role and team. The descriptions below are based on commonly reported candidate experiences at similar AI-EdTech companies in India.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Shortlisting
The team reviews resumes for relevant skills and domain alignment. For tech roles, experience with NLP, conversational AI, or mobile-first products tends to stand out. For non-tech roles, prior exposure to education, government projects, or vernacular content gets noticed.

Stage 2: HR Screening Call
A recruiter typically reaches out for a brief call to check the basics: your background, notice period, location preference, and interest in the mission. This is also your chance to ask about team size and role clarity.

Stage 3: Technical or Domain Round
For engineering roles, candidates often face a coding assignment or a live problem-solving session covering data structures, algorithms, or system design. AI and ML roles may include a case study or a take-home task involving model evaluation or prompt design. For product and content roles, expect a short assignment such as a product critique or a content sample in a regional language.

Stage 4: Panel or Senior Interview
A deeper conversation with a senior engineer, product lead, or hiring manager. This round typically covers your past projects, your problem-solving approach, and how you think about building for users with limited digital literacy. Behavioural questions are common here.

Stage 5: Culture and Leadership Fit
Many candidates report a final round with a co-founder or department head. The focus shifts to values alignment: why EdTech, why ConveGenius, and how you handle ambiguity in a fast-moving team. Offers typically follow within a week of this round.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

ConveGenius looks for people who can build practical things fast, care about real users, and do not need a polished corporate environment to do their best work. A few qualities come up repeatedly in what hiring managers at similar impact-focused startups value.

Genuine interest in the mission. This is not just a talking point. The work involves rural schools, government partners, and students learning in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, and other regional languages. Candidates who engage seriously with those users during interviews tend to move forward faster.

Technical depth in applied AI. For engineering roles, the company builds conversational systems and voice interfaces. Familiarity with large language models, ASR (automatic speech recognition), or vernacular NLP is a real advantage, not just a nice-to-have.

Product thinking with constraints. Many users access ConveGenius products on low-end Android phones with patchy connectivity. Engineers and PMs who have thought through low-bandwidth design, offline-first features, or progressive loading show immediate relevance.

Communication across functions. The team is lean, which means engineers talk to content creators and PMs talk directly to school coordinators. Clear written and verbal communication in cross-functional settings matters more here than at larger companies.

Comfort with ambiguity. Roles and roadmaps shift as government contracts evolve and new partnerships form. Candidates who can show examples of navigating unclear requirements or pivoting without a detailed playbook tend to impress.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before the first call. Download or explore any ConveGenius product you can access. Understand what subjects they cover, which languages they support, and how the conversation flow works. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually used the product versus just read the website.

Brush up on AI and NLP fundamentals. Even for non-engineering roles, a basic understanding of how large language models work, what prompt engineering involves, and how voice interfaces handle Indian accents will help you ask sharper questions and show genuine curiosity.

Prepare a mission-aligned story. Think about a moment in your own life or career where you helped someone learn something, or where you worked on a product that reached users at scale in India. ConveGenius interviewers frequently ask why you want to work in education specifically.

Practice system design with real-world constraints. If you are going for an engineering role, prepare at least one system design answer that addresses low-bandwidth or offline-capable architecture. Think concretely about what 'mobile-first for Tier-3 India' means in technical terms.

Know your notice period and be honest about it. The team moves quickly on offers. Having clarity on your availability avoids delays and signals professionalism.

Prepare questions that show you have done your homework. Ask about specific government partnerships, how they measure learning outcomes, or what the biggest technical challenge on the roadmap is right now. Shallow questions without context tend to fall flat at mission-driven companies.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

ConveGenius operates with a strong sense of purpose that filters into day-to-day work. The team is building for users who are often first-generation smartphone users, children in under-resourced schools, and teachers with limited tech training. That context shapes how the company thinks about product quality, timelines, and tradeoffs.

The culture tends to be collaborative and flat, meaning individual contributors often interact directly with leadership. Decision-making can move quickly when a government project demands it, which some people find energising and others find stressful. If you prefer clearly defined processes and stable roadmaps, this environment may feel unsettled. If you enjoy moving fast on problems that genuinely matter, it will feel natural.

Regional language fluency is valued, both for content roles and for understanding the user base. Being comfortable with Hindi or a South Indian language is a genuine advantage in many teams.

Work is meaningful in a concrete sense: products are actively used in classrooms, and the feedback loop from schools to product teams is shorter than at most companies. That visibility motivates people who care about impact over prestige.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does ConveGenius currently have?

As of mid-2026, ConveGenius has 30 open roles listed across functions. The mix includes engineering, product, content, data, and business development positions. Openings can change quickly as new government contracts or funding rounds are announced, so it is worth checking their careers page regularly or setting up job alerts.

Do I need a background in education to get hired at ConveGenius?

Not necessarily. Many engineering and product roles do not require a formal education background. What helps is genuine curiosity about how people learn and some familiarity with the challenges of reaching users in Tier-2 and Tier-3 India. For content and curriculum roles, prior experience in edtech or teaching is a stronger advantage and is often listed as a direct requirement.

What skills matter most for tech roles at ConveGenius?

Conversational AI, NLP, and voice interface development are directly relevant given the company's product focus. Mobile-first Android development and experience with low-bandwidth or offline-capable systems also stand out. Strong fundamentals in data structures and system design are expected, and candidates who can connect their technical work to real user impact tend to perform well across all interview rounds.

Is knowledge of a regional Indian language required?

It is not a hard requirement for most engineering or product roles, but it is a real advantage. The products are built for learners in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and other regional languages. For content design and curriculum roles, fluency in one or more of these languages is often listed as a requirement. Even a working knowledge of Hindi is useful for understanding user research and field reports from partner schools.

How long does the ConveGenius interview process typically take?

Based on commonly reported experiences at similar AI-EdTech companies in India, the full process from application to offer can run two to four weeks. Government-linked roles may take longer if internal approvals are involved. Reaching out to the recruiter after each stage to confirm timelines is a practical way to stay informed and signal genuine interest.

What salary can I expect at ConveGenius?

ConveGenius does not publicly list salary bands. Based on Glassdoor data and industry surveys for similar-stage EdTech startups in India, compensation tends to be competitive within the startup ecosystem but may trail large product companies. Ask about ESOPs as part of your total package discussion, since equity can be meaningful at a company with government-scale contracts and a growing user base.
