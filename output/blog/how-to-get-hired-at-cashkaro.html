01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

CashKaro is India's largest cashback and coupon platform, co-founded by Swati Bhargava and Rohan Bhargava and headquartered in Gurugram. The company partners with hundreds of top e-commerce and lifestyle brands, helping shoppers earn real cashback on every purchase. Its affiliate revenue model places roles here at the crossroads of technology, performance marketing, and consumer growth.

As of this writing, CashKaro has 46 open roles spanning engineering, product, marketing, business development, and customer experience. That is a healthy pipeline, and the company tends to move with urgency when it finds the right candidate.

If you are looking to join a well-established consumer internet company that still operates with startup speed, CashKaro is a strong option to pursue seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at CashKaro typically runs through four to five stages. The exact flow varies by role, but here is what candidates commonly report:

Resume screening. The recruiter team reviews your application for relevant experience. A resume that closely mirrors the job description, with clear impact statements rather than just a list of responsibilities, moves through faster.

Recruiter call. A short call with HR to discuss your background, notice period, and salary expectations. Expect questions on why you want to join CashKaro and what you are looking for in your next role.

Technical or skills round. For engineering roles, this is typically a coding test or a live problem-solving session. For product and marketing roles, expect a case study or take-home assignment focused on growth, user behaviour, or data analysis.

Panel or functional interview. One or two rounds with the hiring manager and potential colleagues. These go deeper into your past work, domain knowledge, and how you handle ambiguous problems. Behavioural questions appear alongside role-specific ones.

Leadership or culture round. Senior roles often include a final conversation with a co-founder or senior leader. This is less about technical depth and more about values, ownership mindset, and long-term fit.

Offer. Compensation discussions typically happen with HR after the final round clears.

The full cycle from first call to offer commonly takes two to four weeks. Roles with urgent headcount sometimes move faster.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

CashKaro runs on performance data, so comfort with numbers is a baseline expectation across almost every function, not just analytics or engineering.

For tech roles, strong fundamentals in your chosen stack matter more than knowing every framework. Experience building for high-traffic consumer products is a genuine plus. The ability to write clean, scalable code and explain your design choices clearly sets candidates apart.

For product and growth roles, the ability to frame problems clearly, design structured experiments, and collaborate across engineering and marketing teams is what stands out. Interviewers respond well to candidates who can point to concrete results from past work, rather than describing activities in vague terms.

For marketing and BD roles, understanding performance channels, affiliate marketing mechanics, and India's e-commerce ecosystem gives you a real edge. Prior experience with affiliate networks or brand partnership deals is a genuine differentiator.

Across all functions, CashKaro consistently values self-driven candidates who take ownership without waiting for direction and can get things done in a lean team where dedicated support is not always available.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your interview. Sign up for CashKaro if you have not already and make a few purchases through the platform. Understanding the cashback flow, the partner brand experience, and where friction exists will help you speak concretely in interviews rather than generically.

Understand the business model. CashKaro earns affiliate commissions from partner brands and shares a portion as cashback with users. Know what drives revenue for the company: GMV, active user count, partner retention, and repeat purchase behaviour. Showing you understand this signals serious interest.

Prepare impact-first answers for every past role. For each position, be ready to say what you did, how you measured success, and what changed because of your work. Vague answers like 'I managed campaigns' or 'I built features' land weakly here.

Practice case studies if applying for product or marketing. Think through how you would grow the user base, improve cashback redemption, or deepen a key partner integration. Structured thinking (problem, hypothesis, test, measure) is what impresses interviewers.

Research compensation before the recruiter call. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported salary ranges for similar roles at comparable consumer-internet startups. Going in with a clear range shows confidence and avoids awkward back-and-forth.

Prepare sharp questions. Ask about product roadmap priorities, team structure, or what success looks like in the first few months. Questions that reflect genuine research leave a strong impression.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

CashKaro has the energy of a startup that has found product-market fit but still moves with urgency. The co-founders remain closely involved in product and strategic decisions, which keeps the culture accessible and relatively flat compared to large corporations.

Ownership is a lived expectation here, not a wall poster. People are expected to take problems end-to-end rather than hand them off at the boundary of their job description. If you prefer a highly structured environment with clearly defined handoffs, the adjustment may take time.

The pace is fast and priorities can shift. Teams are expected to adapt as business needs change, especially around peak sale events. Candidates who thrive here enjoy variety and the sense of actively building something, rather than maintaining a stable system.

Cross-functional collaboration is central. Engineering, product, and growth teams overlap heavily in an affiliate business, so being easy to work with across functions matters as much as individual skill.

On Glassdoor, commonly cited positives include learning opportunities, direct exposure to business problems at a relatively junior level, and co-founder accessibility. Commonly cited areas for improvement include workload intensity during major sale seasons and internal processes that are still catching up to the company's scale.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does CashKaro currently have?

As of the latest check, CashKaro has 46 open roles across engineering, product, marketing, business development, and operations. This is an active hiring phase for the company. Roles open and close quickly at this scale, so applying early in the cycle improves your chances.

How long does the CashKaro interview process take from application to offer?

The process commonly runs two to four weeks from the initial recruiter call to an offer. Engineering roles with urgent headcount can sometimes move faster. If you have not heard back within a week of any round, a polite follow-up email to your recruiter is completely appropriate.

Does CashKaro hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

CashKaro hires at multiple levels, including freshers and junior candidates for certain engineering, marketing, and operations roles. For product management and senior technical roles, prior work experience is typically expected. Check each job description carefully for the stated experience level before applying.

What should I expect in the technical round for an engineering role?

Candidates commonly report a coding test on an online platform followed by one or two live problem-solving sessions. Questions tend to cover data structures, algorithms, and system design, depending on the seniority of the role. Expect to explain your reasoning out loud, not just arrive at an answer.

How should I handle salary negotiation with CashKaro?

Research publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for similar roles at consumer-internet startups before the recruiter call. Come prepared with a clear range rather than waiting to be quoted a number first. Negotiation is normal and expected, especially if you have a competing offer in hand.

Can I apply to multiple roles at CashKaro at the same time?

Yes, applying to more than one role is generally acceptable if your skills genuinely fit each position. Be transparent with the recruiter about the other roles you are being considered for within the company. Applying to roles that are a clear mismatch with your background can create the impression of an unfocused application.
