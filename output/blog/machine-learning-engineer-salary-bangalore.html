01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Machine Learning Engineer job listings in Bangalore do not show a salary. Across Indian tech hiring, leaving compensation out of the job description is standard practice. Of the 165 ML Engineer openings tracked by knok jobradar in Bangalore as of July 2026, salary disclosure was the exception, not the rule.

This puts the burden on you to arrive at every recruiter conversation with your own benchmark. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry enough publicly reported data to build a usable picture. Compensation for ML Engineers in Bangalore varies widely by company type (product company vs. IT services firm), specialisation (NLP, computer vision, MLOps), and the scale of systems you own. This guide pulls together what is commonly cited across those variables so you can anchor your expectations before the first call.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because most listings in the dataset did not disclose pay, the salary ranges below come from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and industry surveys rather than from job postings directly. Use them as market anchors for your experience band.

Fresher to early career (0-2 years): Glassdoor commonly reports 8-15 LPA for entry-level ML Engineers in Bangalore. Product companies and well-funded startups tend to offer toward the higher end; IT services firms typically land toward the lower end.

Mid-level (3-5 years): Industry surveys commonly cite 18-35 LPA for ML Engineers who have shipped production models and own a pipeline end to end. Roles requiring LLM fine-tuning, MLOps, or real-time inference experience frequently attract offers toward the upper part of this band.

Senior (6-9 years): Levels.fyi commonly reports senior ML Engineer base salaries of 35-70 LPA at top product companies in Bangalore, with the range driven by scope, team size owned, and depth of specialisation.

Staff or Principal (10+ years): At this level, publicly reported compensation packages frequently include ESOPs worth multiples of base salary. Levels.fyi commonly shows base pay of 70 LPA and above for staff-level ML roles at Tier-1 product firms in Bangalore.

Listings won't tell you what a role pays, but knok will tell you which ones you're competitive for.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Know your number before the call. Recruiters will often ask for your 'expected CTC' in the first conversation. Have a specific range ready, grounded in Glassdoor and levels.fyi data for your experience band, not anchored to what you currently earn.

Separate fixed from variable and ESOPs. A high fixed offer beats a lower fixed salary bundled with variable pay that depends on metrics outside your control. Get the fixed component confirmed in writing and understand vesting timelines for any stock before you sign.

Use competing offers. If you have an offer from another company, it is your strongest negotiation tool. You do not need to share the exact number, but mentioning that you are in advanced stages elsewhere moves conversations faster and gives HR a clear reason to go back to the hiring manager.

Ask for a joining bonus to cover notice period loss. If you are forfeiting unvested ESOPs or a bonus by leaving your current employer early, ask the new company for a one-time joining bonus to compensate. Most companies have a process for this and it does not affect your CTC structure.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

ML Engineers in Bangalore are typically paid through a mix of fixed salary, variable pay, ESOPs or RSUs, and annual bonuses. Understanding each component lets you compare offers accurately instead of comparing headline CTC numbers alone.

Fixed CTC: The guaranteed portion and the most important number to compare across offers. Everything else has conditions attached, so always establish this figure first.

Variable pay: Industry surveys commonly report variable pay at 10-20% of CTC at mid-to-large companies, tied to individual and company performance targets. Ask what percentage of employees actually received the full variable payout in the last completed year before counting it as income.

ESOPs or RSUs: At startups, ESOPs are commonly structured with a 4-year vest and a 1-year cliff. At listed tech companies, RSUs are publicly reported to form a significant share of total comp at senior levels, sometimes matching or exceeding base salary.

Joining bonus: A one-time payment separate from CTC, often offered to compensate for unvested stock or a bonus you forfeit by switching employers. It is negotiable and worth asking for explicitly.

Perks: Health insurance, meal allowances, learning budgets, and remote work flexibility do not appear in CTC but add real value and are worth factoring into any side-by-side comparison.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Do Glassdoor salary numbers for ML Engineers in Bangalore reflect real offers?

Glassdoor gives a useful directional benchmark, but sample sizes for any specific company can be small, so treat individual data points with caution. Publicly reported figures also tend to undercount total comp because many employees do not separately report ESOPs or bonuses. Use Glassdoor as a starting anchor and cross-check with levels.fyi and peer conversations for a fuller picture.

Are LLM and generative AI skills paying more than general ML skills right now?

Commonly cited hiring data for 2025-2026 suggests engineers with hands-on LLM fine-tuning, RAG pipeline design, or inference optimisation experience are attracting a noticeable premium over generalist ML Engineers. The gap is most visible at product companies and AI-first startups rather than IT services firms. If you have this experience, make it prominent in your resume and mention it early in recruiter conversations.

What is the difference between CTC and my actual take-home salary?

CTC (Cost to Company) includes your base salary plus employer contributions like PF, gratuity, and sometimes insurance premiums or meal allowances. Your actual monthly take-home is lower after deducting employee PF, professional tax, and income tax based on your chosen tax regime. Commonly cited estimates suggest monthly in-hand is roughly 70-75% of your annual CTC divided by 12, though the exact figure varies by tax slab and your company's pay structure.

How many ML Engineer jobs are currently open in Bangalore?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracked 165 active ML Engineer openings in Bangalore, the highest count of any Indian city in this dataset. Roles span product companies, AI startups, IT services firms, and global capability centres. The actual number of opportunities is higher since many companies hire directly without listing on public job boards.

Should I share my current salary with a recruiter?

You are generally not required to disclose your current CTC during initial screening, and many companies are moving toward discussing expected CTC based on market rates rather than prior salary. Focus the conversation on your expected number, grounded in publicly available benchmarks like Glassdoor and levels.fyi. If pressed, you can share your current package after you have a written offer in hand, which gives you more leverage to negotiate upward.

How much negotiation room is typically built into an ML Engineer offer?

Commonly cited advice from recruiters and hiring managers suggests most companies build a buffer into their initial ML Engineer offers. Pushing back with a data-backed counter citing Glassdoor or levels.fyi figures for your experience band is normal and expected. Offers are rarely rescinded for politely negotiating, particularly in a specialised technical field where demand consistently outpaces supply.
