01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Spinny is one of India's well-known full-stack used-car platforms, operating across multiple cities and offering end-to-end car buying and selling services. As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 7 open roles at Spinny, spanning areas like technology, operations, sales, and business development.

Getting hired here generally involves a structured process with a few rounds of interviews. The company looks for people who are genuinely excited about transforming how India buys and sells cars, and who can handle a fast-moving, target-driven environment.

Spinny hires at various experience levels, from early-career professionals in field and sales roles to experienced specialists in tech, product, and leadership positions. If you have a background in automotive, e-commerce, fintech, or high-growth startups, your profile is likely to get a closer look.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages below are based on publicly reported candidate experiences and may vary by role and team.

Application and resume screening: You apply through the company website or a job portal. HR reviews resumes and shortlists candidates whose backgrounds match the role. Turnaround is commonly reported to be a few days to a couple of weeks.

HR screening call: A recruiter or HR executive contacts you to confirm your interest, verify basic details, and give a brief overview of the role. Expect questions about your current situation, notice period, and salary expectations. This is typically a brief call.

Technical or functional round(s): Depending on the role, there may be one or two rounds here. For tech roles, this typically involves coding problems, system design discussions, or case studies. For sales or operations roles, expect scenario-based questions testing your problem-solving and customer-handling skills. Panels may include the hiring manager and a team lead.

Final round or leadership interview: Some roles include a final discussion with a senior leader or department head. This round often focuses on culture fit, your long-term goals, and your understanding of the used-car market in India.

Offer and background verification: If selected, HR shares a written offer. Background verification is standard and typically happens before or shortly after joining.

The total process commonly takes anywhere from a few weeks to about a month, depending on the role and hiring team availability.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Customer obsession: Spinny's business revolves around making the car-buying experience trustworthy. For any customer-facing or product role, showing that you genuinely care about solving buyer and seller pain points matters a lot.

Comfort with data: Whether you are in operations, tech, or business development, the ability to look at numbers, draw insights, and make decisions based on evidence is a recurring expectation.

Adaptability in a high-growth environment: Things move fast at a company scaling across many cities. Candidates who can handle ambiguity, pick up new tools, and shift priorities without losing focus tend to do well here.

Ownership mindset: Spinny values people who take responsibility for outcomes rather than just completing tasks. During interviews, expect questions that probe whether you follow through on what you commit to.

Industry or adjacent experience (for certain roles): For roles in automotive operations, procurement, or pricing, having experience in the auto industry or in e-commerce logistics is a genuine plus, though not always mandatory.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply: Spend time on the Spinny app or website. Go through a car listing end to end, understand how the pricing works, and look at how they present inspection reports. Being able to speak specifically about the product signals real interest.

Know the used-car market context: India's used-car market has been growing, and Spinny operates in a competitive space. Read publicly available news about the sector so you can hold a conversation about trends, challenges, and where the company might be headed.

Prepare for behavioural questions: These are standard across all roles. Think of specific examples from your past work that show ownership, handling failure, working across teams, and dealing with difficult customers or stakeholders.

For tech roles, practise the fundamentals: Coding rounds at growth-stage companies like Spinny typically test data structures, algorithms, and sometimes system design. Use practice platforms to sharpen your skills. For product roles, practise writing clear product requirement documents and walking through prioritisation decisions.

Have your numbers ready: For sales and business roles, interviewers will ask about your past targets and how you performed against them. Be ready to talk about your track record honestly and specifically.

Prepare questions to ask them: Asking thoughtful questions about team structure, what success looks like in your first few months, and how decisions are made shows that you are evaluating them too, which leaves a strong impression.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Spinny operates with a strong focus on trust and transparency, qualities that also define how they try to work internally. The culture tends to be fast-paced and performance-oriented, especially in sales and operations teams where targets are a daily reality.

Publicly reported employee feedback points to a young, energetic workforce and a relatively flat structure where people can access senior leaders without too many layers. At the same time, some reviews mention that the pace can be demanding, so this environment suits people who enjoy a challenge more than a steady, predictable routine.

Cross-functional collaboration is common since a used-car transaction involves procurement, inspection, pricing, logistics, financing, and sales all working together. If you enjoy working with different teams rather than staying in one silo, you will likely find that natural here.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many interview rounds does Spinny typically have?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, most roles at Spinny involve three to four rounds: an HR screening call, one or two technical or functional rounds, and sometimes a final leadership discussion. The exact number varies by role level and team. Senior positions tend to have more rounds than early-career or field roles.

Does Spinny hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Spinny does hire freshers, particularly for field sales, customer relationship, and operations roles. Tech and product roles usually prefer some prior experience, though internship experience can count. Checking the specific job description is the best way to confirm what a given role requires.

How long does the hiring process take from application to offer?

The process commonly takes a few weeks to about a month in total, based on publicly reported experiences. Timelines can vary depending on how quickly the hiring team schedules rounds and the number of candidates in the pipeline. Following up politely with HR after each round can sometimes help keep things moving.

What salary can I expect at Spinny?

Compensation at Spinny varies significantly by role, level, and city. Glassdoor and publicly reported reviews show a range across different functions, but sample sizes are small, so treat any figures you find there as rough indicators rather than guarantees. Your best bet is to ask HR directly about the band for your specific role during the screening call.

Does Spinny offer remote or hybrid work options?

This depends on the role. Tech and product roles are more likely to offer hybrid arrangements, while sales, procurement, and operations roles are typically in-person or field-based by nature. The job description usually specifies the work mode, and you can confirm during the HR screening call.

How should I follow up after an interview at Spinny?

Send a brief thank-you note to your HR point of contact within a day of your interview. If you have not heard back within a week, a short, polite follow-up email is appropriate. Avoid following up more than once or twice, as the hiring team manages multiple candidates and will reach out when a decision is made.
