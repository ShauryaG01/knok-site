01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Synopsys is one of the world's leading electronic design automation (EDA) companies, building the software and IP that help design and verify chips powering AI hardware, smartphones, automotive systems, and more. It has a strong India presence with major engineering centres in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune, and recruits heavily from Indian universities and experienced talent pools.

As of July 2026, Synopsys has 94 open roles across functions including VLSI design, EDA software engineering, design verification, application engineering, and corporate support. The technical bar is high, reflecting the specialised nature of EDA work. But the process is structured and predictable. If you know what to prepare, your chances improve significantly.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Synopsys hiring process typically moves through the following stages. Steps and sequencing can vary by team and role, so treat this as a general guide.

Application and resume screen. You apply via the Synopsys careers portal or through a referral. A recruiter reviews resumes and shortlists candidates, usually within a few weeks. Tailoring your resume to highlight directly relevant skills, such as RTL design, verification flows, or software engineering depth, helps your application stand out.

Recruiter call. A brief call where the recruiter explains the role, asks about your experience and notice period, and checks basic fit. Have a clear, confident summary of your background ready.

Technical screening. One or two online assessments or a phone screen with an engineer. For hardware roles, expect questions on digital design, Verilog or SystemVerilog, timing concepts, and verification basics. For software roles, expect data structures, algorithms, and possibly a system design question.

Technical interview rounds. Typically two to four rounds with engineers and senior staff. Depending on the role, these cover digital design concepts, UVM-based verification, Python or Tcl scripting, or C++ and algorithms. Publicly reported feedback on Glassdoor describes these rounds as thorough and concept-focused rather than tool-specific.

Hiring manager round. A conversation about your project experience, how you approach ambiguous problems, and team fit. Expect detailed questions about specific work you have listed on your resume.

Offer and negotiation. HR presents a package that commonly includes base salary, annual bonus, and RSU grants. Levels.fyi and Glassdoor carry publicly reported Synopsys India compensation data to help you benchmark before you negotiate.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Synopsys hires specialists. The work is deep and domain-specific, and interviews reflect that. Here is what interviewers commonly look for.

Domain depth for hardware roles. For VLSI, RTL, DFT, or verification positions, candidates need a strong foundation in digital design. RTL coding in Verilog or SystemVerilog, timing closure, power intent using UPF, and verification methodologies including UVM and formal verification are all commonly tested areas.

Software quality for EDA tool roles. If you are joining the tools engineering side, strong C++ and Python skills matter, along with understanding of compiler theory, data structures, and large-scale software architecture. Code quality and problem-solving are prioritised over framework familiarity.

Scripting and flow automation. Many Synopsys roles, including on the design side, expect proficiency in Python, Tcl, or Perl for automating engineering flows and customising tool behaviour.

Clear communication. Synopsys operates globally, with Indian teams collaborating closely with counterparts in the US, Europe, and Taiwan. Written and verbal communication skills matter, especially for application engineering and cross-site roles.

Ownership and curiosity. Interviewers commonly probe whether you drove a problem end-to-end versus just executing assigned tasks. Candidates who can describe what they personally contributed, what went wrong, and what they learned tend to stand out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Go back to fundamentals, not just tools. Synopsys interviews test concepts. Review digital logic, timing analysis, clock domain crossing, and verification theory from first principles, rather than memorising tool commands.

Practise RTL coding and debugging. For hardware roles, write Verilog or SystemVerilog problems from scratch and practise finding and fixing RTL bugs. Being comfortable under time pressure matters in a technical screen.

Know your project work in depth. Interviewers will probe your resume closely. For each project, be ready to explain your specific contribution, the technical challenges you faced, and what you would do differently today.

Solve coding problems regularly. For software and EDA tool roles, practise data structures, graph algorithms, and dynamic programming problems. Medium to hard difficulty problems are a reasonable preparation target.

Research the Synopsys product portfolio. Spend time understanding what tools like Design Compiler, VCS, Fusion Compiler, or PrimePower actually do. You do not need to be a power user, but showing genuine interest in the technology makes a strong impression.

Benchmark your salary expectations. Levels.fyi and Glassdoor carry publicly reported Synopsys India compensation data. Use these to go into negotiations with informed, realistic expectations.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Synopsys describes its culture around innovation, integrity, and customer focus. Publicly available employee feedback points to a few consistent themes.

Strong technical culture. Engineers at Synopsys frequently describe their colleagues as some of the most technically strong they have worked with. The company attracts people who enjoy going very deep into a narrow, complex problem space. Curiosity and rigour are valued highly.

Structured, process-oriented engineering. EDA is a product business with long release cycles. Teams tend to follow structured engineering processes including design reviews, test coverage standards, and clear code ownership. Candidates who thrive in structured environments tend to do well here.

Global collaboration with real ownership. Indian teams hold genuine ownership over major features and products, not just support or execution roles. Cross-site work with the US, Europe, and Taiwan is common, which means some schedule flexibility is useful.

Work-life balance varies by team. Publicly reported feedback on Glassdoor suggests balance depends heavily on the specific team and where it sits in the product release cycle. Intensity periods exist, especially around tape-out deadlines or major product launches.

Merit-driven career growth. Promotions at Synopsys are based on demonstrated impact. Employees who take ownership and deliver outcomes tend to advance. The company rewards depth of contribution over mere visibility.

How many jobs are currently open at Synopsys?

As of July 2026, Synopsys has 94 open roles across engineering, application engineering, verification, and corporate functions. This number shifts regularly as positions are filled and new ones open. Checking the Synopsys careers portal frequently, or using a tool that monitors job listings daily, helps you avoid missing new postings.

Does Synopsys hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Synopsys does hire fresh graduates, primarily through intern-to-hire pipelines and entry-level engineer roles in VLSI design and software engineering. Competition is strong. Strong academic projects in digital design or software development, combined with relevant internship experience, significantly improve your chances over a resume with coursework alone.

What salary can I expect at Synopsys India?

Compensation varies by level, function, and location. Publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi covers a range of Synopsys India roles and is the most reliable source to benchmark against. Packages at Synopsys commonly include base salary, an annual performance bonus, and RSU grants, so compare total compensation and not just base pay when evaluating an offer.

Does a referral help when applying to Synopsys?

Yes, a referral meaningfully improves your chances of getting past the initial resume screen. If you know someone at Synopsys, ask them to refer you directly through the internal referral system. If you do not have a contact yet, connecting with Synopsys engineers on LinkedIn and expressing genuine interest in their work is a reasonable first step toward building that relationship.

How long does the Synopsys hiring process take from application to offer?

The end-to-end process is commonly cited as taking several weeks, though timelines vary by role and team. Specialised technical roles with multiple interview rounds can take longer. Staying in periodic contact with your recruiter for status updates is perfectly acceptable and does not affect your candidacy negatively.

Which cities in India does Synopsys hire from?

Synopsys has major engineering centres in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune, with Bengaluru being the largest hub for engineering roles. Some positions offer hybrid work arrangements, though this depends on the specific team and function. Always check the location details on each individual job listing before applying to avoid surprises after an offer.
