04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

As of July 2026, there are 27 active Data Scientist openings in Hyderabad across major job sites. That places the city fourth among Indian metros, well behind Bangalore (166 roles) but ahead of Mumbai (17) and Chennai (8). Delhi has 46 roles and Pune has 18.

Hyderabad's data science market has a clear identity: it is dominated by Global Capability Centres (GCCs) of large multinational companies, particularly in pharma, healthcare, and media. If your goal is to work on enterprise-scale data pipelines, ML model deployment, or analytics for global business units, the city's GCC cluster is worth targeting seriously. A handful of product companies and tech services startups round out the picture, giving you options at both ends of the stability-versus-growth spectrum.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Match your resume to the company type. For GCC roles at Amgen or Optum, hiring managers want to see experience with large, regulated datasets: clinical data, claims data, or supply chain analytics. For product companies like highradius, emphasise model deployment and measurable business impact. For startups like perceptyne, show breadth and comfort with ambiguity.

Go deep on a relevant industry vertical. Most active Hyderabad employers sit in pharma, healthcare, or media. A portfolio project built on publicly available clinical trial data or media consumption data signals domain fluency in a way that generic Kaggle competition work does not.

Show production-ready skills. What separates mid-level from senior candidates is usually evidence that models were deployed, monitored, and maintained, not just trained. If you have done work with CI/CD for ML, model monitoring, or A/B testing, mention it clearly on your resume.

Pursue referrals at GCCs. GCCs post across many portals but often shortlist faster through internal referrals. A short, specific LinkedIn message to someone currently at Amgen Hyderabad or Optum Hyderabad is worth the five minutes it takes.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With 27 openings across the city, the Hyderabad Data Scientist market is narrow enough to review every active listing in a single sitting. The main risk is missing roles as they go live, since GCC screeners can move quickly and build shortlists from early applicants.

Apply within the first few days of a posting going live. A role posted on Monday may already have screening calls scheduled by end of week at a large GCC. Being in the first wave of applicants matters more than a perfectly polished resume.

Personalise your application, not just your resume. A brief cover note that says 'I have worked on clinical data pipelines and am specifically interested in Amgen's Hyderabad analytics team' is more effective than a generic application, even when the underlying resume is identical.

Follow up once. When you are watching a small pool of roles, a polite follow-up about a week after applying is reasonable and often expected in the Indian job market. A short LinkedIn message to the recruiter or HR contact is the right format.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hyderabad a good city for data science jobs compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has far more Data Scientist openings than Hyderabad right now (166 vs 27 in this snapshot), so if sheer volume of choice matters most, Bangalore is the stronger market. That said, Hyderabad offers a lower cost of living, a well-established GCC ecosystem, and proportionally fewer applicants per opening, which can make it easier to land a role at a large multinational. It depends on whether you prioritise options or conversion rate.

Which companies in Hyderabad are hiring the most Data Scientists right now?

Amgen leads with 10 open roles, followed by Warner Bros Discovery with 6 and sigmoid with 3. These three alone account for more than half the active openings in the city. The remaining roles are spread one or two at a time across ZeMoSo Technologies, perceptyne, highradius, Outreach, Optum, DevLabs Technology, and Bms.

What salary can I expect as a Data Scientist in Hyderabad?

Based on active listings, entry-level roles (0-2 years) typically range from 8-16 LPA, mid-level (3-5 years) from 18-30 LPA, and senior roles (6-9 years) from 30-48 LPA. Lead and principal positions go 45-70+ LPA for the most senior profiles. For company-specific figures, Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported numbers that are worth checking before you negotiate.

Are these roles mostly work-from-office or remote?

GCC roles at companies like Amgen and Optum are largely hybrid or full office, since the GCC model is built around co-located teams working in the same facility. Startups like perceptyne may offer more flexibility. The work arrangement is almost always stated clearly in the job description, so it is worth checking before investing time in an application.

What skills do Hyderabad Data Scientist roles ask for most?

Python, SQL, and core machine learning skills appear in nearly every active listing. GCC roles from pharma and healthcare companies often add domain-specific requirements such as clinical data handling or regulatory familiarity. Product and SaaS companies tend to emphasise model deployment, cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure, and the ability to translate data findings into business decisions that non-technical stakeholders can act on.

How do I get a referral at a GCC like Amgen or Optum in Hyderabad?

Search LinkedIn for employees currently based at the Hyderabad office, filter by shared connections, and send a short message that names the exact role and explains briefly why your background fits. Alumni networks from engineering colleges are a particularly strong channel for GCC referrals in Hyderabad. A referral does not guarantee a hire but typically gets your resume a faster first look, which matters when screeners are moving quickly.
