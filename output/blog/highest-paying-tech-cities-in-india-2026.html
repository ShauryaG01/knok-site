06 Key Findings

Key Findings

  • Bengaluru holds the top position for tech compensation in India, with Glassdoor-reported total packages for mid-to-senior engineers running higher than any other Indian city in the 2026 snapshot.
  • Hyderabad has closed the gap significantly. Publicly reported offers from product companies and global capability centres (GCCs) in the city now rival Bengaluru for several high-demand roles.
  • AI/ML engineering and data science tracks command the largest salary premiums across every major city, with industry surveys placing them well above general software engineering bands at the same experience level.
  • Company type is often a bigger lever than city. A product engineer at a funded startup or MNC in Pune can out-earn an SDE 2 at an IT services firm in Bengaluru, according to publicly reported Glassdoor data.
  • Remote-first roles are increasingly listed with Bengaluru-equivalent pay bands, which widens the opportunity set for engineers who prefer not to relocate.
  • The knok job index snapshot (2026-07-08) shows demand concentrated in product companies and GCCs, with IT services hiring anchoring closer to base-band offers across all cities.
07 Methodology

Methodology

This report draws on the knok job index, a nightly crawl of 150+ Indian job sites that captures active listings, employer names, role titles, and stated or inferred salary bands as of 2026-07-08. Where listings did not include a stated salary, role-level inference was used to bucket them by seniority tier. External cross-checks used Glassdoor and levels.fyi publicly reported data to validate directional trends. All salary figures in this report are presented as ranges rather than point estimates. Readers should treat them as indicators of market direction rather than precise benchmarks. Sample sizes vary by city and role, and thin clusters are flagged where relevant. Figures represent base salary plus fixed allowances; equity, bonuses, and variable pay components are excluded unless noted. The knok job index is the primary source; 'knok jobradar' is the internal label for the nightly data pipeline.

11 What It Means

What It Means

If you are actively job-hunting in Indian tech right now, the city you target and the company type you approach matter more than many job seekers realise.

City choice shapes your ceiling. Bengaluru and Hyderabad are where the highest-paying product and GCC roles cluster. If you are open to relocating, these two cities give you the widest pool of above-median opportunities. That said, cost of living is also highest in both, so take-home purchasing power is not always as different from Pune or Chennai as the gross package suggests.

Role specialisation pays more than tenure alone. The publicly reported data consistently shows that engineers who move into AI/ML, platform engineering, or product management earn more than those on a straight SDE track with the same years of experience. If you are in the 3-6 year range, a deliberate pivot to a high-premium specialisation may matter more than chasing the next promotion in your current track.

Company type is a bigger lever than city. A product engineer at a funded startup or an MNC product team in Pune can out-earn an SDE 2 at an IT services firm in Bengaluru. The knok job index snapshot shows the majority of above-median listings are concentrated in product companies and GCCs, regardless of city.

Remote is real, but verify the band. More listings now carry location-flexible tags, but not all of them offer Bengaluru-equivalent pay. When applying, check whether the employer adjusts compensation by location. Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which city pays the most for software engineers in India in 2026?

Bengaluru remains the top-paying city for software engineers, based on Glassdoor-reported data and industry surveys as of mid-2026. Hyderabad is a strong second, particularly for roles at global capability centres and US-headquartered product companies. The gap between the two has narrowed over the past two years, so both cities are worth targeting if maximum compensation is your priority.

Is the higher salary in Bengaluru worth it given the cost of living?

It depends on your lifestyle and family situation. Publicly reported housing and commute data shows that costs in Bengaluru are among the highest in India, which erodes some of the gross pay advantage. Engineers in Pune or Hyderabad sometimes report comparable savings rates despite lower gross packages. Run a personal cost-of-living calculation before assuming relocation automatically improves your finances.

What is the highest-paying tech role in India right now?

Engineering manager and AI/ML engineer roles consistently show the highest Glassdoor-reported and levels.fyi-reported total compensation in the Indian market. Product managers at senior levels also command strong packages, particularly at product-first companies. Within individual contributor tracks, platform and infrastructure engineers tend to earn more than application-layer developers at the same seniority level.

Do GCCs pay more than Indian product startups?

It depends on the specific company and role. Publicly reported data shows that top-tier MNC GCCs often offer the highest total compensation in India, including equity in the parent company listed on overseas exchanges. Well-funded Indian startups can match or exceed this on base salary and sometimes exceed it on equity upside. IT services GCCs typically pay below both categories at equivalent experience levels.

Can I get Bengaluru-level pay in a remote role from a smaller city?

Some employers do offer location-agnostic pay, but it is not universal. Publicly reported cases on levels.fyi show that certain product companies and funded startups pay the same band regardless of where the employee lives, while others explicitly adjust compensation by city tier. The safest approach is to ask the recruiter directly whether the role uses a location-adjusted pay policy before investing time in the interview process.

How should I use salary data when negotiating my next offer?

Treat publicly reported ranges from Glassdoor and levels.fyi as a floor for your research, not a ceiling. Sample sizes on these platforms skew toward people who voluntarily report, which may not perfectly represent your specific company and role. Gather at least three to five data points for your exact role, level, and city before going into a negotiation. Then anchor to the upper end of the credible range and be prepared to explain why your experience justifies it.
