01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Timesinternet is the digital and technology arm of the Times Group, one of India's most recognizable media conglomerates. It powers brands like Times of India, Economic Times, iDiva, MensXP, Times Prime, and TimesJobs, reaching millions of users across the country. The company sits at the intersection of legacy brand trust and modern product building, making it an interesting employer if you want real scale without joining an early-stage startup.

As of July 2026, Timesinternet has around 20 open roles across engineering, product, content, data, and growth functions. Hiring is ongoing primarily out of its Noida and Mumbai offices, and the company recruits through its own careers portal, LinkedIn, and Naukri. The interview process is structured and typically takes a few weeks from application to offer.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Timesinternet generally runs across four to five stages. The exact structure depends on the role and the team you are applying to, but here is what candidates commonly describe:

Application and resume screening. Your resume is reviewed by the talent acquisition team. Roles are listed on the company careers page and on platforms like Naukri and LinkedIn. A resume that clearly maps your skills to the job description moves through faster. With around 20 open roles at any given time, competition exists but the pipeline is not as flooded as at some of the larger pure-play tech firms.

HR screening call. A recruiter reaches out for a short call to discuss your background, notice period, location preference, and salary expectations. They will also ask why you want to join Timesinternet specifically. This round is primarily about logistics and communication, not deep technical evaluation.

Technical or skills assessment. This is where the real evaluation happens. Engineering candidates typically face a coding test or a live problem-solving session. Product managers are often given a product teardown or a case study to present. Content and marketing candidates may receive a written assignment or be asked to share a portfolio. Prepare this round seriously because it is the biggest filter in the process.

Managerial or panel interview. You meet your potential reporting manager, sometimes along with a cross-functional colleague. Expect a mix of technical questions, past project walkthroughs, and situational questions along the lines of 'tell me about a time when you had to navigate a shifting priority.' Senior roles often have two rounds at this stage.

HR final round and offer. Once the panel signs off, the HR team confirms details, completes any background checks, and rolls out the offer. From first contact to offer, the process commonly takes three to five weeks, though urgent or senior roles can close faster.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Timesinternet looks for people who can operate in a high-traffic, content-heavy digital environment. Here is what tends to matter across roles:

For engineers, strong fundamentals in your core language matter more than framework familiarity. Experience with systems that handle large user volumes is a visible plus because Timesinternet products serve millions of daily users. Familiarity with cloud infrastructure, data pipelines, or recommendation systems is frequently cited in job descriptions.

For product managers, the ability to frame problems in terms of user impact and business metrics is key. Timesinternet has a strong analytics culture, so comfort with data and A/B testing matters. Prior experience in media, e-commerce, or subscription products is a bonus.

For content and editorial roles, domain expertise in your beat (finance, lifestyle, sports, tech) is as important as writing ability. SEO awareness and comfort with publishing platforms are baseline expectations.

Across all roles, a few traits consistently appear in Timesinternet hiring conversations:

  • Ownership mindset: the ability to drive things to completion without constant hand-holding
  • Comfort with ambiguity: priorities shift quickly in digital media, and teams are expected to adapt
  • Cross-functional communication: you will regularly work across editorial, product, and engineering teams
  • Scale awareness: understanding how decisions affect millions of users, not just a small test group
05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product portfolio before your first call. Spend time actually using Times of India, Economic Times, Times Prime, and at least one or two niche properties like MensXP or iDiva. Have opinions about the user experience. Interviewers appreciate candidates who arrive as informed users, not just applicants who read the Wikipedia page.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Timesinternet job descriptions are fairly detailed. Mirror the language of the job description in your resume, especially around metrics and scale. If you have worked on products serving large user bases, make that visible.

Prepare two or three strong project stories. For technical and product roles, be ready to walk through a past project in depth: the problem, your specific contribution, the outcome, and what you would do differently. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions because Timesinternet panel interviews lean heavily on past experience.

Brush up on digital media business models. Timesinternet earns through advertising, subscriptions (Times Prime), and lead generation. Understanding how the business works helps you ask sharper questions and signals genuine interest during the interview.

Take the assignment round seriously. Many roles include a take-home task. Treat it as a real work deliverable: clear structure, backed by reasoning, submitted on time. Rushed or surface-level assignments rarely move forward at this stage.

Stay on top of new openings. Timesinternet opens roles as projects scale, and many close quickly. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Timesinternet operates within a large, well-established group but runs its digital properties with a product-company mindset. The culture is generally described as fast-paced, with short iteration cycles driven by audience and traffic data.

Scale is a defining feature of the environment. Engineers and product managers work on systems serving some of the highest-traffic properties in India. That scale brings both pride and pressure: decisions get tested at real volume quickly, and there is little room for untested assumptions.

The environment is data-driven. Teams rely heavily on analytics to make decisions about content, features, and growth. If you are not comfortable with metrics-led thinking, the culture can feel demanding. People who come in comfortable with dashboards and experimentation tend to settle in faster.

Cross-functional collaboration is the norm. Editorial, product, engineering, and monetization teams work closely together. People who communicate well across these functions tend to do well. Hierarchies exist but are not unusually rigid for a company of this size.

Career growth is real but self-driven. Timesinternet has a large enough portfolio that lateral moves between products are possible over time. Growth tends to reward people who proactively take on scope rather than waiting to be handed opportunities.

Most roles are based out of the Noida headquarters, with some presence in Mumbai. The company follows standard Indian corporate practices around appraisals, leave, and benefits.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs are currently open at Timesinternet?

As of July 2026, Timesinternet has around 20 open roles listed across functions including engineering, product, content, data, and growth. The number shifts as positions are filled and new ones open up. Checking the company careers page and their LinkedIn page regularly gives you the most current picture.

What is the salary range at Timesinternet?

Timesinternet does not publish salary bands publicly. Based on Glassdoor reviews and publicly reported data from similar digital media companies, compensation for mid-level roles is broadly in line with Indian tech and digital media industry norms. For specific numbers by role and experience level, Glassdoor and levels.fyi reviews from recent Timesinternet employees are the most grounded source available.

Does Timesinternet hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Timesinternet hires both. Entry-level and campus roles exist, particularly in content, data, and engineering. However, the majority of open roles at any given time target professionals with a few years of experience. Freshers applying for experienced roles will find it harder, so focusing on roles explicitly tagged as entry-level or trainee gives you a better shot.

Is the Timesinternet interview process difficult?

The difficulty varies by role. Engineering interviews are reasonably rigorous, with a focus on problem-solving and system design for senior positions. Product and content roles emphasize case studies, portfolio quality, and situational judgment more than algorithmic depth. Candidates who prepare their project stories clearly and research the company products before the interview generally find the overall process manageable.

Can I apply to multiple roles at Timesinternet at the same time?

Yes, applying to more than one role is fine. It is worth being selective and applying only where your profile genuinely matches, because recruiters at mid-sized hiring teams notice when the same candidate applies broadly without tailoring. A focused application to two or three relevant roles is more effective than spreading thin across the entire job board.

How can I make my Timesinternet application stand out?

Come in as a genuine user of their products, not just a job applicant. Being able to speak specifically about a feature you found useful or a UX gap you noticed on Times of India or Times Prime makes a strong impression during the screening call. Pair that with a resume that clearly shows scale, ownership, and measurable outcomes, and you are ahead of most applicants.
