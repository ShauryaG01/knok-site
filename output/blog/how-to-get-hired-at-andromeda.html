01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Andromeda is one of India's largest loan distribution companies, connecting borrowers with lenders across home loans, personal loans, and business loans. As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 12 open roles at Andromeda, spanning sales, operations, credit analysis, and relationship management. Getting hired here is a realistic goal if you understand what the company values: strong client relationship skills, comfort with financial products, and the ability to meet targets consistently.

Andromeda operates in a competitive fintech-adjacent space where the team is expected to be both financially literate and people-oriented. Whether you are applying for a field sales role or a back-office position, the hiring process is structured but not overly lengthy, typically wrapping up within a few weeks from first contact to offer.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Andromeda's interview process usually runs through three to four stages, based on what candidates commonly report. Here is what to expect at each step.

Application and resume shortlisting. You apply online or through a job portal. The team looks for relevant experience in BFSI (banking, financial services, and insurance) or direct sales. If your profile matches, expect a call within a week.

Phone or video screening. A recruiter or HR executive typically calls to verify your background, ask about your notice period, and check salary expectations. This is a short conversation focused on fit and logistics, not deep technical questions.

Skills or domain round. Depending on the role, you will likely face a one-on-one interview with a team lead or department manager. For sales roles, expect situational questions about handling client objections, hitting targets, and managing a pipeline. For operations or credit roles, expect questions on loan documentation, verification processes, and regulatory compliance basics.

HR and offer round. If the previous rounds go well, HR schedules a final discussion covering compensation, joining date, and role expectations. Candidates for senior roles sometimes have an additional conversation with a senior leader before the offer is made.

The entire process commonly takes two to four weeks from application to offer letter, though timelines vary by location and how urgently the role needs to be filled. With 12 active openings currently, the team is in active hiring mode, so response times are generally reasonable.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Andromeda consistently looks for a few core traits across most of its roles.

Sales drive and target orientation. Much of what Andromeda does is volume-driven. They want people who are comfortable with numbers, comfortable with rejection, and motivated by closing deals. If you have a track record of meeting or exceeding goals, make that visible on your resume and in every interview round.

Financial product knowledge. You do not need to be a banker, but you should understand the basics: how home loans work, what a credit score means, and how lenders evaluate loan applications. Candidates who can speak confidently about loan products tend to move forward faster than those who are learning on the spot.

Communication skills suited to the local context. Many Andromeda roles involve direct client interaction, often in regional languages. Fluency in the local language for the city you are applying to is a genuine advantage, not just a nice-to-have.

Reliability and follow-through. Loan disbursement involves many moving parts and multiple stakeholders. They value people who can manage several active files at once, follow up without being chased, and keep clients informed at every step.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the loan products Andromeda distributes. Spend time understanding home loans, loan against property, business loans, and personal loans at a working level. Being able to discuss these products naturally in an interview signals that you will hit the ground running from day one.

Prepare STAR-format answers for sales and client scenarios. Questions like 'Tell me about a time you turned a hesitant client into a confirmed customer' or 'How do you manage your pipeline when you have multiple active leads at once' are common. Write out two or three strong real examples from your past work before the interview.

Know your own numbers. If you are coming from a sales background, be ready to quote your targets, actuals, and conversion rates from previous roles. Andromeda's hiring managers pay attention to candidates who know their own performance data without having to think twice.

Dress for a professional environment. Even for field sales roles, first impressions matter in financial services. Business casual or formal is a safe choice for in-person rounds.

Follow up after each round. A brief, polite message to HR after your interview is not yet common practice in India but is noticed positively. Keep it short and specific to the conversation you just had.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Andromeda's work culture reflects the nature of the loan distribution business: target-driven, client-focused, and fast-paced. Teams operate with a fair degree of autonomy, especially in field roles, where you manage your own schedule and client relationships without constant supervision.

The company has a strong performance culture. High performers are recognized and often fast-tracked into leadership positions. At the same time, the environment can be demanding, particularly around month-end targets. Colleagues who thrive here tend to be self-starters who enjoy the energy of closing deals and genuinely helping clients through a significant financial decision.

Andromeda operates across multiple cities, which means there is real room for career movement if you perform well. Internal transfers and promotions are common, based on what employees in the BFSI space commonly report about organizations of this scale.

Within branches, the team environment tends to be collaborative, with regular check-ins and group target reviews. If you enjoy a structured but entrepreneurial work setting where your results speak directly for your growth, Andromeda is worth a serious look.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Andromeda hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Andromeda hires a mix of profiles. For sales and relationship manager roles, they commonly prefer candidates with at least some prior experience in BFSI or direct sales. For support and operations roles, fresher candidates with the right attitude and basic financial knowledge do get through. Always check the specific job description for the role you are targeting, as requirements vary significantly between openings.

What kind of salary can I expect at Andromeda?

Publicly reported salary figures for Andromeda roles vary widely by city, role, and experience level. Glassdoor and similar platforms list figures shared by current and former employees, but these are based on limited self-reported data and may not reflect your specific situation. Research comparable roles in your city on Glassdoor before the HR round, and be ready to discuss your expectations honestly during the offer conversation rather than waiting to be quoted a number first.

How important is it to know a regional language for Andromeda roles?

It depends on the city and the client base for the specific role. For positions in metros, English and Hindi are often sufficient. For roles targeting borrowers in smaller cities or semi-urban areas, the local language is a significant advantage and sometimes a firm requirement. The job description or the HR screening call will usually clarify this early, so ask directly if it is not mentioned.

How long does the Andromeda hiring process take from application to offer?

Most candidates who have gone through the process report a timeline of two to four weeks from application to offer letter. Roles that are urgently open may move faster. If you have not heard back within two weeks of your last interview round, it is reasonable to follow up with HR once. Being polite and specific in your follow-up message tends to get a response.

Is there a written test or assessment in the Andromeda interview process?

For most roles, there is no formal written test. The focus is on interview-based evaluation of your experience, communication, and product knowledge. Some credit or operations roles may include a basic assessment on loan documentation or financial concepts. Confirm this with the HR team during the screening call so you are not caught off guard and can prepare accordingly.

Are there real growth opportunities at Andromeda, or is it a lateral move?

Andromeda has a reasonably strong internal promotion culture, based on what is commonly reported in the BFSI sector. High performers in sales roles often move into team lead or branch manager positions over time. The company's multi-city presence also opens up the possibility of lateral transfers if you want to relocate or explore a different market. Growth here is performance-linked, so it is genuinely available but not automatic.
