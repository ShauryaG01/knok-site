01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Freshworks is a publicly listed SaaS company with deep roots in India, known for building products like Freshdesk, Freshsales, Freshservice, and Freshchat. With large engineering and go-to-market teams in Chennai and Bengaluru, it gives Indian professionals global product exposure without relocating.

As of July 2026, Freshworks has 145 open roles spanning engineering, product management, sales, customer success, and support. Hiring follows product roadmap cycles, so roles open and close quickly. Engineering and product positions attract the most competition, while customer success and support roles typically move faster through the pipeline.

For Indian job seekers, Freshworks stands out because it combines a globally competitive product culture with strong in-office teams in two major Indian cities. The company has a reputation for promoting from within, which makes a first role there a meaningful long-term career move, not just a stepping stone.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Freshworks runs a multi-stage hiring process. Exact rounds vary by function and level, but here is what most candidates typically go through.

Stage 1: Application and screening. Your resume is reviewed against the job description. For technical roles, an online assessment or take-home assignment may arrive at this stage before any human contact.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A short conversation to confirm your basics, salary expectations, and genuine interest in the role. Be ready to explain why Freshworks specifically, not just 'I want a SaaS job.'

Stage 3: Technical or skills round. For engineers, this usually means a live coding interview or a structured problem-solving session. For sales, marketing, or product roles, expect a case study, a mock pitch, or a structured exercise tied to the position. Interviewers focus on how you think, not just the final answer.

Stage 4: Hiring manager interview. A deeper conversation about your past experience, how you handle specific situations, and how you approach your work. Behavioral questions following a situation-task-action-result format come up often here.

Stage 5: Cross-functional or panel round. Some roles, especially at senior levels, include a round with peers or stakeholders from other teams. This tests collaboration style and communication clarity.

Stage 6: HR and offer discussion. Final alignment, reference checks where applicable, and the offer rollout. Total time from application to offer varies widely depending on the team and role urgency.

This sequence is based on commonly reported candidate experiences and may differ for specific teams or roles.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Freshworks builds software for businesses of all sizes, and that shapes exactly what they value in candidates.

Customer empathy. Freshworks products compete on ease of use and fast setup. They want people who genuinely think about the end user's experience, whether you are writing code, closing a deal, or handling a support ticket.

Product thinking in technical roles. Engineers are expected to have opinions on quality and user experience, not just ship code. Understanding how SaaS metrics like retention, activation, and feature adoption work will help you stand out from other candidates.

Ownership and speed. The company rewards people who can move quickly without waiting to be told what to do. Stories in your interviews that show initiative, fixing something broken, or shipping something independently tend to land well with interviewers.

Clear communication. Because teams are cross-functional and span multiple geographies, the ability to write and speak clearly is valued across all roles, not just customer-facing ones.

Engineering fundamentals. For technical roles, strong grounding in data structures, algorithms, and system design is expected at mid and senior levels. Familiarity with cloud-native development, APIs, and SaaS architecture patterns is a meaningful plus.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product firsthand. Sign up for a free trial of Freshdesk or Freshsales, spend time actually using it, and form your own view on what works well and what could be better. This kind of direct product knowledge makes your interview answers more credible and signals genuine interest beyond what a resume can show.

Tailor your resume carefully. Freshworks, like most large companies, uses applicant tracking before a human sees your profile. Mirror words from the job description in your resume where they honestly match your experience.

Prepare structured stories. For behavioral rounds, have several concrete examples ready covering moments where you solved a hard problem, handled a conflict, delivered under pressure, or learned from a failure. Practice saying them out loud in a situation-task-action-result format before the interview.

For engineering candidates: Practice coding problems covering patterns common in SaaS systems, prepare for system design scenarios like designing a ticketing system or a notification service, and get comfortable talking through trade-offs out loud as you work. Freshworks interviewers value thinking process as much as the final solution.

For sales and customer success candidates: Be ready to run through a mock discovery call or a product pitch. Study Freshworks' positioning relative to competitors so you can speak naturally to why customers choose them over alternatives.

Research the team. Look up the hiring manager or team on LinkedIn, read recent Freshworks blog posts and announcements, and understand which products the team you are applying to owns. Interviewers notice when candidates have done real homework.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Freshworks built its identity around a straightforward idea: business software should not be painful to use. That philosophy shapes how the company operates internally too.

'Refreshing business software' as a lived value. Teams are expected to question complexity, cut unnecessary steps, and ship things that feel intuitive. If you thrive in environments that reward simplicity and speed over elaborate process, you will likely feel comfortable here.

Relatively flat structure, fast decision-making. Compared to larger legacy enterprise companies, Freshworks tends to have shorter approval chains. Individual contributors can have visible impact relatively quickly, which employees often cite as a primary reason they stay.

Strong India presence, global mindset. The Chennai office is a major engineering hub, and teams there work on products used by companies around the world. That global-local balance is a real draw for Indian engineers who want international-scale impact without relocating abroad.

Internal mobility. Freshworks has a track record of helping employees move across teams and functions over time. If you join in one role and want to grow into another area, the culture supports having that conversation with your manager.

Work style. The company operates on a hybrid model in its India offices, with specific arrangements varying by team and function. Check the role listing for current in-office expectations and confirm them during the recruiter call rather than assuming.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the Freshworks hiring process usually take?

Timelines vary by team and role urgency, but candidates commonly report the process spanning several weeks from first contact to offer. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds tend to take longer than sales or support roles. If you have not heard back after completing a round, following up politely with the recruiter is a reasonable next step.

Does Freshworks hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Freshworks hires freshers, particularly through campus recruitment for engineering roles and for entry-level customer success and support positions. Experienced hires are more common for product management, senior engineering, and quota-carrying sales roles. With 145 open roles currently listed, there is a mix of levels, so check each listing carefully for experience requirements before applying.

What salary can I expect at Freshworks?

Compensation varies by role, level, and function. Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi show a wide range depending on seniority, and total compensation often includes stock options for permanent roles. Research the specific role and level on those platforms to get a realistic picture before discussing numbers with the recruiter.

How hard is the coding interview at Freshworks?

Candidates commonly describe Freshworks' coding rounds as moderate in difficulty, similar to mid-level problems on standard practice platforms. For senior roles, system design questions carry significant weight alongside coding. Talking through your thinking clearly as you solve problems tends to matter as much as arriving at the correct final answer.

Does Freshworks offer remote or work-from-home options?

Freshworks operates on a hybrid model in its India offices, but specific arrangements depend on the team and role. Customer success and support roles may have more defined in-office requirements than engineering roles. Always confirm the work-from-office expectation with the recruiter early in the process so there are no surprises after an offer is made.

How important is a referral when applying to Freshworks?

A referral from a current Freshworks employee significantly improves the likelihood of your application getting reviewed by a human. If you have a connection at the company, reach out before applying and ask if they are comfortable referring you. If you do not have a referral, a strong and well-tailored resume combined with a direct LinkedIn message to the hiring manager or recruiter are your next best options.
