01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Rapyd is a global fintech company building payment infrastructure that lets businesses collect, store, and send money across dozens of markets worldwide. If you work in payments, embedded finance, B2B SaaS, or financial services technology, Rapyd is one of the more active hiring companies worth tracking in 2026.

As of July 2026, Rapyd has 27 open roles tracked by knok jobradar, spread across engineering, product management, sales, compliance, and customer success. The company hires across experience levels, with mid-level to senior profiles dominating their active listings.

Rapyd is headquartered in Tel Aviv, with offices in London, Singapore, and other global hubs. They have a growing remote and hybrid workforce, and Indian professionals with fintech, payments, or enterprise software backgrounds have found a real foothold here.

The hiring process is structured and thorough. Expect a multi-stage process with a mix of technical evaluation and culture-fit conversations. The company looks for people who can work independently across time zones and move quickly when handed a problem.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Rapyd's interview process typically has four to five stages, though the exact sequence varies by role. Engineering and product roles usually include a technical assessment; business and operations roles may skip it. Treat this as a general guide rather than a guaranteed sequence.

StageWhat to expect
1. Application screeningYour resume is reviewed by a recruiter, often within a week of applying
2. Recruiter callA short introductory call covering your background, current role, and salary expectations
3. Skills or technical assessmentA take-home task or online exercise, most common for engineering and product roles
4. Technical or functional interviewsOne or two rounds with team members or senior leads, covering domain skills and real-world problem-solving
5. Hiring manager or final roundA closing conversation about your experience, work style, and long-term team fit

After the final round, Rapyd typically takes a few days to a couple of weeks to reach a decision. Timelines can stretch if the hiring panel spans multiple time zones, which is common given the global team structure.

Compensation discussions usually happen early. Be ready to share your current and expected package at the recruiter call stage so there are no surprises later.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Rapyd looks for people who combine strong domain knowledge with the ability to move fast and adapt. A few things consistently come up across roles:

Payments or fintech experience. If you have worked on payment gateways, wallets, cross-border transfers, or financial APIs, that gets immediate attention. Adjacent experience in banking tech or lending platforms is also valued.

Problem ownership. Rapyd operates like a scale-up, not a large enterprise. They want people who drive their own work rather than wait for direction.

Cross-functional communication. A lot of Rapyd's work involves coordination between engineering, compliance, and business teams. Clear written and spoken communication matters, especially across time zones.

Technical depth in the right areas. For engineering roles, backend development, API design, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure come up often, based on publicly reported candidate experiences.

Global mindset. The company serves dozens of markets and its teams span multiple continents. Being comfortable working across cultures and time zones is a genuine advantage here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with the product. Spend time with Rapyd's developer documentation and get a working sense of how their payment APIs are structured. Interviewers notice when a candidate understands what the company actually builds, not just what it does.

Refresh your domain fundamentals. For engineering roles, focus on system design, REST APIs, and distributed systems concepts. For product roles, come prepared to discuss how you have prioritised features and navigated trade-offs in a fast-moving environment.

Prepare your STAR stories. Rapyd interviews include behavioural questions around ownership, handling ambiguity, and working across teams. Have three or four strong examples ready from your past work.

Know the local fintech context. If you are an India-based candidate, understanding domestic payment systems, cross-border remittance, and B2B collections can be a real differentiator in the conversation.

Ask informed questions. Good questions about the team's current priorities, the biggest challenges in the role, and how success is measured signal that you are taking the opportunity seriously.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Rapyd moves fast. The culture rewards people who can make decisions with incomplete information, own their outcomes, and course-correct without waiting for permission. It is a genuinely global team, with day-to-day collaboration across Israel, the UK, Singapore, and other locations.

The company has a startup energy despite its scale. Processes are leaner than what you would find at a large bank or established tech firm, and that is by design. If you thrive in environments where things are actively being built or improved, that energy will suit you.

People who have worked at Rapyd commonly describe a strong technical culture, especially on the engineering and product side. There is an expectation that you will stay current in your domain and bring informed opinions to discussions, not just execute what you are told.

Rapyd values directness in communication. Feedback is shared openly and people are expected to do the same. If you want ownership and speed rather than slow sign-off chains, it is a good match.
