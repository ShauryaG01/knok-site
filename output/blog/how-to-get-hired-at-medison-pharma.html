01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Medison Pharma is a specialty pharmaceutical company focused on bringing innovative medicines to patients, particularly in oncology and rare diseases, in markets that have historically had limited access. Across its global and regional teams, the company spans medical affairs, regulatory, clinical, pharmacovigilance, and commercial functions.

As of July 2026, Medison Pharma has 68 open roles listed across its teams. That is a healthy pipeline covering everything from field-based medical science liaison (MSL) positions to regulatory affairs specialists and corporate support functions. Whether you are a life sciences fresher or a mid-career pharma professional looking to move into a purpose-driven organisation, there is a real range of opportunities worth exploring right now.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages below reflect what candidates in specialty pharma commonly report for firms of this type. The exact sequence can vary by role, seniority, and department.

1. Resume screening. The talent acquisition team filters applications for relevant educational background (pharmacy, life sciences, biotechnology, or a related field) and role-specific experience. A CV that mirrors the language of the job description clears this step faster than a generic one.

2. HR screening call. A recruiter reaches out for an introductory conversation to confirm your interest, summarise your background, and cover basics like your notice period and compensation expectations. Keep your answers concise and honest at this stage.

3. Functional or technical interview. This is the core evaluation round. A hiring manager or domain expert will test your knowledge: regulatory submission processes and CDSCO experience for regulatory roles, clinical data interpretation for medical affairs, or field medical protocols for MSL positions. Scenario-based questions are more common here than purely theoretical ones.

4. Panel or cross-functional round. For mid-to-senior positions, a panel interview is commonly included. You may speak with cross-functional leads or regional heads. The focus shifts to how you collaborate across teams and handle ambiguity.

5. Final HR round and offer. A closing conversation covers compensation alignment, joining timelines, and any remaining questions, followed by the formal offer letter.

Total turnaround from application to offer varies widely. Candidates in specialty pharma commonly report a process spanning a few weeks for junior roles and considerably longer for senior or niche positions. Staying responsive after each round and following up politely keeps your application visible.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Medison Pharma hires people who understand the full lifecycle of a specialty medicine, not just one slice of it. Here is what tends to matter across roles.

Domain knowledge that holds up under questioning. For regulatory roles, this means hands-on exposure to CDSCO submissions, dossier preparation, or post-approval change management. For medical affairs, it means genuine comfort with clinical data and KOL engagement. For commercial roles, demonstrated pharma sales or market access experience matters more than general sales ability.

A compliance-first mindset. Specialty pharma operates under tight regulatory and ethical frameworks. Candidates who treat compliance as a working habit rather than a box to tick stand out clearly from those who treat it as a constraint imposed from outside.

Communication that is precise and audience-aware. Whether you are presenting data to a clinician, writing a regulatory response, or coordinating with a global partner team, clarity is non-negotiable. Interviewers assess this across every round.

Adaptability across therapeutic areas. Because Medison works with multiple global partners across disease areas, they value people who can learn new therapy landscapes quickly without losing accuracy or rigour.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the company properly before any round. Understand which therapeutic areas Medison Pharma focuses on and which medicines are part of their portfolio. Interviewers notice immediately when a candidate has done genuine preparation versus a quick Google scan.

Tailor your CV to the specific job description. Use the same terminology the post uses. If the role mentions 'pharmacovigilance case processing,' your CV should reflect that language wherever it applies to your background. Generic CVs get filtered out early.

Prepare scenario-based answers. Specialty pharma interviewers commonly ask about real situations: 'Tell me about a time you handled a regulatory query under a tight deadline' or 'How did you manage a KOL who was sceptical about your data?' Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure your responses and keep them grounded in specifics.

Brush up on GxP basics relevant to your function. Even if your target role is not purely regulatory, working knowledge of CDSCO guidelines, GCP, or pharmacovigilance fundamentals signals pharma maturity and earns credibility with technical interviewers.

Prepare questions to ask. At the close of each round, asking about team structure, the pipeline, or what success looks like in the first year shows genuine interest. It also helps you evaluate whether the role is actually right for you.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Medison Pharma's operating model is built around partnerships with global innovators to bring complex therapies to patients who would otherwise wait years for access. That mission shapes the internal culture in meaningful ways.

Patient focus is taken seriously beyond the slides. In specialty pharma, every team member, whether in regulatory, medical, or commercial, is expected to connect their work back to patient outcomes. This is not a values poster on the wall; it comes up in how teams discuss trade-offs and priorities.

Cross-border collaboration is part of daily work. Because Medison works with global partners, teams routinely interact with colleagues in other countries and time zones. People who thrive here are comfortable operating in that kind of cross-cultural, cross-functional environment without needing heavy hand-holding.

High ownership, broader scope. Specialty pharma firms of this size tend to run leaner than large multinationals. You will likely own a broader scope of work earlier in your tenure than you would at a top-tier MNC. For candidates who welcome that, it is a genuine growth accelerator.

Integrity is a baseline, not a differentiator. In the specialty pharma space, ethical conduct is non-negotiable at every level. Candidates who demonstrate that compliance thinking is woven into how they work, not something they perform during audits, align naturally with the organisation.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Medison Pharma hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Medison Pharma does hire early-career candidates, particularly for roles in regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, and medical information that have structured learning curves. That said, most of their 68 currently open roles target candidates with at least some pharma industry experience. Freshers with a pharmacy or life sciences postgraduate degree have the strongest shot at entry-level openings, and applying early in a hiring cycle improves your odds.

How long does the full hiring process usually take?

Candidates in specialty pharma commonly report that the end-to-end process, from application to offer, spans anywhere from a few weeks to well over a month depending on role seniority and the number of interview rounds required. Senior or niche roles such as regulatory strategy leads or medical affairs heads tend to take longer. Staying responsive and following up professionally after each round helps keep your file active.

What salary can I expect at Medison Pharma?

Publicly reported salary data for Medison Pharma in India is limited given the company's size and specialty focus. Based on Glassdoor and industry surveys, specialty pharma roles at comparable firms vary widely by function and seniority. Research the specific role level on Glassdoor before the HR round and arrive with a realistic, benchmarked expectation rather than a figure you arrived at without reference data.

Is there a written test or online assessment as part of the process?

Written assessments are not commonly reported as a standard step in specialty pharma hiring at companies of this type, though certain roles in medical writing or regulatory affairs may include a short practical task or case study exercise. The main evaluation happens through structured interviews with functional managers. Confirm directly with the recruiter at the screening stage whether an assessment applies to your specific role.

Can I apply for more than one role at Medison Pharma at the same time?

Yes, applying to multiple relevant roles simultaneously is common practice and generally acceptable. The key is to tailor your application for each position rather than submitting a generic CV across all of them. If you are invited to interview, be upfront with the recruiter about the other roles you have applied for so they can coordinate internally and find the best functional fit for your profile.

What are the most common reasons candidates do not clear the interview?

In specialty pharma interviews, the most commonly cited reasons for rejection include weak domain knowledge specific to the role, inability to give concrete real-world examples from past work, and a compliance mindset that appears surface-level rather than genuine. Candidates who seem to be treating the role purely as a short-term stepping stone, without real interest in the therapeutic area or patient-access mission, also tend to struggle to connect with interviewers. Thorough preparation and specific, honest answers address most of these gaps.
