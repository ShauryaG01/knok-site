01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Commvault is a global data protection and cybersecurity company with a significant engineering and product presence in India. The company builds enterprise-grade backup, recovery, and cloud data management solutions used by large organizations across the world.

As of mid-2026, Commvault has 110 open roles spanning software engineering, cloud infrastructure, sales, pre-sales, and technical support. India-based positions are primarily in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, where Commvault runs substantial R&D and support operations.

Most open roles target experienced professionals rather than fresh graduates, especially on the engineering and sales side. If you have a background in cloud platforms, enterprise software, storage, or data security, Commvault is a strong company to target. The products are used by large global enterprises, which means the engineering work is substantive and sales roles carry real responsibility.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Commvault hiring process typically moves through these stages. Timelines vary by role and team, so treat this as a general guide based on publicly available candidate accounts.

Application and resume screening
Your resume goes to a recruiter who checks for relevant domain experience, such as data protection, cloud platforms, or enterprise software. Tailoring your resume to match the job description keywords improves your chances at this stage.

Recruiter call
A short conversation to check your background, current notice period, and compensation expectations. The recruiter will also walk you through the role and give you a sense of the team structure.

Technical screening
For engineering roles, this is usually a coding round or a short system design discussion, sometimes on a shared coding platform. Topics commonly covered include data structures, algorithms, and cloud architecture fundamentals.

Technical interview rounds
Typically two to three rounds with engineers and senior engineers. Expect deeper dives into system design (distributed storage, fault tolerance, replication) and domain knowledge relevant to Commvault's products. For sales and pre-sales roles, this stage involves product scenarios or account management case studies instead.

Hiring manager round
A behavioural and situational conversation. The manager wants to understand how you have handled past challenges, how you take ownership, and how you would fit into the team. Specific examples from your work history matter here far more than general answers.

HR and offer
Final discussion covering compensation, joining date, and documentation. The full process from first call to offer typically spans a few weeks.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Commvault looks for a combination of domain depth and clear communication, especially since the products serve enterprise clients who expect polished interactions.

For software engineers, hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), storage systems, or security tooling is a clear advantage. Strong fundamentals in distributed systems and the ability to talk through design trade-offs matter more than knowing specific frameworks.

For sales and pre-sales roles, enterprise selling experience, the ability to speak technically with IT decision-makers, and a track record of handling long sales cycles are what recruiters prioritize.

Across all roles, Commvault values:

  • Ownership: following through on commitments without needing to be managed closely
  • Customer empathy: understanding the real business problem, not just the technical surface
  • Clarity: communicating complex ideas in plain language, especially with non-technical stakeholders
  • Curiosity: staying current on trends in cloud, data protection, and cybersecurity

Familiarity with backup and recovery concepts, ransomware protection workflows, or compliance frameworks is a bonus for technical applicants.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product portfolio first. Before any Commvault interview, spend time understanding what Commvault Backup and Recovery does, how it differs from cloud-native backup tools, and what problems enterprise IT teams face around data resilience. This knowledge surfaces naturally in technical and behavioural rounds and signals genuine interest.

For engineering candidates, focus your preparation on:

  • System design: distributed storage, replication strategies, fault tolerance, and cloud-native architecture patterns
  • Coding: mid-level problems involving trees, graphs, dynamic programming, and string manipulation
  • Domain knowledge: how backup systems handle large-scale data, deduplication, and recovery point objectives

For behavioural rounds, prepare three to five clear career stories using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Commvault interviewers ask about ownership, handling ambiguity, and cross-functional collaboration. Generic answers do not land well. Specific project examples do.

Use community sources wisely. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry candidate-reported interview experiences for Commvault. These give you a realistic sense of what to expect, but treat individual accounts as directional rather than definitive since processes evolve over time.

Prepare questions to ask your interviewer. Commvault interviewers respond well to candidates who ask thoughtful questions about the team's roadmap, current challenges, and how success is measured in the role. It signals that you are evaluating them as much as they are evaluating you.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Commvault positions its culture around customer obsession, accountability, and innovation in data resilience. In practice, India teams are generally described by candidates as collaborative, with relatively flat hierarchies within engineering units compared to larger IT services companies.

The company has been actively investing in its cloud-first product direction, which means engineers in India are working on live product development rather than purely maintenance work. For professionals who want their contributions to be visible and impactful, this is a meaningful distinction.

Work-life balance feedback on Glassdoor is mixed, as is common across enterprise software companies. Support and customer-facing roles may have demanding schedules depending on client geographies and time zones.

Commvault includes India offices in global engineering discussions, which means your work is not siloed to a local team. This structure appeals to professionals who want cross-geography exposure without relocating.

The company has stated commitments to diversity and runs employee resource groups. India employees are included in company-wide communications and have visibility into broader strategic direction, which tends to matter to professionals who want context beyond their immediate project.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Commvault a good company to work for in India?

Commvault is generally well-regarded among enterprise software employers in India, particularly for engineers interested in cloud and data protection. Glassdoor reviews from India employees frequently highlight collaborative teams and meaningful technical work. The tradeoff, as with most enterprise software companies, is that work-life balance can vary significantly by team and role type. Overall it is considered a solid mid-size employer for professionals who want product work over services.

What technical skills do I need to join Commvault as a software engineer?

Strong fundamentals in distributed systems, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), and data structures are the core requirements. Experience with storage systems, backup technologies, or security tooling gives you a clear edge over other candidates. Commvault does not typically require deep knowledge of its own product stack before joining, since that comes with onboarding. What matters more is that you can reason clearly about large-scale system design.

How long does the Commvault hiring process take?

Based on publicly available candidate accounts, the process from first recruiter contact to offer typically spans a few weeks for most roles. Timelines can stretch longer for senior or specialized positions, or when there are scheduling gaps between rounds. Staying proactive with your recruiter after each stage helps you avoid unnecessary delays. If you have not heard back within a week of a round, a polite follow-up is entirely appropriate.

Does Commvault hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Commvault's open roles are largely targeted at experienced professionals, especially in engineering and sales. Fresher hiring does happen occasionally through campus programs or internship-to-full-time conversions, but it is not the primary hiring channel. If you are a fresher with relevant project work or open-source contributions in cloud or data infrastructure, it is still worth applying to entry-level roles. Targeting an internship first is a practical path in for those who qualify.

What salary can I expect at Commvault India?

Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest that Commvault's compensation for software engineers in India is competitive with other mid-size enterprise software companies, though exact numbers vary by level, city, and total experience. Checking Glassdoor for your specific role and location will give you the most relevant current data to anchor your negotiation. Come prepared to discuss your expectations clearly during the recruiter call, as that sets the tone for the offer stage.

How do I improve my chances of getting a callback from Commvault?

Tailor your resume to highlight cloud, storage, or data protection experience and match the keywords in the job description since the initial screen is often automated. Referrals from current Commvault employees carry real weight, so reaching out to your network before applying is worth the effort. Applying early after a role goes live also helps, as callbacks tend to cluster in the first week or two of a posting. A well-targeted cover note addressing the specific team or product area can differentiate your application in competitive rounds.
