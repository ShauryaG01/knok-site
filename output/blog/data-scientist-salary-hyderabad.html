01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most job listings in India skip the salary entirely. As of 2026-07-08, knok jobradar found 27 Data Scientist openings in Hyderabad across 150+ job sites, and the vast majority came with no pay range attached. That makes benchmarking your worth genuinely difficult before you sit across from a hiring manager.

The figures below are drawn from knok jobradar data. For a Data Scientist in Hyderabad, pay spans from 8 LPA at the entry level all the way to 70+ LPA for lead and principal roles. That is a wide range, and where you land depends on your years of experience, the specific tools you work with, and the type of company, whether that is a product startup, a global capability centre (GCC), or an IT services firm.

Hyderabad has become a serious data science market. Pharma tech, fintech, and large GCCs are all hiring here. The city's cost of living is lower than Bangalore's, which has historically kept salaries a notch below, though industry surveys suggest the gap has been closing in recent years.

Entry level (0-2 years): Fresh graduates and those switching into data science from adjacent roles like analytics or software engineering typically land in the 8-16 LPA range. Roles at this level focus on model building under supervision, data cleaning, and writing SQL.

Mid level (3-5 years): With a couple of production ML systems under your belt, the market pays 18-30 LPA. At this stage, companies expect you to own a project end-to-end, from problem framing through to deployment.

Senior level (6-9 years): The 30-48 LPA band reflects both deep technical skill and the ability to mentor junior data scientists. GCCs and product companies tend to pay toward the top of this range.

Lead and Principal: Roles at 45-70+ LPA come with cross-team scope, hiring responsibility, and often a seat at the strategy table. These positions are less common but do appear in Hyderabad's GCC and tech startup ecosystem.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Know your band before you walk in. Use the ranges in this guide to anchor your expectation. If you are a mid-level Data Scientist, the market band is 18-30 LPA. Do not accept an offer below the floor without a clear non-salary reason such as equity upside, learning opportunity, or employer brand.

Let the employer name a number first. When asked for your salary expectation early in the process, it is fair to say you would like to understand the full compensation structure before quoting a number. This avoids anchoring yourself too low before you know what is on the table.

Negotiate the full package, not just base. Variable pay, joining bonus, and stock options (where applicable) are all negotiable. A company that cannot move on base may still have room on a signing bonus or an accelerated vesting schedule.

Use competing offers as leverage, honestly. If you have another offer, say so and share the number. Most hiring managers will try to match rather than lose a strong candidate at the final stage.

Do not undervalue niche skills. If you know MLOps, time-series forecasting at scale, or NLP applied to Indian languages, these are harder to hire for and justify asking toward the top of your experience band.

Get the offer in writing before you resign. Verbal commitments are not binding. Wait for the formal offer letter before giving notice at your current employer.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Base salary is what most people compare, but total compensation for a Data Scientist in Hyderabad typically includes several other components worth understanding before you weigh two offers against each other.

Base salary is the fixed monthly amount, and all the LPA figures in this guide refer to base unless otherwise noted. It is the most stable and directly comparable number across offers.

Variable pay and performance bonus: Most mid-to-senior roles include a variable component. Industry surveys commonly cite this at 10-20 percent of base for individual contributors at product companies, though the actual payout depends on both company performance and individual targets in any given year.

Stock options or RSUs: GCCs and product startups increasingly offer equity. For publicly listed companies, RSUs vest over a schedule commonly cited as 3-4 years with a one-year cliff. For pre-IPO startups, the value is uncertain but can be meaningful if the company grows.

Joining bonus: Some companies offer a one-time payment to offset what you give up by leaving your current employer early, especially when your notice period is long or you are surrendering unvested equity.

Other benefits: Health insurance for your family, a certification or learning budget, internet reimbursement, and meal allowances vary widely. At mid-to-senior levels, health cover and a learning budget are worth explicitly asking about during negotiation.

When comparing two offers, convert everything to an annualised figure to get a true like-for-like picture. A lower base with a healthy bonus target and RSUs can outperform a higher base with no variable component, once you factor in company performance and vesting timelines.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hyderabad a good market for data scientists in 2026?

Yes. Hyderabad ranks third among Indian cities for active Data Scientist openings, with 27 roles tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026. The city has a strong GCC ecosystem alongside product companies and pharma tech firms, giving data scientists a good variety of employer types. Salaries are competitive with most cities outside Bangalore, and the cost of living makes the effective purchasing power quite reasonable.

What salary can a fresher data scientist expect in Hyderabad?

Entry-level Data Scientists with 0-2 years of experience typically earn in the 8-16 LPA range in Hyderabad, based on knok jobradar data. Where you land within that band depends on company type, your academic background, and the tools you can demonstrate in an interview. Candidates with internship experience on production ML pipelines or a strong project portfolio tend to attract offers toward the upper end of the range.

How does Hyderabad compare to Bangalore for data science pay?

Glassdoor and levels.fyi data commonly cited by professionals suggest Bangalore carries a premium over Hyderabad, particularly at senior and lead levels. The gap can be meaningful in absolute terms, but it narrows when you factor in Hyderabad's lower housing and living costs. If maximising absolute salary is your priority, Bangalore is worth exploring, especially once you have enough experience to target senior roles.

Which skills push a data scientist's salary to the top of the band?

Skills that are scarce or close to production systems tend to command a premium. MLOps, large language model fine-tuning, and deep domain expertise in areas like fintech risk modelling or clinical data analytics are examples that industry surveys commonly cite as top-of-band qualifiers. Strong communication skills and the ability to translate model outputs into business decisions also matter more than many candidates expect, particularly at the senior and lead levels.

Do IT services companies pay less than product companies for data science roles?

Generally yes, according to industry surveys and publicly reported accounts on platforms like Glassdoor. IT services firms offer more structured career paths and often more job stability, but product companies and GCCs tend to offer higher base salaries and equity components. The trade-off is that product roles typically come with more ambiguous scope and greater pressure to show measurable business impact quickly.

How do I know if a data science salary offer is fair?

Cross-check the offer against the experience-band ranges in this guide and look at publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company or sector. Also evaluate the total package, since variable pay, RSUs, and a joining bonus can shift the real value significantly. If the offer sits below the mid-point of your experience band without a clear explanation, it is reasonable to push back before accepting.
