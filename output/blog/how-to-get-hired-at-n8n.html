01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

n8n is an open-source workflow automation platform that lets teams connect apps and automate complex processes without heavy coding. Since its founding, it has grown into one of the more respected names in the automation space, competing with tools like Zapier and Make but positioning itself as the developer-friendly, self-hostable alternative.

As of mid-2026, n8n has 35 open roles spanning engineering, product, design, sales, and customer success. The company is fully remote, meaning Indian candidates can apply for global roles without relocating. Hiring activity picked up through 2025 and 2026 as the product scaled its enterprise offering alongside its free community edition.

For Indian job seekers, n8n is an interesting target: a growing European tech company with a global remote culture, operating in the workflow automation category that is seeing strong demand across IT, finance, and operations teams in 2026.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

n8n's interview process typically runs across four to five stages. The exact steps vary by role, but here is what candidates have commonly reported:

Stage 1: Application and recruiter screen. After you apply, a recruiter typically reaches out within a week or two if your profile is a match. This first call is short and covers your background, interest in a fully remote role, and basic availability.

Stage 2: Hiring manager conversation. You speak with the person who would manage you day to day. Expect questions about your past work, how you approach problems, and why you want to join n8n specifically. For technical roles, this often includes some light exploration of your technical background.

Stage 3: Skills assessment. Engineering candidates typically receive a take-home task or a live coding session focused on practical problem-solving rather than abstract puzzles. Non-engineering candidates (applying to marketing or customer success, for example) generally get a short case study or a written brief to work through.

Stage 4: Team interviews. You meet two or three team members in one or two video sessions. For technical roles these go deeper on domain knowledge. For all roles, there is a clear focus on how you communicate asynchronously and how you handle ambiguity in a distributed team.

Stage 5: Final conversation and offer. Senior roles sometimes include a final call with a founder or senior leader before an offer is extended. Offers are made verbally first, then followed by a written contract.

The full process commonly takes three to five weeks from first contact to written offer, though timelines shift depending on role seniority and the team's current workload. With 35 open roles live right now, n8n is actively hiring and moving pipelines forward.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

n8n looks for people who can work independently in a distributed environment and who are genuinely interested in the problem of workflow automation, not just looking for any remote job.

Async communication skills. The team is spread across time zones. Clear written communication, good documentation habits, and the ability to move work forward without real-time check-ins are treated as core competencies, not nice-to-haves.

Ownership and follow-through. Because the company is still in a growth phase, people are expected to take problems end-to-end. In interviews, concrete examples of projects you drove from kickoff to completion carry more weight than general descriptions of responsibilities.

Technical depth (for engineering roles). n8n's product is built primarily on Node.js and TypeScript. Familiarity with these, combined with an understanding of how REST APIs and webhook-based integrations work, is a practical advantage. Candidates who have contributed to open-source projects also tend to resonate well with the team.

Product curiosity. Across roles, interviewers notice when a candidate has actually used n8n or built something with it. It signals genuine interest in the product and not just the position. Even one working workflow is enough to demonstrate this.

Comfort with direct feedback. Based on publicly reported employee accounts, n8n values specific, honest feedback over vague positivity. Candidates who can give and receive direct feedback comfortably tend to thrive here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A few concrete steps that will put you in a strong position:

Build something with n8n before you apply. Download the community edition and create a working workflow that connects two or three tools you already use. Screenshot or record it, and mention it in your cover letter and interviews. This one step separates candidates who are interested from candidates who are ready.

Study n8n's public content. The company publishes engineering blog posts, product changelog entries, and community forum discussions. Reading these gives you a feel for how the team thinks and what problems they are solving right now. Referencing specific things you found interesting tells interviewers your curiosity is genuine.

Prepare written examples before your interviews. Because async communication is central to how n8n operates, expect questions like 'walk me through a project you led' or 'describe a time you had to change direction mid-project.' Write out two or three clear examples before your first call so you can deliver them concisely over video.

Brush up on relevant technical skills. For engineering roles: Node.js, TypeScript, REST API design, and webhook fundamentals. For solutions engineering or customer success: familiarity with how enterprise software is evaluated and deployed, and comfort talking about workflow automation value with non-technical buyers.

Know the competitive landscape. Be able to explain what makes n8n different from Zapier, Make, and Workato. Understand the self-hosting angle and why developer teams care about it. Interviewers appreciate candidates who understand the market, not just the job description.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

n8n describes its culture around transparency, autonomy, and a strong connection to the open-source community. A few things worth knowing before you apply:

Remote-first is the actual operating model. Decisions are made asynchronously, projects are coordinated through written documentation, and new joiners who expect a lot of real-time hand-holding often find the first few weeks an adjustment. If you have not worked fully remote before, think seriously about how you structure your own workday before joining.

Open source is a core identity. n8n started as a community-first project and that ethos still shapes how the team builds and talks about the product. People who care about open-source software as a philosophy (not just as a distribution strategy) tend to fit in well.

Small team, large scope. Despite growing through 2024 and 2025, n8n remains relatively lean. Individual contributors often own entire features or business areas. This is energizing if you like broad ownership but can feel overwhelming if you prefer well-defined, narrow lanes.

Feedback is direct. Based on publicly reported accounts from current and former employees, n8n values specific, honest feedback over softened or hedged commentary. This applies both to how managers give feedback and how peers communicate with each other.

Overall, n8n tends to attract people who are self-motivated, comfortable with uncertainty, and genuinely excited about building tools that other builders use.

Does n8n hire from India for remote roles?

What is the compensation like at n8n?

n8n does not publish salary bands publicly for most roles. Compensation for remote tech roles at growth-stage European companies is commonly cited on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, and checking those platforms for similar titles will give you a reasonable reference point. How long does the full hiring process take?

Most candidates report the process taking three to five weeks from first recruiter contact to a written offer. Senior or specialized roles can take longer because fewer interviewers have the relevant context to evaluate a candidate properly. If you have not heard back within two weeks of completing an interview stage, a brief follow-up email to your recruiter is appropriate and expected.

Do I need prior n8n experience to apply?

You do not strictly need prior experience with n8n to apply, but candidates who have actually used the product tend to perform better in interviews and move through the process more smoothly. What does the take-home task look like for engineering roles?

Engineering candidates commonly report receiving a practical task involving TypeScript, API integrations, or a problem that resembles real work the team does day to day. n8n generally avoids abstract algorithm puzzles in favour of tasks that test how you think about building and integrating software.
