01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Engineering Manager job postings in India skip the salary details entirely. You are often two interview rounds deep before the first number even comes up. The market data that does exist, however, paints a clear enough picture for Pune.

Based on knok's jobradar data (as of July 2026), Engineering Manager roles in Pune sit across three broad bands depending on seniority. A Manager-level role typically lands in the 35-60 LPA range. Senior Managers command 55-90 LPA. Those stepping into Director territory can expect 90 LPA and above, with packages going beyond 150 LPA at well-funded companies.

Pune's market is more compact than Bangalore's, but that does not mean salaries are dramatically lower. Product companies, captive GCCs (Global Capability Centres), and fast-growing startups in Pune all pay competitively for EMs with strong delivery records and the ability to grow a team.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Your title and years of relevant leadership experience are the two biggest levers on your offer. Here is how the bands break down.

Manager (35-60 LPA): This is the entry point for most first-time EMs, typically covering candidates who manage one or two squads and report into a Senior EM or Director. If you are moving from a Staff Engineer or Senior Software Engineer role, expect offers in the lower half of this range until you build a track record as a people manager.

Senior Manager (55-90 LPA): At this level you are usually owning a full product area, managing other managers, and driving hiring and roadmap decisions. Companies pay a meaningful premium here because the role blend, technical credibility plus org influence, is genuinely hard to fill. The overlap with the Manager band (around 55-60 LPA) reflects real variation based on company size and funding stage.

Director (90-150+ LPA): Director-level Engineering roles in Pune are fewer in number but well-compensated. These packages typically carry a larger variable component and ESOPs, which can push total value well above the base figure.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Negotiating as an Engineering Manager is different from negotiating as an individual contributor. You are not just selling technical skills; you are selling your ability to hire, retain, and grow a team. Lead with that in every conversation.

Know your total comp, not just base. Many Pune companies use a fixed-plus-variable structure where the performance bonus can be a significant part of the package. Ask explicitly: what is the target variable, and what percentage of employees actually hit it last year?

Come in with market context. Saying 'I have seen Senior Manager roles in Pune in the 55-90 LPA range' is more effective than just stating a number you want. It shows you are grounded in the market, not anchoring high without evidence.

Ask about the team you will manage. An EM role with a small, half-formed team is very different from one with a stable squad and a clear roadmap. Understanding scope helps you negotiate not just salary but also title, headcount, and the resources you need to succeed.

Do not accept the first offer without a counter. Industry surveys consistently show that a respectful, evidence-based counter is almost never penalised. The worst realistic outcome is they hold firm.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Base salary is the number most people fixate on, but for Engineering Managers it is often not the majority of total value. Here is what a typical Pune EM package includes.

Base salary: The fixed monthly component, annualised to the LPA figure. This is what the salary bands above refer to.

Performance bonus: Commonly cited as a percentage of base, paid annually or semi-annually. At larger companies this is formulaic. At startups it tends to be discretionary. Always ask for actual payout history, not just the 'target' percentage.

ESOPs or RSUs: More common at product-first companies and startups. Vesting periods are commonly cited as four years with a one-year cliff, though terms vary by company. For Director-level roles especially, this can be a major part of the package.

Joining bonus: Used to offset unvested equity at your current employer. Negotiate this if you are leaving a significant unvested tranche behind.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Pune a good city for Engineering Manager roles in 2026?

Pune has a healthy EM job market, though it is smaller than Bangalore or Delhi NCR. As of July 2026, knok's jobradar found 20 open EM roles in Pune. The city has a strong mix of GCCs, product companies, and mid-stage startups, which means genuine leadership roles rather than pure delivery management positions.

What is the salary range for an Engineering Manager in Pune?

Based on knok's jobradar data (as of July 2026), Manager-level roles in Pune sit in the 35-60 LPA range, Senior Managers command 55-90 LPA, and Director-level packages start at 90 LPA and can go beyond 150 LPA. These figures refer to base salary. Total comp including bonus and ESOPs can be meaningfully higher, especially at product companies.

How does Pune compare to Bangalore for EM salaries?

Bangalore had 182 open EM roles versus Pune's 20 as of July 2026, reflecting a much larger market. Publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggests Bangalore packages run higher on average. That said, Pune's cost of living is lower, and for candidates who want to avoid Bangalore's commute and rental costs, the net take-home difference can be smaller than the gross figures suggest.

What skills help an Engineering Manager reach the upper end of the salary range?

Companies pay a premium for EMs who can hire strong engineers, retain them through growth and recognition, and translate business goals into technical roadmaps. If you have a track record of scaling a team from scratch, reducing attrition, or shipping a product that hit revenue targets, those are the stories that justify offers toward the top of the 35-60 or 55-90 LPA bands.

Do product companies pay more than service companies for EM roles in Pune?

Generally yes, based on industry surveys and publicly reported data. Service companies tend to have more structured pay bands that cap out lower. Product companies and GCCs with global charters typically pay higher base salaries and add ESOPs or RSUs that service firms rarely offer. The gap becomes more pronounced at Senior Manager and Director levels.

How do ESOPs work for Engineering Manager roles in Pune?

ESOPs (Employee Stock Option Plans) are offered by most funded startups and some mid-size product companies. You receive the right to buy shares at a fixed price after a vesting period. Vesting periods are commonly cited as four years with a one-year cliff, though exact terms vary by company. The actual value depends on whether the company gets acquired or goes public, so always ask for the current valuation and latest funding round details before comparing offers.
