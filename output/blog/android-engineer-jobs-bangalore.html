05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Show production work, not just code. Recruiters for Android roles respond best to candidates who can point to live apps on the Play Store. If you have shipped features that improved app retention or reduced crash rates, mention those outcomes in your resume. Concrete results matter more than a list of technologies.

Lead with Kotlin. Almost every active Bangalore listing in this snapshot expects Kotlin as the primary language. If your resume still leads with Java, reorder it. Mentioning Coroutines, Flow, and Jetpack Compose signals that you are working with the current Android stack, which hiring managers notice immediately.

Highlight architecture knowledge. Clean Architecture, MVVM, and modularisation come up repeatedly in Android Engineer requirements. A short summary paragraph that names the patterns you have worked with tells a recruiter you can slot into a professional codebase without a lengthy ramp-up period.

Tailor your pitch to the company stage. Early-stage startups in this list (Vecros, Pixuate, Aspora) are likely looking for generalists who can own the entire Android layer. Larger product companies like Zynga and Housing want deeper specialisation in specific areas. Adjusting your resume emphasis to match the company stage is a quick effort that noticeably improves your shortlisting rate.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply early in the listing cycle. With only 16 open roles in Bangalore at this snapshot, each listing is competitive. Roles at smaller companies often close within days of posting, so a same-day or next-day application meaningfully improves your chances of reaching the first screening round.

Customise your cover note. A one-paragraph note that references the company's specific product (for example, Chalo's bus-tracking app or HealthifyMe's fitness platform) immediately separates your application from generic submissions. Recruiters at startups notice when a candidate has clearly looked at what the company actually does.

Follow up after applying. If you have not heard back within a week, a short LinkedIn message to the hiring manager or recruiter is appropriate and often effective at startups. Keep it brief: your name, the role you applied for, and one sentence on why you are interested.

Prepare for a practical screen. Most Android Engineer interviews in this segment include a take-home or live coding component. Common formats include building a small feature using Retrofit and ViewModel, or debugging a provided codebase. Practising these in advance is time well spent.
