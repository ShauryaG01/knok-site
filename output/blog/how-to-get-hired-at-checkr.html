01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Checkr is a technology company that builds background check and screening software, primarily serving US-based businesses that need to verify candidates before hiring. As of July 2026, Checkr has 62 open roles listed, spanning engineering, product, data, sales, and operations. The company is headquartered in San Francisco and most of its open positions are US-based or remote roles open to US residents. For Indian professionals, the most realistic path is applying to remote-eligible roles or relocating, though Checkr has occasionally listed contract or vendor positions that can be done from India. If you are in India, filter carefully by 'Remote' and confirm the geographic eligibility before applying.

Checkr sits at the intersection of HR technology and compliance, which means its engineering and product teams work on problems like API reliability, data privacy, ML-driven screening decisions, and large-scale background record processing. If you have experience in fintech, compliance tech, or high-throughput data pipelines, your background maps well to what Checkr builds.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Checkr's interview process typically runs across several stages, though the exact structure can vary by team and role level.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. A recruiter or hiring manager reviews your resume. For engineering roles, expect a focus on your experience with distributed systems, APIs, or data pipelines. Tailor your resume to the specific role rather than sending a generic one.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. If shortlisted, a recruiter reaches out for a brief screening call covering your background, interest in Checkr, and logistics like timeline and location. This is also your chance to ask whether the role is open to your location.

Stage 3: Technical screen. For engineering roles, this is usually a live coding round on a shared coding platform. Expect data structures, algorithms, and sometimes a short system design question. Product and data roles may get a take-home case study instead.

Stage 4: Interview loop. This is the main evaluation stage, typically involving several back-to-back interviews. Engineering candidates face a mix of coding, system design, and behavioral interviews. Product managers go through product sense, execution, and cross-functional collaboration rounds. Behavioral rounds at every level use structured questions aligned to Checkr's values around fairness and accountability.

Stage 5: Offer and background check. Checkr runs its own background check on every hire, which is standard given what the company does. Offers typically come after the final loop, though exact timelines vary by team.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Checkr looks for people who can build reliable, scalable systems and who care about the social impact of their work. Background screening affects real people's ability to get jobs, so Checkr emphasizes fairness and accuracy as core engineering values, not just business metrics.

Technical depth. For engineers, strong fundamentals in distributed systems, APIs, and database design matter more than familiarity with any specific language. Checkr's stack is primarily Ruby on Rails and Go on the backend, with React on the frontend, but they hire for problem-solving ability over language expertise.

Domain awareness. You do not need prior experience in background screening, but showing that you understand the compliance and privacy dimensions of the work signals cultural fit. Read about fair chance hiring and FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act) basics before your interviews.

Collaboration and communication. Checkr's teams are cross-functional and distributed. The ability to explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders and work across time zones comes up in behavioral rounds.

Ownership mindset. Interviewers want to see examples where you drove a project end to end, navigated ambiguity, and took responsibility for outcomes.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the company before anything else. Understand what Checkr does, who its customers are, and why background screening is a socially sensitive area. Interviewers notice when candidates have done this research, and it separates you from those who just applied to 'a tech company'.

For engineering roles, prepare data structures and algorithms at a medium-to-hard level. System design preparation should cover topics like designing high-throughput data ingestion systems, webhook delivery, and audit logging, since these map to real problems Checkr solves. Glassdoor and levels.fyi have commonly cited interview questions from candidates who have gone through the process.

For product roles, practice product sense frameworks: define the user, identify the pain point, prioritize solutions, and measure success. Checkr's core users are HR teams and compliance officers, so frame your answers around their problems.

Prepare your behavioral stories. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for questions about past experience. Have a few strong examples ready covering: a time you handled ambiguity, a time you disagreed with a decision and how you resolved it, and a time you improved a process or system.

Apply through the right channel. Checkr's careers page lists all 62 current openings. If you are applying from India or as an international candidate, confirm eligibility upfront with the recruiter rather than going through multiple rounds only to find the role is not open to your location. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Checkr's culture is shaped heavily by its mission: to build a fairer future for workers by making background screening faster, more accurate, and less biased. This is not just marketing language. The company has publicly invested in fair chance hiring initiatives, meaning they actively advocate for giving people with criminal records a second chance at employment.

Fairness as a product value. Engineers at Checkr are often asked to think about the downstream impact of their decisions on real candidates. A bug in a screening algorithm is not just a technical error, it can affect someone's livelihood. This gives the work a different weight than at a typical SaaS company.

Transparency and accountability. Checkr values direct communication and expects employees to take clear ownership of their work. In interviews, vague answers about team contributions or deflecting responsibility tend to score poorly.

Distributed and async-friendly. The company has remote and hybrid setups across time zones. Written communication is important, and employees are expected to document decisions clearly.

Diversity commitment. Checkr has publicly reported commitments to building diverse teams, and this shows up in structured interviews designed to reduce bias, consistent rubrics, and diverse hiring panels.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Can I apply to Checkr from India?

Most of Checkr's 62 open roles are based in the US or are remote roles restricted to US residents. A small number of contract or vendor positions may be open to India-based candidates. The safest approach is to filter by 'Remote' on the careers page and confirm geographic eligibility with the recruiter in the very first call, before investing time in technical rounds.

What is the salary range at Checkr?

Checkr does not publicly list salary bands for most roles. Based on commonly cited figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, software engineering compensation at mid-level US tech companies in Checkr's space tends to be competitive with the broader SF Bay Area market. If you are relocating from India, research the specific level and location to get a realistic picture of total compensation including equity.

How long does the Checkr hiring process take?

The full process from application to offer typically spans a few weeks, though this can stretch longer for senior or specialized roles. The recruiter call and technical screen often happen quickly if you are shortlisted. The final interview loop is usually scheduled as a single day or across consecutive days. Ask your recruiter for a timeline estimate after the screening call.

Does Checkr hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Checkr primarily hires experienced professionals, especially for engineering and product roles. Entry-level roles do appear occasionally, usually under titles like 'Associate Software Engineer' or 'University Hire', but they are less common. If you are a recent graduate, look for internship listings or entry-level openings specifically, rather than applying to mid or senior roles.

What technology stack does Checkr use?

Checkr's backend is primarily built with Ruby on Rails and Go, with a React frontend. They also work heavily with cloud infrastructure, large-scale data pipelines, and third-party API integrations. You do not need to know every tool in their stack, but familiarity with distributed systems, REST APIs, and relational databases will help you in technical rounds.

How does Checkr's own background check work for new hires?

Checkr runs a background check on every person they hire, which makes sense given that is their core product. The check typically covers criminal history, employment verification, and education verification. If you have any concerns about your background, it is better to be upfront with your recruiter than to face a surprise at the offer stage. Checkr's own fair chance hiring values mean they evaluate background results in context, not as automatic disqualifiers.
