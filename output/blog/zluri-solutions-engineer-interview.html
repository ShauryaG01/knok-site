01 Overview

Overview

zluri is a Bangalore-based SaaS Management Platform that helps IT and finance teams discover every app in use, control SaaS spend, and automate employee onboarding and offboarding. Enterprise customers rely on it to replace scattered spreadsheets with a single real-time view of their entire software stack.

The Solutions Engineer role sits at the crossroads of sales, product, and IT consulting. Day to day, you run live product demos, build proof-of-concepts for enterprise prospects, handle technical objections from IT heads and security teams, and work hand-in-hand with account executives to move deals forward. Comfort with identity providers like Okta and Azure AD, REST APIs, SCIM protocols, and SaaS licensing models is expected from day one.

As of July 2026, zluri has 10 open roles. Across India, 1,270 Solutions Engineer openings are currently active, with Bangalore leading at 55 postings, followed by Mumbai (23) and Delhi (20). Pre-sales technical profiles like this one are in strong demand across the B2B SaaS segment.

02 Most Asked Questions

Most Asked Questions

Candidates report a mix of scenario-based, product-knowledge, and behavioural questions across rounds. Interviewers typically want to see how you balance technical depth with the ability to make a CFO or IT head feel confident. These are the questions that come up most often:

  1. Walk me through how you would demo zluri's SaaS discovery feature to a CFO who is concerned about shadow IT.
  2. A prospect's IT admin says they already track apps in a spreadsheet. How do you make the case for a dedicated SaaS management platform?
  3. How does zluri technically discover apps across a company's environment? What data sources does it rely on?
  4. Explain the difference between zluri and a traditional IT Asset Management (ITAM) tool.
  5. A POC is in progress and the customer's Okta integration keeps failing. How do you handle it?
  6. You are mid-demo and the prospect asks about a feature zluri does not yet have. What do you do?
  7. A large enterprise wants a custom integration with their in-house HRMS. How do you scope and respond to this request?
  8. The security team has paused the deal because they want to know how zluri stores OAuth tokens and what data leaves the customer environment. How do you respond?
  9. How would you build a business case for a CIO to justify zluri's cost? What metrics would you highlight?
  10. A competitor's product scored higher on one feature during a bake-off evaluation. How do you keep the deal alive?
  11. Tell me about a time you turned a skeptical technical buyer into a strong internal champion.
  12. How do you prioritise which integrations to highlight during an early discovery call when time is limited?

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Q: Tell me about a time you turned a skeptical technical buyer into a strong internal champion.

*Situation:* At my previous company, I was presenting our SaaS management platform to the IT head of a mid-sized fintech. He had already trialled a competitor and was burned by integrations that broke silently without any alerts.

*Task:* My goal was to rebuild trust quickly and prove that our integration layer was genuinely solid, not just a checkbox on a marketing slide.

*Action:* Before our next meeting, I asked for a list of their top ten SaaS apps. In the session itself, I connected each app live rather than using a pre-recorded demo. I also shared anonymised uptime data and proposed a time-boxed POC with clear, written exit criteria so the customer felt in control of the process.

*Result:* The IT head agreed to the POC, personally validated the Okta and Slack integrations, and became an internal champion. The deal closed within two months.

---

Q: A POC is underway and the customer's Okta integration keeps failing. How do you handle it?

*Situation:* During a POC at a logistics company, their Okta instance sat behind a strict IP allowlist and our discovery agent kept timing out with no clear error message.

*Task:* I needed to unblock the POC quickly or risk losing the momentum built with our champion.

*Action:* I set up a focused troubleshooting call with the customer's IT admin and our product engineer. I documented every error log, identified the IP allowlist gap, and walked the customer through whitelisting our CIDR range in that same session. I then ran a live test sync on the call to confirm the fix worked.

*Result:* The integration was live the same day. The customer noted our responsiveness in their evaluation scorecard, and it directly influenced their final selection decision.

---

Q: A prospect asks about a feature zluri does not yet have. What do you do?

*Situation:* In a competitive demo, a prospect's IT director asked whether zluri could generate automated audit reports in a custom format required by their internal compliance team.

*Task:* The feature did not exist. I needed to be honest without derailing the deal.

*Action:* I acknowledged the gap directly rather than deflecting. I then showed the closest existing functionality, a configurable export, and walked through how their team could adapt it to their format with minimal effort. I committed to checking the product roadmap and following up in writing within one business day.

*Result:* The prospect appreciated the honesty. My follow-up confirmed the feature was planned for the coming quarter. The deal moved forward and the customer signed an annual contract.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

STAR for behavioural questions: Keep the Situation brief (one or two sentences), spend most of your time on the Action (what you personally did), and always close with a concrete, specific Result. Vague endings like 'it went well' are a common mistake that makes answers hard to evaluate.

Demo structure for scenario questions: Open with a discovery question ('What is the biggest SaaS pain point for your team right now?'), then tailor the demo flow to that specific pain point. Handle objections with a clear pattern: 'I hear that concern, here is how zluri addresses it.' Close every scenario with explicit next steps.

Technical objection handling: Acknowledge the concern, restate it to confirm you understood correctly, answer directly with evidence (logs, architecture diagrams, documentation), and offer a concrete next step such as a written security response or a dedicated review call with the engineering team.

Business case framing: Lead with the customer's own data rather than generic ROI claims. Tie the value to metrics IT and finance leaders actually care about: unused licence spend recovered, hours saved per offboarding event, and audit readiness. Numbers the customer gave you during discovery are far more persuasive than industry benchmarks.

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

zluri interviewers are typically looking for four qualities:

Product fluency without a script. Can you explain SaaS discovery, licence optimisation, and automated workflows in plain language to both a developer and a CFO? Candidates who only know the marketing pitch rarely clear the technical scenario round.

Enterprise sales instinct. Solutions Engineers at zluri deal with multi-stakeholder enterprise deals. Interviewers want to see that you understand procurement cycles, security reviews, and the difference between managing a technical champion and an economic buyer.

Composure under pressure. Expect scenarios where the demo breaks, the prospect is hostile, or a competitor is clearly ahead on a specific feature. Interviewers are watching for calm, creative problem-solving, not panic or over-promising.

Customer empathy before product evangelism. The best Solutions Engineers at zluri think like the customer first. The typical buyer is an IT or finance leader overwhelmed by SaaS complexity. Interviewers want to see that you start from the customer's pain, not from a feature checklist.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Week 1: Build product depth
Request a sandbox or explore zluri's publicly available resources. Map out the core modules: app discovery, licence management, onboarding and offboarding automation, and the integrations marketplace. Practise explaining each module in two sentences to someone outside IT.

Weeks 1 and 2: Study the competitive landscape
Research how zluri compares to other SaaS management tools in the market. Know zluri's key differentiators: the depth of its integration library, workflow automation capabilities, and the finance-friendly spend analytics layer.

Week 2: Practise live demos
Record yourself doing a short demo of zluri's discovery feature. Watch it back, trim filler words, and practise handling two common objections out loud: 'We already use Okta reports for this' and 'This feels expensive for what it does.'

Weeks 2 and 3: Sharpen STAR stories
Prepare at least three STAR stories covering: a complex technical integration you personally owned, a time you saved a deal that was close to falling apart, and a time you had to set honest expectations with a customer about a product gap.

Before the interview
Review zluri's recent product announcements and company news. Prepare one or two thoughtful questions about how the team measures Solutions Engineer success and what a typical POC process looks like.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Giving a generic pre-sales pitch. Saying 'I am good at demos and technical conversations' without connecting it to SaaS management, IT workflows, or zluri's specific buyer is a fast way to get screened out in early rounds.

Over-promising on missing features. A common trap in demo scenarios is saying 'yes, we can do that' to avoid awkwardness. Interviewers at zluri specifically probe for honesty here. Acknowledge the gap, show what exists, and follow up with a roadmap timeline.

Skipping discovery. Jumping straight into a product walkthrough without asking what pain the customer is trying to solve is a clear red flag. Every good Solutions Engineer demo starts with a discovery question.

Being thin on technical specifics. Solutions Engineers at zluri are expected to handle security and integration questions directly, not pass them to a product team. Study how zluri handles OAuth scopes, data residency, and API authentication before your interview.

No concrete results in STAR answers. Ending a story with 'the customer was happy' instead of a specific outcome (deal closed, POC passed, renewal signed) makes your answers easy to forget and hard to evaluate.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many interview rounds does zluri typically have for a Solutions Engineer role?

Candidates report around three to four rounds. This typically includes an initial screening call with HR, a technical or scenario-based round where you demo the product or handle a live objection, and one or two rounds with sales leadership or the hiring manager. Some candidates also report a take-home exercise before the final round, so be prepared for that possibility.

What salary can I expect for a Solutions Engineer at zluri?

Zluri has not publicly disclosed salary bands for this role. Compensation for Solutions Engineers at Bangalore-based B2B SaaS companies is commonly cited on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, and varies based on total experience, current package, and the specific focus of the role. Benchmark using those platforms and negotiate based on your current CTC and target LPA.

Do I need a deep engineering background to clear the zluri SE interview?

Not necessarily. Candidates report that zluri values the ability to explain technical concepts clearly over the ability to write code. You should be comfortable with APIs, SSO protocols like SAML and OAuth, and integration concepts, but the interview is not a coding round. Prior experience in technical pre-sales, solutions consulting, or IT consulting tends to count more than a computer science degree alone.

Is prior experience with SaaS management platforms required?

You do not need prior experience with zluri specifically, but you should understand the problem it solves before your first call. Spend time getting familiar with concepts like SaaS sprawl, shadow IT, and licence optimisation from an enterprise IT perspective. If you have used any SaaS management or ITAM tool in a previous role, highlight that clearly in your resume and during the interview.

What does the day-to-day of a zluri Solutions Engineer actually look like?

Publicly available employee reviews suggest the role is heavily customer-facing, with a mix of discovery calls, live product demos, POC management, and RFP responses. You will work closely with account executives and product managers throughout the sales cycle. Depending on deal size and stage, some travel to customer sites in metro cities is possible.

How competitive is the zluri Solutions Engineer role compared to the broader market?

As of July 2026, zluri has 10 open roles across all positions. The broader India market currently has 1,270 Solutions Engineer openings tracked across job sites, so demand for this profile is healthy. Within zluri, the Solutions Engineer team is relatively lean, which means each hire is evaluated carefully for both technical fit and cultural alignment with the sales team.
