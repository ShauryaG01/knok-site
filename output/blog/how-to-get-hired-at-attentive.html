01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Attentive is a conversational commerce platform best known for its SMS and email marketing tools, used by thousands of e-commerce and retail brands globally. The company is headquartered in the US and hires across engineering, data, product, sales, and customer success functions. As of mid-2026, Attentive has 52 open roles posted, signalling active, broad-based hiring rather than a narrow seasonal push.

For Indian professionals, Attentive roles are a mix of remote-eligible positions and openings based at their international offices. Engineering and data roles tend to have the highest volume of postings. If you are targeting a product or go-to-market role, expect a smaller pool but an equally structured process.

The company grew rapidly on the back of the SMS marketing boom and still carries a high-growth, execution-first culture. Hiring managers look for people who can ramp quickly, take ownership without being asked, and care about measurable outcomes for customers.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Attentive's process typically runs across four to five stages, though the exact order can shift by team and role. The stages below are based on publicly reported candidate experiences and may differ for your specific application.

Application and resume screen. A recruiter reviews your profile for role fit. Tailoring your resume to the job description, especially highlighting any marketing technology, SaaS, or growth-related experience, improves your chances of moving forward.

Recruiter call. Expect questions about your background, why you are interested in Attentive, and your compensation expectations. This is also your chance to ask about team structure and day-to-day expectations. Be ready to walk through your career story clearly and concisely.

Hiring manager interview. This round goes deeper into your relevant experience and how you think. For engineering roles, there may be a light technical discussion here. For sales or customer success roles, expect questions about how you have handled difficult client situations or met targets in past positions.

Technical assessment or skills interview. For engineering candidates, this is usually a live coding round or take-home assignment covering data structures, algorithms, or system design depending on seniority. For non-engineering roles, this stage might be a mock demo, a case study, or a writing exercise.

Panel or final interviews. A set of back-to-back conversations with cross-functional teammates, covering both technical depth and behavioural questions. Interviewers typically include a peer, a skip-level manager, or someone from an adjacent team. After the panel, successful candidates usually receive feedback within a week, followed by a formal offer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Attentive consistently hires for a handful of qualities across all functions.

Ownership mindset. The company grew fast and rewards people who spot a problem and fix it without waiting to be told. In interviews, expect to be asked about times you took initiative on something that was not strictly your job.

Data fluency. Whether you are an engineer, a product manager, or in customer success, Attentive expects you to back decisions with data. Being comfortable reading dashboards, running basic queries, or at least asking the right questions about metrics will help you stand out.

Customer obsession. Attentive's product sits directly in the path of consumer purchases. Interviewers want to see that you genuinely care about the end customer experience, not just the internal product roadmap.

Clear written communication. Because many teams work across time zones, async written communication is heavily valued. In take-homes or case studies, how you structure your thinking matters as much as the conclusion you reach.

Domain knowledge for specialist roles. For engineering, strong fundamentals in the language stack you are applying for matter a lot. For go-to-market roles, prior SaaS or marketing technology experience is a clear differentiator.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before any call. Explore Attentive's platform through their website, product demos, or customer case studies so you can speak naturally about what the company does and why it matters. Interviewers notice immediately when a candidate has actually engaged with the product.

Map your experience to outcomes. Attentive wants to see impact, not just activity. For each major project on your resume, prepare a short summary of the result: what changed, what grew, what got faster, or what got cheaper.

Practice structured answers. Panel rounds often use behavioural formats. Preparing a handful of strong stories using the Situation-Task-Action-Result structure will cover most questions you encounter.

For engineering roles, revisit system design. Depending on seniority, system design questions often appear. Think through how you would design a high-throughput notification delivery system or a real-time analytics pipeline, since these map directly to what Attentive builds.

Prepare thoughtful questions for every stage. Good ones include asking about the team's biggest current challenge, how success is measured in the role during the first few months, and what the onboarding experience looks like.

Track your applications carefully. If you are applying to multiple roles at Attentive or across companies at the same time, a simple spreadsheet keeps your context fresh before each call. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Attentive operates with a high-performance culture common in US-origin SaaS companies. The pace is fast, feedback is direct, and the expectation is that you will figure things out with limited hand-holding. The company has invested in structured onboarding and cross-team collaboration tools as it has scaled.

Growth over perfection. Attentive values people who ship, learn, and iterate rather than those who wait for the perfect solution. If you come from a background where speed was rewarded over heavy process, you will feel at home here.

Transparency with context. Internally, Attentive tends to share business context broadly with employees, including goals and product strategy. Individual contributors are expected to connect their day-to-day work to company-level outcomes.

Inclusion and belonging. The company has publicly reported commitments to building diverse teams, with employee resource groups and structured hiring practices aimed at reducing bias. Candidates from underrepresented backgrounds are actively encouraged to apply.

Remote-first mindset. Much of Attentive's team is distributed, and async communication through written updates and recorded meetings is a normal part of how work gets done. Strong writers and self-directed workers tend to thrive here.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the full hiring process at Attentive take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process from application to offer typically takes two to four weeks. Technical roles with multiple interview rounds can sometimes run a little longer. Following up politely with your recruiter after each stage is normal and expected, not pushy.

Does Attentive hire engineers based in India?

Attentive has historically hired engineering talent in India, primarily for remote roles. Availability of India-based positions depends on the specific team and headcount plan at any given time. As of mid-2026, Attentive has 52 open roles, so it is worth reviewing the full list on their careers page and filtering by location to see what fits your profile.

What salary can I expect at Attentive?

Attentive does not publish India-specific salary bands publicly. For US-based roles, both levels.fyi and Glassdoor carry community-submitted compensation data that can give you a ballpark by level and function. For India-based or remote roles, industry surveys for SaaS companies of similar scale are a useful reference. Always confirm compensation details directly with the recruiter during the first call.

Is there a coding test for every engineering role at Attentive?

Most engineering roles include at least one technical assessment, either a take-home assignment or a live coding session. The format and difficulty vary by seniority. Senior and staff-level roles typically include a system design component in addition to coding. Asking your recruiter upfront about the exact format is completely appropriate and shows preparation.

What is the best way to get noticed after submitting my application?

Tailoring your resume to mirror the language in the job description helps get past the initial screen. Beyond the application, reaching out to the hiring manager or a recruiter on LinkedIn with a brief, specific note about why the role matches your experience can improve visibility. Referrals from current Attentive employees carry significant weight, so check if anyone in your network works there before you apply.

Does Attentive sponsor visas for candidates outside the US?

Visa sponsorship policies vary by role and change over time, so it is important to ask the recruiter directly during the first call whether sponsorship is available for your specific position. Many current openings at Attentive are remote-eligible, which can reduce the need for relocation in some cases. Do not assume either way without confirming.
