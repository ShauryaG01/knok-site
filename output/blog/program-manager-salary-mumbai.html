01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

The honest starting point: the knok jobradar scan (as of 2026-07-08) found just 1 Technical Program Manager opening in Mumbai, and that listing, like the vast majority of Indian job posts, does not disclose salary. Pay opacity is the norm across Indian hiring. Companies treat compensation as a negotiation variable and keep listings silent, which means any precise figure you see online without a clear source deserves healthy scepticism.

What we can draw on is publicly reported data and Glassdoor figures, which consistently place Technical Program Manager among the higher-paying individual contributor tracks in Indian tech. Mumbai's job market leans heavily toward BFSI (banking, financial services, and insurance), fintech, and global capability centres. TPMs embedded in these sectors are commonly cited as earning packages that reflect the sector's premium rather than a simple city-average multiplier.

The thin supply of Mumbai listings, just 1 in the current scan, is itself a signal. Scarcity works in your favour once you reach the offer stage: a company that has completed a full interview process with you has invested significant effort. That is real negotiating leverage. But it also means you cannot afford to wait passively for roles to appear.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Because the current scan returned no disclosed salary data from the Mumbai listing, the figures below are drawn from Glassdoor and publicly reported industry surveys. Use them to set a floor for your negotiation, not as guaranteed benchmarks, and cross-check against peer networks and recent offer letters.

Associate or Junior TPM (0-4 years): Glassdoor figures commonly cited for early-career TPMs at mid-size product companies in Indian metros show packages in the 14-22 LPA range. Entry into a formal TPM title in Mumbai at this stage is competitive and usually requires prior experience in software engineering, technical delivery, or project coordination. BFSI firms often additionally want domain knowledge in banking or payments alongside programme management skills.

TPM or Senior TPM (4-8 years): This is the core hiring band for the role. Publicly reported data and industry surveys suggest experienced TPMs at established product companies or global capability centres in India commonly earn in the 28-55 LPA range, with the upper end tied to managing large cross-functional programmes or multiple engineering teams.

Staff TPM, Principal TPM, or TPM Manager (8+ years): At senior levels, total compensation diverges sharply by company tier. Levels.fyi data for senior individual contributor and people-manager TPM roles at large tech firms shows packages where equity and performance bonuses can outweigh base salary, making CTC-only comparisons unreliable. Negotiate on total comp at this level.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Anchor on market data before any conversation. Look up Glassdoor figures and publicly reported ranges for your experience band before you receive any offer. Walk in knowing the number, not hoping the company will be generous.

Ask for the full comp breakup early. Mumbai BFSI and GCC roles often include annual bonuses, retention bonuses, shift allowances, and benefits that add meaningfully to the stated CTC. Comparing two offers at the headline number only can mislead.

Use the scarcity of the market. There is only 1 active TPM listing in Mumbai right now. If a company has extended you an offer, they have invested weeks in evaluation. Requesting time to consider, or countering with a specific number, rarely kills a genuine offer.

Quantify your past delivery impact. TPM salaries at the senior end grow fastest when candidates can point to specific programmes, timelines compressed, costs saved, or team scale managed. Translate your experience into business language before negotiating.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

The headline CTC figure in a Mumbai TPM offer is rarely the full picture. Here is what typically makes up the package.

Fixed base salary: The guaranteed monthly component. Glassdoor and publicly reported breakdowns for Indian tech roles commonly show base salary at 50-65 percent of total CTC, though this varies significantly by company and sector.

Annual variable or performance bonus: Tied to individual and company targets. BFSI and GCC roles in Mumbai often carry a higher bonus weight, with variable components publicly reported as 10-20 percent of fixed salary at mid-senior levels.

Joining or retention bonus: Common for senior hires and candidates with competing offers. Companies rarely volunteer this. Ask directly.

Benefits and allowances: Medical insurance for self and family, meal allowances, fuel, and phone reimbursement are standard at most firms. These add to CTC but do not land in your bank account as cash.

ESOPs or RSUs: Less common at traditional Indian firms, but standard at funded startups and FAANG GCCs. If equity is part of the package, ask for the vesting schedule and the last known valuation before factoring it into your decision.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why are there almost no TPM jobs listed in Mumbai?

The formal 'Technical Program Manager' title is heavily concentrated in Bangalore, where global tech firms have standardised it. Mumbai's market, dominated by BFSI, consulting, and GCCs, often uses equivalent titles like 'Programme Manager', 'IT Delivery Manager', or 'Senior Project Manager' for the same function. If you search only the TPM title, you will miss a large share of equivalent roles. Broadening your title search while keeping your resume TPM-focused is a practical strategy.

Does a TPM earn more in Bangalore than in Mumbai?

In most cases, yes, despite Mumbai's higher cost of living. Bangalore has 41 active TPM openings versus 1 in Mumbai in this scan, and that volume creates competitive bidding among tech companies that pushes packages higher, particularly at senior levels. Glassdoor figures and publicly reported compensation data generally show Bangalore TPM salaries matching or exceeding Mumbai for comparable experience. If you have location flexibility, the data suggests Bangalore offers more openings and a higher ceiling.

What credentials or skills increase a TPM's salary?

PMP, PMI-ACP, and SAFe Agile are commonly cited in TPM job descriptions and can support your case during salary negotiation. Beyond certifications, demonstrated delivery impact matters more at senior levels: managing programmes with large engineering team counts, cross-functional leadership at CXO level, and domain depth in fintech or cloud infrastructure all carry weight in Mumbai's market. Certifications open doors; a quantified delivery record closes the offer.

Am I required to disclose my current salary when applying?

Several Indian states have placed restrictions on employers asking for current salary, though enforcement varies. You are generally not obligated to volunteer the number. If pressed, redirect to your expected CTC based on Glassdoor figures and publicly reported industry benchmarks for your experience band. Anchoring on market rate rather than your current package is especially important if you are moving from a lower-paying employer or switching from a related but different title.

What is the difference between CTC and my actual take-home salary?

CTC (cost to company) is the total annual expense the employer bears, including PF contributions, gratuity provisions, medical insurance premiums, and other benefits. Your actual in-hand monthly amount is lower: commonly cited estimates put take-home at 65-75 percent of fixed CTC after PF deductions and income tax, though the exact figure depends on your tax bracket and company structure. Always ask for a full breakup and a monthly take-home estimate before accepting any offer.
