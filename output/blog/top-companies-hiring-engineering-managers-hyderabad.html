04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Hyderabad had 16 Engineering Manager openings in the knok jobradar snapshot as of 8 July 2026. Among the six cities tracked, Bangalore leads by a wide margin (182 openings), followed by Delhi (53), then Pune and Chennai (20 each), with Hyderabad at 16 and Mumbai at 12. The smaller pool means less competition in some cases, but you need to be targeted rather than relying on volume applications.

The city's EM hiring is split across three employer types: GCC offices of global MNCs, product and SaaS companies, and IT services firms. Each looks for a different leadership profile. GCC offices tend to want EMs who can manage distributed teams and align with global delivery processes. Product companies want EMs who own roadmaps and collaborate closely with product managers. IT services firms look for EMs who can handle client relationships alongside people management. Knowing your target segment before you apply makes your resume and cover note much sharper.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Show delivery outcomes, not just team size. Hiring managers for EM roles want to see what shipped under your leadership. Alongside team size, call out concrete outcomes: latency improvements, release velocity, uptime gains, or business impact. Vague claims like 'built high-performing teams' do not land the way specifics do.

Match the employer type in your positioning. For GCC roles (Roche, L'Oreal India, Veeva), emphasise cross-cultural team management, global stakeholder alignment, and process discipline. For product companies (HighRadius, Progress Software, Cendyn), lead with ownership: roadmap influence, hiring decisions, and architecture calls you made. For IT services firms (Wipro), client management and SLA delivery language will resonate more.

Get your people management story right. Most EM job descriptions in Hyderabad explicitly ask for experience building teams. Be specific about how you have hired, how you structure 1:1s, and how you handle underperformance. This separates candidates who have managed teams from those who have genuinely led them.

Maintain your technical credibility. Product and SaaS companies in Hyderabad expect EMs to review architectures and unblock engineers. Call out the stack your teams work on and the technical decisions you weigh in on, even if you are no longer writing code yourself.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With only 16 openings in Hyderabad, this is not a market where spray-and-pray works. You need to be precise.

Prioritise by employer type first. Decide whether you want a GCC role (structured, global exposure, slower pace), a SaaS or product role (faster, more ownership), or an IT services role (client-facing, project-driven). Apply to your preferred segment with a tailored resume before moving to the others.

Let LinkedIn work for you. Most Hyderabad-based EMs are found by recruiters on LinkedIn before they ever apply to a job post. Set your profile to 'Open to Work' (recruiter-visible only if discretion matters), update your headline to reflect your current team scope, and post a short reflection on a delivery challenge every few weeks. Recruiter inbounds tend to pick up with consistent profile activity.

Follow up on applications. EM hiring timelines are long. A short, polite follow-up to the recruiter a week after applying is normal at this level and signals exactly the communication skill the role requires.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hyderabad a good city to find Engineering Manager roles right now?

Hyderabad has a real but compact EM market: 16 openings in the knok jobradar snapshot, versus 182 in Bangalore. The smaller pool means less competition in some cases, but also fewer chances to be selective about role fit. If you have location flexibility, running a parallel Bangalore or Delhi search alongside Hyderabad will give you more options and negotiating leverage.

Which companies in Hyderabad are hiring the most Engineering Managers?

Cendyn leads with 3 open roles, followed by Wipro with 2. The other companies in the current snapshot each have 1 open role. The market is spread thin, so check listings regularly as EM roles at this level open and close faster than individual contributor positions.

What salary should I expect as an Engineering Manager in Hyderabad?

Based on knok jobradar data, the Manager band is 35-60 LPA and the Senior Manager band is 55-90 LPA. Your position within the band depends on team scope, company stage, and how you negotiate. Hyderabad EM salaries are commonly cited as slightly lower than Bangalore, but lower living costs and less commute friction can offset some of that difference.

Should I target GCC companies or product companies for EM roles in Hyderabad?

It depends on what you want from the role. GCC offices (Roche, Veeva, L'Oreal India) offer structured processes, global exposure, and strong benefits, but with less product ownership. Product and SaaS companies (Cendyn, HighRadius, Progress Software) give you more roadmap influence and tend to move faster. If you are stepping into an EM role for the first time, a GCC environment can be a steadier place to build your leadership foundation, while experienced EMs wanting broader scope usually find product companies more demanding and rewarding.

Do I need to be actively coding to get hired as an Engineering Manager in Hyderabad?

Not writing production code, but you need credible technical judgment. Product and SaaS companies in Hyderabad expect EMs to review system designs, weigh in on architecture decisions, and have enough context to unblock their teams. Be clear in your resume about the stack your teams work on and the technical calls you make. Candidates without a technical foundation find it harder to clear the interview loop at product companies.

How long does the hiring process typically take for EM roles in Hyderabad?

EM hiring cycles are longer than individual contributor hiring. Industry surveys suggest multiple interview rounds are common at this level, often including a recruiter screen, a technical or case-study discussion, a hiring manager conversation, and a leadership panel. Expect several weeks between first contact and offer. Polite follow-ups after each round and crisp communication at every stage help keep things moving.
