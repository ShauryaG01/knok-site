01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Mobile Engineer listings in Bangalore hide their salary. Of the 21 roles knok jobradar tracked as of July 2026, none published a pay range. This is typical: most Indian employers assess fit first and negotiate later. The result is that job seekers often walk into interviews without a real benchmark to anchor on.

The salary ranges in this guide come from publicly reported sources (Glassdoor, levels.fyi) and industry surveys. They do not come from the 21 listings themselves, which disclosed nothing. Keep that context in mind when using any number here as a target.

Bangalore remains the highest-paying city for mobile engineers in India, driven by a dense mix of product companies, MNCs, and funded startups. All 21 active roles in this snapshot were based here, confirming it as the primary hiring hub for this role.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Know your number before the call. Pull Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company. Ask peers in LinkedIn groups, Discord communities, or r/developersIndia what they actually earned in recent offers. Walk in with a target range, not a vague expectation.

Do not share your current salary if you can avoid it. You are not obliged to. A stronger response: 'I am targeting a range based on market rates for this role and my experience.' If they press, redirect to the value you bring rather than where you are coming from.

Negotiate on total comp, not just base. Ask specifically about ESOPs, annual bonus, joining bonus, and variable pay. A lower base at a well-funded startup with meaningful ESOPs can be worth more over time than a higher base at a slow-growth company.

Counter at least once. Industry surveys suggest most candidates accept the first offer without negotiating. Employers often leave a little room knowing that stronger candidates will ask.

Get the written offer before you resign. Verbal commitments get revised. Only the written offer letter with a joining date and full CTC breakup is binding.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Base salary is the number on your offer letter. For mid-to-senior mobile engineering roles in Bangalore, base is commonly cited as making up 60-70% of total CTC, with the rest split across several other components.

Variable pay is a performance bonus, usually a percentage of base. Always ask what the actual payout history has been. Industry surveys suggest variable components are paid at 70-90% of the stated percentage in a good year, and sometimes less during a slow year.

ESOPs matter most at funded startups and pre-IPO companies. Before counting them as compensation, ask about the current strike price, the vesting schedule (commonly cited as 4 years with a 1-year cliff), the last funding valuation, and whether there is a buyback or secondary-market option.

Joining bonus is a one-time payment to help bridge a CTC gap when the company cannot raise its base ceiling. Check for clawback clauses if you leave within the first year.

Benefits such as health insurance for you and your family, meal coupons, internet reimbursement, and learning budgets add real value. Factor these into your comparison, especially between a startup and an MNC where perks can differ significantly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most companies in India not list salaries on job postings?

Most Indian employers prefer to assess a candidate first and then negotiate based on current CTC and expectations, which keeps information in the company's favour. Salary transparency is growing slowly, particularly at startups competing for international talent, but it is still not the norm across the industry. Until it becomes standard, publicly reported sources like Glassdoor and peer communities are your best alternative.

Does my tech stack (iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native) affect the salary I can negotiate?

It can, but less than most people expect. Publicly reported data from levels.fyi shows native iOS engineers sometimes earn a slight premium at senior levels, partly because strong Swift expertise is harder to find. For mid-level roles, industry surveys show the gap between native and cross-platform specialists is relatively small. The company's stage and your overall seniority tend to matter far more than the specific framework you use.

How do I find out what a specific company pays before I apply or interview?

Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi by company name and role. Communities on LinkedIn, Reddit's r/developersIndia, and niche Discord servers often have people sharing actual offer letter details. If the role came through a referral, ask your contact directly. A peer who recently interviewed at the same company is your best source of current, company-specific data.

Should I share my current salary when HR asks for it?

You are not legally required to in most situations, and sharing it anchors the negotiation to your past rather than your market value. A better response: 'I am targeting a range based on market benchmarks for this role and experience level.' If you are pressed very hard, you can share but follow it immediately with your target range to shift the conversation forward.

What should I ask about ESOPs to know if they are actually worth something?

Ask four things: the current strike price, the full vesting schedule, the most recent funding round valuation, and whether there is a buyback or secondary-market option. Industry surveys suggest most employees only see ESOP value at an IPO or acquisition event, so understand the realistic timeline before counting them as part of your compensation. ESOPs at a company with a very high last-round valuation and no liquidity path can sit underwater for years.

Hyderabad shows zero listings in this snapshot. Does that mean there are no mobile jobs there?

No, it just reflects the timing of this particular data pull. Hyderabad has a substantial tech ecosystem with several MNCs and product companies that hire mobile engineers regularly. A zero count in one snapshot is not a trend. If Hyderabad works for you geographically, check job boards regularly since availability shifts week to week.
