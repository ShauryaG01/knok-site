01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Postman is the world's leading API platform, trusted by millions of developers to build, test, and document APIs. With 136 open roles listed as of July 2026, the company is actively growing across engineering, product, design, marketing, and go-to-market teams. Postman has a strong India footprint, with Bengaluru being a key engineering hub.

Because Postman's product is itself a developer tool, the hiring bar leans heavily on people who genuinely live and breathe APIs and developer workflows. Whether you are an engineer, a technical writer, or a product manager, showing that you understand the developer experience matters far more than a polished resume alone.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Postman's process typically runs across four to five stages. Details vary by role and team, so treat this as a general guide rather than a guaranteed sequence.

Stage 1: Application review. Your resume and profile are screened by a recruiter or through automated filtering. Tailor your resume to mention API workflows, developer tools, or relevant Postman features you have actually used. A cover note that shows product knowledge goes a long way here.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A brief introductory conversation where the recruiter checks your background, current situation, and interest in Postman. Be ready to talk about your experience with APIs, any tools you have built or tested, and why you want to join a developer-tools company specifically.

Stage 3: Technical screen. For engineering roles, this is usually a coding round focused on data structures, algorithms, or a take-home problem. For product or design roles, expect a case study or portfolio walkthrough. The interviewer may also ask you to walk through a system design or API architecture scenario.

Stage 4: Virtual onsite. This is the main loop, typically spanning multiple back-to-back interviews in a single day. Engineering candidates usually face a mix of coding, system design, and behavioural rounds. Non-engineering candidates typically go through a presentation or case exercise plus cross-functional interviews with potential stakeholders.

Stage 5: Offer and references. If the loop goes well, a recruiter reaches out with an offer. Reference checks may happen in parallel with or just before the offer stage.

Candidates who have interviewed at Postman publicly report the process feeling thorough but straightforward, with interviewers who are genuinely curious rather than trying to trip you up.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

API and developer-tool fluency. Postman's product is a developer tool, so they look for people who have actually used APIs, built integrations, written tests, or worked in environments where APIs are central. Even non-engineering roles benefit from knowing what a REST endpoint is and why developer experience matters.

Strong fundamentals. For engineers, this means solid data structures, algorithms, and system design. Postman's product handles scale, collaboration, and real-time sync, so distributed systems thinking is valued for senior roles.

Ownership and initiative. Postman culture rewards people who take end-to-end ownership of problems. In interviews, they tend to probe for examples where you drove something to completion without being told every step.

Clear communication. The team is globally distributed, so written and verbal clarity matters. Interviewers notice whether you can explain a complex technical idea simply, especially in product, design, and customer-facing roles.

Genuine product curiosity. Candidates who have explored Postman's features, tried the API builder, or read their public engineering blog stand out. This is a company where product enthusiasm is noticed and rewarded.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your first interview. Download Postman, create a free account, and walk through building a simple API collection. If you have never done this, an afternoon of exploration is worth more than a week of reading. Interviewers frequently ask what you think of the product or how you would improve it.

Brush up on API concepts. Know the difference between REST, GraphQL, and gRPC. Understand authentication patterns like OAuth and API keys. For engineering roles, be ready to discuss how you would design a system that handles a high volume of API calls reliably.

Prepare STAR stories around ownership. Think of three or four moments where you owned a project end to end, dealt with ambiguity, or pushed through a blocker without waiting for direction. Postman's behavioural questions consistently probe for initiative and autonomy.

Practice system design for senior roles. If you are applying for a senior or staff engineering position, spend time on distributed systems topics: caching, database sharding, event-driven architecture, and API gateway patterns. These come up in Postman's design rounds according to publicly shared interview experiences.

Look at Postman's engineering blog and changelog. Understanding what the team has shipped recently gives you talking points in every interview, from the recruiter call to the final loop. It also signals that you are the kind of person who follows the craft.

Prepare your own questions. Postman interviewers appreciate candidates who ask thoughtful questions about product direction, team challenges, and engineering culture. Coming in with no questions reads as low enthusiasm.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Postman describes itself as a company built by developers for developers, and that shows up in how the team operates internally. A few themes come through consistently in employee reviews and public content.

Developer empathy as a north star. Every team, including sales and marketing, is expected to understand the developer perspective. If you join Postman, you will be around people who debate API design opinions the way sports fans debate players.

Distributed and async-friendly. With teams across the US, India, and other regions, Postman leans on strong written communication and documentation. Meetings tend to be purposeful rather than frequent, and a lot of alignment happens over Slack and shared documents.

High ownership, low hand-holding. Postman tends to attract and reward people who are comfortable defining their own scope. If you prefer a highly structured environment where tasks are handed to you, this may feel like a stretch. If you enjoy running with a problem and reporting back with results, you will likely thrive.

Growth stage with enterprise scale. Postman crossed into enterprise sales territory a few years ago, which means the company now balances startup speed with the demands of large customers. This creates interesting tension that shows up in product and go-to-market decisions.

Glassdoor reviews commonly cite good work-life balance for an engineering-focused company, though this can vary by team and project phase.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Postman currently have?

As of July 2026, Postman has 136 open roles listed across functions including engineering, product, design, sales, and operations. The number shifts regularly, so it is worth checking Postman's careers page directly for the latest listings. Engineering and go-to-market roles tend to have the highest volume of openings.

Does Postman hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Postman's open roles skew toward experienced hires, especially at the senior level. That said, the company does post roles for new graduates or early-career professionals, particularly in engineering and customer success. If you are a fresher, focus on showcasing side projects that involve APIs, integrations, or developer tooling, since practical experience with the domain matters more than years on a resume.

What salary can I expect at Postman India?

Postman does not publicly publish salary bands for India roles. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list compensation data shared by employees and candidates, but sample sizes are limited so treat those figures as rough signals rather than guarantees. Total compensation at Postman typically includes base salary, equity in the form of RSUs, and benefits, so compare the full package rather than just the base when evaluating an offer.

Is there a coding test before the recruiter call?

Some engineering roles at Postman include an online assessment as an early filter before the recruiter call, while others move straight to a recruiter conversation first. The format can vary by role level and hiring team. If you apply and hear nothing for a couple of weeks, a brief follow-up note to the recruiter on LinkedIn is generally acceptable.

Do I need to know Postman's product deeply to get hired?

You do not need to be a power user, but you should have hands-on familiarity before any interview. Download the app, build a simple collection, and explore the collaboration features. Interviewers at Postman notice quickly whether a candidate has actually touched the product. For non-engineering roles like sales or marketing, being able to articulate why developers love Postman and where the product could improve is a real differentiator.

How long does Postman's hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on publicly shared candidate experiences, the full process from application to offer commonly takes a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on the role and how quickly each stage is scheduled. Engineering loops tend to be more intensive and may take longer to coordinate. Staying responsive to recruiter messages and being flexible on scheduling generally speeds things up from the candidate side.
