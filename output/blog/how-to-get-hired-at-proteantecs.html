01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

proteanTecs is a semiconductor deep-tech company whose core technology embeds tiny monitoring 'agents' inside chips to track health, predict failures, and improve manufacturing yield. Founded in Israel, the company runs a significant engineering hub in Bengaluru that handles real product work, not back-office support.

As of mid-2026, proteanTecs has 24 open roles across engineering, product, and business functions. The team is relatively small globally, which means each hire is deliberate and the interview bar is high.

If you come from semiconductor design, embedded systems, EDA tools, machine learning, or data engineering and want to work on problems that matter to chipmakers and device manufacturers worldwide, proteanTecs is worth a close look. The India team collaborates directly with global counterparts on core platform development.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs 4 to 6 rounds over 2 to 4 weeks. Timelines vary by role and team availability. Here is what candidates generally report:

Resume screen: The talent team looks for domain relevance. For engineering roles, keywords like RTL, DFT, SPICE, Python, machine learning, and data pipelines carry weight depending on the specific opening. A well-structured resume that names specific tools and outcomes helps your application move forward.

Recruiter call: A short conversation, usually around half an hour, to confirm your interest, explain the role, and check basics like notice period and location. This is also your first real chance to ask questions about the team and the work.

Technical screen: For software and data roles, expect data structures, algorithms, and possibly a live coding or take-home task. For hardware roles, domain questions on chip design, characterisation, or test methodology are more common. Difficulty is moderate to high.

Technical deep-dive rounds (2 to 3 rounds): These are the heaviest stages. Senior engineers or team leads probe your past projects, your problem-solving approach, and your domain knowledge in detail. Candidates consistently say the team cares more about how you reason through a problem than whether you instantly know the right answer.

Hiring manager round: A conversation about your motivations, your interest in the semiconductor domain specifically, and how you see yourself growing within the team. Being genuinely curious about chip technology and its real-world applications helps here.

Offer and background check: If all rounds go well, an offer typically follows within a week. Standard background verification applies before joining.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

proteanTecs hires people who can operate at the intersection of hardware and software. Pure software generalists without any exposure to chips or electronics may find certain roles a stretch, but positions in data engineering or machine learning are accessible if the domain curiosity is genuine.

Technical depth: Strong fundamentals matter across the board. For hardware roles: RTL design, DFT, failure analysis, or test engineering. For software roles: Python, C++, data pipelines, or applied machine learning. For mixed roles: comfort moving across the stack and translating between hardware and software contexts.

Problem ownership: This is a startup-style environment even as the company scales. They want engineers who pick up an unclear problem, define it, and drive it to a solution without waiting for a detailed spec to land in their inbox.

Clear communication: Because teams span Israel and India, written and verbal clarity is a real requirement. Engineers who document their thinking well and can sync effectively across time zones settle in faster and are genuinely valued.

Curiosity about semiconductors: You do not need a chip-design PhD to join, but a genuine interest in understanding why chip health monitoring matters gives you a clear edge in interviews and on the job.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand what proteanTecs actually does. Read about in-chip monitoring, predictive analytics for silicon, and manufacturing yield improvement. Being able to explain specifically why this problem interests you shows the team you are not applying everywhere at once.

Brush up on your core domain. For hardware candidates: revisit DFT basics, scan chains, ATPG, and reliability concepts. For software and data candidates: practise data structures and algorithms at a mid-to-senior level, and be ready to discuss system design for data-heavy applications.

Prepare project stories. The deep-dive rounds are largely project-based. For each major project on your resume, be ready to explain the problem, your specific contribution, the trade-offs you made, and what you would do differently now. A structured format covering Situation, Task, Action, and Result works well.

Know your resume cold. Interviewers often pick a single line from your resume and go deep on it. If you listed a tool or technology, be ready for detailed follow-up questions on that specific item.

Prepare smart questions. Ask about the team's current technical challenges, how the India and Israel teams collaborate day-to-day, and what success looks like in the first few months on the job. These questions signal genuine interest.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

proteanTecs is solving a genuinely novel problem in a niche that most large companies have not yet entered. The culture reflects that: high ownership, fast feedback loops, and an expectation that engineers engage with the 'why' behind what they build, not just the 'how'.

Collaboration across time zones: The Bengaluru team works closely with Israel-based colleagues. This means some overlap hours in the morning or evening and a real need to communicate asynchronously with clarity. Engineers who have worked in cross-border setups tend to settle in faster and are valued for that experience.

Startup pace, growing structure: proteanTecs has raised multiple funding rounds and is scaling, but it still operates with startup-level speed in many areas. Processes are maturing, which means you can shape how things get done, but some ambiguity is normal and expected as part of the job.

Learning-heavy environment: Because the technology is niche, on-the-job learning is baked into the role. The team welcomes people who ask questions, share what they discover, and are comfortable saying they need to go investigate something before answering.

Recognition for impact: Glassdoor reviews mention that contributions get noticed on a smaller team, and equity is typically part of the compensation package for engineering roles. Being at this stage of a company means your work has visible, traceable consequences on the product.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the hiring process at proteanTecs take?

Most candidates report the full process takes 2 to 4 weeks from the recruiter call to the offer. The timeline can stretch if a specific team lead is unavailable or if multiple strong candidates are being evaluated in parallel. Staying responsive and following up politely after each round helps keep things moving on your end.

Does proteanTecs hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

The majority of roles require prior experience, especially in semiconductor, embedded, or data engineering domains. The company does occasionally hire strong freshers from top engineering programmes, particularly for software or data roles where fundamentals are solid. Always check the specific job description for experience requirements before applying, as they vary significantly by role.

Is the Bengaluru office a real engineering team or just a support hub?

The Bengaluru office is a core engineering location where active product development happens, not a support or services centre. Engineers in India work on the same platform as their counterparts in Israel and contribute directly to the core product. This makes the India roles more substantive than what you typically find at offshore centres of larger companies.

What salary can I expect at proteanTecs?

Specific figures depend on role, level, and experience. Glassdoor and levels.fyi commonly report that deep-tech semiconductor startups at this funding stage pay competitively for India, often above what IT services firms offer for comparable experience. Equity in the form of ESOPs is typically part of the package for engineering roles. Check current figures on Glassdoor before entering salary discussions so you negotiate from an informed position.

Is there a coding test in the interview process?

For software and data engineering roles, a coding round is standard. It is usually a live session or a take-home assignment covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes domain-relevant scenarios. For hardware and chip-design roles, the technical rounds tend to be discussion-based with design problems and debugging exercises rather than a traditional coding test.

How competitive is it to get a job at proteanTecs?

The company is selective because the team is small and the domain is specialised. With 24 open roles currently listed, there is real hiring activity, but each position gets careful attention from the team. A tailored application that speaks directly to your semiconductor or data engineering background, combined with solid interview preparation, gives you a meaningful advantage over generic applicants.
