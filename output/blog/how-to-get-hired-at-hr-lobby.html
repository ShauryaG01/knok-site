03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Since HR LOBBY is a recruitment firm, the people who interview you understand hiring deeply. Here is a typical stage-by-stage breakdown, based on what is commonly reported for HR consulting companies of this type. Individual experiences may vary.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Shortlisting
Your resume is the first filter. For HR and recruitment roles, the team looks for signs of communication ability, attention to detail, and any prior exposure to hiring or client-facing work. A tailored, clean resume makes a real difference at this stage.

Stage 2: Initial HR Call
This is a short introductory call to confirm your interest, check basic fit, and explain the role. Be ready to talk about why you want to work in recruitment or HR consulting and what you know about HR LOBBY. This call also sets expectations on location and compensation.

Stage 3: Functional or Skills Round
This round goes deeper into your abilities. For recruitment roles, you might be asked how you would source candidates for a hard-to-fill position, how you handle candidate dropouts, or how you manage multiple open roles at the same time. For business development roles, expect questions on client acquisition and relationship management. This round is typically with a team lead or senior recruiter.

Stage 4: Final Round with Management
A face-to-face or video call with a senior manager or founder-level person. This round covers your attitude, long-term interest in HR as a career, and your fit with the team culture. Compensation and growth expectations are often confirmed here.

With 19 open roles currently active, the team is in growth mode, which generally means shorter gaps between rounds and faster offer timelines.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

People Skills Above Everything
HR LOBBY's core business is people. They need team members who are genuinely comfortable starting conversations, building rapport quickly, and maintaining professional relationships over time. If you enjoy talking to people and can keep a candidate or client engaged, you are already ahead.

Recruitment or HR Domain Knowledge
For experienced hires, a background in end-to-end recruitment, sourcing, or HR operations is valued. Familiarity with job portals like Naukri, LinkedIn, or Shine, and an understanding of how hiring pipelines work, will come up in interviews.

Target-Oriented Mindset
Recruitment involves real deadlines and numbers. HR LOBBY looks for people who can work under targets, stay motivated through slow periods, and close positions on time. If you have specific hiring numbers or placement counts from a previous role, be ready to share them.

Clear Communication
You will be writing job descriptions, messaging candidates, and coordinating with client HR teams. Both written and spoken communication need to be professional and confident.

For Freshers: Attitude and Willingness to Learn
At entry level, HR LOBBY, like most recruitment firms, often values attitude over experience. Show that you understand what the job involves, that you are comfortable with rejection as part of the process, and that you are ready to put in the effort to learn.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research HR LOBBY Before Your First Call
Go through their website, LinkedIn page, and any public reviews on Glassdoor or AmbitionBox. Understand the industries they serve, the types of roles they fill, and how they position themselves to clients. Mentioning specific details about their work shows genuine interest.

Prepare Your 'Why Recruitment' Answer
Almost every interview at a recruitment firm starts with: 'Why do you want to be in HR or recruitment?' Have a clear, honest answer ready. If you have relevant experience, connect it to specific moments where you enjoyed the people-facing side of work. If you are a fresher, talk about your communication strengths and interest in career development.

Know Your Numbers
If you have worked in recruitment before, be ready to share how many positions you handled at once, your typical time-to-fill, or how many candidates you placed in a given period. Concrete numbers make your experience credible.

Practice Scenario-Based Answers
Expect questions like: 'How would you source a Finance Manager for a mid-sized FMCG company in Pune?' or 'A candidate accepts an offer but drops out before joining. What do you do?' Think through realistic answers that show process thinking and calm problem-solving.

Polish Your Resume and LinkedIn Profile
Since HR LOBBY works with LinkedIn regularly, make sure your profile is updated and professional. Your resume should be clean, concise, and tailored to the specific role. Avoid a generic objective statement at the top and use a short summary that reflects your actual experience.

Follow Up After Each Round
A short thank-you note after an interview round is a small gesture that stands out at a recruitment firm, because they recognise good candidate behaviour when they see it.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

HR LOBBY operates in a fast-paced, results-driven environment, typical of recruitment and staffing companies. The work is relationship-heavy, with significant time spent on calls, follow-ups, and coordination between candidates and clients. If you enjoy people-centric work and the satisfaction of placing someone in the right role, this environment suits you well.

Collaborative but Target-Driven
Teams at recruitment firms often work closely together, sharing leads and candidate pipelines. At the same time, individual performance is tracked through placements and outcomes. You will be both a team player and an individual contributor.

A Strong Learning Ground for Freshers
HR consulting firms often train new joiners on sourcing techniques, client communication, and CRM tools. If you are early in your career, the on-the-job exposure here can build your HR foundation faster than a traditional corporate role would.

Growth Tied to Performance
Promotion cycles at recruitment companies tend to reflect results rather than seniority. If you consistently hit your targets and build strong client relationships, growth can come faster here than in many other HR roles.

Client-First Mindset
HR LOBBY serves multiple client companies, which means the team needs to be responsive, professional, and solution-oriented at all times. The culture rewards people who treat every client interaction as a reflection of the company's reputation.
