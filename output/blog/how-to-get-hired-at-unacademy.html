01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Unacademy is one of India's largest edtech platforms, built around live classes, competitive exam prep, and skill-based learning for millions of learners across the country. Over the past couple of years the company has restructured its operations and sharpened its focus on sustainable, profitable growth. This means hiring today is more targeted and selective than it was during the earlier rapid-expansion phase.

As of July 2026, Unacademy has 24 open roles listed across functions. Roles span product, engineering, content, sales, and operations. This is not bulk hiring: each open position tends to be deliberate, and competition from motivated edtech professionals is real.

For job seekers who genuinely care about education and have a strong professional track record, Unacademy offers the chance to work on problems that affect learners at real scale. The interview process is structured and moves at a reasonable pace once you are in the pipeline. Your motivation for joining an edtech company will be tested early, so go in with a clear and specific answer.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically spans four to five rounds depending on the seniority of the role, based on publicly reported candidate experiences. Here is what each stage usually looks like.

Application and Resume Screen

Your resume goes through an initial filter, either via an ATS or a recruiter review. A clean, role-specific resume that highlights measurable outcomes (not just responsibilities) gets through faster. Tailor it to the specific job description before applying.

Recruiter Call

This is a short call (commonly cited as 20-30 minutes) covering your background, notice period, location preference, and salary expectations. The recruiter also walks you through the role and timeline. Be direct and honest about your current CTC and what you are expecting. Publicly reported feedback suggests Unacademy recruiters are straightforward, so brief and clear answers work better than long ones.

Technical or Domain Round

This round is tailored to your function. Engineering candidates typically face a coding problem or a system design discussion. Product candidates get a product thinking exercise or a case study. Content and educator candidates may be asked to deliver a short sample lesson or critique educational material. Sales candidates usually do a role-play or a situational exercise. This stage can span one or two sessions depending on the role.

Managerial Round

A structured conversation with the hiring manager covering your past work, how you handled specific challenges, and your problem-solving approach. Expect a mix of behavioural questions and role-specific ones. Candidates who walk in with crisp, well-structured stories from their past experience consistently report better outcomes here.

Final or Leadership Round

For mid-to-senior positions, a final round with a senior leader or a small panel is common. This round tests cultural alignment, strategic thinking, and your understanding of the edtech space. Some candidates for leadership roles are given a case or a short presentation task to complete ahead of this round.

Offer and Onboarding

If selected, the offer letter typically follows within a few days to a week based on commonly cited timelines. Standard background verification happens before your joining date. Tech and product roles are largely remote-friendly during onboarding.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Unacademy looks for people who care about education and can operate independently in a fast-moving environment. A few traits show up consistently in hiring feedback and job descriptions.

Mission alignment. Interviewers ask why you want to work in edtech specifically, and generic answers do not land. If you have used the Unacademy platform, taught students, or worked in education before, bring that up early and concretely.

Ownership mindset. After the restructuring period, teams are leaner and more accountable. The people who thrive here take initiative without being told. Come prepared with examples of problems you spotted and solved on your own, not just tasks you were assigned.

Data orientation. For product, growth, and business roles, being comfortable with metrics and outcome-based thinking is expected. You do not need to be a data scientist, but you should be able to talk through decisions you made based on numbers.

Communication clarity. For educator, content, and operations roles especially, how clearly and simply you express ideas is part of the evaluation. The company's core product is explanation, and that standard carries into hiring.

Adaptability. Unacademy has pivoted and restructured more than once. Interviewers look for candidates who have navigated change, absorbed feedback, and kept delivering without losing focus. Evidence of resilience across your career matters here.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the current product, not the old one. Unacademy's focus has shifted. Before your interview, spend time on the platform itself: explore the live classes, the test series, the subject verticals that are currently active. Knowing what the company is building right now, as opposed to what it announced two years ago, signals genuine interest.

Prepare your stories using the STAR format. Situation, Task, Action, Result. Interviewers at every stage use behavioural questions. For each major role in your career, have two or three tight stories ready covering ownership, handling conflict, a failure you recovered from, and measurable impact.

Prepare by role type:

Software EngineeringData structures, system design, live coding
Product ManagementProduct teardowns, metrics framing, prioritisation

OperationsProcess improvement stories, cross-team coordination

Prepare your 'why edtech' answer in advance. This comes up in almost every round. Connect it to something real: a learning experience of your own, a student or mentee you helped, or a gap you noticed in how skills are taught in India. The more specific the story, the better it lands.

Apply to the right role among the 24 open positions. Check Unacademy's careers page and apply to the one that maps most closely to your background. Applying to several unrelated roles at once can signal a lack of focus to the recruiter and reduce your chances.

Follow up politely if you do not hear back. A brief, courteous email to the recruiter after a week is appropriate. Publicly reported experiences suggest the recruitment team is responsive when candidates follow up professionally.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Unacademy's culture is built around learning, speed, and direct impact. From publicly reported employee experiences, a few themes come through consistently.

High ownership, lean teams. After restructuring, teams are smaller and each person carries more accountability. You will likely handle a wider scope than your job title suggests. People who prefer very structured, role-defined environments sometimes find this challenging. People who like building things from scratch tend to enjoy it.

Mission-driven atmosphere. A noticeable portion of the workforce came from teaching backgrounds or has personal connections to education access in India. This creates a culture where the 'why' behind the work is discussed openly. If your interest in the company is purely transactional, you may feel that disconnect during interviews and on the job.

Direct feedback culture. Publicly reported reviews mention that performance expectations are clear and feedback is given plainly. Managers tend to evaluate output over activity. This suits self-starters and can feel abrupt for people used to slower, more formal review cycles.

Flexible but not fully remote. Many tech and product roles allow hybrid working arrangements. Bangalore is the primary hub. Some teams prefer periodic in-person collaboration. Confirm the specific policy for your role during the recruiter call rather than assuming a company-wide standard.

Internal growth depends on the team. Some teams have active mentorship and internal mobility. Others are more independent and lean. Ask your interviewer directly about growth paths for the specific role, rather than relying on general company statements.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the full interview process at Unacademy take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the end-to-end process from application to offer commonly takes two to four weeks, though timelines vary by role and team. Senior or specialised roles with additional panel rounds can take longer. Following up politely with the recruiter after each completed round helps keep things moving without being pushy.

Does Unacademy hire freshers or mainly experienced professionals?

Unacademy hires both, but the mix depends on the current openings. Engineering fresher hiring typically happens through campus placements or specific graduate programmes. The 24 open roles listed as of July 2026 appear to largely target professionals with prior work experience. Check each job description for the experience requirement before applying, as it varies across functions.

What salary can I expect at Unacademy?

Salary figures vary widely by role, level, and negotiation. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list publicly reported compensation ranges for engineering and product roles at Unacademy if you want a reference point before your recruiter call. Come prepared to share your current CTC and expected CTC in LPA. The company tends to benchmark against current market data, so knowing industry ranges strengthens your negotiation.

Is prior edtech experience required to get hired at Unacademy?

It is a plus but not a hard requirement for most roles. What matters more is a genuine interest in the education space and the ability to transfer your skills to Unacademy's specific problems. If your background is in consumer internet, SaaS, or teaching, frame it around how your experience connects to what the company is building. Interviewers consistently look for a real and specific 'why education' story, not a polished one.

How competitive is it to get a job at Unacademy right now?

With 24 open roles as of July 2026, hiring is selective rather than broad. The company is in a phase of focused growth, which means each role tends to have a clear internal brief and a relatively small shortlist. Candidates who tailor their applications, prepare role-specific content, and can articulate a strong motivation for edtech stand out. Generic applications that could go to any company rarely make it past the recruiter screen.

Does Unacademy offer remote work?

Many tech and product roles offer hybrid or flexible working arrangements based on publicly reported employee experiences. Bangalore is the main office hub, and some teams prefer periodic in-person collaboration for planning or reviews. The policy varies by team, so confirm the working arrangement for your specific role during the recruiter call rather than assuming it applies uniformly across the company.
