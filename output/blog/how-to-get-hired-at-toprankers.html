01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Toprankers is one of India's fast-growing EdTech platforms, best known for helping students crack competitive exams like CLAT, banking entrance tests, and other high-stakes assessments. As of mid-2026, the company has around 100 open roles across teaching, content creation, sales, technology, and operations teams.

The volume of openings signals that Toprankers is in active expansion mode, which generally means faster hiring timelines than at larger, more established firms. Roles are spread across Delhi, remote, and hybrid arrangements, and the company recruits from a wide pool: fresh graduates, experienced educators, sales professionals, and engineers. If you are serious about EdTech and want to grow in a startup environment, this is a good window to apply.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs three to four rounds, though this can vary by role and seniority. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Round 1: Application and resume screening. The recruiter team reviews your resume against the job description. For teaching and content roles, subject knowledge and any prior EdTech experience carry the most weight at this stage. A well-matched resume moves to the next round faster.

Round 2: Recruiter or HR call. A brief introductory call where the recruiter checks your availability, current CTC, expected CTC, and general fit. Keep your numbers ready and ask about team size and role scope. This round is usually informal but it sets the tone.

Round 3: Domain or skills assessment. For educators and content writers, this commonly means a demo session or a written assignment. For tech roles, expect a coding or product exercise. Sales candidates may be asked to do a mock pitch or walk through how they would handle a student objection.

Round 4: Final interview with a senior manager or department head. This round goes deeper into your experience and often includes scenario-based questions: how you handle pressure, how you hit targets, or how you respond when a student is struggling. For content and teaching roles, expect questions about pedagogy and student outcomes.

With around 100 open roles at the time of writing, Toprankers is processing a high volume of applications. The full process from application to offer commonly wraps up within a few weeks, though timelines depend on the role and the specific team's availability.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Toprankers looks for people who understand the exam-prep space and genuinely care about student results. Here are the qualities that come up across roles:

For educators and content teams: Deep subject knowledge in your domain (law, banking, maths, English, reasoning, etc.), the ability to simplify hard concepts, and comfort with online teaching tools. Prior experience as a faculty member or content creator for a competitive exam platform is a clear advantage.

For sales and business development: Strong communication in English and Hindi, comfort with targets, and ideally some exposure to the EdTech or education sector. Toprankers sells to students who are often anxious about their future, so empathy matters alongside persuasion.

For technology roles: Solid fundamentals in your stack, an ability to ship fast, and comfort working in a startup environment where requirements can shift quickly.

Across all roles, the company values people who are self-driven, pick up new tools without needing hand-holding, and want to grow with the company rather than simply fill a fixed slot.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Do your homework on the product. Spend time on the Toprankers platform before your interview. Browse their test series, watch a sample class, or read student reviews. Being able to speak to specific features or compare their approach to competitors shows genuine interest, not just a rehearsed answer.

Match your resume to the role keywords. Toprankers uses an applicant tracking system like most companies of this size. Make sure your resume mentions the specific exam categories or tools listed in the job description, for example 'CLAT preparation', 'banking exam content', or 'LMS platforms'.

Prepare for a demo or assignment. Teaching and content roles almost always include a practical round. Practice explaining a tough concept clearly and concisely, the way you would to a nervous student preparing for an exam, not to a fellow expert.

Know your numbers. If you are applying to a sales role, be ready to discuss targets you have hit, conversion rates you have achieved, or revenue you have contributed. Vague claims about 'good performance' do not land well in these rounds.

Prepare sharp questions for them. Ask about the student satisfaction metrics the team tracks, how the content team decides what to build next, or what the growth path looks like for your role. These questions signal that you are already thinking like a team member.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Toprankers operates at startup pace even as it scales. Teams are lean, which means individuals often take on work that would be split across multiple people at a larger company, especially in the early months. If you prefer clearly defined role boundaries and a predictable schedule, go in with eyes open about the startup reality.

The mission, helping students clear life-changing exams, gives the work a clear sense of purpose. Many employees cite this as what keeps them motivated through the pressure of a growth-stage company. When a student clears CLAT or cracks a bank exam, the impact is tangible and personal.

Feedback cycles tend to be short and direct. Managers are generally accessible, and decisions get made faster than at traditional education companies. The tradeoff is that processes and tools are sometimes still being built as you use them, and you may need to figure things out with limited documentation.

For people who want to take on responsibility early, grow fast, and work on something with direct student impact, Toprankers can be a strong fit. The company is at a stage where good performers get noticed quickly and career growth can move faster than the industry average.
