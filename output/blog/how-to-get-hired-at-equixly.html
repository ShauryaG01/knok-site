Hiring Overview

The company is in an active hiring phase. As of July 2026, Equixly has 6 open roles, which signals a focused, deliberate build-out rather than rapid mass hiring. If you are a security engineer, backend developer, or someone with a strong interest in API security and AI, Equixly is worth putting on your radar. Getting in early at a funded security startup can mean meaningful equity, real ownership of your work, and the kind of career acceleration that is harder to find at larger companies.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Based on patterns commonly reported for early-stage security startups, here is how the hiring process at Equixly likely unfolds. Exact stages vary by role, so treat this as a general guide.

Application and Resume Review
The team looks for relevant security or engineering experience. A clear resume that highlights API work, security testing, or AI projects will stand out. Tailor your application to the specific role rather than sending a generic version.

Recruiter or Founder Screening Call
This is typically a short call to check alignment on role expectations and your motivation for joining. Be ready to explain why Equixly specifically interests you, not just why you want a security role in general.

Technical Interview
For engineering roles, expect questions on API security concepts, secure coding, and system design. You may be asked to reason through a vulnerability scenario or explain how you would approach securing a specific API. For non-engineering roles, expect a mix of domain knowledge and situational questions.

Practical Assignment or Take-Home Task
Many security startups include a hands-on task. This could be a mini security audit, a coding problem related to API testing, or a short written analysis. This is your chance to show applied skill, not just theoretical knowledge.

Final Round: Culture and Team Fit
A conversation with senior leadership or founders. They want to see that you can work well in a small, fast-moving environment. Expect questions about how you handle ambiguity, take ownership, and collaborate without heavy process.

The full cycle from application to offer typically spans a few weeks, based on what is commonly reported for companies at this stage.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Equixly sits at the crossroads of AI and application security, so the profile they seek reflects both worlds.

Deep API Security Knowledge
Understanding REST, GraphQL, and gRPC APIs is foundational. Familiarity with the OWASP API Security Top 10, common vulnerabilities like broken object-level authorization, and how to test for them will immediately signal that you are ready to contribute from day one.

Security Testing and Research Skills
Hands-on experience with tools like Burp Suite, Postman, or custom scripts for API fuzzing and vulnerability discovery is highly valued. Bug bounty experience or open-source security project contributions are strong signals worth mentioning prominently on your resume.

AI and Automation Mindset
Because Equixly's core product is AI-powered, they look for people who understand how machine learning fits into security workflows, even if you are not a data scientist. An interest in building or working with automated testing systems is a clear advantage.

Engineering Fundamentals
For technical roles, strong programming skills (Python is common in security tooling), understanding of HTTP internals, and comfort with cloud environments matter. Clean code and documentation habits count in a small team where everyone reviews everyone else's work.

Ownership and Startup Mentality
With 6 open roles at this stage, they are hiring people who will help shape the company. You should be comfortable picking up undefined problems, making decisions without a manager approving every step, and learning fast in an environment where the playbook is still being written.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparing well for Equixly gives you a real edge, because most applicants will not go this deep.

Know the Product
Read everything publicly available about how Equixly works. Understand their AI-driven approach to API security testing. If a free trial or demo is available, try it. Being able to say 'I tested your platform and noticed X' is far more impressive than a generic answer about your interest in security.

Study the OWASP API Security Top 10
This is the industry-standard reference for API vulnerabilities. Know each category, how to detect it, and how to remediate it. Interviewers at security companies frequently base technical questions on this list, so knowing it cold saves you in the room.

Build or Polish a Relevant Project
If you have a GitHub project involving API security, security automation, or AI-assisted testing, clean it up before you apply. If you do not have one, consider building a small API fuzzer or writing a technical breakdown of a vulnerability you analyzed. Concrete work beats claimed experience every time.

Practice Talking Through Security Scenarios
In interviews, security companies often ask you to 'walk me through how you would test this endpoint' or 'what would you do if you found an authentication bypass?' Practice narrating your thinking aloud. Be structured: describe what you observe, what you suspect, and how you would confirm it.

Research the Team
Look up the founders and early team members on LinkedIn. Understand their backgrounds and the problems they have worked on before. This helps you ask sharp questions in the final round and signals genuine interest rather than a copy-paste application.

Prepare Your 'Why Equixly' Answer
At a small company, motivation matters. Generic answers like 'I want to grow' will not land. Connect your specific career goals to what Equixly is building, whether that is a passion for API security, belief in AI-driven automation, or domain experience that directly applies to their customer base.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Equixly operates as a lean, mission-driven security startup. That shapes day-to-day culture in specific ways worth understanding before you join.

Security-First Thinking
The company exists to make software more secure, and that mindset runs through every role, not just engineering. You will be expected to think about risk, trust, and adversarial scenarios even if your job title is not 'security engineer.'

Startup Pace and Ownership
With a small team, there is no large support structure. People own problems end-to-end, from identifying an issue to shipping a fix. If you thrive on autonomy and get frustrated by slow processes, this environment will suit you. If you prefer well-defined roles and structured review cycles, a larger company might be a better fit right now.

Collaborative and Direct
Small teams communicate directly. Feedback is quick and frank, which can feel intense if you are used to corporate formality. For people who want to learn fast and ship quality work, that directness is a feature, not a friction point.

Mission Alignment
API security is increasingly critical as more of the digital economy runs on APIs. The team at Equixly tends to be motivated by the actual problem they are solving, not just the commercial opportunity. Shared belief in the mission makes a real difference in daily work at a company this size.
