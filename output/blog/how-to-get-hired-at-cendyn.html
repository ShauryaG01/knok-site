01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Cendyn is a global hospitality technology company, best known for its CRM, sales, and event management platforms built specifically for hotels and resorts. If you are targeting a role here, you are looking at a company that sits at the intersection of tech and travel, serving hotel chains and independent properties across the world.

As of July 2026, Cendyn has 24 open roles across functions including engineering, product management, customer success, sales, and professional services. India is a key delivery and technology hub for them, with Pune being a significant location, so a good share of those openings are accessible to Indian professionals.

This guide walks you through what the hiring process typically looks like, what Cendyn values in candidates, and how to put your best foot forward.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Cendyn's hiring process typically runs across four to five stages, though the exact structure can vary by team and seniority level. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Application and resume screen. Your resume goes through an initial review by the talent acquisition team. Roles in engineering and product tend to be competitive, so a clean, achievement-focused resume helps you get past this stage.

Recruiter call. If your profile is shortlisted, a recruiter or HR business partner usually reaches out for a short introductory call. This is mostly a fit check covering your notice period, location preference, broad experience, and compensation expectations.

Technical or skills round. For engineering roles, this is typically a coding or system design assessment, sometimes take-home, sometimes on a video call with a technical interviewer. For product or customer success roles, this stage may involve a case study or a written exercise tied to hospitality workflows.

Hiring manager interview. A deeper conversation with the team lead or department head. Expect questions about your past work, how you have handled difficult situations, and how your experience maps to what they are building.

Final round. Some candidates go through one more panel discussion or a cross-functional interview, particularly for senior or client-facing roles. Offer discussions typically happen shortly after.

The full process can stretch across two to four weeks depending on the role and team availability.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Cendyn hires people who combine solid technical or functional skills with a genuine interest in the hospitality industry. You do not need a hotel background, but curiosity about how hotels operate and how technology solves real problems for them goes a long way.

Across most roles, these qualities stand out:

Domain awareness. Candidates who understand hospitality workflows, even at a surface level, such as how hotels manage group bookings, loyalty programmes, or revenue management, tend to advance further in the process.

SaaS product experience. Cendyn sells B2B software to enterprise hotel clients. Experience working with or supporting SaaS products, especially in customer-facing roles, is valued across the board.

Communication skills. Many roles involve working with global hotel teams or US-based colleagues. Clear, professional written and spoken English is expected, and for customer-facing roles it is non-negotiable.

Problem-solving mindset. Whether you are in engineering or customer success, Cendyn wants people who diagnose problems rather than just escalate them. Show that you own outcomes, not just tasks.

Comfort with distributed teamwork. Their teams span India, the US, and Europe. Comfort with async communication tools and flexibility around overlapping hours is a practical requirement, not a nice-to-have.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A little upfront research can separate you from candidates who apply cold. Here is a practical prep plan:

Learn the core products. Cendyn's main platforms include their CRM, eInsight (email marketing), and Cendyn Proposals (event sales). Look up what each product does and who the end user is at a hotel property. Their public website has product overviews worth spending an hour on.

Understand the buyer. Cendyn sells to hotel revenue managers, sales directors, and marketing heads. Knowing what problems these personas face shows you have thought beyond the job description, and interviewers notice this.

Prepare STAR-format stories. For behavioural rounds, structure your answers as Situation, Task, Action, Result. Pick examples where your work had a measurable impact, for example a tool you built that reduced manual work, a customer issue you resolved, or a process you improved.

Brush up on relevant technical skills. For engineering roles, practise SQL, REST APIs, and any stack mentioned in the job description. For product roles, be ready to discuss prioritisation frameworks and how you gather customer requirements from non-technical stakeholders.

Prepare smart questions. Ask about the team structure, how product decisions are made, what the biggest challenge in the role is, and what success looks like in the first six months. These show genuine interest and help you evaluate the fit too.

Check open roles before your interview. With 24 openings currently listed, there may be adjacent roles that are a better fit than the one you applied to. Mentioning this awareness in your recruiter call can work in your favour.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Cendyn describes itself as customer-obsessed and innovation-driven, with a focus on helping hospitality businesses grow through smarter use of data. In practice, this translates into a culture where people are expected to understand the client's world, not just deliver a technical output.

A few things that characterise working at Cendyn:

Global and distributed teams. You will regularly work with colleagues and clients in different time zones. The culture leans on written communication, shared documentation, and structured check-ins rather than relying on everyone being in the same office.

Ownership mindset. Cendyn teams tend to value people who take end-to-end ownership of their work. In engineering, this might mean caring about the deployed product, not just the code you wrote. In customer success, it means following a problem through to resolution rather than handing it off.

Continuous learning. The hospitality tech space evolves fast, and Cendyn's product roadmap reflects that. Employees are expected to stay curious about both the technology and the industry trends shaping hotel operations globally.

Practical collaboration. The work culture is reportedly collaborative and relatively flat in structure for a company of their size. India-based employees often interact directly with global clients and US-based product teams, which means real exposure rather than siloed delivery work.

For Indian professionals, Cendyn offers a strong combination of enterprise SaaS experience and international client exposure, which can be a meaningful career accelerator for those targeting global product or customer success roles.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Cendyn hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Cendyn primarily hires experienced professionals for most of its open roles, especially in customer success, product, and senior engineering positions. However, they do occasionally open associate or entry-level positions for recent graduates, particularly in technical support and implementation roles. Checking their careers page regularly is the best way to catch these openings when they come up.

Where are Cendyn's India offices located?

Cendyn has a significant presence in Pune, which serves as one of their main technology and delivery hubs in India. Some roles may be listed as remote or hybrid, and the exact work arrangement can vary by team and function. It is worth confirming the location setup with the recruiter during the initial call so there are no surprises later.

What salary can I expect at Cendyn?

Cendyn does not publicly list compensation for most India-based roles. Figures shared on Glassdoor and publicly reported data suggest pay is broadly in line with other mid-sized SaaS companies operating out of India, though the exact range depends heavily on the function and years of experience. It is best to state your expectations clearly during the recruiter call rather than waiting until the offer stage.

How long does the Cendyn hiring process take?

Based on commonly cited candidate experiences, the full process from application to offer typically takes two to four weeks for most roles. Senior or specialised positions may take longer if multiple rounds or panel interviews are involved. If you have not heard back within two weeks of your last interview, a polite follow-up email to your recruiter contact is entirely appropriate.

Is a hospitality or hotel background required to work at Cendyn?

A formal hospitality background is not required for most roles. Cendyn hires engineers, product managers, and customer success professionals from a wide variety of industries. What matters more is a genuine willingness to learn how hotels operate and how Cendyn's products solve real problems for them, and candidates who demonstrate this curiosity during interviews consistently stand out.

What is the best way to apply to Cendyn?

The most direct route is applying through Cendyn's official careers page. LinkedIn is also widely used for their job listings and gives you visibility into mutual connections who might offer a referral, which can meaningfully improve your chances of getting an initial screen.
