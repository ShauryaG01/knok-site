03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at TELUS International typically runs across four to five stages, though the order and depth can vary by role. Here is what candidates commonly report.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screening
You apply online or through a job portal. Recruiters look at your communication skills on paper, relevant experience (or educational background for fresher roles), and whether you match the language or domain requirements of the job. A focused, readable resume with concrete examples of past work gets through faster than a generic one.

Stage 2: HR Screening Call
If shortlisted, a recruiter calls you for a short introductory conversation. They verify your basic details, ask about availability, expected salary, and shift flexibility. Be straightforward here. Shift readiness is a genuine filter at this stage, not just a formality. Trying to seem flexible when you are not creates problems later.

Stage 3: Skills Assessment
Many roles include an online assessment. Depending on the role, this can cover typing speed and accuracy, English grammar and comprehension, basic computer literacy, or domain knowledge. Customer-facing roles sometimes include a voice or accent round. This stage is a real filter, so prepare for it rather than walking in cold.

Stage 4: Technical or Domain Round
For IT helpdesk and technical support roles, you are tested on troubleshooting logic and basic technical concepts. For content moderation, you may be shown sample content and asked to make judgment calls. For AI data roles, the focus is on attention to detail and logical consistency. This round is less about memorized answers and more about showing clear, structured thinking.

Stage 5: Final HR or Operations Round
The last round is typically with an HR manager or operations lead. They assess your communication confidence, cultural fit, and long-term intent. Salary negotiation usually happens here. Offers for batch hiring roles commonly follow within a few days. Specialist or senior roles can take a bit longer.

Overall, the process from application to offer is commonly reported to take one to three weeks for standard roles, with specialist hiring running longer on the higher end of that range.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

TELUS International hires across a wide range of functions, so what they value shifts by team. But a few qualities come up across almost every opening.

Strong Communication Skills
This is non-negotiable, regardless of role. You need to express yourself clearly in English, both spoken and written. For some roles, fluency in a regional Indian language or another global language is a requirement, not just a bonus.

A Customer-First Mindset
TELUS International works with large consumer-facing brands. Interviewers want to see that you lean toward solving the person's problem rather than following a script mechanically. They test this through scenario-based questions where the 'right' answer involves empathy, not just process.

Attention to Detail
For content moderation, AI data labeling, and quality roles, this is the primary skill. You will make judgment calls on borderline cases regularly. Candidates who can walk through their reasoning clearly, not just give a verdict, stand out.

Technical Aptitude (for tech roles)
For helpdesk and IT support, basic networking concepts, OS familiarity, and logical troubleshooting matter. You do not need to be a developer. But you should be able to walk a non-technical user through a fix without confusing them further.

Shift Readiness
Many TELUS International operations support clients in North America or Europe, which means evening or night shifts for India-based staff. Candidates who are clear and realistic about their shift availability do better in HR rounds than those who say yes to everything and back out post-offer.

Stability and Intent
Attrition is a known challenge in the CX industry. Interviewers will probe why you want this specific role and what your plans look like. Honest, grounded answers carry more weight than rehearsed ones.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Focused preparation across each stage makes a real difference here.

Tailoring Your Resume
Highlight communication-heavy experience, any customer-facing work, and outcomes you can describe concretely. If you have handled complaints, resolved tickets, or managed any stakeholder interaction, lead with those. Avoid padding the resume with vague responsibilities. Specific situations and outcomes are more convincing than a list of duties.

For the Skills Assessment
If your role requires typing, practice using free online tools until your speed and accuracy are comfortable. For grammar rounds, revisit common errors: subject-verb agreement, tense shifts, and preposition use. For domain assessments, read the job description carefully and revise the specific concepts mentioned in it. The assessment is not designed to be tricky but it does filter out underprepared candidates.

For the Interview Rounds
Prepare for behavioral questions using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Common questions at TELUS International include: 'Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult customer,' 'How do you handle pressure during peak hours,' and 'Why do you want a role in customer experience.' Practice saying your answers aloud. Thinking through them is not the same as being able to articulate them clearly on a call.

For Technical Rounds
If you are going for an IT helpdesk role, revise common Windows and Mac troubleshooting steps, what DNS and DHCP do in plain English, and how to walk someone through a fix over a call or chat. Deep coding knowledge is not expected for most TELUS International tech support roles. Clear, patient explanations matter far more.

On Shift Discussions
Decide before your HR call exactly which shifts you can manage and for how long. Interviewers respect clarity here. If you can only do morning shifts for a specific reason, say so plainly. Vagueness tends to slow down or stall offers.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

TELUS International describes itself as a people-first company, with visible emphasis on diversity, inclusion, and community impact. The parent company, TELUS, runs a large social responsibility program globally, and that culture filters into how teams operate at TELUS International.

The Work Environment
India offices run in a structured environment with clear performance metrics, team leads, and regular feedback cycles. If you like knowing exactly what is expected of you and being measured against defined targets, this structure suits you well. If you prefer creative autonomy or loosely defined roles, the BPO-style setup can feel limiting.

Growth and Learning
TELUS International invests in internal training. Freshers typically go through a well-structured onboarding period, covering product knowledge and communication skills, before going live with customers. For experienced hires moving into senior roles, leadership development programs exist, though availability varies by location and team.

Team Culture
Day-to-day, teams are generally described as collaborative within the immediate group or shift. Because many employees handle similar work, there is a natural camaraderie at the pod level. Managers at the India offices are reported to be approachable, especially at the team lead level.

Inclusion and Diversity
TELUS International has publicly stated commitments to workplace inclusion and participates in community-building initiatives as part of their broader corporate values. For candidates who want to work somewhere that takes this seriously beyond just a policy statement, it is a meaningful differentiator relative to smaller employers.

Work-Life Balance
This varies most by role and shift. Day-shift employees generally report a manageable balance. Employees on night or rotating shifts often cite schedule unpredictability as the hardest part of the job. Before accepting an offer, ask specifically about the schedule for your team, not just the general policy. It is a fair question and a good sign if your interviewer answers it honestly.

Frequently asked

Is TELUS International good for freshers in India?

Yes, TELUS International regularly hires freshers for customer support, content moderation, and AI data roles. The onboarding structure is designed to bring candidates up to speed even without prior work experience. If you have strong communication skills and are open to shift-based work, your chances as a fresher are solid. Focus your resume on any internship, part-time work, or project that shows you can communicate clearly and handle tasks with care.

How long does the TELUS International hiring process take?

For batch hiring, which is common for customer support roles, the process from application to offer is commonly reported to take one to three weeks. Specialist or senior roles can run a bit longer. The skills assessment and HR round usually happen close together once you clear the initial screening. If you have not heard back after a reasonable gap following your last interview, sending a polite follow-up email to your recruiter is completely fine.

What salary can I expect at TELUS International in India?

Salary varies by role, level, and city. According to publicly reported data on platforms like Glassdoor, entry-level CX roles at comparable companies in India are commonly cited in the lower LPA range, while technical support and senior roles command noticeably higher packages. Check recent Glassdoor reviews for TELUS International India specifically before your HR round, since figures shift year to year. Going in with a number in mind, backed by current data, puts you in a better position to negotiate.

Do I need prior BPO experience to join TELUS International?

Not always. Many entry-level roles are open to freshers or candidates from non-BPO backgrounds, as long as communication skills are strong and domain requirements are met. For technical support, a relevant degree or certification can substitute for direct experience. For content moderation and AI data annotation roles, attention to detail and clear reasoning matter more than industry background. The job description usually signals how strict they are on prior experience.

Are night shifts mandatory at TELUS International?

It depends on the client and team you join. Many TELUS International clients are based in North America or Europe, so a significant share of India-based roles involve evening or night shifts. During the interview, ask specifically about the shift pattern for the team you are applying to. Some teams run fixed day shifts, others rotate on a cycle. Knowing this before you accept an offer saves a lot of friction later.
