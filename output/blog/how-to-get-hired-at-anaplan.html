01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Anaplan is a cloud-based connected planning platform used by large enterprises to coordinate finance, sales, supply chain, and HR planning in a single system. As of mid-2026, Anaplan has 209 open roles, which signals active hiring across multiple functions including software engineering, solutions consulting, customer success, sales, and corporate roles. India is a key hiring market for the company, with a significant presence in Bengaluru and other metro cities. If you are a mid-to-senior professional looking for a global product company with enterprise clients, Anaplan is worth targeting seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Anaplan's interview process typically runs across four to five stages, though the exact format can vary by role and seniority level.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. A recruiter or hiring coordinator reviews your resume for relevant experience. For tech roles, they look at your stack and product background. For consulting or customer success roles, the focus is on domain knowledge and client-facing experience.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. If shortlisted, a talent acquisition team member will contact you for a short introductory call. Expect questions about your background, current notice period, and salary expectations. This is also your chance to ask about the role scope and team structure.

Stage 3: Hiring manager conversation. This is a deeper discussion with the person you would report to. Tech candidates should expect questions about past projects and architecture thinking. Go-to-market candidates should prepare scenario-based answers about client handling and stakeholder management.

Stage 4: Technical or skills assessment. Depending on the role, you may receive a take-home assignment, a live coding round, or a case study presentation. Solutions consultants are often asked to prepare a mock demo or business case. Engineers typically work through algorithmic or system design problems.

Stage 5: Panel or cross-functional round. A small group of stakeholders, sometimes from different teams, will speak with you. This round evaluates your communication style, collaboration approach, and cultural alignment alongside technical depth.

Offer and background check. If all rounds go well, HR will share a verbal offer followed by a written letter. Anaplan typically runs a standard employment background check before the formal offer is finalised.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Anaplan looks for people who can operate in a fast-moving, client-facing environment and who genuinely enjoy working through complex data and planning problems.

Domain expertise. For solutions consulting and customer success roles, prior exposure to enterprise planning, ERP systems, or financial modelling gives you a clear advantage. For engineering, experience with scalable SaaS product development is highly valued.

Clear communication. Anaplan's platform is sold to and used by business leaders, not only IT teams. Candidates who can translate technical concepts into plain business language consistently stand out.

Ownership mindset. Interviewers look for evidence that you have taken initiative without being asked, driven projects through ambiguity, or resolved problems outside your direct remit. Prepare concrete examples of this.

Cross-functional collaboration. Many Anaplan roles sit at the intersection of technology and business. Showing that you can manage expectations across clients, product teams, and leadership is a strong signal.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product first. Before any interview, spend time learning what Anaplan actually does. Read publicly available case studies and watch product demos to build familiarity. Candidates who can speak fluently about connected planning leave a stronger impression.

Prepare structured stories. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. Anaplan's interviews rely heavily on past behaviour as a predictor of future performance, so vague or generic answers will hurt you.

For engineering candidates. Brush up on data structures, system design, and the languages listed in the job description. Review concepts around multi-tenant SaaS architecture if you are applying for back-end or platform roles.

For consulting and customer success candidates. Be ready to walk through a past implementation project end to end. Know the metrics by which your past work was measured. If you have experience with planning tools such as Hyperion, SAP BPC, or Adaptive Insights, mention that clearly.

Follow up after each round. A short thank-you note to the recruiter after interviews is a step many candidates skip. It keeps you visible and signals genuine interest.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Anaplan describes its culture as curious, collaborative, and action-oriented. Employees across publicly available reviews commonly describe the environment as energetic and performance-driven, with a strong focus on customer outcomes.

The Bengaluru office gives Indian employees meaningful global exposure, and many employees publicly report appreciation for the hybrid work flexibility. That said, client-facing and delivery roles can be demanding during project cycles, so this is not a slow-paced environment.

Career growth within Anaplan tends to reward people who build deep expertise in the platform itself. Internal certifications and participation in the Anaplan Community are visible signals of commitment that managers actively notice and support.

If you prefer a structured, hierarchical organisation with clear role boundaries, you may find the pace challenging. If you thrive on complexity, client interaction, and continuous learning, the culture is likely to suit you well.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does Anaplan's hiring process take from application to offer?

The exact timeline varies by role and team, but candidates commonly report the full process from first recruiter contact to a verbal offer takes a few weeks. Consulting and senior-level roles may take longer due to additional panel or stakeholder rounds. Staying in regular contact with the recruiter after each stage helps prevent unnecessary delays.

Does Anaplan hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Anaplan's open roles in India are weighted toward mid-to-senior experience, particularly in solutions consulting and engineering. The company does run early-careers programmes for select functions, but these are less common. With 209 open roles currently listed, it is worth checking current postings carefully since requirements vary widely across functions.

What salary can I expect at Anaplan in India?

Compensation depends on role, level, and location. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported salary figures for Anaplan India roles, and industry surveys suggest engineering and consulting pay is competitive with other global SaaS companies of similar scale. Always cross-reference those sources before deciding whether an offer is fair, and do not hesitate to negotiate.

Is the Anaplan Model Builder certification useful before applying?

Yes, especially for solutions consulting, customer success, and implementation roles. A Model Builder or Solutions Architect certification signals genuine product commitment and sets you apart from candidates with only generic planning tool experience. You can pursue these certifications independently before you even apply, and they are worth the effort if you are serious about building a career at Anaplan.

What is the work-from-home policy at Anaplan India?

Anaplan has adopted a hybrid model across most of its India offices, with some roles being fully remote depending on the function. Publicly available employee reviews suggest reasonable flexibility, though consulting and customer-facing roles may require travel or on-site presence during project delivery phases. Confirm the specific arrangement with the recruiter during your first call.

How competitive is it to get a role at Anaplan?

With 209 open roles currently listed, Anaplan is actively recruiting across multiple functions, not just maintaining a passive pipeline. That said, consulting and senior engineering roles attract candidates with strong enterprise SaaS or planning domain backgrounds, so the bar is real. A resume that speaks directly to Anaplan's platform and client use cases will outperform a generic application by a meaningful margin.
