01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

FPT Asia Pacific Pte Ltd is the regional arm of FPT Corporation, one of Asia's largest IT services and technology groups. The company delivers software development, digital transformation, cloud migration, and AI-powered solutions to enterprise clients across the Asia Pacific region. Headquartered in Singapore, FPT Asia Pacific bridges FPT Group's global delivery capacity with clients in sectors like banking, manufacturing, retail, and telecommunications.

As of July 2026, FPT Asia Pacific has around 30 open roles. That is a meaningful pipeline of active positions spanning software engineering, project and delivery management, business development, pre-sales, and IT consulting. The company hires both fresh talent and experienced professionals, making it a realistic target for Indian candidates at different career stages.

For Indian professionals in particular, FPT Asia Pacific is worth a close look. The company's delivery model involves coordinating between offshore development centres and regional enterprise clients, a working style that many Indian engineers and project managers already know well. Cross-cultural communication and multi-time-zone delivery experience, which Indian IT professionals often bring naturally, are genuinely valued here.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at FPT Asia Pacific typically runs through three to four stages. The exact format varies by role and seniority, but the pattern below reflects what candidates commonly go through.

Application and screening. Your resume is reviewed by the recruiter or hiring manager for how well it matches the role requirements. A clear alignment between your stated skills and the job description, especially around your technical stack or domain expertise, is what gets you to the next stage. Tailor your resume before applying.

Recruiter call. This is a short conversation covering your background, current role, notice period, and salary expectations. The recruiter also uses this call to walk you through the role in more detail. Be ready to explain why you are interested in FPT's work in the region and what you are looking for in your next move.

Technical or functional interview. For software engineering roles, expect a coding round or system design discussion, usually with a senior engineer or technical lead. For delivery, consulting, or client-facing roles, expect scenario-based questions about managing client escalations, scope changes, or cross-team coordination. Panels at this stage typically include the hiring manager and one or two team members.

Final interview. A conversation with a senior stakeholder or regional head, focused on culture fit, your longer-term career direction, and your understanding of FPT Asia Pacific's market position. Some roles compress the final two stages into a single panel interview, so ask the recruiter about the format early so you can prepare accordingly. An offer discussion often follows quickly after this stage if things go well.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

FPT Asia Pacific looks for a mix of technical strength and the ability to work effectively across cultures and geographies. A few qualities stand out across most roles.

Technical or domain expertise. For engineering roles, depth in relevant languages, frameworks, or cloud platforms is the baseline. For consulting and delivery roles, hands-on experience with enterprise software delivery or a specific vertical like banking or manufacturing carries weight.

Cross-cultural communication. FPT's model involves teams spread across Vietnam, Singapore, Japan, and other markets. Candidates who communicate clearly with diverse teams and adapt naturally to different working styles are noticed. Indian professionals with global or offshore delivery experience already have this in their toolkit.

Client orientation. Whether you are an engineer or a project manager, FPT Asia Pacific values people who stay focused on what the client actually needs, not just what the task document says. Comfort with client-facing communication, even in technical roles, is a distinct plus.

Problem-solving in ambiguous situations. The company works on complex, multi-year digital transformation programmes. The ability to work out the right approach when requirements are incomplete or shifting is valued more than rigid process adherence.

Language flexibility. English fluency is a baseline for almost all roles. Working knowledge of other regional languages, especially Japanese or Mandarin, can open additional opportunities within the company, though it is not a hard requirement for most positions.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparation for FPT Asia Pacific interviews does not need to be complicated, but it should be specific to the company and the role.

Research FPT's regional work. Get familiar with the sectors FPT Asia Pacific serves and the kind of digital transformation engagements they lead in the region. Being able to speak to why their specific work interests you, rather than giving a generic 'I want to grow in tech' answer, lands much better in the recruiter call and the final interview.

Tailor your resume to the role. FPT Asia Pacific posts roles with specific technical or domain requirements. Map your resume directly to those requirements. If a role calls for Agile delivery experience and cloud platform knowledge, those skills should appear clearly and early in your resume, not buried at the bottom.

Prepare for scenario-based questions. For delivery and consulting roles, think through two or three real situations from your past where you handled a client escalation, a scope change, or a team coordination challenge. Structure each example around what the situation was, what you did, and what resulted. Concrete outcomes make your answers credible.

Brush up on system design if you are in engineering. Mid-to-senior engineering roles at IT services companies commonly involve system design discussions covering scalability, API design, data flow, and trade-offs. Even if your coding is sharp, do not skip this preparation.

Ask good questions. Show that you have thought about the role beyond just getting an offer. Ask about the team structure, how client engagement works, or what growth looks like within FPT Asia Pacific's regional expansion. Prepared questions signal seriousness.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

FPT Asia Pacific operates as part of a Vietnamese-origin technology group with a strong emphasis on delivery, growth, and long-term client relationships. A few aspects of the culture are worth understanding before you join.

Delivery focus. At its core, FPT Asia Pacific is an IT services and outsourcing company. Teams are measured on client outcomes and delivery quality. If you perform well in environments with clear goals and direct client accountability, you will likely find the pace motivating.

Collaboration across borders. The work involves regular coordination with colleagues across different countries and time zones. The internal culture encourages knowledge sharing and cross-team support, but you need to be comfortable with asynchronous communication and working alongside people from different professional backgrounds.

Room to grow. FPT as a group has expanded rapidly across Asia and into global markets. Employees commonly describe an environment where taking initiative is noticed and where you can take on more responsibility as you prove yourself. For Indian professionals looking to build regional or international exposure, that kind of environment can move your career forward meaningfully.

Respect for process. The company has structured approaches to delivery, client reporting, and escalation management. There is room for creativity within those frameworks, but delivery discipline is non-negotiable. Coming in willing to learn and work within the existing structure helps new joiners earn trust faster.

Investment in skills. FPT supports internal training and certification, particularly around emerging technologies like cloud and AI. If you want to build new capabilities while working on live client projects, the company offers that combination.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does FPT Asia Pacific currently have?

As of July 2026, FPT Asia Pacific has around 30 open roles. This reflects active hiring across technical, delivery, and business functions rather than a single large recruitment push. Roles are added on a rolling basis, so it is worth checking back regularly if the specific position you want is not listed yet.

Does FPT Asia Pacific hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

FPT Asia Pacific hires across experience levels, though many of the current open roles target professionals with prior work experience in IT services, consulting, or specific technology domains. Fresh graduates with strong technical skills or relevant internship experience are considered for entry-level engineering or analyst positions. If you are a fresher, focus on roles that explicitly mention 'entry level' or 'associate' in the title.

What salary can I expect at FPT Asia Pacific?

Publicly reported salary data for FPT Asia Pacific is limited given the company's size in the region. Glassdoor listings commonly cite compensation in line with mid-tier IT services companies operating in the Singapore and Southeast Asia market. For numbers specific to your role and seniority, the best approach is to ask the recruiter directly during the first call. Having a clear sense of your own expectations going in makes that conversation much easier.

Is there a coding test during the interview process?

For software engineering roles, a coding round or technical assessment is a common part of the process at FPT Asia Pacific. The format typically involves algorithm or data structure problems, sometimes followed by a system design discussion for senior positions. Non-engineering roles such as project management or business development do not usually include a coding test, though scenario-based problem solving is common across most functions.

How long does the FPT Asia Pacific hiring process typically take?

Based on commonly reported candidate experiences, the process spans a few weeks from application to offer. The timeline can be shorter for urgent or senior roles and longer if multiple stakeholder approvals are needed. Following up with the recruiter after each stage to confirm next steps is a practical way to keep the process moving and stay top of mind.

Can I apply from India for roles at FPT Asia Pacific?

Yes, FPT Asia Pacific considers applications from candidates based in India, particularly for roles that involve offshore coordination or remote delivery functions. For Singapore-based on-site positions, the company typically expects candidates to already hold the right to work in Singapore or be open to relocation. Clarify the work authorisation requirement with the recruiter before investing heavily in the interview process.
