01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Capri Global Capital is a fast-growing Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) headquartered in Mumbai, focused on MSME lending, affordable home loans, and construction finance. The company serves underserved borrowers across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities, which means it hires heavily across field sales, credit underwriting, collections, and branch operations.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs in three to four stages, though the exact flow varies by role and location.

Application and Resume Shortlisting
Your resume is screened for relevant experience in NBFC, banking, or financial services. For sales roles, prior field experience and geographic familiarity matter. For credit roles, loan appraisal or underwriting exposure is key. Make sure your resume clearly calls out the loan products you have handled and the client segments you served.

HR Screening Call
A recruiter or HR executive reaches out for a brief call. Expect questions about your current role, notice period, salary expectations, and willingness to travel or relocate. This round is a fit check, not a technical assessment.

Functional or Technical Interview
Depending on the role, you may speak with a branch manager, zonal head, or credit manager. For sales roles, expect scenario questions around lead generation, customer handling, and meeting targets. For credit roles, expect questions on financial statement reading, KYC norms, and RBI guidelines. Bring specific examples from past loan cases if you have them.

Senior Leadership or Panel Round
For mid to senior profiles, a final round with a regional head or senior management is common. This round tests culture fit, stability (why you are leaving, long-term goals), and your grasp of the NBFC lending space.

Offer and Onboarding
Offers typically include a fixed component and a variable linked to targets, especially for sales roles. Background verification is standard. Based on publicly reported candidate timelines, the process from application to offer can take two to four weeks.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Capri Global Capital looks for a mix of domain knowledge, hustle, and stability. Here is what tends to matter across roles.

Sales and Relationship Manager Roles
Prior experience in home loan, LAP (loan against property), or MSME lending is a strong advantage. They want people who can source customers independently, not just manage inbound leads. Knowledge of the local market and comfort with field work is essential.

Credit and Underwriting Roles
Strong grasp of income assessment for salaried and self-employed borrowers, familiarity with CIBIL and bureau reports, and accuracy in financial analysis. Prior exposure to MSME or housing loan ticket sizes is valued.

Operations and Branch Roles
Attention to detail, knowledge of loan disbursement processes, and ability to work within TAT (turnaround time) commitments.

Across All Functions
Communication clarity, a stable work history (frequent job changes can raise flags in NBFC hiring), and willingness to be based in the assigned geography. Given their Tier 2 expansion, comfort working outside metro cities is often a plus.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Getting ready for a Capri Global Capital interview is straightforward if you focus on the right areas.

Know Their Business
Capri Global Capital operates across MSME loans, affordable housing loans, and construction finance. Understand the basic difference between these products, their typical borrower profiles, and how underwriting differs across them. This signals genuine interest, not just a job hunt.

Brush Up on Regulation Basics
RBI guidelines on NBFCs, the Fair Practices Code, and NPA (non-performing asset) norms come up in credit and compliance interviews. You do not need to memorise policy documents, but knowing the key terms and their implications is enough.

Prepare Your Numbers
For any sales or credit role, be ready to state your past portfolio size, number of cases processed monthly, NPA levels in your portfolio, and your average ticket size. Interviewers at NBFCs respond well to candidates who can quantify their work.

Think Through Field Scenarios
Questions like 'How would you source a new customer in a market where you have no contacts?' or 'How do you handle a borrower whose income documents are incomplete?' are common. Prepare two to three real examples from your past.

Research the Geography
If the role is tied to a specific city or region, know the local market dynamics, key employer segments, and the competitive lenders operating there.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Capri Global Capital has the energy of a company that is still building. It is not a large PSU bank with fixed hierarchies. Decisions often move faster, and individuals tend to get more ownership over their accounts or regions compared to larger institutions.

The culture is field-heavy on the business side. Relationship managers are expected to be visible with customers and channel partners, not desk-bound. There is a clear performance orientation, particularly in sales roles, where variable pay is a real part of the compensation package.

On the credit and operations side, the culture emphasises accuracy and process discipline, which is typical of regulated lending businesses. Compliance with RBI norms is taken seriously at every level.

The company has been expanding into smaller cities, so there is a culture of building from the ground up in newer markets. If you enjoy being among the early team members in a city or branch, this environment can be rewarding. For those who prefer the stability and structure of a large bank, the pace may feel different at first.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many roles are currently open at Capri Global Capital?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar shows 17 open positions at Capri Global Capital. Roles span sales, credit, operations, and support functions. The number changes as positions fill and new ones open, so it is worth checking regularly if you are actively applying.

Is prior NBFC experience mandatory to get hired?

Not always. For credit and senior sales roles, NBFC or banking experience is a strong plus, but candidates from insurance, microfinance, or other financial services sometimes make the transition successfully. Entry-level roles may also hire fresh MBA graduates, particularly for sales trainee programmes. Tailor your resume to show transferable skills like income assessment, customer acquisition, or portfolio management.

What does the salary structure look like at Capri Global Capital?

Glassdoor and publicly reported data suggest compensation varies by role, seniority, and location. Sales roles typically follow a fixed plus variable structure, where the variable component is tied to disbursement or sourcing targets. It is best to check recent Glassdoor listings for role-specific figures and confirm the fixed-variable split with the recruiter during the HR screening call.

How long does the hiring process usually take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process typically takes two to four weeks from application to offer. Senior roles may take longer depending on panel availability and internal approvals. Following up politely after each round can help keep things moving, especially for branch-level roles where local managers handle scheduling.

Does Capri Global Capital offer remote or hybrid work?

Most roles, especially in sales and branch operations, are on-site and often field-based. Technology and certain corporate roles at the Mumbai head office may offer more flexibility. Confirm the work arrangement during the HR screening call before the process goes too far, since field mobility is a core expectation for a large share of their openings.

What should I highlight in my resume to get shortlisted?

Quantify your work clearly: loan portfolio size, number of cases handled per month, NPA levels, ticket sizes, and any targets you met or exceeded. For credit roles, specify the borrower types and income segments you assessed. A resume that mirrors the job description language and leads with numbers performs better in NBFC hiring than one that only lists responsibilities.
