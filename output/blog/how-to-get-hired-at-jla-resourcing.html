01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

JLA Resourcing is a recruitment and staffing company, meaning they match candidates with roles across various industries rather than being tied to one sector. Because JLA is in the business of hiring others, they tend to run a sharp, no-nonsense process. They know what a good interview looks like from the inside, so they hold their own candidates to a similarly high standard. Roles at a resourcing firm typically span recruitment consulting, talent acquisition, client servicing, business development, and back-office support. 03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Most candidates at staffing firms go through two to three rounds, though the exact number can vary by seniority and function. Below is a commonly seen structure, hedged because JLA has not published a fixed process publicly.

Round 1: Recruiter or HR Screen
This is usually a short phone or video call. The goal is to check basic fit: why you want to work in recruitment (or the specific function you are applying for), your current notice period, and salary expectations. Be ready to talk clearly and concisely about your most recent role.

Round 2: Hiring Manager or Panel Interview
This round goes deeper. For recruitment roles, expect questions about how you source candidates, how you handle a difficult client, and how you manage targets or deadlines. For non-recruiting roles, expect competency-based questions relevant to your function. Some firms at this stage use a short case study or a practical task, for example reviewing a job description and writing a sourcing plan.

Round 3: Final or Leadership Round (for senior roles)
Senior hires often meet a director or business head. This round usually covers your long-term career goals, leadership style, and how you would contribute to the team beyond your immediate KPIs. Not every role will have this round.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Communication that is clear and direct. Recruitment is a people-facing job. Whether you are a consultant, an account manager, or in operations, you will be expected to communicate crisply, follow up without being prompted, and represent the firm well to clients and candidates alike.

Target orientation. Staffing companies run on numbers: roles filled, time-to-hire, client satisfaction scores. If you have hit targets in a previous job, whether in sales, recruitment, or operations, talk about it with specifics. Even if the exact numbers are modest, showing that you tracked and owned them matters.

Problem-solving under pressure. Resourcing work involves tight deadlines and last-minute changes. Interviewers will probe whether you stay calm when a candidate drops out at the offer stage or a client changes requirements overnight.

Adaptability across roles and sectors. Because JLA works across industries, generalist skills travel well here. Showing that you can quickly understand a new domain or switch between client accounts without losing momentum will be valued.

Ownership and follow-through. A common red flag in recruitment firms is candidates who wait for instructions. They want to see initiative: did you follow up, did you close the loop, did you solve the problem without being told?

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research what resourcing firms actually do. If this is your first time applying to a staffing company, spend time understanding the business model. They earn by placing candidates with client firms. Understanding this helps you answer 'why JLA' in a way that sounds genuine, not generic.

Prepare two or three strong examples from your past work. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. Pick examples that show you handled pressure, delivered results, or worked well with different kinds of people. Concrete stories always land better than vague claims.

Practise talking about your achievements in measurable terms. Even if your past targets were in a different field, frame your impact as clearly as you can. Interviewers at resourcing firms respond well to candidates who can quantify what they delivered.

Have clear answers ready for the basics. Why do you want to work in recruitment or in this function? What is your notice period? What are your salary expectations? These will come up early, and vague answers create a poor first impression.

Set up professionally for video calls. Many first rounds happen on video. A clean background, good lighting, and stable internet go a long way in making a strong initial impression before you even say a word.

Send a short follow-up note after each round. Recruiters notice this more than most hiring managers do. A brief, warm email thanking the interviewer and reaffirming your interest takes two minutes and sets you apart from most candidates.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Recruitment firms typically run fast-paced, target-driven environments. JLA Resourcing, being in the staffing space, likely carries that energy: results matter, relationships matter, and consistency matters. If you thrive when your effort is directly visible in outcomes, this kind of culture can be genuinely rewarding.

Within resourcing companies, team culture often splits between 'hunter' personalities who enjoy building new client relationships and 'farmer' personalities who prefer managing and deepening existing accounts. Both are usually valued, but it helps to know which type you are before you walk into the interview room.

Staffing firms tend to emphasise integrity in how they represent candidates and honesty with clients about realistic timelines and fit. If you have examples from your past where you chose to do the right thing over the convenient thing in a people-facing situation, those stories resonate well in this environment.

Growth in resourcing firms is often tied directly to performance rather than tenure. Promotions and expanded responsibilities tend to follow those who consistently deliver, which can be motivating if you prefer clear feedback and visible outcomes over slow, seniority-based ladders.

Frequently asked

How many open roles does JLA Resourcing currently have?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 20 open positions at JLA Resourcing. This is an active count, which suggests the company is in a growth or backfill phase. The actual number may shift as roles are filled or new ones open, so it is worth checking regularly.

Do I need prior recruitment experience to apply at JLA Resourcing?

It depends on the specific role you are targeting. Senior recruitment consultant positions will almost certainly require prior experience in talent acquisition or staffing. However, many resourcing firms also hire freshers or career-changers into junior roles and train them internally. Check each job description carefully and apply even if you are slightly below the stated experience, especially if your communication and people skills are strong.

What salary can I expect at JLA Resourcing?

JLA Resourcing has not published a fixed pay scale publicly, and compensation at staffing firms often includes a base plus a variable component tied to targets filled or billed. For benchmarking, check Glassdoor or AmbitionBox for recruiter and staffing roles in your city, as publicly reported ranges vary widely by function and seniority. Going into the interview with a clear expectation in mind, rather than leaving it open-ended, will help you negotiate more confidently.

How long does the interview process usually take from application to offer?

Recruitment firms tend to move faster than corporate companies since hiring is literally their core business. A two-to-three week turnaround from application to offer is commonly cited for staffing roles, though this depends on the seniority of the role and whether the panel is available. Following up politely after each round is a good way to signal interest and keep the process from stalling.

Should I apply even if my background is in a completely different industry?

Yes, especially for roles in talent acquisition, account management, or operations support. Resourcing firms value transferable skills: clear communication, target ownership, and the ability to manage multiple stakeholders at once. If your past industry gave you domain knowledge that JLA's clients care about, that can actually be a bonus since you will better understand the roles you are helping fill.
