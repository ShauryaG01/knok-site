01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Fastly builds the edge cloud infrastructure that keeps the internet fast, secure, and reliable. Its platform handles content delivery, DDoS protection, edge computing, and real-time observability for some of the world's most-visited websites and apps. As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracks 54 open roles at Fastly, spread across engineering, product, sales, solutions, and operations functions.

Fastly is a mid-sized global company with a distributed workforce. It is not a high-volume recruiter, which means each role draws a focused, competitive applicant pool. The upside is that hiring managers tend to be genuinely involved in the process, and successful candidates often describe the experience as substantive rather than purely transactional.

India-based candidates find the most traction in software engineering, technical account management, and solutions engineering roles, though availability shifts with each hiring cycle. If a Fastly role aligns with your background, it is worth keeping a close eye on their careers page.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Fastly's hiring process is thorough and typically covers four to five stages. The structure varies by role and seniority, but here is what candidates commonly report:

StageWhat to Expect
Application ScreenResume reviewed for role fit. Keywords around networking, CDN, distributed systems, or your relevant domain help you stand out.
Recruiter CallA short introductory call to discuss your background, motivations, and logistics like location, visa status, and notice period.
Technical ScreenA coding assessment or short live session for engineering roles. Expect data structures, algorithms, and sometimes a basic system design question.
Technical PanelOne to three rounds with engineers or the hiring team. Senior roles tend to include a dedicated system design round with deeper architecture questions.
Hiring Manager RoundA conversation focused on your experience, how you work through ambiguity, and your fit with the team. Behavioural questions are standard here.

For non-engineering roles such as sales, solutions engineering, or customer success, the process follows a similar structure but replaces coding rounds with scenario-based interviews. You may be asked how you would handle a difficult customer situation, explain a technical concept to a non-technical stakeholder, or walk through a past deal or project.

Timelines vary, but the full process commonly takes a few weeks from first contact to offer. Delays tend to happen between the technical panel and the final round, especially when multiple interviewers need to align on feedback. Staying in light contact with your recruiter during that gap is perfectly reasonable.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Fastly builds products that run at the edge of the internet, where performance and reliability are non-negotiable. The traits that hiring teams look for reflect this environment.

Technical depth over surface-level experience. For engineering roles, Fastly values candidates who understand why things work the way they do, not just candidates who have used popular cloud tools. If you can explain the trade-offs behind a caching strategy or describe how protocol behaviour affects latency, that signals the kind of thinking they want.

Ownership and initiative. Fastly teams are distributed and autonomous. Interviewers look for evidence that you have driven outcomes without being told exactly what to do. Examples of problems you identified and fixed on your own, not just tasks you were assigned, carry real weight in these conversations.

Customer focus. Fastly's business depends on its customers trusting the platform with their most critical traffic. Across engineering, sales, and support roles, candidates who demonstrate genuine care about the end customer experience consistently get positive signals.

Clear communication. In a remote-first, globally distributed company, the ability to write clearly and explain complex ideas without jargon is a practical skill, not a soft one. For technical roles this shows up in how you walk through your reasoning during interviews. For customer-facing roles it is central to the job itself.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with Fastly's own resources. Their developer documentation and engineering blog are detailed and public. Reading these before your interviews helps you pick up the vocabulary the team uses daily and shows genuine interest in the product area, not just the job title.

For engineering roles, sharpen the fundamentals. Data structures and algorithms appear in early rounds. For senior roles, the system design round is often where hiring decisions are made. Practice designing systems that prioritise high availability, low latency, and graceful failure. Scenarios involving caching layers, CDN architecture, and real-time traffic routing are directly relevant to what Fastly builds.

Prepare strong behavioural answers. Fastly interviewers commonly ask for specific examples from your past work: a time you handled a production incident, a disagreement you had with a teammate, or a project where the requirements changed mid-way. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and keep answers concrete. Vague answers tend not to land well here.

Know the domain basics even for non-engineering roles. If you are applying for solutions engineering, technical account management, or sales roles, understanding what a CDN does, how caching works at a high level, and why latency matters to businesses makes a visible difference. A few hours of reading puts you ahead of most candidates at this stage.

Come with sharp questions. Fastly interviewers tend to notice the quality of what you ask. Questions about how the team measures success, what a strong first quarter in the role looks like, or how cross-functional collaboration works signal that you are thinking like someone ready to contribute, not just someone looking for any open seat.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Fastly describes its culture as one built around speed, transparency, and a shared commitment to keeping the internet open and fast. From what is publicly reported, the working environment is engineering-led, globally distributed, and expects a high degree of self-direction.

Because teams are spread across time zones, written communication matters more than it does in a traditional office setup. Candidates who are comfortable working asynchronously, documenting their thinking clearly, and making decisions without constant check-ins tend to do well here.

The culture is generally described as low-ego and collaborative. Interviewers at Fastly are commonly noted for being respectful and curious rather than adversarial during technical discussions. Asking a clarifying question is seen as engaged thinking, not a sign of weakness.

Fastly also takes its role in the broader internet ecosystem seriously. Publicly reported commitments around diversity, inclusion, and responsible infrastructure use are part of how the company presents itself externally. Candidates who care about the social impact of technology tend to find this resonates with the team's actual values.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Fastly hire in India?

Fastly does hire internationally and has posted India-based roles in past hiring cycles, particularly in engineering and technical functions. As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracks 54 open roles at Fastly globally, and availability of India-specific positions depends on the current cycle. Checking Fastly's careers page regularly or setting up a job alert is the most reliable way to catch openings as they appear.

How long does the Fastly interview process take?

The full process commonly takes a few weeks from your first recruiter call to a final offer, based on what candidates publicly report. The gap between technical panel rounds and the final decision tends to be where timelines stretch, often because multiple interviewers need to align on feedback. Keeping in light contact with your recruiter during this phase is a good way to stay informed without coming across as impatient.

What salary can I expect at Fastly in India?

Fastly does not publish India-specific salary bands publicly. Based on what is commonly cited for similar roles at global infrastructure companies operating in India, compensation tends to be competitive for the CDN and edge tech space. For specific figures, Glassdoor and levels.fyi are the most useful sources, as they include data shared directly by current and former employees.

Is a coding test required for all Fastly roles?

Coding assessments are standard for software engineering roles but are generally not part of the process for sales, marketing, operations, or account management positions. For solutions engineering roles, the technical component tends to focus more on explaining concepts and handling customer scenarios than on writing code. Ask your recruiter early in the process what the assessment format looks like for your specific role.

What should I focus on for the Fastly system design round?

For senior engineering roles, system design at Fastly tends to focus on distributed systems with a real-world flavour: caching architectures, high-throughput content delivery, fault-tolerant services, and low-latency data pipelines. Interviewers commonly look for how you reason through trade-offs rather than whether you arrive at a single correct answer. Practising designs for CDN-style systems, URL shorteners, and real-time event processing builds the right kind of thinking for this round.

Can I apply to Fastly without CDN or networking experience?

Yes, particularly for roles in software engineering, product, or business functions where your core domain expertise matters more than CDN-specific knowledge. That said, showing some curiosity about how edge networks and content delivery work does strengthen your application for most roles. Reading through Fastly's developer blog or documentation before interviews is a practical way to pick up the context that helps you connect your existing experience to what the company actually does.
