05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Certifications that get attention. AWS, Azure, or GCP certifications are the fastest credibility signal for a recruiter scanning your resume. Add Kubernetes (CKA or CKAD) if you have it. For roles at security-focused companies like Zscaler, a cloud security specialty certification can be a real differentiator.

Show infrastructure-as-code work in your portfolio. Terraform and Ansible experience should be visible in your GitHub or project descriptions, not just listed under 'skills'. Even a personal project automating a simple cloud setup demonstrates genuine hands-on ability.

Quantify your pipeline improvements. Saying you 'improved deployment frequency' is vague. Describing how you cut a weekly release cycle to daily by building a proper CI/CD pipeline is concrete and memorable for an interviewer.

Highlight cloud cost work. Many teams are under pressure to reduce cloud spend. If you have experience with cost optimisation, rightsizing, or tagging strategies, call it out explicitly. Most candidates skip this and it stands out.

Tailor your resume to the company type. For a startup like Tessell, lean into ownership and breadth. For Oracle or State Street, emphasise compliance, reliability, and process rigour.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply to fresh listings first. Roles posted recently get more recruiter attention than ones that have been sitting for weeks. Check for new listings regularly and prioritise the newest ones.

Reach out directly on LinkedIn. Many DevOps roles are filled partly through networking. A short message to the hiring manager mentioning your specific experience with their tech stack (for example, Kubernetes for a cloud-native team) often gets a faster response than applying through the portal alone.

Do not overlook smaller companies. Ansrsource, Tessell, and StarRez have fewer applicants per role than Oracle or Wipro. The match rate is often higher and the process moves faster.

Run parallel applications. With 28 openings across multiple companies in Hyderabad right now, applying to several roles at once keeps your options open and gives you negotiating room at the offer stage.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hyderabad a good city for DevOps Engineers compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has much higher volume, with 187 DevOps openings compared to Hyderabad's 28 at the same point in time. However, Hyderabad offers less competition per role and a solid mix of product companies, global capability centres, and fintech firms. If you are based in Hyderabad or prefer the city for personal reasons, there is genuine demand here and you do not need to relocate to build a strong DevOps career.

Which companies are actively hiring DevOps Engineers in Hyderabad right now?

Based on current knok jobradar data, Zeta leads with 6 openings. Zscaler, Tessell, Oracle, and Ansrsource each have 2 openings. Other active hirers include highradius, Wipro, Warner Bros Discovery, State Street, and StarRez. The mix spans fintech, cloud security, enterprise software, and media companies, so there is variety in the type of work on offer.

What salary can I expect as a mid-level DevOps Engineer in Hyderabad?

For 3-5 years of experience, the typical range is 15-28 LPA based on current market data. Where you land in that range depends on your cloud platform expertise, certifications, and the type of company. Product companies and global firms tend to offer more than IT services companies, and strong Kubernetes or Terraform skills can push you toward the higher end of the band.

Do I need certifications to get a DevOps job in Hyderabad?

Certifications are not strictly required but they are a strong signal, especially for companies like Zscaler where cloud security expertise matters. An AWS, Azure, or GCP certification is the most commonly requested credential. A Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) certification helps for product company roles. If you have solid hands-on experience and a visible GitHub portfolio, you can compete without certifications, but having at least one speeds up initial screening considerably.

What skills are most in demand for DevOps roles in Hyderabad?

The most commonly requested skills across Hyderabad DevOps openings include Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, and CI/CD tools like Jenkins or GitLab CI, alongside at least one major cloud platform. Scripting in Python or Bash is expected at most companies. Monitoring tools like Prometheus and Grafana appear frequently in job descriptions, and DevSecOps experience is a growing requirement, particularly at security-focused companies.

Should I apply to multiple companies at the same time?

Yes, applying to several companies simultaneously is the right approach. DevOps hiring typically involves multiple interview rounds spread over several weeks, and companies often make offers while you are still mid-process elsewhere. Running several active applications at once puts you in a stronger negotiating position and protects you if one opportunity falls through late in the process.
