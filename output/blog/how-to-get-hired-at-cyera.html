01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Cyera is a cloud data security company focused on helping large enterprises discover, classify, and protect sensitive data wherever it lives in the cloud. The company has been on a fast-growth path, and as of July 2026, Cyera has roughly 120 open roles across engineering, product, sales, and go-to-market functions. That volume of open positions signals active, ongoing hiring, not a token headcount exercise. For Indian professionals in cloud, security, or enterprise software, this is a company worth targeting now while the hiring window is wide open.

Cyera's product sits at the intersection of data security posture management (DSPM) and cloud security, a segment that has grown sharply as enterprises move sensitive workloads to AWS, Azure, and GCP. Most roles require either deep cloud infrastructure knowledge or enterprise sales experience, so the fit is strongest for professionals with a few years of relevant work behind them rather than fresh graduates.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Cyera's interview process is reported by candidates to run four to five stages. Formats and timelines vary by role and hiring team, so treat this as a general guide, not a fixed sequence.

Stage 1: Application and screening. Your resume goes through an initial review, either by a recruiter or an automated filter. Tailor your resume to highlight cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), data security tools, or enterprise B2B experience, depending on the role you are applying for.

Stage 2: Recruiter introduction call. If shortlisted, a recruiter reaches out for a short introductory conversation. This is mostly a fit check covering your background, what you are looking for, and your expectations around role and location. Be ready to explain why Cyera interests you and why now. Ask about the stages ahead and the likely timeline.

Stage 3: Hiring manager conversation. This is a deeper discussion with the person you would directly report to. For technical roles, expect questions on your approach to cloud security problems, past projects involving data classification or infrastructure, and how you handle ambiguity. For sales or go-to-market roles, expect questions on enterprise deal cycles and how you manage a territory.

Stage 4: Technical or skills-based assessment. Engineering candidates typically get a take-home or live coding exercise focused on cloud infrastructure, security tooling, or backend systems. Sales candidates may be asked to run a mock discovery call or present a territory plan. The exact format depends on the role.

Stage 5: Final panel or leadership round. Shortlisted candidates usually meet a cross-functional panel or senior leadership. This is where culture fit, communication style, and strategic thinking get evaluated alongside technical depth. Candidates who have researched Cyera's product and market position tend to stand out here.

After the final round, offer timelines vary by team. A polite follow-up with your recruiter after about a week is reasonable if you have not heard back.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Cyera hires people who understand enterprise security problems at real technical depth and can communicate that understanding clearly to non-technical stakeholders. The skills that appear consistently across Cyera's job postings include the following.

Cloud platform experience. Hands-on work with AWS, Azure, or GCP is almost always required for engineering and solutions roles. Relevant certifications from these platforms are worth listing prominently on your resume.

Data security or DSPM knowledge. Familiarity with how sensitive data is discovered, classified, and governed in cloud environments is a strong differentiator. Experience with tools like Macie, Purview, or similar products is a clear plus.

Enterprise software mindset. Cyera sells to large organisations with complex procurement cycles. Engineers and sales professionals who understand enterprise-grade scalability, compliance requirements (SOC 2, GDPR, and similar), and customer accountability are highly valued.

Clear communication and cross-functional collaboration. Because Cyera is still in a high-growth phase, roles tend to have broad scope. People who can work across product, engineering, and customer success without waiting for a detailed playbook do well here.

For sales and GTM roles. A track record of closing or supporting enterprise deals, preferably in cybersecurity or cloud SaaS, is the primary credential. Experience with CISO-level or security-buyer conversations is a meaningful advantage.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research Cyera's product and market positioning. Go through their public website, any available case studies, and analyst coverage of the DSPM space. Being able to speak to why data security posture management matters right now, and how Cyera fits into a broader cloud security stack, signals that you are a serious candidate rather than a spray-and-pray applicant.

Match your resume to the role language. Cyera's job descriptions use specific terms like 'data security posture,' 'cloud-native,' and 'sensitive data discovery.' Mirror that language in your resume where it honestly applies to your experience.

Prepare cloud security scenarios. For technical interviews, think through a handful of projects where you worked on data protection, access control, or cloud infrastructure at scale. Be ready to walk through your reasoning process, not just the outcome.

Practice behavioral questions with a security context. Questions like 'Tell me about a time you explained a complex security risk to a non-technical stakeholder' or 'Describe a situation where you had to balance security with developer velocity' come up often in security company interviews.

Know the competitive landscape. Cyera competes with other DSPM and cloud security vendors. Knowing who the main players are and how Cyera differentiates itself shows that you understand the business, not just the technology.

For salary discussions. Publicly reported ranges for cloud security roles vary widely by level and location. Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for current figures before your recruiter call so you can speak confidently about your expectations.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Cyera is still in its high-growth phase, which means the culture tends to reward people who are comfortable with ambiguity, move quickly, and take ownership without waiting to be told exactly what to do. Based on public hiring materials and employee reviews, a few themes come through consistently.

Security as a mission, not just a product. Employees at Cyera tend to care genuinely about the problem they are solving. The team takes protecting sensitive data seriously at a personal level, not just as a pitch to enterprise buyers.

Collaboration across functions. Because the company is focused and relatively lean compared to legacy security vendors, engineers often interact directly with customers, and sales teams work closely alongside product. There is less siloing than you might encounter at a large enterprise software company.

High performance expectations. Cyera operates in a competitive, well-funded segment of the cybersecurity market. The pace is fast, and people who thrive here tend to be self-motivated and comfortable being held accountable to results.

Openness to diverse technical backgrounds. The company has hired across geographies and disciplines. Indian professionals with cloud infrastructure or enterprise software experience bring exactly the kind of technical depth Cyera is actively building toward.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Cyera currently have?

As of July 2026, Cyera has roughly 120 open roles. This is a significant number for a focused cybersecurity company and suggests active expansion across multiple functions, not just backfill hiring. Roles span engineering, sales, product, and operations, giving candidates a reasonable range of entry points to consider.

Does Cyera hire candidates based in India?

Cyera's primary offices are outside India, but several engineering and operations roles have been listed as remote-eligible, and the company has hired internationally. Check the location field carefully on each individual role. If a role specifies an office city, it is still worth reaching out to the recruiter to ask about flexibility, as policies can vary by team.

What salary should I expect at Cyera?

Cyera does not publicly publish salary bands for most roles. Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for cloud security companies at a similar growth stage vary considerably by level, function, and location. Research those sources before your recruiter call so you can speak confidently about your expectations rather than anchoring low.

How long does the full interview process take from start to offer?

Candidates report the process typically spans a few weeks from first recruiter contact to offer, though timelines vary by team and role urgency. Following up politely with your recruiter after each stage helps keep momentum going. Delays often happen around quarter-end periods when hiring managers are focused on business targets rather than interviews.

Do I need a pure cybersecurity background to get hired at Cyera?

Not for every role. Engineering positions often value strong cloud infrastructure skills even without a pure security background. For roles involving customer-facing security advisory work or product management in the DSPM space, however, domain familiarity matters more. Read each job description carefully to see which security skills are listed as required versus preferred, since the bar differs significantly by team.

What makes a Cyera application stand out from the rest?

The strongest applications show a clear connection between the candidate's past experience and Cyera's specific focus on cloud data security. Mentioning real projects involving data classification, cloud access control, or enterprise security governance is more compelling than broad cloud experience listed without context. In interviews, candidates who can explain why the DSPM space matters right now, not just describe past job duties, tend to leave a stronger impression with the panel.
