01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Business Analyst job listings in Delhi NCR do not disclose pay. As of July 2026, knok's job radar tracked 48 active BA openings in Delhi, and compensation data was absent from virtually all of them. That matches a broader pattern: employers in India are not required to show salary ranges in job postings, and the majority choose not to, leaving candidates to guess before their first HR call.

So where do you find real numbers? Glassdoor collects self-reported salaries from current and former employees, and levels.fyi has growing coverage for tech-product BA roles. Industry surveys from compensation research firms add macro-level context, though city-specific, role-specific breakdowns are harder to find. Where this guide cites figures, it draws on those publicly reported sources and flags clearly where data is thin. If a range feels off for your sector or company type, trust your own research.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Pay for Business Analysts in Delhi NCR climbs steeply with experience and with the type of employer. knok's July 2026 job radar did not return salary band data for this search, so the ranges below are drawn from Glassdoor and industry surveys for Delhi NCR BA roles and should be treated as directional. Sample sizes for this specific city-role combination are small.

Entry level (0-2 years). Glassdoor-reported figures commonly cited for Delhi NCR put starting BA salaries in the 4-7 LPA range. IT product companies and large consulting firms tend toward the higher end; traditional enterprises and early-stage startups can be lower.

Mid-level (3-6 years). Industry surveys and Glassdoor data commonly cited for this bracket suggest 9-16 LPA, with significant variation based on domain specialisation (BFSI, e-commerce, consulting) and company size. Strong domain expertise can push offers toward the top of this range.

Senior or lead (7+ years). Senior BAs, BA leads, and those moving into functional consulting are commonly cited on Glassdoor at 16-28 LPA in Delhi NCR. The upper end generally requires a mix of stakeholder ownership, cross-functional leadership, and a measurable track record of business impact.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Research before you name a number. Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for your combination of role, experience, and company type in Delhi NCR before the first HR call. Having publicly reported data as your anchor makes a counter-offer feel logical rather than arbitrary.

Let the recruiter go first. If asked for your expectations, ask them to share the band first. Once you know their range, you can position yourself at the upper end based on your experience and any competing interest.

Counter on the whole package, not just base. Variable pay, joining bonus, ESOPs (for startups and listed tech companies), and work-from-home flexibility all have real monetary value. If fixed pay is firm, ask what can move on these components.

Use a competing offer honestly. An offer from another company is the strongest negotiation lever you have. If you have one, mention it. Do not invent one: Delhi NCR's hiring community is smaller than it looks, and recruiters sometimes verify through shared contacts.

Time your counter after the written offer. Your negotiating position is strongest once the company has committed to paper. Pushing hard in the first screening call rarely helps.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

A Business Analyst's CTC (Cost to Company) in Delhi NCR is rarely just one number. Understanding each component helps you compare offers that look different on the surface.

Basic salary is the fixed core of your pay. It determines your take-home after PF deductions and is the base on which several other components are calculated. Negotiating basic upward has a compounding effect across your total package.

House Rent Allowance (HRA) is standard for Delhi NCR roles. If you rent, HRA provides a meaningful income-tax exemption. The exact benefit depends on your rent amount, city classification, and total basic salary.

Special allowance or flexi benefit basket allows you to allocate a portion of your CTC toward tax-advantaged expenses like fuel, internet, or books. Larger employers and MNCs are more likely to offer a structured flexi basket.

Variable pay or performance bonus is common in consulting, BFSI, and tech product companies for BA roles. It is typically paid quarterly or annually and tied to individual and company performance. Ask the recruiter what share of employees actually receives the full variable payout: targets are sometimes set above what most people hit.

ESOPs or RSUs are offered mainly by startups and listed technology companies. They vest over multiple years and carry both upside potential and risk. Ask about strike price, vesting schedule, and any cliff period before factoring them into your decision.

Joining bonus is sometimes offered to cover unvested stock or a mid-year bonus you are leaving behind. It is worth asking for explicitly if you are switching before your annual bonus date.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most BA job listings in Delhi NCR not show a salary?

Indian employers are not legally required to disclose pay ranges in job postings, and most choose not to. Showing a salary publicly can create internal pressure if existing employees see new joiners being paid more, and it removes negotiation flexibility for the employer. Glassdoor and levels.fyi partially fill this gap with self-reported figures, though their Delhi NCR coverage for BA roles is thin. Your best move is to research these platforms before the first HR screen so you know the market rate before anyone asks for your number.

Is Delhi NCR a good market for Business Analyst jobs right now?

Yes, it is one of the stronger cities. As of July 2026, knok's job radar showed 48 active BA openings in Delhi, second only to Bangalore (53). The Gurgaon-Noida corridor hosts consulting firms, BFSI companies, technology giants, and e-commerce players that hire BAs consistently. The market is competitive at entry level but opens up meaningfully for candidates with a few years of experience and a clear domain focus.

How do I find out what a company actually pays before applying?

Glassdoor is the starting point: search for the company name plus 'Business Analyst' and filter by location. levels.fyi has better coverage for product-focused and tech companies, and LinkedIn salary insights can supplement both. Industry surveys from compensation research firms are typically paywalled or reported at sector level, making them less useful for company-specific research. Talking to people who currently work or recently worked at that company, through LinkedIn or mutual contacts, is often more accurate than any platform.

How much should I negotiate above the first offer?

There is no fixed rule, and the right counter depends on how close the initial offer is to your target. That said, industry surveys and recruiter communities commonly cited across hiring forums suggest that countering in the 10-20% range above the initial offer is within normal bounds for mid-level BA roles in India. A polite, research-backed counter almost never results in an offer being withdrawn. If the base is truly fixed, shift the conversation to joining bonus, variable pay, or an early performance review timeline.

Does an MBA help Business Analysts earn more in Delhi NCR?

An MBA from a recognised institution, particularly from an IIM or a top private business school, does carry a premium, especially for consulting and BFSI roles. Publicly reported placement data from premier institutes is commonly cited as significantly above the median for their graduating cohorts, though those figures reflect a wide mix of roles beyond pure BA positions. For professionals already in the workforce, domain expertise and a track record of measurable outcomes typically matter more than the degree when negotiating pay at the mid-senior level.

What is the pay difference between a Business Analyst and a Product Manager in Delhi NCR?

Product Managers generally earn more than BAs at equivalent experience levels in Delhi NCR, particularly in technology product companies. Glassdoor data commonly cited for Delhi NCR PM roles puts them in a noticeably higher bracket compared to equivalent BA positions, especially at the mid-senior level. The roles overlap significantly in smaller companies, and many BAs transition to PM roles over time. If a PM title is your goal, focusing on product discovery, roadmap ownership, and close collaboration with engineering teams strengthens both your case for the title and the pay that comes with it.
