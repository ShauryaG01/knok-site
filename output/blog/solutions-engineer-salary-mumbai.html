01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Solutions Engineer job listings in Mumbai do not show a salary range. Of the 23 Solutions Engineer openings tracked by knok jobradar in Mumbai as of July 2026, almost none displayed a fixed pay figure. This is the norm across India: companies prefer to discuss compensation after screening, leaving candidates negotiating without a public benchmark.

Solutions Engineers sit at the intersection of sales and technical delivery. They run product demos, answer deep technical questions from prospects, and own post-sale onboarding. That hybrid skill set, part engineer and part communicator, tends to command a premium over a pure software-development role at similar seniority levels, according to industry surveys.

Because verified Mumbai-specific pay data is thin in this sample (23 live roles, very few disclosed figures), the ranges in this guide are drawn from Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and publicly reported compensation data. Treat them as directional benchmarks, not guarantees.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

The salary_bands for this sample are not available, so the figures below come from Glassdoor and publicly reported data for Solutions Engineers in Mumbai. They represent total fixed CTC before variable pay and equity.

Junior SEs with 0-2 years of experience are commonly cited at 8-14 LPA on Glassdoor. Most enter through pre-sales associate or technical support tracks, often with an engineering or computer science background. Mid-level SEs with 3-6 years who independently own a sales territory or product vertical are publicly reported at 15-25 LPA on Glassdoor. The step from junior to mid is often the largest jump in this career path, driven by the ability to handle complex technical objections without escalation. Senior and Principal SEs with 7 or more years, particularly those managing enterprise accounts or leading pre-sales teams, are publicly reported at 26 LPA and above on levels.fyi and in industry surveys, with top earners at large SaaS companies publicly reported above 40 LPA.

Listings won't tell you what a role pays, but knok will tell you which ones you're competitive for.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Start with market data, not a personal number. When a recruiter asks your expected CTC, say you are looking at market rates and cite Glassdoor or publicly reported ranges for your band. This anchors the conversation without making you sound inflexible.

Know the full package. Solutions Engineer offers often include a variable component tied to sales targets. Before comparing two offers, convert them to a common base by asking what percentage of the target variable is typically paid out. A lower fixed with a strong variable history can beat a higher fixed with uncertain targets.

Leverage competing offers. Mumbai has 23 active openings in this role right now. If you are in late stages with two companies, it is reasonable to let both know. Recruiters expect this in a competitive hiring market.

Ask about the sales cycle length. A company with a very long enterprise sales cycle means your variable pay clears later. A product-led or mid-market SE role may pay out quarterly. That timing affects your real take-home more than a small LPA difference on paper.

Negotiate a joining bonus for mid-year joins. If you switch before your annual bonus vests, ask for a joining bonus to cover the amount you leave behind. This is a standard ask and most companies in Mumbai's BFSI and SaaS sectors have a policy for it.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

A Solutions Engineer's total compensation in Mumbai typically has three parts: fixed salary, variable pay linked to sales targets, and long-term incentives such as equity or ESOPs at funded startups.

Fixed salary is the guaranteed monthly payout and forms the base of every offer letter. For this role in Mumbai, Glassdoor and industry surveys suggest fixed pay forms the largest share of the package at junior levels, with that proportion shifting as variable grows at senior levels.

Variable pay is tied to your personal deal closure, the sales team's attainment, or a combined metric. Industry surveys commonly cite SE variable at 10-20% of fixed for junior roles, rising toward 20-35% for senior SEs, though actual payout depends on how the sales team performs. Always ask the recruiter for the last couple of years of actual payout as a percentage of target, not just the stated on-target earnings.

ESOPs or RSUs are common at funded startups and listed product companies. The value depends on company stage and vesting schedule. At pre-IPO companies these can be meaningful; at early-stage startups they are speculative. Publicly reported data from levels.fyi shows significant equity variation across companies.

Perks such as health insurance, mobile reimbursement, and travel allowance are standard in Mumbai SE roles. Some enterprise software companies also offer a car lease or petrol allowance for client-facing SEs.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most Solutions Engineer job postings in Mumbai not show a salary?

Indian companies, including large MNCs operating here, are not legally required to disclose pay in job listings. Many prefer to screen candidates first and negotiate individually, giving employers more flexibility but leaving candidates at a disadvantage. Your best counter is to research Glassdoor and publicly reported data before the first recruiter call so you already have a range in mind. This guide's estimates are a starting point for that preparation.

Is Mumbai a good city for Solutions Engineer roles compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has more than double the openings, with 55 roles versus Mumbai's 23 in the July 2026 knok snapshot, so if volume of choice is your priority, Bangalore wins. Mumbai SE roles tend to concentrate in BFSI-facing positions, covering banking software, insurance platforms, and fintech. If you have domain experience in those sectors, Mumbai can give you a stronger match rate and a better negotiating position than you would get as a generalist elsewhere.

What is the difference between a Solutions Engineer and a Sales Engineer?

The titles are used interchangeably at most Indian companies, especially in SaaS and cloud software firms. Both roles involve running demos, handling technical objections during the sales process, and supporting the account executive through to closure. Some companies use 'Solutions Engineer' for a slightly broader scope that includes post-sale delivery or integration work. When reviewing a job description, focus on the actual responsibilities listed rather than the title itself.

How does variable pay work for Solutions Engineers in Mumbai?

Most SE variable pay is tied to the performance of the sales team rather than individual deal closure, so your payout is partly outside your direct control. Industry surveys commonly cite SE variable at 10-20% of fixed for junior roles. Always ask the recruiter for the last couple of years of actual average payout as a percentage of target, not just what the on-target earnings figure looks like on paper. That history tells you whether the variable is real money or a notional number.

What skills help a Solutions Engineer negotiate a higher salary?

Domain expertise that matches the product vertical commands a premium. An SE with deep BFSI knowledge applying to a fintech-focused platform in Mumbai is harder to replace than a generalist. Certifications from relevant cloud or platform vendors are publicly reported to improve offer quality in this role. The ability to run enterprise demos independently, without handholding from the product team, is another differentiator that recruiters at Mumbai companies explicitly mention in job descriptions.
