01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

MyHQ is a flexible workspace platform that connects individuals and companies to coworking spaces, private cabins, managed offices, and day passes across Indian cities. The company sits at the intersection of real estate, technology, and the fast-growing hybrid work movement, which means hiring tends to blend operational thinking with product and growth sensibilities.

With around 30 open roles currently on their listings, MyHQ is actively building out teams across sales, operations, product, engineering, and customer success. Roles range from city-level operations managers to backend engineers and growth marketers, so the intake is broad. Most hiring happens in Bengaluru, but roles in other metros do appear.

MyHQ's interview process is structured but not excessively long. Candidates typically move through a recruiter screen, one or two functional rounds, and a final conversation before receiving an offer. Candidates who prepare well and respond promptly tend to move through the process faster.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

MyHQ's process generally follows four stages, though the exact number of rounds can shift depending on the role and team.

Stage 1: Recruiter or HR Screen
A brief introductory call with someone from the talent or HR team. Expect questions about your background, your interest in MyHQ specifically, your current notice period, and broad compensation expectations. This round is about basic fit, not deep skills. Come ready to explain why flexible workspaces interest you and what draws you to the company over others in the space.

Stage 2: Hiring Manager Round
A longer conversation with the person who would directly manage you. For business and operations roles, expect questions about past experience, how you have handled specific challenges, and how you think about the problem space MyHQ operates in. For tech roles, this round often includes a mix of system design discussion and questions about past projects. The manager wants to know whether you can do the job and whether you will thrive in the team.

Stage 3: Skills Assessment or Case Study
Many roles include a practical component. Business and growth candidates may get a take-home case or a live problem to work through, for example sizing a market, building a sales plan, or diagnosing a retention drop. Engineering candidates typically face a coding exercise or an architecture walkthrough. Treat this stage seriously. MyHQ is a product-led company, so they care about how you structure your thinking, not just the final answer.

Stage 4: Culture and Leadership Round
A final conversation, often with a senior leader or a cross-functional stakeholder. This round focuses on values alignment, long-term intent, and how you handle ambiguity. Questions tend to be open-ended: what kind of environment brings out your best work, how you have navigated fast-changing priorities, and what you want to build over the next few years. With around 30 open roles currently, the team is growing and they want people who see this as more than a short stop.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Ownership and initiative. MyHQ operates like a startup, even though it has scaled meaningfully. They want people who identify problems and solve them without waiting to be told. In interviews, back every claim with a specific example of something you drove end to end.

Comfort with ambiguity. Job descriptions at MyHQ are often broader than at larger companies. The expectation is that you will shape your role over time. Candidates who come with rigid ideas about what falls 'in their scope' tend to struggle.

Customer and market empathy. MyHQ's customers include freelancers, small teams, and large enterprises all at once. Anyone touching product, sales, or operations needs a genuine understanding of why people choose flexible workspaces and what frustrates them. Surface that understanding in your answers.

Analytical thinking. Even non-technical roles involve data. Whether it is occupancy rates, member churn, or campaign performance, MyHQ expects its team to be comfortable reading numbers and making decisions from them. If you have used SQL, Excel, or dashboards to drive a business decision, mention it.

Collaboration across functions. The company is not siloed. Sales talks to product, operations talks to tech. People who have worked across teams and can show they made those collaborations work stand out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product deeply. Before your first round, sign up for a MyHQ account or try a day pass if you can. Walk through the booking flow. Read recent coverage of the company. Know their core offerings: coworking, managed offices, enterprise plans, and the MyHQ app. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually used the product versus just read the homepage.

Prepare for the 'why MyHQ' question. This will come up. A strong answer connects your career direction to something specific about what MyHQ is building, not just 'I want to work at a startup.' Reference the hybrid work shift in India, the enterprise push, or something from recent news.

Practice structured answers. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. MyHQ interviewers respond well to crisp, story-driven answers over long rambling responses.

Prepare smart questions. Questions like 'What does success look like in your first few months here?' or 'What is the biggest unsolved challenge the team is working on right now?' show genuine interest and help you assess fit. Avoid questions whose answers are already on the website.

For tech roles, brush up on system design. If you are applying for engineering, revisit how you would design a booking and availability system at scale. Think about concurrency, real-time updates, and payment reliability. These are directly relevant to MyHQ's core platform.

Respond quickly and follow up professionally. MyHQ, like many growing companies, moves faster when candidates do. Acknowledge every interview invite promptly, send a brief thank-you note after your final round, and follow up politely if you have not heard back within a week.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

MyHQ has the energy of a company that has found product-market fit and is now scaling, which creates a particular kind of culture. There is urgency without chaos, and a genuine belief that flexible work is reshaping how India operates professionally.

Move fast, figure it out. The team values speed and pragmatism. Perfect is rarely the enemy of done here. If you prefer to wait for complete information before acting, the pace may feel uncomfortable. If you enjoy building systems while running in them, you will likely thrive.

Flat and accessible. Leadership at MyHQ is reportedly approachable, and input from individual contributors shapes decisions more than at comparable-stage companies. People with strong opinions, held loosely, tend to do well.

Mission-connected. Many people at MyHQ are genuinely excited about the flexible workspace category. The day-to-day work connects visibly to the product that customers use, which keeps motivation high across teams. If you have a personal connection to remote work or have navigated the freelance or startup workspace yourself, that tends to resonate in interviews.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many rounds does the MyHQ interview process have?

Most candidates go through three to four rounds: a recruiter screen, a hiring manager conversation, a skills or case study round, and a final culture round. The exact number can vary by role and team. Tech roles sometimes combine the skills and hiring manager rounds into a single longer session.

Is there a coding test for non-engineering roles at MyHQ?

Non-engineering roles typically do not include a coding test, but they often include a case study or take-home assignment. Sales and growth roles may be asked to build a go-to-market plan or respond to a business scenario. Operations roles may get a problem involving process design or data interpretation.

Does MyHQ hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

MyHQ does hire early-career candidates, particularly for sales, operations support, and customer success roles. That said, the company generally expects a baseline level of ownership and initiative, so freshers who can demonstrate real projects, internships, or side work tend to stand out over those relying solely on academic credentials.

How long does the MyHQ hiring process take from first contact to offer?

This varies by role and team availability. Candidates who are responsive and prepared typically move through in a few weeks. Roles with fewer decision-makers involved tend to close faster. If you have a competing offer or a notice period concern, it is fine to mention it early so the team can plan accordingly.

What should I wear or how formal should I be for a MyHQ interview?

MyHQ has a startup culture, so business casual is a safe choice for in-person rounds. Video interviews are typically relaxed in terms of dress. More important than clothing is coming prepared, being specific in your answers, and showing genuine interest in what the company is building.

How do I find out when new roles open at MyHQ?

MyHQ posts openings on their careers page and on major job platforms. With around 30 open roles currently, it is worth checking regularly as positions shift with team priorities. Setting up alerts on job boards or using a tool that scans listings nightly can help you catch new postings as soon as they go live.
