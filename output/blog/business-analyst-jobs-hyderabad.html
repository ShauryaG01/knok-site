04 Salary Range

Salary Range

No salary data was captured in knok's current jobradar snapshot for Hyderabad BA roles, so the figures below come from publicly reported and Glassdoor data rather than this dataset.

For entry-level Business Analysts in Hyderabad with 0-3 years of experience, Glassdoor commonly cites a range of 4-8 LPA. Mid-level BAs with 3-7 years can expect 8-15 LPA based on industry surveys, while senior BAs and lead analysts with strong domain specialisation are commonly reported at 15-25 LPA on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi.

Hyderabad salaries are generally in line with Pune and slightly lower than Bangalore, according to publicly reported comparisons. Domain expertise in healthcare IT (relevant given Medtronic's presence), strong SQL proficiency, and hands-on experience with Power BI or Tableau tend to push offers toward the higher end of any band. Keep in mind that these ranges are based on small sample sizes in some segments, so treat them as a rough guide rather than a guarantee.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Match the domain, not just the title. Hyderabad's hiring mix right now includes healthcare (Medtronic), data analytics (sarasanalytics, MLOPS Solutions), and engineering services. If you have any relevant domain experience, lead with it in your resume summary rather than burying it in the work history section.

Show your tools clearly. Most BA job descriptions in this market ask for SQL, Excel, and at least one BI tool such as Power BI or Tableau. List the tools you have actually used and pair each with a brief example of the business problem it helped you solve.

Quantify your impact. Generic phrases like 'gathered requirements' or 'worked with stakeholders' are common and forgettable. Replace them with specific outcomes: the process you improved, the decision your analysis supported, or the time a team saved because of your work.

Get SQL-sharp before interviews. Many Hyderabad BA roles, especially at analytics-first companies, include a basic SQL screening round. Practising common queries and window functions will help you clear this step confidently and without stress.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start with the active recruiters. Mount Talent Consulting, Medtronic, and ABS Groups each have 2 open roles right now. Apply to these directly through their career pages or LinkedIn, and tailor your resume to each company's domain before sending.

Cast a wider net by city if needed. With 21 roles in Hyderabad versus 53 in Bangalore and 48 in Delhi, considering a remote-friendly or hybrid role in another city can significantly increase your options without requiring an immediate move.

Apply early. With only 21 open roles in the city, competition per role is high. Most shortlists fill within the first few days of a posting going live. Checking job boards daily and applying on day one matters far more here than it would in a larger market.

Track every application. Keep a simple spreadsheet with the company name, role, date applied, and follow-up status. It prevents duplicate applications and keeps your pipeline visible at a glance.
