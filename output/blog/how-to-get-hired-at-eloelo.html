01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

eloelo is one of India's fastest-growing live social gaming and entertainment platforms. Built around interactive games, live shows, and creator communities, the company has carved out a strong position among young Indian users who want social entertainment beyond passive scrolling.

As of July 2026, eloelo has 28 open roles across engineering, product, design, growth, marketing, and operations. The company is in an active hiring phase, which means interview pipelines can move briskly and teams are being built out in real time. Most openings are based out of Bengaluru, though some remote-friendly roles exist depending on the function.

The typical eloelo hire is someone who is comfortable with ambiguity, moves fast, and genuinely enjoys the live entertainment or gaming space. If you have experience at a consumer internet startup, a gaming company, or a creator economy platform, that background translates well here.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The interview process at eloelo is relatively lean compared to larger tech companies, which is common for high-growth startups at this stage. Candidates typically report going through three to five rounds depending on the role and seniority.

Application and resume screen: Your application is reviewed by the talent team, sometimes within a few days of applying. A resume that clearly shows consumer internet or gaming experience tends to move faster through this stage.

Recruiter or HR call (Round 1): A short call, roughly half an hour, to confirm your interest, discuss your background at a high level, and align on compensation expectations and timelines. Be ready to talk about why you want to work in live social gaming specifically.

Hiring manager or functional interview (Round 2): This is the core conversation with the team you would join. For engineers, expect questions around system design, data structures, or past project deep-dives depending on the role. For product and growth roles, expect case discussions around user acquisition, retention, or feature decisions in a consumer app context.

Technical or assignment round (Round 3, role-dependent): Some roles include a take-home assignment or a live coding or product exercise. Candidates report these are typically focused and practical rather than abstract puzzles.

Culture or leadership round (Rounds 4-5, senior roles): For senior or cross-functional positions, there may be an additional conversation with a founder or department head. This round is less about testing skills and more about fit, ambition, and how you think about building products for Indian consumers at scale.

Total timeline from application to offer is commonly reported as two to four weeks, though it can compress significantly if the role is urgent.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

eloelo hires for a specific kind of builder: someone who is hands-on, consumer-obsessed, and comfortable working in a fast-moving environment where the product is constantly evolving based on user feedback.

For engineering roles: Strong fundamentals in backend or mobile development matter, but so does a track record of shipping features quickly in a product-driven team. Experience with live streaming infrastructure, real-time systems, or high-concurrency applications is a strong differentiator.

For product and growth roles: The ability to think in terms of user behaviour, engagement loops, and retention is valued highly. If you have worked on a consumer app with a large daily active user base, that experience is directly relevant. Comfort with data is expected, not optional.

For design and content roles: A portfolio that shows you understand how Indian users consume entertainment on their phones is more valuable than a polished portfolio built for a Western audience.

Across all roles: eloelo looks for people who are genuinely excited about the live entertainment and social gaming category. Knowing the product as a user, understanding why people spend time on platforms like this, and having strong opinions about what could be better are all things that stand out in interviews.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the product deeply before your first call. Spend time on the eloelo app before any interview. Play the games, watch a live show, and notice how creators interact with their audience. Interviewers frequently ask candidates what they think about the product and what they would improve.

Prepare specific examples from consumer internet. eloelo is building for a large, diverse Indian audience. If you have worked on apps with a similar user profile, prepare two or three concrete stories about decisions you made, metrics you moved, and what you learned from both successes and failures.

Brush up on live streaming and real-time product concepts. Even if you are not an engineer, understanding the basics of how live platforms work such as latency, concurrent users, and creator monetisation shows you are thinking seriously about the space.

Research the competitive landscape. Know who else operates in the live social gaming and entertainment space in India. Having a point of view on how eloelo is positioned and where you see opportunity signals intellectual engagement with the category, not just interest in a job.

Salary expectations. Come prepared with a clear number or range. For mid-level and senior roles in Bengaluru, Glassdoor and industry surveys suggest compensation at live entertainment startups of this scale varies widely based on function and seniority. Research comparables before the HR call so you are not caught off guard.

Ask good questions. eloelo is in a growth phase, so questions about team structure, product roadmap, and how the role will evolve in the next year show you are thinking like an owner, not just a candidate filling a seat.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

eloelo operates with the energy you would expect from a young, consumer-focused startup: fast decisions, direct communication, and a strong bias toward shipping and learning rather than planning and perfecting.

Creator and community first. The company's core belief is that live entertainment is most powerful when creators and their communities are at the centre of the experience. This shows up in how the product team prioritises features and how internal decisions get made. If you have a background working with creators or community-driven products, that perspective resonates here.

High ownership, low bureaucracy. Employees at early and mid-stage startups like eloelo typically wear multiple hats. If you prefer clearly defined lanes, this may feel uncomfortable at first. If you enjoy picking up problems outside your job description and running with them, you will likely thrive.

Pace is a feature. eloelo moves quickly by design. Roadmaps can shift based on what users are responding to in real time. People who are energised by that pace tend to do well; those who need long planning cycles often find it difficult to settle in.

Bengaluru startup culture. The team is largely based in Bengaluru and reflects the collaborative, informal culture common to the city's consumer internet ecosystem. Cross-functional collaboration is expected and the hierarchy is relatively flat at the working level, with founders accessible to teams.
