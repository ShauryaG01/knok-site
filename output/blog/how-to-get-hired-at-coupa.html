01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Coupa Software is a cloud-based business spend management platform used by enterprises worldwide to handle procurement, invoicing, and expenses. Their India operations span engineering, professional services, customer success, and support, making India a core part of their global delivery model.

As of July 2026, Coupa has 122 open roles across locations and functions. This is a meaningful volume of hiring, signalling active growth and backfill across teams. Roles range from software engineers and QA professionals to implementation consultants, finance analysts, and sales engineers.

If you have a background in enterprise software, ERP systems, or cloud-based SaaS products, Coupa is worth putting on your target list. The company operates in a niche (spend management) that is growing as large companies tighten cost controls, which generally means stable demand for skilled hires.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Coupa interview process typically runs across 4 to 5 rounds based on what candidates have publicly reported, though the exact structure varies by role and function. The stages below are a general guide and your own experience may differ.

StageWhat Happens
1. Application ScreenRecruiter or ATS reviews your resume for role fit. Tailoring your resume to the job description matters here.
2. Recruiter CallA short call covering your background, salary expectations, and basic role alignment.
3. Technical or Functional RoundFor engineers, expect coding questions or system design. For functional roles, expect scenario-based questions on procurement or ERP workflows.
4. Hiring Manager RoundDeeper discussion on your work experience, problem-solving approach, and fit with the team's current priorities.
5. Final or Panel RoundSome roles include a final round with a senior leader or cross-functional panel, particularly for senior or specialist positions.

The process can take a few weeks end to end, based on how quickly scheduling aligns. Keep follow-up emails brief and professional if you do not hear back within a week after any round.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Coupa looks for people who understand enterprise software and can work well with large, complex customers. The specific skills depend on the track you are applying for.

For engineering roles: Strong fundamentals in Java, Python, or similar backend languages. Experience with cloud platforms (AWS or Azure), REST APIs, and microservices architecture. Familiarity with agile development practices and CI/CD pipelines is a common expectation.

For implementation and consulting roles: Domain knowledge in procurement, accounts payable, or supply chain. Hands-on experience with ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, or Workday is a strong differentiator. The ability to manage customer relationships and explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders is equally important.

For support and customer success roles: Strong written and verbal communication in English, a methodical troubleshooting approach, and patience with enterprise clients who may have complex environments.

Across all roles: Coupa values people who take ownership, communicate proactively, and can handle some ambiguity. If you have experience working with global teams or across time zones, call that out clearly on your resume.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product before you apply. Coupa's platform covers procurement, invoicing, expense management, and supply chain risk. Read their public product pages and a few customer case studies so you can speak to real use cases in your interview. Candidates who understand what Coupa actually sells stand out immediately.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Coupa posts detailed job descriptions. Match your resume's language to the keywords in the job description, especially for terms like 'P2P' (procure-to-pay), 'ERP integration', or the specific cloud platforms they mention.

Prepare for behavioural questions using the STAR format. Coupa interviewers frequently ask questions such as 'Tell me about a time you handled a difficult customer' or 'Describe a project where you had to work across teams.' Prepare several strong stories from your past work that you can adapt to different questions.

For technical roles, practise system design. Senior engineering candidates at enterprise SaaS companies like Coupa often face system design questions about scalability and multi-tenant architecture. Practise designing systems that handle large transaction volumes, as that is central to what Coupa's platform does.

Research salary ranges before the recruiter call. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry India-specific compensation data for Coupa roles. Having a clear number in mind when the recruiter asks avoids leaving money on the table.

Send a follow-up after each round. A short thank-you note to your interviewer, via your recruiter or LinkedIn if you are connected, keeps you visible and signals genuine interest.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Coupa operates with a customer-first mindset baked into most of its processes. Internally, the culture is described by employees on publicly available review platforms as collaborative and results-driven, with a strong emphasis on ownership. Teams are expected to move quickly and communicate clearly, particularly because many India-based teams work closely with customers and colleagues in the US and Europe.

Work style: Most India roles at Coupa follow a hybrid model, with some office presence expected depending on team and location. Enterprise software timelines mean some roles involve overlap hours with Western time zones, so be realistic about this if work-life balance is a priority for you.

Career growth: Coupa has historically promoted from within for senior individual contributor and management roles, based on what employees have publicly reported. The presence of 122 open roles at this point in time also suggests internal mobility may be active.

Diversity and inclusion: The company publicly states commitments to building diverse teams. Women returning to work and professionals from non-metro cities have reported positive recruitment experiences in community forums, though individual experiences vary.

One honest note: Enterprise software companies can have periods of rapid change, including restructuring or shifts in strategic priorities. Going into your interviews with a clear understanding of the team's roadmap and how your role fits into it is a smart move.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs does Coupa have open right now?

As of July 2026, Coupa has 122 open roles across functions and locations. This includes engineering, consulting, customer success, and support positions. The volume suggests the company is actively hiring rather than doing selective backfill, so it is a good time to apply if you have the relevant background.

Does Coupa hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Coupa primarily hires experienced professionals, especially for its India offices which tend to focus on mid-to-senior level engineering and implementation roles. That said, some customer support and QA roles do appear with lower experience requirements. If you are a fresher, look specifically for internship or associate-level listings rather than applying to senior roles.

What salary can I expect at Coupa in India?

Coupa does not publish pay bands publicly for India roles. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry compensation data submitted by employees and these are your best reference for role-specific ranges. Go into the recruiter call with a number based on that research rather than waiting for them to make the first offer.

Is domain knowledge in procurement or ERP mandatory?

It depends on the role. For implementation consultants and functional analysts, procurement or ERP experience with SAP, Oracle, or Workday is effectively required. For software engineering roles, product domain knowledge is helpful but not always mandatory. If you come from a different enterprise software background, emphasise transferable skills like large-scale data handling or customer-facing delivery.

How long does the Coupa hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process typically takes a few weeks from first recruiter contact to offer, assuming scheduling goes smoothly. Delays are common if the hiring manager is travelling or if the role requires approvals from multiple stakeholders. Following up politely after each round helps keep things moving.

Should I apply directly on Coupa's website or through job portals?

Applying directly on Coupa's careers page is generally recommended as your primary method, since recruiters often screen from there first.
