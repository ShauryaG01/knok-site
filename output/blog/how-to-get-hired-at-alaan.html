01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Alaan is a UAE-based corporate spend management platform that helps businesses issue corporate cards, track expenses, and manage company finances in real time. The company operates primarily in the Middle East market and has been growing steadily through 2025 and 2026 as more Gulf businesses shift toward modern fintech solutions.

As of July 2026, Alaan has 22 open roles across engineering, product, sales, operations, and finance functions. If you are an Indian professional considering a move to Dubai or exploring remote-first fintech opportunities, Alaan is worth a close look. The team is internationally distributed, and the company's pace of hiring signals continued expansion into 2027. Roles span the full business, so whether your background is in backend engineering, B2B sales, or finance operations, there is likely something relevant in their current openings.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Most candidates report a four to five stage process, though the exact steps vary by role and seniority. Here is what to expect, based on patterns commonly seen at early-to-mid stage fintech startups in the Gulf region.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. After you apply online, a recruiter reviews your resume for relevant experience. Turnaround is typically within a week for roles where you are a strong match. Tailoring your resume to the specific job description meaningfully improves your chances of moving forward.

Stage 2: Recruiter or HR call. A short conversation, usually around twenty to thirty minutes, to confirm your background, location or visa status, salary expectations, and interest in the role. Be ready to explain clearly why you want to work in the Middle East fintech space and what draws you to corporate spend management specifically.

Stage 3: Technical or functional assessment. For engineering roles, this is often a take-home coding task or a live coding round covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes system design. For product and operations roles, expect a case study or a written exercise based on a real business problem Alaan might face. Sales candidates sometimes get a role-play scenario involving a prospect objection.

Stage 4: Panel or manager interview. A deeper conversation with the hiring manager and one or two team members. This round covers both domain knowledge and how you approach problems. Expect questions like 'walk me through a time you shipped something under pressure' or 'how would you prioritize competing requests from two different teams?'

Stage 5: Leadership or final round. Senior leadership, sometimes a co-founder, may be involved at this stage for mid-to-senior roles. This round often focuses on values alignment, long-term thinking, and your understanding of where the corporate spend management space is heading.

The full process commonly takes two to four weeks from first contact to offer, though urgent roles can move faster.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Alaan is building infrastructure for how Middle Eastern businesses manage money, so the traits they value most are a blend of technical depth and genuine business context.

Ownership mindset. Startup teams move fast and expect everyone to take end-to-end responsibility. Candidates who frame their experience in terms of outcomes ('I redesigned the onboarding flow and reduced drop-off') stand out over those who only describe their tasks.

Fintech or SaaS domain knowledge. You do not need prior Gulf experience, but understanding how spend management, corporate cards, or B2B SaaS products work is a real advantage. Familiarity with concepts like expense approval workflows, ERP integrations, or accounting reconciliation helps in interviews.

Communication across cultures. Alaan's customers and team span the UAE and beyond. Hiring managers look for people who can adapt their communication style and work effectively in an international environment without friction.

Data-driven thinking. Whether you are in product, sales, or engineering, expect to be asked how you would measure success or how you would use data to make a decision. Vague or gut-feel answers do not land well at a company this analytically oriented.

For engineering roles specifically. Comfort with distributed systems, APIs, and cloud infrastructure is commonly expected at fintech companies at Alaan's stage. Strong opinions on reliability and data consistency are a bonus.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A focused two-week prep plan will cover most of what Alaan's interviewers are likely to probe.

Know the product cold. Spend an hour with Alaan's website and any publicly available demos or product walkthroughs. Understand what problems they solve for CFOs and finance teams. Be ready to say what you would improve and why, not just what you like.

Study the competitive landscape. Corporate spend management is a crowded space globally, with players like Spendesk, Navan, and Airbase active internationally. Know where Alaan fits and what makes their Middle East focus a strategic advantage over global generalists.

Prepare STAR stories. For behavioral rounds, have three to five stories ready that show ownership, cross-functional collaboration, and handling ambiguity. Structure each one as Situation, Task, Action, Result. Practice saying them out loud so they do not sound scripted.

For engineers: sharpen your DSA and system design. Practice medium-to-hard problems on coding platforms and be ready to walk through the design of a payments or notification system end to end. System design questions at fintech startups often center on reliability, consistency, and handling partial failures.

For non-technical roles: prepare a case study. Pick a real problem in the spend management space (for example, how would you reduce duplicate expense submissions across a company with many subsidiaries?) and prepare a structured answer covering diagnosis, proposed solution, and how you would measure success.

Sort out your logistics early. Many Alaan roles are based in Dubai, so think through visa sponsorship, relocation timelines, and salary expectations in AED before the recruiter call. Arriving with clear, confident answers to these questions signals you are serious and ready to move.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Alaan carries the energy of a growth-stage startup: high ownership, fast decision cycles, and a genuine belief that they are changing how businesses in the Middle East manage money. The team is relatively small and internationally distributed, which means your work is visible and your impact is direct from day one.

Move fast, learn faster. Processes are still being built, which is exciting if you like shaping things and challenging if you prefer established playbooks. People who thrive here tend to enjoy figuring things out rather than waiting to be told.

Customer obsession. Corporate finance teams have high expectations and low tolerance for tools that create more work. Alaan's culture reflects that by pushing employees to deeply understand customer pain points before building or selling anything.

International by default. With offices and customers across the Gulf, the work environment is genuinely multicultural. English is the working language, and team members commonly come from India, the UAE, Egypt, and Europe. If you are used to navigating diverse teams, you will feel at home.

Growth as a real perk. Early-stage roles at fast-growing fintechs offer exposure and career progression that a large bank or IT services company cannot match on the same timeline. If you want to build cross-functional skills and move up quickly, the environment actively supports that.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Alaan hire candidates based in India?

Alaan's primary offices are in the UAE, so most roles involve relocation to Dubai. That said, some engineering and product roles have been offered on a remote or hybrid basis to candidates in India, depending on the team's needs at the time. Check the job description carefully for a location requirement, and clarify the arrangement during your recruiter call. Do not assume remote is on the table unless it is explicitly stated.

What kinds of roles are typically open at Alaan?

Alaan tends to hire across engineering (backend, frontend, mobile), product management, sales (account executives and business development), customer success, finance, and operations. With 22 open roles as of July 2026, there is a reasonable spread across functions. Tech and go-to-market roles are usually the most active. Check their careers page directly for the freshest listings, since roles at a company this size open and close quickly.

How long does it take to hear back after applying?

Response times vary considerably. Candidates who are a strong match for an urgent role sometimes hear within a few days of applying. Others wait two to three weeks before a recruiter reaches out, and some receive no response if the role fills before they are reviewed. Applying early in a hiring cycle and tailoring your resume to the specific job description are the two best ways to improve your chances of a faster response.

What salary can I expect at Alaan?

Compensation at Alaan is typically denominated in AED since most roles are Dubai-based. Salary benchmarks for fintech roles in Dubai are publicly reported on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi, and those are the best places to calibrate your expectations by function and seniority. Indian professionals relocating to the UAE often find that the tax-free structure of UAE income meaningfully improves effective take-home compared to equivalent LPA figures back home. Come into the recruiter call with a clear number in mind based on that research.

Is the interview process heavily technical for non-engineering roles?

Non-technical roles at Alaan (sales, operations, customer success, finance) do not involve coding rounds, but they do expect structured, analytical thinking. Interviewers will probe how you diagnose a problem, design a process, or handle a difficult customer situation. A case study or short written exercise is common for product and operations roles. Coming in with a clear point of view about the corporate spend management market, rather than generic enthusiasm for fintech, will set you apart from most other candidates.

What is the best way to stand out as an applicant at Alaan?

Specificity wins at a company this focused. Generic applications that could apply to any fintech startup get filtered out quickly. Show that you understand Alaan's product, their core customer (mid-sized Gulf businesses managing complex expense needs), and why the Middle East is a distinctive market. In your cover note or recruiter call, connect your past experience directly to a problem Alaan is solving. A candidate who references Alaan's actual product and offers a concrete idea will always outperform one whose application could have been sent to ten other companies.
