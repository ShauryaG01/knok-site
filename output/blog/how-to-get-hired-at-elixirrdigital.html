03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Most candidates at digital firms like Elixirrdigital go through a process that looks something like the stages below, though exact steps can vary by role and team.

Application and Resume Screening
Your resume goes through an initial filter, usually handled by HR or a recruiter. They are checking for role fit, relevant experience, and the right keywords. A tailored resume that mirrors the job description language clears this stage faster.

HR Screening Call
If your profile shortlists, expect a short call with HR. This is mostly a fit check covering your notice period, current role, salary expectations, and why you want to join. Be ready with clear, honest answers. Do not bluff on notice period or current CTC.

Skills or Technical Round
This is where the real evaluation happens. Depending on the role, you may face a written test, a live coding or design task, a portfolio review, or a case study. Digital agencies care deeply about what you can actually do, not just what you have listed on paper. Prepare practical samples of your work before this stage.

Interview with Team Lead or Hiring Manager
A deeper conversation about your past work, how you handle projects, and how you think through problems. You may be asked to walk through a past project in detail. This round often makes the final call.

Final Round or Offer Discussion
Some roles add a final conversation with a senior stakeholder or founder before an offer goes out. After this stage, HR typically reaches out with a verbal or written offer. If you plan to negotiate, come prepared with data from Glassdoor or industry surveys for your role and experience level.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Digital agencies like Elixirrdigital look for a specific blend of skills and attitude. Here is what typically matters most across departments.

Relevant, demonstrable skills. Whatever your role, you need proof of work. A developer should have a GitHub profile or project portfolio. A designer should have polished samples ready to share. A marketer should be able to point to campaigns or results they contributed to directly.

Ownership mindset. Small to mid-size digital firms expect people to take full ownership of their tasks. They rarely have room for people who wait to be told every next step. Show that you can run with a brief and deliver without constant hand-holding.

Communication skills. Digital agencies work closely with clients and cross-functional teams. You will be expected to write clearly, present ideas confidently, and manage expectations when timelines shift. Good written English is a must for most roles.

Adaptability. Priorities shift fast in a digital company. If you have worked in environments where scope changed often and you handled it well, lead with those examples in your interview.

Up-to-date knowledge. Digital tools, platforms, and best practices evolve quickly. Hiring managers will notice if your knowledge feels dated. Stay current with what is happening in your domain before you walk in.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Getting hired at a company like Elixirrdigital comes down to preparation, clarity, and showing real work.

Tailor your resume to each role. With 22 open roles at Elixirrdigital right now, there is likely a position that fits your profile closely. Do not send a generic resume. Read the job description carefully and rewrite your bullet points to match the language and priorities the company uses.

Build or update your portfolio. Before you apply, make sure your portfolio, GitHub, or work samples are current and easy to navigate. Remove old or weak work. If you have nothing to show yet, build something small but polished specifically for the application.

Research the company. Look at Elixirrdigital's website, social media, and client work before your interview. Showing genuine knowledge about what they do signals motivation that generic candidates rarely demonstrate.

Prepare your introduction. The 'tell me about yourself' question is almost always the first one. Write out a short version covering your background, what you do well, and why you are excited about this specific company. Practise it out loud before the interview.

Have salary data ready. Before the offer stage, check Glassdoor and industry surveys for salary ranges in your role and experience bracket. Know your number going in so you are not caught off guard during the discussion.

Follow up after each round. Send a short, polite note to HR within a day of completing an interview round. It is a small step most candidates skip, and it keeps you top of mind while the team is evaluating.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Digital agencies tend to reward speed, creativity, and people who take initiative. We do not have direct employee survey data specific to Elixirrdigital, but the pattern at similar companies paints a useful picture of what to expect.

Fast-paced and output-driven. Expect deadlines to be real, not suggestions. The teams are typically small, which means your contribution is visible quickly. Good work gets noticed fast; so does underperformance.

Collaborative but independent. You will work closely with designers, developers, marketers, and account managers depending on your role. At the same time, you will be expected to manage your own time and workload without much micromanagement from above.

Self-driven growth. In lean digital firms, people who raise their hand for new responsibilities tend to move up quickly. Waiting for a formal promotion cycle can mean waiting a long time. The people who grow fastest are the ones who take on more before they are asked.

Client-facing pressure is real. Many roles at digital agencies involve some level of client interaction. Being calm under pressure, professional in communication, and solution-oriented when things go wrong are all qualities that will serve you well from day one.
