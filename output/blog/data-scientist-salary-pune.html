01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

The honest starting point: most Data Scientist job listings in India do not show a salary figure. Recruiters prefer to 'discuss compensation later,' which puts you at a disadvantage when you walk into a negotiation without knowing the market band.

Knok's jobradar tracked 18 active Data Scientist openings in Pune as of July 2026. The figures below draw from listings that did disclose pay, combined with publicly reported industry surveys and platforms like Glassdoor. Treat them as informed benchmarks rather than guarantees, and always cross-check against the specific company you are interviewing with.

Pune sits in the mid-tier of Indian tech cities for Data Scientist compensation. Bangalore sets the ceiling nationally, but Pune's lower cost of living and typically shorter commutes mean your in-hand salary often stretches further here than the same LPA figure would in Whitefield or Andheri.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Your years of hands-on experience drive your band more than almost anything else in Data Science hiring. Specialisations and business impact matter, but experience is the primary gate.

Entry level (0-2 years) typically lands in the 8-16 LPA band. Freshers from strong programmes or with a solid Kaggle or GitHub portfolio can aim for the upper portion of that range. Mid-level professionals (3-5 years) who own a model end to end, from data cleaning to production deployment, tend to command 18-30 LPA, especially when they can show measurable business outcomes rather than just technical output. Senior Data Scientists (6-9 years) who lead cross-functional projects and mentor junior team members can target 30-48 LPA. Lead and Principal roles blur into titles like Head of Data Science or Staff Scientist and commonly reach 45-70+ LPA, with equity forming a meaningful share of total compensation at that level.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Most candidates accept the first number they hear. That single habit costs mid-level professionals meaningful money every year. A few things that actually shift the outcome:

Research the band before any call. Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company and role. Walk into the conversation knowing the range, not guessing it.

Let the recruiter name a number first. When asked for your salary expectation, it is completely reasonable to say 'I would like to understand the band for this role before I share a number.' Many recruiters will share it when you ask this way.

Counter with data, not personal need. 'Based on Glassdoor data for similar roles in Pune and my experience leading production ML projects, I am targeting X' lands better than 'I need more because of my expenses.'

Negotiate beyond base pay. If the base is fixed, push on joining bonus, variable payout percentage, remote work days, stock options, or an earlier performance review date. Asking 'can we revisit salary at the six-month mark if I hit specific targets?' sometimes gets a yes.

Get the written offer before you resign. Verbal confirmations have disappeared before the paperwork arrived. Wait for the formal letter.

Stay polite even if you decline. Pune's Data Science hiring circles are smaller than Bangalore's, and a courteous note keeps the door open for later.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

The LPA headline in a job posting is almost never what lands in your account each month. A typical Data Scientist offer in Pune includes several components worth understanding before you compare offers.

Fixed base salary is the guaranteed portion and the number you should compare across offers on an apples-to-apples basis.

Variable pay or performance bonus is commonly cited as 10-20% of base for individual contributors in technology roles, according to industry surveys, but the actual payout depends on both company performance and your individual rating. Always ask: 'What was the average payout percentage for this band last year?'

ESOPs or RSUs are increasingly common at mid-size Pune product companies and startups. The vesting schedule matters as much as the grant value. A four-year vest with a one-year cliff means you receive nothing if you leave before completing twelve months.

Gratuity is a statutory benefit that pays out only after five continuous years of service. It is not immediately liquid but adds up meaningfully if you stay long-term.

Provident Fund (PF) involves contributions from both you and your employer. Many candidates mentally subtract their PF share from in-hand pay, but factor the employer contribution back in when comparing total CTC across offers.

Benefits and perks vary widely. Health insurance with solid family coverage, a meal card, cab allowance, and a learning and development budget add real value beyond the salary line. Compare offers on monthly in-hand plus the annualised expected value of variable pay and equity, not on the headline CTC alone.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is 18 LPA a good salary for a Data Scientist in Pune with 3 years of experience?

18 LPA sits at the entry point of the mid-level band for Pune, so it is market rate but not particularly strong for someone with 3 years of solid experience. If you have end-to-end ownership of production ML models or a specialisation like NLP or computer vision, you have room to push higher within the 18-30 LPA mid-level range. Check Glassdoor for the specific company's pay data before deciding whether to negotiate or accept.

How does Pune compare to Bangalore for Data Scientist salaries?

Bangalore consistently shows higher salary ceilings according to publicly reported data on levels.fyi, especially at senior and lead levels where equity from funded startups and large MNCs pushes total compensation well above what Pune typically offers. That said, Pune's lower cost of living means the day-to-day financial position on many Pune salaries is comparable to equivalent Bangalore salaries once rent and commute are factored in. Bangalore also had 166 active listings versus Pune's 18 in the July 2026 snapshot, giving you far more negotiating options there.

What skills help a Data Scientist earn more in Pune specifically?

Pune's hiring concentrates in BFSI, manufacturing analytics, and automotive tech, so skills in time-series forecasting, anomaly detection, and domain modelling for credit risk or supply chain carry a noticeable premium here according to industry surveys. On the tools side, proficiency in PySpark, MLflow, and cloud platforms such as AWS SageMaker or Azure ML is commonly cited in higher-paying job descriptions in the city. Data Scientists who can translate model output into business decisions also tend to get promoted faster than those who stay purely technical.

Do Pune companies offer ESOPs to Data Scientists?

Increasingly yes, especially at product-focused startups and VC-backed firms operating out of Hinjewadi, Baner, and Kharadi. However, ESOPs at unlisted companies carry real liquidity risk: you need an acquisition or IPO to convert them to cash. Always ask about the company's current funding stage, last valuation, and the expected timeline for a liquidity event. RSUs at publicly listed companies are easier to value and more straightforward to plan around.

Should I negotiate on CTC or in-hand salary?

Anchor the negotiation on CTC first to establish a clear headline number, then ask HR for a full breakup sheet to calculate your actual monthly in-hand. Two offers with identical CTC can differ by thousands of rupees per month depending on how PF, gratuity, variable pay, and allowances are structured. Once you have the breakup, compare offers on monthly in-hand plus the annualised expected value of any variable pay and equity, not on the headline CTC alone.

Knok's jobradar showed 18 active Data Scientist listings in Pune as of July 2026. The number shifts week to week as companies open and close positions. If your Pune search is moving slowly, Hyderabad had 27 listings and Delhi NCR had 46 in the same snapshot, both of which are solid alternatives if you are open to relocation or remote-first roles.
