01 Role Overview

Role Overview

A Solutions Engineer (SE) sits between the sales team and the engineering team. Your job is to understand what a customer needs, then show them exactly how your company's product solves that problem. You run product demos, build proof-of-concept setups, answer tough technical questions during sales calls, and sometimes write custom integrations.

In India, the SE role is growing steadily. As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracked 1270 open Solutions Engineer positions across the country. Here is where most of those openings are concentrated:

02 Skills You Need

Skills You Need

Technical skills you will need

  • Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP): Most SE roles in India involve cloud or SaaS products. Knowing at least one platform well is essential.
  • APIs and integrations: You will spend a lot of time connecting systems, reading API docs, and debugging REST or GraphQL calls.
  • Scripting: Python, Bash, or JavaScript. You do not need to be a full-stack developer, but you must be comfortable writing quick scripts and reading code.
  • Networking and security basics: Especially relevant if you work at infrastructure or cybersecurity companies.
  • Demo tools: Postman, your company's own product, and presentation tools should feel natural to you.

Non-technical skills that set you apart

  • Communication: You explain complex technical ideas to non-technical buyers. This is the core of the job.
  • Discovery and listening: Great SEs ask the right questions before jumping into a demo.
  • Project management: Proof-of-concept projects can run for weeks. You need to keep them on track.
  • Business awareness: Understanding ROI, procurement cycles, and how Indian enterprises buy software will give you an edge over purely technical candidates.

03 Step By Step Path

Step By Step Path

  1. Pick your starting point. Most SEs in India come from one of three backgrounds: software development, technical support, or IT consulting. If you are a fresher, aim for a technical support or junior developer role at a product company. This gives you hands-on product knowledge.
  1. Go deep in one product domain. SaaS, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, data analytics: pick one vertical and commit to it. Read documentation, get certified (AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Fundamentals, or similar), and build small projects you can demo.
  1. Learn to present and communicate. Join your company's demo calls as an observer. Volunteer to run internal knowledge-sharing sessions. Practice explaining technical concepts to friends or family who are not in tech. Record yourself and review.
  1. Build a portfolio of technical wins. Document proof-of-concept projects, integration work, or customer-facing solutions you have delivered. Even internal projects count. An SE hiring manager wants to see that you can bridge the gap between product and customer.
  1. Network with working SEs. Reach out to Solutions Engineers on LinkedIn. Most SEs in India are happy to chat about their day-to-day work. Ask what skills their team values most and what gaps they see in candidates.
  1. Apply strategically. Target companies where you already know the product or domain. Tailor your resume to highlight both technical depth and customer-facing experience. On Naukri, update your profile headline to include 'Solutions Engineer' so recruiters can find you.
04 Timeline And Milestones

Timeline And Milestones

First year: Build your foundation
Start in a technical role (support engineer, junior developer, or IT consultant). Focus on learning one product domain deeply. Earn at least one cloud certification. By the end of this phase, you should be able to run a basic product demo and handle technical questions on the spot.

Second year: Position yourself for the move
Shift toward customer-facing work, even if your title has not changed yet. Volunteer for pre-sales calls, proof-of-concept projects, or partner enablement sessions. Build a portfolio of demos and technical presentations. Start applying to SE openings at product companies.

Third year onward: Grow as a Solutions Engineer
Once you land an SE role, focus on mastering enterprise sales cycles, deepening your technical expertise, and mentoring newer team members. Many SEs in India eventually move into SE management, product management, or customer success leadership.

05 India Specific Tips

India Specific Tips

Where to look for SE roles. Bangalore has the most openings (55 out of 1270 tracked as of July 2026), followed by Mumbai (23) and Delhi (20). SaaS companies, global tech firms with India offices, and cloud providers are the biggest employers.

Naukri and LinkedIn both matter. Keep both profiles current. On Naukri, use 'Solutions Engineer' in your headline and skills section. On LinkedIn, follow SE leaders at companies you admire and engage with their posts. Recruiters in India actively search both platforms.

Certifications that carry weight. AWS Solutions Architect Associate, Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect, and Salesforce platform certifications are commonly cited in Indian SE job listings. These are not mandatory, but they help you stand out when switching from a non-SE role.

Communities to join. Look for local chapters of Pre-Sales Collective, which has members across India. SaaS community meetups in Bangalore and Mumbai are good places to meet working SEs. Online, the r/presales subreddit and LinkedIn groups focused on solutions engineering can be useful.

College background. Most SEs in India hold a B.Tech or B.E. degree, but this is not a strict requirement. If you bring strong product knowledge and communication skills, companies will consider you regardless. Tier 1 and Tier 2 engineering colleges give you a network advantage, but your demo skills and domain expertise matter more in interviews.

Speed up your applications. Indian job markets move quickly for in-demand roles like SE. When a listing goes live, apply as soon as possible. Companies often shortlist candidates within the first few days. Q Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a coding background to become a Solutions Engineer?

You do not need to be a full-stack developer, but you should be comfortable reading code, writing scripts in Python or JavaScript, and working with APIs. Most SE interviews include a technical component where you debug or explain a piece of code.

What is the difference between a Solutions Engineer and a Sales Engineer?

In India, these titles are often used interchangeably. Both roles involve supporting the sales team with technical expertise. Some companies use 'Solutions Engineer' for more product-focused work and 'Sales Engineer' for roles closer to account management, but always check the job description rather than relying on the title.

Can I become a Solutions Engineer right after college?

It is uncommon but possible. Some larger SaaS companies hire fresh graduates into associate or junior SE programs. More commonly, candidates spend a year or two in a technical role first to build the product knowledge and customer-facing confidence that hiring managers look for.

Which cities in India have the most Solutions Engineer openings?

Based on 1270 tracked openings as of July 2026, Bangalore leads with 55 roles, followed by Mumbai (23), Delhi (20), Pune (12), Hyderabad (6), and Chennai (5). Many SE roles also support remote or hybrid work arrangements.

How much do Solutions Engineers earn in India?

SE salaries vary widely depending on the company, city, and your experience level. Publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest a broad range, with top-tier SaaS companies paying significantly more than mid-size firms. Salary data for this specific role in India is still thin, so research individual companies rather than relying on market averages.

What certifications help the most for SE roles in India?

AWS Solutions Architect, Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect, and Salesforce platform certifications are commonly cited in Indian SE job listings. Pick the one that aligns with the product domain you want to work in. Certifications alone will not land you the role, but they validate your technical foundation.
