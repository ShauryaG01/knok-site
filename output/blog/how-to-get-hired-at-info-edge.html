01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Info Edge (India) Ltd is the company behind Naukri.com, Jeevansathi.com, Shiksha.com, and their real estate listings platform. As a pioneer of India's internet economy, Info Edge has grown into a multi-product company that also holds investments in several well-known Indian startups. Joining Info Edge means working on platforms that a large number of Indians use every day to find jobs, life partners, homes, and educational courses.

As of July 2026, Info Edge currently has 28 open roles across product management, engineering, data, sales, marketing, and operations. Most positions are based at their Noida headquarters, with select openings in Mumbai, Bengaluru, and other cities. The variety of open roles means there is often a relevant position if your profile fits any of their core platforms.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process typically runs across four to six stages. The exact number varies by function and seniority.

Resume screening. Your application is reviewed by a recruiter or HR team member. A strong Naukri.com profile helps, as Info Edge often sources candidates directly through their own platform.

HR introductory call. A short conversation to confirm your interest, current and expected salary, notice period, and general fit with the role.

Online assessment. Engineering candidates face a coding round covering data structures and algorithms. Product and business candidates may receive an analytical case or a short written exercise to complete.

Technical or functional interview (one or two rounds). Engineering roles focus on data structures, system design, and questions tied to the specific stack. Product roles typically involve metric framing, prioritisation discussions, and critiquing Info Edge's own platforms.

Hiring manager round. A deeper conversation about your approach to solving problems, past project impact, and how you think about consumer internet products in India.

HR and compensation discussion. The final stage covers your long-term goals, motivation for joining, and offer terms. Candidates typically hear back within one to two weeks of this round.

Timelines are not guaranteed. Some candidates move through the process in two to three weeks; others have reported a longer wait depending on team availability and scheduling.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Info Edge builds platforms that serve Indians across job search, real estate, matrimony, and education. They look for people who bring the following:

Empathy for the Indian internet user. Their products serve job seekers in smaller cities, families arranging marriages, and first-time home buyers. Candidates who genuinely understand this audience stand out over those who only know global product patterns.

Comfort with data. Whether in product, marketing, or operations, you will be expected to look at metrics, form a hypothesis, and validate it. Strong analytical thinking is valued across all functions.

Clear communication. Info Edge products bridge candidates with recruiters, buyers with sellers, and students with colleges. The internal culture reflects the same expectation of clarity.

Ownership mindset. Managers value people who spot a problem and drive it to resolution rather than waiting for step-by-step direction.

Domain curiosity. If you are joining the jobs platform team, they expect you to have views on how hiring is evolving in India. The same applies to their real estate and education verticals.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the products before your first call. Spend time on Naukri.com as a job seeker and explore their real estate listings platform and Shiksha.com. Come with genuine observations about what works, where the experience breaks, and what you would fix first.

Quantify your past impact. Info Edge interviews reward specificity. Instead of saying 'I improved retention,' describe exactly what you changed and what shifted as a result. Every project you discuss should have a clear before-and-after.

Revise product and analytical fundamentals. For product roles, be ready for questions on activation funnels, A/B testing logic, cohort analysis, and prioritisation frameworks. For engineering roles, revise system design patterns relevant to high-traffic consumer platforms.

Research Info Edge's investment portfolio. The company has backed several well-known Indian startups. Understanding why they made those bets, and how those companies grew, signals that you think about the broader business and not just your immediate function.

Prepare thoughtful questions for each round. Ask about team structure, how success is measured, and what the team's biggest current challenge is. Surface-level questions leave a weak impression.

Follow up once after each round. A brief, professional note to your recruiter keeps you visible without being pushy.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Info Edge has a reputation as a stable, process-oriented employer compared to early-stage startups. A few things characterise the working environment:

Long tenures are common. Many employees spend years across different teams within Info Edge. There is a fair degree of internal movement between platforms, which means institutional knowledge is genuinely valued and rewarded.

Ownership is expected at every level. The company has historically run lean teams relative to the scale of its platforms. Individuals carry real responsibility from early in their time here.

Learning is largely self-directed. Info Edge does not have the structured training programmes of a large IT services firm. Growth comes from working on difficult problems close to the product, not from formal course tracks.

The pace is steadier than a startup. If you thrive on the uncertainty of an early-stage company, Info Edge may feel measured. If you want to build skills on products that millions of users rely on without existential startup risk, the trade-off works in your favour.

Salary benchmarks vary by function and level. For specific compensation data, Glassdoor and levels.fyi have publicly reported figures from current and former Info Edge employees that give a reliable starting point for negotiation.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Info Edge hire freshers?

Yes. Info Edge hires fresh graduates for engineering, sales, and business development roles. Naukri.com conducts campus placements at select engineering and management institutes. If you are a fresher, focus on demonstrating product curiosity and an understanding of the Indian internet market rather than experience you have not yet built.

What is the typical timeline from application to offer?

Most candidates report a process spanning a few weeks from the first HR call to receiving an offer. This varies by team and interview slot availability. If you have not heard back within a week of completing any round, one polite follow-up with your recruiter is appropriate. Avoid sending multiple follow-ups in quick succession.

Is there a coding test for non-engineering roles?

Generally no. Product, marketing, sales, and operations candidates are not given coding tests. However, analytical exercises or short written case studies are common for product and business roles. Engineering candidates at all levels should expect a coding assessment covering data structures and algorithms.

How important is prior internet or e-commerce experience?

It helps, but it is not always required. What matters more is demonstrating how consumer internet products grow and retain users in India. Candidates from IT services backgrounds have successfully joined Info Edge by showing strong product thinking or domain knowledge relevant to the specific role they applied for.

Can I apply to multiple roles at Info Edge at the same time?

Yes. Be transparent with the recruiter about your interests and why each role appeals to you. Applying to clearly mismatched positions can hurt your credibility, so focus on roles where your background is a genuine fit rather than applying broadly across every open listing.
