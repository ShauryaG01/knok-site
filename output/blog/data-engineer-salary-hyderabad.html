01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Data Engineer job listings in India do not show a salary number. Recruiters either leave the field blank or write 'as per industry standards,' which tells you nothing useful. The ranges below come from knok jobradar, which tracked 23 active Data Engineer openings in Hyderabad as of 8 July 2026, combined with publicly reported figures from Glassdoor and industry surveys.

Hyderabad has quietly built one of India's stronger data engineering markets. The city hosts major product companies, GCCs (Global Capability Centres), and a growing number of analytics startups. That mix means salaries vary widely depending on whether you are joining a services firm, a GCC, or a product company.

The broad range for a Data Engineer in Hyderabad runs from 6 LPA at entry level to 65+ LPA for lead or staff roles. Where you land depends on your years of experience, the stack you know (Spark, dbt, Airflow, Snowflake), and the type of employer.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Knowing a range is only useful if you use it. Here is how to negotiate effectively as a Data Engineer in Hyderabad.

Anchor on the upper half of your band. If you are a mid-level engineer with solid pipeline experience, name a number near the top of the 14-26 LPA range rather than the middle. Recruiters expect negotiation. Starting low just means you end up lower.

Get competing offers, or signal that you are working on them. Even one other offer letter changes the conversation. If you do not have one yet, mentioning that you are in process with other companies is enough to keep the recruiter moving.

Ask about joining bonuses and variable pay separately. Some companies cap base but are flexible on a one-time joining bonus or a higher variable component. These are negotiable even when 'the band is fixed.'

Use your notice period as a bargaining chip. A short notice period is worth something to a company that wants you quickly. You can ask for a higher joining bonus in exchange for a faster start or for buying out your notice.

Lead with your most in-demand tools. If you are proficient in Databricks or Snowflake, say so early. Industry surveys show these skills command a premium, and many non-technical recruiters are not fully aware of the difference.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

Base salary is only part of what you take home or build over time. For Data Engineers in Hyderabad, a typical total compensation package breaks down as follows.

Base salary. The primary fixed component, paid monthly. This is what the LPA figure refers to, and it is the number to anchor your negotiation on.

Variable pay / performance bonus. Commonly cited at 10-20% of base at mid and senior levels, paid annually or semi-annually. Actual payout depends on individual ratings and company performance, so treat this as a range rather than a guarantee.

Provident Fund (PF). Your employer contributes toward PF, commonly cited as 12% of your basic salary. This is deferred income, not take-home, but it adds up meaningfully over a long tenure.

ESOPs or RSUs. Product companies and well-funded startups often offer equity. At a GCC, you are unlikely to get ESOPs. At a Series B or later startup, equity can add significant value over a multi-year vesting period, though the outcome depends on the company's trajectory.

Health insurance. Most mid-size and large companies cover the employee plus family. Coverage amounts vary widely across employers, so ask for the specific policy details before signing.

Meal allowances and remote work stipends. Smaller items, but worth confirming if the role is hybrid or fully remote.

When comparing two offers, build a quick table: base, variable at full target, PF contribution, and equity value. A lower base with strong equity at the right company can outperform a higher base at a slower-growth firm.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the average salary for a Data Engineer in Hyderabad in 2026?

Based on knok jobradar tracking 23 active openings as of July 2026, ranges run from 6-12 LPA for entry-level roles to 42-65+ LPA for lead and staff positions. Mid-level engineers with 3-5 years of experience typically see offers between 14 and 26 LPA. These are offer-level ranges from active listings, not true averages, since most postings do not disclose pay publicly.

Is Hyderabad a good city for Data Engineers compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has roughly 4 times the open roles (92 vs 23 as of July 2026) and, according to Glassdoor and levels.fyi, tends to pay 10-15% more at senior and lead levels. Hyderabad is competitive for GCC roles in BFSI, pharma, and manufacturing sectors. If you prefer lower cost of living with stable corporate work, Hyderabad remains a solid market, especially if you are targeting large GCC employers.

Which skills help a Data Engineer earn more in Hyderabad?

Industry surveys consistently highlight cloud-native tools as the strongest salary drivers: Databricks, Snowflake, Apache Iceberg, dbt, and real-time streaming with Kafka or Flink. Python and SQL are table stakes at this point. Certifications on Databricks or AWS and Azure data services can help you negotiate into the upper half of your experience band, particularly when switching companies.

Do GCCs in Hyderabad pay as well as product companies?

GCCs generally offer structured pay bands, reliable annual increments, good benefits, and more predictable work hours. Product companies and startups often pay more at the top of each band and add equity, but with more variability in outcomes. According to publicly reported compensation data on Glassdoor, the base salary overlap between GCCs and mid-size product firms is significant at the mid level, so the real difference often comes down to equity and long-term growth, not just base.

How do I find out the salary range when a job listing does not show it?

Check Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the specific company and role, and ask the recruiter directly in the first call. Most recruiters will share at least a rough band if you ask plainly. knok checks 150+ job sites nightly, applies to jobs matching your resume, and messages HR for you, which means you get into multiple recruiter conversations faster and can build a clearer picture of market rates from several processes at once.

What salary jump should I expect when switching jobs as a Data Engineer?

Publicly reported data and industry surveys suggest job switches typically yield much larger increases than in-house annual increments, which are commonly cited at 8-12% in Indian tech. The exact jump depends on your current CTC, your updated skill set, and how active hiring is at the time. Always negotiate on the basis of the role's market rate rather than just a fixed percentage over your current salary.
