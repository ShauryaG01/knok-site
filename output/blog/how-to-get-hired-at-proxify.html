01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Proxify is a remote talent network that connects vetted software developers and designers with European and global clients. Unlike a typical job board, Proxify handles the matching, contracts, and payments on your behalf. You go through their screening once, and they actively connect you with long-term remote projects that fit your stack.

As of mid-2026, Proxify has 15 open roles across frontend, backend, full-stack, and mobile development tracks. The hiring process is genuinely competitive. They publicly report accepting only a small fraction of applicants, so this is not a platform where you upload a resume and wait. It is a structured vetting process that checks your technical depth, English fluency, and readiness to work independently for international clients.

For Indian developers looking to collaborate with European companies without relocating, Proxify offers a well-established path. But you need to prepare specifically for their process, which looks quite different from a typical Indian product company interview.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Proxify process typically has five stages, though the exact order may vary by role and the client involved.

Stage 1: Profile Application
You begin by building your Proxify profile, covering your tech stack, years of experience, portfolio links, and current availability. The team reviews profiles manually, so an incomplete or vague application often gets filtered before anyone contacts you. Treat this like a product page for yourself, not a standard resume upload.

Stage 2: English Communication Screening
If your profile is shortlisted, you will be invited for a short call focused almost entirely on spoken English. Proxify's clients are based outside India, so clear and confident communication is non-negotiable. Be calm, structured, and specific when describing your past work. Avoid over-rehearsed scripts and vague filler phrases.

Stage 3: Technical Assessment
This is typically an online coding test or a take-home task. Frontend candidates can expect challenges around component design, state management, or UI logic. Backend candidates may face API design problems, database queries, or system-level thinking. Complete this honestly on your own, because the live round that follows will reference your work closely.

Stage 4: Live Technical Interview
A Proxify interviewer walks through your assessment, asks follow-up questions, and may give you a fresh problem to solve on the spot. This round tests how you think and communicate under pressure, not just whether your code runs. Expect questions on design trade-offs, system architecture basics, and how you handle ambiguous requirements, all of which are common in remote client work.

Stage 5: Profile Approval and Client Matching
Once you clear the technical round, your profile enters Proxify's vetted talent pool. You are then matched with client projects based on your skills and availability. Some candidates get matched within days; others wait a few weeks depending on current demand for their specific stack.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Proxify screens for three things above everything else: technical depth, English communication, and remote-work discipline.

Technical Depth
Proxify wants developers who genuinely understand the tools they list on their resume. Surface-level familiarity is not enough. If you claim expertise in React, you should be comfortable discussing hooks, performance optimisation, and state management patterns in detail. The same standard applies across backend, mobile, and data roles. Listing ten frameworks and knowing none of them deeply is a quick way to fail the live interview.

English Communication
This is where many strong technical candidates trip up. Proxify's clients expect clear written and spoken updates throughout a project. You do not need to sound like a native speaker, but you must express ideas precisely, ask the right questions, and write professional messages without heavy reliance on filler words or long pauses.

Remote-Work Maturity
Proxify values people who have worked remotely before or can clearly demonstrate they understand what it requires: proactive updates, meeting deadlines without supervision, managing time zone differences, and flagging blockers early. Any freelance, contract, or distributed-team experience should be front and centre in your profile.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Polish Your Profile Before You Apply
Your Proxify profile is the first thing screeners see. Write a crisp professional summary, list your stack honestly (depth over breadth), and link to real projects with code on GitHub or live demos where possible. Vague or incomplete profiles get filtered fast, before any technical evaluation begins.

Practice Speaking About Your Work
Record yourself explaining a past project in a couple of minutes: what the problem was, what you built, and what trade-offs you made. Watch it back. If you stumble or reach for vague words like 'stuff' or 'basically', rewrite and re-record until you sound confident and specific. This exercise directly prepares you for the English screening call.

Revise Core Fundamentals
Go back to basics in your primary stack. For developers, that means data structures, algorithms (focus on arrays, strings, graphs, and dynamic programming), and system design concepts like caching, load balancing, and database indexing. Proxify's technical bar is publicly reported as high relative to typical Indian product companies.

Simulate the Online Test Under Time Pressure
Practice on platforms like LeetCode or HackerRank with a timer running. The goal is not to memorise solutions but to stay calm, read problems carefully, and write readable, well-commented code that shows your reasoning clearly to the reviewer.

Prepare Remote-Work Stories
Have a few concrete examples ready of times you managed your own schedule, handled a miscommunication with a teammate, or delivered something independently without being chased. Structure each around the situation, the action you took, and the result you achieved.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Proxify operates as a fully remote company with no physical offices and no fixed-hour check-ins. The culture is built around trust, professional ownership, and consistent delivery to clients.

Ownership Over Oversight
Proxify developers are expected to behave like partners, not contractors waiting for a task list. Clients value people who spot issues early, propose solutions, and take initiative without needing to be nudged. If you prefer a heavily structured environment where work is assigned to you daily, this culture may feel uncomfortable at first.

Quality and Consistency
Proxify's reputation with clients depends on every developer it places. There is a strong internal emphasis on clean code, professional communication, and keeping commitments. Missing a deadline or sending a sloppy client update reflects on the platform, and Proxify takes that seriously during both hiring and ongoing work.

Global Mindset
Working through Proxify typically means collaborating with teams across Europe and occasionally other regions. Comfort with asynchronous communication, sensitivity to different working styles, and willingness to adapt to client time zones are all valued traits. Indian developers who have previously worked with international clients or in globally distributed teams tend to adjust quickly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does the Proxify process take from application to first project?

The vetting process, from profile submission to final approval, typically takes one to three weeks depending on how quickly you complete each stage. After approval, client matching can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks, based on your tech stack and current demand. Niche or in-demand skills tend to get matched faster than common ones.

Does Proxify hire freshers or only experienced developers?

Proxify sets a high bar aimed at professionals with solid, demonstrable experience, not entry-level candidates. Their clients expect developers who can contribute from day one with minimal hand-holding. If you are early in your career, building a strong portfolio and contributing to open-source projects first will make your application significantly stronger before you apply.

What kind of pay can I expect working through Proxify?

Proxify typically pays developers in USD or EUR, and rates vary by role, stack, seniority, and client. Publicly reported figures on forums and review sites suggest the pay is competitive relative to Indian market rates, especially at the senior level. For realistic benchmarks specific to your stack, check Glassdoor or levels.fyi before entering any rate negotiation.

Can I work with Proxify while holding a full-time job in India?

Proxify projects are typically long-term and full-time commitments, not side gigs. Taking on a Proxify engagement while already employed elsewhere could violate your current employment contract and create direct conflicts of interest. Review your existing agreement carefully and make a clean transition before committing to a client project through Proxify.

What happens when a client project ends? Do I have to go through screening again?

When a project wraps up, Proxify will try to match you to a new client from their network, so you do not restart the full vetting process from scratch. The speed of re-matching depends on demand for your skills at that point in time. Keeping your profile updated and staying responsive to Proxify's team helps speed things up considerably.

Is Proxify's technical interview harder than what Indian startups typically ask?

Candidates who have been through both often describe Proxify's process as more rigorous, especially because the English communication screening is layered on top of the coding round as an independent pass or fail gate. That combination is not common in domestic hiring. Prepare as you would for a mid-to-senior product company interview, then add dedicated spoken English practice on top of your technical prep.
