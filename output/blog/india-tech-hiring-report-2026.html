06 Key Findings

Key Findings

As of 8 July 2026, the knok jobradar index points to the following patterns across Indian tech hiring.

  • AI and machine learning engineering roles are the fastest-moving category in the index. Postings attract applicants within hours and frequently close within days of going live.
  • Bengaluru accounts for the single largest share of senior tech listings. Hyderabad and Pune follow, while Kochi and Coimbatore are showing steady growth in IT services roles.
  • Remote-friendly openings remain a meaningful portion of total tech listings, concentrated in SaaS, cloud, and IT services companies. Most require candidates already based in India.
  • Demand for candidates who can pair AI tool fluency with a core domain skill (data, backend, DevOps) is publicly reported as rising sharply compared to 2024 and 2025.
  • Early-stage startup hiring is up relative to 2024, according to industry surveys tracking Indian venture-backed companies. Growth is concentrated in fintech, deeptech, and B2B SaaS.
  • Contract and project-based roles are a growing slice of tech postings, especially in data engineering, QA automation, and cloud infrastructure.
07 Methodology

Methodology

This report draws from the knok jobradar snapshot dated 8 July 2026. knok scans 150+ Indian and global job boards nightly, indexes postings by role, location, and employer, and surfaces patterns for job seekers. 11 What It Means

What It Means

What this snapshot means for your job search, honestly stated.

The knok jobradar index is a directional signal, not a guarantee. Job counts shift daily, and a 'very high demand' signal for AI roles does not mean every AI candidate will land an offer quickly. Competition in those categories is also high, and many applicants are targeting the same postings.

A few things the data does suggest for right now. First, skilling up in AI-adjacent areas (prompt engineering, ML pipelines, AI-powered product features) appears to widen your pool of relevant roles regardless of your core function. Second, Bengaluru listings are the most numerous, but if cost of living or relocation is a constraint, Pune and Hyderabad offer credible alternatives with broad hiring activity. Third, the growth in contract roles means a short-term engagement can serve as a practical entry point into a company or sector you are targeting.

On salaries: benchmarking is best done with real offers in hand. Ranges commonly cited on Glassdoor and levels.fyi for similar roles give a starting reference, but they reflect a wide mix of company sizes and experience levels. Use them as a floor for negotiation, not a promise.

Honest limitation: the knok snapshot captures what is posted publicly, not what is filled internally or through referrals. A meaningful share of senior roles never appear on any job board. Building a referral pipeline alongside applying through job sites is consistently cited in industry surveys as the highest-return job search activity. Both matter.

How fresh is the data in this report?

The underlying knok jobradar snapshot was taken on 8 July 2026. Job postings move fast, so treat role demand signals as a directional read for mid-2026 rather than a precise daily count. Which tech roles have the most openings right now?

Based on the knok jobradar index, AI/ML engineering and data engineering show the strongest demand signals in mid-2026. Backend engineering remains consistently active across both product companies and IT services. QA roles are shifting toward automation-first hiring, so a purely manual testing profile faces more competition than it did two years ago.

Which city gives the best chance of landing a tech job?

Bengaluru has the highest volume of listings across seniority levels, especially for product and SaaS companies. Hyderabad is strong for MNC and IT services roles. If you are open to relocation, those two cities offer the widest top-of-funnel. Pune and Chennai are solid for mid-level roles with slightly less competition per opening compared to Bengaluru.

What salary can I expect in these roles?

Salary ranges for Indian tech roles are commonly cited on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, and they vary widely by company type, city, and years of experience. Use publicly reported ranges as a reference point, and always cross-check with real offers from people in your network. The knok index tracks job postings, not compensation data, so we do not publish specific LPA figures from our own dataset.

Is 2026 a good time to switch jobs in Indian tech?

Industry surveys and publicly reported hiring trends suggest the market is more active in 2026 than the cautious period of 2024. AI-related roles are particularly buoyant, but competition for those same roles is also higher. Switching tends to go better when you have a clear skill upgrade, a target company in mind, or a referral lined up, rather than simply responding to market optimism.
