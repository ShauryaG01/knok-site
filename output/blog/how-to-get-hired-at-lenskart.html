01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Lenskart is one of India's fastest-growing consumer tech and eyewear companies, best known for making prescription glasses accessible and affordable across the country. The company runs a large network of retail stores alongside its e-commerce platform, and it has expanded into markets beyond India in recent years. Peyush Bansal, the founder, is a familiar face for many Indians as a judge on Shark Tank India, which has made the brand even more recognizable.

As of 2026, Lenskart has 228 open roles across a wide range of functions. Tech and product roles, retail and store operations, supply chain, customer support, marketing, and corporate functions like finance and HR are all active hiring areas. This spread means the company is relevant whether you are a software engineer, a sales professional, a logistics expert, or a fresh graduate looking to start your career in retail.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The interview process at Lenskart varies by function and seniority, but a few common stages appear across most hiring tracks.

Step 1: Online application and resume screening. You apply through the Lenskart careers page or a job platform. The recruiter reviews your resume to check for relevant experience and a basic match with the role requirements. For tech roles, an online assessment or coding test is commonly added before any human conversation.

Step 2: HR screening call. A recruiter typically reaches out to confirm your interest, current location, notice period, and salary expectations. This round is usually brief. Being clear and honest here saves everyone time.

Step 3: Technical or functional round(s). For tech roles, this usually means one or two rounds covering data structures, system design, or domain-specific skills depending on the level. For non-tech roles such as retail management, supply chain, or marketing, expect case-based or situational questions relevant to that function.

Step 4: Manager or leadership round. This is where the hiring manager, and sometimes a senior leader, assesses your problem-solving approach, how you have handled past challenges, and how well you understand Lenskart's business context. For senior roles, this round can include a business case or a short presentation.

Step 5: HR final round and offer. Once technical and functional rounds clear, HR closes out with a final conversation around compensation, joining timelines, and expectations. Offers are typically rolled out within a week or two after this stage, though timelines can vary.

For retail store roles, the process is often shorter: an initial HR call followed by a direct interaction with the area or store manager, sometimes within the same week.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Customer obsession. Lenskart's entire model depends on delivering a smooth experience, whether someone is shopping online, trying frames in a store, or getting their eyes tested. They want people who genuinely care about the customer outcome, not just completing a transaction.

Execution and ownership. This is a fast-moving company that has scaled rapidly. Candidates who take ownership of their work, follow through without being chased, and can work with ambiguity tend to do well here.

Comfort with data. Even for non-tech roles, Lenskart uses data to drive decisions. Being able to read a sales dashboard, understand conversion numbers, or explain performance trends is valued across functions.

Retail or consumer brand experience. For operations, store management, and customer-facing roles, prior experience in retail, FMCG, or consumer services is a clear advantage. They are looking for people who understand how things work at the ground level.

Tech curiosity (for tech and product roles). Lenskart positions itself as a tech company that happens to sell eyewear. Engineers and product managers are expected to think about scale, user experience, and how technology can improve both the shopping journey and internal operations.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Know the business inside out. Before any round, understand how Lenskart makes money: glasses sold online and in stores, eye tests as a traffic driver, and value-added products like blue-light lenses and coatings. Read recent news about their expansion plans, new markets, and any partnership announcements.

Prepare role-specific stories. Use the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. Think of a few clear examples from your past work that show ownership, handling pressure, working with teams, and delivering results.

For tech roles, practise fundamentals. Brush up on data structures, algorithms, and system design basics. If you are applying for a senior role, be ready to discuss how you would architect a feature end to end, including scaling considerations.

For retail or sales roles, think in outcomes. Be ready to discuss your previous targets, what you achieved, how you handled slower months, and how you coached your team. Concrete results build credibility in these rounds.

Research compensation ranges. Publicly reported data on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi can give you a sense of what the market looks like for your function. Go into your HR call with a clear expectation in mind, not a vague range.

Show you care about the product. Try on Lenskart frames, use the app, check out a store if you can. Candidates who have actually used the product and have genuine feedback stand out from those who have only read the website.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Lenskart operates with a startup energy that has not fully dissolved even as the company has grown into a large employer. Speed matters here. Teams move quickly, iterate fast, and are expected to adapt when priorities shift. If you have only worked in large, process-heavy organizations, the pace can feel jarring at first.

Founder-driven culture. Peyush Bansal is very visible inside the organization, and his priorities filter down clearly. There is a strong emphasis on frugality, customer impact, and getting things done without waiting for perfect conditions.

Diverse team. The employee base ranges from freshers in retail stores to senior tech talent. This creates an unusual mix of perspectives, and day-to-day collaboration often crosses hierarchies more than it does at traditional companies.

Growth focus. Lenskart is still in a growth phase, and many roles come with the chance to take on more responsibility faster than you might elsewhere. That upside comes with the expectation that you will stretch yourself, especially in the first year.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many jobs are currently open at Lenskart?

As of mid-2026, Lenskart has around 228 open roles. These span tech, retail, supply chain, marketing, customer support, and corporate functions. The number shifts regularly as positions close and new ones open, so it is worth checking the careers page often.

Is Lenskart a good company for freshers?

Lenskart hires freshers actively, especially in retail store roles and some entry-level corporate tracks. The environment is fast-paced, which means you can learn quickly and take on responsibility early. That said, freshers should be comfortable with performance targets and direct feedback from the start.

How long does the Lenskart hiring process take?

For retail and frontline roles, the process can move in under a week. Corporate and tech roles typically take a few weeks from application to offer, depending on interviewer availability and how many rounds are involved. Staying responsive to recruiter messages usually speeds things up.

What salary can I expect at Lenskart?

Compensation varies widely by role and level. Based on publicly reported data on Glassdoor and industry surveys, tech roles at mid-senior levels are commonly cited in ranges competitive with other consumer tech companies in India. The best move is to research your specific function and experience band before your HR call so you can give a clear number.

Does Lenskart hire for remote roles?

Some corporate and tech roles have flexible or hybrid arrangements, but many Lenskart positions in retail, supply chain, and operations require in-person presence. Check the specific job description for the work location requirement before applying, since it varies significantly by function.

What are common reasons candidates do not get through at Lenskart?

Candidates often get filtered out for going in with little knowledge of the business model, giving vague behavioural answers without clear outcomes, or having misaligned salary expectations. For tech roles, weak fundamentals in data structures or system design are a common rejection point. Preparing specific examples and genuinely understanding what Lenskart does goes a long way.
