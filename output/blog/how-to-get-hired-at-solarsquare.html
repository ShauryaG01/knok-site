01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

SolarSquare is one of India's fastest-growing residential solar companies, helping homeowners cut electricity bills with rooftop solar panels. The company has expanded aggressively across Indian cities, and as of mid-2026, it has 177 open roles spanning sales, operations, engineering, finance, and customer success.

Most hiring happens in sales and field operations, reflecting the company's focus on acquiring homeowners as customers. But there is also steady demand for engineers, project managers, data analysts, and corporate support functions.

With 177 roles open at once, the hiring team is active and processes move relatively quickly for a company of this size. If your profile fits, you are likely to hear back within days of applying.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process typically runs across a few rounds, though the exact number varies by role and seniority. Here is what candidates commonly report, with the caveat that each team may run things slightly differently.

Initial Screening (HR Call)
A recruiter or HR team member reaches out first. This is a brief call covering your background, notice period, location preference, and salary expectations. It moves quickly if your profile matches the job description.

Hiring Manager Round
This is a conversational round. The manager wants to understand your work history, your reason for wanting to join SolarSquare, and whether you have the domain knowledge the role needs. Sales candidates often get scenario questions here, such as how you would explain solar to a hesitant homeowner.

Technical or Functional Round
For engineering and product roles, expect questions on your core skills and past projects. For sales and business roles, this may be a roleplay or a case exercise. The goal is to see how you handle real work, not just how you talk about it.

Final Round with Leadership
A senior leader or a founding team member typically joins the last round. This is less about testing skills and more about culture fit, long-term thinking, and your understanding of where the solar industry is heading in India.

Offer and Onboarding
If selected, you get a verbal offer followed by a formal letter. Background verification is standard. Onboarding is structured but moves fast, especially for sales roles where targets begin early.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

SolarSquare hires for pace and purpose. Most successful candidates share a few traits.

  • Drive in customer-facing roles. A large share of their openings are in sales. They want people who can build trust with homeowners, explain a technical product simply, and close deals in a competitive market. Prior B2C or field sales experience stands out.
  • Operational discipline. Installation, project management, and supply chain roles need people who can coordinate across teams and keep projects on schedule without constant oversight.
  • Technical grounding. Engineering and product candidates are expected to have strong fundamentals and the ability to work in a lean team. They value people who can own problems end to end.
  • Genuine interest in clean energy. The interview team can tell the difference between a candidate who memorized a few solar facts and one who actually cares about the mission. If you are curious about how India's energy story is playing out, say so with specifics.
05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Spend some time understanding how the residential solar business works before your interview. Know the basics: how rooftop solar is installed, what the typical homeowner objection sounds like (upfront cost, roof ownership concerns, maintenance worries), and how SolarSquare positions itself against competitors.

For sales roles, practice explaining solar in plain language to someone unfamiliar with the product. The best candidates can describe why solar makes financial sense in a few sentences, without using technical jargon. Prepare for a roleplay where you handle objections live.

For technical roles, be ready to walk through past projects in detail. SolarSquare interviewers tend to ask follow-up questions that test whether you actually built something or just observed it get built.

Before your final round, look up recent SolarSquare news, their expansion markets, and any press coverage about their product or fundraising. Asking one sharp, informed question at the end signals real interest and separates you from candidates who only researched the company on the morning of the call.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

SolarSquare runs like a startup even as it scales. The culture is high-energy, target-driven, and mission-oriented. People who thrive here tend to be self-starters who do not need a lot of structure to get things done.

The team is relatively young and the company has grown fast. This means roles can evolve quickly, and you may pick up responsibilities that were not in the original job description. If you prefer a clearly scoped role with defined processes, expect some adjustment in the early months.

The mission of making solar accessible to Indian homeowners is a genuine motivator for many employees. You will hear it come up in interviews, in onboarding, and in how leadership talks about the business. Candidates who connect with that purpose, and can point to why, tend to find the pace more sustainable long term.

Feedback from employees on public review platforms commonly cites good learning opportunities and a strong sense of purpose as positives. High targets and a fast pace come up as challenges, particularly in sales roles.
