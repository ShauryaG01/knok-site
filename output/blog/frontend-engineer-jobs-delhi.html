05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Show working projects, not just skills listed on paper. A GitHub repo or live demo of something you built tells a recruiter far more than a list of technologies. Interviewers at companies like Sprinklr and Adobe will almost certainly ask you to walk through past work, so have something ready before you apply.

Go deep on React (or your primary framework). Delhi NCR roles skew heavily toward React. Know hooks, rendering behaviour, state management libraries (Redux, Zustand, or Context), and how to structure a large application. Shallow knowledge gets filtered out at the screening stage.

Add performance and accessibility to your vocabulary. 'I restructured lazy loading and reduced initial load time' is concrete. 'I care about performance' is not. Even one measurable improvement you drove makes your resume stand out from the pile.

Brush up on fundamentals before every round. HTML semantics, CSS layout (flexbox and grid), browser APIs, and JavaScript basics (closures, the event loop, promises) come up repeatedly, especially at mid-level and above.

Tailor your resume to the company type. A product company like Paytm wants to see user-facing impact and fast iteration. An enterprise lab like Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs may weigh code quality, documentation habits, and long-term maintainability more heavily. One generic resume does not serve all these hiring contexts equally.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply early in the hiring cycle. With only 36 live roles in Delhi NCR, openings at well-known companies fill fast. Aim to apply within the first week of a posting going live so you get in front of a smaller applicant pool.

Write a focused cover note, even a short one. Most candidates skip this entirely. Two or three sentences explaining why this specific company interests you, plus one line connecting your background to their product, can move your application to the top of a recruiter's review pile.

Use LinkedIn and company career pages together. Some Delhi NCR roles appear on company sites before they hit job boards. Checking directly saves you from competing with a flood of later applicants.

Follow up once, politely. If you can find a recruiter or hiring manager on LinkedIn, a brief message referencing your application is acceptable. Keep it to one sentence about the role and one about what you bring. Do not follow up more than once.

Practise a timed coding exercise. Most frontend hiring processes in Delhi NCR include a take-home or live coding round. Build small components under time pressure: a debounced search bar, an infinite scroll list, or a form with validation.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Delhi NCR a strong city for frontend engineers, or should I target Bangalore?

Delhi NCR has a real and active frontend market with 36 live roles as of July 2026, including positions at Adobe, Sprinklr, and Paytm. Bangalore leads nationally with 102 openings, so if sheer volume of choice is your priority, it is worth considering a move or targeting remote roles there. That said, Delhi NCR is a solid second city, and if you prefer to stay in the region, the quality of companies hiring here is genuinely strong.

Which frontend skills are most in demand in Delhi NCR right now?

React is the dominant framework in Delhi NCR's frontend job listings, followed by TypeScript and strong core JavaScript proficiency. Companies hiring at mid-level and above frequently look for experience with state management libraries, REST or GraphQL APIs, and basic performance optimisation. Adding unit testing (Jest, React Testing Library) and familiarity with build tools like Webpack or Vite rounds out a competitive profile.

How long does a typical frontend hiring process take in Delhi NCR?

Most Delhi NCR frontend processes run two to four weeks from application to offer, though larger companies can stretch longer. A common pattern is an initial recruiter screen, one or two technical rounds including a coding exercise, and a final HR or culture-fit call. Product companies like Sprinklr sometimes add an additional system design or product thinking round on top of that.

How should I negotiate salary as a frontend engineer in Delhi NCR?

Anchor your negotiation to commonly cited ranges for your experience band: 5-11 LPA for entry level, 12-22 LPA for mid level, and 24-40 LPA for senior roles, per knok's jobradar data. Come in with a specific number rather than a range, and be ready to justify it with your years of experience, the complexity of products you have built, and any competing offers you hold. Do not accept the first offer without at least one polite counter.

Are the 36 Delhi NCR roles remote, hybrid, or in-office?

The knok jobradar data counts roles posted for Delhi NCR locations, which covers Gurugram, Noida, and central Delhi. The mix of remote, hybrid, and in-office arrangements varies by company. Larger enterprises like Adobe and Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs tend to have structured hybrid policies, while startups may offer more flexibility. Always check the individual job description for the specific arrangement before applying.

Should I apply to startups or established companies in Delhi NCR?

It depends on what you are optimising for at this stage of your career. Established companies like Adobe and Sprinklr offer structured growth paths, a strong brand name on your resume, and more predictable compensation bands. Startups like wishlink and webengage typically offer faster progression, more ownership over product decisions, and sometimes equity. If you are early in your career and want to build breadth quickly, a well-funded startup is a strong choice; if you want stability and a recognisable name, go for the established players.
