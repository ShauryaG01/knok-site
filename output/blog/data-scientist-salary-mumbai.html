01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Data Scientist job listings in India do not publish a salary figure. Recruiters hold that number back until the interview stage, which leaves candidates guessing whether a role is worth their time before investing days in rounds. The salary bands below are drawn from knok jobradar, which tracked 17 active Data Scientist openings in Mumbai as of 2026-07-08, alongside what professionals in this field publicly report at each career stage.

In broad terms, a Data Scientist in Mumbai can expect anywhere from 8 LPA at the entry level up to 70+ LPA at the lead or principal level. Mumbai's market skews toward BFSI (banking, financial services, and insurance), e-commerce, and media companies, where domain knowledge in finance or content analytics is valued. Industry surveys commonly cite this as a factor that lifts mid-to-senior pay in Mumbai relative to purely tech-focused markets. If you are just starting out, target the 8-16 LPA band. If you have three or more years of solid ML and analytics work, the 18-30 LPA range is realistic. Senior professionals and team leads can negotiate well above that.

Entry-level hires typically join product companies or analytics firms where the work involves data cleaning, basic modelling, and building dashboards for business teams. The pay jump from entry to mid is significant, and so are the expectations. Mid-level data scientists are expected to own end-to-end pipelines, choose the right model for the problem, and communicate findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

Senior roles demand deep ML knowledge plus the ability to mentor junior team members and push back constructively on product decisions when the data says otherwise. At the lead and principal level, you are setting the data strategy for a product line or a business unit, often managing a small team and influencing hiring. That scope of responsibility is what pushes the ceiling to 70+ LPA.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Most Indian employers expect you to negotiate. Coming in with a number backed by market data puts you in a much stronger position than saying 'I am flexible.'

Know the band before the call. The salary bands in this guide give you a starting anchor. If a recruiter asks for your expectation without sharing the budget, it is fair to ask them for the band first. Many will share it.

Cite results, not just tools. 'I built a churn model that reduced customer drop-off' lands better than listing Python and SQL on a slide. Quantified business impact is the strongest negotiation lever for a data scientist.

Variable pay is real money. Many BFSI and product companies in Mumbai offer a variable or performance bonus on top of base salary. Always confirm whether the number a recruiter quotes is fixed pay, fixed plus variable, or total CTC, because the difference can be significant.

ESOPs at startups need scrutiny. If a startup offers ESOPs as a big chunk of the package, ask about the vesting schedule, the cliff period, and when the company last had a valuation update. ESOPs are only valuable if the company grows and provides a liquidity event.

Counter with total CTC, not just base. If the base is non-negotiable, push on a joining bonus, extra variable, remote flexibility, or a performance review after six months. Compensation is a package, not a single number.

You do not have to share your current salary first. Some employers ask for your current CTC early in the process. You can decline politely and redirect the conversation to market rates and the value you bring.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

In India, especially in Mumbai's BFSI and tech sectors, the number a recruiter quotes is rarely what lands in your account each month. Understanding the structure of a CTC (cost to company) package helps you compare offers accurately.

Base salary is the fixed monthly pay before tax. This is the most reliable number in any offer and the one to anchor your comparison on.

Variable or performance bonus is paid quarterly or annually, tied to individual performance ratings and company results. The size varies by company and role level. Always ask for the CTC breakup to understand how much of your package is guaranteed versus performance-linked.

ESOPs or RSUs are common in product companies and startups. They vest over a multi-year period, usually with a cliff in the first year. The paper value can look large, but it only becomes real money at a liquidity event or when the stock can be freely sold.

PF (Provident Fund) is a mandatory deduction taken from both the employee and employer sides. It reduces your monthly in-hand amount but builds a long-term retirement corpus. Factor this in when comparing in-hand salary across offers.

Gratuity and allowances such as HRA, medical, and LTA are often built into the CTC structure. Always ask for a full CTC breakup so you can calculate your actual in-hand salary, since the headline CTC and the in-hand figure can differ significantly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the average Data Scientist salary in Mumbai in 2026?

Based on knok jobradar data tracked as of 2026-07-08, Data Scientist salaries in Mumbai range from 8 LPA at the entry level to 70+ LPA at the lead and principal level. The mid-level band of 3-5 years experience sits at 18-30 LPA, which is the most active bracket in current listings. These figures reflect what employers are offering or what professionals publicly report, since most Indian job listings do not publish pay ranges.

Is Mumbai a good city for Data Scientist jobs compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore had 166 active Data Scientist openings versus Mumbai's 17 as of 2026-07-08, so the raw job volume is significantly higher in Bangalore. Mumbai's openings cluster in BFSI and media companies where domain expertise is valued, as commonly cited in industry compensation surveys. If you want a larger volume of options and a stronger tech startup ecosystem, Bangalore is the better market. If you prefer finance or media and want to stay in Mumbai, the roles available tend to be focused and well-paying for the right profile.

How much does a fresher Data Scientist earn in Mumbai?

Entry-level Data Scientists in Mumbai with 0-2 years of experience can expect offers in the 8-16 LPA range based on knok jobradar data. The actual number within that band depends on company type, your academic background, projects, and whether you have internship experience with real business impact. Freshers joining BFSI firms may start closer to the higher end of the band if they bring domain knowledge in credit, risk, or fraud detection.

What skills help a Data Scientist negotiate a higher salary in Mumbai?

Employers in Mumbai, especially in BFSI and product companies, value a combination of technical skills (Python, SQL, ML frameworks) and domain knowledge in finance, fraud detection, or customer analytics. Candidates who can show quantified results from past projects negotiate from a much stronger position than those who list tools alone. Communication skills matter too, since data scientists in Mumbai often present findings directly to business stakeholders who are not technical.

What is included in a Data Scientist CTC in Mumbai?

A typical CTC offer in Mumbai includes base salary, a variable or performance bonus, mandatory PF contributions from both employee and employer, gratuity, and allowances like HRA and LTA. Product companies and startups may also include ESOPs or RSUs that vest over a multi-year period. Always ask for a full CTC breakup and calculate your actual in-hand monthly salary before comparing offers, since the headline CTC and the in-hand figure often differ significantly.

Why do most companies in Mumbai not post salary ranges in their job listings?

Salary disclosure is not legally required for most private sector employers in India, so companies default to withholding it to preserve negotiating leverage and avoid discontent among existing employees who might see the posted range. This puts candidates at a disadvantage because they go through multiple interview rounds before knowing if the budget fits their expectations. Platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi publish self-reported compensation data you can use to benchmark pay before you apply or before you receive an offer.
