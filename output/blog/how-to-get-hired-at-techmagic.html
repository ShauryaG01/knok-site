01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Techmagic is a software development company that builds web and mobile products for startups and scale-ups around the world. If you enjoy working on real client products, communicating with international teams, and owning your work end to end, Techmagic is worth a serious look.

As of July 2026, Techmagic has 26 open roles across engineering, design, and related technical functions. The company works on a client-engagement model, meaning you join Techmagic as a full-time employee and are staffed on projects that match your skills. This setup gives you variety in the work you do and exposure to different industries and tech stacks, while keeping you within a stable team structure.

Techmagic has built a reputation for strong engineering standards and a product-minded culture. They hire people who can think beyond the code and genuinely care about delivering something that works well for the end user.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Techmagic typically moves through four to five stages, though the exact steps can vary by role and seniority. Here is what most candidates can expect.

Recruiter screening: A recruiter or HR contact usually reaches out after your application to verify your experience, availability, and rough salary expectations. This call is short and fairly informal. It is mainly to confirm you are worth moving forward before taking up an engineer's time.

Technical assessment: Many roles include a take-home task or a live coding exercise. Frontend candidates commonly work with React; backend candidates may be asked to build a small API or solve a Node.js-related problem. Some senior roles skip the take-home and go straight to a technical interview round.

Technical interview: This is a video call with a senior engineer or tech lead. Expect questions on your core stack, how you approach system design, and how you have handled real technical challenges in past projects. For senior candidates, the conversation often goes deeper into architecture and trade-offs.

Culture and team fit interview: A final conversation with a manager or team lead focuses on how you communicate, how you handle ambiguity, and whether your working style fits the team. This round sometimes includes a discussion on compensation.

Offer: If everything goes well, most candidates receive an offer within one to two weeks of the final round. Timelines can stretch depending on role availability and ongoing project needs.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Techmagic builds products for international clients, so the bar for technical skill and professional communication is high. Here is what they consistently value.

Strong command of a core stack: Depth in at least one major technology, whether that is React, Node.js, TypeScript, AWS, or a mobile framework, is expected. They want engineers who understand the 'why' behind their choices, not just the syntax.

Clear communication in English: Since most projects involve international clients, the ability to explain your reasoning, ask good questions, and give clear status updates matters as much as your code. This is about professional communication habits, not just language fluency.

Visible problem-solving process: Interviewers tend to care about how you think through a problem, not just whether you arrive at the right answer. Practice walking through your reasoning out loud, especially in technical rounds.

Ownership and follow-through: Techmagic values engineers who treat client problems as their own. Flagging risks early, suggesting better approaches, and following through without hand-holding are all traits that stand out during interviews.

Adaptability: Since you may move across different client projects over time, comfort with switching contexts and picking up new domains quickly is a genuine plus.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparation for a Techmagic interview is straightforward if you focus on the right areas.

Revisit your core stack: Whether your focus is frontend, backend, or full-stack, go back to the fundamentals. For React developers, hooks, state management, and rendering performance are commonly tested. For backend candidates, API design, database modelling, and scalability patterns come up often.

Practice system design: Even at mid-level, Techmagic interviewers like to see that you can think in systems. Practice designing simple end-to-end architectures and be ready to discuss trade-offs (for example, SQL vs. NoSQL, or REST vs. GraphQL).

Prepare two or three project stories: Pick real projects where you solved a meaningful technical problem. Know the business context, the choices you made, and the outcome. Be ready to go deep if the interviewer asks follow-up questions.

Work on your English communication: Record yourself explaining a recent project for two minutes and listen back. This simple exercise helps you sound more confident and clear in video interviews.

Research Techmagic's work: Look at the kinds of products they build and the clients they work with. Understanding their typical project profile helps you align your answers to what they actually value on the job.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Techmagic describes itself as a product-minded company, which in practice means engineers are expected to think beyond writing code and care about what gets delivered to the end user. The culture tends to be collaborative, with a relatively flat structure where engineers have a real voice in technical decisions rather than simply executing instructions from above.

The team operates in a remote or hybrid setup, so self-discipline and async communication habits matter. You are expected to be proactive, whether that means raising a blocker early, suggesting a better technical approach, or simply keeping teammates informed without being asked.

Learning is treated as continuous, not optional. Code reviews, internal knowledge-sharing, and staying current with your craft are all part of how the team works day to day. Engineers who actively invest in their skills tend to do well here.

For Indian candidates, the international client exposure Techmagic offers is a genuine differentiator. Working on products used by global users, within a structured and process-driven team, builds the kind of experience that is hard to get at a purely domestic-facing company.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Techmagic hire engineers based in India?

Techmagic works with a distributed team and has hired engineers from multiple geographies. If you have strong technical skills and communicate well in English, your location is generally not a barrier. Check the specific job listing to confirm whether the role you are targeting is open to remote candidates in India, as this can vary by position.

What tech stack does Techmagic mainly use?

Techmagic's core technology focus is JavaScript and TypeScript, with React on the frontend and Node.js on the backend. They also work extensively with AWS for cloud infrastructure. Depending on the client project, you may encounter other tools and frameworks, so adaptability matters alongside your primary stack expertise.

How long does the full hiring process take at Techmagic?

Most candidates who go through the full process report a timeline of two to four weeks from initial recruiter contact to offer. The process can move faster if there is an urgent opening, or stretch a little longer if the team is between client projects. Following up politely after each round is a reasonable way to stay visible without being pushy.

What salary can I expect at Techmagic?

Publicly reported compensation at Techmagic varies by role, experience level, and geography. Glassdoor and LinkedIn Salary are good starting points to benchmark what the market is paying for your specific skills and seniority. Come to the recruiter screening with a range in mind based on your own research rather than waiting for them to name a number first.

Is the Techmagic interview hard to crack?

The technical bar is solid but not unusual for a company of Techmagic's reputation. Candidates who do well tend to have real project experience they can speak to in depth, clean fundamentals in their core stack, and the ability to communicate their thinking clearly during technical rounds. Preparing two or three strong project stories and brushing up on system design concepts puts most candidates in a good position.

Does Techmagic offer full-time roles or only contract positions?

Techmagic primarily hires full-time employees who are then staffed on client engagements. This is different from pure freelancing or contracting. You get the stability of a permanent role while working across a variety of projects, which is a model many engineers prefer when they want variety without giving up job security.
