01 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

As of 2026-07-08, 21 live iOS Engineer openings were active in Delhi NCR. Across other cities in the same scan, Bangalore had 6 openings, Mumbai had 3, and Chennai had 2. Hyderabad and Pune showed no active iOS listings at the time.

One number stands out immediately: Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs accounts for 13 of those 21 openings. Remove that single employer and the rest of the market is 8 roles spread across Zomato, Jio Platforms, Classplus, and a handful of others.

This is a thin but active market. Twenty-one openings is not a flood, but the employer mix is genuinely diverse. You have a large MNC-backed tech lab on one side and growth-stage consumer apps on the other, which means a real choice between corporate stability and startup pace.

If you are open to other cities, Bangalore remains the deeper iOS market nationwide with 6 openings in this snapshot. But if Delhi NCR is your base, the roles here are real and worth acting on quickly.

04 Salary Range

Salary Range

Salary figures are not available from this specific dataset, so what follows is based entirely on publicly reported figures and industry surveys.

Glassdoor and industry surveys commonly place mid-level iOS Engineers (3-6 years of experience) in the 15-28 LPA range at mid-to-large tech companies in Delhi NCR. Senior engineers with 6-plus years are commonly cited at 30-45 LPA at large MNCs and well-funded startups, per Glassdoor and levels.fyi. Entry-level roles are commonly cited in the 6-12 LPA range.

These are market-level estimates, not verified figures from the specific openings listed above. Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs and Jio Platforms tend to pay at the corporate benchmark end, based on publicly reported employee data. Startup roles at WinZO or Classplus may offer equity alongside base pay, which can make the total package more competitive than the base salary alone.

Before you negotiate with any employer on this list, check their specific salary data on Glassdoor or levels.fyi rather than relying only on broad market averages.

05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Build a portfolio that speaks for itself. Publish two or more personal apps on the App Store, or put clean, well-documented projects on GitHub. Recruiters at Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs and Jio Platforms look for proof of work beyond a resume, and a live App Store project is hard to argue with.

Know your UIKit versus SwiftUI split. Enterprise teams in Delhi NCR often still run UIKit-heavy codebases, but SwiftUI is increasingly expected for new feature work. Be ready to discuss the tradeoffs confidently in interviews.

Match your domain knowledge to the company you are applying to. If it is Snapmint, understand how fintech apps handle KYC flows and payment SDKs. If it is Zomato, know how consumer apps use deep linking and push notifications at scale. Specific domain knowledge signals genuine interest and saves interview time.

Make performance work visible on your resume. High-traffic iOS apps care about startup time, memory footprint, and crash rates. If you have tuned these in a real product, describe the work concretely using your own measured results rather than vague claims.

Complete Apple's developer certification paths if you are earlier in your career. They will not replace hands-on experience, but they signal commitment to hiring managers reviewing a thin resume.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Move fast on the Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs openings. Thirteen roles at one company suggests a concentrated quarterly hiring push. These windows close when headcount targets are met, sometimes before the job posts are even taken down. Apply within the first few days of seeing a role live.

Tailor your resume for each company type. For Deutsche Telekom Digital Labs, emphasise scalability, SDK integration, and enterprise engineering practices. For Zomato or WinZO, lead with consumer app experience, A/B testing, and fast iteration cycles. For Classplus or Snapmint, highlight edtech or fintech domain knowledge.

Message the hiring manager directly after applying. Many iOS roles in India get filled through referrals or direct outreach before the formal pipeline closes. Find the iOS lead or engineering manager on LinkedIn and send a short, specific note about why their product interests you.

Do not skip the smaller employers. Zoca and remotestar-team each have one opening. Smaller applicant pools, faster interview cycles, and sometimes meaningful equity make these worth a closer look.
