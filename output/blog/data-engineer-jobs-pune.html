05 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Show real pipelines, not toy projects. Recruiters for Data Engineer roles respond to candidates with visible work: a GitHub repo with a working ELT pipeline, a dbt project with documentation, or a Spark job that solves a real problem. A working project carries more weight than a list of certifications.

Name your tools explicitly. Pune employers in this data span IT services and product companies, so versatility matters. Your resume should name the exact tools you have used (for example, Apache Airflow, dbt, Spark, Kafka, BigQuery, Redshift, Snowflake) rather than vague phrases like 'cloud experience'.

Certifications help most at entry level. If you are early in your career, cloud data certifications from AWS, GCP, or Azure give hiring managers a fast signal. At senior level, interviewers care more about system design and measurable impact than certificates.

Match your target company to your salary goal. The Pune market has both IT services and product companies hiring right now. If you want the upper end of the salary bands, prioritise product companies and global firms. Both types are present in the current openings, so you have a real choice to make.

06 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Apply early. Most roles see their heaviest application volume in the first few days after posting. Getting your application in while a role is fresh gives it more visibility before a shortlist forms.

Tailor your resume to the company type. For IT services firms like Zensar, emphasise delivery track record, client exposure, and tool breadth. For product companies like Snowflake or Mastercard, lead with system design, scale, and ownership of data infrastructure decisions.

Message the hiring manager on LinkedIn. A short, specific note (two to three sentences referencing the role and one relevant thing you have built) gets attention in ways that a submitted application alone does not. This step is often skipped, which means doing it gives you a real edge.

Track every application. With 23 openings in Pune right now, you can realistically apply to every relevant one within a week. Keep a simple list and send one follow-up if you have not heard back after two weeks.
