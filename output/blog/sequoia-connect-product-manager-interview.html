01 Overview

Overview

Sequoia Connect is a talent and community platform associated with Peak XV Partners (formerly Sequoia Capital India), connecting operators, founders, and professionals across the startup ecosystem. As of July 2026, knok jobradar tracks 121 open roles at Sequoia Connect across all functions.

Candidates typically report a process of 3-5 conversations: a recruiter screen, a hiring manager discussion, one or two product case rounds, and a culture or leadership conversation. The full process is commonly reported to take 3-6 weeks. Most rounds are virtual, with some final stages in person.

PM roles at Sequoia Connect typically span community platform features, talent matching, engagement and growth, and data products serving a two-sided ecosystem. On one side are professionals seeking opportunities; on the other are portfolio companies and founders looking for senior talent. Interview questions blend consumer product thinking with B2B platform instincts.

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Q: Tell us about a time you said no to a feature request from a senior stakeholder.

*Situation:* At a previous company, the VP of Sales asked us to build a custom analytics dashboard for a single enterprise client, right before our annual planning cycle.

*Task:* As the PM for the data platform, I needed to decide whether to take it on or protect the team's existing roadmap commitments.

*Action:* I pulled up our OKRs and showed that this request served one client but would block three features for our largest user segment. I proposed a lightweight workaround: exporting raw data in a format the client's analyst could use immediately. I also flagged a self-serve reporting module for the next planning cycle.

*Result:* The VP agreed to the workaround. The client stayed satisfied, the team shipped on schedule, and the reporting module was prioritised in the next cycle.

---

Q: A core engagement metric drops sharply. How do you diagnose it?

*Situation:* At a B2B SaaS company, weekly active users dropped noticeably for two consecutive weeks after a platform update.

*Task:* I owned the engagement OKR and needed to find the root cause before the next sprint planning.

*Action:* I started with funnel analysis to locate where the drop-off happened, then segmented by user cohort, device, and geography. The drop was concentrated in users on a newly released app version. A UI change had buried a core action three taps deeper. I confirmed this with a few quick user calls and a heatmap review.

*Result:* We rolled back the UI change and ran an A/B test on a revised flow. Engagement recovered within the following week, and the revised flow outperformed the original baseline.

---

Q: How would you improve talent matching on a platform like Sequoia Connect?

*Situation:* I was given this as a case question. I framed Sequoia Connect as a two-sided marketplace connecting operators with portfolio companies.

*Task:* The goal was to improve match quality so both sides find value faster.

*Action:* I proposed segmenting match signals into three tiers: stated preferences (role, location, company stage), inferred signals (past company trajectory, function depth), and ecosystem signals (warm connections within the network). I argued for weighting ecosystem signals most heavily because they are rare, high-trust, and core to Sequoia Connect's advantage over generic job boards. I also proposed a feedback loop where match outcomes (accepted, ignored, replied) retrain the ranking model over time.

*Result:* The interviewer asked me to go deeper on the feedback loop. Candidates report that this style of structured, opinionated thinking lands well in Sequoia Connect case rounds.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) is the baseline for all behavioural questions. Keep Situation and Task brief and spend most of your time on Action and Result.

CIRCLES (Comprehend, Identify, Report, Cut, List, Evaluate, Summarise) works well for open-ended product design questions. Candidates report that starting with a clear user definition before jumping to features resonates with Sequoia Connect interviewers.

North Star Metric + Input Metrics is the right frame for measurement questions. State your north star first, then name two or three leading indicators, and explain why you chose them over alternatives.

For prioritisation questions, use a value-versus-effort grid and be explicit about which stakeholder or strategic goal you are optimising for. Sequoia Connect operates at the intersection of talent, community, and portfolio value, so linking features back to ecosystem health tends to land well.

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

Interviewers at Sequoia Connect typically look for PMs who can think across a two-sided platform, balancing individual professionals' needs with portfolio companies' hiring goals.

Structured thinking under ambiguity. State your assumptions out loud, define the user, and then build your answer. Jumping straight to solutions is a common red flag.

Comfort with community and ecosystem products. Community platforms have different engagement loops than consumer apps. Show you understand network effects, trust signals, and the value of warm connections in a professional context.

Data literacy without over-indexing on metrics. You should be able to set up a measurement framework, but also recognise when qualitative signals matter more, especially for hard-to-survey users like founders and VCs.

Clear ownership stories. Tie your behavioural answers to outcomes you drove, not just features you shipped.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Week 1: Sign up for and use Sequoia Connect's platform as an actual user. Map the core journeys for both sides: a professional seeking opportunities and a company team searching for talent. Note what feels smooth and what feels broken. This gives you authentic product observations to draw on in case questions.

Week 2: Prepare one strong STAR answer for each of these: a metric you owned and moved, a prioritisation decision with clear trade-offs, a time you navigated stakeholder conflict, and a product you would redesign. Practise keeping each answer under 3 minutes.

Week 3: Do two or three mock case rounds with peers or in PM communities. Focus especially on notification design and matching improvement questions, which are commonly cited as recurring themes in Sequoia Connect interviews. Also revisit two-sided marketplace and community platform fundamentals.

Before the interview: Briefly review the Peak XV Partners portfolio. Knowing which portfolio companies are actively hiring through Sequoia Connect signals genuine interest and gives you concrete product examples to use in case discussions.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Treating it like a pure consumer PM interview. Sequoia Connect serves professionals and companies, not a mass-market audience. Generic consumer frameworks without platform or B2B nuance will miss the mark.

Jumping to features before defining the user. Candidates who open case answers with a feature list often lose points early. Start with who you are building for and what their core problem is.

Vague metrics in behavioural answers. Saying 'we improved engagement' without naming the metric you owned and explaining why you chose it leaves interviewers unconvinced.

Ignoring the two-sided nature of the platform. Every product decision affects both supply (professionals) and demand (hiring teams). Answers that optimise only one side get challenged quickly.

Over-rehearsed answers that do not adapt. Interviewers commonly follow up with 'why that metric' or 'what would you do differently.' Practise your follow-up responses, not just the opening answer.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many rounds does the Sequoia Connect PM interview typically have?

Candidates report between 3 and 5 rounds in total. This typically includes a recruiter screen, a hiring manager discussion, one or two product or case rounds, and a final culture or leadership conversation. The exact number varies by seniority level and the specific team.

Is there a take-home assignment in the Sequoia Connect PM process?

Some candidates report a short take-home case study, while others move straight to live case rounds. Ask your recruiter what to expect after the screening call so you can prepare the right format. Preparing a written case framework in advance will help either way.

How important is it to know the Peak XV Partners portfolio before the interview?

Helpful but not mandatory for most PM roles. Knowing two or three portfolio companies relevant to your target role shows genuine interest and gives you concrete product examples to draw on in case questions. Do not try to memorise the full portfolio.

What background do Sequoia Connect PMs typically come from?

Based on publicly reported profiles, many PMs at ecosystem and community platforms come from product roles at startups or from consulting and business backgrounds. Startup experience is valued because the platform's core users (founders, operators) expect the PM to understand their world firsthand.

Should I expect questions about AI or machine learning in the interview?

Possibly, especially for mid to senior roles on the matching or recommendations side. You do not need to be an ML engineer, but you should be comfortable discussing recommendation and ranking systems at a product level: what signals matter, how you measure match quality, and how you handle cold-start problems.

What salary can I expect as a PM at Sequoia Connect?

Based on knok jobradar data for PM roles in India, Associate PMs typically see ranges of 12-20 LPA, mid-level PMs with 3-6 years of experience see 24-40 LPA, Senior PMs see 40-60 LPA, and Group or Principal PMs can reach 55-90+ LPA. Actual offers depend on your level, the specific role, and negotiation.
