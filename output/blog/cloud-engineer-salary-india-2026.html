01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Most Cloud Engineer job listings in India skip the salary field entirely. Knok's jobradar found 102 active Cloud Engineer openings as of July 2026, and the pay was blank on nearly every one. This is not an exception. It is the default across Indian job boards, where employers routinely write 'as per industry standards' or leave compensation fields empty, giving job seekers nothing to anchor on before they even apply.

Building a realistic salary picture means going outside the listing itself. Glassdoor carries engineer-reported pay data for specific companies. Levels.fyi tracks detailed comp breakdowns including base, variable, and stock. Publicly reported findings from industry surveys by staffing firms add broader context. This guide pulls from all three, flags where sample sizes are small, and does not invent precision the data cannot support. The aim is a realistic anchor you can use in a negotiation, not a number HR laughs at.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Pay transparency is rare in India, which means negotiations often start with HR asking you to name a number first. A few approaches that hold up in practice:

Anchor on market data, not your last salary. Come in with a range built from Glassdoor, publicly reported industry surveys, and levels.fyi. Be ready to explain how you arrived at that range, because 'I looked at market data' is a stronger position than 'I want more than my current CTC.'

Ask about each comp component separately. A company may hold firm on base but have flexibility on joining bonus, variable pay, or stock. Asking 'what does the full package look like?' early in the conversation reveals where the room is.

Certifications are negotiating currency. An active AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect, or GCP Professional certification is something concrete you can point to when justifying the upper end of a band. Employers who need certified headcount have less room to low-ball.

Get the offer in writing before you resign. Verbal offers in India are common and occasionally fall through. Wait for a written offer letter before giving notice at your current employer.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

A Cloud Engineer offer in India typically has several components beyond the headline CTC number. Understanding each one helps you compare offers that look similar on the surface.

Basic salary forms the core. According to commonly cited HR practice, it is usually 40-50% of CTC. This figure matters because PF contributions and gratuity calculations are based on it.

HRA (House Rent Allowance) is a tax-saving component. How much you can actually claim depends on the city you live in and the rent receipts you can produce.

Variable or performance pay is tied to individual and company targets. Publicly reported breakdowns from tech companies show it can be a small percentage at junior levels and a more significant chunk for senior roles. It is not guaranteed.

ESOPs or RSUs appear more at product companies and funded startups. Levels.fyi data shows these can be meaningful in senior total comp, but vesting schedules matter. A four-year vest with a one-year cliff is a common structure.

Joining bonus is sometimes offered to cover unvested equity or notice-period gaps. A commonly cited rule of thumb is that in-hand salary runs at roughly 70-75% of CTC for mid-level tech roles, though the exact figure depends on your tax slab and how components are structured.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How do I find out what a company actually pays if the listing shows no salary?

Search Glassdoor and levels.fyi for the company name along with 'Cloud Engineer' and focus on submissions from the past 12-18 months, since older data may not reflect recent band revisions. You can also ask in LinkedIn groups or Telegram communities where engineers share anonymised offer letters. Going into a salary conversation with a range from multiple sources is a much stronger position than going in with no data at all.

Is a Cloud Engineer in Delhi NCR paid the same as one in Bangalore?

Not always. Bangalore has historically commanded a slight premium for tech roles, according to industry surveys, because of tighter talent competition and a larger base of high-paying product companies. Delhi NCR has narrowed the gap in cloud specifically, driven by enterprise IT and public-sector cloud projects. The difference, where it exists, tends to be smaller at mid-level and wider at senior and principal bands.

What skills push an offer toward the upper end of the salary band?

Active certifications such as AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect, or GCP Professional Cloud Architect are commonly cited by recruiters as a direct lever for higher offers. Beyond certs, hands-on Terraform, Kubernetes, and multi-cloud architecture experience stands out in screening. Cloud security skills and FinOps experience, meaning you can show you reduced cloud spend at a past employer, give you a concrete business-value story that is hard for hiring managers to ignore in a negotiation.

Do I have to share my current salary when asked?

You are not legally required to share it. A common approach is to say you are targeting a specific range based on market data and the scope of the role, rather than anchoring on your current CTC. If the interviewer pushes, you can confirm you are looking within a range without revealing the exact current number. This protects you especially if your existing salary is below market.

What is the difference between CTC and the salary I actually take home?

CTC (Cost to Company) includes employer PF contributions, gratuity provisions, insurance premiums, and other benefits that never appear in your bank account. A commonly cited rule of thumb is that in-hand salary is roughly 70-75% of CTC for mid-level tech roles, though the actual figure depends on your tax slab, the city you work in, and how your salary components are structured. Always ask HR for a full CTC breakup and use that to compare offers rather than the headline number.
