01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

VAST Data is a storage and data platform company that has grown quickly in the enterprise infrastructure space. As of July 2026, VAST Data has 247 open roles across engineering, sales, marketing, and operations, which means the company is actively hiring and pipelines move faster than at companies in a hiring freeze.

For Indian job seekers, most software engineering and solutions engineering roles are based in the US or Europe, but VAST Data does hire remotely and has been expanding its global footprint. If you are applying from India, watch for roles tagged as 'Remote' or check whether the job description lists time zone flexibility.

VAST Data competes with companies like NetApp and Pure Storage in the data infrastructure market. Their technology is genuinely differentiated, so interviewers expect candidates to care about storage, distributed systems, and performance at scale. This is not a company where you can get by with surface-level technical knowledge.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at VAST Data typically follows these stages. Details vary by role and team, so treat this as a general guide rather than a fixed script.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen
A recruiter or hiring manager reviews your resume. For engineering roles, they look for experience with distributed systems, storage, or infrastructure. Keywords like NVMe, object storage, or file systems can help your resume get noticed.

Stage 2: Recruiter call
A short introductory call to cover your background, location, notice period, and salary expectations. Be ready to explain why you are interested in VAST Data specifically, not just any infrastructure company.

Stage 3: Technical phone screen
For engineering roles, expect a coding round covering data structures and algorithms, plus some system design questions. The difficulty is commonly reported as similar to mid-level LeetCode. For solutions or sales engineering roles, this stage may involve a scenario-based discussion instead of coding.

Stage 4: Technical deep dive
This is usually a longer session with two or three engineers. Expect system design, distributed systems concepts, and questions about how storage systems work. You may be asked to design a component of a data pipeline or explain trade-offs in a distributed architecture.

Stage 5: Final round
A set of conversations with senior engineers, a hiring manager, and sometimes a cross-functional partner. This round tests both technical depth and how you communicate trade-offs. Behavioural questions using the STAR format are common here.

Stage 6: Offer
If selected, the recruiter walks you through the offer. Equity is part of compensation at VAST Data, so ask the recruiter to explain the vesting schedule and most recent valuation if that matters to you. Send a brief note to your recruiter promptly after each stage to stay on their radar.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Deep infrastructure knowledge is the single biggest differentiator. VAST Data builds storage systems that sit at the core of AI and analytics workloads. They want engineers who understand why a design choice matters, not just how to implement it.

Ownership mindset. Interviewers ask about times you drove a project from idea to production, handled an outage, or made a call with incomplete information. Generic answers about 'working collaboratively' are not enough here.

Communication clarity. VAST Data is a distributed team across multiple time zones. They value people who write well and can explain complex technical ideas to both engineers and customers. If you are applying for a solutions engineering or pre-sales role, this matters even more.

Curiosity about the product. Candidates who have read VAST Data's technical blog, watched their conference talks, or can speak to how their Universal Storage model differs from traditional NAS or SAN systems consistently get stronger feedback.

Startup pace comfort. VAST Data has grown fast and the pace inside the company reflects that. They look for people who thrive with some ambiguity and can prioritise without being micromanaged.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Start with the technology. Read VAST Data's technical blog and any publicly available whitepapers on their storage architecture. Understand the basics of NVMe-oF, disaggregated storage, and why low-latency matters for AI workloads. You do not need to be an expert, but you need to sound like someone who finds this genuinely interesting.

Prepare your system design answers. Common topics for infrastructure companies include designing a distributed file system, handling consistency vs. availability trade-offs, and scaling a metadata service. Practice talking through your thought process aloud, not just arriving at an answer.

Brush up on coding fundamentals. For software engineering roles, focus on arrays, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming. Aim for clean, readable code and explain your approach as you go. Interviewers at VAST Data commonly report caring more about how you think than whether you reach the optimal solution immediately.

Prepare STAR stories for five or six situations. Pick examples that show ownership, a technical decision under uncertainty, a time you disagreed with a team and how you handled it, and a time you learned something quickly. These cover most behavioural questions.

Research the competitive landscape. Know who VAST Data competes with and what their positioning is. For customer-facing roles, this knowledge signals business maturity alongside technical skill.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

VAST Data has a reputation for being technically demanding and fast-moving. The culture is built around the idea that storage is not a boring commodity but a foundational layer that makes modern AI and analytics possible. People who thrive here tend to be engineers at heart, even if they work in sales or marketing.

Transparency is a value the company talks about openly. Leaders share company direction and financial milestones with employees more than is typical at a startup of this stage.

High standards without hierarchy. VAST Data is not a heavily process-driven company. Junior engineers can and do influence architectural decisions if they make a strong technical case. This is exciting for some people and uncomfortable for others. Know which one you are before you join.

Remote-first in practice, but time zones matter. Many teams span the US, Europe, and Israel. Expect some early morning or late evening calls if you are based in India. Ask the hiring manager during the final round what the actual meeting cadence looks like for the specific team.

Growth orientation. The company has been expanding rapidly and employees commonly report that responsibilities grow quickly. If you want a stable, clearly bounded role, this may not be the right fit. If you want to grow fast alongside the company, VAST Data is worth pursuing seriously.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does VAST Data hire from India or for roles based in India?

VAST Data's engineering and go-to-market teams are primarily based in the US, Europe, and Israel. Most open roles list a US or European location. That said, some roles are tagged as fully remote, and the company does hire globally. Check each job description carefully for location flexibility and time zone requirements before applying.

How long does the VAST Data hiring process take from application to offer?

The full process commonly takes a few weeks from first recruiter contact to offer, though this varies by role and how quickly both sides move. Engineering roles with multiple technical rounds can stretch longer if scheduling is difficult across time zones. Staying responsive and confirming each next step with your recruiter helps keep momentum.

What is the best way to get noticed as an applicant?

Applying through the company's careers page is the standard route, but a referral from a current VAST Data employee significantly increases the chance your resume gets reviewed. Connect with VAST Data engineers on LinkedIn and have a genuine conversation before asking for a referral. Reaching out cold for a referral without any prior interaction rarely works well.

Is VAST Data a good company for someone early in their career?

VAST Data is better suited to candidates with a few years of experience, particularly in infrastructure, storage, or distributed systems. Entry-level roles exist but are not the majority of open positions. Early-career candidates who have done strong internships in infrastructure or have personal projects involving systems programming have the best shot.

How should I negotiate the offer if I get one?

VAST Data offers typically include base salary, equity, and benefits. Equity at a high-growth storage company can be significant, so understand the vesting schedule and compare the most recent publicly reported valuation before weighing it against a cash-heavy offer elsewhere. It is completely acceptable to ask the recruiter for a few days to review the offer before responding.

What happens if I do not get the role this time?

VAST Data, like most companies, keeps applications on file and recruiters do reach back out for future roles that are a better match. Ask your recruiter for feedback after the final round. Not all recruiters will share detailed feedback, but even a general sense of where you fell short helps you prepare better. With 247 open roles, there may be another position that fits you well right now.
