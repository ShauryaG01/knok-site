01 Hiring Overview

For Indian professionals, TELUS Digital offers a combination of international client exposure, stable employment, and a collaborative team environment. It is a particularly strong fit for candidates who want to work with global clients without relocating.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The TELUS Digital hiring process commonly runs across five to six stages. The full cycle, from application to offer, is commonly reported to take three to six weeks depending on the role and team availability.

StageWhat happensFormat
1. Application screeningATS filter and recruiter shortlistAutomated or manual review
2. Recruiter callNotice period, location, expectations checkShort phone or video call
3. Technical assessmentCoding or domain test for technical rolesOnline platform
4. Technical interviewDomain questions, problem-solving, past projects1:1 or panel video call
5. Managerial roundBehavioural and cultural fit discussion1:1 or panel video call
6. HR final roundCompensation, joining date, documentationPhone or video call

Stage 1: Application and screening. Your resume goes through an ATS filter before a recruiter reviews it. With 7 roles currently listed, the pool is focused rather than open-ended, so tailoring your resume closely to the job description gives you a meaningful edge at this stage.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A preliminary conversation to check your notice period, location preference, and rough salary range. This is also a good moment to ask about the role scope, team size, and reporting structure.

Stage 3: Technical assessment. Engineering and QA candidates are typically given an online coding or domain test. Non-technical candidates may receive a written scenario or communication assessment instead. Commonly reported tests focus on fundamentals rather than advanced competitive programming.

Stage 4: Technical interview. One or two rounds with senior team members or the hiring manager. Expect questions on your core domain, past project work, and for engineering roles, system design or debugging scenarios. Be ready to explain your thought process clearly, not just arrive at the right answer.

Stage 5: Managerial or cross-functional round. A conversation covering how you handle ambiguity, collaborate across locations, and manage competing priorities. Behavioural questions using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) are standard here. Prepare real examples from your past work.

Stage 6: HR final round and offer. Compensation discussion, joining date confirmation, and document verification. Offer letters commonly follow within a few business days of this round.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Clear communication skills. Across almost all roles at TELUS Digital, the ability to communicate clearly in spoken and written English is a baseline requirement, not just a nice-to-have. Teams work directly with Canadian and North American clients, so this is a practical daily necessity.

Strong fundamentals over tool familiarity. For engineering and QA roles, interviewers focus on how well you understand core concepts rather than whether you have used one specific tool. Being able to walk through your reasoning clearly during a problem-solving exercise often carries as much weight as arriving at the correct answer.

Adaptability and cross-functional thinking. TELUS Digital serves clients across industries. Candidates who can shift context quickly, pick up new domains, and collaborate across functions consistently stand out. If you have experience working across multiple client verticals or departments, make that visible in your resume and interviews.

Attention to detail. For AI data services and content operations roles, accuracy and consistency are core competencies that interviewers specifically screen for. Concrete examples from your past work where precision made a real difference are worth preparing.

Cultural fit with a people-first mindset. The company's values, inherited from TELUS, emphasise inclusion and community. Candidates who can speak naturally to team contribution, mentorship, or community involvement tend to leave a stronger impression in the managerial round.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the business unit first. TELUS Digital is not a single monolithic team. It spans AI data services, customer experience, IT delivery, and digital transformation consulting. Knowing which part of the business your role belongs to helps you frame your answers and ask sharper questions in each round.

Revise domain fundamentals for your function. For software engineering, focus on data structures, algorithms, and system design concepts. For QA, brush up on test planning, automation frameworks, and defect management. For support and operations roles, practise situational scenarios and structured written communication.

Prepare STAR-format answers. Behavioural interviews are consistent across TELUS Digital rounds. Common themes include managing difficult client expectations, coordinating across time zones, and handling competing priorities. Prepare three to four concrete stories from your work history that you can adapt to different questions.

Check Glassdoor and AmbitionBox before each round. Both platforms carry recent interview reviews from TELUS Digital candidates in India, often including the actual questions asked in specific rounds. This is one of the most practical ways to calibrate your preparation and know what level of difficulty to expect.

Tailor your resume to the job description. Highlight any global client work, remote collaboration experience, or cross-functional project history. For technical roles, make sure your tech stack is listed clearly and maps to what the job description asks for.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

TELUS Digital's culture is strongly shaped by its Canadian parent. The values that come through most clearly in publicly reported employee reviews are inclusion, community giving, and genuine care for employee well-being.

The 'TELUS Cares' philosophy drives volunteering programs and social impact initiatives that extend to Indian operations. If corporate social responsibility matters to you professionally, mentioning it naturally in your managerial round is worthwhile. It aligns with values the company actively promotes and measures internally, not just lists on a website.

Work-life balance is one of the most frequently cited positives in employee reviews on Glassdoor and AmbitionBox. The process-driven culture means expectations tend to be clearly defined, which suits professionals who prefer structure and predictability over ambiguity.

Distributed teamwork is a daily reality. Most teams collaborate across multiple time zones, so comfort with asynchronous communication and tools like Slack or Microsoft Teams is a practical expectation. Candidates who mention experience with remote or globally distributed teams tend to come across as immediately ready to contribute.

Career progression at TELUS Digital is generally described as steady rather than fast. If you are looking for stability, international exposure, and a clear performance review process, it is a strong fit. If you prefer the pace of a startup or rapid promotion cycles, set your expectations carefully before accepting an offer.
