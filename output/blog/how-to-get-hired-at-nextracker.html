01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Nextracker builds the solar tracking systems that help large-scale solar plants generate more power across the day. The company is publicly listed and has grown into one of the most recognised names in clean energy infrastructure, with operations, engineering, and supply chain teams spread across the world.

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 4 open roles at Nextracker. The hiring bar is high for a focused team like this, which means your resume, your prep, and your ability to show domain awareness all matter from the very first step.

Roles at Nextracker tend to fall into a few broad families: engineering (mechanical, electrical, software, systems), project and program management, supply chain and procurement, and business functions like finance and HR. Most roles require strong fundamentals in their domain combined with some familiarity with renewable energy or large-scale project environments.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at Nextracker is reported to run across four to five stages, though the exact shape varies by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly describe.

Stage 1: Resume screen and recruiter call. A recruiter or HR contact typically reaches out to confirm your background, current compensation, and interest in the role. This is a short initial conversation. Be ready to walk through your experience and explain why you are interested in solar or clean energy.

Stage 2: Hiring manager interview. A conversation with the direct manager for the role. Expect a mix of experience-based questions ('tell me about a project where...') and role-specific technical questions. For engineering roles, this is where your domain knowledge gets tested at a conceptual level.

Stage 3: Technical or skills assessment. Depending on the role, this could be a take-home case study, a coding round, a design exercise, or a presentation on a relevant topic. For supply chain or project management roles, candidates sometimes report a business scenario walkthrough instead.

Stage 4: Panel interview. A round with two to four team members, often cross-functional. Expect questions about how you collaborate, how you handle ambiguity, and how you have dealt with specific situations in past roles. Behavioural questions using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) come up regularly here.

Stage 5: Final conversation and reference check. Some candidates report a final call with a senior leader before an offer is extended. Reference checks are standard. Offer timelines vary, but candidates generally report hearing back within a few weeks of completing the final round.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Nextracker looks for people who combine strong technical ability with the kind of practical, execution-focused mindset that keeps large infrastructure projects on track. A few things come up consistently across publicly shared candidate experiences.

Domain awareness. You do not need to be a solar expert, but showing that you understand how solar plants work, what drives energy yield, and why tracker technology matters signals genuine interest rather than a generic application.

First-principles thinking. Engineering and technical roles at Nextracker involve solving real-world problems with physical systems. Interviewers tend to probe how you think through a problem, not just whether you arrive at the right answer.

Cross-functional collaboration. Nextracker's products sit at the intersection of hardware, software, and large construction projects. They want people who can work across teams that speak different professional languages.

Ownership and accountability. Candidates who can point to specific outcomes they drove, including things that went wrong and what they learned, tend to land better than those who describe team efforts without a clear personal contribution.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Learn the product before your first call. Understand what a solar tracker does, how Nextracker's flagship product is positioned in the market, and what the company's recent announcements have been. This shows up naturally in your answers and signals real interest rather than a rushed application.

Prepare STAR stories covering five to six situations. Pick stories that cover: a technical challenge you solved, a time you worked across teams, a project that did not go as planned, and a time you influenced without authority. These cover the most common behavioural questions that come up in panel rounds.

Review your domain fundamentals for the technical stage. For mechanical or systems engineers, be ready for questions on structural loads, fatigue analysis, or tolerance stack-ups depending on your specialisation. For software roles, data structures, system design, and API integration are commonly tested areas.

Research the clean energy market. Being able to speak to solar capacity additions, government policy directions, and competitive dynamics shows you understand the business context, not just the technology. Indian candidates can connect this to domestic solar policy and the push toward renewable energy targets.

Prepare questions that show strategic interest. Ask about how the team measures project success, what the biggest technical or operational challenges look like right now, or how cross-functional teams are structured. Avoid questions whose answers are already on the company website.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Nextracker's culture reflects its origins as an engineering-first company building hardware that operates outdoors in demanding conditions for decades. The work is tangible, the stakes around energy output and project timelines are real, and the teams tend to be direct and outcome-focused.

A few cultural traits that candidates and employees commonly cite: a bias toward action over prolonged deliberation, a respect for people who bring data to a discussion, and a genuine belief in the mission around clean energy. The company is still in a growth phase in many ways, which means processes are not always fully formed. People who thrive tend to be comfortable creating structure where it does not yet exist.

For Indian professionals, Nextracker's India presence is primarily in engineering and technical functions. The work environment is collaborative but fast-paced, and the expectation is that you manage your own deliverables with relatively light oversight once you have settled in.

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Nextracker have right now?

As of July 2026, knok jobradar is tracking 4 open roles at Nextracker. The number changes as roles are filled and new ones open, so checking regularly gives you the best picture. Applying early in the window a role is posted tends to get more attention from recruiters.

Does Nextracker hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Most publicly listed Nextracker roles target candidates with some prior work experience, particularly in engineering, project management, or supply chain. The company does hire interns and fresh graduates for specific programmes, so look for associate or graduate-level postings if you are early in your career. Applying to senior individual contributor roles without the relevant experience is unlikely to move you to an interview.

How long does the Nextracker hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on publicly shared candidate accounts, the process from first recruiter contact to offer typically spans several weeks, with additional time if panel scheduling runs across time zones. Following up politely after each stage is reasonable and generally well received. If you have not heard back after a week following any round, a short note to the recruiter is appropriate.

What salary can I expect at Nextracker in India?

Specific compensation figures for Nextracker India roles are not widely published. Glassdoor and levels.fyi occasionally carry data points shared by employees, and those are your best starting point for role-level benchmarks. Come into salary discussions knowing your own market rate and be ready to share your current CTC along with your expectations.

Is a background in solar or renewable energy required to get hired?

No, prior solar experience is not a hard requirement for most roles. Nextracker hires mechanical, electrical, software, and supply chain professionals whose core skills transfer from other industries. What helps is showing genuine curiosity about clean energy and doing enough homework to speak intelligently about why the company's technology matters.

What is the best way to stand out in the Nextracker interview?

Prepare specific examples from your past work, know the product and the company's recent news, and ask thoughtful questions that show you have thought about the role's real challenges. Interviewers consistently mention that candidates who combine technical depth with clear communication stand out more than those who only tick the credential boxes. Showing genuine interest in the clean energy mission, not just the job title, also makes a real difference.
