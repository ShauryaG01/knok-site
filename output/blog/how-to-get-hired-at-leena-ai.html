03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Leena AI's interview process follows a structured but startup-style flow, typically running four to six touchpoints depending on the role and seniority. The stages below are based on commonly reported patterns for enterprise AI SaaS companies of this size, so treat them as a guide rather than a guarantee.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. The recruiter or an automated system reviews your application for role fit. Tailor your resume to the specific job description and highlight any experience with enterprise software, AI products, or HR technology.

Stage 2: Recruiter screening call. A short conversation with someone from the talent team covering basics: your notice period, salary expectations, and motivation for applying. Be ready to speak clearly about why Leena AI specifically and what you bring to the role.

Stage 3: Hiring manager conversation. A deeper discussion about your experience, past projects, and how you approach problems. For engineering roles, expect some technical framing here. For non-technical roles, expect behavioural questions about how you have handled specific situations.

Stage 4: Technical round or case discussion. This is the core evaluation and runs noticeably longer than the earlier calls. For engineers, expect a coding or system design exercise. For product managers, a product case. For sales or customer success, a scenario-based roleplay or business discussion. Ask clarifying questions before diving in.

Stage 5: Culture and leadership interview. Often with a senior leader or cross-functional peer. They assess whether you will thrive in a fast-moving startup. Prepare examples of times you took initiative or navigated ambiguity without a clear playbook.

Stage 6: Offer and reference check. If all rounds go well, HR shares a verbal offer followed by a formal letter. Reference checks may happen before or after the offer depending on the team.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Leena AI is building AI-first products for enterprise clients, so they look for people who understand both the technology and the customer context.

For engineers, they want comfort with large language models, API integrations, and building at scale. Experience with conversational AI, NLP, or enterprise SaaS backends is a strong differentiator. Clean code and the ability to explain design choices matter as much as raw speed.

For product and growth roles, they want someone who can translate enterprise customer pain into clear product decisions. Background in working with senior HR or IT stakeholders, or direct experience with workflows like onboarding or ticketing, will stand out.

For sales and customer success, enterprise selling experience is key. They want people who can navigate long deal cycles, handle procurement conversations, and build lasting relationships with large accounts.

Across all roles, Leena AI values ownership. Candidates who show they have figured things out with limited guidance, moved fast, and owned outcomes end up doing well in the process.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before every round. Spend time with Leena AI's website and any publicly available demos or case studies. Understand how their AI agent works, which problems it solves, and how it compares to competitors in the enterprise HR tech space. Speaking specifically about their product signals genuine interest.

Prepare STAR-format answers. Most behavioural questions at Leena AI follow a Situation-Task-Action-Result structure. Have four or five strong examples ready covering ownership, cross-functional collaboration, handling failure, and delivering under pressure.

Brush up on enterprise AI concepts. If you are applying to an engineering or product role, know how large language models are deployed in enterprise settings, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and data privacy constraints that large companies care about.

Know your numbers. Whether you are in sales, product, or engineering, come prepared to talk about outcomes you have driven. What did you ship, how did it perform, what was the business impact? Enterprise context is valued everywhere in the company.

Ask sharp questions at each stage. Good ones include how the team measures success for this role, what a strong first few months looks like, and what has changed in the product roadmap recently. Thoughtful questions consistently impress Leena AI interviewers.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Leena AI operates with the intensity of a growth-stage startup. The team is relatively small compared to the scale of clients they serve, which means individuals carry more responsibility than they might at a larger company. If you prefer clearly defined scope and handoffs, this may feel uncomfortable at first. If you enjoy seeing your work directly affect customers, it can be very satisfying.

The company has a global client base, which means customer-facing roles often involve interactions across time zones. Strong English communication skills matter for most positions.

Leena AI is building AI products in a space (enterprise HR) that is changing quickly. Teams are expected to keep up with developments in the LLM world and bring new capabilities into the roadmap. There is genuine respect for people who combine technical depth with business awareness.

The environment rewards people who speak up, propose solutions rather than just raising problems, and take end-to-end ownership of their work. Mid-level roles tend to have significant autonomy, which is both an opportunity and a responsibility.
