01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Cloudanix is a cloud security startup that helps businesses find and fix misconfigurations, compliance gaps, and vulnerabilities across their cloud infrastructure. They serve companies running workloads on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. As of mid-2026, Cloudanix has 11 open roles, which signals active hiring across engineering, security research, sales, and operations. This is a startup environment, so hiring moves relatively quickly and decisions often involve senior leadership directly.

If you are a cloud security professional or an engineer looking to move into the security space, Cloudanix is worth a serious application. The company serves a global customer base from India, which means roles often require strong written communication and the ability to work across time zones.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Cloudanix interview process typically runs across three to five stages, though the exact number can vary by role and seniority. Here is what candidates generally report:

Application and resume screening. You apply through their website or a job board. The recruiter reviews your profile for relevant cloud or security experience. If your background fits, expect a call from HR within a week or two of applying.

Screening call. Shortlisted candidates get a brief introductory call covering your background, current role, notice period, and salary expectations. This is not heavily technical. It is mainly to check fitment and set expectations on both sides.

Technical assessment. For engineering and security roles, there is usually a take-home task or an online assessment. This tests your understanding of cloud platforms, security concepts, and problem-solving. The difficulty is moderate to high, and taking your time to submit thorough answers is worthwhile.

Technical interview. A senior engineer or security lead goes deeper into your technical knowledge. Expect questions about cloud architecture, common misconfigurations, threat detection, and your past project experience. They may present real-world scenarios and ask how you would respond.

Hiring manager or leadership round. This round focuses on your thinking process, how you handle ambiguity, and whether your goals align with the company direction. For senior roles, the founder or CTO may be involved at this stage.

Offer. If all rounds go well, the recruiter will make a verbal offer followed by a written one. Salary discussions typically happen during the HR screening and again at the offer stage.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Cloudanix hires for genuine depth in cloud and security, not just surface-level familiarity. Here is what they consistently value:

Cloud platform hands-on experience. Candidates who have actually built, managed, or secured workloads on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud stand out. Knowing the theory is not enough; they want people who have dealt with real configurations and seen what breaks.

Security mindset. Understanding how attackers think, knowing common cloud misconfigurations, and being familiar with cloud security frameworks and compliance standards (such as cloud-native security benchmarks and industry security guidelines) is important. You do not need to be a pure security researcher, but you should be comfortable reading and interpreting security findings.

Problem-solving and product thinking. Because this is a startup, they value people who think beyond their immediate task and understand how their work affects the product and the customer.

Communication skills. Many Cloudanix customers are outside India, so clear written and spoken English is a practical requirement for most roles.

Ownership and initiative. Startup hiring managers look for candidates who take responsibility without being asked. Examples of side projects, open-source contributions, or self-directed learning go a long way in standing out.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research Cloudanix's product deeply. Sign up for their free tier or read their documentation before any interview. Understand what problems they solve: cloud misconfiguration detection, compliance monitoring, and security posture management. Interviewers will expect you to know what the product does and why it matters.

Review cloud security fundamentals. Brush up on IAM policies, storage bucket permissions, network security groups, logging and monitoring, and identity federation across major cloud platforms. Know what a misconfiguration looks like and why it creates risk.

Practice explaining your past projects clearly. Cloudanix interviewers often ask you to walk through a project where you improved security or fixed a cloud issue. Prepare two or three strong examples with specific outcomes.

Prepare for scenario-based questions. You may be asked: 'A client's storage bucket is publicly accessible. What do you do?' or 'How would you detect a compromised access key?' Practice talking through your reasoning step by step, out loud.

Work on your take-home task seriously. If you receive a technical assessment, do not rush it. Structure your answers, explain your thinking, and cover edge cases. This is often the biggest differentiator between candidates who move forward and those who do not.

Know your numbers. Have your current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period ready before the first call. Cloudanix HR typically asks these early, and being unprepared can slow the process down.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Cloudanix operates as a product-led security startup, which means the culture tends to be fast-moving, outcome-focused, and lean on process. Teams are small, which gives individual contributors a lot of visibility and ownership but also means there is less hand-holding than at larger companies.

Ownership over hierarchy. People are expected to make decisions without waiting for approval at every step. If you prefer clear top-down direction, this may take some adjustment.

Customer obsession. Because Cloudanix works with security-conscious enterprise customers, there is a strong emphasis on reliability, accuracy, and trust. A false positive in a security alert has real consequences for a customer's team, so quality matters.

Remote-friendly but communicative. The team works across locations, so async communication, documentation, and responsiveness are valued. Being reachable and responsive during working hours is expected even in remote or hybrid setups.

Learning-first environment. Cloud security is a fast-changing field. The company values people who read, experiment, and share knowledge. Staying current is part of the job, not an extra credit activity.

For freshers or those very early in their career, Cloudanix may be a stretch unless you have already built relevant skills independently. For experienced professionals looking to grow fast in cloud security, it can be a strong move.

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Cloudanix currently have?

As of mid-2026, Cloudanix has 11 open roles. These span engineering, security research, sales, and operations. The number changes as positions are filled and new ones open, so check their careers page or job boards regularly for the latest listings.

How long does the Cloudanix interview process take from application to offer?

Candidates commonly report the full process taking anywhere from two to four weeks, depending on the role and how quickly rounds are scheduled. Startup hiring can move faster than at large companies, especially when there is urgency to fill a role. Staying responsive and following up politely after each round helps keep things moving.

Do I need a background in cybersecurity to get hired at Cloudanix?

Not necessarily, but a security mindset is essential. Many engineers at cloud security startups come from cloud infrastructure or DevOps backgrounds and developed security knowledge on the job or through self-study. What matters is that you understand cloud platforms deeply and are genuinely interested in security, not just using it as a keyword on your resume.

What cloud platforms should I know for a Cloudanix interview?

AWS is the most commonly referenced platform in cloud security roles, so it is your safest starting point. Azure and Google Cloud knowledge is a bonus. Focus on IAM, storage permissions, logging and monitoring, and networking concepts. Cloudanix's product works across major platforms, so breadth is valued alongside depth.

Is Cloudanix a good company for freshers or recent graduates?

Cloudanix is primarily a product company hiring for depth, so most roles require at least some hands-on cloud or security experience. Freshers with strong cloud certifications, personal projects, or open-source contributions may have a shot at junior roles. Building a home lab, earning a cloud certification, and contributing to security-related open-source projects will strengthen your profile significantly before applying.
