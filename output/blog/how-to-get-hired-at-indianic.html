01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Indianic is an Ahmedabad-based IT services and product engineering company that builds web applications, mobile apps, UI/UX products, and digital transformation solutions for clients across the US, UK, and Europe. It has grown into a well-regarded mid-size firm known for long-term client relationships and a relatively stable work culture compared to larger IT services giants.

The company hires across engineering, design, QA, and business functions. As of mid-2026, Indianic has 25 open roles listed, which gives you a realistic picture of current hiring activity. Openings come up on a project-demand basis rather than in large annual batches, so it pays to apply as soon as you spot a role that fits your profile.

Most positions are based in Ahmedabad, though the company has expanded hybrid and remote options for senior roles. If you are based in Gujarat or open to relocating, your chances improve considerably for on-site positions.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Indianic's hiring process is typically three to four rounds, though the exact structure varies by role and team. Here is what most candidates report going through:

Application and resume screening. The recruiter reviews your resume for relevant skills and experience. A strong portfolio or GitHub profile helps you clear this step faster, especially for developer and designer roles. Candidates who tailor their resume to the specific job description tend to hear back sooner.

Technical assessment or assignment. For most engineering and design roles, you will receive a take-home task or an online coding test. This is meant to gauge practical ability rather than textbook theory. Expect tasks tied to the tech stack mentioned in the job posting, such as React, Angular, Node.js, PHP, or mobile frameworks like Flutter or React Native.

Technical interview. This is usually a one-on-one or panel call with a senior developer or tech lead. Topics commonly covered include your approach to the assignment, core language concepts, system design basics for senior-level roles, and how you handle real-world scenarios like debugging, code reviews, or working with client feedback. Communication matters here because Indianic teams interact directly with international clients.

HR and culture fit interview. The final round is with HR or a delivery manager. Expect questions about your notice period, salary expectations, past project experience, and how you handle deadlines and team conflicts. This round also gives you a chance to ask about the team structure, growth path, and the kind of projects you would be working on.

With 25 open roles right now, the company is actively hiring across multiple tracks, so the pipeline tends to move at a reasonable pace. The full process from application to offer typically spans two to four weeks, though urgent roles can move faster.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Indianic looks for professionals who can work independently, communicate clearly, and deliver quality work that reflects well on the company in front of international clients. Here is a breakdown of what tends to matter:

Technical depth in your core stack. Whether you are a frontend developer, backend engineer, mobile developer, or UI/UX designer, Indianic expects a solid command of the tools listed in the job description. Generic skills matter less than specific, demonstrable experience with the technologies the team actually uses.

Client communication ability. Because the company serves clients in the US, UK, and Europe, comfort with written and spoken English is important across all technical roles, not just business development. Being able to explain your work clearly to a non-technical stakeholder is a real advantage.

Portfolio and shipped work. For developers and designers, past projects carry more weight than your degree. Candidates who can point to live applications, case studies, or open-source contributions tend to stand out at the screening stage.

Problem-solving approach. Interviewers often care more about how you think through a problem than whether you arrive at the perfect answer. Walking the interviewer through your reasoning, asking clarifying questions, and showing you can adapt are all viewed positively.

Stability and ownership mindset. Indianic values professionals who see projects through to completion and take ownership of their deliverables. A pattern of frequent job changes without clear reasons can raise questions during the HR round.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Polish your portfolio and GitHub before you apply. If you are in a technical role, make sure recent work is visible and well-documented. Even one or two strong projects with a clear readme go a long way. For designers, a clean Behance or Dribbble profile showing end-to-end UI/UX work is worth more than a lengthy resume.

Study the tech stack in the job description. Indianic roles span a wide range of technologies. Before your technical interview, revisit core concepts for the stack mentioned in the posting, whether that is React hooks, REST API design, SQL query optimisation, or Flutter state management.

Prepare to walk through a real project in detail. Pick one or two past projects you know inside out and be ready to explain the problem, your approach, the trade-offs you made, and the outcome. Interviewers at Indianic commonly ask candidates to narrate a project from their resume.

Research Indianic's client industries. The company works across e-commerce, healthcare, fintech, and logistics verticals. Showing awareness of the domain during your interview signals that you understand the business context behind the code.

Practice communicating in English. Since client interaction is part of the job, your ability to explain technical decisions in plain English will be evaluated. Practice mock interviews out loud rather than only rehearsing answers on paper.

Prepare thoughtful questions for HR. Ask about team size, the type of client you would work with, and opportunities for skill development. This shows genuine interest and helps you assess whether the role is actually a good fit for your goals.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Indianic has a reputation for a relatively stable, process-oriented work culture compared to the high-churn environment at some larger IT services firms. Employees commonly describe the atmosphere as professional but not cut-throat, with reasonable working hours for most roles and a management style that values delivery over visibility.

Client-first mindset. Because the company's revenue comes from long-term international client relationships, teams are expected to take client feedback seriously and maintain a high bar for quality. Code reviews, documentation, and regular client updates are part of the normal workflow, not afterthoughts.

Learning and skill development. Indianic encourages employees to grow within the company, and a number of engineers have moved from junior to lead roles over multi-year tenures. The company reportedly supports certifications and training, though the pace of formal learning programmes can vary by team and manager.

Small, cross-functional teams. Most teams are compact and cover design, QA, and engineering together on the same product. This gives developers broader exposure than roles where you only write code in isolation, and it builds the kind of full-context thinking that international clients value.

Ahmedabad roots. The company has a strong identity within Gujarat's tech ecosystem. This tends to create a tight-knit team culture, especially at the main office, and is worth factoring in if you are considering relocation.

How many open roles does Indianic currently have?

What is the typical salary range at Indianic?

Indianic does not publish salary bands publicly. Does Indianic hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Indianic primarily hires experienced professionals, but the company does take on freshers for certain junior and trainee positions, particularly in development and QA. Is the interview process conducted online or in person?

Initial rounds, including the technical assessment and first technical interview, are commonly conducted online via video call. The final HR round may be in person if you are based in or near Ahmedabad, particularly for on-site roles. Confirm the format with the recruiter when you schedule each round, as practices can vary by team and urgency of the hire.
