01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Skydo is a fintech startup that helps Indian freelancers, creators, and small businesses receive international payments at low cost. The company competes with traditional wire transfers and platforms like Wise by offering a simpler, cheaper way to collect foreign currency and convert it to rupees.

As of early July 2026, Skydo has 8 open roles across engineering, product, growth, and operations. That is a lean team, so each hire carries real weight. Expect a process that moves at startup pace but takes quality seriously. Because the team is small, you are likely to meet founders or senior leaders early, and a strong culture fit matters as much as technical skill.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Skydo's interview process is typical of Indian B2B fintech startups at this stage, though exact steps vary by role and team. Here is what candidates commonly report.

Step 1: Application or referral screen
Your resume is reviewed, often quickly. A strong match on fintech or payments experience can move you to the next step within a few days.

Step 2: Recruiter or hiring manager call
A short introductory call to check role fit, notice period, and expectations. This is conversational, not deeply technical. Have your career story ready and know why you want to work at a cross-border payments company specifically.

Step 3: Technical or domain round
For engineering roles, expect a coding or system design exercise. For product and growth roles, expect a case study or product thinking exercise. This is where most of the real screening happens.

Step 4: Founder or senior leadership round
At a startup of Skydo's size, a co-founder or senior leader often joins one round. They look at how you think, whether you take ownership, and whether you align with the company's direction. Be ready to share opinions, not just facts.

Step 5: Offer discussion
If you clear the technical and leadership rounds, HR shares a written offer. Negotiation is possible if you have competing offers or strong market data from sources like Glassdoor or levels.fyi.

Total rounds usually sit between 3 and 4, though this can shift depending on the seniority of the role.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Skydo is solving a real pain point in cross-border payments, so they want people who understand the problem from the inside. Here is what matters most.

Fintech or payments context
Prior exposure to payment rails, forex, compliance, or banking APIs gives you a strong edge. You do not need to have worked at a bank, but you should be able to talk about how money moves across borders and where friction lives.

Startup mindset
Skydo is still building. They want people who are comfortable with ambiguity, can set their own direction, and do not need a large team around them to get things done. If you have only worked inside large structured organizations, be ready to explain how you handle open-ended problems.

Product thinking for non-product roles
Even engineers and growth folks are expected to ask 'why are we building this' and push back when something does not make sense. Shallow execution without curiosity is a red flag.

Clear communication
The team works cross-functionally. Whether you are in engineering, operations, or marketing, being able to explain your work simply and quickly matters a great deal.

Ownership over hierarchy
A small team means you will own outcomes, not just tasks. They look for people who follow through without being chased.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product deeply
Sign up for a Skydo account or watch a demo before your interview. Know the core flow: how a freelancer receives a dollar payment, what happens to the exchange rate, and how the money lands in their bank. Interviewers notice when you have actually used the product.

Research the competitive landscape
Know who Skydo competes with and why a customer would choose them. This comes up in product and growth interviews, but even engineers are sometimes asked to show they understand the market context.

Prepare a fintech story
If you have worked on anything adjacent to payments, banking, or financial products, prepare a crisp story about what you built, what broke, and what you learned. If you have not, find the closest analogy in your experience and draw the connection explicitly.

Practice case questions for product and growth roles
Skydo-style product questions tend to be metric-driven. Practice answering questions like 'how would you improve activation' or 'what would you track to know this feature is working.'

For engineering roles, revise system design for financial systems
Topics like idempotency, double-entry accounting, and payment retries come up at fintech companies. Being able to speak about data consistency and failure handling at a transaction level sets you apart from general software engineers.

Check salary benchmarks before negotiation
Use Glassdoor or levels.fyi to know what comparable roles pay at Indian fintech startups before your offer call. Going in without a number puts you at a disadvantage.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Skydo is a product-led, mission-driven startup built around a clear customer problem: Indian earners lose too much money when they get paid from abroad. That focus shapes everything about how the team works.

Speed and pragmatism
Decisions get made fast. People who prefer lengthy approval chains or detailed specs before moving tend to find startup culture uncomfortable. At Skydo, you are expected to make reasonable calls and course-correct quickly rather than wait for perfect information.

Customer empathy
The customer is a freelancer or small business owner who is not a finance expert. The team talks about making things simple and trustworthy, not technically impressive. If you can explain a feature in terms a non-technical user would immediately understand, you will fit in well.

Transparency
Small teams share a lot of context. You will likely know how the business is performing, what is not working, and where the company is headed. That transparency is a feature of the culture, not a side effect.

Learning over credentials
Skydo hires for intellectual curiosity and growth. If you come in humble and show you can pick things up fast, you will fit in better than someone with an impressive CV who thinks they already know the answers.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many roles is Skydo hiring for right now?

As of early July 2026, Skydo has 8 open positions. Roles span engineering, product, growth, and operations. Given the team size, each role is meaningful, and hiring tends to move faster than at larger companies where decisions sit in committee.

Does Skydo hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Skydo is a growth-stage startup, so most roles skew toward candidates with at least some professional experience. However, a strong internship in fintech or payments, combined with genuine product curiosity, can make a fresher competitive for certain roles. Always check the specific job description for stated experience requirements before applying.

Is the Skydo interview process fully remote?

Most rounds are conducted online, which is standard for Indian fintech startups. A final in-person meeting with founders is possible if you are based in or near their office city. Confirm the format with the recruiter early in the process so you can plan accordingly.

What salary can I expect at Skydo?

Skydo does not publish salary bands publicly. For market context, check Glassdoor or levels.fyi for roles at comparable Indian fintech startups. Go into your offer call with a number in mind based on your research and any competing offers, rather than waiting for them to anchor first.

How important is fintech experience for a Skydo role?

It depends on the role. For payments-specific or compliance roles, fintech experience is a strong advantage. For general engineering or growth roles, a strong track record in a related domain combined with genuine curiosity about how payments work can be enough. Show that you have taken time to understand the product and the problem it solves.

How do I stand out when applying to Skydo?

A tailored application that shows you understand cross-border payments and the specific pain point Skydo solves will stand out over a generic resume. A referral from someone inside the company also moves applications to the top of the queue. If you can speak to specific product decisions Skydo has made and share a grounded opinion on them, that signals exactly the kind of thinking they want on the team.
