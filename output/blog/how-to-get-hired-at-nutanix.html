01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Nutanix builds cloud infrastructure software, mainly hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) and multi-cloud management platforms. Its products are used by large enterprises globally, which means the engineering work here runs at genuine scale. India is a core hub for Nutanix, not just a support centre, with offices in Bangalore and Pune handling significant product engineering.

As of the latest data, Nutanix has 122 open roles across its global operations, with a healthy share based in India. These span software engineering (distributed systems, storage, networking, frontend), product management, pre-sales engineering, customer success, and cloud sales. If you are a tech professional who wants to work on infrastructure that powers real enterprise workloads, Nutanix is worth targeting seriously.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Nutanix hiring process typically runs through several stages, though the exact number of rounds can vary by role and level. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Application and resume screen: Your resume is reviewed by a recruiter who checks for role fit. A strong match on relevant technologies (cloud, virtualisation, distributed systems, or enterprise software sales) improves your chance of moving forward.

Recruiter call: A recruiter calls to confirm your background, discuss compensation expectations, and explain the role. Be ready to talk about your current work and why you are interested in Nutanix.

Technical phone screen: For engineering roles, expect one or two coding or system design questions. For sales and pre-sales roles, this is often a product or scenario-based discussion.

Technical interview rounds (typically 2-4 rounds): These are conducted over video call. Engineering candidates usually face data structures and algorithms problems, system design questions (especially at senior levels), and in-depth discussions about past projects. Pre-sales and support roles involve technical scenario walkthroughs and sometimes a short presentation.

Hiring manager round: A discussion focused on your experience, how you work in teams, and your career goals. Expect behavioural questions around ownership, customer impact, and how you handle ambiguity.

HR and final round: Compensation discussion, offer finalisation, and background verification.

The full process commonly takes two to four weeks from first screen to offer. Follow up politely with your recruiter if you do not hear back after a week at each stage.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Nutanix is an infrastructure company, so technical depth matters more here than at a typical product startup.

For engineering roles: They look for solid understanding of distributed systems, storage, or networking concepts depending on the team. Strong coding ability in C++, Go, Java, or Python is commonly expected. Beyond raw skill, Nutanix values engineers who think about scale, such as how your system behaves when handling thousands of nodes or large volumes of data. Past experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) or virtualisation (VMware, KVM) stands out.

For pre-sales and customer-facing roles: They look for people who can translate complex infrastructure concepts into business value for a CIO or IT head. Confident product demo skills, strong communication, and some understanding of enterprise IT procurement cycles matter here.

Across all roles: Nutanix consistently looks for ownership. They want people who take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks. If you can point to projects where you drove something end-to-end, bring those stories into every interview round.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Brush up on distributed systems: For any engineering role, topics like consistency models, replication, consensus algorithms (Raft, Paxos), and fault tolerance are relevant. Being able to discuss these signals the right depth even if your day-to-day work does not involve them directly.

Practice system design for scale: Focus on designs involving large-scale storage systems, virtual machine scheduling, or multi-cloud deployments. These map directly to what Nutanix builds and ships.

Review Nutanix's product portfolio: Understand what AOS (Acropolis Operating System), Prism, and Nutanix Cloud Platform do at a high level. Read their publicly available documentation and engineering blog posts. Interviewers notice when a candidate knows what the company actually ships.

For coding rounds: Practice data structures and algorithms on standard coding platforms. Focus on trees, graphs, dynamic programming, and string manipulation. Medium to hard difficulty problems are a reasonable target for senior engineering roles.

For pre-sales and sales roles: Prepare a clear story about handling a complex technical objection or closing a large enterprise deal. Know how to position cloud infrastructure value to a non-technical decision-maker.

Leverage your network: A referral from a current Nutanix employee helps your application move faster through the initial screen. Reach out before you apply, not after.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Nutanix describes its culture around customer obsession, integrity, and a bias toward action. In practice, employees in the India engineering teams commonly describe the environment as collaborative and product-focused, with meaningful ownership over features rather than just isolated component work.

The company runs a global distributed model, so you will work closely with teams in the US. Comfort with asynchronous communication and occasional overlap hours is useful, especially for senior or lead roles.

Nutanix has gone through a significant business model shift in recent years, moving from hardware-bundled appliances to a pure software subscription model. This has sharpened the internal focus on cloud metrics, customer retention, and product-led growth. If you enjoy working in a company that is actively reinventing how it goes to market, this is an interesting moment to join.

Work-life balance is generally described as reasonable for an enterprise software company. Infrastructure teams do have on-call rotations and project deadlines, so it is worth asking about this for your specific team during the hiring manager round. The India offices have active employee communities and resource groups.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Nutanix hire freshers or only experienced professionals?

Nutanix does hire freshers and recent graduates, primarily through campus recruitment and dedicated new-grad engineering programmes. Most of the 122 current open roles are for experienced professionals, but it is worth checking their careers page for new-grad or intern listings if you are just starting out. Strong problem-solving skills and relevant internship or project experience improve your chances significantly.

Which cities in India does Nutanix hire for?

Nutanix's main India offices are in Bangalore and Pune, and most India-based roles are located in these two cities. Some customer-facing roles like pre-sales engineers may be based in other major metros depending on the sales territory they cover. For remote or hybrid arrangements, it varies by team and role level, so ask the recruiter directly during your first call.

What is the typical salary at Nutanix India?

Specific compensation varies by role, level, and business unit. Based on publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, software engineering salaries at Nutanix India are commonly cited as competitive with other US-listed enterprise software companies at similar stages. You will get a clearer picture during the recruiter call, where compensation expectations are discussed early in the process.

How long does the full interview process take?

The process from application to offer commonly takes two to four weeks, though it can stretch longer for senior or specialised roles. If you clear the recruiter screen, the technical and hiring manager rounds are usually scheduled within one to two weeks. Follow up with your recruiter if you do not hear back after a week at each stage.

Does a referral really help at Nutanix?

A referral from a current Nutanix employee meaningfully improves the speed at which your resume gets reviewed, especially when a role has a high volume of applicants. It does not guarantee an offer, but it helps you get past the initial screen. If you know someone at Nutanix, reach out and ask if they are comfortable referring you before you submit your application.

What technologies should I focus on for a software engineering role?

The exact stack depends on the team, but commonly expected skills include C++, Python, Go, or Java, along with knowledge of distributed systems concepts and cloud platforms. For core infrastructure teams, familiarity with virtualisation (KVM, VMware ESXi) and storage architecture is a clear plus. For cloud platform roles, Kubernetes, Terraform, and hands-on experience with AWS or Azure are commonly relevant.
