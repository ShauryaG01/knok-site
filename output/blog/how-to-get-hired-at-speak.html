01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Speak is an AI-powered language learning company building tools that help people actually speak a new language, not just study it indefinitely. As of mid-2026, Speak has 44 open roles listed across engineering, product, design, growth, and operations. This is a live hiring window for Indian professionals with the right skills, especially those excited about AI and language technology.

Speak's process is selective but not unusually long by startup standards. Because the team is distributed globally, almost all interviews happen over video call, which means your location is rarely a barrier to getting through the rounds.

If you are an engineer, ML or AI specialist, product manager, or designer, the current openings are worth a close look. The company is growing its AI capabilities actively, and roles touching language models, speech recognition, and product personalisation are particularly in demand right now.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Speak's hiring process runs several rounds. Stages and timelines vary by role, so treat this as a general guide based on publicly reported candidate experiences.

Stage 1: Application and resume screen. Speak's recruiting team filters for role fit and relevant experience. A resume that highlights outcomes (what you built, what improved, what shipped) gets shortlisted faster than one listing only responsibilities.

Stage 2: Recruiter or hiring manager call. A short intro call to check your background and motivation for the role. Be ready to explain why language learning or AI products interest you specifically, not just 'exciting startup.'

Stage 3: Technical or skills assessment. For engineering roles, expect a take-home or live coding task focused on your core stack. ML roles often include a challenge around model evaluation, fine-tuning, or prompt design. Product roles may get a short case study or product critique exercise. Design roles typically involve a portfolio walk-through plus a brief design challenge.

Stage 4: Team interview loop. This is the core of the process. You meet multiple team members across the relevant function. Expect technical depth questions alongside behavioural questions. Because Speak's product is AI-native language learning, product sense comes up even in engineering loops.

Stage 5: Values and leadership round. A final conversation with a senior leader or sometimes a co-founder, focused on working style, long-term thinking, and culture fit. This round often decides close calls, so come prepared with thoughtful questions about the company's direction.

Offer stage. Speak moves at startup pace, so offers and overall timelines tend to be faster than at large tech companies.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Speak hires for three things above everything else: genuine product curiosity, strong execution, and clear communication.

Product curiosity means you have actually thought about how people learn languages, where existing apps fall short, and how AI can close that gap. Candidates who have used Speak, or who can discuss the language-learning space with real depth, stand out from day one.

Strong execution means a track record of shipping. For engineers, this means code that reached production and made a measurable difference. For product managers, it means features that users actually adopted. Speak is not a research lab. They want builders who finish things.

Clear communication matters because the team is distributed. Writing well, explaining your thinking clearly on a call, and pushing back constructively on ideas are all signals that interviewers actively look for. Candidates who are vague or who cannot defend their technical choices tend to struggle in the loop.

For technical roles, comfort with AI and ML concepts is increasingly important even when the role is not explicitly an ML position. Speak's product is deeply AI-native, so a working understanding of how language models, speech recognition, and evaluation pipelines function is an asset across every function.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Use the product before your first call. Download Speak, spend real time with it, and come with a genuine opinion on what works and what does not. Interviewers consistently notice when candidates have thought critically about the product. This single step separates prepared candidates from the rest.

Tailor your resume to outcomes. Replace 'responsible for X' with 'built X, which led to Y.' Even rough impact figures help. Speak is metrics-driven, and evidence of results reads better than a list of duties.

Brush up on AI and NLP basics if you are applying for an engineering or product role. You do not need to be an ML researcher. Understanding concepts like embeddings, fine-tuning, and model evaluation will make your technical conversations noticeably stronger.

Prepare your 'why Speak' answer seriously. This question comes up in every round. A generic answer about the 'exciting AI space' will not land well. Connect your answer to a specific belief about language learning, a gap you see in existing tools, or a personal experience with language barriers.

Practice system design or product critique out loud, not just in your head. Speak's rounds are conversational, and thinking clearly under mild pressure is something you can build before the interview.

For behavioural rounds, prepare stories in the situation-action-result format for three scenarios: a time you disagreed with a decision and how you handled it, a project that did not go as planned and what you learned, and your most impactful shipped work.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Speak runs on high ownership. People are expected to spot problems and move on them without waiting to be told. If you prefer structured environments with clear top-down direction, the pace here may feel uncomfortable. If you prefer doing over discussing, you will likely feel at home.

The team is international and mostly remote, which means written communication carries real weight. Slack messages, design docs, and async updates replace a lot of what would happen face-to-face. People who write with clarity and precision tend to grow faster within the organisation.

Speak does not glorify long hours as a badge of honour. The focus is on what shipped and whether it moved the metric. This makes it a workable environment for Indian professionals who want meaningful career growth without sacrificing all personal time.

The company's mission (helping people actually speak a language rather than study it indefinitely) attracts team members who genuinely care about the problem. The culture is warm but not casual about quality. Standards for the work itself are high, and feedback tends to be direct.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Speak hire candidates based in India?

Speak is a remote-first company and has hired internationally, including from India. Most roles are listed as remote or remote-friendly, which means your location is typically not a blocker. Check each job description carefully, as some roles may have timezone or regional requirements depending on the team you are joining.

How long does the full hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, Speak's process is commonly cited as faster than at large tech companies, given the startup pace. Being responsive between rounds and proactively asking your recruiter for expected timelines can help keep things moving on your side. The number of rounds varies by role, so it is worth clarifying this with the recruiter early in the process.

What salary can I expect from Speak?

Speak does not publicly list salary bands for most roles. According to Glassdoor and publicly reported data, compensation at AI-focused startups at Speak's stage varies widely by role and location. For roles hired on an Indian cost basis, public data is thin, so the best move is to ask the recruiter directly during the first call. Always clarify whether the figure is fixed pay, whether it includes equity, and what the equity vesting schedule looks like.

Is the interview process the same for engineering and non-engineering roles?

The broad structure (recruiter call, skills assessment, team loop, leadership round) is similar across functions, but the content differs significantly. Engineering roles have a heavier technical component with coding and system design questions. Product roles lean on case studies and product critique. Design roles involve a portfolio review and a design challenge. Behavioural questions and the 'why Speak' question appear across every function.

How competitive is it to get an offer from Speak?

Speak is a well-funded AI startup with a focused product, which attracts strong applicants, particularly for engineering and ML roles. With 44 open roles as of mid-2026, there are real opportunities across the business. Candidates who have genuinely used the product, can articulate a clear point of view on language learning, and have a track record of shipping work tend to move further through the process than those who cannot.

What is the best way to apply to Speak?

The most reliable route is through Speak's official careers page, where all current openings are listed. A referral from a current employee increases your visibility, so reaching out thoughtfully to Speak team members on LinkedIn before or alongside your application is worth trying. Make your resume outcome-focused and tailored to the specific role rather than sending a generic document across all openings.
