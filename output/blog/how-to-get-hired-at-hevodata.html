01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Hevodata builds a no-code data pipeline platform that connects sources like Salesforce, MySQL, Google Sheets, and many others into warehouses like BigQuery, Snowflake, and Redshift. The company is headquartered in San Francisco, with its core engineering and product teams based in Bengaluru, making it one of the more attractive product-first SaaS companies for Indian tech professionals who want global-scale exposure without relocating.

As of mid-2026, Hevodata has 40 open roles spanning software engineering, product management, customer success, sales, and marketing. Engineering positions make up a large share of these openings, covering backend systems, DevOps, and data infrastructure profiles. Hiring runs year-round rather than in fixed intake windows.

Hevodata is at a growth stage where the product serves a global customer base and teams are expanding to match that demand. Interviews reflect this: they are practical and thorough, focused on whether you can build and own things, not just talk about them.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The stages below reflect commonly reported candidate experiences. Exact structure and order may vary by role and team.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Shortlisting
Hevodata's recruiting team reviews applications for role fit, relevant tech stack, and experience level. For engineering roles, strong backend fundamentals and any exposure to data systems tend to move resumes forward. Referrals from current employees are commonly reported to get faster attention at this stage.

Stage 2: Recruiter Screening Call
A short call to confirm your interest, availability, notice period, and salary expectations. Be straightforward about your current CTC and what you are targeting. This is also a good time to ask about the team structure and the role's day-to-day scope.

Stage 3: Online Coding Assessment or Take-Home Task
For engineering roles, expect a timed coding assessment covering data structures, algorithms, and sometimes SQL. Some roles get a take-home problem instead, often involving a mini pipeline or API design scenario. Publicly reported assessments are commonly cited as running around 90 minutes to 2 hours of focused work.

Stage 4: Technical Interview Round 1
A live coding session with an engineer from the team. Problems lean toward practical scenarios: parsing data, writing efficient queries, or building a small system component. Hevodata interviewers are commonly reported to value clear reasoning over perfect syntax, so explain your thinking as you go.

Stage 5: System Design Round
For mid-level and senior engineering roles, a system design discussion is standard. Topics commonly cited by candidates include designing a data ingestion pipeline, handling large event streams, and building fault-tolerant ETL workflows. Prior exposure to distributed systems concepts, message queues, or warehouse connectors gives you a meaningful edge here.

Stage 6: Hiring Manager or Cross-Functional Round
This round focuses on how you think, collaborate, and handle ambiguity. Expect questions about past projects, the ownership you have taken, and trade-offs you have navigated. For customer-facing roles, this round often tests communication clarity and business understanding.

Stage 7: Offer and Reference Checks
If rounds go well, the recruiting team shares a verbal offer followed by a formal letter. Reference checks typically happen in parallel or just before the offer. Offers at Hevodata are publicly reported to include a base salary, a performance-linked variable, and ESOPs for select roles.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Hevodata's product sits at the intersection of distributed systems, data engineering, and developer experience. What they value in candidates reflects that focus directly.

Strong backend and data fundamentals. For engineering roles, proficiency in Java, Go, or Python is expected. You should understand how data flows through systems: from sources, through transformations, into storage. Concepts like idempotency, schema evolution, exactly-once delivery, and backpressure come up in technical discussions. SQL fluency is treated as a baseline, not a bonus.

Product mindset across all roles. Engineers at Hevodata are expected to understand why a feature matters to the customer, not just how to build it. If you have owned a feature end-to-end, worked directly with users, or driven improvements based on real feedback, those are stories worth telling in your interview.

Technical fluency for non-engineering roles. Customer success managers who can explain pipeline errors to a client, and sales engineers who can demo an ETL flow live, are profiles Hevodata actively seeks. Domain knowledge about data teams (analysts, BI leads, data engineers) sets you apart from candidates who only know the product surface.

Ownership and initiative. This comes up consistently in candidate feedback. Hevodata is scaling fast, and they want people who move without waiting to be told. Examples of proactively solving a problem, building something without a formal ask, or stepping up during an ambiguous situation land well in any interview round.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand data pipeline concepts before the interview. Hevodata's product is the implicit context for every technical conversation. Read up on what CDC (change data capture) means, why idempotency matters in ETL jobs, and how warehouses handle schema changes. You do not need to have built these things at scale, but you should be able to reason through them out loud.

Brush up on distributed systems fundamentals. For senior engineering roles, topics like consistency vs. availability trade-offs, message queue design, and horizontal scaling patterns are commonly cited by candidates who have been through the process. A confident handle on CAP theorem trade-offs and real-world architecture decisions will help you in the system design round.

Make SQL a strength, not an afterthought. Window functions, CTEs, query optimisation, and joins on large datasets are standard expectations. Practice writing clean, readable SQL under time pressure, because live SQL rounds at Hevodata test both correctness and clarity of approach.

Prepare three strong project stories. For every interview round, you will likely be asked about past work. Pick projects where you owned meaningful parts, faced real technical challenges, and can speak to the impact of your decisions. Hevodata interviewers are commonly reported to go deep with follow-up questions, so choose projects you know inside-out and can defend at any level of detail.

Ask questions that signal domain curiosity. Asking about how Hevodata handles schema drift at scale, or what the team is focused on in connector reliability, shows you understand the problem space. Generic questions carry less weight with a technically strong panel.

For non-engineering roles, study Hevodata's customer base and public case studies. Understand the pain points that data teams bring to the table and be fluent in the language of analysts, data engineers, and BI leads. This domain knowledge sets you apart from candidates who know only the product surface.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Hevodata describes itself as customer-obsessed, and that orientation shows up in how teams are structured. Engineers are expected to read customer tickets, get involved in support escalations when needed, and build with real user pain points in mind rather than with purely elegant abstractions.

The pace is fast but not chaotic. Hevodata has moved past the early stage where everything is on fire. It is a scaling company with real processes, but decisions still move quickly and ownership sits with individual contributors rather than with layers of management. People who thrive here tend to be self-directed and comfortable making calls with incomplete information.

The Bengaluru team is technically strong and collaborative. Candidates who have joined Hevodata commonly cite their colleagues as one of the top reasons they stayed. Code reviews are thorough, architecture discussions are open, and there is a genuine learning culture rather than a gatekeeping one.

Work-life balance sits in the 'startup-reasonable' range. Crunch periods exist, especially around major releases or enterprise commitments, but Hevodata is not commonly associated with a grind culture. Industry surveys suggest the team rates internal transparency and manager quality above average for a company at this stage.

ESOPs are part of the picture for senior roles. For candidates thinking about long-term upside, Hevodata does offer equity participation at certain levels. The company's growth trajectory has made this a meaningful part of total compensation for some employees, based on publicly reported accounts.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Hevodata currently have?

As of mid-2026, Hevodata has 40 open roles across engineering, product, customer success, sales, and marketing. The majority are based in Bengaluru, with some go-to-market positions open to remote or hybrid arrangements. New roles get added throughout the year, so checking their careers page regularly is worthwhile.

How long does the Hevodata hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process is commonly cited as taking 3-5 weeks end to end for most engineering roles. The recruiter screen and coding assessment usually happen within the first two weeks, with technical rounds and the hiring manager conversation following after. Offers typically come within a week of the final round, though senior roles with more stages can run longer.

What salary can I expect at Hevodata?

Hevodata does not publish salary bands publicly. Based on Glassdoor reviews and industry surveys, compensation at growth-stage SaaS product companies in Bengaluru is commonly cited as competitive relative to service firms, with ESOPs adding meaningful upside for senior hires. Ask the recruiter for the band during the screening call so there are no surprises at the offer stage.

Does Hevodata hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Based on their publicly listed job descriptions, Hevodata primarily hires for roles that require prior professional experience. Fresher hiring happens occasionally, typically through campus partnerships or internship-to-full-time conversions. If you are early in your career, applying for an internship first and converting is a more reliable path than applying directly for a full-time engineering role.

Is data engineering or ETL experience required to get a job at Hevodata?

For engineering roles, prior ETL or pipeline experience helps but is not strictly required. You do need to understand the core concepts: data movement, pipeline reliability, idempotency, and schema handling. Candidates with strong backend or distributed systems backgrounds and a genuine interest in data infrastructure are commonly reported to make the transition well.

How much do referrals matter when applying to Hevodata?

Referrals help at Hevodata, as they do at most product companies. Referred candidates are commonly reported to get faster recruiter callbacks and sometimes skip the initial resume screening step. If you know someone at Hevodata, reaching out before you apply is worth doing. If you do not have a referral, a strong resume with clear backend or data experience and a note explaining your interest in the problem space still gives you a solid shot.
