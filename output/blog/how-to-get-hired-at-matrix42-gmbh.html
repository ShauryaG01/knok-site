01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Matrix42 GmbH is a German enterprise software company focused on unified endpoint management (UEM), IT service management (ITSM), and workspace security. The company serves large enterprise clients, primarily in the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and broader Europe, with a growing footprint in other markets.

As of July 2026, Matrix42 has 17 open roles listed across job boards, spanning software engineering, product management, sales, professional services, and customer success. The company hires on a rolling basis, and technical roles in particular can take several weeks to fill given the specialised skill sets required.

For Indian candidates, the most relevant opportunities are usually in software development, QA, and technical support. Many roles require candidates to be based in Europe or willing to relocate, though some engineering positions have been listed as hybrid or remote-friendly. Always check the job description for location requirements before applying.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Matrix42 typically spans several stages, though the exact steps vary by role and team.

Application screening. Your resume and any cover letter are reviewed by the HR or talent team. For technical roles, recruiters commonly look for relevant experience with enterprise software platforms, endpoint management tools, or ITSM environments.

Initial HR call. A recruiter or HR business partner reaches out for a brief introductory call to discuss your background, salary expectations, and availability. This is also your chance to confirm whether the role is remote, hybrid, or fully on-site.

Technical or functional interview. For engineering roles, this typically involves a coding exercise, architecture discussion, or a take-home task. For sales, consulting, or customer success roles, expect questions about domain knowledge and past client engagements.

Panel or hiring manager interview. A more in-depth conversation with the team lead or hiring manager. Expect behavioural questions alongside role-specific topics. Some candidates report this stage as two separate conversations rather than one combined session.

Final discussion or offer. A final round with a senior leader or a cross-functional panel is common before an offer is extended. References may be checked at this stage.

The complete process can take a few weeks from first contact to offer, depending on team availability and the seniority of the role.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Matrix42 looks for candidates who combine technical depth with a practical understanding of enterprise IT environments. Several qualities consistently matter across roles.

Domain knowledge. Familiarity with endpoint management, IT asset management, or ITSM frameworks such as ITIL is a strong plus for most technical and consulting roles. Direct experience with UEM or workspace security software is even better.

Problem-solving mindset. Matrix42 works with large enterprise clients who have strict compliance and security requirements. They value candidates who can think through complex scenarios methodically and propose practical, scalable solutions.

Clear communication. Roles often require explaining technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders, both internally and with clients. Written and verbal communication skills matter at every level.

Language ability. German is a significant advantage for roles serving the DACH market or based in Germany. English fluency is expected across most roles regardless of location.

Ownership and initiative. Hiring managers look for people who take responsibility for outcomes and drive work forward without needing constant direction.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product stack. Before your interview, spend time understanding what Matrix42 actually builds. Go through their product documentation for tools like the Matrix42 Enterprise Service Management platform and their endpoint security offerings. Being able to speak to specific features signals genuine interest rather than a generic application.

Brush up on ITSM and UEM concepts. Many roles touch ITIL processes, endpoint lifecycle management, or software asset management. Reviewing these fundamentals will help you answer scenario-based questions with confidence.

Prepare examples using the STAR method. For behavioural questions, structure your answers around Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Focus on examples where you solved a complex technical or client-facing problem, ideally in an enterprise context.

Know your own numbers. If you are applying for a sales or customer success role, be ready to discuss targets you have hit, accounts you have managed, and measurable outcomes from your past work. Use your own real figures from previous roles.

Prepare thoughtful questions. Ask about the team structure, how the product roadmap is decided, or what success looks like in the role after the first few months. This signals genuine interest and helps you evaluate whether the role fits your goals.

Sort out your salary expectation. For Germany-based roles, research publicly reported salary ranges on platforms like Glassdoor or Levels.fyi before the HR call. It is standard practice in Germany to be asked for a Gehaltsvorstellung (salary expectation) early in the process, so prepare a specific range in advance.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Matrix42 operates as a mid-size enterprise software company headquartered in Germany, and that shapes a great deal about how the organisation runs.

Engineering-led and customer-focused. The company's products are deeply technical, and there is genuine respect for people who understand the craft of building reliable software for large enterprises. Customer feedback plays a meaningful role in how the product roadmap is shaped.

European work culture. Expect clear boundaries between work and personal time, structured processes for decision-making, and a relatively flat hierarchy compared to large multinationals. Meetings tend to be purposeful and well-prepared rather than frequent and open-ended.

Flexible work options. In recent years, Matrix42 has offered hybrid work arrangements for a number of roles, particularly in engineering and product. The specifics vary by team, so confirm the arrangement for any role you apply to before accepting an offer.

Diverse teams. The company serves clients across multiple countries, so teams tend to include people from varied national and professional backgrounds. Adaptability and cross-cultural awareness are valued qualities.

Stability with a focus on cloud growth. Matrix42 has a long track record in the enterprise software space and a stable client base. Based on publicly available information, the current strategic direction points toward expanding cloud and SaaS capabilities within the core product portfolio.

Does Matrix42 hire remotely for candidates based in India?

Most Matrix42 roles are based in Germany or elsewhere in Europe, and many require you to be physically present or at least in a nearby time zone. Some engineering and product roles have been listed as remote-friendly in recent years, but remote-from-India arrangements are uncommon for this company. Your best approach is to apply to roles explicitly marked as remote and clarify the geography policy during the initial HR call.

How long does the Matrix42 hiring process take?

Based on commonly reported candidate experiences, the process from application to offer typically spans a few weeks. Technical roles may take a little longer if multiple rounds are involved. Delays are usually due to interviewer availability or internal approvals rather than lack of interest, so a polite follow-up after each stage is a reasonable move.

Is German language required to work at Matrix42?

English is widely used at Matrix42, especially in engineering and product teams. However, German is a strong advantage for roles involving direct client interaction in the DACH market, or for on-site roles in Germany where day-to-day team communication may happen in German. Language requirements are usually stated clearly in the job description, so read it carefully before applying.

What salary can I expect at Matrix42?

Matrix42 does not publicly advertise salary bands for most roles. For Germany-based positions, you can find commonly cited ranges on Glassdoor or Levels.fyi by searching for comparable roles at similar enterprise software companies. It is standard practice in Germany to discuss salary expectation during the first HR call, so prepare a specific range in advance rather than leaving it open-ended.

How many open roles does Matrix42 currently have?

What is the best way to stand out in a Matrix42 application?

Tailor your resume to highlight experience with enterprise software, IT management platforms, or ITSM frameworks. A brief cover letter that references specific Matrix42 products and explains what draws you to the company's focus area makes a clear impression. If you have hands-on experience with endpoint management, workspace security, or service management tooling, call that out explicitly rather than leaving it for the interviewer to infer.
