01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

The first thing to know about Mobile Engineer salaries in India: most job listings simply do not show the pay. Of the 79 Mobile Engineer openings tracked by knok jobradar as of July 2026, salary figures were absent from the listings. This is the norm across Indian job boards, where companies prefer to negotiate in private rather than publish a number upfront.

Because the listings are silent on pay, the ranges in this guide draw from Glassdoor reviews, levels.fyi submissions, and industry surveys. They reflect what candidates have reported receiving, not what employers officially advertised. Use them to anchor your expectations and your negotiations, not as a promise.

Mobile Engineers in India primarily specialise in iOS (Swift, Objective-C) or Android (Kotlin, Java), with a fast-growing cross-platform segment covering Flutter and React Native. Pay varies meaningfully between these tracks at senior levels, with iOS and Flutter roles at product companies typically commanding a small premium, per industry surveys. The city you work in matters too, and Bangalore continues to host the largest share of openings in this snapshot.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Research before you walk in. Check Glassdoor, levels.fyi, and LinkedIn salary insights for your specific role, city, and company size. Walk in with a range backed by publicly reported data, not just a gut number.

Let the company make the first offer. If asked for your expected CTC upfront, say you would like to understand the full role before discussing compensation. Once you have a written offer, you are in a much stronger position to push back.

Total comp is more than base. Ask specifically about variable pay, joining bonus, ESOPs, and how frequently performance reviews happen. A lower base with strong ESOPs at a funded startup can outperform a higher base at a slower-growth company.

Use competing offers. If you are in multiple processes, time them to overlap. A real competing offer is the single most effective negotiation lever available to you.

Negotiate joining bonus separately. Companies that cannot move on base salary often have flexibility on a one-time joining bonus. Always ask, even if the written offer looks final.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

When a company quotes a CTC number, it almost never means that amount lands in your bank account each month. Understanding the components helps you compare offers accurately.

Fixed base salary is the core monthly pay and typically makes up the largest share of CTC for roles below senior level.

Variable or performance bonus is paid quarterly or annually and is often tied to both individual and company targets. For many roles, a portion of the quoted CTC is this variable component, and it is not guaranteed.

ESOPs (Employee Stock Options) are common at startups and can form a meaningful share of comp at senior levels, per industry surveys. Vesting schedules, strike price, and the company's funding stage all affect actual value.

Joining bonus is a one-time payment, sometimes used to offset notice-period losses from your current employer.

Gratuity and PF are statutory components that are technically part of CTC but are deferred or restricted in access. Always ask for the in-hand monthly figure, not just the headline CTC.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

What is the salary for a Mobile Engineer fresher in India?

Freshers entering Mobile Engineering typically see commonly cited starting packages in the 4-9 LPA range on Glassdoor, depending on the company and city. Product startups and MNCs tend to pay higher than IT services firms at the entry level. Bangalore generally offers stronger fresher packages than most other cities, according to industry surveys. Building a portfolio with at least one published app or a solid open-source contribution significantly improves your starting offer.

Is iOS or Android better paid in India?

The difference is small at junior levels, but iOS engineers tend to command a slight premium at senior and lead levels, per industry surveys. This is partly because the iOS developer talent pool in India is smaller relative to Android. Flutter and React Native engineers are increasingly competitive, especially at startups that want a single codebase across platforms. Your actual pay will depend far more on the company and your experience than on the platform you specialise in.

Why do most Mobile Engineer job listings not show salary?

Indian employers have historically kept compensation confidential so they can offer different packages based on a candidate's current salary and negotiation style. This practice is being challenged by newer platforms and by candidates who refuse to share their existing CTC first. You can research publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi so you are not negotiating blind. Knowing the market rate before the conversation starts is your strongest tool.

How much does location affect a Mobile Engineer's salary?

Bangalore remains the highest-paying city for Mobile Engineers in India, according to industry surveys, largely because it has the highest concentration of well-funded product companies. Mumbai also tends to pay above average for the right employers, per Glassdoor data. Pune and Chennai are competitive but typically sit slightly lower on average. With remote work more widely accepted now, senior engineers can sometimes negotiate a Bangalore-equivalent package while staying in a lower-cost city.

What skills push a Mobile Engineer's salary higher?

Ownership of features end to end, including performance optimisation and release management, is what separates a higher-paid senior from a mid-level engineer. Expertise in Jetpack Compose (Android) or SwiftUI (iOS) is increasingly valued, as is experience with CI/CD pipelines for mobile. Publicly reported offers on levels.fyi show that engineers who can also contribute to system design discussions tend to command higher packages. Demonstrated impact, such as reducing app crash rates or improving load times, carries more weight than a long list of frameworks.
