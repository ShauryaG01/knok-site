01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Consensys is one of the most recognized names in the Web3 and blockchain space, best known for building MetaMask, Infura, and developer tools that power the Ethereum ecosystem. The company operates as a fully remote, globally distributed team and regularly hires software engineers, product managers, designers, and business operations roles.

As of mid-2026, Consensys has 8 open roles listed across job sites. If you are a blockchain developer or someone actively transitioning into Web3, this is one of the most credible companies to have on your resume. The hiring bar is high, but the process is structured and candidate-friendly by most accounts.

Consensys hires globally and does not require candidates to relocate. For Indian professionals, this means you can apply for roles that would otherwise be geographically out of reach.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Based on what candidates have publicly reported, the Consensys hiring process typically moves through these stages:

Application and resume screen: Your resume goes to a recruiter who checks for relevant experience, particularly any blockchain or Ethereum exposure. A short note in your application explaining your Web3 interest can help you stand out from generic applicants.

Recruiter call: A talent team member walks through your background, explains the role, and gauges your motivation for joining a Web3-native company. They may ask about your familiarity with Ethereum, smart contracts, or decentralized systems.

Technical screen: For engineering roles, expect a coding challenge or take-home assignment. This commonly involves writing or reviewing Solidity code, working with JavaScript and TypeScript, or demonstrating backend systems knowledge. For non-engineering roles, this stage may be a portfolio review or a short written case study.

Panel interviews: You typically speak with several people including your potential manager, a senior engineer, and sometimes a cross-functional stakeholder. Questions cover both technical depth and how you think about decentralization as a concept.

Final culture interview: A conversation focused on values alignment, remote work habits, and how you collaborate across time zones. Consensys places significant weight on async communication skills.

Offer stage: If all rounds go well, HR sends a written offer. Equity in the form of tokens or options is common at this stage, so come prepared to discuss total compensation rather than just base salary.

The full process can take a few weeks to over a month depending on the role and team. With 8 open roles currently active, there are live hiring pipelines running right now.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Consensys looks for people who genuinely believe in decentralization, not just those chasing a Web3 trend. A few things that matter across most roles:

Blockchain fluency: For technical roles, hands-on experience with Ethereum, Solidity, or EVM-compatible chains is a strong signal. Even for non-engineering roles, understanding how wallets, gas fees, and smart contracts work gives you a clear edge over general candidates.

Remote-first mindset: The team is spread across many countries. Consensys values people who document their work, communicate clearly in writing, and can manage their own time without constant check-ins.

Open-source contributions: If you have GitHub activity related to Ethereum, DeFi protocols, or developer tooling, highlight it prominently. Public contributions carry more weight here than at most traditional tech companies.

Problem ownership: They favor candidates who identify problems independently and propose solutions rather than waiting for direction. Examples from past work where you drove something end-to-end are very useful in interviews.

Genuine curiosity about the ecosystem: Interviewers can tell the difference between someone who follows Ethereum developments closely and someone who memorized a few facts the night before. Depth of interest matters.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Learn the product stack: Spend time with MetaMask and Infura before your interview. Create a test wallet, interact with a testnet, and understand what Infura provides to developers. Interviewers appreciate candidates who have actually used the products.

Brush up on Ethereum fundamentals: Even if you are applying for a non-engineering role, know the basics: what a smart contract is, how gas works, and why the Ethereum upgrade roadmap matters. For engineers, review Solidity patterns, common audit vulnerabilities, and ERC token standards.

Prepare remote-work stories: Think of specific examples where you collaborated across time zones or resolved a miscommunication in an async setting. Consensys asks about this directly and takes it seriously.

Research the Web3 landscape: Know who the key competitors are, what Layer 2 chains are gaining traction, and where Consensys sits in the broader ecosystem. Showing genuine curiosity about the space signals strong culture fit.

Ask smart questions: Questions about the team's roadmap, how they approach decentralization trade-offs, or how they measure developer adoption show that you think beyond the job description.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Consensys has a culture built around the idea that the internet's financial infrastructure should be open and permissionless. Day-to-day, this shows up in a few visible ways:

Fully remote and async: There is no central office. Teams are expected to communicate clearly in writing, use video calls purposefully, and respect teammates across different time zones. If you thrive in a structured office environment, this culture shift requires deliberate adjustment.

Mission-driven, not just product-driven: Many employees at Consensys are here because they believe Ethereum changes how value moves in the world. Candidates who show only surface-level interest in crypto tend to stand out for the wrong reasons during interviews.

Flat-ish and fast-moving: Decision-making is often distributed. Engineers and designers can influence product direction more directly than at large enterprises, but this also means more ambiguity and less hand-holding day-to-day.

Diverse and globally distributed: The team spans multiple continents and time zones. Inclusive communication and cultural sensitivity are everyday expectations. For Indian professionals used to working with international clients, this environment often feels familiar.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Consensys hire freshers or entry-level candidates?

Consensys occasionally hires junior engineers and entry-level roles, but most openings expect some prior experience with blockchain development or relevant software engineering. If you are early in your career, building a personal project on Ethereum or contributing to an open-source Web3 repository significantly improves your chances. Internship and graduate programs do appear from time to time, so check their careers page regularly.

What salary can I expect at Consensys in India?

Consensys does not publicly disclose India-specific salary bands. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list compensation figures for various roles globally, and industry surveys suggest Web3-native companies often pay above market rates for strong blockchain talent. Equity in the form of tokens or options is a meaningful part of total compensation at many crypto-native firms, so factor that into your evaluation alongside the base salary.

Is the Consensys interview process difficult?

Candidates commonly report the technical rounds as thorough, particularly for smart contract and backend engineering roles. Non-technical rounds tend to focus heavily on Web3 knowledge and remote-work habits. Preparing with real Ethereum projects and having concrete examples of async collaboration tends to reduce the difficulty significantly.

Does Consensys hire from India?

Yes, Consensys hires globally and has team members in India. Since the company is fully remote, location is rarely a barrier for roles that do not require physical presence. Check the specific job listing for any location restrictions, as some roles tied to regulated financial products may have residency requirements in certain markets.

How important is Solidity knowledge for getting hired at Consensys?

For smart contract engineering roles, Solidity is essentially required. For other engineering positions such as backend, DevOps, or frontend, Solidity knowledge is a bonus but not always mandatory. Non-engineering roles like product management, marketing, and operations do not require coding ability, though general blockchain literacy is expected across the board.

How do I stand out in the Consensys application process?

The most effective approach is to demonstrate genuine engagement with the Ethereum ecosystem before you apply. Public GitHub contributions, writing about Web3 topics, or building something on a testnet all serve as strong signals. Consensys recruiters commonly look for candidates who are already participants in the ecosystem, not just job seekers who discovered crypto recently.
