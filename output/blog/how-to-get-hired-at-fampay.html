01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

FamPay is a Bengaluru-based fintech startup that built India's first payments card and app designed for teenagers, giving them a way to spend and save digitally before they hold a standard bank account. The company has grown steadily since its launch and is backed by well-known investors in the Indian startup ecosystem. As of July 2026, FamPay has around 20 open roles across engineering, product, growth, design, and operations. That is a healthy pipeline for a focused fintech startup, and it means genuine opportunities exist for candidates who fit the profile.

FamPay hires through its own careers page and LinkedIn, and referrals carry meaningful weight here as they do at most Indian startups of this size. Roles can open and close quickly, so checking listings regularly pays off. If you are serious about joining, getting on the radar of the team before a specific role opens is often more effective than cold-applying.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process at FamPay typically runs through four to five stages, though the exact sequence can vary by role and team. The stages below are based on publicly reported candidate experiences and are a reasonable guide rather than a guarantee.

Stage 1: Application and resume screening. The recruiter reviews your resume for basic role fit. For engineering roles, this means your tech stack and past projects. For business roles, they check for domain experience and any fintech exposure.

Stage 2: Recruiter call. A short introductory call to check your background, current notice period, and expectations. This is also your chance to ask basic questions about the team and role.

Stage 3: Technical or skill round. Engineers typically face a coding round covering data structures, algorithms, and system design. Product managers can expect a case study or product sense exercise. Growth and marketing candidates are often given a brief or a task to complete.

Stage 4: Hiring manager round. A deeper conversation with the team lead or the manager who will be your direct report. Expect questions about past work, how you make decisions under uncertainty, and how you handle a fast-moving environment.

Stage 5 (for senior and cross-functional roles): Leadership round. FamPay is a founder-led company, and senior hires sometimes speak with a co-founder. This round is usually a culture and vision alignment conversation rather than a skills test.

Once rounds are cleared, an offer and background verification follow. If you are waiting for feedback after a round, a polite follow-up to your recruiter is perfectly normal.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

FamPay solves a specific problem for a specific user group, and they tend to hire people who genuinely understand that context. A few qualities that come up repeatedly in what the team values:

Ownership mindset. Teams at FamPay are small, and there is no room for someone who waits to be told what to do. They want people who identify a problem and drive it to resolution.

Product curiosity. Whether you are an engineer, a designer, or someone in growth, FamPay values people who think about the user experience and not just their own function in isolation.

Comfort with ambiguity. Processes at a startup of this stage are still being built. If you need clear structure and defined playbooks to do your best work, it is worth thinking carefully about whether this environment suits you.

Domain knowledge in fintech. For technical and product roles, experience with payments, UPI, prepaid cards, or banking APIs is a genuine differentiator. For growth roles, having worked on consumer apps in India is valued.

Clear communication. Startup teams move fast and collaborate closely. Being able to explain your thinking clearly, especially in writing, matters more here than it might at a larger company.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A few concrete steps that will put you in a stronger position before and during your interviews:

Use the product before your interview. Download the FamPay app, explore the card and payments experience, and form a point of view on what works and what could be better. Interviewers notice candidates who have actually used the product and have genuine observations.

Know the fintech landscape. Understand who FamPay's competitors are and what makes their user segment distinct. Keep up with recent news on UPI, RBI guidelines on prepaid payment instruments, and the broader digital payments space in India.

For engineers: Practice data structures, algorithms, and system design. Candidate accounts commonly describe the technical rounds as requiring solid fundamentals. Understanding how payment systems work at an architectural level is a useful addition.

For product and growth roles: Prepare a structured answer for 'how would you grow FamPay.' Use a framework such as AARRR to organise your thinking, and come with specific ideas grounded in how Indian teenagers and parents actually behave, not just generic growth tactics.

Prepare your 'why FamPay' answer. This is a startup that could easily ask why you are not joining a larger company. Have a genuine answer ready about what draws you to the problem space, the user group, or the stage of the company.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

FamPay operates with a classic early-stage startup culture. A few things candidates and employees consistently describe:

Fast pace and shifting priorities. The team moves quickly, and what is important today may change next month. The people who thrive here tend to be adaptable and energised by that pace rather than frustrated by it.

Flat and collaborative. The team is relatively young and the hierarchy is informal in practice. Junior employees regularly interact with founders and senior leaders. Ideas are expected to come from everywhere, not just the top.

Mission-driven work. The founders built FamPay to give young Indians a head start on financial literacy and independence. Employees who connect with that mission tend to find the work more meaningful.

Bengaluru-based with some flexibility. The core team works out of Bengaluru. Like many Indian startups since 2024, there is some flexibility in work location for certain roles, but candidates should confirm the arrangement for their specific position during the recruiter call.

High ownership, high visibility. Because the team is small, your work is seen. That is a significant positive for people who want to build their career quickly, and it is a reason many candidates specifically target companies at this stage.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does FamPay currently have?

As of July 2026, FamPay has around 20 open roles across engineering, product, growth, design, and operations. This number shifts as the startup hires and new projects come up, so check their careers page or LinkedIn for the most current listings. The mix of roles at any given time reflects where the company is investing most actively.

Is FamPay a good company for freshers or recent graduates?

FamPay occasionally hires strong freshers for engineering and growth positions, but most open roles expect some prior work experience. If you are a recent graduate, look for internship opportunities first, since FamPay has run internship programmes in the past. A solid portfolio, open-source contributions, or a notable college project can help you stand out when applying early in your career.

What salary can I expect at FamPay?

FamPay does not publish official salary bands. Glassdoor and similar platforms have some figures reported by current and former employees, but sample sizes are small, so treat them as rough indicators. Your best move is to ask the recruiter directly during the first call. Industry surveys suggest fintech startups at a comparable stage in India pay competitively, especially for engineering roles, but your specific band will depend on experience and the role level.

How long does the FamPay hiring process take from application to offer?

Candidates commonly report the full process taking a few weeks from first contact to offer, though urgent roles sometimes move faster. The biggest variable is scheduling: rounds can stack up quickly if both sides are available, or stretch out if there are calendar conflicts. Following up with your recruiter after each round is normal and usually welcome.

Does FamPay hire for remote roles?

FamPay is headquartered in Bengaluru and most of the core team works from there. Some roles allow partial remote or hybrid arrangements, but this varies by team and function. Clarify the work-from-office expectation during your recruiter call rather than assuming, especially if you are applying from outside Bengaluru.

What is the best way to get a referral at FamPay?

LinkedIn is the most practical starting point. Search for FamPay employees in the team or function you are targeting, and send a short, specific message explaining what draws you to the role and what you bring to it. Avoid generic 'please refer me' messages, as these are rarely acted on. Attending fintech meetups or startup events in Bengaluru where FamPay team members speak is another route that can open doors without feeling transactional.
