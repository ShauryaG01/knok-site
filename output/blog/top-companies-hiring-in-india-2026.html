07 Methodology

Methodology

This report draws from the knok job index, a nightly crawl of 150+ Indian job boards, company career pages, and aggregators. The snapshot date is 2026-07-08. Listings are deduplicated by URL and content fingerprint, then tagged by role family, experience band, and city. Posting frequency per employer is counted across the active window, not cumulative history, so a company with many open roles right now ranks higher than one that hired heavily last quarter but has since paused. No role or city filter was applied for this report. Because job postings open and close daily, all figures reflect a point-in-time view. Treat counts as directional signals, not precise market-share data.

11 What It Means

What It Means

A few honest takeaways if you are using this to decide where to focus your job search.

High posting volume does not always mean the fastest offer. IT services firms post in large batches tied to project pipelines. Cycle times can stretch longer than at product companies or GCCs, and it is harder to distinguish roles from the outside. Apply, but keep your pipeline diverse.

GCCs are the underrated option. The India development centres of Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and SAP hire heavily in Hyderabad and Bengaluru. Roles often carry global scope, modern tech stacks, and compensation commonly cited in industry surveys as meaningfully higher than traditional IT services packages. They are less visible to candidates who are only watching the IT services names.

City choice matters more than most people admit. If you are in Bengaluru or Hyderabad, your shortlist from this snapshot is widest. Pune is strong for cloud and IT services. Delhi NCR is better for fintech and consulting than for deep product engineering. Chennai is a solid base for IT services and analytics. If you are stuck in a smaller city, the remote share of product company postings has grown since 2024 and is worth targeting directly.

Skills signal faster than titles. Across every sector in this snapshot, postings mentioning AI, LLMs, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), or MLOps appear at higher frequency than traditional SDLC roles. Even one real project on your resume in these areas changes your match rate.

This is a snapshot, not a permanent ranking. Hiring cycles shift week to week. The employers on this list are active right now. Some will slow down; others not on the list will accelerate. The freshest data always beats a static report.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company hires the most software engineers in India right now?

By posting volume in the knok job index, the large IT services firms (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Accenture) consistently show the highest raw counts. Among product and GCC companies, Amazon India and Microsoft India are frequently cited in publicly reported hiring announcements as among the largest tech employers in the country. Specific lateral intake numbers shift quarterly, so check each company's careers page for the current picture.

Is it better to target IT services companies or product companies for a tech role?

It depends on what you want. IT services companies offer high volume, structured career ladders, and relative stability. Product companies and GCCs typically offer higher compensation (commonly cited in industry surveys) and faster skill growth. Many candidates apply to both and decide based on the actual offer, which is a reasonable approach given how different the day-to-day work can feel.

Which Indian city has the most tech job openings?

Bengaluru leads in the knok snapshot across software engineering, AI/ML, and product roles. Hyderabad is close behind, driven heavily by GCC hiring from global tech companies. Pune is strong for IT services and cloud. These three cities together cover the majority of active tech postings in the index.

Are there strong tech job markets outside the top cities?

Yes, and the share is growing since 2024. Chennai has a solid IT services and analytics base. Mumbai is the right city for BFSI tech and fintech product roles. Delhi NCR (Noida and Gurgaon) has a healthy GCC and consulting presence. Smaller cities like Coimbatore and Indore are picking up volume, though their total share is still smaller than the major metros.

How quickly does the list of top hiring companies change?

It shifts more than most candidates expect. A company can move from quiet to high posting activity within two to three weeks when a new project approval or headcount budget releases. The knok job index is updated nightly, so yesterday's data is more reliable than a monthly report. Use this list as directional guidance, and verify on the company's careers page before investing time in applications.

Which skills give candidates the best advantage in the current market?

AI and ML engineering, cloud architecture (AWS, Azure, GCP), and cybersecurity are commonly cited in industry surveys as the hardest roles to fill right now. That shortage improves negotiating leverage for candidates with those skills. LLM fine-tuning and data engineering specialisations are similarly in demand across both IT services and product companies in this snapshot.
