01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

ABB is a global technology leader in electrification, motion, process automation, and robotics, with a significant footprint in India. For Indian engineers and technology professionals, ABB India is a recognized employer known for technically demanding work, long-term career stability, and exposure to large industrial and infrastructure projects. Hiring happens across key locations including Bangalore, Vadodara, Chennai, and Pune. ABB recruits through both direct applications on its careers portal and campus placements at premier engineering institutions. Whether you are a fresher or a mid-career professional, the company runs structured recruitment cycles throughout the year.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

ABB's hiring process typically runs across four to five stages, though the exact flow can vary by role and location. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Application and Resume Screening
The first step is submitting your application through ABB's careers portal or via a job board. The HR team reviews resumes against role requirements. Tailoring your resume to highlight relevant technical skills and project experience matters at this stage.

Online Assessment or Aptitude Test
For many engineering and technical roles, shortlisted candidates are invited to complete an online test. This commonly covers aptitude (numerical and logical reasoning), domain knowledge, and sometimes a coding section for software roles. Prepare for time-pressured questions.

Technical Interview (Round 1)
A panel or individual technical interviewer will go deep into your domain knowledge. For electrical and automation roles, expect questions on power systems, PLCs, SCADA, drives, or related areas. For software roles, expect questions on data structures, coding, and system design. Interviewers often ask you to walk through past projects in detail.

Managerial or Cross-functional Interview
A second technical or managerial round focuses on how you approach problems, how you have handled real project situations, and how you work in teams. Behavioral questions in the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) are common here.

HR Interview and Offer
The final round covers compensation expectations, notice period, location preferences, and cultural fit. ABB HR typically moves to a formal offer letter once this round clears internal approvals.

The full process from application to offer can span a few weeks, especially for senior roles that require multiple stakeholder sign-offs.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

ABB places strong emphasis on engineering fundamentals. For core technical roles, a solid grip on your domain, whether that is power electronics, control systems, industrial automation, or software development, matters more than certifications alone. Beyond technical knowledge, interviewers consistently look for:

Problem-solving ability. Can you break down an unfamiliar industrial or software problem and work toward a structured solution? ABB's work involves complex real-world systems where textbook answers rarely fit neatly.

Project ownership. Candidates who can speak in detail about a project they led or contributed to, including what went wrong and how they fixed it, stand out. ABB values engineers who take accountability.

Communication and stakeholder management. Many roles involve coordinating with clients, vendors, or cross-functional teams. Being able to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical people is a visible differentiator.

Safety and quality mindset. ABB operates in industrial environments where safety standards are non-negotiable. Showing that you understand why processes and compliance matter, not just what they are, signals maturity.

Adaptability. With ABB's business spanning multiple technology domains and geographies, interviewers appreciate candidates who have stretched outside their comfort zone, whether by learning a new tool, entering a new industry vertical, or relocating for a role.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research ABB's business lines. ABB has four main divisions. Understanding which one your target role falls under, and what products or services that division delivers, lets you frame your experience in directly relevant terms. Check ABB India's official website for recent project announcements and press releases.

Brush up on domain fundamentals. For electrical and automation roles, revisit power systems basics, motor drives, protection relays, and PLC or SCADA concepts. For software and digital roles, refresh data structures, system design, and any relevant industry protocols mentioned in the job description.

Prepare STAR-format answers. Behavioral questions are a consistent part of ABB interviews. Write out three to five strong examples from your work history covering leadership, handling a technical failure, managing a difficult stakeholder, and delivering under pressure. Practice saying them aloud so they come out naturally.

Know your resume cold. Every project, tool, and technology you have listed is fair game. If you mention a particular software platform or controller, be ready to explain what you actually did with it, not just that you 'have experience' with it.

Research salary before the HR round. Check Glassdoor or levels.fyi for ABB India compensation data so you can give a well-reasoned number. Knowing the market range helps you negotiate confidently rather than guessing.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

ABB publicly anchors its culture around four values: courage, care, curiosity, and collaboration. In practice, employees describe an environment that is process-oriented and technically rigorous, with a strong emphasis on following safety standards and quality protocols.

Structured but not rigid. ABB runs mature engineering processes, which means there are established ways of doing things. New joiners often appreciate the clear documentation and mentorship available. Those who prefer a pure startup-style improvisation environment may find the structure a bit formal at first.

Long-term career paths. ABB is known for internal mobility. Professionals who perform well often move across divisions, geographies, or into leadership tracks over time. The company invests in training, and many senior employees have grown within the organization over a decade or more.

Diversity and inclusion focus. ABB India has publicly committed to increasing women's representation in engineering roles and runs targeted programs for early-career women in STEM.

Sustainability as a real priority. ABB's core business covers electrification and efficiency technology, which aligns directly with global decarbonization goals. Employees working on products like EV chargers, energy-efficient drives, or smart grid solutions are doing work with a visible climate impact. For engineers who care about that connection, it adds genuine meaning to daily work.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How long does ABB's hiring process usually take?

The timeline varies by role and seniority. For individual contributor roles, candidates commonly report receiving an offer within a few weeks of applying. Senior or specialist roles can take longer due to additional interview rounds and internal approvals. Following up politely with HR after each stage is completely acceptable and often speeds things along.

Does ABB hire freshers straight from college?

Yes. ABB India participates in campus placement drives at engineering colleges including IITs, NITs, and other reputed institutions. Off-campus freshers can also apply directly through ABB's careers portal. Freshers are typically evaluated on aptitude tests, core subject knowledge, and communication ability rather than prior work experience.

What is the salary range at ABB India?

Publicly reported and Glassdoor data for ABB India suggests compensation varies widely by role, level, and location. Engineering and software roles at entry level are commonly cited in industry surveys as competitive with other large MNCs in the same domain. Check Glassdoor or levels.fyi for ABB India specifically before your HR round to get the most current picture and negotiate with confidence.

Is a GATE score useful for getting into ABB?

GATE is not a formal requirement for ABB's direct hiring process, unlike public sector units. However, a strong GATE score signals solid domain fundamentals, which ABB interviewers do value. Some campus placement processes at partner institutions may use GATE-adjacent shortlisting criteria, so strong preparation for it is never wasted.

How important is it to know ABB's products before the interview?

You do not need to memorize product catalogs, but knowing which ABB division you are interviewing for and being able to name one or two key product lines shows genuine interest. Interviewers notice when a candidate has done basic research, and it signals motivation rather than spray-and-pray applying. Five minutes on ABB India's website before the call goes a long way.

Can I apply to multiple roles at ABB at the same time?

Yes, applying to more than one relevant role at the same time is generally fine and quite common. Make sure each application is tailored to the specific job description rather than sending one generic resume everywhere. If you are called for interviews for two roles simultaneously, let the HR team know, as ABB may be able to coordinate scheduling or even combine certain stages.
