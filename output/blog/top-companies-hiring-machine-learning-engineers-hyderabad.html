04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Hyderabad had 27 active Machine Learning Engineer openings as of early July 2026, according to knok jobradar. That places it third among major Indian tech hubs, behind Bangalore (165 openings) and Delhi (50), but well ahead of Mumbai (15), Pune (14), and Chennai (14).

The city's MLE market is a genuine three-way mix: Global Capability Centres (GCCs) set up by multinationals, mid-size product companies and EdTech firms, and early-stage startups. Each bucket offers a different kind of work, a different interview style, and a different growth path. Knowing which environment suits you lets you focus your search rather than spray applications everywhere.

08 Salary Range

Salary Range

The data snapshot for this role does not include salary figures, so treat what follows as directional context from publicly reported sources only. Glassdoor and levels.fyi commonly cite a wide compensation range for Machine Learning Engineers in Hyderabad, with the spread depending on years of experience, ML specialisation, and company type.

GCC vs startup pay structures: Industry surveys and publicly reported data suggest GCCs like Amgen and Warner Bros Discovery tend to offer stronger fixed pay and better structured benefits. Startups like aquaexchange or perceptyne may offer a lower base CTC but include ESOPs that could add meaningful value if the company grows. Always ask for a full breakdown: base, variable, joining bonus, ESOPs, and medical cover.

Specialisation premium: Publicly reported salary data from levels.fyi commonly shows higher compensation for engineers with hands-on MLOps, LLM fine-tuning, or production-grade model deployment experience versus those with only research or academic project exposure. Naming your specific stack clearly in your resume and interviews makes a real difference at offer time.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

Hyderabad's MLE job market is smaller than Bangalore's but also less crowded with applicants. That is an advantage if you position yourself well.

Match your profile to the company type: For GCC roles at Warner Bros Discovery or Amgen, highlight experience with large-scale systems, cross-functional collaboration, and any prior global team exposure. For startups like aquaexchange or perceptyne, focus on your ability to work with messy real-world data, ship quickly, and own end-to-end ML workflows. For Infosys, structured delivery and client communication experience help.

Make your production experience visible: Many MLE candidates list projects and research papers but bury the one thing hiring managers care most about: did you actually deploy a model that people used? If yes, say so clearly in your resume summary, mention the scale, and describe the business impact in plain terms.

Pick a credible specialisation: Being a generalist is fine for junior roles, but once you have a few years in, narrowing your pitch to NLP, computer vision, recommendation systems, or MLOps makes you far easier to shortlist. Hyderabad's employer mix points to demand for MLOps (GCCs running large pipelines), pharma-domain ML (Amgen), and agri-tech data work (aquaexchange).

Keep your GitHub active: Several Hyderabad ML teams, especially at product companies and startups, look at GitHub before the first call. Even one clean, well-documented project with a working demo is worth more than a list of bullet points on a resume.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

With only 27 openings active at any given time in Hyderabad, you cannot afford to miss a posting or apply late. Many MLE roles in this city fill within a couple of weeks of going live.

Apply in the first week: The further you are from the posting date, the more filtered the pile gets. Set up job alerts on LinkedIn and Naukri for 'Machine Learning Engineer Hyderabad' and check daily. If you spot a role from any company in the table above, treat it as time-sensitive.

Customise your resume per company type: A single generic resume will underperform. Keep two versions: one that leads with scale and system reliability (for GCCs), and one that leads with ownership and end-to-end delivery (for startups). Swap in the most relevant projects depending on the role.

Do not skip the cover note or LinkedIn message: For smaller companies like perceptyne or Ritually, a short, specific message to the hiring manager or founder explaining why you are interested in their particular problem can separate you from applicants who just clicked 'Apply'. Keep it under five sentences and reference the actual product or domain.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Which company is hiring the most Machine Learning Engineers in Hyderabad right now?

Warner Bros Discovery leads this snapshot with 5 open MLE roles, more than any other single employer in Hyderabad. The company runs a GCC in the city focused on media and streaming technology, so roles here typically involve large-scale recommendation and content systems. This makes it a strong target if you have experience with data pipelines, model serving, or personalisation at scale.

Is Hyderabad a good city for Machine Learning Engineer jobs compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has significantly more MLE openings (165 vs 27 in this snapshot), so raw volume is much higher there. Hyderabad does have a genuine mix of GCC, product, and startup roles, and the applicant competition is lower than in Bangalore. If you prefer to avoid Bangalore's cost of living or are already based in Hyderabad, the market is real and the employer variety is worth exploring.

What types of companies are hiring MLEs in Hyderabad?

This snapshot shows three types: GCCs (Warner Bros Discovery, Amgen, Cornerstone), which are India offices of multinational companies building global tech; product and EdTech companies (ZeMoSo, Ansrsource); and early-stage startups (aquaexchange, perceptyne, Ritually). Each type offers a very different work culture, ownership level, and career trajectory. Knowing which type suits you lets you tailor your resume and outreach rather than applying the same way everywhere.

Do Hyderabad MLE roles require specific ML skills or frameworks?

Job descriptions across Hyderabad MLE roles commonly ask for Python, TensorFlow or PyTorch, and some MLOps exposure such as MLflow, Kubeflow, or cloud ML services. GCC roles at Amgen or Warner Bros Discovery often add requirements around large-scale data pipelines and model monitoring. Startup roles like at aquaexchange may involve domain-specific data (IoT sensor readings, for example) on top of standard ML tooling, so adaptability to unusual datasets matters there.

How should I approach a GCC MLE role differently from a startup MLE role?

For GCC roles, emphasise reliability, process, and cross-team collaboration. These organisations care about how your model fits into a larger system, not just whether it works in a notebook. For startups, lead with speed, ownership, and real-world impact. Mention projects you shipped, not just built, and be ready to talk about what broke and how you fixed it. Adjust your resume summary and your interview stories to reflect which environment you are targeting.

Are there MLE jobs in Hyderabad for freshers or early-career candidates?

Most MLE roles in Hyderabad, particularly at GCCs like Warner Bros Discovery or Amgen, expect prior production experience or a strong portfolio of deployed projects. Infosys, being an IT services firm, is more open to fresher or early-career candidates and often has structured training programmes. Startups vary: some will take a sharp fresher with strong fundamentals and solid GitHub projects, while others want at least a year or two of hands-on experience before interviewing.
