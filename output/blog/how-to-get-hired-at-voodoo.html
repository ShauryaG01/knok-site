01 Hiring Overview

Voodoo is one of the most recognizable names in mobile gaming, best known for hypercasual and hybrid-casual titles that reach players across the globe. 03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The hiring process at Voodoo typically runs through several stages, though exact steps vary by team and seniority. Here is what candidates commonly describe:

Application and resume screen. Recruiters look at your portfolio, past projects, and any shipped titles. A strong note explaining why mobile gaming excites you helps you stand out in the initial review.

Recruiter call. A video or phone conversation covering your background, motivation for joining Voodoo, and basic role-fit questions. Expect this to be conversational rather than technical.

Skills or take-home assessment. Depending on the role, you may receive a case study, a data problem, or a design brief. For game roles, you might be asked to analyze a game concept or prototype a mechanic. For data and product roles, expect questions around retention and monetization logic.

Team interview. Video calls with the hiring manager and team members. These go deeper into your past work, how you made decisions, and how you handle failure. Concrete examples from past projects carry more weight than theoretical answers.

Final round or culture fit. Some candidates report a final conversation with a senior leader or cross-functional stakeholder, especially for senior roles.

Timelines vary. Some candidates move through all stages in a few weeks; others report longer gaps between rounds. Following up politely after each stage is reasonable.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Voodoo values people who are comfortable making decisions from data, moving fast, and iterating without waiting for perfect information. Across roles, a few traits show up consistently in publicly available candidate feedback:

Data fluency. Even non-technical roles benefit from comfort with metrics. Knowing how to read retention curves, understand monetization logic, and connect player behavior to business outcomes will serve you in interviews and on the job.

Bias for action. Voodoo ships frequently. Candidates who can describe rapid test-and-learn cycles, where they shipped something imperfect, learned from it, and improved quickly, tend to resonate with interviewers.

Mobile-first thinking. A genuine interest in mobile games and apps matters. Being able to discuss what makes a game loop satisfying or why a particular title succeeded is a real differentiator.

Ownership mindset. Teams at Voodoo are lean and move fast. Interviewers look for people who take responsibility end-to-end, not those who pass problems to other departments.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Study their portfolio before your first call. Download and play several Voodoo titles. Think about what makes the core loop engaging, where ads appear, and how the difficulty curve is tuned. You do not need to be a hardcore gamer, but showing genuine curiosity about their products matters.

Learn the mobile analytics vocabulary. Retention metrics like day-one and day-seven performance, ARPU, LTV, and CPI come up frequently in interviews for product, data, and growth roles. Being fluent in this language signals you can contribute immediately.

Prepare structured examples. Use a simple situation-action-result format for behavioral questions. Voodoo interviewers want specifics, so vague stories about 'leading a project' will fall flat. Name the game, describe the problem, explain your decision, and share the outcome.

Brush up on the competitive landscape. Know who Voodoo's main competitors are in hypercasual and hybrid-casual. Be ready to discuss market trends from 2024 onward and how mobile gaming monetization has shifted.

Tailor your portfolio if you are in a creative or design role. Show work that is mobile-native. PC or console portfolio pieces are fine to include, but lead with anything relevant to short-session, touch-first experiences.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Voodoo describes itself as entrepreneurial and data-driven, and candidate accounts broadly support this. Teams are lean by design, which means individuals carry significant responsibility early. There is relatively little bureaucracy compared to larger studios, and decisions tend to be made with speed over consensus.

The Paris headquarters sets a certain cultural tone, and the company has a strong international presence with teams and hires across Europe and beyond. English is the working language for most roles.

Pace is genuinely fast. Products can go from concept to live test in a matter of days. If you prefer long planning cycles and stable roadmaps, Voodoo's culture may feel uncomfortable. If you thrive on rapid feedback and enjoy seeing your work live quickly, it is likely a strong fit.

Work-life balance feedback is mixed, as it often is at high-growth gaming companies. Checking recent employee reviews on Glassdoor before your final decision is a good idea. Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Voodoo hire from India?

Voodoo is a global company and does consider international candidates, particularly for roles in engineering, data science, and product. Most roles are based in Paris or are remote with a European time-zone preference. Check each job listing carefully for location requirements before applying, and raise the question explicitly during your recruiter call.

How many open roles does Voodoo have right now?

As of the latest data tracked by knok, Voodoo has 34 open roles across game design, engineering, product, growth, and data. The mix changes frequently as the company ships new titles and tests new ideas, so checking their careers page regularly is worthwhile.

What kind of experience do I need to get into Voodoo?

It depends on the team. For game design and product roles, shipped titles or strong portfolio projects carry more weight than years of experience alone. For data and engineering roles, a solid grasp of mobile analytics and fast-paced development environments matters. Entry-level roles do exist, though they are competitive.

Is the Voodoo interview process technical?

For engineering and data roles, yes, expect technical assessments. For game design, product, and growth roles, the focus shifts to portfolio reviews, case studies, and behavioral interviews. Most candidates report that the process is conversational overall, with the technical depth proportional to the role.

How long does it take to hear back after applying?

Timelines vary widely. Some candidates report hearing back within a few days of applying; others wait several weeks. If you have not heard back after a reasonable wait, a polite follow-up email to the recruiter is entirely appropriate and often effective.

Does Voodoo offer remote work for candidates outside France?

Some roles at Voodoo are remote or hybrid, but many are Paris-based with an expectation of regular in-office presence. The job listing is the most reliable source for remote eligibility. For Indian candidates applying from India, ask about the working arrangement in detail during your first recruiter call so there are no surprises later.
