01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Mirvac is one of Australia's leading integrated real estate groups, operating across residential development, commercial office towers, retail centres, and industrial assets. The company manages the full property lifecycle, from land acquisition and design through to construction, leasing, and long-term asset management.

As of July 2026, Mirvac had 32 open roles listed across its business lines. These cover functions such as development and construction, property management, finance, technology, human resources, and sustainability. Whether you are an experienced real estate professional or a corporate specialist moving into the sector, there is a good chance one of those teams fits your background.

For Indian professionals, Mirvac is a strong benchmark employer in the Australian property sector. If you are already based in Australia on a work visa, or exploring international real estate pathways, understanding how Mirvac hires gives you a practical edge in a competitive market.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Mirvac's hiring process typically runs through four to five stages, though the exact steps vary by role and seniority level. Here is what candidates commonly report:

Stage 1: Online application. You submit your resume and a cover letter through Mirvac's careers portal. The team screens for relevant experience and qualifications. A cover letter that references specific Mirvac projects or their sustainability commitments tends to stand out from generic applications.

Stage 2: Recruiter screen. If shortlisted, an internal recruiter will reach out for a short phone or video call. This covers your background, notice period, visa status if relevant, and salary expectations. Be ready to explain clearly why Mirvac appeals to you specifically.

Stage 3: First-round interview. This is usually a video or in-person interview with the hiring manager, sometimes alongside a small panel. Expect competency-based questions using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Role-specific technical questions are common here, particularly for finance, project management, or engineering positions.

Stage 4: Second or final-round interview. For mid-to-senior roles, there is often a second interview with a more senior leader or a broader panel. This round focuses on cultural fit, strategic thinking, and how you approach complex problems. Some roles include a short written task or a presentation.

Stage 5: Reference and background checks. Mirvac typically requests two to three professional references and runs standard background verification before a formal offer is made.

With 32 open roles currently active, Mirvac's hiring pipeline is busy. Candidates commonly report hearing back within one to two weeks after each stage, though senior roles can take longer to progress.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Mirvac looks for people who combine strong functional skills with a genuine interest in how built environments shape communities. A few traits come up consistently in candidate feedback and job descriptions:

Domain relevance. Whether you come from finance, construction, leasing, or technology, Mirvac wants to see real estate or infrastructure context in your experience. If your background is from a different sector, draw clear lines between what you have done and how it applies to property.

Sustainability awareness. Mirvac has publicly committed to ambitious environmental targets under their 'This Changes Everything' strategy. Candidates who understand ESG principles, green building standards, or carbon reduction in a business context have a clear advantage over those who cannot speak to these topics.

Collaboration and stakeholder management. Real estate projects involve architects, contractors, tenants, investors, and government bodies working across long timeframes. Mirvac values people who communicate well across these groups without losing clarity or accountability.

Attention to detail in delivery. Whether managing a construction timeline or a leasing negotiation, Mirvac roles often involve decisions where accuracy matters significantly. Evidence of rigorous, process-oriented work strengthens any application.

Long-term thinking. As a company that holds assets for decades, Mirvac tends to hire people with a patient, quality-first mindset rather than a short-term transactional approach.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Good preparation for a Mirvac interview goes well beyond reading the job description. Here is a practical approach:

Research their portfolio. Mirvac's residential communities, commercial towers, and retail assets are publicly documented. Knowing two or three specific projects and being able to say clearly what impressed you about them signals genuine interest and initiative to the interview panel.

Understand their strategy. Mirvac's sustainability commitments are central to how the company presents itself. Referencing this naturally in conversation, rather than as a rehearsed line, makes a strong impression on interviewers who live this strategy daily.

Prepare STAR examples. Competency-based interviews are standard at Mirvac. Prepare four to five strong examples from your career covering: delivering a complex project, managing a difficult stakeholder, adapting to unexpected change, and showing initiative without being asked. Each example should be specific, with a clear outcome.

Be ready to discuss salary. Australian property roles are competitive, and Mirvac recruiters will likely ask about your expectations early. Research market rates through sources like Glassdoor or industry salary surveys before your recruiter call, so you can give a confident and informed range.

Tailor your resume. If the job description mentions specific tools, certifications, or methodologies relevant to the role, make sure your resume reflects your experience with these clearly, placed near the top of each relevant entry.

Prepare thoughtful questions. Asking about team structure, current project priorities, or how success is measured in the role shows you are evaluating the opportunity seriously, not just hoping to land any job.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Mirvac's culture sits at the intersection of professional rigour and a clear commitment to purpose. The company describes itself as being in the business of reimagining urban life, and that aspiration shapes how teams work together day to day.

Sustainability is not a side initiative. It is embedded in decision-making from design through to long-term asset management. Colleagues who take ESG outcomes seriously as a core part of their work, rather than treating it as a compliance checkbox, tend to thrive here.

Collaboration is the default mode. Mirvac's projects span multiple disciplines over long timeframes. The culture rewards people who communicate openly, share credit, and keep broader project goals in view even when under pressure.

Performance is taken seriously. Mirvac is a publicly listed company with investor accountability. There is a results-oriented culture underneath the values-led messaging. Coming prepared to discuss concrete outcomes, not just activities, will resonate with interviewers at every level.

Diversity and inclusion are actively pursued. Mirvac has publicly reported on its diversity targets and received external recognition for inclusion programs. This is a genuine part of the workplace culture, particularly at leadership levels.

For Indian professionals, Mirvac's multicultural workforce in cities like Sydney and Melbourne means you are unlikely to stand out for the wrong reasons. The company's focus on long-term community outcomes also resonates with values many Indian professionals bring from careers in engineering, finance, and urban development.

Frequently asked

How many roles is Mirvac currently hiring for?

As of July 2026, Mirvac had 32 open roles across its business units. These span development, property management, finance, technology, and corporate functions. The number shifts regularly as projects are launched and teams grow, so tracking openings consistently gives you the best chance of applying at the right moment.

Does Mirvac sponsor work visas for international candidates?

Mirvac does not make blanket commitments to visa sponsorship, and individual job postings usually state whether sponsorship is available for that specific role. If you are already on a working visa in Australia, that removes one barrier from the process entirely. It is worth asking the recruiter directly during the initial phone screen, so you do not invest significant time in a process that cannot move forward.

What kinds of roles does Mirvac typically hire for?

Mirvac hires across residential and commercial development, property and asset management, leasing and sales, finance and accounting, technology and digital transformation, sustainability, and corporate support functions including HR and legal. How long does the Mirvac hiring process usually take?

Candidates commonly report a process of four to six weeks from application to offer, though this varies by role seniority and how quickly the interview panel can align on schedules. Senior or specialist roles can take longer if multiple decision-makers are involved in the final round. Staying in touch with the recruiter after each stage is a reasonable way to stay informed without coming across as impatient.

Is prior real estate experience required to get a job at Mirvac?

For technical and operational roles such as development, construction, asset management, and leasing, relevant domain experience is generally expected by the hiring team. For corporate and enabling functions like technology, finance, HR, and legal, Mirvac hires from a broader range of industries. The key is showing clearly in your application how your specific background applies to the property and infrastructure context.

How important is sustainability knowledge when applying to Mirvac?

It is increasingly important across all roles, not just specialist sustainability positions. Mirvac has publicly committed to net zero targets and other environmental goals, and they look for candidates who genuinely understand and support that direction. You do not need to be a sustainability expert, but showing real awareness of ESG issues in a business context will strengthen almost any application you submit.
