01 Overview

Overview

Guesty is a property management platform (PMS) built for short-term rental operators, vacation rental managers, and hospitality businesses. The platform connects to major OTAs like Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com through a unified channel manager, and packs in tools like a unified inbox, automation workflows, and revenue management. As of early July 2026, Guesty has 19 open roles across product and tech, pointing to active hiring at multiple seniority levels.

For PM candidates, the process typically spans product sense, execution, analytical thinking, and behavioral rounds. Candidates report a take-home case or product design exercise, followed by live rounds with PMs, cross-functional partners, and leadership. Because Guesty is a B2B SaaS product with deep OTA integrations, interviewers pay close attention to how well you understand complex customer workflows, multi-sided marketplace dynamics, and API-heavy product decisions.

02 Most Asked Questions

Most Asked Questions

Guesty interviews combine product design, prioritization, and domain knowledge about the short-term rental market. These are the questions candidates most commonly report:

  1. How would you prioritize the roadmap for Guesty's channel manager when property managers and OTA partners have conflicting needs?
  2. Design a dashboard for a property manager running a large portfolio of listings across multiple OTAs. What metrics do you surface and why?
  3. A major OTA partner changes its API without notice. Walk me through how you handle this as a PM.
  4. How would you measure the success of Guesty's unified inbox feature?
  5. How would you improve the onboarding experience for a new property manager joining Guesty for the first time?
  6. Guesty wants to enter a new market segment, say boutique hotels. How do you decide whether to build, buy, or partner?
  7. How would you handle a situation where sales is promising customers features that are not on your current roadmap?
  8. Walk me through how you would approach building a dynamic pricing or revenue management feature for short-term rental operators.
  9. Describe a time you shipped a product that required heavy coordination across engineering, design, and third-party integrations.
  10. How would you define and track the North Star metric for Guesty's automation suite?
  11. How do you balance the needs of independent hosts against large property management companies on the same platform?
  12. If Guesty sees rising churn among mid-market customers, how do you diagnose the root cause and respond?

03 Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Sample Answers (STAR Format)

Q: How would you prioritize Guesty's channel manager roadmap when property managers and OTA partners have conflicting requests?

*Situation:* At my previous company, I managed a SaaS integration product that connected to multiple third-party platforms, each with its own API constraints and release schedules.

*Task:* I had to decide which integration features to prioritize when our enterprise clients and our API partners were pushing in opposite directions.

*Action:* I built a prioritization matrix scoring requests by revenue impact, retention risk, and integration stability. I ran structured interviews with a sample of property manager clients to understand which OTA-related pain points caused the most direct booking loss. I then shared the findings jointly with the sales team and the partnerships team, so both sides could see the customer evidence behind each decision.

*Result:* We shipped a phased roadmap that tackled the highest-impact OTA integration gaps first. Support tickets related to sync failures dropped in the following quarter, and stakeholder alignment improved because the decision rested on customer data, not internal opinion.

---

Q: How would you measure the success of Guesty's unified inbox feature?

*Situation:* At a prior role, I owned a multi-channel messaging feature for a marketplace product, and the team had been tracking vanity metrics that masked real adoption problems.

*Task:* I needed to redefine success metrics so we could catch issues early and tie feature performance to business outcomes.

*Action:* I separated vanity metrics like messages sent from outcome metrics: response time, booking conversion from a guest conversation, and host satisfaction scores. I set up a weekly review with the customer success team to layer in qualitative signals, and I pushed engineering to instrument time-to-first-response as a core health metric.

*Result:* Within two quarters we had a clear leading indicator that predicted host retention. When the metric dipped, we could investigate quickly. The framework was later adopted by two other feature teams in the org.

---

Q: Describe a time you handled a cross-functional conflict where sales had committed to a feature you had not planned.

*Situation:* A regional sales lead had promised a large enterprise client a custom reporting module within a tight deadline, without looping in the product team.

*Task:* I had to balance the risk of losing the client against the engineering bandwidth already committed to a core roadmap item.

*Action:* I first met with the sales lead to understand the client's underlying need, not just the stated feature request. It turned out the client needed faster visibility into occupancy data, not a fully custom report. I proposed a lighter-weight addition to our existing analytics dashboard that the team could ship quickly, and got sign-off from the VP of Sales on the revised proposal.

*Result:* The client accepted the revised solution, which we shipped on schedule. We also created a lightweight intake process so sales could flag high-priority client asks before making commitments, which reduced similar surprises in later quarters.

04 Answer Frameworks

Answer Frameworks

Prioritization: RICE or a weighted matrix

Guesty deals with many customer segments, from solo hosts to enterprise PMCs, and each has competing demands. When asked to prioritize, use Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort scoring, or a simpler weighted matrix. Always anchor your framework to a North Star metric first, then score features against it. Saying 'it depends' without a structure will not land well.

Product design: CIRCLES or your own structured version

For 'design a feature' questions, walk through: Customers (who are they), Identify needs, Revenue or metric impact, Cut scope based on constraints, List solutions, Evaluate trade-offs, Summarize. For Guesty specifically, always distinguish between the host (property manager) and the guest as two separate users with different jobs to be done.

Metrics: North Star plus guardrails

Guesty's business health ties to listings managed, bookings processed, and automation adoption. When you define success for any feature, propose a North Star metric, one or two supporting metrics, and at least one guardrail metric to prevent gaming. For example: 'North Star is revenue per managed listing. Supporting metric is OTA sync uptime. Guardrail is host support ticket volume.'

Behavioral: STAR

Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep Situation and Task brief, spend most of your time on Action (what you specifically did, not what the team did), and always quantify Result where you have real data.

05 What Interviewers Want

What Interviewers Want

Domain curiosity about the short-term rental market

Guesty sits in a niche but fast-growing vertical. Interviewers want to see that you have explored the product, understand the OTA ecosystem, and appreciate the pain points of a property manager juggling multiple platforms. Candidates who treat it as a generic SaaS PM interview miss this signal.

Comfort with API-heavy, integration-driven products

Guesty's core value is syncing inventory, pricing, and messages across OTAs in real time. You do not need to be an engineer, but you should be able to reason about integration trade-offs, understand why third-party dependencies create risk, and think about reliability and uptime as product concerns, not just engineering concerns.

Customer empathy grounded in evidence, not assumptions

Property managers have real operational constraints: managing cleaners, handling late check-ins, responding to guest messages across platforms, and keeping OTA calendars in sync. Show that you talk to customers, synthesize feedback systematically, and prioritize based on observed pain rather than internal opinion.

Clear, structured communication

Guesty PMs typically work across engineering, design, sales, customer success, and external partners. Interviewers watch for how clearly you structure a problem, whether you listen and adjust, and whether your trade-off reasoning is explicit. Vague answers like 'I would collaborate with stakeholders' without specifics do not score well.

06 Preparation Plan

Preparation Plan

Week 1: Know the product and market

Sign up for a Guesty trial or watch demo videos to understand the channel manager, unified inbox, automation rules, and reporting features. Read about the short-term rental market and the role OTAs play. Understand how a property manager's day-to-day workflow maps to Guesty's feature set.

Week 2: Practice product sense and metrics

Pick a few Guesty features and practice defining their North Star metric, supporting metrics, and guardrails. Work through one 'design a feature' question per day using a structured framework. Record yourself or practice with a peer to tighten your answers.

Week 3: Behavioral prep and mock interviews

Write out several STAR stories covering prioritization, stakeholder conflict, shipping under constraints, using data to make a decision, and handling a failure. Tailor each story to connect naturally to B2B SaaS or integration product contexts. Do at least a couple of mock interviews with someone who can give honest feedback.

07 Common Mistakes

Common Mistakes

Going generic on Guesty-specific questions

The most common mistake candidates make is answering Guesty questions as if they were interviewing at a generic SaaS company. When asked about the channel manager or unified inbox, interviewers want you to demonstrate that you understand what a property manager actually does and why OTA sync failures are painful. Saying 'I would talk to users and iterate' without showing domain awareness reads as unprepared.

Skipping the 'why' behind your prioritization

Candidates often list features they would build without connecting the choice to a business metric or customer outcome. At Guesty, every prioritization decision should map to something measurable: occupancy rates, booking conversion, host retention, or automation adoption. Show your logic, not just your conclusion.

Ignoring the B2B sales dynamic

Guesty sells to property management companies, not to individual consumers. That means sales cycles, enterprise contracts, and customer success relationships all matter. If you answer stakeholder questions as if the only stakeholders are users and engineers, you miss the commercial reality of the role.

Overcomplicating the take-home

Candidates report receiving a product case or design exercise as part of the process. A common mistake is submitting an exhaustive deck when a sharp, focused narrative would be more effective. Interviewers want clarity and judgment, not coverage of every edge case.

Underselling your impact in STAR answers

Many candidates describe what their team did rather than what they specifically contributed. In STAR answers, use 'I' deliberately. Explain your role, your decision, your trade-off, and your result. If you led a team effort, state exactly what you owned.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many rounds does the Guesty PM interview typically have?

Candidates report that the process typically runs across several stages, including a recruiter screen, a take-home product case or design exercise, and multiple live rounds covering product sense, metrics, and behavioral questions. Some candidates also report a final round with senior leadership. The exact structure can vary by level and team, so it is worth asking the recruiter to outline the process upfront.

Does Guesty give a take-home assignment for PM roles?

Candidates commonly report receiving a take-home exercise, typically a product design or prioritization case related to the short-term rental space. The assignment is usually scoped to a few hours of work. Focus on clear structure, well-reasoned trade-offs, and a specific recommendation rather than trying to cover every possible angle.

Do I need experience in hospitality or proptech to get a PM role at Guesty?

Not necessarily, but genuine curiosity about the domain matters a great deal. Candidates who have taken time to understand Guesty's product, the OTA ecosystem, and the workflows of a property manager tend to stand out over those with hospitality experience but no product intuition. If you lack domain background, invest a week learning the market before your interview rounds begin.

What salary can I expect as a PM at Guesty in India?

Exact Guesty-specific figures are not publicly confirmed, but based on knok jobradar data and industry surveys, PM roles in India typically fall in the 24-40 LPA range for mid-level candidates with 3-6 years of experience, and 40-60 LPA for senior roles. Associate PM roles typically start in the 12-20 LPA range. Actual offers depend on your experience, the specific team, and how well you negotiate.

How important is technical knowledge for a PM role at Guesty?

You do not need to write code, but you should be comfortable reasoning about APIs, third-party integrations, and the trade-offs that come with real-time data sync. Guesty's product relies heavily on OTA API integrations, so understanding concepts like webhooks, sync reliability, and rate limits at a conceptual level will help you in product design and metrics questions. Candidates who can speak to integration risk without being prompted tend to stand out.

Where are most PM roles based in India, and does Guesty hire remotely?

Based on knok jobradar data from July 2026, PM roles across companies in this space are concentrated in Bangalore (271 openings) and Delhi (177 openings), with smaller numbers in Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai. Guesty specifically has 19 open roles as of that data pull. Many roles in this space offer hybrid or remote options, so it is worth confirming the exact work model with the recruiter during your first call.
