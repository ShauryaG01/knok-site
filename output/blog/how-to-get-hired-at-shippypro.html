01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Shippypro is a multi-carrier shipping platform that helps e-commerce businesses automate how they manage couriers, track shipments, and handle returns. The product sits at the intersection of logistics software and marketplace integrations, which means the team tends to hire people who are comfortable working in a fast-moving, product-led environment.

As of July 2026, Shippypro has 10 open roles. That is a healthy pipeline for a focused SaaS company and suggests they are in a deliberate growth phase rather than a hiring freeze or a chaotic scale-up. The openings span engineering, sales, and operations, so there is room for both technical and non-technical profiles.

Shippypro is headquartered in Italy but operates with a distributed, international team. Remote-friendly roles are common, and the hiring process tends to move at a startup pace, meaning you can expect faster decisions compared to large Indian IT firms.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The exact number of rounds varies by role, but based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process at Shippypro generally follows a pattern like this.

Application and initial screening. After you apply, a recruiter or HR contact typically reaches out for a short call. This is a culture and motivation check. They want to know why you are interested in Shippypro specifically, not just in any SaaS job. Prepare a clear answer about why the logistics-tech space appeals to you.

Hiring manager or team lead interview. This round goes deeper into your background. Expect questions about past projects, how you handled specific challenges, and your familiarity with the domain. For engineering roles, this is often where a take-home task or live coding question is introduced. For sales and operations roles, the conversation usually focuses on how you have handled customer situations or cross-functional work.

Technical or skills round. Depending on the role, this could be a coding exercise, a case study, a product walkthrough, or a written assignment. Shippypro tends to keep assessments practical and grounded in real scenarios related to shipping, e-commerce integrations, or customer workflows. This is not the place for abstract puzzles.

Final interview. A conversation with a senior leader or founder. This round typically covers longer-term thinking: where you want to grow, how you approach ambiguity, and whether your working style fits a small international team. Questions about Shippypro's product and competitors sometimes come up here, so spend time using the platform or reading their blog before this round.

Total rounds are commonly cited as three to four, and the full process from first call to offer can take two to four weeks.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Shippypro looks for a specific combination of skills and attitude, regardless of the function you are applying for.

Domain curiosity. The logistics and e-commerce space has its own vocabulary: couriers, manifests, tracking events, marketplace connectors, returns portals. You do not need years of industry experience, but candidates who have taken the time to understand how shipping software works tend to stand out. Even reading a few of Shippypro's help articles or watching a product demo puts you ahead of most applicants.

Hands-on problem solving. Shippypro is a product-led company, which means teams move fast and expect people to own their work end to end. Interviewers often look for evidence that you have dug into a hard problem yourself rather than waiting for someone else to define the solution.

Clear communication. Because the team is distributed and works across time zones, written and verbal communication skills matter a lot. Being able to explain a technical issue or a customer situation in plain, structured language is valued across all roles.

For engineering roles specifically, familiarity with API integrations, SaaS architecture, and shipping carrier APIs is a plus. Experience with PHP, Python, or JavaScript frameworks comes up frequently in job descriptions.

For sales and customer-facing roles, fluency in English is usually required, and experience with e-commerce merchants or logistics clients is a strong differentiator.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product before your first call. Sign up for a Shippypro trial or watch demo videos. Be ready to describe, in your own words, what the product does and who it serves. Interviewers notice when a candidate has actually engaged with the tool.

Prepare your 'why Shippypro' story. Generic answers like 'I want to work at a fast-growing SaaS company' do not land well. Connect your interest to something specific: the logistics problem they solve, the type of customers they serve, or a feature you found interesting.

For technical candidates, revisit integration patterns. Shippypro's core product connects e-commerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and Amazon with shipping carriers. If you can speak to how webhooks, REST APIs, or data transformation pipelines work in this context, you will be better positioned.

Practice structured answers for behavioural questions. Questions like 'tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult customer' or 'describe a project where things did not go as planned' are common at this stage of a company. Use a simple situation, action, result format and keep answers focused and concrete.

Prepare questions that show genuine interest. Ask about how the team handles incident response, what the onboarding experience looks like, or how product priorities are set. Avoid questions whose answers are already on the website.

Follow up professionally. A short thank-you note after each round is not mandatory, but it signals professionalism and genuine interest, which matters in a small-team environment.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Shippypro operates with the energy of a focused product company. The team is relatively small and international, which creates a working culture that is collaborative but also expects a high degree of individual ownership.

Ownership and autonomy. People at Shippypro are expected to take responsibility for their area without needing constant direction. If you are coming from a large Indian IT services firm where tasks are tightly scoped and escalation paths are clearly defined, this shift can feel significant. The reward is that your work has visible impact quickly.

Customer-first thinking. Shippypro's customers are e-commerce merchants who depend on shipping to run their business. There is a strong internal emphasis on understanding customer pain points and solving them directly. This shows up in how product decisions get made and how support and engineering teams interact with each other.

Remote and async work norms. The team works across geographies, so written communication is important. Meetings tend to be purposeful rather than frequent. Candidates who are comfortable working independently and communicating proactively through written channels tend to thrive.

Growth mindset. As a growing SaaS company, Shippypro expects employees to grow with the product. People who enjoy picking up new things, whether that is a new programming pattern, a new courier integration, or a new market, tend to fit well here.
