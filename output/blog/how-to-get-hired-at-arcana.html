01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Arcana has 19 open roles as of 8 July 2026, according to knok's job radar. The company builds Web3 infrastructure focused on data privacy and user-controlled access, giving developers tools so their users can own their own data. Most active hiring is in engineering and product, though growth and business development roles appear too.

Getting hired here is not just about matching a skills checklist. Because the team is lean, every new hire carries real responsibility from day one. Arcana wants people who can own problems end to end, move quickly, and genuinely care about what the company is building. Strong domain knowledge in blockchain, cryptography, or developer platforms is a clear advantage. 03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The exact stages vary by role and hiring manager, but the pattern candidates typically describe follows this broad sequence:

Application and resume screen. Your resume goes to a recruiter or a founding team member. At a company this size, a short note explaining why you want to work in this specific space can move your application higher up the pile than a generic submission.

Introductory call. A short conversation, usually with a recruiter or an early team member. Expect questions about your background, what you have built or shipped, and why you want to work in Web3 or at Arcana specifically. This round is as much about culture fit as it is about skills.

Technical assessment or take-home task. Engineering candidates commonly receive a coding problem or a system-design task tied to what the team is actually building. Product or growth candidates may receive a case study or a short written exercise. A well-considered submission matters far more than a rushed one.

Technical interview. A deeper conversation with one or two engineers or senior team members. Depending on the role, expect questions on distributed systems, cryptography, API design, or front-end or back-end fundamentals. If there was a take-home task, this round often digs into the choices you made and why.

Leadership or culture round. A conversation with a founder or a senior leader. They want to understand how you think, how you communicate under pressure, and whether your values align with what the company is building.

Offer stage. If the team is aligned, an offer typically follows within a week or two. Startups at this stage often have room to negotiate, so come prepared with publicly reported ranges from Glassdoor or levels.fyi as your reference before the call.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Arcana hires people who are genuinely interested in the problem the company is solving, not just looking for a job with a Web3 label on it. The following qualities show up consistently in how teams like this evaluate candidates:

First-principles thinking. Can you break down a problem from scratch without waiting for a playbook? Early-stage teams rarely have documented processes for every situation, so the ability to reason from basics is valued highly.

Builder mindset. Work you have shipped carries more weight than work you contributed to at the edges. Open-source projects, side products, or anything you launched and maintained independently are strong signals.

Strong written communication. At a team that likely operates across cities or time zones, your ability to write clearly and update people proactively is noticed from the first email you send. Vague or absent communication is a red flag at this stage.

Comfort with ambiguity. Roadmaps shift and priorities get reprioritised. People who need very structured environments sometimes find this kind of pace exhausting rather than energising.

Genuine domain curiosity. You do not need to be a blockchain expert to apply, but you should be curious enough to have explored the space on your own. Being able to explain what Arcana does and why you find it interesting is a baseline expectation before the first call.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A few focused steps will give you a noticeable edge over candidates who apply cold without doing their homework:

Use the product and read the documentation. Know what Arcana actually builds, not just what the website says at a high level. If there are public GitHub repositories or developer forums, spend time there. Walk into your interview able to describe one specific thing you found interesting or one problem you noticed.

Tailor your resume for each role. With 19 open positions, Arcana is hiring across functions. A resume that directly addresses the responsibilities listed in the job description will always outperform a generic one.

Brush up on relevant fundamentals. For engineering roles, distributed systems, cryptography basics, and API design are commonly tested areas. Know which domain matters most for the specific role you are targeting and go deep there rather than covering everything shallowly.

Prepare a strong 'why this company' answer. This question carries more weight at startup interviews than most candidates expect. A vague answer like 'I want to learn' is a red flag. Tie your answer to something specific about what Arcana is building and what problem it is solving for developers or users.

Research compensation benchmarks. Industry surveys and publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi can give you a directional reference before salary discussions. Know your number before you need to give one.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Arcana operates with the pace and ownership culture typical of an early-stage Web3 startup. The team likely spans multiple cities and possibly time zones, which places a premium on async communication and self-directed work.

Ownership over process. You are expected to take a problem from first principles to completion without needing to be managed through each step. Managers here are often contributors themselves, so dedicated mentorship is something you may need to actively seek rather than expect by default.

Continuous learning. The Web3 space moves faster than most tech verticals. People who thrive here read constantly, experiment with new tools, and are willing to revise their views when the evidence changes.

Flat, collaborative structure. Early-stage teams tend to have fewer layers, which means your ideas get a hearing regardless of seniority. The flip side is that you need to be comfortable defending your thinking in open discussion rather than deferring to hierarchy.

Mission alignment. Arcana's core proposition, giving users control of their own data, is part of the pitch to both users and employees. Candidates who connect with this goal tend to ramp up faster and stay longer than those primarily motivated by the broader Web3 hype cycle.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does Arcana have right now?

Arcana has 19 open roles as of 8 July 2026. The openings span engineering, product, and likely growth or business functions. Because the team is relatively small, new roles open and close faster than at large companies, so checking the careers page regularly is worth your time.

Do I need a Web3 or blockchain background to apply?

A Web3 background is helpful but not always required. For core engineering roles touching smart contracts or cryptography, relevant experience is a strong differentiator. For product, growth, or support roles, a genuine interest in the space backed by self-study or side projects can be enough to clear the initial bar. Be ready to speak specifically about what drew you to the space and what you have done to learn about it.

Is Arcana remote-friendly for Indian candidates outside major tech hubs?

Web3 startups at this stage commonly operate with remote or hybrid arrangements, and many Indian candidates have joined similar companies from cities outside Bangalore or Mumbai. The job description for each role will clarify location requirements. If the listing does not specify, raising it early in the introductory call is a fair and expected question.

How long does the hiring process typically take?

At startups of this size, the full process from application to offer commonly takes two to four weeks, though it can move faster if the team has an urgent need. Delays most often happen between the take-home task and the technical interview round. Following up politely after a week of silence is reasonable and usually well received.

What compensation can I expect at Arcana?

Arcana has not publicly disclosed salary bands, so specific figures are not available here. For Web3 startups at this stage, publicly reported ranges on Glassdoor and levels.fyi, as well as community salary threads on tech forums, can give you a directional sense before negotiations. Equity is commonly part of the package at companies this early, so factor that into your total evaluation alongside the fixed component.

Should I apply if I am a fresher or recent graduate?

Early-stage startups typically prefer candidates who can contribute quickly with limited ramp-up time, which makes it harder but not impossible for freshers. If you are a recent graduate, strong internship projects, open-source contributions, or a portfolio of personal products can substitute for full-time experience. Focus your application on engineering or technical roles where your work can speak for itself rather than roles that require commercial judgment built over years.
