01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Supabase is an open-source database platform that gives developers a Postgres backend with authentication, storage, and real-time features built in. The company is fully remote and async-first, with team members spread across dozens of countries. As of July 2026, Supabase is actively growing with 52 open roles across engineering, developer relations, marketing, and operations.

Hiring at Supabase is competitive because they look for people who already use the product, care deeply about developer experience, and can work independently across time zones. The team is lean for the scale they operate at, which means each hire carries significant responsibility and visibility.

For Indian candidates, Supabase hires globally and does not restrict roles by location. Your application is reviewed on merit, and being comfortable with clear, async English communication matters as much as technical skill.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The process candidates have publicly described typically runs through these stages, though exact steps can vary by role and team:

Stage 1: Application and resume screen
Your resume goes to a recruiter or hiring manager. A strong GitHub profile, contributions to open-source projects, or hands-on work with Postgres-based tools helps you stand out at this stage.

Stage 2: Recruiter or hiring manager call
A short video call to understand your background, your motivation for joining Supabase, and whether your experience fits the role. Expect questions about past projects and how you work in a fully remote setup.

Stage 3: Technical assessment
For engineering roles, this is usually a take-home task or an async code review. You might be asked to build a small feature, debug a piece of code, or write SQL for a realistic scenario. For non-engineering roles, this could be a written exercise or a short case study.

Stage 4: Technical deep-dive
A live or async review going deeper into your submission or your general technical knowledge. For backend and platform roles, Postgres internals, TypeScript, and distributed systems come up frequently. For developer relations roles, expect questions on how you explain technical concepts to different audiences.

Stage 5: Team and culture fit
A conversation with future teammates or a member of leadership. Supabase values people who are direct, curious, and comfortable giving feedback openly. This round often explores how you handle ambiguity and disagreement.

Stage 6: Reference check and offer
If all rounds go well, references are checked quickly and an offer follows. The full process, as publicly reported by candidates, typically spans a few weeks from first contact to offer.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Supabase consistently looks for a few qualities across roles:

Technical depth, not just breadth. Engineers are expected to go deep on Postgres, understand query planning, and know when to reach for Rust versus TypeScript. Surface-level knowledge does not pass their technical bar.

Open-source mindset. The company ships in the open. They want people who have contributed to open-source projects, who read other people's code critically, and who default to sharing rather than keeping knowledge private.

Async communication skills. Because the team is distributed across time zones, clear and concise writing matters enormously. A GitHub comment, a pull request description, or a recorded video often replaces a meeting. Poor written communication is a genuine filter at every stage.

Product sense for developers. Even non-technical roles need people who understand what a developer actually cares about. Using Supabase before you apply is a real and noticeable advantage.

Ownership without hand-holding. Supabase is lean. You are expected to define the problem, propose a solution, ship it, and monitor it, without waiting for direction at every step.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Build something with Supabase before you apply. Deploy a real project using Supabase auth, storage, and the Postgres backend. Reference it in your application and be ready to walk through the decisions you made. Interviewers notice when a candidate has firsthand product experience.

Get comfortable with Postgres internals. Study EXPLAIN ANALYZE, index types, row-level security, and connection pooling. These topics come up in technical rounds for engineering roles and sometimes in developer relations interviews too.

Contribute to the Supabase GitHub repository. Even a documentation fix or a well-reasoned bug report shows you have engaged with the codebase. It is not a hard requirement, but it is a genuine differentiator in a competitive applicant pool.

Practice async writing. Write a short blog post or a detailed README for a personal project. Your ability to explain technical ideas in writing is evaluated throughout the Supabase process, not just in one round.

Prepare stories using the STAR format. Supabase interviewers ask about past decisions, particularly moments where you disagreed with a team, had to debug something difficult under pressure, or owned a project from start to finish. Prepare a couple of concrete examples with clear outcomes.

Read their public roadmap and changelogs. Go through the Supabase blog, GitHub discussions, and release notes before your interviews. Speaking to a recent product decision signals genuine interest and separates you from candidates who only Googled the company the night before. 06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Supabase runs on a fully remote, async-first model. Most coordination happens through written channels, pull requests, and recorded videos rather than live meetings. If you prefer structured schedules and in-person energy, this culture will feel different at first. Indian professionals often find the async model works well because you can do your best work during Indian business hours without being pulled into late-night calls.

The company is deeply committed to open source. Their core product and most tooling are open source, and they publish engineering decisions, incident reports, and product milestones publicly. This transparency extends internally: teams share context openly and debate ideas in writing rather than in private conversations.

Developer empathy is the core value. Every decision, from product to marketing to hiring, is filtered through the question of what a developer would actually want. If you have spent time as a developer or worked closely with developer communities, that experience will resonate in every conversation you have with the Supabase team.

Direct feedback is the norm. Team members are expected to share honest opinions, including disagreements with leadership. If you are used to softening feedback heavily or waiting to be asked, you may need to adjust to a more direct and open communication style.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Supabase hire from India?

Yes, Supabase hires globally for most remote roles and does not restrict candidates by geography. Indian professionals have joined across engineering and other functions. Your application goes through the same process as anyone else, and is evaluated on technical skill and communication ability, not location.

How long does the Supabase interview process take?

Candidates who have publicly shared their experience report a process that typically spans a few weeks from first contact to offer. The async nature of the company means some stages happen over written exchanges rather than live calls, which can speed things up or slow them down depending on turnaround time on both sides.

What salary does Supabase pay?

Supabase has not published formal salary bands publicly. Publicly reported data on sites like Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggests compensation is competitive with other international remote-first tech companies. Roles are typically paid in USD regardless of your location, which is a meaningful advantage for Indian professionals.

Do I need open-source contributions to get hired at Supabase?

Open-source contributions are not a hard requirement, but they are a real advantage. Contributing to Supabase's own GitHub repository, or to other developer tools projects, signals the kind of public technical work the company values. If you have no contributions yet, building a well-documented personal project using Supabase is a strong alternative to show during interviews.

What kinds of roles are currently open at Supabase?

As of July 2026, Supabase has 52 open roles. These span software engineering, developer relations, marketing, design, and operations. Engineering roles typically require strong Postgres and TypeScript expertise, while other roles call for clear writing ability and a developer-first mindset.

Can freshers or early-career candidates apply to Supabase?

Most Supabase roles expect several years of relevant experience, and the company is too lean to run structured fresher programs. Early-career candidates are rarely a fit for direct hires right now. Building a strong portfolio with Supabase projects, contributing to open source, and gaining experience at other companies first is a more realistic path before applying.
