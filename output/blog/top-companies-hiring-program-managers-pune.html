01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

Technical Program Manager compensation in India varies by city, company stage, and whether the role is product, platform, or growth-focused. Fixed pay is quoted in LPA (lakhs per annum); variable pay and ESOPs are common at startups. Always clarify fixed vs CTC before negotiating.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

  1. Anchor with data from 3+ offers or trusted peers at the same level.
  2. Negotiate fixed first; treat variable and ESOP separately.
  3. Ask for signing bonus if base is capped.
  4. Get the offer letter with break-up (basic, HRA, PF) before resigning.
  5. If competing offers exist, share timelines, not numbers, to accelerate decisions.
05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

CTC typically includes: Basic, HRA, special allowance, PF employer contribution, gratuity provision, and projected variable. Ask for in-hand estimate after tax. ESOPs at startups may not appear in CTC, model dilution and vesting cliff separately.

06 Market Snapshot

Market Snapshot

Pune had 13 open Technical Program Manager roles as of 8 July 2026, according to knok jobradar. That puts it third among major cities, behind Bangalore (41 openings) and Delhi (14), but ahead of Hyderabad (12) and Chennai (5). Mumbai, with just 1 posting, barely registers for this role.

What makes Pune interesting is the variety of employers. You will find a global bank's GCC, fast-growing product companies, and early-stage startups all hiring TPMs at the same time. If you are open to different environments, Pune gives you genuine options without the intensity of Bangalore's competition.

The TPM role itself is gaining ground across sectors. Companies that once managed programs through project managers are now specifically looking for someone who can own technical execution end to end, coordinate engineering teams, and communicate trade-offs to leadership. That shift is visible in Pune's current hiring mix.

09 How To Stand Out

How To Stand Out

The TPM role sits at the intersection of engineering and delivery. Hiring managers in Pune are looking for a few things that go beyond a generic project management background.

Show technical depth alongside program skills. You do not need to write production code, but you must be credible in engineering conversations. If your resume only shows timelines and status reports, it will not cut through at companies like Checkmarx or Perforce, where the domain is technical by nature.

Quantify delivery impact. 'Managed a team' is a weak phrase. Showing that you restructured a delivery process and measurably reduced cycle times, with specific numbers from your own work, is what gets you shortlisted.

Call out cross-functional coordination. TPMs at GCCs like Mastercard routinely work across time zones and business units. Show that you have done this, not just within a single team.

Match your background to the company's domain. A fintech background helps for Mastercard. Familiarity with SaaS or B2B products helps for Mindtickle and Cornerstone. Tailor your resume summary to the company's specific domain, not just the generic job description copy.

Certifications are secondary but not useless. PMP, CSM, or SAFe credentials show structure and commitment, but they will not substitute for demonstrated delivery outcomes.

10 Application Strategy

Application Strategy

Start by mapping the nine companies in Pune against your own experience. Group them into 'strong fit,' 'stretch,' and 'learn more' buckets. Apply first to the ones where your domain matches, since those will give you the fastest signal.

For each application, rewrite your resume summary to mirror the specific role's language. A Mastercard application should highlight governance, risk awareness, and global stakeholder management. An Ergobite or Mihup application should highlight speed, ambiguity tolerance, and end-to-end ownership.

Reach out to TPMs already at those companies on LinkedIn. A brief, specific message about their delivery culture is far more likely to get a response than a generic connection request.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Pune a good city for TPM roles compared to Bangalore?

Bangalore has 41 TPM openings versus Pune's 13 as of July 2026, so the sheer volume is higher there. Pune's advantage is lower competition and a genuine mix of GCCs, product companies, and startups. If you are based in Pune and do not want to relocate, the market is active enough to find a strong role without stretching to Bangalore.

Which types of companies are hiring TPMs in Pune right now?

The current openings span a GCC (Mastercard), SaaS product companies (Mindtickle, Cornerstone, Checkmarx, Perforce), an AI startup (Mihup), a food-tech startup (Ergobite), and IT services firms (R Systems, Sunware Technologies). That spread means you can target the environment that fits your working style rather than being limited to one sector.

Do I need a technical degree to get a TPM role in Pune?

Most TPM job descriptions list a B.E. or B.Tech as preferred, but they are rarely a hard filter if your program management track record is strong. What matters more is your ability to hold technical conversations with engineering teams, understand architecture trade-offs, and translate those into delivery plans for business stakeholders. A strong portfolio of delivered programs often outweighs a degree mismatch.

What tools and skills should I highlight in my application?

Focus on tools like JIRA, Confluence, and whatever delivery framework your target company uses (Agile, SAFe, or Kanban). Beyond tools, highlight risk management, cross-functional stakeholder alignment, and the ability to write clear technical specs or PRDs. For security-focused companies like Checkmarx, any familiarity with SDLC or DevSecOps concepts is a strong bonus.

Should I target startups or GCCs for a TPM role in Pune?

It depends on what you want from the role. GCCs like Mastercard offer structured career paths, global exposure, and stronger total compensation packages based on publicly reported data. Startups like Ergobite and Mihup offer broader ownership, faster visibility, and the chance to shape program management from the ground up. The right choice depends on your career stage and what kind of environment helps you grow fastest.

How often do new TPM roles open up in Pune?

The 13 openings in the knok jobradar snapshot from 8 July 2026 reflect a single point in time. Technology hiring moves in waves tied to funding rounds, product launches, and annual headcount planning cycles. Checking weekly rather than monthly ensures you catch roles before the applicant pool builds up, especially at smaller companies that close positions quickly once they find a fit.
