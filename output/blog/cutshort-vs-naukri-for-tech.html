01 Overview

Overview

Naukri.com and Cutshort are two very different tools for the same goal: finding a tech job in India. Naukri is a long-established, general-purpose job portal with a massive database covering every industry and city. Cutshort is a newer, tech-focused platform that tries to match candidates to roles using skill-based scoring rather than keyword searches.

For a software engineer or product manager, choosing between them is not obvious. Naukri gives you raw reach: more jobs listed, more recruiters searching, more companies posting. Cutshort gives you signal: fewer but more relevant matches, less inbox noise, and a community built specifically for tech talent.

This guide breaks down what each platform actually does well, where each falls short, and which one makes more sense depending on where you are in your career.

03 Best For

Best For

Pick Naukri if you are open to roles across industries, you are actively hunting and want maximum exposure, you are in a non-metro city where Cutshort has thin listings, or you target IT services companies where Naukri dominates hiring.

Pick Cutshort if you are a developer, designer, or product manager targeting product startups or funded companies, you want fewer but higher-quality recruiter conversations, you have side projects or a GitHub portfolio you want recruiters to see, or you are tired of spam calls from irrelevant consultancies.

Use both if you are in an active search, since the recruiter pools barely overlap. Setting up a strong profile on each takes a few hours, and many job seekers find their eventual role through whichever platform they expected less.

04 Pros And Cons

Pros And Cons

Naukri

Strengths: The recruiter database is the largest in India by a wide margin, publicly reported across multiple industry surveys. Nearly every large IT services company, BFSI firm, and MNC uses Naukri as a primary sourcing channel. Job alerts are fast and plentiful. If you are switching between IT services companies, Naukri is the default channel.

Weaknesses: Keyword matching produces many irrelevant calls. Expect daily outreach from consultancies pitching roles that do not match your skills or salary expectations. The interface feels dated and the resume parser often garbles formatting. Premium features add cost without a clear return for most candidates.

Cutshort

Strengths: The skill-matching system filters out a large share of irrelevant applications. Startup and product company recruiters use Cutshort because candidate quality is higher on average. The platform shows you a match percentage, helping you self-filter before applying. Company pages include culture signals and team size.

Weaknesses: Job volume is much lower than Naukri. If you are in a tier-2 city or looking for an IT services role, listings will be thin. The platform is less known among traditional HR teams, so some companies will never appear here. Setting up a complete Cutshort profile takes real effort, and an incomplete profile hurts your match scores.

05 Recommendation

Recommendation

For most tech professionals in India, the honest answer is: start with Cutshort for quality, keep Naukri active for volume.

If you are a developer or PM targeting product companies or funded startups, put your best effort into Cutshort. The signal-to-noise ratio is genuinely better, and the recruiter conversations you get are more likely to go somewhere.

If you are a fresher, in IT services, or open to a wide range of roles and companies, Naukri remains the highest-reach option in India. The spam is real but manageable if you use a secondary number.

In practice, a serious job search uses both. Upload your resume to Naukri, build a full profile on Cutshort, set alerts on both, and track applications carefully to avoid duplicates.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Is Cutshort only for startups?

Cutshort started with a startup focus but now lists roles at mid-size product companies and some larger tech firms too. That said, the platform's strength is still in the funded startup and product company space. If your target is a large IT services firm, Naukri will have far more relevant listings.

Will my Naukri resume show up on Cutshort too?

No. The two platforms are completely separate. Your Naukri resume does not sync to Cutshort and vice versa. You will need to build profiles on each independently, which is worth doing since their recruiter pools barely overlap.

Why do I get so many spam calls after uploading to Naukri?

Naukri sells resume database access to a large number of recruiters and consultancies, many of whom do mass outreach. When your resume is visible, any subscribed recruiter can see your phone number and call you. A common workaround is to use a secondary SIM for job-search platforms, or to set your profile to 'not actively looking' once you enter late-stage interviews.

Which platform is better for checking salary ranges?

Neither platform publishes reliable aggregate salary benchmarks. For market data, Glassdoor and levels.fyi are more useful, especially for product and startup roles. That said, Cutshort job posts tend to display CTC ranges more transparently than Naukri, where many posts hide the salary band entirely.

Should a fresher use Cutshort or Naukri?

Freshers generally get more traction on Naukri because it has wider recruiter coverage including campus hiring teams and IT services companies that hire in bulk. Cutshort is worth setting up if you have strong projects or a portfolio, since it rewards skill signals over credentials alone. Many freshers use Naukri as their primary channel and Cutshort as a secondary one.

Can I use both platforms at the same time?

Yes, and most active job seekers do. The recruiter pools are largely different, so being on both increases your total reach without much duplication. The main downside is tracking applications manually across two platforms becomes tedious, especially once you add LinkedIn and company career pages. Keeping a simple spreadsheet or using an application tracker helps you avoid sending the same CV twice to the same company.
