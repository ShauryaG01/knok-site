01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Legora is a legal-AI company building software that helps law firms and in-house legal teams research cases, draft documents, and review contracts faster. The company has been growing rapidly and currently has 241 open roles across engineering, product, design, legal, and go-to-market functions. While Legora is headquartered in Europe, it hires remote candidates globally, which opens real doors for Indian professionals with the right background. Competition for spots is real, especially in engineering, but the volume of openings means multiple entry points exist at any given time.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Legora's hiring process typically follows a structured sequence, though the exact stages can vary by role and team.

Application and screening. You apply online and a recruiter reviews your resume and cover letter. If shortlisted, expect a short recruiter call to discuss your background, motivations, and basic role fit.

Technical or skills assessment. For engineering roles this is usually a take-home coding task or a live problem-solving session focused on real-world scenarios. Legal or product roles may receive a case study or writing exercise instead. This stage tests whether you can do the actual work, not just talk about it.

Hiring manager interview. A deeper conversation with the person you would directly report to. Expect questions about past projects, how you handle ambiguity, and why you want to work on legal-AI specifically.

Panel or cross-functional round. You meet a small panel of teammates from different functions. This round evaluates collaboration style, communication, and culture fit. Prepare to walk through a past project in detail.

Offer and reference check. Strong candidates receive a written offer relatively quickly. Reference checks are standard and taken seriously.

The full process commonly takes a few weeks, depending on role level and team availability.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Legora looks for people who combine domain strength with genuine curiosity about how AI changes knowledge work. A few qualities that come up consistently in their job descriptions:

Comfort with ambiguity. Legora is still defining what legal-AI can do. They want people who can make decisions without a complete playbook.

Legal or regulatory context. For non-engineering roles, familiarity with how law firms or in-house legal teams operate is a real advantage, even if your background is primarily in tech.

Strong written communication. The company's product is fundamentally about language and precision. Sloppy writing in interviews or take-home tasks is a red flag.

Ownership mindset. Legora operates with small teams that own large problems. They look for candidates who have driven things to completion independently, not just contributed to a group effort.

Technical depth (for engineers). Backend engineers should be solid in Python or TypeScript. Familiarity with LLMs, embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation, or similar AI/ML patterns is valued but not always mandatory.

For Indian candidates applying remotely, clear asynchronous communication is especially important since much of the collaboration happens across time zones.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

A few practical steps that improve your chances significantly:

Understand the product before you apply. Legora offers a free trial or demo on their website. Use it. Being able to say 'I noticed your citation checker does X, I have worked on similar problems' sets you apart from candidates who only read the homepage.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Legora has 241 open roles right now, spanning very different functions. Do not send the same resume to an engineering role and a legal success role. Highlight the skills most relevant to each specific job description.

Prepare a project walkthrough. Pick one past project where you drove meaningful impact. Be ready to describe the problem, your specific contribution, the tradeoffs you made, and what you would do differently. This story will come up in almost every interview stage.

Brush up on legal-AI basics. You do not need a law degree, but knowing how contract review, legal research, and due diligence work, and where AI fits in, shows genuine interest. LegalTech news sites and law firm blogs cover these topics clearly.

Write a specific cover letter. Generic cover letters hurt your chances. One paragraph on why Legora, one on what you bring, one on what you want to learn. Keep it short and direct.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Legora's culture centres on a few visible themes that show up in how they hire and how they talk about their work.

High ownership, small teams. Legora does not believe in large coordination layers. Engineers own features end to end, and non-technical roles own their domain similarly. If you thrive in a highly structured environment with clear role boundaries, this might feel uncomfortable.

Mission-driven work. The founding thesis is that access to legal expertise should not depend on whether you can afford a large law firm. People who work at Legora often mention this mission as a reason they joined. Genuine belief in the mission comes across in interviews and is noticed.

Intellectual honesty. The company builds a product that has to be correct, not just impressive. They value people who say 'I do not know' and investigate, over people who project false confidence.

Remote-first habits. Legora operates across multiple time zones. Written communication, documentation, and structured async updates are part of how work gets done. Strong writing is not just a nice-to-have, it is a core job skill.

Pace and growth. The company is growing fast. Roles evolve, priorities shift, and people are expected to adapt. If you prefer predictability over variety, factor this in before applying.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Legora hire from India?

Legora hires remote candidates globally, and Indian professionals have joined the company in engineering and other functions. Your best bet is to apply directly through their careers page and clearly state your time zone availability. Strong async communication skills and a reliable home setup matter more than your location for most roles.

How long does the Legora interview process take?

Most candidates who receive an offer report a process of a few weeks from application to offer. Engineering roles with a take-home task can sometimes run longer depending on reviewer availability. Following up politely after each stage is fine and generally appreciated by the team.

Do I need a legal background to work at Legora?

Not for most technical roles. Engineers, designers, and data professionals do not need legal training to apply. However, any candidate who has worked with legal teams, compliance processes, or contract management has a genuine edge because they understand the customer's world. For legal success or go-to-market roles, prior exposure to law firms or in-house legal is usually expected.

What salary can I expect at Legora?

Legora does not publicly list salary ranges for all roles, and publicly reported compensation data for this company is limited in sample size. Industry surveys and sites like Glassdoor suggest AI-focused startups at a similar stage offer competitive packages to attract strong talent, but exact figures vary widely by role, level, and location. Ask the recruiter about the salary band early in the process so there are no surprises later.

How many jobs are open at Legora right now?

Legora currently has 241 open roles across engineering, product, legal, design, and go-to-market. The volume of openings means there are multiple ways in, even if your primary skill set fits only a subset of roles. Check their careers page regularly since new positions are added frequently as the company grows.

What is the biggest mistake candidates make when applying to Legora?

The most commonly cited mistake is sending a generic application without engaging with the product. Legora builds a product you can actually try, and not mentioning it signals low interest to the hiring team. The second common error is underestimating the importance of written communication. Every take-home task and cover letter is also a writing sample, and precision matters deeply to a company whose product is built on language accuracy.
