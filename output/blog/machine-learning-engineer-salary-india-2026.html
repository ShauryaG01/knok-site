01 Salary Overview

Salary Overview

The honest starting point for any ML Engineer salary guide in India: most job postings hide the pay. Of the 803 Machine Learning Engineer roles tracked in the July 2026 jobradar snapshot, salary disclosure was the exception, not the rule. This is the norm across Indian tech hiring, which means any salary figure you read, including on this page, draws from self-reported data on platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi, not verified offer letters.

That context matters because self-reported data skews toward people willing to share, often higher earners at well-known companies. Treat any range here as directional guidance, not a guarantee. Industry surveys consistently place ML Engineers among the better-paid technical roles in India, but the gap between a fresher at an IT services firm and a senior at a well-funded product startup is large enough to make averages misleading. The sections below break this down by experience level and city so you can calibrate to your situation.

02 By Experience Level

By Experience Level

Since the July 2026 jobradar snapshot did not capture disclosed salary bands (most listings skip this field), the figures below come from publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi. Sample sizes on these platforms are limited, so treat the ranges as a starting reference rather than a hard benchmark.

Fresher (0-2 years): Glassdoor commonly cites entry-level ML Engineer salaries in India between 6-15 LPA at IT services companies and early-stage startups. For campus placements at top product companies, publicly reported figures on levels.fyi for premier institute graduates can be higher at well-funded firms.

Mid-level (3-5 years): Industry surveys and Glassdoor data commonly cite mid-level ML Engineers at product-focused companies earning 20-45 LPA in fixed pay, with the range driven by specialisation. Skills in MLOps, LLM fine-tuning, and production model serving are commonly cited as factors that push offers toward the higher end.

Senior (6-10 years): Publicly reported data on levels.fyi places senior ML Engineers at established tech firms in the 40-80 LPA range before equity. At multinational product companies, Glassdoor data commonly cites total compensation, including RSUs, significantly above the fixed component alone.

Staff or Principal (10+ years): At this level, levels.fyi data shows packages that are heavily equity-weighted. Industry surveys commonly cite total compensation at top-tier firms well above 80 LPA, with the fixed base making up a smaller share of overall pay.

04 Negotiation Tips

Negotiation Tips

Know your number before the conversation starts. Look up publicly reported offers on levels.fyi for your role, experience, and city. This gives you a credible reference point rather than a vague expectation. Your current fixed salary is your floor, not your target.

Separate fixed pay from the total package. Many Indian ML offers bundle variable pay, a joining bonus, or equity to inflate the headline CTC. Ask for the fixed, in-hand monthly amount explicitly, and evaluate equity and variable pay separately before comparing offers.

A competing offer moves the needle more than any argument. The single most reliable lever in Indian tech salary negotiations is a second offer letter. It removes ambiguity about your market rate and gives the hiring company a concrete reason to improve their number.

Negotiate beyond the base if the base is stuck. If the company cannot move on fixed pay, ask about a higher joining bonus, an accelerated equity cliff, or an earlier increment review date. These are often easier for HR to approve than a revised salary band.

Ask about the next appraisal cycle upfront. Some companies have long gaps between increment reviews. Knowing this before you sign helps you decide whether to push harder now or plan your next move after joining.

05 Total Comp Breakdown

Total Comp Breakdown

ML Engineer offers in India typically have several components. Understanding each one helps you compare offers across companies on equal terms.

Fixed salary: The guaranteed annual pay, divided into monthly instalments. This is the most important number to negotiate, since variable and equity components are less predictable.

Variable pay: Usually a percentage of fixed, paid quarterly or annually based on individual and company performance. Industry surveys commonly cite variable components for tech roles at around 10-20% of fixed pay, though this varies by company and seniority level.

Joining bonus: A one-time payment, often given to offset unvested equity or an impending increment you are leaving behind. It is sometimes split across the first year. Always check for a clawback clause in case you exit early.

Equity (ESOPs or RSUs): At startups, you typically get ESOPs with value only on a liquidity event. At larger product companies, RSUs vest on a schedule commonly cited in industry surveys as 4 years with a 1-year cliff. The value depends on the stock price at vesting, not at grant.

PF and gratuity: Both are mandatory by law. Provident Fund contributions reduce your monthly take-home from the gross salary. Gratuity accrues after 5 years of service. Always ask for a CTC breakup that shows the in-hand monthly figure clearly, since the CTC headline can include amounts you never see directly.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Why do most ML Engineer job listings in India not show a salary?

Indian employers are not legally required to disclose salary in job postings, so most choose not to. This gives companies flexibility to offer different packages based on a candidate's current salary and negotiation style. The result is that job seekers must rely on self-reported data from platforms like Glassdoor and levels.fyi to gauge their market rate. Some startups now publish salary bands, but they remain the exception rather than the norm.

How much does company type affect ML Engineer pay?

It makes a large difference. Publicly reported data on Glassdoor and levels.fyi consistently shows product companies, funded startups, and MNC R&D centres paying more than IT services or consulting firms for the same ML role title. Targeting product-first employers is one of the highest-impact moves if salary growth is a priority. Two roles with identical titles at different company types can have very different pay scales.

What skills push an ML Engineer's salary higher in 2026?

Industry surveys and publicly reported hiring data commonly cite hands-on production experience as the biggest differentiator over academic credentials alone. Skills in MLOps, large language model deployment, real-time inference, and data pipeline reliability are in strong demand. Domain expertise in fintech, healthcare, or e-commerce can further add a premium. Demonstrable projects and open-source contributions carry more weight than certifications in most ML hiring conversations.

Does Bangalore really pay more than other cities for ML roles?

Bangalore has the highest ML role count in this snapshot (165 of 803 total) and a high density of product companies and MNC tech centres, which Glassdoor data commonly cites as higher-paying employer types. But the city itself is not the driver. An ML role at a Bangalore services firm may pay less than one at a product company in Hyderabad or Pune. Focus on the employer type first, and Bangalore simply gives you more of those employers to choose from.

How is CTC different from the salary I actually take home?

CTC, or cost to company, includes everything the employer spends on you: base salary, employer PF contributions, gratuity accrual, and sometimes insurance and perks. Your actual monthly in-hand is lower than CTC divided by 12. Variable pay and bonuses further add to the gap between the headline CTC and what lands in your account each month. Always ask for a detailed CTC breakup and the in-hand monthly figure before accepting any offer.

Are ML Engineer roles in India growing in 2026?

The 803 ML Engineer roles in the July 2026 snapshot reflect active hiring, concentrated heavily in Bangalore and Delhi. Industry surveys and publicly reported hiring data commonly cite generative AI, MLOps, and production AI systems as the fastest-growing sub-areas within ML. Roles focused on model deployment and real-world reliability appear to be gaining ground over pure research positions at many Indian tech firms.
