01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Deloitte India is one of the most active professional services employers in the country right now. With 444 open roles as of July 2026, the firm is hiring across audit and assurance, tax, consulting, financial advisory, risk advisory, and technology. Whether you are a fresh graduate or a seasoned professional with years of experience, there is likely a Deloitte opening that matches your profile.

Deloitte runs two broad hiring tracks. Campus hiring targets students from IITs, NITs, IIMs, top commerce colleges, and law schools, with recruitment drives typically happening between October and February. Lateral hiring for experienced professionals happens year-round, driven by project needs and practice growth. Technology and consulting roles are especially active given the current demand for digital transformation and AI-related work.

The Deloitte name in India covers both Deloitte India (the local entity, also known as Deloitte Haskins and Sells) and Deloitte USI (US India), which supports Deloitte's global offices with analytics, technology, and consulting work. Understanding which entity you are applying to helps you set the right expectations for work culture, clients, and career progression.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Deloitte's hiring process varies by role and service line, but most candidates go through a version of the following stages. The full process commonly takes anywhere from two to six weeks, depending on the volume of applicants and the urgency of the position.

Stage 1: Application and Resume Screening
You apply through Deloitte's careers portal or via a campus drive. Recruiters screen resumes for relevant academic background, internships, certifications, and work experience. A specific, well-matched resume that reflects the job description clears this stage more reliably than a generic one.

Stage 2: Online Assessment
For campus hiring and many lateral roles, Deloitte runs an online test covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and sometimes domain knowledge. Technology roles typically include a coding section. Performing well here is the gate to everything that follows.

Stage 3: Group Discussion or Case Study Round
This round is common for consulting and advisory roles. You may be given a business problem to discuss as a group, or asked to present your analysis of a short case. Assessors are watching how clearly you think, how well you listen, and whether you can build on others' ideas constructively.

Stage 4: Technical or Domain Interview
One or two rounds focused on your area of expertise. Audit roles bring questions on accounting standards and financial statements. Technology roles cover programming, system design, or data. Consulting roles mix structured case questions with behavioral ones.

Stage 5: Partner or Senior Manager Interview
A conversation with a senior leader, often exploratory in tone. They want to understand your career goals, your fit with the practice, and how you handle ambiguity. This is also your best chance to ask genuine questions about the team and the kind of work you will do.

Stage 6: HR Discussion and Offer
The final step covers compensation, joining timelines, and background verification. Be ready to discuss your current and expected compensation clearly and without hesitation.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Deloitte is not just looking for technical skills. They hire for a combination of analytical ability, communication skills, and the willingness to learn in fast-moving project environments.

Analytical thinking is central to almost every role. Whether you are reviewing financial statements, writing code, or advising a client on a business problem, Deloitte expects you to break problems down logically and explain your reasoning clearly.

Communication and collaboration carry significant weight. Most Deloitte projects involve client-facing work, cross-functional teams, or both. Candidates who write and speak clearly, ask sharp questions, and contribute meaningfully in group settings consistently stand out from the pool.

Domain credibility is critical for lateral hires. If you are applying for a tax role, your working knowledge of GST, direct tax, or transfer pricing needs to be solid and current. If you are applying for a technology role, your command of the relevant stack or platform is closely examined.

Adaptability is something Deloitte values explicitly. Projects shift scope, client priorities change, and new tools arrive constantly. Candidates who can show they have navigated change well in past roles are viewed very favourably.

Certifications add meaningful weight, especially for technology and risk roles. Credentials like AWS, Azure, CISA, or CFA (depending on the service line) signal focused investment in your professional area and are worth pursuing before you apply.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Tailor your resume to the specific service line. A resume built for an audit role will not stand out for a technology consulting role. Read the job description carefully and reflect the same language and priorities in your resume, wherever they accurately describe your experience.

Practice aptitude tests in advance. The online assessment filters out many candidates who underestimate it. Practice quantitative reasoning, logical sequences, and data interpretation consistently in the two weeks before your scheduled test.

Prepare structured answers for behavioral questions. Deloitte interviewers commonly ask questions like 'tell me about a time you worked under pressure' or 'describe a situation where you disagreed with a colleague.' Use the situation, action, result format to keep your answers focused and specific.

For consulting roles, work on case interview skills. Start with simple market-sizing problems and build up to full business cases. Read business news regularly so you can discuss industries and companies with confidence when the topic comes up in a round.

For technology roles, sharpen coding and system design basics. Even if the role is not purely engineering-focused, Deloitte technology practices value candidates who understand code and architecture. Practise on platforms like LeetCode or HackerRank.

Research Deloitte's published reports and thought leadership. Look up their publicly available industry outlooks, point-of-view papers, and survey findings. Referencing a specific Deloitte insight during your interview shows genuine preparation, not just generic enthusiasm.

Follow up after each round. A short, professional message to your recruiter after each stage keeps your name visible and signals that you take the process seriously.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Deloitte's culture centres on what the firm calls its 'shared values,' which include integrity, outstanding value to clients, commitment to one another, and strength from cultural diversity. In practice, these values show up in day-to-day work in ways worth understanding before you join.

Client focus is intense. A large portion of roles are project-based, meaning your deliverables, timelines, and often your working hours are tied to what clients need. If you thrive in a deadline-driven environment with clear goals, this suits you well. If you prefer a steady, predictable pace, the adjustment can be demanding.

Learning and development are genuine priorities. Deloitte invests consistently in training, from structured onboarding programs to online learning platforms available to all employees. Many people who have worked here cite the breadth of exposure across industries and functions as one of the firm's biggest advantages.

Hierarchy exists but is not rigid. Titles and promotion cycles follow a defined structure, but Deloitte also encourages junior employees to contribute in client meetings and bring forward ideas. The culture rewards those who show initiative and take ownership early.

Diversity and inclusion are active, visible commitments. Deloitte India runs programs supporting women in leadership, LGBTQ+ inclusion, and accessibility. Candidates who value working in an environment where these conversations happen openly tend to find the firm a genuine fit.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does Deloitte hire freshers straight from college?

Yes, Deloitte runs active campus recruitment drives at IITs, NITs, IIMs, and top commerce and law colleges across India. Fresher roles are available in audit, tax, technology, and consulting practices. The campus hiring season generally runs between October and February each year. You can also apply through Deloitte's careers portal outside of campus season if your profile matches an open entry-level or junior role.

What is the difference between Deloitte India and Deloitte USI?

Deloitte India (formally Deloitte Haskins and Sells) serves Indian clients directly across audit, tax, and advisory services. Deloitte USI (US India) is a global delivery centre that supports Deloitte offices in the US, UK, and other countries with analytics, technology, and consulting work. The two entities differ in client exposure, work timings, and career trajectories. When you apply, confirm which entity the role belongs to so your expectations are aligned from the start.

How long does the entire Deloitte hiring process take?

Most candidates complete the process in two to six weeks from initial application to offer letter. Campus hiring tends to move faster because multiple rounds happen in concentrated drives over a few days. Lateral hiring for senior or specialised roles can take longer if multiple partner-level stakeholders need to be involved in the decision. Stay in regular contact with your recruiter so you always know where you stand in the pipeline.

Is a high CGPA required to get a call from Deloitte?

For campus hiring, Deloitte typically sets a minimum CGPA cutoff that varies by college and role, commonly cited as around 6.5 to 7.0 on a 10-point scale based on publicly reported campus drive criteria. Meeting the cutoff gets your resume into consideration, but the online assessment and interviews carry far more weight in the final decision. For lateral hires with meaningful work experience, CGPA matters much less than your demonstrated skills and project track record.

What salary can I expect at Deloitte India?

Salary at Deloitte varies significantly by service line, role level, and city. Glassdoor and levels.fyi list commonly cited salary ranges for various Deloitte roles in India, and checking those for your specific practice and experience level gives you a realistic benchmark before you negotiate. Technology and consulting roles generally pay differently from audit and tax roles at comparable seniority. Negotiation is possible for lateral hires, especially when you bring specialised or certified skills the team needs urgently.

Can I apply to more than one Deloitte service line at the same time?

Generally, Deloitte prefers that candidates focus on one service line per application cycle, and their internal systems often flag duplicate applications across practices. If you are genuinely interested in more than one area, the best approach is to be upfront with your recruiter about it early in the process. Applying to multiple lines without flagging it can create confusion and may slow down your progress across all of them.
