01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

KPMG India is one of the Big Four professional services firms, with offices across major Indian cities. The firm hires across audit and assurance, tax, advisory, technology consulting, deals, and enabling functions such as HR, finance, and legal.

As of July 2026, KPMG India has 233 open roles listed on its careers portal. These span entry-level analyst positions for fresh graduates, mid-level roles for professionals with a few years of experience, and senior positions for domain specialists and managers.

Hiring happens throughout the year, with a noticeable uptick during campus recruitment season (typically between January and March) and post-appraisal cycles when lateral movement picks up. KPMG India recruits from top engineering institutes, commerce colleges, MBA programmes, and law schools, depending on the role.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The interview process at KPMG India typically runs through four to five stages, though the exact number varies by role type (campus versus lateral) and the service line you apply to.

Stage 1: Application and shortlisting
Submit your application through the KPMG India careers portal or via your campus placement cell. For experienced hires, referrals from current employees are a common entry point. The recruiting team screens resumes for relevant qualifications, certifications (such as CA, CPA, CISA, or MBA), and domain experience.

Stage 2: Online assessment (mainly for fresher roles)
Many entry-level roles include an online aptitude test covering verbal reasoning, quantitative ability, and logical reasoning. Some technical roles may also include a domain-specific test. This stage is time-bound, so practise under timed conditions beforehand.

Stage 3: Technical or domain interview
This is the core round. Expect questions on your area of expertise, whether that is auditing standards, tax regulations, financial modelling, data analytics, or IT risk. For consulting roles, case-based questions testing structured thinking are common. Interviewers are usually managers or senior managers within the relevant team.

Stage 4: Partner or leadership round
For mid-senior positions, a round with a partner or associate director is standard. This is less about technical depth and more about fit, career goals, and how you handle ambiguity. Expect scenario-based questions about past work and how you managed difficult situations with stakeholders.

Stage 5: HR and offer discussion
The final stage covers compensation expectations, joining timelines, background verification requirements, and documentation. Offers are typically rolled out within a few weeks of the final round, though timelines can vary by team and seniority.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

KPMG India looks for a mix of technical competence and professional maturity across all levels.

Domain knowledge: For audit roles, a CA qualification or strong accounting fundamentals is often non-negotiable. For advisory and consulting, an MBA or relevant industry experience carries weight. Technology roles value certifications in cloud, cybersecurity, data, or ERP systems.

Analytical and structured thinking: The firm expects candidates to break down problems clearly and communicate findings in a structured way, whether in a client presentation or an internal report.

Communication skills: KPMG client-facing roles involve working with senior stakeholders. Clear written and spoken English, along with the ability to simplify complex information, matters a great deal at every grade.

Integrity and attention to detail: Given the nature of audit and advisory work, the firm places a premium on accuracy, ethical conduct, and following process rigorously. This comes up in interviews, not just onboarding.

Teamwork and adaptability: Projects at KPMG involve cross-functional teams, often spread across locations. The ability to collaborate, take feedback, and adapt to fast-changing client needs is valued at every level.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Tailor your resume to the service line. KPMG runs distinct service lines: audit, tax, deals, advisory, and technology. Read the job description carefully and highlight experience most relevant to that specific line. Generic resumes get screened out early.

Know your domain cold. For audit and tax roles, brush up on Ind AS, IFRS, income tax provisions, or GST as relevant. For consulting roles, practise structuring answers to case questions using frameworks like issue trees or MECE logic. For technology roles, revisit core concepts in your area (cloud architecture, ITGC, data modelling, and similar topics).

Research KPMG India's recent work. The firm publishes thought leadership reports on tax changes, ESG, digital transformation, and risk. Reading a few of these before your interview gives you talking points and signals genuine interest to the interviewer.

Prepare STAR-format answers. KPMG interviewers regularly ask behavioural questions. Structure your answers as Situation, Task, Action, Result to keep responses crisp and credible, especially for the partner round.

Have questions ready for your interviewer. Asking about the team's current focus areas or what success looks like in the first six months signals maturity and engagement, two things the firm actively looks for.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

KPMG India operates with a strong emphasis on professional ethics, client service, and continuous learning. The firm follows global KPMG values around integrity, excellence, courage, and working together. These values shape how teams are structured and how performance is evaluated through the year.

The work environment is generally structured and process-driven, as you would expect in a firm that serves regulated industries. Deadlines are real, especially during audit seasons or tax filing periods, and the pace can be intense. At the same time, the firm invests in learning through internal training programmes, professional certification support, and access to global knowledge resources.

Since 2024, KPMG India has been expanding its technology and advisory practices, creating more cross-functional collaboration between traditional service lines and digital teams. This is opening new career paths for professionals who combine domain knowledge with technology skills.

Culture can vary noticeably between teams and office locations. Teams in metro cities and those working on large client mandates tend to move faster. Formal feedback cycles and mentorship structures are in place to help early-career professionals grow and find their footing.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

Does KPMG India hire freshers directly from college?

Yes. KPMG India actively recruits from campus, especially from top CA institutes, engineering colleges, and MBA programmes. Campus drives typically happen between January and March, though timelines differ by year and location. Check the KPMG India careers portal and your college placement cell for the latest schedule.

How long does the KPMG India hiring process take?

For lateral hires, the process from application to offer commonly takes a few weeks to about six weeks, depending on the service line and seniority of the role. Campus hires receive offers on placement day but join several months later. Background verification typically adds more time after the offer letter is issued, so keep your documents ready.

Do I need a CA qualification to join KPMG India?

Not necessarily. A CA qualification is important for audit and direct tax roles, but KPMG India also hires MBAs, engineers, lawyers, and domain specialists for advisory, technology, deals, and enabling function teams. The requirement depends entirely on the specific role you are applying to, so read the job description carefully before applying.

What salary can I expect at KPMG India?

Compensation varies widely by role, city, and experience level. Glassdoor and levels.fyi carry publicly reported figures for specific roles and grades at KPMG India, and those are worth checking before your HR discussion. It is best to research the exact role and level you are targeting on these platforms to set realistic expectations going into the offer stage.

Is a referral important to get hired at KPMG India?

A referral from a current employee helps your application get noticed, especially for lateral roles where competition is high. That said, strong applications through the careers portal do get shortlisted without one. Connecting with KPMG professionals on LinkedIn before you apply is a practical way to explore referral possibilities without cold-approaching strangers.
