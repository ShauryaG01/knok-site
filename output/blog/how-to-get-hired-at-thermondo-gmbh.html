01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Thermondo GmbH is one of Germany's leading providers of modern heating systems, specialising in heat pumps, solar thermal energy, and gas heating replacements. Founded in Berlin, the company has grown significantly as Europe's push toward cleaner energy has made home heating upgrades a high-demand service.

As of July 2026, Thermondo has 199 open roles spanning field technicians, software engineers, product managers, sales specialists, customer support staff, and operations professionals. This is one of the larger active hiring pipelines among European climate-tech companies right now. Whether you are a hands-on trade professional, a backend engineer, or someone with a background in customer success, there is a good chance a role exists that matches your profile.

For Indian professionals, the most common entry points are in software engineering, data, and product roles, some of which are open to remote or hybrid arrangements. Field technician and customer-facing roles are typically based in Germany and require relocation plus a working level of German.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Thermondo's hiring process follows a structured sequence, though exact stages vary by role. Here is what candidates commonly report, based on publicly shared experiences:

StageFormatWhat to Expect
Online ApplicationCV, cover note, portfolio linkScreening against role requirements; response within one to two weeks
Recruiter ScreenShort video or phone callBackground, motivation, notice period, language level
Technical AssessmentTake-home task or live sessionCoding challenge, case study, or skills test depending on role
Hiring Manager InterviewIn-depth video callDomain knowledge, behavioural questions, role-specific scenarios
Final RoundPanel or team callCulture fit, your questions, offer conversation

Online Application. Submit your CV through Thermondo's careers portal. For engineering roles, a GitHub link or project portfolio adds real weight. A short cover note explaining why sustainable heating interests you tends to perform better than a generic template.

Recruiter Screen. A short call with someone from the talent team. Expect questions about your current role, what draws you to Thermondo, your notice period, and for Germany-based roles, your German language level.

Technical or Role Assessment. Engineering candidates typically receive a take-home problem or a live coding session focused on real-world scenarios. Field candidates may face a practical demonstration or a scenario walkthrough. Sales candidates are sometimes asked to run a short mock pitch.

Hiring Manager Interview. This round goes deeper into your experience and how you think. Behavioural questions framed around specific examples from your past are common. Thermondo interviewers reportedly prefer structured answers, so the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) works well here.

Final Round. A panel discussion or casual team call before the offer stage. Use this round to ask about team structure, how success is measured in the role, and what onboarding looks like.

The full process from application to offer commonly takes three to five weeks, though fast-track timelines are possible for urgent openings.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Thermondo hires across very different functions, but a few qualities come up consistently regardless of role:

Genuine interest in climate and energy. The company's mission is accelerating Germany's shift away from fossil-fuel heating. Candidates who can speak naturally about why this matters, rather than reciting it from the job description, tend to stand out in interviews.

Practical problem-solving. Thermondo operates at the intersection of physical installations and digital systems. Whether you are a field technician dealing with an unexpected site condition or an engineer debugging a production issue, staying calm and finding solutions under pressure is highly valued.

Clear communication across functions. Engineers collaborate closely with field operations teams, and sales staff work alongside technical support. Explaining complex ideas simply, to people without your specialist background, is a real asset at this company.

German language skills. For most roles based in Germany, German at a working level is important. Publicly shared candidate feedback commonly describes B2 level or above as the expectation. Remote-friendly tech roles may be more flexible, but check the specific job description carefully.

Ownership and initiative. Thermondo is a scaling company where processes are still being built. People who spot problems and act on them, rather than waiting to be told, tend to progress quickly.

Relevant technical depth. For engineering roles, this means solid experience in the stack listed in the job description. For field roles, hands-on knowledge of heating systems, heat pumps, or electrical installation is expected.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product and market. Thermondo's core offering is installing and maintaining modern heating systems for German homeowners. Read up on how heat pumps work and what the publicly reported German government subsidy programmes for home energy upgrades involve. Being able to reference this context naturally in an interview signals genuine motivation rather than a last-minute search session.

Build your STAR answer bank. Thermondo interviewers rely heavily on behavioural questions. Before your interview, prepare a handful of specific stories from your career that demonstrate: solving an unexpected problem, working across teams, taking ownership of a difficult situation, and delivering results under pressure. Practice these aloud until they feel natural and not scripted.

Match your preparation to the role type. For engineering roles, review the core technologies in the job description, prepare for system design discussions, and have a recent project ready to walk through in detail. For field technician roles, brush up on installation protocols and safety standards relevant to German trade work, and be ready to discuss how you have handled on-site complications. For sales and customer success roles, prepare a short value pitch for Thermondo's service and be ready to discuss how you have managed long sales cycles or difficult client conversations.

Practice your German. If the role lists German as a requirement, prepare to use it during the interview. Even switching into German for a few sentences about your background shows effort and seriousness.

Prepare thoughtful questions. Have two or three questions ready that show you have researched the company. Good examples: how the team measures success in this role, what the biggest challenge the team is currently working through, and how Thermondo supports new joiners who are relocating to Germany.

Sort out logistics early. If you are applying from India for a Germany-based role, confirm visa sponsorship availability during the recruiter screen. Asking this question early, with your qualifications clearly documented, prevents wasted time on both sides.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Thermondo operates with the energy of a scaling startup and the discipline a technical services company requires. The Berlin headquarters has a relatively flat, informal culture where contribution tends to matter more than job title.

Mission-driven work. Sustainability is not a marketing line at Thermondo. The company's entire business model is built on replacing old, high-emission heating systems with cleaner alternatives. Employees who are genuinely motivated by climate impact tend to feel at home here. Those who treat it as just another corporate job may find the pace demanding.

Cross-functional collaboration. Thermondo connects a physical field workforce with a digital product and engineering team. Engineers regularly interact with trade professionals, and customer-facing staff work closely with operations. The company values people who are comfortable operating across these boundaries without needing a formal handoff process.

Comfort with change. The business has grown rapidly, and internal processes evolve frequently. People who adapt well to shifting priorities and who can work with some ambiguity are a better cultural fit than those who prefer highly structured, stable environments.

International and diverse teams. The Berlin tech team is reported to include people from across Europe and beyond. English is commonly used in engineering and product teams, though German dominates in field operations.

For Indian professionals considering relocation, Berlin is among the more welcoming German cities for international hires. Cost of living, visa pathways, and local community networks are all worth researching independently before committing to a move.

How many jobs is Thermondo currently hiring for?

As of July 2026, Thermondo has 199 open roles listed across its hiring channels. These span field operations, engineering, product, sales, customer support, and corporate functions. Do I need to speak German to work at Thermondo?

For most roles based in Germany, particularly in field operations, sales, and customer support, German at a working level is a firm requirement. Publicly shared candidate feedback commonly describes B2 level or higher as the expectation. Some engineering and product roles in Berlin may be more flexible, but this varies by team. If language is a concern, clarify it during the recruiter screen rather than waiting until later in the process.

Thermondo has hired internationally before, and Germany's skilled worker visa pathways make it possible for qualified candidates from India to be sponsored.
