01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

IDfy is a Bengaluru-based company building identity verification and background screening infrastructure for India. Banks, fintechs, NBFCs, insurance companies, and large enterprises use IDfy to verify customers, screen job candidates, and detect fraud before it happens.

The company sits at the intersection of compliance, data, and product engineering, which makes it attractive to professionals in tech, product, operations, and enterprise sales. As of July 2026, IDfy has 13 open roles across teams. If you have been keeping an eye on them, now is a good time to apply.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the IDfy hiring process typically runs through 4 to 5 rounds. The exact stages vary by role and seniority level.

Round 1: Recruiter screening
A call with HR covering your background, notice period, location preference, and why you want to join IDfy. This round is a quick filter, so keep your answers clear and to the point.

Round 2: Technical or domain round
For engineering roles, expect coding problems and sometimes system design, particularly around API architecture or processing high-volume data reliably. For sales, product, or operations roles, this round focuses on domain knowledge and your track record.

Round 3: Managerial round
A senior manager or team lead explores how you approach problems, collaborate across teams, and handle pressure or ambiguity. Behavioral questions are common here. Prepare specific examples from past roles.

Round 4: Leadership round (senior roles only)
For specialist or senior positions, a VP or founding team member may join. The conversation usually centers on strategic thinking and alignment with the company's direction.

Offer stage
Since IDfy runs background verification for other organizations, they are notably thorough about their own background checks. Keep employment documents, education certificates, and references ready before the offer letter arrives.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

IDfy operates in a trust-critical industry. They look for a specific mix of skills and mindset across roles.

Attention to detail: Their products handle identity data for regulated industries. Errors are costly, so they consistently value people who are careful and precise.

Problem-solving under ambiguity: Engineering and product teams often work with incomplete or inconsistent data from third-party sources. They want people who can navigate uncertainty without freezing up.

Domain familiarity: Experience in KYC, AML compliance, background verification, fintech, or fraud detection is a genuine advantage, especially for product, sales, and operations roles. It is not always mandatory for tech roles, but it helps.

Ownership mindset: IDfy is a growth-stage company. They look for people who spot a gap and close it without waiting to be told. Proactiveness is consistently valued in interviews and on the job.

Communication skills: For client-facing and cross-functional roles, the ability to explain technical or compliance concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders (such as a bank's procurement team) carries real weight in the interview process.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Understand the product before your first round. Go through IDfy's offerings: background checks, KYC APIs, address verification, onboarding solutions, and fraud risk intelligence. Candidates who connect their experience to a specific product problem stand out from those who give generic answers about 'wanting to work in fintech.'

For engineering roles, practice coding with a focus on API design, data pipelines, and handling scale. System design questions around verification workflows and third-party integrations come up. Review your experience with cloud platforms and microservices if relevant to the role.

For sales and business development roles, prepare concrete examples of enterprise sales cycles you have managed or contributed to. Be ready to explain how you navigated procurement, legal reviews, and technical evaluations inside large banks or enterprises. Vague answers do not land well here.

For operations and compliance roles, revisit your experience with SLA management, process documentation, and coordination across internal teams and external vendors. The more specific you can be about outcomes you owned, the better.

Follow IDfy on LinkedIn and scan recent press releases or product announcements. Mentioning something current in the interview signals genuine preparation, not just surface-level interest.

Tailor your resume to the specific role. Highlight fintech, compliance, data, or verification experience clearly. A generic resume rarely clears the initial screening for a company that deals with regulated enterprise clients.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

IDfy builds infrastructure for trust, and that priority shows in the internal culture. Employees commonly describe the environment as fast-paced with a strong emphasis on accuracy and accountability.

The company has a relatively flat structure for its size. Individual contributors often interact directly with senior leadership, which creates opportunity for people who want visibility and meaningful impact. The flip side is that a high degree of autonomy is expected: you will not be micromanaged, but you are also fully responsible for your outcomes.

Because their clients include banks and regulated financial institutions, there is a deep culture of rigor around data handling and compliance. People who are comfortable working within regulatory constraints while still moving quickly tend to thrive here.

The team is primarily based in Bengaluru, with some hybrid arrangements depending on the role. Collaboration across product, tech, and operations is frequent, so clear cross-functional communication matters more than it might at a purely engineering-first company.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open roles does IDfy currently have?

As of July 2026, IDfy has 13 open positions across different teams and functions, including engineering, product, sales, and operations. This number shifts as roles are filled and new ones open. Checking their careers page regularly or using a job aggregator is the easiest way to stay updated.

What salary can I expect at IDfy?

Specific IDfy compensation data is limited in public sources, but industry surveys and Glassdoor reviews suggest that mid-stage product companies in Bengaluru typically offer packages competitive with the broader fintech and B2B SaaS market. Your offer will depend on role, seniority, and the specific skills you bring. It is worth looking up the role on Glassdoor and levels.fyi before you enter salary discussions.

Does IDfy hire freshers or entry-level candidates?

IDfy primarily hires experienced professionals, particularly for technical, sales, and client-facing roles. That said, they do occasionally bring in junior candidates for data operations, QA, and entry-level engineering positions. If you are a fresher, look specifically for roles in the job description that mention 0 to 1 year of experience.

How long does the IDfy hiring process take?

Based on publicly reported candidate experiences, the process typically takes 2 to 4 weeks from the first recruiter call to receiving an offer. Senior or specialist roles may take slightly longer due to additional rounds. Following up with the recruiter after each stage is completely normal and often helps move the process along.

Do I need background verification experience to apply?

For most engineering and product roles, direct background verification experience is a plus but not a hard requirement. IDfy often hires from adjacent spaces like fintech, SaaS, banking technology, and data platforms. For sales and operations roles, fintech or compliance experience carries more weight and is frequently listed as preferred in the job description.

What should I do if I do not hear back after applying?

Wait about a week, then follow up directly with the recruiter on LinkedIn or by email. IDfy receives a large volume of applications, and a polite nudge often helps your profile get reviewed. Make sure your resume is tailored to the specific role, with any fintech, compliance, or verification experience highlighted prominently near the top.
