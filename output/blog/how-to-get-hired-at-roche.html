01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Roche is one of the largest pharmaceutical and diagnostics companies in the world, and it has a meaningful and growing footprint in India. The company hires across a wide range of functions: medical affairs, commercial, diagnostics, supply chain, regulatory affairs, data science, technology, and finance. As of mid-2026, Roche has 49 open roles listed, which points to active hiring across levels and functions.

India operations are mainly based in Mumbai, Pune, and Bengaluru, with some roles offering remote or hybrid flexibility. Roche is known for being selective, but candidates who understand the company's mission and can clearly link their experience to patient impact tend to do well. The hiring timeline can range from a few weeks to a couple of months depending on the role and seniority.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

The Roche interview process is typically structured across four to five stages, though the exact sequence depends on the role, function, and level you are applying for. Here is how it commonly unfolds.

Application and initial screening
You apply through Roche's careers portal. Recruiters review resumes for both technical fit and cultural alignment. A tailored resume that mirrors the job description significantly improves your chances of moving forward.

Recruiter call
If your profile clears the screening, a Roche recruiter or HR partner will reach out for an introductory call. This conversation covers your background, your reason for wanting to join Roche specifically, your location preferences, and a broad check on expectations. Be honest and prepared to explain why Roche over other pharma companies.

Functional or technical interview
This is the core of the process. You will speak with the hiring manager or a domain expert about your work experience, technical knowledge, and how you have handled challenges in your field. For roles in data, tech, or analytics, expect a short exercise or case study. For commercial or medical roles, expect scenario-based questions.

Panel or leadership interview
Mid-level and senior roles often include a round with a cross-functional panel or a senior leader. The focus here shifts to leadership behaviour, strategic thinking, and how you embody Roche's values in your day-to-day work.

Offer and background check
Once a decision is made, Roche typically extends a verbal offer before the formal letter. A background verification process follows before your joining date is confirmed.

With 49 open roles currently listed, there are positions across multiple teams, so the exact process you go through will vary. Candidates for global or hybrid roles may also have an additional video interview with an international stakeholder.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Roche looks for a specific combination of domain depth and personal values. Being technically strong is important, but it is rarely enough on its own. Here is what stands out in a Roche interview.

Patient-first thinking
Roche's entire identity is built around improving patient outcomes. Whether you are in supply chain, IT, or sales, interviewers will probe for moments when you connected your work to a larger purpose. Have a story ready about how your work helped a customer, a patient, or an end user.

Cross-functional collaboration
Roche operates in a matrixed environment where teams across departments need to work together. Candidates who show they can influence without authority and build relationships across functions tend to do well.

Problem-solving with data
Across most functions, Roche values structured, evidence-based thinking. Be ready to walk through how you approach ambiguous problems, what information you would seek, and how you arrive at a recommendation.

Adaptability and a learning mindset
The pharma and diagnostics space moves fast. Roche values people who actively seek to learn, adapt to change, and can operate in uncertain conditions without losing focus.

Integrity and transparency
This is non-negotiable at Roche. The company has a strong compliance culture. Any signal that a candidate bends rules or downplays failures is likely to be a dealbreaker.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Preparing well for a Roche interview is about showing both capability and character. Here is a practical approach.

Research Roche's pipeline and recent news
Before any interview, read about Roche's key therapeutic areas, recent product launches, and any news from their India operations. Mentioning something specific about Roche's work, rather than generic pharma talking points, signals genuine interest.

Prepare STAR stories for your key experiences
The Situation, Task, Action, Result format works well for Roche interviews. Prepare four to six strong stories covering collaboration, handling failure, driving results, and showing patient or customer impact. Rehearse them until they feel natural, not scripted.

Know Roche's values
Roche's stated values are Integrity, Courage, and Passion. Think about how your past work reflects each of these. Interviewers often ask directly about moments that illustrate these qualities.

Prepare smart questions
Ask about team structure, current challenges the team is solving, and how success is measured in the role. Avoid asking about salary in early rounds. Questions that show you have genuinely read about Roche's work are always well received.

Practice for the functional round
For technical or functional roles, revisit core concepts in your domain. If the job description mentions a specific tool, methodology, or therapeutic area, prepare to speak fluently about it. For commercial roles, brush up on the market landscape for Roche's key products.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Roche describes its culture around a simple idea: 'Doing now what patients need next.' This is not just a tagline; it shapes how teams are structured and how decisions get made internally.

Innovation over hierarchy
Roche has made deliberate efforts in recent years to reduce bureaucracy and give teams more autonomy to move quickly. Employees across levels are encouraged to challenge assumptions and propose new approaches.

Diversity and inclusion
Roche has publicly committed to building diverse teams. For India operations, this includes representation across gender, background, and experience. New joiners often cite inclusive team environments as a consistent highlight.

Collaboration across borders
Many India-based roles connect to global teams. You will likely work with colleagues in Switzerland, the US, or Southeast Asia. This cross-border exposure is one of the more valued aspects of working at Roche India.

Learning and development
Roche invests in employee growth through internal mobility, training platforms, and mentorship. Publicly available employee reviews on Glassdoor note learning opportunities as a consistent positive across multiple years.

Work-life balance
As commonly cited in industry surveys and employee forums, work-life balance at Roche varies by function. Commercial and field roles can be demanding, while corporate and tech roles tend to offer more flexibility. Hybrid and remote options exist for several India-based positions.

Q Questions

Frequently asked

How many open positions does Roche currently have?

As of mid-2026, Roche has 49 open roles listed across functions including medical affairs, diagnostics, technology, commercial, supply chain, and more. The number shifts regularly as roles are filled and new ones open. Not all listings are India-specific, so read each one carefully to confirm the location before applying.

How long does the Roche hiring process typically take from application to offer?

The timeline varies by role and seniority. For individual contributor roles, the process can wrap up in a few weeks. Senior or specialist roles with multiple rounds, including global stakeholders, can take a couple of months. Roche's recruiters are generally responsive, so if you have been waiting more than two weeks after completing a round, a polite follow-up is appropriate.

Do I need a pharma background to get a job at Roche?

It depends on the role. For medical, regulatory, and commercial positions, a pharma or life sciences background is typically required or strongly preferred. For technology, data science, finance, and supply chain roles, transferable skills from other industries are often accepted. Roche values the ability to learn the domain quickly, so demonstrating genuine eagerness and a willingness to upskill can help if your background is from outside pharma.

Does Roche hire freshers or only experienced candidates?

Roche does hire freshers, primarily through internship-to-full-time conversions and specific graduate programs. Most open roles, however, require some work experience. If you are a fresher, look for Roche's internship programs or entry-level openings in functions like medical information, diagnostics support, or technology. Relevant certifications and project work can meaningfully strengthen a fresher application.

What is the best way to get noticed by Roche recruiters?

Applying directly through Roche's careers portal gives you the cleanest paper trail. Tailoring your resume to mirror keywords in the job description helps pass initial screening tools. Connecting with current Roche employees on LinkedIn and expressing genuine interest in their work can also put you on a recruiter's radar. Referrals from existing employees carry significant weight, so if you know anyone at the company, a short conversation is worth pursuing.

What kind of interview questions should I expect at Roche?

Roche interviews are a mix of behavioural and functional questions. Behavioural questions follow a 'Tell me about a time when...' format and probe for moments of collaboration, handling failure, patient or customer focus, and integrity. Functional questions test your domain knowledge and problem-solving approach. For senior roles, expect strategic questions about how you would tackle a specific challenge the team is currently facing.
