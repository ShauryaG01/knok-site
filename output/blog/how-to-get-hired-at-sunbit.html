01 Hiring Overview

Hiring Overview

Sunbit is a US-based fintech company that powers buy-now-pay-later technology at physical point-of-sale locations, covering auto dealerships, healthcare clinics, and specialty retail stores. The company connects consumers with financing options at the moment of service, making it distinct from purely e-commerce BNPL players. As of mid-2026, Sunbit has 56 open roles across engineering, product, sales, and operations. Most positions are based in the US and Israel, though some remote-friendly technical and operations roles may be accessible to candidates in India or other geographies.

If you are eyeing Sunbit, this is an active hiring window. The company is growing its product and go-to-market teams, and competition for roles is lower than at large tech giants, which works in a well-prepared candidate's favour.

03 Interview Process

Interview Process

Sunbit's process typically runs across several stages. The exact structure varies by role and team, so treat this as a general guide based on commonly reported candidate experiences.

Application review: Your resume is screened against the job description. Tailoring your application to highlight fintech, payments, or SaaS experience helps you clear this step faster.

Recruiter screen: A talent acquisition partner reaches out for a short introductory call. Expect questions about your background, availability, and basic expectations around location and compensation. Be ready to explain clearly why you want to join Sunbit specifically, not just any fintech company.

Technical or skills assessment: For engineering roles, this usually takes the form of a coding challenge or a take-home assignment. For business, product, or operations roles, you may receive a case study or a role-specific task. This stage is designed to verify hands-on ability before both sides invest more time.

Hiring manager interview: This is a deeper conversation with the person who would be your direct manager. Expect a mix of experience-based questions ('walk me through a time when...') and questions about your approach to the role's core problems. Fintech context, even from adjacent domains like banking or insurance, tends to resonate well here.

Panel or cross-functional interviews: You may meet a few team members in back-to-back conversations. These rounds typically combine technical depth with questions about collaboration and culture. Sunbit values people who can work across functions, so be ready to talk about how you have partnered with other teams in past roles.

Reference check and offer: If the panel rounds go well, the recruiter typically follows up for references and moves to the offer stage. The full process from first contact to offer commonly takes a few weeks, though timelines vary by role and pipeline.

04 What They Look For

What They Look For

Sunbit is building financial technology that works in the physical world, which means they care about candidates who understand both the technical and human sides of a product.

Fintech or payments context: Direct experience with lending, payments, or point-of-sale technology is a strong plus. If you do not have it, adjacent experience in banking software, insurance tech, or e-commerce platforms is the next best thing.

Ownership and initiative: Sunbit is a growth-stage company, not a large corporation with a defined playbook for every situation. Candidates who show they can identify a problem and drive it to resolution, without waiting for instructions at each step, stand out in interviews.

Cross-functional collaboration: Many roles at Sunbit sit at the intersection of product, engineering, and business. Interviewers frequently ask how you have worked with people outside your immediate team to ship something or solve a hard problem.

Clear communication: Whether you are in engineering or sales, the ability to explain complex ideas simply is valued. This matters especially if you are working across time zones with US-based colleagues.

Data fluency: Even non-technical roles benefit from comfort with data. Being able to look at a metric, form a hypothesis, and propose a test is a skill the company prizes across functions.

05 How To Prepare

How To Prepare

Research the product deeply: Understand how Sunbit's financing flow works at a dealership or clinic before your interview. Interviewers notice when a candidate has thought about the product beyond the job description.

Prepare STAR-format stories: Sunbit interviewers rely heavily on behavioural questions. Have several stories ready that cover: a time you delivered under pressure, a time you disagreed with a decision and how you handled it, a time you worked across teams, and a time you failed and what you learned.

Know your numbers: For any role that touches business outcomes, be ready to talk about the impact of your past work in concrete terms. If you improved a process, how did you measure it? If you built a feature, what happened after launch?

Brush up on fintech basics: If your background is not in finance, spend time understanding terms like APR, underwriting, credit risk, and point-of-sale lending. You do not need to be an expert, but you should be able to have an informed conversation.

Prepare smart questions: Ask about team structure, how product priorities are set, and what success looks like in the first few months on the job. Avoid asking questions whose answers are already on the careers page.

Follow up after each round: A short, specific thank-you note after each interview round is good practice. Reference something concrete from the conversation to show you were genuinely engaged.

06 Culture And Values

Culture And Values

Sunbit positions itself around making financing accessible at the moment a person needs it, whether that is a car repair, a dental procedure, or a home appliance. The internal culture reflects this mission: teams tend to move quickly, decisions are made close to the work, and people are expected to take ownership rather than escalate everything upward.

Growth-stage energy: Sunbit is past the scrappy startup phase but not yet a large enterprise. That means you will likely wear more than one hat, processes are still being built, and your contributions are visible. Candidates who thrive here tend to be comfortable with some ambiguity and see it as an opportunity rather than a problem.

Global team, US-centric decisions: Sunbit has offices in the US and Israel. If you are joining from India or another remote location, expect your primary stakeholders to be in a significantly different time zone. Flexibility in working hours and strong async communication habits matter.

Mission alignment matters: Sunbit interviewers genuinely want to know if you believe in what the company is doing. Being able to articulate why accessible consumer financing is a meaningful problem, and why Sunbit's approach is interesting, goes a long way in culture-fit conversations.

Frequently asked

Does Sunbit hire candidates based in India?

Sunbit's primary offices are in the US and Israel, and most roles are designed for candidates in those locations. That said, some remote-friendly technical and operations roles have been listed without a strict location requirement. Your best approach is to apply directly, note your location clearly, and ask the recruiter early in the process about remote eligibility for the specific role you are targeting.

What is the typical salary range for Sunbit roles?

Sunbit does not publicly disclose salary bands for most roles. For US-based positions, publicly reported figures on Glassdoor and levels.fyi suggest compensation varies widely by function and seniority. If you are applying for a role based in India or as a remote contractor, the compensation structure is likely to differ from US packages, so it is worth clarifying this directly with the recruiter during the initial screen call.

How long does Sunbit's hiring process take from application to offer?

Based on commonly reported candidate experiences, the process from first contact to offer typically takes a few weeks. Engineering roles with a take-home assessment can run slightly longer than business or operations roles. If you have not heard back after submitting your application, a polite follow-up email to the recruiter after a couple of weeks is reasonable.

What technical skills does Sunbit look for in engineering roles?

Sunbit's engineering stack is not fully disclosed publicly, but the company builds web and mobile products in the fintech space, so experience with backend services, APIs, and data pipelines is commonly relevant. Job descriptions for specific roles are the most reliable source for required skills. Review the tech stack mentioned in each posting carefully before you apply.

Is there a coding test in Sunbit's interview process?

For engineering roles, a coding assessment is a commonly reported part of the process, either as a timed online challenge or a take-home assignment. The format can vary by team and role level. Practising data structures, algorithms, and system design concepts is a safe preparation strategy regardless of the exact format you encounter.

How competitive is it to get a job at Sunbit?

Sunbit is a growth-stage company with a focused product, which means it tends to attract candidates who are specifically interested in fintech rather than a broad pool chasing brand names. With 56 open roles currently listed, the company is actively hiring, which generally improves your odds compared to periods when hiring is frozen. A tailored resume and solid preparation for behavioural questions are your strongest tools going in.
